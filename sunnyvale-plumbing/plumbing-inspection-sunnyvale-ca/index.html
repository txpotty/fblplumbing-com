    A plumbing inspection is a deeper look at one system. Home inspectors cover the basics. A general home inspector runs faucets and flushes toilets, while our plumbers run sewer cameras, test pressure, identify pipe types and flag code issues that fall outside a general inspection.

    Looking for drips and watching the meter is worth doing on your own. It has limits. A plumber's tools reach the sewer and the pipe inside walls, which is where the costly problems usually are.

    Watch for a water bill that jumps for no reason, low pressure, slow drains or brown water. Noisy drains are a clue. A damp wall, a musty cabinet or a warm spot on the floor can mean a hidden leak, and an inspection finds it before it spreads.

    A standard home inspection takes about one and a half to three hours. Sewer video adds time. Big houses, long sewers or a leak that needs tracking can stretch it, and we give you a time estimate when you schedule.

    Most residential inspections in Sunnyvale take one to two hours. Age and size matter. The exact time depends on how big the house is, how old the plumbing is and whether we find something that needs extra tracing, such as a hidden leak or a sewer defect.

    High water bills, repeat clogs, slow drains and discolored water are common signs. Noisy pipes are another.
