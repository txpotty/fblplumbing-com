    Emergency Water Softener Repair in Sunnyvale

    A softener stuck in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours or flood a utility room. We handle these calls quickly.     Softener Not Regenerating in Sunnyvale

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. A failed timer motor, a bad circuit board or a stuck meter turbine are the usual causes, along with settings lost in a power outage. We test each part. After the repair, the valve is programmed for your household size and hardness so you have soft water by the next cycle.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Sunnyvale

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. Older systems on chlorinated city water see it most. FBL Plumbing pulls the old resin and fills the tank with new media. Capacity comes back right away.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Sunnyvale

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads so they cannot grab hardness. We test the resin, clean it with a resin cleaner or replace it. New resin restores full capacity.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Sunnyvale, CA

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Its small ports clog with sediment and salt. Once it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying, and the softener regenerates with plain water that does nothing to the resin. The injector and screen get cleaned or replaced, and the brine line gets checked.

    Control Valve Repair in Sunnyvale

    Water that runs to the drain all day usually means the control valve is stuck between cycles. FBL Plumbing rebuilds stuck valves with the correct seal and spacer kit for your model. Mechanical or electronic, we fix the timing. Parts for the common valves are stocked, which keeps most repairs to one visit.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Service in Sunnyvale, CA

    The tank looks full but makes no brine. A hard crust has formed and is keeping the salt from dissolving. Bridges get broken up, mushy salt gets cleaned out and the float, brine well and safety valve get checked. In a hot or damp garage, switching to pellets cuts down on bridging.
