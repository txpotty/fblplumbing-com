    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Sunnyvale

    Hard water builds scale on tankless heating elements, which cuts efficiency and can trigger error codes. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. We recommend a softener or a scale inhibitor ahead of the unit, and every install gets service valves so the heater can be flushed with a descaling solution once a year. It is a short service.
