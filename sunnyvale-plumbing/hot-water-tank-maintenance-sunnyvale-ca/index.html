    Electric Hot Water Tank Element Check in Sunnyvale

    Electric hot water tanks rely on heating elements to warm the water. During maintenance, our plumbers inspect these elements for signs of wear, corrosion or burnout. We also check the thermostat settings and wiring connections to confirm they are functioning correctly and safely. This preventive check can identify failing elements before they stop heating altogether, avoiding unexpected cold showers for your family in Sunnyvale.

    Sediment Flushing & Cleaning in Sunnyvale, CA

    Over time, minerals and sediment settle at the bottom of hot water tanks, reducing efficiency and possibly leading to premature failure. Our maintenance includes a thorough flushing of the tank to remove these deposits. This process helps your tank heat water more evenly and quietly, a vital step for any hot water tank in Sunnyvale, CA, where mineral content in the water is a frequent concern.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Service in Sunnyvale, CA

    Many modern plumbing systems include an expansion tank connected to the water heater. This tank takes up excess pressure created during the heating cycle, protecting your hot water tank and other plumbing fixtures. Our maintenance includes checking the expansion tank's air pressure and overall condition. Proper expansion tank function is key to preventing premature wear on your hot water tank and maintaining system integrity.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Sunnyvale, CA

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is essential for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank. Water chemistry varies from one neighborhood to the next, and it changes the rod's lifespan. Our technicians inspect the anode rod for wear and recommend replacement if it's badly corroded, typically every 3 to 5 years. Replacing a worn anode rod is a cost-effective way to protect your tank from rust and leaks, saving you from a full tank replacement.

    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Sunnyvale

    A properly working temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank. Our technicians will test this valve to confirm it's not stuck or leaking, preventing dangerous pressure buildup within the tank. We verify it opens and closes correctly, and if it fails, we will suggest immediate replacement to keep your Sunnyvale home safe and compliant with plumbing codes.

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Sunnyvale, CA

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can block the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a steady blue flame and lower gas bills.
