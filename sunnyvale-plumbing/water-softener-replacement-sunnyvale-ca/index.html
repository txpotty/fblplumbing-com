    Picking the Right Softener for Sunnyvale, CA Water

    Replacing Pelican Water Systems in Sunnyvale

    Pelican systems usually combine a carbon filter with either a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner in one setup on the main line. Depending on how old it is, we swap media or the full unit. Installed to spec, the warranty stays valid.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Sunnyvale, CA

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in Northern California homes. If the valve is old enough that parts are hard to find, a new valve on a new tank is the smarter buy. Newer Fleck heads drop right in.

    Salt-Free Alternatives in Sunnyvale, CA

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. In those cases a TAC conditioner works, though we are upfront that it stops scale and does not make water feel soft. Sodium concerns are another reason.

    Commercial Softener Replacement in Sunnyvale

    Espresso machines and commercial dishwashers scale up fast on hard water, and the service bills for descaling them are steep. Sizing comes from peak flow and daily use. Sometimes a carbon filter goes ahead of it.

    Softener Failure Diagnosis in Sunnyvale, CA

    A clogged injector, a dead motor or a wrong setting can make a good softener look finished. Hardness gets tested on both sides of the unit, and the cheap fixes get tried before we talk replacement. Obsolete valves are another story.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Sunnyvale, CA

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. Rebedding with new resin can be cheaper than a new system. It depends on the valve. New resin under a tired valve rarely lasts.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Sunnyvale

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico runs them without electricity, using water flow to drive the valve. They cost more.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Sunnyvale

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. A metered valve, Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1, tracks gallons and regenerates when the resin actually needs it. Salt bills usually drop in the first month.

    A local plumber can also check how the softener ties into your water heater and main line. Leaking loops, missing air gaps and bypass valves left half open all hurt performance, and we catch them. You get a clear quote.

    Local water varies. City water and well water a few miles apart can test very differently, and iron or chlorine changes the setup. The system matches your test, not a guess.
