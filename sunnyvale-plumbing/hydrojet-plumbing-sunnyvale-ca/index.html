    Why Choose FBL Plumbing for Hydrojet Plumbing in Sunnyvale, CA?

    We match pressure and flow to the pipe in front of us. Fragile pipe gets lower pressure. PVC and sound cast iron can take full pressure, but thin-wall pipe, old Orangeburg or a line with a known crack gets lower pressure and a different nozzle, or no jetting at all if the camera shows it cannot handle it.

    • Fewer Repeat Clogs, Removing the buildup cuts down on repeat clogs.

    Drain and Sewer Jetting Services in Sunnyvale, CA

    Whole-House Drain Jetting in Sunnyvale

    Slow drains in several rooms point to buildup in the main or a branch line. The main gets jetted from the cleanout, and branches from vents or fixtures. Most homes take two to three hours.

    Root Removal Jetting in Sunnyvale

    Shade trees and shrubs send roots toward small cracks in clay and cast iron pipe. Root-cutting nozzles clear them. Jetting does not stop roots from returning, because the crack is still there, but it clears the pipe completely and lets us see on camera whether a spot repair or a liner would stop the regrowth.

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Sunnyvale, CA

    Restaurant kitchen lines send warm grease downstream where it cools and sticks. By the time it reaches the interceptor, a lot of it has already coated the pipe. We jet the line from the kitchen to the interceptor in short passes, which keeps loosened grease from plugging the pipe farther down, and we coordinate with your pump-out company. Quarterly works for most.

    Sewer Lateral Jetting in Sunnyvale, CA

    The lateral from your house to the street carries everything, so buildup there causes backups everywhere at once. The whole lateral gets jetted to the tap. Low spots where the pipe has settled hold water and collect sludge, so we spend extra passes there and mark the location on the camera footage in case a repair makes sense later.

    Storm and Yard Drain Jetting in Sunnyvale, CA

    Yard drains, channel drains and downspout lines fill with sand, silt and leaves between storms. Drought years alternate with very wet years here, so conservation rules and efficient fixtures are a constant part of plumbing decisions. A clogged yard drain sends that water back toward the house, so we jet the lines from the outlet end and flush the catch basins before the wet season starts. We also check the pop-up emitters.

    Emergency Hydrojet Services in Sunnyvale, CA

    A full sewer backup needs a truck today. We clear it first. A cable opens it, then the jetter removes the grease, roots or wipes that caused it.

    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Sunnyvale

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. Jetting the main clears roots, grease and sludge all the way to the city connection. Four-inch mains get a heavy nozzle and high flow to push debris downstream.

    Sewer Camera Inspection Before Hydrojetting in Sunnyvale

    Jetting a broken pipe can make the break worse. The camera always goes in first. Footage shows the material and joint condition, which sets our nozzle and pressure.
