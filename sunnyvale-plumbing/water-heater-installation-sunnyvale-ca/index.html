    Tank and Tankless Water Heaters in Sunnyvale, CA

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Sunnyvale

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it. Sizing is based on the first-hour rating on the yellow label, which tells you how much hot water the tank can deliver during a busy hour. Either fuel works. We connect the fuel, set the vent and haul the old tank away.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Sunnyvale

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. An attic heater needs a pan with its own drain line, a properly routed relief line and enough access to get the old tank out and the new one in. A leak lands on drywall. If moving it makes sense, we can relocate the heater.

    Commercial Water Heater Installation in Sunnyvale

    Restaurants, salons and offices burn through hot water at rates no household tank can keep up with. Our commercial work covers high-BTU tanks and multi-unit tankless racks, along with the larger gas lines, venting and code inspections that come with them. We schedule around your hours. Night installs are available.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Sunnyvale, CA

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. We confirm the breaker and wire size match the new unit before connecting it, since many older homes have the wrong gauge. No venting needed. That makes placement easier.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Sunnyvale

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. Each gas swap gets a new connector, a drip leg, a draft test at the hood and a leak check with a detector at every fitting. Venting is what keeps CO out. All work follows local code.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Sunnyvale

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Every job gets a fresh connector, a drip leg, a shutoff and a vent connector with the right rise toward the chimney or flue. Then a draft test. Big families do well with gas.

    Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in Sunnyvale

    Each manufacturer publishes its own install rules, and they are not identical. Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White and Navien each spell out clearances, vent lengths and relief valve placement, and a warranty claim can be denied if those were ignored. Our crews install to spec. Your warranty stays intact.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Sunnyvale, CA

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match the voltage and breaker size to your panel, and because electric tanks need no vent, they can go in a closet, an attic or a laundry room where a gas unit could not. Closets and attics get a pan. Quiet and simple.
