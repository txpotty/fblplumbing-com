    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Sunnyvale, CA

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Vibration and settling loosen threaded joints over time, and pipe in damp garages or outside walls can rust through from the surface. Bad sections get replaced with new pipe. Outdoor runs get coated or painted after the repair so rust does not start again.

    Permits and Code Compliance for Gas Work in Sunnyvale, CA

    Most gas piping work beyond a simple connector swap needs a permit in Sunnyvale, CA. The inspector checks pipe sizing, material, support spacing, shutoff locations, sediment traps and the pressure test result before signing off. We handle the paperwork. A permitted repair also gives you a paper trail for insurance and for any future sale.

    Honest Gas Line Repair Pricing in Sunnyvale, CA

    Propane Gas Line Service in Sunnyvale, CA

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. We repair buried and above-ground propane lines, replace leaking regulators and fittings and check that each appliance has been converted with the correct orifices for propane. Wrong orifices make a burner run dirty and unsafe. The test procedure is the same as for natural gas.

    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Sunnyvale

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. We back out the leaking joint, clean the threads, apply gas-rated thread sealant and reassemble it, or replace the fitting or valve if the threads are damaged. Every repair gets a soap test and a system pressure test before we leave.

    Gas Permits and Inspections in Sunnyvale

    Repairs that change piping, and all new gas lines, usually need a permit and inspection from Sunnyvale building officials. We pull the permit, book the inspection and meet the inspector at the gauge so you do not have to. Inspection usually happens within a few days. After it passes, the gas company can restore service, and we can return to relight the pilots.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Sunnyvale

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. New coated stainless connectors go on, sized to the appliance, with a reachable shutoff valve if the old one is stuck or missing. This is a quick fix. Furnaces, water heaters, ranges and dryers each need a sound connection, and most need a drip leg before the appliance valve.
