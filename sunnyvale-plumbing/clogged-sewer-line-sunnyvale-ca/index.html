    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Sunnyvale, CA

    One slow drain is a branch clog. Several slow drains is the main. Water rising in the shower when you flush is a strong sign the clog is past the point where those two lines join, so the pattern of slow drains tells us where to go in. The right entry point saves time.

    Hydro-Jetting for Stubborn Clogs in Sunnyvale

    Some lines get cabled three times a year and still clog. The cable is not the fix. Those lines are usually coated in grease or scale, and the cable only bores a narrow path through the middle that closes up again within weeks. Jetting removes the coating down to the pipe wall. The results hold up far better.

    Cleaning vs. Repairing a Sewer Line in Sunnyvale, CA

    A sound pipe with a grease or root clog just needs cleaning. Broken pipe needs repair. Offset joints, cracks and collapsed sections keep clogging no matter how often they get cleared, so the camera footage decides whether you spend a few hundred dollars on cleaning or more on a spot fix, liner or replacement. We walk you through it.

    Grease and Wipes Sewer Blockages in Sunnyvale, CA

    Flushable wipes do not break down the way toilet paper does. They stay intact for weeks. Mixed with grease that hardened in the main, they form a dense plug that a cable can poke a hole through but not fully remove from the pipe. Jetting scours the walls and flushes the debris downstream. Wipes belong in the garbage.
