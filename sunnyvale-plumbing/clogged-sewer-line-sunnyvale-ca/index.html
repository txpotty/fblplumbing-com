    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Sunnyvale, CA

    Understanding the difference between a main sewer line clog and a branch line clog is vital for proper diagnosis and repair in Sunnyvale, CA. A main line clog affects all drains in your home, often causing backups in lower-level fixtures. Branch line clogs are usually isolated to a single fixture or area. Our technicians are skilled at pinpointing the exact location of the blockage, whether it is in the main line or a specific branch, for a targeted and efficient repair.

    Tree Root Sewer Clogs in Sunnyvale

    Roots from thirsty trees and shrubs seek out moisture and push into sewer pipe joints. Clay and older cast iron pipe are especially prone to root intrusion because of their many joints. We cut roots with a sewer machine fitted with root saws and then camera the line to see how much damage remains. For ongoing root problems, we can recommend a root treatment schedule or pipe lining.

    Grease and Wipes Sewer Blockages in Sunnyvale, CA

    Cooking grease cools and hardens in the main line, and so-called flushable wipes do not break down the way toilet paper does. Together they form dense plugs that a cable may only punch a hole through. Hydro jetting at high pressure scours the pipe walls clean and flushes debris downstream. We also share simple habits that help avoid another grease or wipe clog.
