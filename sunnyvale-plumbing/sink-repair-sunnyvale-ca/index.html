    Pop-Up Drain and Stopper Repair in Sunnyvale, CA

    When the stopper will not close, will not open or lets water slowly escape, the pop-up linkage is typically to blame. The pivot rod nut can leak, the clevis strap can slip, and hair wraps around the stopper body. We clean or replace the assembly and adjust it so the lift rod works smoothly. If the drain body itself is corroded, we replace it with a matching finish.

    Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Sunnyvale, CA

    A slow or completely blocked sink drain is a common nuisance in Sunnyvale homes. This can be caused by grease, food particles, hair, or sometimes mineral buildup from hard water. Our plumbers use professional tools like augers and hydro-jetting equipment to safely and effectively clear even the toughest clogs, restoring proper drainage to your kitchen or bathroom sink in Northern California.

    Leaking P-Trap Repair in Sunnyvale

    Most puddles under a bathroom sink start at the P-trap. Slip-joint washers flatten over time, plastic traps crack, and old chrome traps rust through from the inside. We replace the trap with a correctly sized 1-1/4 inch assembly, align the tailpiece and wall arm, and run water to confirm a dry seal. This is one of the fastest and least expensive fixes we do.

    Supply Line and Shutoff Valve Repair in Sunnyvale, CA

    Braided supply lines and angle stops are rated for years, not decades, and the rubber inside breaks down in warm cabinets. A failing line can burst without warning and flood a bathroom. We replace worn lines with stainless braided connectors and swap stuck multi-turn valves for quarter-turn stops. It is a small job that prevents a very expensive one.
