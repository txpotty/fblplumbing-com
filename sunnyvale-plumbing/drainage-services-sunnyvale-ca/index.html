    Kitchen, Bath and Laundry Drain Clearing in Sunnyvale

    Slow sinks, gurgling tubs and overflowing laundry standpipes typically come from grease, hair, soap scum and mineral scale. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe.     Channel and Trench Drains in Sunnyvale, CA

    Driveways and patios that slope toward the house can send storm runoff directly into a garage or under a door. A channel drain with a grate catches that sheet flow before it reaches the building.     Dry Well Installation in Sunnyvale, CA
