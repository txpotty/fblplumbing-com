    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. A worn 1222 causes drips or weak hot water, and replacing it takes about an hour with a puller. Older Moentrol valves take another cartridge. We carry both.

    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Sunnyvale, CA

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. A new valve makes sense for discontinued parts, a cracked body or an old faucet with no scald protection. You see both prices. Most people choose repair first.

    Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in Sunnyvale

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting. The old valve comes out, a pressure-balance valve goes in, and a remodel plate covers the extra holes. We usually work from behind the wall. Tile around the plate stays intact.

    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Sunnyvale

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler. The supply lines come up through the floor under the filler, which means cutting the slab or working from a crawlspace or basement, and the filler needs a mounting bracket anchored to the subfloor. Deck-mount fillers sit on the rim. Tub-only fillers need a temperature limit.

    Brand Tub Faucet Replacement in Sunnyvale
