    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Sunnyvale

    A reroute abandons the leaking pipe under the slab. New line goes overhead. For older Sunnyvale homes, this works well with aging copper or galvanized pipe, since it bypasses every foot of buried line on that run. We keep drywall cuts small at fixture walls.

    Ground Radar and Acoustic Locating in Sunnyvale

    Ground-penetrating radar shows what is inside the slab before we cut. Radar maps steel in the concrete. Combined with a listening device, radar finds leaks in any pipe type, even with traffic noise, since radar maps the route and the mic confirms the spot. We use both in Sunnyvale.

    Slab Leaks in Stores and Offices in Sunnyvale

    Retail stores, offices and restaurants on slabs develop the same leaks as houses. Tenants notice right away. We locate and repair commercial slab leaks with as little disruption as we can manage, often after hours, and we provide written documentation with photos for property managers and insurers.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Sunnyvale, CA

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Expansion wears them out. Each time hot water runs, the pipe expands against concrete and gravel, and recirculation pumps keep that cycle going all day, which grinds the pipe wall thin at the rub points. We repair or reroute the line and can adjust the pump to cut wear.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Sunnyvale, CA

    Spot repairs fit leaks in otherwise good pipe. It is a single small opening. The floor gets opened, the pipe replaced and tested, and the trench compacted and patched, ready for your flooring contractor.

    Thermal and Acoustic Tools in Sunnyvale

    A thermal camera shows the warm streak a hot water leak leaves on the floor. Cold lines show no heat. For those, an acoustic sensor listens through the concrete for the sound of water escaping, and we mark the spot where it peaks, whether the house is an older ranch or a newer two-story. Both tools ride on every Sunnyvale truck.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Sunnyvale

    Older copper under a slab tends to leak again. Rerouting ends that. New PEX runs through the attic and down the walls while the old pipe stays buried, with openings placed behind vanities or in closets.

    Slab Leak Detection in Sunnyvale

    The first test is the water meter. We shut off everything first. A meter that keeps turning tells us to isolate hot from cold, test each side and walk the losing line with a ground mic. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor.
