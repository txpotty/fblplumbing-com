    Frequently Asked Questions About Kitchen Sink Installation in Sunnyvale

    A basic drop-in kitchen sink install typically costs $300 to $600 for labor. Undermount and farmhouse installs usually run $450 to $1,200, and adding a disposal or relocating the drain adds $150 to $600.

    While DIY sink installation is possible, it is often best to hire a professional, especially for under-mount or farmhouse sinks. Improper installation can lead to leaks, water damage, and costly repairs down the line. Our plumbers deliver a correct, leak-free, and code-compliant setup.

    A simple drop-in swap is doable for experienced DIYers. Undermount, farmhouse and double-bowl installs with disposals are more complex and more likely to leak.

    Signs you might need a new kitchen sink include persistent leaks around the drain or edges, visible cracks or chips, severe staining that won't clean, rust spots, or a desire to upgrade your kitchen's look. If your current sink is old or damaged, replacement is often the best solution.

    We install all major brands of kitchen sinks, including but not limited to Kohler, Elkay, Moen, Blanco, Franke, and InSinkErator for disposal-ready sinks. Our plumbers are experienced with various materials and mounting styles from these well-known manufacturers.

    When choosing a new kitchen sink, consider your kitchen's style, material durability, and whether you need features like multiple basins or a disposal. Also, think about water hardness in your area, as some materials might show mineral deposits more than others. Make sure the sink size is compatible with your cabinet and countertop.

    FBL Plumbing can install all common types of kitchen sinks, including stainless steel, cast iron, composite, fireclay, and copper. We handle various mounting styles like top-mount, under-mount, and farmhouse (apron-front) sinks, delivering a professional fit for any material.

    Most standard kitchen sink installations can be completed within 2 to 4 hours. However, more complex jobs, such as installing a farmhouse sink, modifying cabinetry, or replacing significant plumbing lines, might take longer, potentially up to a full day.
