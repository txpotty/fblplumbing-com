    Efficient PEX Repiping in Sunnyvale, CA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and highly popular choice for repiping projects in Sunnyvale, CA. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also highly resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a reliable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Sunnyvale

    Copper piping offers exceptional durability, corrosion resistance, and a long lifespan, making it a premium choice for many homeowners in Sunnyvale. While the upfront cost is higher than PEX, copper provides superior rigidity and resistance to UV light if exposed. Our team meticulously solders each joint, creating a leak-proof and robust plumbing system that can withstand the test of time, including changing temperatures and local water conditions. Copper also naturally inhibits bacterial growth, contributing to cleaner water.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Sunnyvale

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to three days.

    Repiping for Hard Water Areas in Sunnyvale, CA

    Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. Water in many areas contains minerals that contribute to scale buildup inside pipes over time. This hard water scale narrows the pipe's diameter, reduces water pressure, and can speed up corrosion, especially in galvanized and older copper pipes. Our repipe specialists consider local water conditions, recommending materials like PEX or properly installed copper that are more resistant to mineral accumulation, supporting a longer-lasting plumbing system.

    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Sunnyvale

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Expansive clay soils are common across Northern California, and seasonal swelling and shrinking can crack slabs and buried sewer laterals. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the compromised pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use advanced detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.
