    Standard Disposal Installation in Sunnyvale

    Most households in Sunnyvale do well with a 1/3 to 1/2 horsepower continuous-feed unit.     Commercial Disposal Installation in Sunnyvale

    Commercial disposals run 1 to 5 horsepower and usually need a dedicated circuit, a control panel and a water solenoid. We check the requirement before we order anything.

    High-Capacity Disposal Installation in Sunnyvale, CA

    Busy kitchens get the most from a 1 HP unit, like an InSinkErator Evolution or a Waste King Legend model. These units weigh more. They get supported during mounting.

    High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in Sunnyvale
