    Frequently Asked Questions About Septic Tank Repair in Sunnyvale

    The cost of septic tank repair in Sunnyvale, CA can vary widely, typically ranging from $300 for minor issues like a broken baffle to $5,000 or more for major problems such as drain field remediation or tank replacement. Factors include the nature of the problem, required excavation and parts needed. FBL Plumbing provides upfront, transparent pricing after a thorough on-site diagnosis.

    The length of a septic tank repair depends on the complexity of the issue. Simple clog removals might take a few hours, while more extensive repairs like drain field work could span several days. We will provide an estimated timeline during our first assessment.

    Common signs include sewage odors around the tank or drain field, slow drains or backed-up toilets, lush green patches of grass over the drain field, gurgling sounds from pipes and standing water in your yard. If you notice any of these, call FBL Plumbing right away.

    We install effluent pumps from Zoeller, Liberty, Goulds and similar quality brands, along with matched float switches and alarm panels.

    Minor repairs like replacing a tee or pump usually do not. Tank replacement, field replacement or system changes usually require a permit from the local health authority, and we take care of the coordination.

    Frequent causes of septic tank failure include infrequent pumping, which lets solids accumulate, tree root intrusion into pipes or the tank, excessive water usage overloading the system, driving heavy vehicles over the drain field and using harsh chemicals that kill beneficial bacteria.     Soil type has a big effect on septic system performance.
