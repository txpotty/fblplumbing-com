    Kitchen Sink Clog Repair in Sunnyvale

    Kitchen sinks clog from grease that cools and hardens on the pipe wall. Then food scraps and soap pile on.     Floor Drain and Garage Drain Clearing in Sunnyvale

    Floor drains in garages, utility rooms and basements collect sand, dirt and debris. A dry trap lets sewer gas in. We clear the drain and the line behind it, refill the trap and install a trap seal if it rarely gets water. A sewer odor usually means the trap is empty.

    Bathroom Sink & Shower Clog Repair in Sunnyvale, CA

    Shower drains slow down a little each week as hair collects on the crossbars. One day the water just sits. We pull the strainer, hook the hair out with a drum auger made for small drains and run the cable through the trap to clear what has moved further down. A hair catcher costs a few dollars.

    Bathroom Sink and Vanity Drain Clogs in Sunnyvale, CA

    Vanity drains clog at the pop-up stopper first. Hair wraps around the rod. We pull and clean the stopper, clean the trap, cable the drain arm and swap out worn parts while we are there. Scale buildup is common there.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Clogs in Sunnyvale

    Cooking grease, coffee grounds and starchy food stick to the walls of a kitchen drain and harden. The pipe shrinks over time. We remove the trap, run a cable through the trap arm and branch line and clear the buildup back to the main stack, then follow with a small jetter on heavy grease. Wipe greasy pans before washing.

    Tub and Shower Drain Clogs in Sunnyvale

    Slow tubs are almost always hair and soap scum. Every so often it is a lost bottle cap.
