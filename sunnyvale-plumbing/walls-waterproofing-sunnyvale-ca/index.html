    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Sunnyvale, CA

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. We set a lidded basin below slab level, install a cast iron pump sized for the inflow, and add a battery backup or water-powered backup for the night the power goes out during a storm. Check valves matter. A missing check valve lets the discharge line drain back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed at least 10 feet from the house.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Sunnyvale

    Older neighborhoods in Northern California include prewar and postwar homes with galvanized supply lines and cast iron or clay sewer pipe that are past the end of their life.     Basement Wall Sealing in Sunnyvale, CA

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge.     Crack repairs take a few hours. Interior drain tile usually takes one to three days, and a full exterior dig around the whole house can run three to seven days or more depending on weather and how deep the footing sits. We give you a timeline at the first visit.

    Bubbling paint is a big one. Soft drywall near the floor, stained baseboards, a musty smell and a chalky white crust on block or stucco all point to moisture, and in a shower, loose tiles or cracked grout usually mean water is already behind the wall. A moisture meter confirms it in minutes.

    Xypex, Tremco, Sika and Henry products show up on a lot of our jobs, covering crystalline products, elastomeric membranes and sealants. The product depends on the wall. Poured concrete takes crystalline coatings well, while block often needs an elastomeric membrane or a drainage board.

    Waterproofing takes away the moisture mold needs to grow. Existing mold should be removed and the area dried before we seal anything, and for larger growth, roughly anything over ten square feet, we recommend a remediation company. We remove smaller affected areas ourselves.

    Interior systems manage water after it has come through the wall, with drain tile along the footing and a sump to pump it out.
