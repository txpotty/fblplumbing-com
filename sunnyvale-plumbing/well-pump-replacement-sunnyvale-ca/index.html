    What We Fix on Well Systems in Sunnyvale, CA

    Jet Pump Suction Line Repair in Sunnyvale, CA

    Foot valves and suction lines cause most jet pump trouble, not the pump. A pinhole in the suction pipe lets air in, the pump loses prime, and it runs dry until the seal burns up. We pressure test the line. A well that is too deep for suction needs a submersible, and we will quote that instead.

    Control Box and Pressure Switch Replacement in Sunnyvale, CA

    Not every dry faucet means a dead pump. Pressure switches with burned contacts or a clogged pickup tube can leave the pump sitting idle while the tank drains to zero. The switch itself is a cheap part. You get to see the old part.

    Emergency Well Pump Service in Sunnyvale, CA

    Lost water at the house? Call (669) 319-2034 and tell us what the pressure gauge reads. Zero with the pump running points one way, zero with the pump silent points another, and that tells us which parts to load. We try to restore water the same day.

    Well Pump Brands We Install in Sunnyvale, CA

    Goulds, Grundfos, Franklin Electric and Sta-Rite cover nearly every well pump we install. Each brand has strengths, and we choose based on your well. Parts for Goulds and Franklin are easy to get, which matters when a control box dies on a weekend.

    Constant Pressure Well Pump Installation in Sunnyvale, CA

    Constant pressure systems hold the house at one pressure, say 50 psi, whether one faucet is open or the shower and the irrigation are running together. A variable frequency drive speeds the pump up and slows it down to match demand. Showers stop going weak.

    Sandy Wells and Submersible Pumps in Sunnyvale

    Sand kills submersibles early. Grit in a well chews up impellers fast, so we look for it on the old pump and may set the new one higher or add a sand separator. Where the pump hangs matters.

    Well Pump Efficiency Upgrades in Sunnyvale, CA

    Older 1 HP pumps running against a 60 psi switch use more power than most people expect, especially on a well with a long horizontal run to the house. A correctly sized pump, or a variable-speed drive, can cut the run time. The math does not always work. We will show you the payback.

    Well Pump Sizing in Sunnyvale

    Sizing starts with the well log. The log, or a sounder down the casing, gives us static water level and well depth, and a draw-down test tells us roughly how many gallons per minute the well can actually give. Pump more than the well yields and it runs dry.
