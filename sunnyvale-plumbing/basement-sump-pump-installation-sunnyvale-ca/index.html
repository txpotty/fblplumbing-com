    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Sunnyvale

    Prices in Sunnyvale, CA usually land between $800 and $2,500. Pedestal pumps cost less than cast iron submersibles, and the biggest swing comes from whether we have to excavate a new pit and whether you want a battery backup added to the same basin. Every quote is itemized.

    Every two to three months, and before and after the wet season. Dump a bucket of water into the pit slowly, then watch the float lift, the pump start, the water drop, and the pump shut off cleanly without humming or rattling. That is the whole test.

    Watch for constant running, rapid cycling or grinding noises. A sticking float or rust streaks on the housing also mean trouble, as is water sitting above the switch level without the pump starting. Replace it before the next storm.

    Our installs carry a written warranty on both parts and labor. You will see the exact length in your service agreement, and the pump manufacturer adds its own coverage, which on a quality cast iron pump usually runs three to five years from the date of purchase. We register it for you.

    We strongly recommend professional installation. The job combines electrical, plumbing and concrete work, and a bad seal on the lid, an undersized pit or a discharge that drains toward the house can leave you worse off than having no pump at all. Mistakes show up in storms.

    For some homeowners, a same-model pump swap is a fair weekend job. Cutting a new pit or adding a backup is not, because those mistakes end in flooding, cracked slabs or electrical hazards, and fixing them costs more than paying for the install up front. Leave those to us.

    A straight pump replacement usually does not need a permit. New pits, new discharge routes and new circuits may, so we look up the local rules and file for a permit when one is needed.

    Labor on our sump pump installs is warrantied, typically for one year.
