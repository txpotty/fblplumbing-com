    New Sump Pit Excavation in Sunnyvale

    If your basement has never had a pump, we start by finding the low point where water collects. We saw cut the slab, excavate a pit roughly 18 to 24 inches wide and 24 to 30 inches deep, and set a perforated or solid liner depending on whether we are collecting groundwater or surface seepage. Gravel goes around the liner to help water flow in while keeping fine soil out. We then patch the slab flush with new concrete so the floor remains clean and level.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost Comparison in Sunnyvale, CA

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Sunnyvale

    A great pump is useless if the water it moves runs right back toward the foundation. We route rigid PVC discharge pipe out of the basement and extend it at least 10 feet from the house or into a pop-up emitter or dry well. A spring or swing check valve keeps the column of water from dropping back into the pit after every cycle, which saves wear on the motor.     Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Sunnyvale

    The cost for basement sump pump installation in Sunnyvale, CA typically ranges from $800 to $2,500, depending on the type of pump, pedestal or submersible, whether a new pit needs to be excavated and whether a battery backup is included. We provide detailed estimates after an on-site assessment.

    A submersible sump pump sits entirely within the sump pit, making it quieter and less noticeable. A pedestal sump pump has its motor above the pit, making it easier to access for maintenance and usually more affordable. Both types are effective for removing water from your basement.

    Swapping a pump in an existing pit typically takes two to three hours. A new pit with slab cutting and concrete patching usually takes most of a day.

    We recommend having your sump pump inspected and maintained annually, ideally before the rainy season. Regular maintenance includes checking the float switch, cleaning debris out of the pit, testing the check valve and confirming the discharge line is clear. This helps prevent unexpected failures and lengthens the life of your pump.

    Common signs you might need a new sump pump include unusual noises during operation, visible rust or corrosion on the unit, frequent cycling on and off, or a pump that fails to turn on during heavy rain. If your pump is more than 7 to 10 years old, it is also smart to consider a replacement before it fails completely.

    Replacing a pump usually does not need a permit, but new pits, new discharge routes or new electrical circuits may. We check local requirements and pull permits when they are needed.

    FBL Plumbing offers a warranty on our labor for basement sump pump installations, typically for one year. The sump pump itself comes with a manufacturer's warranty, which can range from 1 to 5 years depending on the brand and model. We will provide you all warranty details at the time of installation.

    Watch for a pump that runs constantly, cycles on and off rapidly, makes grinding noises or never seems to turn on. Rust stains, a stuck float and water sitting above the switch level are also red flags.
