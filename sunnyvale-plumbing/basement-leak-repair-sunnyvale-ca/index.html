    How We Repair Basement Leaks in Sunnyvale, CA

    Waterproofing for Older Northern California Homes in Sunnyvale, CA

    Older basements usually went in with block walls and little more than tar on the outside. Newer homes across Northern California mostly use PEX or copper with plastic drains, and many owners are repiping older houses to improve water pressure. Block cores fill with water and weep through the mortar joints, so we often drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores into an interior channel. Clay tile drains fail too.

    Floor Drain and Backwater Valve Repair in Sunnyvale

    A floor drain that backs up when the washer drains or during a storm usually points to a blockage downstream or a failed backwater valve. We cable or jet the line, then camera it to confirm. Where the city sewer surcharges in storms, a backwater valve on the building drain keeps sewage from coming up through the floor drain, and it needs its flapper cleaned once a year. Dry traps smell.

    Exterior Membrane Waterproofing in Sunnyvale, CA

    Excavating the outside of a foundation puts the repair where the water actually gets in. Inland valleys get hot summers that work water heaters and outdoor lines hard, while coastal areas stay mild and damp year round. The membrane is matched to those conditions, the footing drain is replaced, and the backfill is graded to slope away. We extend the downspouts while the yard is open.

    Drain and Sewer Pipe Leak Repair in Sunnyvale, CA

    Cast iron drain stacks in older homes rust through from the inside over decades. A wet spot at the base of the stack or a crack along the hub is common, and we replace the damaged section with PVC or new cast iron joined with no-hub couplings. Plastic joints can fail too. Loose PVC joints usually trace back to poor support or a fitting that was never fully seated.

    Wall and Slab Seepage Repair in Sunnyvale

    Seepage at the wall or the slab joint is usually surface water pooling against the foundation. Earthquakes and hillside soil movement are real risks in Northern California, so flexible connections and seismic straps help protect plumbing from sudden movement. We look at gutters and grading first, since fixing them outside often stops the seepage for far less money. Cheap fixes come first.

    Supply Line Leak Repair in Sunnyvale

    Water lines along a basement ceiling can leak at fittings, valves or pinholes. Rural properties around Sunnyvale often rely on private wells, which may need treatment for iron, sediment, or other minerals. Bad sections get cut out and replaced, and a run with repeated pinholes gets repiped rather than patched. Repiping a basement ceiling is usually a one-day job.

    Basement Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Sunnyvale, CA

    A simple fitting or supply line repair usually costs $200 to $600. Drain stack repairs run $500 to $2,000 depending on access. Sump installs run $1,200 to $2,500, and perimeter drain systems cost several thousand more because of the concrete and digging. Prices vary with access.

    Commercial Basement Waterproofing in Sunnyvale

    Commercial basements often hold stock, files or equipment rooms that cannot sit in water. We look at floor drains, elevator pits and sump systems as one system, since a single failed check valve or a clogged pit inlet can flood the whole lower level. Duplex pumps help. Alarms on the pit tell staff before water reaches the floor.
