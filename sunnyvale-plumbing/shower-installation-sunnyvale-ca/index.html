    Walk-In Shower Installation in Sunnyvale

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. Without a curb to hold water back, the floor has to pitch a quarter inch per foot toward the drain, and the waterproofing has to run well past the shower opening. The slope is our job. We add blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Sunnyvale

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on. We install the valve, the head, an optional handheld and the drain, then set the mortar bed or foam pan to slope toward the drain at a quarter inch per foot. Testing follows. The valve gets a pressure test and the pan a 24-hour flood test before tile.

    Shower Valve and Trim Installation in Sunnyvale, CA

    The valve sits behind the tile for decades.     Digital Shower Systems in Sunnyvale, CA

    Digital shower systems like U by Moen and Kohler DTV need a little more planning.     Custom Shower System Installation in Sunnyvale, CA
