    Jetting Versus Cabling in Sunnyvale, CA

    Cabling and jetting solve different problems. A cable cuts through a blockage. Jetting strips grease and scale off the walls and carries it out of the line, so we cable first on a hard root mass and jet afterward when the line has years of buildup on it.

    Night and Holiday Drain Service in Sunnyvale

    Backups love holidays, mostly because of guests and grease. We work holidays too.     Kitchen Sink Drain Cleaning in Sunnyvale

    Coffee grounds and eggshells are a sink's worst enemies. They settle in low spots and act like sand.     Clogged Toilet Repair in Sunnyvale, CA

    Toilets clog at the trapway, a tight bend inside the porcelain. A closet auger reaches it without scratching the bowl. When a cleared toilet still backs up into the shower, the real blockage is farther down the line, and we move to a bigger machine at that point. Kids' toys come out too.

    Tub and Shower Drain Cleaning in Sunnyvale, CA

    Showers clog slowly, then all at once. Hair builds a mat under the strainer. For tubs, the cable goes in through the overflow plate rather than the drain, which protects the tub surface, and we clear the trap and the branch beyond it, then run the shower for a few minutes to confirm it drains at full speed with no gurgle.

    Laundry and Floor Drain Cleaning in Sunnyvale, CA

    Washers push lint and softener residue into the standpipe. That gunk builds until water spills onto the floor. Our tech cables the standpipe and its branch, and checks the trap and vent while there, and garage floor drains full of grit and gravel get cleared and flushed so they can take a full washer discharge without backing up.

    Bathroom Sink, Tub and Shower Drains in Sunnyvale, CA

We pull the stopper, clean the trap and send a small cable down the branch line toward the stack, and if the stopper keeps catching hair we can adjust it or swap it for one with a simpler design. Usually under an hour.
