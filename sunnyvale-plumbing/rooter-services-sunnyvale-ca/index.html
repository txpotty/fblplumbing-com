    Key signs include very slow drains, gurgling sounds coming from drains or toilets, foul odors from drains, and water backing up in sinks, tubs, or toilets. If multiple fixtures are draining slowly, it often indicates a main sewer line blockage requiring professional rooter service.

    Our technicians take every precaution to keep your home clean during rooter service. We use drop cloths and protective gear to contain any mess, so the work area is tidy after the job is done. We respect your property and aim for a clean, careful service.

    Rooting cuts roots back but does not seal the pipe joints, so roots will eventually regrow. Root treatments slow regrowth, and pipe lining or replacement provides a lasting fix.

    Drain snaking, also known as augering, uses a long, flexible cable with a cutting head to physically break through or retrieve blockages like hair or tree roots. Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to clean the entire interior of the pipe, removing grease, mineral buildup, and sludge, leaving pipes much cleaner than snaking alone. Hydro-jetting is usually more effective for severe or recurring clogs.

    No permit is required for routine rooting. Installing a new cleanout or repairing pipe may require one. We handle permits for any repair work.
