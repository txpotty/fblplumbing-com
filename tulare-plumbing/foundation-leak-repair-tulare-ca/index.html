    Direct Foundation Leak Repair in Tulare, CA

    When the leak is in an accessible spot and the rest of the pipe is in good condition, direct repair is often the most economical choice.     Water Line Reroute in Tulare

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the foundation and runs a new line through the attic or walls. This skips breaking concrete and removes the risk of future leaks under the slab. We typically use PEX for reroutes because it is flexible and resists corrosion. Drywall openings are kept small and neat for simple patching.

    Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Tulare, CA

    Many older homes in Tulare, CA have copper water lines beneath their foundations, which can corrode over time due to soil conditions or water chemistry.
