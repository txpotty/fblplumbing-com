    Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Tulare, CA

    Older homes in Tulare, CA often have soft copper running under the slab. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. Copper usually pinholes where it rubs on rock. We cut out the damaged length and sweat or press in new Type L copper, then pressure test the whole line before backfilling so you know the repair and the pipe on either side are tight.

    Drain Line Leak Under Foundation in Tulare, CA

    Drain leaks under a slab are harder to find. They aren't pressurized. We plug the line and fill it with water to see if the level drops, then run a sewer camera through it to spot the cracked hub, offset joint or root intrusion that is letting water out. Fixes include tunneling, slab cuts or a pipe liner, and we lay out the trade-offs.

    Leak Detection Tools We Use in Tulare, CA

    Infrared cameras, acoustic listening gear and tracer gas cover most leaks we chase in Tulare, CA. Tracer gas helps on tiny leaks. A hydrogen and nitrogen blend goes into the empty pipe, and a sniffer picks it up where it rises through the slab, even when the leak is silent.

    Crawl Space Foundation Leak Repair in Tulare, CA

    Crawl spaces hide leaks from supply lines, drains and AC condensate lines. Sagging, wet insulation is a common clue. Water sitting under the house rots sill plates and floor joists and feeds mold on the subfloor, so we find the source, fix the pipe and check whether the vapor barrier needs replacing. A sump pump may make sense in a Tulare, CA crawl space that floods from outside.

    Water Line Reroute in Tulare

    A reroute leaves the old pipe under the slab and runs new line through the attic or walls. The floor stays whole. We usually run PEX because it bends around framing and does not corrode, and the drywall openings are cut small, often just a few squares at fixture walls, so patching goes fast.

    Advanced Leak Detection Technology in Tulare, CA

    A ground microphone picks up the hiss of water leaving a pipe under four inches of concrete. It does most of the work. FBL Plumbing pairs it with a line tracer that follows the pipe route and a thermal camera that shows hot water spreading, which narrows the spot to a foot or two in most houses we test, in Tulare, CA or anywhere else.

    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Tulare

    Slab leaks show up quietly. A warm tile, a higher bill. FBL Plumbing isolates the hot and cold sides, then uses acoustic and infrared gear to locate the break under your concrete. For Tulare homes, the fix might be a spot repair through the floor, a reroute through the attic, or a tunnel from outside, depending on where the leak sits and how old the pipe is.

    PEX and PVC Foundation Leak Repair in Tulare

    Newer houses use PEX or PVC under the slab. Plastic pipe fails too. Usually the culprit is a fitting that was not crimped all the way or a glued joint that separated when the ground shifted. FBL Plumbing replaces the fitting with a proper crimp, expansion or solvent-weld connection so the line under your Tulare foundation is sound again.
