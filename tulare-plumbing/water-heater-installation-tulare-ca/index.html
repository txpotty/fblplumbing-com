    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Tulare

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it. Sizing is based on the first-hour rating on the yellow label, which tells you how much hot water the tank can deliver during a busy hour. Gas or electric both work. We connect the fuel, set the vent and haul the old tank away.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Tulare, CA

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match voltage and breaker size to the panel, and since there is no vent, an electric tank can sit in spots a gas unit cannot. Closets and attics get a pan. Quiet and simple.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Tulare

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Every job gets a fresh connector, a drip leg, a shutoff and a vent connector with the right rise toward the chimney or flue. Then a draft test. Big families do well with gas.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Tulare

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. An attic heater needs a pan with its own drain line, a properly routed relief line and enough access to get the old tank out and the new one in. A leak lands on drywall. Relocation to a safer spot is an option.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Tulare

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. Code here requires seismic strapping on water heaters and water-efficient fixtures, and many local agencies require sewer lateral inspections at sale. A small expansion tank on the cold line absorbs that extra volume, which keeps the relief valve from weeping and saves wear on faucets and fittings. We pre-charge it to your house pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Tulare, CA

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. When the water heats and expands with nowhere to go, pressure climbs and stresses the relief valve, the toilet fill valves and every supply line in the house. The tank soaks up that extra volume. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.
