    Underground Water Line Repair in Tulare, CA

    A leaking underground water line can lead to high water bills, reduced water pressure and even foundation damage, a serious concern for any home.     Galvanized and Polybutylene Line Replacement in Tulare

    Galvanized steel pipe rusts from the inside, and polybutylene pipe becomes brittle and splits without warning. If your underground service is either material, repairs are usually temporary. We replace the line with PEX or Type K copper, often by pulling the new pipe through the old path to limit digging. A new line restores pressure and removes the risk of repeated leaks.

    Main Water Line Repair in Tulare

    Your main water line runs from the meter box to the house and is under constant pressure. When it leaks, you may notice a spinning meter dial with all fixtures off, low pressure or a wet spot that never dries. We locate the leak, expose the pipe and splice in a new section with approved fittings. If the line is old galvanized or polybutylene, we will recommend a full replacement before more breaks happen.

    Pool and Outbuilding Supply Line Repair in Tulare, CA

    Many properties have buried water lines feeding a pool auto-fill, a guest house, an outdoor kitchen or a detached garage. These lines are usually longer than the main service and harder to locate. We use tracer wire and acoustic equipment to find leaks along the full run. Repairs are made with direct-bury rated pipe and tested before we close up.

    Underground Pipe Repair Cost Comparison in Tulare, CA

    A single point repair on a shallow water line is the most economical option. Pipe bursting or pulling a new line through the old path costs more but avoids open trenching and long-term risk. Full trench replacement is sometimes needed when the old route is blocked or the pipe is too damaged. We explain each method and its cost so you can choose the best value.
