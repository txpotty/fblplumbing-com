    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in Tulare

    A standard sump pump replacement typically costs $500 to $1,200 installed. Battery backup systems usually add $800 to $1,800. Sewage ejector pumps generally cost $1,200 to $2,500.

    We install Zoeller, Liberty and Wayne pumps. Cast iron models are preferred for durability.

    In many areas, minor like-for-like sump pump replacements usually do not require a permit. However, if the replacement involves significant changes to the electrical system, discharge piping or the sump pit itself, a permit might be necessary. Our team will advise you on local code requirements in Tulare.

    We install a variety of proven sump pump brands known for their reliability and performance, including Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty Pumps and Little Giant. Our plumbers can help you choose the best brand and model for your home's requirements and budget.

    While it may seem like a simple task, sump pump replacement involves electrical connections, proper plumbing and making sure the pump is correctly sized and installed to prevent future flooding. DIY replacement can lead to improper operation, electrical hazards or system failure. It is best to hire a professional plumber for a safe, proper and effective installation.

    To maintain your new sump pump, regularly check the pit for debris, test the pump monthly by pouring water into the pit, and make sure the discharge pipe is clear. An annual professional inspection by FBL Plumbing can also help extend its lifespan.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from 3 to 5 years. We also offer a labor warranty.

    It is recommended to test your sump pump at least every three months, and especially before and after rainy seasons. To test, simply pour a bucket of water into the sump pit.
