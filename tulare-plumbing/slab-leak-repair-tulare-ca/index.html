    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat expands and contracts the pipe against concrete and gravel.     Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Tulare

    For more complex or recurring slab leaks, or when the original piping is badly deteriorated, pipe rerouting is an effective long-term solution. This eliminates future slab leak risks from that particular line and is often preferred for older homes in Tulare with aging copper or galvanized pipes.
