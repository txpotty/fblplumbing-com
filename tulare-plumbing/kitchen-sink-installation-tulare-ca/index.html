    Undermount Sink Installation in Tulare, CA

    Undermounts hang from clips anchored into the underside of the stone, with silicone run around the rim.     Top-mount Sink Installation in Tulare

    Drop-in sinks are common in Northern California homes with laminate or tile counters. The rim carries the weight. After checking the cutout, we lay silicone under the lip and draw the clips down evenly so the whole rim sits flat. Then the drain and faucet get connected and tested.

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Tulare

    Sitting on the counter by their rim, drop-in sinks are a natural fit for tile, laminate and butcher block counters. It is often the quickest swap. We check the cutout size, seal the rim and tighten the clips evenly, and in most rentals and older homes the job is done in a couple of hours.
