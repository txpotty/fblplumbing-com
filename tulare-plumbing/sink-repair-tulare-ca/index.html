    Bathroom Sink Repair in Tulare, CA

    Bath sinks have small parts that wear. Pop-up linkage parts sit in a damp cabinet all day, and the pivot nut is the leak we find most often on bathroom sinks. We check pedestal and wall-hung sinks the same way. Vanity sinks just hide the leak better.

    Pop-Up Stopper Repair in Tulare, CA

    Stoppers that will not hold water have a linkage problem. The clevis strap slips on the lift rod, the pivot ball loses its seal, or hair wraps the stopper so tightly it cannot seat on the flange. Cleaning fixes half of them. The other half get a new pop-up drain in a matching finish.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Tulare

    A faucet that drips once a second wastes thousands of gallons a year. Cartridges for Moen, Delta, Pfister and Kohler are on the truck. Odd brands take a day to order.

    Kitchen Sink Repair in Tulare

    Kitchen sinks take the most abuse in the house. Strainers lose their putty seal, sprayer hoses split inside the cabinet, and disposal flanges vibrate loose over the years. The wet part is the one we fix. A cracked composite or porcelain bowl is a replacement, and we will say so.
