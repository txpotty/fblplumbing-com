    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Tulare

    The cost of tankless water heater replacement in Tulare, CA usually ranges from $3,000 to $6,000, depending on the unit's capacity, brand, complexity of installation, such as converting from a tank model, and any needed electrical or gas line upgrades.     Well-maintained tankless units can last 15 to 20 years. In hard water areas without regular descaling, heat exchangers can fail much sooner. Annual flushing makes the largest difference.

    Common signs that your tankless water heater needs replacement include repeated error codes, inconsistent water temperature, reduced hot water flow or a complete lack of hot water. If your unit is over 15 to 20 years old and repairs are becoming frequent and costly, replacement is often the more economical choice.
