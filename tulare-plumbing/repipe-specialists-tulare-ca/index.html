    Repiping Services We Handle in Tulare, CA

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Tulare, CA

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. It fails without warning. Brittle pipe walls and cracked fittings mean one polybutylene leak usually signals risk across the whole house. Full replacement is the fix. Many insurers ask about it.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Tulare

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Both still turn up in older homes. We can usually identify it from the pipe at the water heater, and full replacement makes the house easier to insure and sell.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Tulare, CA

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Because PEX flexes, it absorbs some of the shock that causes water hammer, and long runs with few fittings mean fewer places for a leak to start inside a wall. Attic PEX gets kept away from flues and out of UV.

    Residential Repiping Plans in Tulare

    Every house is piped a little differently, so a repipe starts with a walk-through. We count fixtures, check attic and crawlspace access, look at where the main comes in and ask about plans for future remodels before we draw a route. Then we size the lines. Bigger homes may need a 1-inch trunk so the shower does not drop when the dishwasher fills.

    Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Tulare, CA

    A whole-home PEX repipe for an average house often runs $5,000 to $12,000, with copper costing more. A single slab leak repair can run $1,000 to $4,000 once access and restoration are included. Do the math. After two or three leaks, a repipe often pays for itself, and we give you a side-by-side comparison so you can make that call with real numbers in front of you.

    Copper Repiping in Tulare, CA

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes, with lead-free solder or ProPress fittings. Copper takes a bit longer. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a day longer than PEX, mostly because every turn needs a fitting.

    Repiping for Hard Water in Tulare, CA

    Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe, and over years it narrows the bore and speeds up corrosion in metal lines. Pressure suffers. For homes with hard water, we usually recommend PEX-A, which resists scale and does not corrode, and we can add a softener loop or a whole-house filter during the repipe. Planning it now saves a second visit later.

    Drain Line Repiping in Tulare

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Repeat clogs, sewer smells and several slow drains at once often mean the drain pipe itself is failing. We camera the lines first. Failing sections are replaced with PVC or ABS sloped and vented to code, which drains faster and resists buildup much better than the pipe it replaces.
