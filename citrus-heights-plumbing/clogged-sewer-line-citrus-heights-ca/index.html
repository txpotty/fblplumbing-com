    Main Line Hydro Jetting in Citrus Heights

    A jetter cleans the full diameter of the pipe, not just a hole through the middle. That is the difference. Water at thousands of psi cuts roots, grease and scale, and the rear-facing spray carries all of it downstream as the nozzle pulls itself along the line. We camera the pipe first so jetting only goes into lines that can take it. Many lines stay clear for years after.

    Cleaning vs. Repairing a Sewer Line in Citrus Heights, CA

    A sound pipe with a grease or root clog just needs cleaning. Broken pipe needs repair. Offset joints, cracks and collapsed sections keep clogging no matter how often they get cleared, so the camera footage decides whether you spend a few hundred dollars on cleaning or more on a spot fix, liner or replacement. We walk you through it.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Citrus Heights, CA

    Jetting sends water at thousands of psi through a nozzle that sprays backward and drives itself down the pipe. It scrubs the whole wall. A cable punches a hole through a grease plug, and a jetter clears the coating that starts the next clog. The camera goes in before the jetter. Badly cracked pipe gets a gentler approach.

    Grease and Wipes Sewer Blockages in Citrus Heights, CA

    Flushable wipes do not break down the way toilet paper does. They stay intact for weeks. Mixed with grease that hardened in the main, they make a plug that a cable can only partly clear. Jetting scours the walls and flushes the debris downstream. Wipes belong in the garbage.
