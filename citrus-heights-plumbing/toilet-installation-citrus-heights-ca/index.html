    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Installation in Citrus Heights

    The cost of toilet installation in Citrus Heights, CA usually ranges from $200 to $500, not including the toilet fixture itself. This price can vary based on factors like the type of toilet, any necessary plumbing modifications, or the complexity of removing an old unit.

    Installing a new toilet can offer several benefits, including improved water efficiency, reduced water bills, better flushing power, a better look, and increased comfort. Modern toilets are also designed to be more durable and easier to clean.

    Toilets carry manufacturer warranties, often 1 to 5 years on parts and longer on porcelain. We also guarantee our installation workmanship and will return to fix any leak caused by the install.

    FBL Plumbing installs all major toilet brands, including Kohler, Toto, American Standard, Delta, and Gerber. We can help you choose a model that fits your budget, style tastes, and water efficiency goals.

    The rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, typically 12 inches but sometimes 10 or 14. Buying a toilet with the wrong rough-in means it will not fit or will sit too far from the wall.

    Replacing a toilet in the same location usually does not need a permit, but new bathrooms, additions and drain relocations typically do. Requirements vary by city, and we check with the local building department for your project.

    While DIY toilet installation is possible, hiring a professional makes sure the job is done correctly. Professionals have the tools and expertise to prevent leaks, ensure proper sealing, and comply with local plumbing codes, saving you potential headaches and costly repairs down the road.

    A comfort height toilet has a bowl that is taller than standard models, usually around 17 to 19 inches from the floor to the top of the seat. This height is closer to a standard chair, making it more comfortable for many adults, especially those with mobility issues.
