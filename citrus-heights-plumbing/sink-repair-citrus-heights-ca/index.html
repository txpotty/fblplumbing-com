    Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in Citrus Heights

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather.     Garbage Disposal Repair in Citrus Heights

    A disposal that hums but will not spin is usually jammed.     Low Water Pressure at the Sink in Citrus Heights, CA

    Unscrew the aerator before anything else. A clogged aerator screen causes most low flow at a single faucet, and if cleaning it does not help we check the cartridge and the angle stop for debris. Low pressure everywhere points to a different problem. We test the pressure regulator in that case.

    Loose or Wobbly Sink Repair in Citrus Heights

Pedestals get blocking behind the wall when needed.
