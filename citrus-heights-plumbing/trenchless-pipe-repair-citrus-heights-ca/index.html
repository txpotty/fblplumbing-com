    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Citrus Heights

    CIPP lining is a popular trenchless method where we insert a resin-saturated felt tube into the existing damaged pipe. The liner is then cured in place using heat or air, creating a new, seamless pipe within the old one. This method is well suited for repairing cracks, breaks, and root intrusion without digging up your yard in Citrus Heights.

These repairs are resistant to corrosion, root intrusion, and chemical damage.
