    The InSinkErator lineup runs from Badger units to the Evolution series, and we repair both.     Leak Detection and Repair in Citrus Heights, CA

    Disposal leaks come from four places: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Most leak points can be repaired. When the housing leaks, the unit has to go.

    Electrical Problems and Resetting in Citrus Heights

    No hum at all usually means no power reaching the motor. The red reset button on the bottom is the first stop, then check the breaker, the wall switch and the outlet or junction box under the sink with a meter. Cabinets are damp places. In that damp cabinet, wire nuts corrode and cord connections loosen, so we replace them instead of retightening.

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Citrus Heights, CA

    Leaks show up at the flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube or body. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. Water from the bottom usually means a failed internal seal, which makes replacement the smarter move. That seal cannot be replaced.

    Emergency Disposal Repair in Citrus Heights, CA

    Water on the floor beats every other problem.     Jammed Disposal Repair in Citrus Heights

    A jam usually comes from one hard object, like a peach pit, a chicken bone or a twist tie wrapped around the impeller. We free the flywheel from below with the hex key and pull the object with pliers, never a hand, then run it with water to make sure nothing else is stuck. The reset button may need a press. Check the trap arm if it jams again.
