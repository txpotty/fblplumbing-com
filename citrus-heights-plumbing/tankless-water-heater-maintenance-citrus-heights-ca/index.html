    Service Valve Kit Installs in Citrus Heights, CA

    Plenty of tankless heaters went in without service valves, and without them there is no clean way to hook up a descaling pump.     Annual Tankless Checkup in Citrus Heights, CA

    The annual checkup is a top-to-bottom pass on the heater. We read gas pressure at the inlet with a manometer while the burner is at full fire and compare it to the rating plate. The flue gets a look for obstructions. A written report with every reading goes in your file for next year's comparison.

    Tankless Vent Inspection in Citrus Heights

    Gas tankless heaters push exhaust out through a vent that has to stay sealed from the heater all the way to the termination outside the house. Condensing units use PVC or polypropylene vent pipe, pitched back toward the heater so condensate drains. Every joint gets checked, and we test for carbon monoxide by the heater. Wasp nests in vent caps are common.

    Yearly Tankless Inspection in Citrus Heights

    A yearly inspection costs less than one service call for a dead heater. We soap-test the gas connections and try the pressure relief valve. We pull the air filter and check the igniter. Anything that is wearing out goes on a written list with a plain price, and you decide what gets done now and what can wait until next year.

    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in Citrus Heights, CA

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. We start a Noritz tune-up at the exhaust, checking for soot and any blocked termination.     Rinnai Tankless Service in Citrus Heights

    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in Citrus Heights
