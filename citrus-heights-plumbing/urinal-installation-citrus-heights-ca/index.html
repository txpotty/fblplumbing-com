    Frequently Asked Questions About Urinal Installation in Citrus Heights

    Replacing a urinal on existing lines typically costs $500 to $1,200 including a manual flush valve. New installs with carriers and new drain lines usually range from $1,500 to $3,500, and sensor valves add $250 to $600.

    FBL Plumbing installs a wide variety of reputable urinal brands, including American Standard, Kohler, Zurn, Sloan, and Toto. We are familiar with the specific requirements of most major manufacturers and can also recommend the best brand for your specific needs and budget.

    A replacement takes two to four hours. A new install with rough-in and carrier typically takes one to two days, plus inspection time.

    Regular cleaning with appropriate cleaners is essential for both flush and waterless urinals. Waterless urinals also require regular replacement of their sealant cartridges, typically every 1 to 3 months depending on usage. Proper maintenance prevents odors and clogs and keeps the fixture sanitary.

    A washdown urinal uses a simple flushing action to rinse the bowl, relying on gravity and the slope of the drain. A siphonic urinal creates a siphonic action during flushing, which pulls waste more forcefully down the drain, offering more effective waste removal and usually better odor control.

    Most standard urinal installations can be completed within 2 to 4 hours. However, if new plumbing lines need to be run, or if there are significant modifications to the existing wall or drainage system, the process could take longer, possibly a full day.

    For commercial settings, look for durability, water efficiency (like low-flow or waterless models), easy maintenance, and ADA compliance if applicable. Brands like Sloan and Zurn are popular for their robust commercial-grade fixtures and flushometers.

    New urinals and restroom changes usually require a plumbing permit. Simple like-for-like replacements usually do not, and we will confirm with your local building department.
