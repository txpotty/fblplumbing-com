    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Citrus Heights, CA

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. Jetting costs more up front.     Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Citrus Heights, CA

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. Jetting cleans the whole pipe. For a single clog in an otherwise clean line, snaking is cheaper and works fine, but for a line that clogs every few months, jetting removes the buildup that keeps catching debris.

    Scheduled Jetting Maintenance in Citrus Heights

    Properties with chronic drain issues do better on a jetting schedule. Restaurants jet every three to six months. Root-prone home sewers usually jet every year or two, with camera records to track regrowth.

    Sewer Camera Inspection Before Hydrojetting in Citrus Heights

    Jetting a broken pipe can make the break worse. We camera every line before we set up the jetter. The video shows the pipe material, the condition of each joint, and any cracks, offsets or sags, which tells us the nozzle and pressure to use or whether we should not jet that line at all.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydrojetting in Citrus Heights

After cutting, the jet flushes the root pieces down to the main, and the camera shows which joints let the roots in so you know where a future repair or liner would go.
