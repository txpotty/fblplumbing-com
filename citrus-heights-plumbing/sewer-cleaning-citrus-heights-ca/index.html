    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Cleaning in Citrus Heights

    Main sewer cleaning in Citrus Heights usually costs $200 to $500 with a cable machine from an accessible cleanout. Hydro jetting a sewer line typically runs $400 to $900, and a camera inspection adds about $150 to $350.

    Most standard sewer cleaning jobs in Citrus Heights can be finished within 1 to 3 hours, depending on the severity and nature of the clog. More complex issues, like extensive root removal or a needed camera inspection, might take longer. We will provide an estimated timeline during our initial assessment.

    In most cases the homeowner owns the lateral from the house to the city main or property line. Your city utility can tell you where its responsibility begins.

    We firmly advise against chemical drain cleaners for sewer lines. These products are often ineffective against severe sewer clogs and can corrode pipes, especially older ones, causing more damage. They can also pose a health risk to you and our plumbers during service.

    Cleaning does not require a permit. Installing a cleanout or repairing the sewer typically does, and we handle that paperwork.

    Usually, standard home insurance policies do not cover routine sewer cleaning or damage from gradual wear and tear. However, if a sudden, unexpected event causes sewer damage, it might be covered. It is best to check with your insurance provider first.

    Common signs include multiple slow drains throughout your home, gurgling noises from toilets or drains, foul odors coming from drains, and sewage backing up into sinks, tubs, or toilets. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a professional.

    While minor drain clogs can sometimes be handled with DIY solutions, main sewer line blockages need professional tools and expertise. Trying to clear a sewer line without proper equipment can aggravate the problem or damage your pipes.
