    Picking the Right Softener for Citrus Heights, CA Water

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Citrus Heights, CA

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. They do keep scale from sticking inside pipes and water heaters. They need no drain line. We can replace one or switch you to a true softener.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Citrus Heights, CA

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. The valve needs your hardness in grains, the number of people and the regeneration time set correctly, or it will regenerate too often or not enough. We set it before we leave.

    Salt-Based Softener Swaps in Citrus Heights, CA

    Swapping a salt-based softener in Northern California homes means disconnecting the old resin and brine tanks, cutting in new fittings if the loop is corroded, and setting the new valve. We pressurize and check every joint. We set an air gap on the drain line.

    Salt-Free Alternatives in Citrus Heights, CA

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. In those cases a TAC conditioner works, though we are upfront that it stops scale and does not make water feel soft. Sodium concerns are another reason.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Citrus Heights, CA

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in Northern California homes. If the valve is old enough that parts are hard to find, a new valve on a new tank is the smarter buy. Newer Fleck heads drop right in.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Citrus Heights

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. Metered valves like the Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 count gallons and regenerate only when the resin is close to spent. Most people notice less salt in a few weeks.

    Softener Failure Diagnosis in Citrus Heights, CA

    A clogged injector, a dead motor or a wrong setting can make a good softener look finished. We check those first and test hardness before and after the unit, so you know whether it is softening at all. Discontinued valves usually mean replacement.

    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Citrus Heights

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. The tank goes on the main line downstream of the filter, with no power or drain needed. Magnets and clamp-on gadgets are a different story.

    Water Softener Replacement Near Me in Citrus Heights, CA

    Local water varies. Hardness can be very different between a city supply and a private well a few miles out, and iron or chlorine changes what else the system needs. The system matches your test, not a guess.
