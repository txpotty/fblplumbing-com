    Expansion Tank Installation in Citrus Heights, CA

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. When the water heats and expands with nowhere to go, pressure climbs and stresses the relief valve, the toilet fill valves and every supply line in the house. An expansion tank absorbs it. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Citrus Heights

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. Each gas swap gets a new connector, a drip leg, a draft test at the hood and a leak check with a detector at every fitting. Venting is what keeps CO out. We follow code on all of it.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Citrus Heights, CA

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. They need roughly 700 cubic feet of air around them and a condensate drain, so a warm garage or a large utility room usually works better than a closet. Some qualify for tax credits. We help you decide if one fits.

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Citrus Heights

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it. The fuel hookup, the venting and haul-away are all part of the job.
