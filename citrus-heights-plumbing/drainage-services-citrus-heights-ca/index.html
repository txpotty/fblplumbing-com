    Jetting for Scale and Grease in Citrus Heights

    Jetting uses water at high pressure to strip a pipe back to its original diameter. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. Scale and grease on the pipe wall catch debris, and stripping them off keeps the line clear far longer. Plan on about two hours.

    Sump Pump Services in Citrus Heights, CA

    A sump pump protects a basement or crawlspace from groundwater. Our test covers the float, the check valve and the discharge line. Never pipe it to the sewer. Many sump floods come from a blocked discharge line, not a dead pump.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Citrus Heights

    A hydro jetter sends water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full pipe wall. Cables punch through clogs, but jetting removes the grease and scale coating the pipe, which is what keeps causing them. Grease lines benefit most. We camera the line first to make sure the pipe is sound enough to jet.
