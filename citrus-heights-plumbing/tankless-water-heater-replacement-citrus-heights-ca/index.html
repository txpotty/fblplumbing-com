    Picking a Tankless for Your Citrus Heights, CA House

    Rinnai Condensing Upgrades in Citrus Heights, CA

    Rinnai Sensei condensing models replace older non-condensing Rinnai units in a lot of homes, but the venting changes from stainless to PVC. We handle that conversion. Gas and water connections usually line up.

    Commercial Tankless Racks in Citrus Heights, CA

    Restaurants and salons run tankless racks hard. Four units together can pull over 600,000 BTU, which means checking gas pressure with all of them running. Installs can happen after closing.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Citrus Heights, CA

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Their combi boilers handle both space heat and domestic water. We set up both.

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in Citrus Heights

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. A small under-sink unit needs one 20 or 30 amp circuit and suits a distant bath or break room. Swaps take an hour or two.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Citrus Heights, CA

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Sun and weather wear on the covers and wiring. Mountain and foothill homes in Northern California see real winters, so pipes there need insulation, heat tape, or proper draining when a cabin sits empty. On every outdoor swap, we check freeze protection and the pipe cover. Often the simplest job we do.

    Tankless vs. Tank Replacement Cost in Citrus Heights, CA

    Tankless costs more up front than a tank, often twice as much installed. Across 20 years the gap narrows, because one tankless may outlast two tanks, but your usage decides the payback. We price both.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Citrus Heights

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. The gas line usually has to be 3/4-inch or larger. We check meter capacity and pipe length against the manufacturer's sizing chart, then confirm pressure with the unit running. Starved units throw error codes.

    Navien Venting and Install in Citrus Heights

    Navien allows 2-inch or 3-inch PVC venting on its condensing units, with maximum lengths that drop as you add elbows. Our vent runs stay within spec. Go past them and the warranty suffers.
