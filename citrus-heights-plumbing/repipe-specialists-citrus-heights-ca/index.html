    What Our Repipe Crews Do in Citrus Heights, CA

    Commercial Repiping in Citrus Heights, CA

    Commercial buildings in Citrus Heights, CA often have larger pipe, risers that serve several floors and tenants who cannot lose water for a full day. Our crew breaks commercial repipes into zones, working one riser or suite at a time while the rest of the building keeps water. Night work is possible. Materials are usually Type L copper or commercial PEX, sized to the fixture count and installed to commercial code.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Citrus Heights, CA

    Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Low pressure, brown water and leaks at threaded joints are the typical signs. We remove or abandon the galvanized lines and replace them with PEX or copper, and your water pressure and water quality usually improve the same day the new lines go into service.

    Drain Line Repiping in Citrus Heights

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Slow drains in several fixtures, sewer odors and repeated clogs in the same line are common signs of a failing drain system. We camera the lines first. Failing sections are replaced with PVC or ABS sloped and vented to code, which drains faster and resists buildup much better than the pipe it replaces.

    Drywall Patching and Restoration in Citrus Heights, CA

    Repiping needs small openings in walls and ceilings to reach fixtures and pull pipe. We cut clean, square openings, protect the area around them and patch every hole once the plumbing passes its test. Textures get matched. We match knockdown, orange peel or smooth walls, so the patches are ready to prime and paint.

    Copper Repiping in Citrus Heights, CA

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes, with lead-free solder or ProPress fittings. Copper takes a bit longer. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a day longer than PEX, mostly because every turn needs a fitting.

    Type L Copper Repiping in Citrus Heights

    Copper has been plumbing homes for generations, and done right it lasts a long time. Our copper repipes use Type L with proper hangers and isolators so the pipe does not wear against framing. Lead-free solder only. Because some water chemistries eat copper, we look at your leak history before recommending it over PEX.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Citrus Heights

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water and long attic runs. Our crews use Uponor or similar PEX-A, where the pipe end gets expanded and shrinks back over the fitting for a full-diameter connection. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. Most homes are repiped with PEX-A in two to three days.

    PEX Versus Copper Repipe Pricing in Citrus Heights, CA

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper because the material is cheaper and installation goes faster. Copper costs more. For a typical house, copper often runs 20 to 40 percent above PEX, though some buyers and homeowners like the look and feel of metal pipe and are willing to pay for it. Both prices include permits, fittings, patching and cleanup.

    Our repipes come with a written workmanship warranty on parts and labor, usually covering several years. Manufacturer coverage adds more. Certified PEX-A installs carry the manufacturer's long-term warranty on the pipe and fittings, and the exact terms of both warranties are on your estimate.

    Copper is rigid metal pipe with a long track record. PEX bends instead. PEX is cheaper, quicker and quieter and handles freezes better, while copper tolerates sun and heat and suits owners who want metal, so it depends on your house.
