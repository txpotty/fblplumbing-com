    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Citrus Heights, CA

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Citrus Heights

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. A 1-1/2 inch discharge line handles the flow from either size without straining the motor.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Citrus Heights

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. Because everything comes assembled on a single riser, they fit into an 18-inch pit that would be too tight for two separate pumps and two check valves. Install time is shorter too. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Citrus Heights, CA

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. A small sump basin at the low point fixes that. Access is often tight. The discharge exits through the rim joist and drains away from the house.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Citrus Heights

    No pit yet? We cut one at the low corner. Washed gravel goes around the basin so water can reach it. Then we patch the slab. The pump, lid and discharge go in last.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Citrus Heights, CA

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. When we replace a pump we also check the basin for silt, look at the check valve flapper and run water through the discharge line to find any clogs. If the old one could not keep up, we size up. Testing is done with a full pit.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Citrus Heights, CA

Swapping a pump in an existing pit is the cheapest job, while a new pit means saw cutting, digging, gravel and a concrete patch, which roughly doubles the labor. Battery backups and alarms add to the total. Your written quote shows each option and its price.
