    We firmly advise against chemical drain cleaners for sewer line clogs.     The length of sewer line cleaning varies depending on the severity and accessibility of the blockage. We will provide an expected timeline after our initial inspection.

    Slow drains in several fixtures, gurgling toilets, sewage odors and backups in the lowest drains are typical signs.

    We use sectional and drum cable machines, hydro jetters and color camera systems with locators from trusted professional brands.

    For most homes in South San Francisco, annual or twice-yearly sewer line cleaning is sufficient, especially if you have mature trees nearby or an older plumbing system.     During our diagnostic camera inspection, if we discover that your sewer line is broken, collapsed, or severely damaged, we will tell you immediately.
