    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Installation in South San Francisco

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon gas water heater usually costs $1,800 to $4,000 installed. Power vent and tankless gas units often cost more due to venting and gas line requirements.

    Gas heaters usually recover faster and can cost less to operate where gas rates are reasonable. Electric units are simpler and do not require venting.

    Regular maintenance, such as annual flushing of the tank to remove sediment and checking the anode rod, can significantly extend your gas water heater's lifespan. Our team can advise on best practices and offer maintenance services.

    It's recommended to have your gas water heater professionally serviced annually. Regular maintenance can extend its lifespan, improve efficiency and catch potential issues before they become major problems, ensuring safe operation.

    The main difference is the type of gas they use and the burners and orifices required for combustion. Natural gas is typically supplied by a utility line, while propane is stored in a tank. We install both types and ensure correct setup for your fuel source.

    Power vent units use a fan to push exhaust outside, while direct vent units use a sealed system that draws outside air. Both allow more flexible placement compared to atmospheric vents.
