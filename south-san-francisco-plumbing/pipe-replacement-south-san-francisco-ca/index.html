    Repiping and Pipe Replacement Options in South San Francisco, CA

    Commercial Pipe Replacement in South San Francisco

    Commercial buildings often have larger supply lines and more fixtures. Work has to fit around your hours. We plan pipe replacement in phases whenever the layout allows it, so only part of the building is offline at a time. Night work available.

    Whole-Home Re-Piping in South San Francisco

    A whole-home repipe replaces every supply line from the main shutoff to each fixture, including the water heater connections. Most single-story homes take two to three days. We run new trunk lines through the attic or crawlspace, branch down to fixtures through small wall openings, with service restored each evening when possible. Then patching.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in South San Francisco, CA

    Copper still has fans. It handles heat well and lasts decades. We install Type L copper for most interior supply lines, sweat or press the joints, while strapping each line properly, so the new pipe does not rattle or sag. Costs more than PEX.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in South San Francisco, CA

    Galvanized steel was common before the 1960s. The zinc wears off and the steel rusts inside. Over decades the opening shrinks, pressure drops and the water turns brown after sitting, while threaded joints corrode through first. Replacing it is the only fix.

    Water Line Replacement vs. Repair in South San Francisco, CA

    A single bad spot can usually be fixed. Several leaks in different rooms, rusty water throughout, or pipe that crumbles when we cut it means the rest is close behind. We show you what we find.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in South San Francisco

    PEX bends around corners and comes in long rolls, so a typical repipe needs fewer fittings than copper would on the same layout. Freezing is uncommon along the coast and on valley floors in Northern California, but frosty nights do happen and can crack unprotected outdoor pipes. It often survives freezing where copper would crack. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings where possible, keeping flow strong at each joint. Faster install.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in South San Francisco

    Polybutylene is gray or blue plastic installed from roughly the late 1970s to mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside. Failures tend to be sudden splits at fittings rather than slow drips, and some insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Replace it.

    Full Repipes for Older Homes in South San Francisco

    Homes from the 1950s and 1960s often still have original galvanized pipe. It is near the end. A full repipe brings new PEX or copper to every fixture, improves pressure, clears up discolored water, and removes the leak risk from aging threads. Worth it.
