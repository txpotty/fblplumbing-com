    Sump Pump Basin and Discharge Line Installation in South San Francisco

    A complete sump pump system needs a properly installed basin and discharge line. We handle the excavation and placement of the sump basin, setting it at the lowest point of your basement floor so it collects water well. We also install the discharge line, ensuring it is correctly sloped, sealed and routed away from your foundation to a suitable drainage area, so water does not find its way back into your South San Francisco property.

    Sump Pump Alarms and Smart Monitoring in South San Francisco, CA

    A high water alarm is an inexpensive part that gives you warning before water reaches the floor. We install standalone alarms or Wi-Fi monitors that send a text if the water level rises, the pump fails or power drops. These are a favorite with homeowners who travel or own second homes. We test the float trigger in front of you so you know how the alarm sounds.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in South San Francisco

    Pedestal sump pumps are an excellent choice for narrow or shallow sump pits, as their motor sits above the water level. This design makes them easier to maintain and can often offer a longer lifespan because the motor stays out of the water. FBL Plumbing can properly install pedestal sump pumps in your South San Francisco home, with the right sizing and setup for effective water removal without crowding your pit.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost Comparison in South San Francisco, CA

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit is the least expensive option, usually a few hundred dollars plus the pump. Digging a new pit through the slab adds labor for saw cutting, excavation and concrete patching. Adding a battery backup increases the total but often costs less than one cleanup after a flooded basement. We give you an itemized quote so you can compare a basic primary pump against a full primary and backup system before we start.

    Pedestal Sump Pumps in South San Francisco

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a good fit for narrow pits in older homes. They usually last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we usually recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod set to your pit depth.
