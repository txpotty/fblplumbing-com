    Perma-Liner and Picote Solutions in South San Francisco

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Both matter. Before lining, Picote heads mill out roots and scale, and after curing they reopen the lateral connections so each drain flows. A clean pipe holds a liner best.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in South San Francisco

    The old pipe gets cleaned first. After that, we insert the liner. We inflate an epoxy-soaked liner against the wall and cure it with heat or UV, leaving a smooth pipe with no joints. Lining works well for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in South San Francisco, CA

    Bursting works when the pipe has collapsed or is too broken to line. It can go up a size. Our crew sets an entry pit and an exit pit, then pulls a bursting head and new HDPE through on a hydraulic winch, which replaces the full run in a day or two for most homes. FBL Plumbing fuses the HDPE into one length.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Costs in South San Francisco, CA

    On paper, the two methods can price out close. Then add restoration. A trench under a driveway or through an established garden means paying to replace concrete, plants and irrigation after the plumbing is done, and trenchless avoids most of those costs. We price both side by side.

    Commercial Trenchless Solutions in South San Francisco

    Commercial sites in South San Francisco have longer runs and more laterals. Pavement covers most of it. Schools, offices and stores keep lots and walkways open because we work from cleanouts and manholes, cutting only small pits. We plan work for slow periods.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in South San Francisco

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. Once inside the cleaned pipe, it gets inflated against the wall and cured with heat or UV, forming a new pipe with no joints. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your South San Francisco yard.

    Sewer Lateral Lining in South San Francisco

    Laterals are the most common candidates for trenchless repair. Clay and Orangeburg fail often. Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe crack, sag or fill with roots over time, so we jet the line clean, inspect it by camera and choose lining or bursting based on what we see. The new line restores full flow and resists roots.

    Pipe Bursting Services in South San Francisco, CA

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe. As a winch drags the head through, the old pipe fractures into the soil and new HDPE is pulled into the same path behind it. HDPE does not rust or leak at joints in South San Francisco, CA soil.
