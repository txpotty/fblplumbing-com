    A spot repair on an accessible service line typically runs $350 to $1,500 depending on depth and access. Full replacement from meter to house usually costs $1,800 to $5,500, and trenchless replacement often falls between $3,000 and $7,000. We give you a written price before starting.

    Full service line replacement typically requires a plumbing permit and inspection from your city or county. Small spot repairs often do not. We pull the permit and schedule the inspection when needed.

    For water line replacements, we mainly use durable and code-compliant materials such as PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) and copper. PEX is flexible and resistant to freezing, while copper offers excellent longevity and corrosion resistance. The best material for your home in South San Francisco will depend on your specific needs, budget, and local regulations.

    We use only high-quality, reputable brands for all our water line repairs and installations.     Trenchless water line repair is a method of fixing or replacing underground pipes without extensive digging. Techniques like pipe bursting involve pulling a new pipe through the old one, while pipe lining inserts a new, cured-in-place pipe inside the existing one. It's an excellent option for preserving landscaping and minimizing disruption, especially on properties in South San Francisco with established gardens or driveways.

    Most standard water line repairs can be completed within a few hours to a full day, depending on the severity and location of the leak.
