    Gas Water Heater Replacement in South San Francisco

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. Each gas swap gets a new connector, a drip leg, a draft test at the hood and a leak check with a detector at every fitting. Carbon monoxide is the risk. All work follows local code.

    Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in South San Francisco

    Each manufacturer publishes its own install rules, and they are not identical. Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White and Navien each spell out clearances, vent lengths and relief valve placement, and a warranty claim can be denied if those were ignored. We follow them exactly. Your warranty stays intact.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in South San Francisco, CA

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Most gas tankless units need a 3/4 inch gas line and their own PVC or stainless vent, which is different from what a tank uses. They cost more to install. We check gas capacity before quoting.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in South San Francisco

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Each one gets a new gas connector, a sediment trap and a shutoff valve, plus a vent connector pitched up toward the flue at a quarter inch per foot. We test draft before leaving. Big families do well with gas.

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in South San Francisco

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it. Sizing is based on the first-hour rating on the yellow label, which tells you how much hot water the tank can deliver during a busy hour. Either fuel works. We connect the fuel, set the vent and haul the old tank away.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in South San Francisco, CA

    Water on the garage floor and no hot water usually means the tank has rusted through. Shut the cold supply valve on top of the tank and turn the gas knob to pilot or flip the breaker, then call us. Common tanks ride on our trucks. Same-day replacement is normal.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in South San Francisco

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. Code here requires seismic strapping on water heaters and low-flow fixtures, and many local agencies require sewer lateral inspections at sale. A small expansion tank on the cold line absorbs that extra volume, which keeps the relief valve from weeping and saves wear on faucets and fittings. We pre-charge it to your house pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in South San Francisco

    Water heater installation in South San Francisco, CA runs about $1,200 to $4,000 or more. The biggest factors are whether it is a tank or tankless unit, its size, its fuel and whether the gas line or panel needs work. Tankless sits at the high end. You get a written quote after we see the space.

    A tank heater usually lasts 8 to 12 years with maintenance. Tankless models tend to last 15 to 20 years.

    Start with correct sizing and a thermostat set at 120 degrees. Insulating the first few feet of hot pipe and flushing the tank once a year both help, and a tankless model cuts standby loss.

    For two to four people, a 40 or 50 gallon tank usually does the job. Larger families may need a 75 gallon tank or a tankless unit sized by flow rate.

    Homes with a pressure reducing valve or check valve usually need one. It protects the heater and pipes from pressure spikes.

    A standard tank replacement typically runs two to four hours. Tankless or relocation work takes longer.

    An expansion tank absorbs the extra pressure created as water heats. Closed systems usually need one by code.

    A like-for-like tank swap usually takes three to four hours. Converting from tank to tankless, or any job with major gas, vent or electrical changes, can take a full day or more. We give you a timeline first.
