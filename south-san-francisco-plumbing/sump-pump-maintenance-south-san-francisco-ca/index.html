    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in South San Francisco, CA

    Backup pumps sit in the pit next to the main pump and run off a 12-volt battery when the power drops or the primary fails. Our plumber load tests the battery instead of just reading its voltage. Then we unplug the main pump and let the backup empty the pit on its own. Alarms get tested too.

    Elevator Pit and Garage Pumps in South San Francisco

    Elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms all collect water that has nowhere to go by gravity, so a pump has to lift it out.     Submersible Sump Pump Maintenance in South San Francisco
