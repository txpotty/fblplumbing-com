    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in South San Francisco, CA

    Gray polybutylene went into millions of homes from the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. Chlorinated water breaks the pipe and its plastic fittings down from within, which means it can look perfectly fine until a fitting suddenly fails. Insurers ask about it. Swapping it for PEX can help with insurance and with resale.

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in South San Francisco, CA

    Opening a wall for a kitchen remodel is the cheapest time to replace old supply lines.     Full Home PEX Repiping in South San Francisco

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside out. Give it four or five decades and the inside is so packed with rust that a half-inch pipe barely flows, and the first draw in the morning runs brown. A full PEX repipe replaces all of it. Pressure comes back at the fixtures and the rust stops.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in South San Francisco, CA

    Price on a repipe comes down to a few things: bathroom count, square footage, how many stories, and how easy the attic and walls are to work in.     Low pressure at every faucet, not just one, usually points at the pipe and not the fixtures.     Uponor PEX System Installation in South San Francisco

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool and a cold-expansion ring. We stretch the pipe and ring, seat the fitting, and within seconds the plastic shrinks back around the barb into a tight seal. There is no crimp gauge to check. The fittings have a wide bore, so flow loss through each joint stays low.
