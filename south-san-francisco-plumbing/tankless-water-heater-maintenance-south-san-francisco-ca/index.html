    Common signs include reduced hot water flow, fluctuating water temperatures, strange noises coming from the unit, or error codes displayed on the control panel. If you notice any of these, it's a strong sign that your tankless water heater in South San Francisco is due for professional maintenance.

    A typical tankless water heater maintenance service, including flushing and inspection, usually takes 1 to 2 hours. The exact time can vary depending on the unit's condition, the amount of scale buildup and whether any minor adjustments or repairs are needed during the visit.

    It is best to have your tankless water heater professionally serviced at least once a year. In areas with very hard water, or for heavy usage, more frequent maintenance, such as every six to nine months, might help to prevent scale buildup and maintain efficiency.

    A softener or scale prevention system greatly reduces buildup in the heat exchanger. Some brands even recommend treatment when hardness is high. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe.

    Many tankless water heater manufacturers require proof of regular, professional maintenance to keep your unit's warranty valid. Skipping annual service can void the warranty, leaving you responsible for costly repairs that would otherwise be covered. Always check your specific warranty terms.
