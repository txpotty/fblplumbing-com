    Noritz Descaling in South San Francisco, CA

    Noritz exchangers scale up fast on hard water. We use a descaler that the manufacturer approves and keep the cycle inside the time limit in the manual, because too strong a mix can etch the metal. While the cycle runs, we check the fan motor and exhaust path.

    Multi-Unit Tankless Checks in South San Francisco, CA

    Restaurants and salons often run two or more tankless heaters on a shared manifold, and one weak unit can drag the whole bank down. Gas pressure gets read with every unit firing together. Each heater gets its own flow and temperature reading. Then we compare them.

    Tankless Banks for Restaurants and Apartments in South San Francisco

    Cascade systems link several tankless units to one controller that fires them in turn. Heavy use means scale builds faster than in a single home unit. Each heater gets descaled, and we check that the controller spreads the load evenly. Quarterly service is common.

    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in South San Francisco

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing. Solution goes from a bucket into the cold port, out the hot port and back to the bucket. After the cycle we rinse with clean water and pull the inlet screen. Then we check for leaks.

    Inlet Screen and Air Filter Cleaning in South San Francisco

    Grit gets in. The small screen on the cold inlet catches sand and loose scale after a water main repair. On units with an air intake filter, dust and lint load it up and the burner runs rich or throws a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.

    Commercial Tankless Maintenance in South San Francisco, CA

    Commercial kitchens and salons burn through hot water all day, so their tankless units scale up much faster than a house unit. We test gas pressure under full fire, not at idle.
