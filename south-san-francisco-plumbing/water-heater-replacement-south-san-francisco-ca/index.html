    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in South San Francisco

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is a popular move, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually has to be upsized, and the old metal vent gets replaced with stainless or PVC run to the maker's specs. You see both numbers.

    Upsizing Your Water Heater in South San Francisco

    Running out of hot water usually means the house outgrew the tank. A bigger tank or a higher first-hour rating handles more back-to-back showers, and sometimes a 50-gallon high-recovery unit matches a 75 in the same footprint. Recovery rate counts too.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in South San Francisco, CA

    Gas water heaters recover faster than electric, and in South San Francisco, CA homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. Gas gets shut off and capped, the old tank drained and removed, and the new one set with a fresh connector, sediment trap and properly sloped vent. Then we leak test.

    Bradford White Water Heater Replacement in South San Francisco

    Bradford White sells only through the trade, so you will not find one at a big-box store. They hold up well, and warranty support runs through the installing plumber, which makes a future claim easy for us to handle. Parts are easy to get.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in South San Francisco, CA

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters move heat from the surrounding air into the tank, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need space and airflow, often a room of about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they work best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. The space runs cooler.

    Water Heater Replacement Cost Comparison in South San Francisco, CA
