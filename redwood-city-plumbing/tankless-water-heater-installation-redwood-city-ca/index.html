    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Redwood City, CA

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Redwood City, CA homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. A whole-home electric unit draws heavy amperage.     Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Redwood City, CA

    Calcium scale is what kills most tankless units early. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. Once scale coats the heat exchanger, efficiency drops and error codes start, and over time it can crack the exchanger and void the warranty. Flush it yearly. Service valves make it easy.
