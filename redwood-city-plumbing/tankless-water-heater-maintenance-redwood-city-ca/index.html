    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Redwood City

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. That second exchanger makes acidic condensate. We clear the condensate trap and check the neutralizer media. On A-series models with the built-in recirculation pump, we test the pump and the buffer tank too.

    Inlet Screen and Air Filter Cleaning in Redwood City

    Grit gets in. The small screen on the cold inlet catches sand and loose scale after a water main repair. On units with an air intake filter, dust and lint load it up and the burner runs rich or throws a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.

    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in Redwood City, CA

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. A Noritz tune-up starts at the exhaust, where we look for soot and blocked terminations. Next comes the combustion air intake and the fan. Last, we check the temperature setting and run a few fixtures at once, which shows whether the unit holds temperature between back-to-back showers.

    Same-Day Tankless Help in Redwood City, CA

    A blinking error code and a cold shower usually mean the heater protected itself from something. Usually it is a blocked exhaust or heavy scale. Emergency calls get common parts and a descaling kit, and most heaters run again before we leave.

    Flush Now or Replace the Exchanger Later in Redwood City, CA

    A new heat exchanger can run $600 to $1,500 or more installed, and some manufacturers will not cover one that failed from scale. A descaling visit costs a fraction of that.     Home Tankless Flushing in Redwood City

    Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running hard. Inside a tankless heater, that water runs through a small copper or stainless coil while the burner fires, and every minute of hot water leaves a little calcium behind. We hook a pump to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for about an hour. Flow comes back. Temperatures stop swinging, and the unit quits shutting down on high-limit.

    Noritz Descaling in Redwood City, CA

    Noritz exchangers scale up fast on hard water. We use a descaler that the manufacturer approves and keep the cycle inside the time limit in the manual, because too strong a mix can etch the metal. While the cycle runs, we check the fan motor and exhaust path.

    Recirculation and Combi Unit Service in Redwood City, CA
