    Kitchen Sink Drain Replacement in Redwood City

    Kitchen drains start failing at the basket strainer. The putty under a 3-1/2 inch strainer dries out, the locknut rusts, and water seeps down the outside of the tailpiece where it looks like the trap is leaking even when it is not. Strainer and trap get swapped as a pair. Disposal sinks get a new flange instead.

    Leaking P-Trap Replacement in Redwood City

    Traps leak at the joints first. The beveled washers inside the slip nuts flatten and harden over the years, and a trap that has been taken apart several times to clear clogs often has stripped plastic threads or a cracked nut. Trap kits are cheap. Labor is most of the bill.

    Cost of Sink Drain Replacement in Redwood City, CA

    Scope sets the price. Bath pop-up and trap swaps cost the least, and kitchen drains or rusted wall stubs cost more. Designer finishes add some. The quote lists each part.

    Utility and Basement Sink Drain Replacement in Redwood City

    Basement sinks often drain to a pump. Sinks below the sewer line drain through a pump, so we check its valve and discharge line during the job. Pumps fail quietly. A gurgling pit is a clue.

    Code Updates During Drain Replacement in Redwood City

    Old drains sometimes break current rules. S-traps and drum traps were common decades ago, and when we replace the drain we bring it up to a vented P-trap with the right trap arm length. That can mean an air admittance valve. Local rules decide.

    Bathroom Sink Drain Replacement in Redwood City, CA

    Bathroom drains wear out at the pop-up. Pivot nuts leak and drain bodies corrode at the overflow holes, so a sink that will not hold water needs a new pop-up. Patching one piece rarely works. The new drain matches your faucet finish.

    Pop-Up Drain Replacement in Redwood City

    Lift-rod pop-ups are the most common bath drain. We pull the old body, clean the flange seat down to bare porcelain, reset the new flange in putty or silicone, and adjust the clevis so the lift rod opens and closes the stopper with one easy motion. Finish matches the faucet. Overflow-style drains are standard.
