    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Redwood City, CA

    Converting from a 40 or 50 gallon tank to tankless frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss in a garage or closet. The catch is that tankless units often need a larger gas line, often three quarter inch or bigger, plus new venting. We check your meter size and pipe run before quoting so there are no surprises mid-job. We also cap or reroute the old flue and seal the roof penetration if it is no longer used. Expect a conversion to take a full day.

    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Redwood City

    When your existing tankless has a cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board that is not worth repairing, a direct replacement is often the quickest fix. We match the new unit to your current gas line, venting type and mounting location whenever possible. Many Rinnai and Navien models share similar footprints, which keeps wall patching to a minimum. We also replace the old service valves if they are corroded or stuck. Most like-for-like swaps are finished in four to six hours.

    Recirculation Tankless Upgrades in Redwood City, CA

    If you are tired of running the tap while you wait for hot water, a tankless with a built-in recirculation pump can bring heat to the fixture in seconds. Navien A series and Rinnai units with internal pumps can use a dedicated return line or a crossover valve under the farthest sink. We program schedules so the pump runs only during your morning and evening peaks. That keeps gas use reasonable while saving thousands of gallons of wasted water a year. This upgrade is popular in long single-story ranch homes.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Redwood City, CA

    For properties without natural gas access or those looking for an all-electric solution, electric tankless water heaters are a viable choice. FBL Plumbing offers electric tankless water heater replacement services in Redwood City, CA. This often involves careful assessment of your home's electrical panel capacity, as these units require substantial amperage. We make sure your new electric tankless unit is installed safely and effectively, providing on-demand hot water without the need for gas lines.

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Redwood City

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by capturing heat from exhaust gases. They vent through low-cost PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Inland valleys get hot summers that work water heaters and outdoor lines hard, while coastal areas stay mild and damp year round. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth it.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Redwood City, CA

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside. These units face sun, rain and temperature swings, which can wear out plastic covers and wiring over time. Mountain and foothill homes in Northern California see real winters, so pipes there need insulation, heat tape, or proper draining when a cabin sits empty. When we replace them, we check the pipe cover, freeze protection settings and the electrical connection. We can add a protective pipe cover kit to shield the water and gas lines. Exterior models are often the simplest swap when the gas line is already sized correctly.
