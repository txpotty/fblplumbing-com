    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydrojet Plumbing in Redwood City

    The cost of hydrojet plumbing in Redwood City, CA can vary, usually ranging from $300 to $800, depending on the severity and location of the clog, as well as the length and diameter of the pipe. Complex jobs or main sewer line blockages may cost more, sometimes up to $1,500. We provide a clear estimate after an initial assessment.

    When performed by a trained professional, hydrojetting is generally safe for most types of pipes, including older cast iron or PVC. However, severely damaged, corroded, or fragile pipes might not withstand the high pressure. This is why FBL Plumbing always recommends a pre-service camera inspection to assess pipe integrity before proceeding with hydrojet plumbing.

    FBL Plumbing utilizes professional hydrojetting equipment from reputable manufacturers known for their power and reliability, such as Spartan Tool and MyTana. These professional-grade machines ensure we can deliver high quality, effective drain cleaning services for our clients in Redwood City.

    Usually, a permit is not required for standard hydrojet plumbing services that involve clearing existing drain or sewer lines within a private property in Redwood City. Permits are usually only necessary for new installations, major repairs, or alterations to the plumbing system. FBL Plumbing will always let you know if any permits are needed for your specific job.

    We use professional cart and trailer jetters from manufacturers like Spartan and RIDGID, paired with rotating, penetrating and root-cutting nozzles. Our cameras record video of the line before and after. This equipment lets us handle both homes and businesses.

    Key signs you might need hydrojet plumbing include often recurring clogs that snaking doesn't fully resolve, multiple slow drains throughout your home, gurgling sounds from drains or toilets, or foul odors coming from your drains. A severe sewer backup is also a strong indicator that hydrojetting is needed to clear the main line.

    For most homes, hydrojetting every year or two is sufficient for preventive maintenance, especially in areas with hard water or older plumbing.     While hydrojetting provides a thorough clean, some underlying issues like severely damaged or collapsed pipes might cause clogs to return.
