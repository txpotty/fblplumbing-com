    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Redwood City, CA

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. The jet is the bigger bill. Because jetting removes the buildup that causes repeat clogs, many homeowners spend less over a few years than they would on repeated snaking visits for the same line.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydrojet Plumbing in Redwood City

    Most hydrojetting jobs in Redwood City, CA fall between $300 and $800. We usually include the camera. Long lines, heavy roots or roof-only access push it higher.

    Most homes in Redwood City only need jetting every one to three years, or when clogs start returning. Busy kitchens need more. Grease lines often need quarterly service, and root-prone sewers usually go a year or two.

    No approval is needed for routine jetting. It is treated as maintenance. Permits come into play only if the job turns into a repair, like replacing a section of sewer, installing a new cleanout or relining the pipe, and we pull those when the jurisdiction requires them.

    It is safe for most sound PVC, ABS, cast iron and clay. Pressure varies by pipe type. Thin-wall pipe, old Orangeburg fiber pipe and any line with cracks or separated joints get lower pressure or no jetting at all, which is why the camera inspection happens before we ever turn the jetter on.

    Usually, if the pipe is sound. The camera tells us. Clay, cast iron and even some Orangeburg can be jetted at reduced pressure when the walls are intact, but a pipe with cracks, missing sections or separated joints should be repaired rather than jetted.

    Every few years is plenty for most homes. Commercial kitchens are different. Busy commercial kitchens often jet every three to six months, and apartment buildings with shared stacks may jet once or twice a year depending on what the camera shows.

    Most home jetting jobs take one to three hours. Camera time is included in that. Heavy commercial lines can take most of a day since we work in passes.

    We run professional jetters from manufacturers like Spartan Tool and RIDGID. Cart units handle home lines. Trailer jetters handle mains and commercial lines, paired with SeeSnake cameras.
