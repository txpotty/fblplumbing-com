    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair & Replacement in Redwood City, CA

    Trenchless technology, such as pipe bursting or pipe lining, offers a less invasive option to traditional methods. Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one, fracturing the old pipe in place. Pipe lining installs a new, durable resin liner inside the existing pipe. Both methods significantly reduce the need for excavation, preserving your yard and landscaping in Redwood City, CA while providing a long-lasting solution.
