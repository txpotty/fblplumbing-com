    Well Pressure Tank Leak Repair vs. Replacement in Redwood City

    Leaks at the tank tee, a gauge or a fitting can be fixed. Once the shell leaks, it is done. Bladder tanks with a fixed bladder cannot be rebuilt, and a pinhole in the shell means replacement, while a leaking tee or relief valve is a simple part swap we can usually do in under an hour.

    Amtrol Pressure Tank Replacement in Redwood City

    Replacing an Amtrol tank with another Amtrol is often straightforward. Fittings tend to line up. Even so, we confirm the model is still the right size for your pump, because the old tank may have been too small all along. Check before swapping.

    Bladder-Style Pressure Tank Replacement in Redwood City, CA

    A bladder tank holds water inside a rubber balloon surrounded by compressed air. The air pushes water out when you open a tap. Bladders crack with age, especially if the tank ran with low pre-charge, letting water fill the whole shell and kill the air cushion. Replacement is the fix.

    Well-X-Trol Pressure Tank Installation in Redwood City, CA

    Amtrol's Well-X-Trol line uses a diaphragm design. We put in plenty of them. Its stainless or polypropylene water connection keeps water off the steel shell, limiting internal corrosion, and the factory pre-charge still gets checked and adjusted to your switch on install.

    Emergency Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Redwood City

    A tank that bursts or a pump that runs nonstop needs attention fast. Shut off the breaker to the pump. A pump that keeps running against a failed system can overheat and burn out, so we isolate the problem and get a new tank in, often the same day. Water back tonight.

    Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Redwood City

    Flex-Lite tanks have a composite shell. They will not rust. That makes them a good pick for damp basements, crawlspaces and well houses where steel tanks tend to corrode from the bottom up, plus they are easier to move into cramped utility rooms. Same plumbing as steel.

    Large Capacity Tanks for Ranch and Commercial Wells in Redwood City

    Big properties need big drawdown. Stock tanks, irrigation and barns add up. We install large capacity tanks or banks of tanks on a manifold, so heavy-use systems still keep the pump from short cycling. Floor space matters.

    Preventative Well Pressure Tank Maintenance in Redwood City, CA

    Check the air charge once a year. Shut off the pump, drain the system to zero, and put a tire gauge on the valve at the top of the tank, topping it up to two psi below the cut-in if it reads low. Water out of the valve means a torn bladder.
