    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Redwood City, CA

    Think of a manifold like an electrical panel, but for water lines. Each fixture gets its own line from a central header, and each line gets its own valve, so you can shut off one toilet without killing water to the kitchen. You buy more pipe. The trade-off is almost no fittings hidden in the walls and steadier pressure when two showers run at once.

    Leak Repair with PEX in Redwood City, CA

    A single bad section is not always a reason to repipe. For one split or pitted stretch of copper, we cut it out, transition to PEX with a listed fitting, and usually restore water within a couple of hours. That works for one leak.     Uponor PEX System Installation in Redwood City

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool and a cold-expansion ring. We stretch the pipe and ring, seat the fitting, and within seconds the plastic shrinks back around the barb into a tight seal. There is no crimp gauge to check. Because the fittings are wide inside, each joint costs very little flow.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Redwood City

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. Since a crimp is only as good as the tool that made it, every ring gets a go/no-go gauge check, and any ring that fails gets cut off and redone. It is a five-second check. Skipping it is how leaks start.

    PEX Main Water Line Installation in Redwood City

    The main from the meter to the house is the one line everything else depends on. When an old galvanized or polybutylene service starts leaking underground, the yard stays soggy, the water bill climbs, and the pressure drops at every tap. We trench and lay new PEX. At the foundation we install a new full-port ball valve as the main shutoff.

    Full Home PEX Repiping in Redwood City

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside out. Give it four or five decades and the inside is so packed with rust that a half-inch pipe barely flows, and the first draw in the morning runs brown. A full PEX repipe replaces all of it. Pressure comes back at the fixtures and the rust stops.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Redwood City

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and it really depends on the house. PEX-A bends tighter, recovers from a kink with a heat gun, and its expansion fittings flow more water, while PEX-B costs less and its crimp joints are just as reliable when they are gauged properly. Both last decades. In a cramped attic, we usually pick PEX-A.
