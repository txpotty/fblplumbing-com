    Float Switch Checks in Redwood City, CA

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. Our plumber watches the float rise and drop through a complete cycle. Then we set the on and off points so the pump runs longer, less often.

    Zoeller and Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Redwood City

    Brand matters less than people think, but parts do not cross over cleanly between Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty and Little Giant. We match the float and seals to the model number on the label. A submersible Zoeller and a pedestal Wayne get two different checklists. We know both.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Redwood City, CA

    Grinding, clicking and a hum with no water moving each point to a different failure. We start with the outlet and the cord, then the float, then the motor. A tripped GFCI is a quick fix. A seized motor means a new pump, and we keep replacements on the truck so the pit is never left unprotected overnight.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Redwood City

    The check valve sits on the discharge pipe a foot or so above the pump and keeps the water in the line from falling back into the pit. When it fails, the pump pumps the same water twice. Motors wear out faster. We test the valve, look for a weep hole between the pump and the valve, and check every joint up to the wall.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Redwood City, CA

    Backup pumps sit in the pit next to the main pump and run off a 12-volt battery when the power drops or the primary fails. We check the battery with a load tester, not just a voltmeter. Next, we cut power to the main pump and watch the backup clear the basin by itself. Alarms get tested too.

    Home Sump Pump Maintenance in Redwood City

    A typical house pit is about 18 inches across and two feet deep, and most of what goes wrong happens inside it. We pull the pump, clear the intake screen and scoop out the silt. After that, the pump gets a real water test. Many older homes here sit on raised foundations with crawlspaces, which make supply and drain repairs more accessible than slab work.

    Sump Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Redwood City

    Silt and gravel wash into a pit through the drain tile every time it rains, and over a few years that layer can bury the intake screen. Our plumber drains the basin and digs the sediment out by hand. A clean pit lets the float move freely. The pump runs cooler too.

    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Redwood City

    Submersible pumps sit at the bottom of the pit, which keeps them quiet but hides the problems. Our plumber pulls the unit out so we can see the cord and the shaft seal. A cracked cord usually means a new pump. The intake screen gets cleaned, and we look over the housing for corrosion, since pumps sitting in standing water for years rust from the bottom up.
