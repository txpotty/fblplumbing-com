    Recurring Drain Clogs and Camera Diagnosis in Redwood City, CA

    The same drain clogging every few weeks is a pipe problem, not bad luck. There is a reason it keeps happening. We run a small inspection camera through the line, and you watch the screen with us as it passes sags, broken fittings, scale or roots coming through a joint. The choice is yours from there. A deeper cleaning, a spot repair or a new section are the usual options.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Clogged Drain Repair in Redwood City

    Most clogs cleared with a cable or auger cost $150 to $500. Bigger jobs cost more. Main sewer blockages that need jetting, or a repair dug from the yard because the pipe itself has failed, can run from $500 to $2,000 or more. We give you the number before starting.

    Most single fixture clogs take 30 to 90 minutes. Some take longer. Lines with heavy buildup, limited access or old chemicals in the trap slow things down. We tell you if it will run long.

    Under two hours for most household clogs. Many are done in 45 minutes. Severe blockages in the main that need a camera and jetting can take a few hours or most of a day if the cleanout is hard to reach or buried. We tell you up front if a job will run long.

    Clearing a clog does not need a permit. It is just maintenance. A permit may be needed if the fix turns into replacing pipe, digging up part of the sewer line or changing the layout of the drain system in the house. We tell you in advance if that applies in Redwood City.

    Enzyme cleaners can help. They are safe for pipes. Used regularly, they slow the buildup of grease and soap. They will not clear a full clog, though.

    Hydro jetting sends water at very high pressure through a nozzle that scrubs the pipe wall. It cleans, not just opens. We use it on lines with heavy grease, thick mineral scale or soft roots that a cable would only punch through and leave behind on the pipe walls. We check pipe condition first.

    Water backing up into a different fixture. That one matters most. Gurgling, sewage odors, and repeat clogs in multiple drains usually mean the main. Those need prompt attention.

    Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. Scale narrows the pipe and gives grease and hair something to cling to. Rusty pipe adds to it. Older homes may have cast iron that rusts and roughens inside, and rough walls trap debris quickly.
