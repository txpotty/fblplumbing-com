    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Redwood City, CA

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas.     Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Redwood City

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. We back out the leaking joint, clean the threads, apply gas-rated thread sealant and reassemble it, or replace the fitting or valve if the threads are damaged. We soap-test the repaired joint and pressure test the system before leaving.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Redwood City

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Expansive clay soils are common across Northern California, and seasonal swelling and shrinking can shift slabs and buried sewer laterals. Rusted buried steel gets replaced with polyethylene and tracer wire, and we use a listed transition riser where it rises to the house. Above-ground runs stay in steel. Everything is tested before the trench is backfilled, so a bad joint gets caught while it is still easy to reach.

    CSST Repair in Redwood City

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing is a flexible gas pipe with a plastic jacket, often yellow, that runs through attics and walls in newer homes. It can be damaged. Common damage comes from nails, rodents or lightning, and repairs require cutting out the bad section and using fittings from the same manufacturer. We check bonding on every CSST job. A bonding clamp and conductor tie the CSST to the home's grounding system, following the maker's instructions and code.
