    Why Choose FBL Plumbing for Grease Trap Cleaning in Redwood City, CA?

    A grease trap works by slowing the water down long enough for fat to float and food to sink. Past a quarter full, that stops. The grease then slips past the outlet into the lateral, cools into a hard white crust, and a cable machine only bores a narrow hole through it.

    • System Longevity, Less grease in the line means fewer line jettings and a longer life for the drain pipe.
    • Prevent Backups, Pumping on schedule keeps grease from slipping past the outlet and setting up in the line.
    • Complete Pump-Outs, We pull grease, water and the settled solids every time.

    Citywide Coverage

    Interceptors under parking lots need room for the truck. The visit gets planned around your lot. If customers park over the lid during the day, we come before opening or after close so nobody has to move a car and the hose does not cross a busy drive aisle.

    Experienced Team

    Our techs have pumped enough traps to spot a cracked baffle by the way the grease cap sits. It shows in the details. They know that a cap floating unevenly usually means a baffle has broken loose, and that grease in the outlet chamber means the outlet tee is missing or plugged.

    An open interceptor hatch in a parking lot is a serious fall hazard. Cones go out first. We keep the lid off only while the hose is in, and we check it is seated flat and bolted down on locking models before we leave.
