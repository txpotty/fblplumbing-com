    Drain Line Leak Under Foundation in Redwood City, CA

    Drain leaks under a slab are harder to find. Drains carry no pressure. A hydrostatic test shows whether the line holds water, and a camera run shows us the cracked fitting, offset joint or roots causing it. Fixes include tunneling, slab cuts or a pipe liner, and we explain each one.

    Direct Foundation Leak Repair in Redwood City, CA

    Direct repair means cutting into the floor right over the leak. It is often the cheapest fix. A small opening gets cut, the pipe is exposed and repaired, the line is tested, and the hole is backfilled in layers and patched level. Your flooring contractor can finish the surface afterward.

    Foundation Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Redwood City, CA

    Direct repairs usually cost least up front. But flooring repair adds cost. A reroute costs somewhat more but avoids concrete work and future leaks in the abandoned pipe, and a repipe fits homes that have already leaked more than once. We compare total costs, flooring and drywall included.

    Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Redwood City, CA

    Older homes in Redwood City, CA often have soft copper running under the slab. We cut out the damaged length and sweat or press in new Type L copper, then pressure test the whole line before backfilling so you know the repair and the pipe on either side are tight.
