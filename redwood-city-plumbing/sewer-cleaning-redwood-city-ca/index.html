    What Our Redwood City, CA Sewer Techs Handle

    Root Removal From Sewer Lines in Redwood City

    Trees send roots toward any moisture they can find. Leaky joints feed them all year. We use a sectional cable with a root saw sized to the pipe to cut the mass free and pull it out, then follow with a jetter if fine root hairs are left behind, and a camera shows where roots are entering so you can choose between lining and repair.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Redwood City

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. Cabling just opens a hole. A jetter sends high-pressure water around the full circumference of the pipe, removing buildup that a cable would leave behind.

    Preventative Sewer Maintenance in Redwood City

    Roots come back after cutting. Usually within a year or so. Putting a root-prone main on a yearly cleaning schedule keeps the growth small enough that it never stops the line, and it costs far less than an after-hours emergency call.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Redwood City

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Each fails in its own way. Clay joints invite roots, and Orangeburg, a tar-paper pipe from the mid-1900s, flattens under the weight of the soil over time, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Redwood City, CA

    On a repeat backup, the camera goes in first. Guessing costs money. The camera feed shows roots, grease, breaks and bellies in real time, and the locator marks the spot on the ground above.

    Clogged Main Sewer Line Solutions in Redwood City, CA

    Several drains backing up at once is a main line problem. One pipe, many fixtures. We clear it with a sectional cable or a jetter, then camera the line to see whether the cause was roots, debris or a structural problem like a broken joint or an offset section.

    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Redwood City, CA

    Cleaning cannot fix a broken pipe. A crack stays a crack. When a line keeps backing up, we put the cost of regular cleaning next to the cost of a repair so you can see which makes sense, with the numbers in writing.
