    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Upgrades in Redwood City, CA

    Condensing gas tanks run around 90 percent efficient or better and vent through PVC, while a standard atmospheric tank sits closer to 60 percent. They cost more and need a condensate drain and an outlet. Payback depends on usage.

    Tankless Gas Conversion in Redwood City

    Going tankless usually means a bigger gas line, since a whole-house tankless unit can draw 199,000 BTU against the 40,000 a tank burner uses. Most homes need the line upsized from the meter.     Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Redwood City, CA

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through 2 or 3-inch PVC, so they can sit far from a chimney. A standard outlet has to be within reach of the cord. Vent length and elbows stay inside the manufacturer's limits.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Redwood City, CA

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. A natural gas unit does not go on propane unless the manufacturer allows a conversion. Pressure gets checked with a manometer.
