    Water Softener Leak Detection & Repair in Redwood City, CA

    Leaks around your water softener can be caused by cracked tanks, loose fittings, faulty O-rings, or pressure issues. Even a small leak can lead to water damage and wasted water. Our team uses specialized tools to quickly locate the source of any leak, whether it's a slow drip or a significant pool. We then perform precise repairs, from tightening connections and replacing seals to patching cracks or replacing damaged components, protecting your home from water intrusion.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Redwood City

    A softener using far too much salt or water usually has settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units may regenerate on a schedule regardless of use. We retest hardness, reprogram the valve or upgrade the head to a metered control. Proper settings can noticeably cut salt and water use.

    Hard Water Symptoms & Diagnostics in Redwood City

    Control Valve Troubleshooting & Repair in Redwood City, CA

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, regulating the flow of water and initiating regeneration cycles. When it malfunctions, your system can stop softening water, regenerate too often, or leak. We diagnose issues like stuck pistons, worn out seals, motor failures, or programming errors. Our plumbers carry a range of replacement parts for popular brands like Fleck, Clack, and Autotrol, allowing a quick and effective repair to restore proper operation.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Redwood City, CA

    A salt bridge is a hard crust that forms in the brine tank and keeps salt from dissolving. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. We break up bridges, clean out mushy salt, inspect the float and brine well and repair the safety valve. We also recommend salt types that bridge less in warm or humid spaces.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Redwood City, CA

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. A new quality softener installed usually costs $1,500 to $3,500. If the tank and valve are sound, repair usually wins. If the valve is obsolete and parts are scarce, replacing the head or the whole unit may make more sense.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Redwood City, CA

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates suction to draw brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. When it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying. We clean or replace the injector and screen and check the brine line for leaks.
