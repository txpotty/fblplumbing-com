    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Lodi

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Scale on the tank bottom traps heat and causes water to pop and rumble as it heats. We flush until it runs clear. Bad tanks need a few rounds.

    Gas Burner and Venting Checks in Lodi

    A blue flame is what we want to see. Poor flames mean dirty burners, so we clean them, test the sensor and check the draft. Bad venting is dangerous. We test the gas connection too.

    Tankless Descaling in Lodi, CA

    Scale is the tankless unit's main enemy. Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running hard. We close the isolation valves, connect a small pump and circulate descaling solution through the heat exchanger for the time the manufacturer recommends, then flush it with clean water. We clean the inlet filter. Igniter, burner and venting get a safety check.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Lodi

    Some water eats anode rods fast. Depending on your water chemistry, a rod that should last five years might be spent in three, so we pull it on every visit instead of assuming it is fine because the tank is still young. Spent rods get replaced. It keeps rust off the tank lining.
