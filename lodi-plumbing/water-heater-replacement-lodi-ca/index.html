    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Lodi

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is common, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. Since tankless can pull four times the gas of a tank, the meter line usually gets bigger, and the old vent is swapped for stainless or PVC. We price both options.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Lodi, CA

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.

    Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith in Lodi, CA

    We install Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith on the majority of our swaps. All three hold up.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Lodi, CA

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Lodi, CA homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Then we leak test.

    Rheem and AO Smith Water Heater Replacement in Lodi, CA

    Rheem and AO Smith cover nearly every size and fuel, from 30-gallon short tanks to 80-gallon tall models. We install both, and the right pick often comes down to what fits the space, which models carry the warranty length you want and which ones the local supply house keeps on the shelf for fast replacement. We lay out the options.
