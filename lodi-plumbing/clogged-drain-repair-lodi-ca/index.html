    Bathroom Sink and Vanity Drain Clogs in Lodi, CA

    Vanity drains clog at the pop-up stopper first. Hair wraps around the rod. We pull and clean the stopper, clean the trap, cable the drain arm and swap out worn parts while we are there. Scale buildup is common there.

    Laundry and Utility Drain Clogs in Lodi, CA

    A washing machine dumps up to 20 gallons in a couple of minutes. Fibers and lint go with the water.     Outdoor Drain & Storm Drain Clearing in Lodi, CA

    Patio drains, driveway trench drains and downspout lines fill with leaves, grit and mud.     Toilet Clog Repair in Lodi

    A toilet clog usually sits in the trapway, the S-shaped channel inside the bowl. Plungers often miss it. We use a closet auger with a rubber sleeve, which reaches through the trapway and pulls out the blockage without scratching the porcelain. Toys and wipes come out this way. If the auger finds nothing, the clog is past the toilet and we check the main.

    Kitchen Sink Clog Repair in Lodi

    Kitchen sinks clog from grease that cools and hardens on the pipe wall. Food and soap stick to it. We pull the trap, run a cable through the trap arm and down the branch line to the main stack, and check the disposal for a jam or worn impeller. Some lines get a short jetting to clean the walls.
