    Gas Leak Detection in Lodi

    A rotten egg smell, hissing near a line, dead grass in a stripe across the yard or a gas meter that moves with everything off are the common leak signs. Trust your nose. We isolate sections of the system and use electronic combustible gas detectors to narrow the source, then confirm with soap solution at fittings, valves and unions. After we locate the leak, we price the repair in writing.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Lodi

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Many older homes here sit on raised foundations with crawlspaces, which make supply and drain repairs easier to reach than slab work. We replace corroded sections with polyethylene pipe laid with tracer wire, using an approved transition riser where the line comes up out of the ground at the house. Above-ground runs stay in steel. We test before backfilling so any problem joint is fixed while the trench is still open.

    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Lodi, CA

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Years of vibration from a furnace or a house settling can loosen threaded fittings, and pipe in a damp garage or an exterior wall can rust from the outside in. We reseal or replace the bad sections. Outdoor runs get coated or painted after the repair so rust does not start again.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Lodi

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. New coated stainless connectors go on, sized to the appliance, with a reachable shutoff valve if the old one is stuck or missing. This is a quick fix. Ranges, dryers, water heaters and furnaces all need proper connections, and most also need a sediment trap ahead of the appliance gas valve.

    Emergency Gas Line Repair in Lodi

    If you smell gas, leave the building, skip the light switches and call your gas utility or 911 from outside. Then call us. Once the utility has shut off the meter and made the area safe, we locate the leak, make the repair and run the pressure test needed before service can be restored. Pilots get relit once gas is back. Most emergency repairs are done in a single visit with parts from the truck.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Lodi, CA

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. Our work covers propane yard lines, regulator replacement and fitting repairs, plus a check that every appliance has its propane orifices installed. Wrong orifices make a burner run dirty and unsafe. We test the system the same way we test natural gas piping.

    Gas Line Extensions and Relocations in Lodi

    Adding a gas fireplace, moving a laundry room or switching a range from electric to gas usually means extending the gas line. Sizing comes first. We add up the BTU load of every appliance on the system, measure the longest run and pick a pipe size that keeps pressure up at the farthest appliance when everything runs together. Then we route it, tie it in and test it.

    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Lodi

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. With every appliance off, a moving test dial on the meter tells us gas is escaping somewhere, and then we isolate branches to narrow it down. An electronic detector does the rest. Soap solution at the suspect fitting confirms it with bubbles, and then we repair the joint, replace the section or swap the connector before testing again.
