    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Lodi, CA

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Two access holes, sometimes one. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one and splits the old pipe outward, while cured-in-place lining pushes a resin-soaked felt tube into the old pipe and hardens it into a new wall. Both save driveways and mature trees. We tell you which one your pipe can take.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Lodi

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. It flattens with age. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, and once the camera shows failures along the whole run, patching it stops being worth the money. PVC goes in, usually end to end. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Lodi, CA

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. Fixing the joint treats the cause.     Pipe Burst & Collapse Repair in Lodi, CA

We dig at the mark and replace the broken length with PVC. Lining cannot fix a full collapse.
