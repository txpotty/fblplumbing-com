    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Lodi, CA

    Electrical faults cause a big share of well calls, from a tripped breaker to a burned contact in the pressure switch. After that, we look at the capacitor and relay in the control box.     Pressure Switch Replacement in Lodi

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, a clogged sensing tube and ant nests are the usual reasons one fails. After swapping the switch and clearing the tube, we set cut-in and cut-out to match the tank, usually 40 and 60 psi. This is among the least expensive well repairs.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Lodi

    Short cycling is the classic pressure tank symptom, with the pump clicking on and off every few seconds while a faucet runs. Usually the bladder has failed or the tank has lost its air charge. We check the precharge with the tank drained. It should sit about 2 psi below the cut-in pressure, so a 40/60 switch wants roughly 38 psi in the tank.

    Submersible Pump Pulling and Repair in Lodi

    Sand wears impellers down over time, and a pump that once filled the tank in a minute starts taking three. The motor, the wire splice and the check valve are the other common failures. Before pulling anything, we test the motor and wire from the wellhead. Replacement pumps get sized to the yield and depth of the well.
