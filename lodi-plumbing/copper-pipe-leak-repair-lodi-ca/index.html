    Preventive Copper Pipe Inspection in Lodi, CA

    An inspection catches thin spots before they leak. Plan on an hour.     Copper Pipe Joint Leak Repair in Lodi, CA

    Joints leak from poor original soldering, thermal expansion or a house that settles. Expansive clay soils are common across Northern California, and seasonal swelling and shrinking can shift slabs and buried sewer laterals. Look for white crust.     Hot Water Copper Line Repair in Lodi, CA

    Hot water lines corrode faster than cold lines. Heat speeds it up. Recirculation pumps that run around the clock push water through the copper at high velocity and wear it from the inside, which is why we often add a timer to the pump after fixing the leak. Less run time, less wear.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in Lodi

    Pinholes form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. Hot lines get it worst. They often appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher, which we fix by replacing that length with heavier pipe. One usually means more are coming.

    Commercial Copper Pipe Repair in Lodi

    Many commercial buildings use larger diameter copper for domestic water supply. Leaks affect more than one unit. Leaks in these systems require careful shutdown planning, and press fittings shorten the shutdown and skip the torch. Isolation valves get added if missing.

    Solder Joint Leak Repair in Lodi, CA

    Older solder joints can fail from poor original work, thermal expansion or vibration. Crusty fittings are a clue. A leaking joint usually weeps for a while before it drips, so we either resolder it or replace the fitting and a short length of pipe. Then we pressure test it.
