    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Lodi, CA

    Bursting fits old cast iron or galvanized lines that will shatter under the head. Pulling suits softer pipe like poly or copper. Either way the new pipe follows the old path, which matters when the route runs under a patio or a neighbor's fence, and the yard work shrinks to two pits. Rock can stop it.

    Damage Assessment Before Replacement in Lodi

    We start at the meter. With every tap closed, the leak indicator should sit still.     Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Upgrades in Lodi, CA

    Street pressure varies a lot from one neighborhood to the next. Code says no more than 80 psi inside the house. If your new line comes in above that, we add a pressure reducing valve right after the main shutoff, set it around 55 to 65 psi, and swap the old gate valve for a full-port ball valve. A quarter turn and the water is off.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Lodi

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. It does not corrode or build scale. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, use only fittings listed for burial, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    Trenchless Bores Under Driveways in Lodi

Heavy rock is the exception.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Lodi, CA
