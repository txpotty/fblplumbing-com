    Exterior Waterproofing Solutions in Lodi, CA

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing, cleaning the wall, and applying a membrane with a drainage board over it. It is the most thorough fix and the most disruptive one. Expect a few days of work. We bring in a waterproofing partner for the membrane and handle the footing drain and its tie-in to a sump or daylight outlet ourselves.

    Commercial Basement Waterproofing in Lodi

    Commercial basements hold inventory, records or mechanical rooms that cannot sit in water. We look at floor drains, elevator pits and sump systems as one system, since a single failed check valve or a clogged pit inlet can flood the whole lower level. Duplex pumps help. A high-water alarm warns staff before the floor gets wet.

    Waterproofing for Older Northern California Homes in Lodi, CA

    Older basements were often built with block walls and no exterior membrane. Older neighborhoods in Northern California include prewar and postwar homes with galvanized supply lines and cast iron or clay sewer pipe that are past the end of their life. Water collects inside hollow block cores, and weep holes at the base let it drain into a perimeter channel. Clay tile drains fail too.

    Hydrostatic Pressure Management in Lodi, CA

    Water pressing against the outside of a basement wall works into the weakest spot, usually the cove joint where wall meets floor. Tree roots seek out sewer laterals during dry spells, which is why heavy rains often reveal damaged lines through sudden backups. We relieve it with a perimeter channel at the cove joint that carries water to a sump pit. No pressure, no seepage.

    Exterior Membrane Waterproofing in Lodi, CA

    Excavating the outside of a foundation puts the repair where the water actually gets in. Coastal fog and moisture can speed corrosion on exposed fittings, so exterior valves and hose bibs deserve an occasional check. We pick a membrane rated for that exposure, lay new perforated drain pipe at the footing in clean stone, and backfill so the grade slopes away from the house at least six inches over the first ten feet. We extend the downspouts while the yard is open.

    Clogged Drain Tile Repair in Lodi

    Footing drains clog with silt and roots over the years, and once they stop flowing the water table at the wall rises. We camera the tile from the sump inlet to see where it is blocked. Jetting clears it. If the old clay tile has collapsed, it gets replaced with new perforated pipe.

    Wall and Slab Seepage Repair in Lodi

    Seepage at the wall or the slab joint is usually surface water pooling against the foundation. Expansive clay soils are common across Northern California, and seasonal swelling and shrinking can shift slabs and buried sewer laterals. Before anyone cuts the floor, we check the gutters, downspout extensions and the grade along that wall, because fixing those outside often stops seepage for a fraction of the cost. We always try the cheap fixes first.
