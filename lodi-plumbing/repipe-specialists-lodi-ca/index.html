    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Lodi

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Expansive clay soils are common across Northern California, and seasonal swelling and shrinking can shift slabs and buried sewer laterals. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Drain Line Repiping in Lodi

    While often overlooked, your home's drain lines are just as important as your supply lines. Old cast iron or corroded drain pipes can lead to frequent clogs, slow drainage, and even sewage backups. FBL Plumbing provides complete drain line repiping in Lodi, replacing failing waste pipes with new, smooth PVC or ABS lines. This supports efficient waste removal and prevents future drainage problems, protecting your home from foul odors and unsanitary conditions.

    Efficient PEX Repiping in Lodi, CA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and very popular choice for repiping projects in Lodi, CA. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also very resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a dependable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Lodi

    Copper piping offers exceptional durability, corrosion resistance, and a long lifespan, making it a premium choice for many homeowners in Lodi. While the upfront cost is higher than PEX, copper provides superior rigidity and resistance to UV light if exposed. Our team carefully solders each joint, ensuring a leak-proof and robust plumbing system that can withstand the test of time, including changing temperatures and local water conditions. Copper also naturally inhibits bacterial growth, contributing to cleaner water.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Lodi

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to three days.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Lodi, CA

    Polybutylene pipes, installed in many homes between the 1970s and 1990s, are known for premature failure due to chemical reactions with disinfectants in the water supply. If your home in Lodi, CA has polybutylene, a full replacement is essential to prevent catastrophic water damage. FBL Plumbing offers specialized polybutylene plumbing replacement, carefully removing the old, brittle pipes and installing a dependable new system.

    Copper Repiping in Lodi, CA

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes. Joints are soldered with lead-free solder or pressed with ProPress fittings. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a bit longer than PEX.

    Drywall Patching and Restoration in Lodi, CA

    Repiping requires small openings in walls and ceilings to reach fixtures. Our crews cut clean openings, protect surrounding areas and patch every hole when the plumbing is done. We match common textures like knockdown and orange peel. Your walls are ready for paint when we finish.
