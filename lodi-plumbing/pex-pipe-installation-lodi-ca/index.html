    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Lodi, CA

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A bends the easiest and uses expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and uses crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C handles more like B. Every one is listed for potable water. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in Lodi, CA

    Price on a repipe comes down to a few things: bathroom count, square footage, how many stories, and how easy the attic and walls are to work in. PEX usually beats copper on price because the pipe costs less and a crew finishes in two to four days, not a full week. Drywall patching may be separate. Ask and we will price all three, PEX-A, PEX-B and copper, side by side.

    Pinhole leaks in copper tend to come in clusters.
