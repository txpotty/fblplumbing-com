    What We Install in Lodi, CA

    Sump Pump and Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Lodi

    Basements and crawl spaces that take on water need a sump pump, and fixtures below the sewer line need an ejector. One moves groundwater, the other moves sewage, and they are not interchangeable. Sizing comes first. We match pump capacity to how fast the pit fills, put the pump on its own circuit and run the discharge with a check valve.

    PEX Repiping in Lodi

    Copper repipes cost more and take longer, which is why most whole-house repipes today use PEX-A or PEX-B tubing with brass or poly fittings. You also hear less of it, since PEX softens water hammer. It also handles a freeze better. Freezing is uncommon along the coast and on valley floors in Northern California, but frosty nights do happen and can crack exposed outdoor pipes. Fittings are matched to the tubing manufacturer.

    Water Heater Replacement in Lodi

    Most tank heaters give out between 8 and 12 years, sooner with heavy sediment. We install 40 and 50 gallon tanks, plus 75 gallon units for big households, from Rheem and Bradford White, with new flex connectors, a relief line piped to code and a vent check on every job. An expansion tank check is part of every job. One visit covers most installs.

    Expansion Tank and Sewage Ejector Installs in Lodi, CA

    A closed water system has nowhere for heated water to expand, so pressure climbs every single time the heater cycles. An expansion tank absorbs that swing and keeps the relief valve from dripping. A PRV usually means you need one. Fixtures below the sewer line get an ejector system with a sealed basin, a check valve and a vent.

    Water Service Line Installation in Lodi, CA

    New water service lines run from the meter to the house, and the old one usually comes out in the same trench. Many older homes here sit on raised foundations with crawlspaces, which make supply and drain repairs more accessible than slab work. We bring the digging equipment the ground calls for. A new main shutoff goes in where the line enters. Depth and bedding protect the pipe. We test pressure and flow at the house before we backfill.

    Gas Line Installation for New Appliances in Lodi, CA

    Water heaters, ranges, dryers, fireplaces and pool heaters all pull from the same gas service. Each branch is sized from the total BTU load and the run length so the last appliance on the line is not starved. Every new line is pressure tested. A load that outgrows the meter needs an upgrade, which we coordinate with the gas company.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Lodi, CA

    Navien and Rinnai tankless units hang on a wall and free up the floor space a tank used to take. Gas models usually need a bigger gas line and new stainless or PVC venting. Electric models need amperage. Sizing is based on flow in gallons per minute and how far the incoming water has to be heated. Inland valleys get hot summers that work water heaters and outdoor lines hard, while coastal areas stay mild and damp year round. Isolation valves go on every unit for descaling.

    Gas Line Installation in Lodi, CA

    A new range or an outdoor kitchen all start with the same math: total BTU load and the length of the run. From there we size the pipe, choose between black iron and CSST, and dig the trench for any buried section. Each joint is checked with leak solution. Then the whole line holds pressure on a gauge before the inspector sees it.
