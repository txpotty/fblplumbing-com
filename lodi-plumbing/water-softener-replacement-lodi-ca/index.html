    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Lodi, CA

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more economical than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes better sense.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Lodi, CA

    For homeowners in Lodi, CA looking for an alternative to traditional salt-based systems, salt-free water conditioners offer a different approach. These units, sometimes called descalers, condition water to prevent mineral scale without removing the minerals or adding salt. While they do not produce soft feeling water, they effectively protect pipes and appliances from scale. We can replace your existing salt-free system or help you switch from a salt-based unit.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Lodi

    Large households and homes with very hard water can benefit from a dual tank system that provides soft water around the clock. While one tank regenerates, the other stays in service. Kinetico and similar non-electric twin tank systems are popular for this reason. We size dual tank systems to your peak demand. They cost more upfront but prevent hard water bleed-through during regeneration.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Lodi

    The cost of water softener replacement in Lodi, CA varies based on the type of system, its capacity and installation complexity. Generally, homeowners can expect to pay between $1,500 and $4,000 for a quality unit and professional installation. We provide clear, upfront quotes after assessing your needs.

    We install systems with Fleck, Clack and Kinetico valves, among others. These brands have good parts availability and proven performance.

    Rural properties around Lodi often rely on private wells, which may need treatment for iron, sediment, or other minerals. Hard water contains dissolved minerals like calcium and magnesium. These minerals can lead to scale buildup in pipes, reduced efficiency of water heaters and appliances, spots on dishes, and dry skin and hair. A working water softener is the best defense for combating these issues in Lodi homes.

    New water softeners usually come with two types of warranties. There is a manufacturer's warranty on the equipment itself, which can range from 5 to 10 years or more for tanks and control valves, and an installation warranty from FBL Plumbing. We stand behind our work with a solid warranty for your peace of mind.

    Most standard residential water softener replacements can be completed within 3 to 5 hours. This includes disconnecting the old unit, installing the new one, making plumbing connections and testing the system. More complex installations may take a bit longer.

    While it might seem like a straightforward task, water softener replacement involves working with water lines, drain connections and sometimes electrical components. Improper installation can lead to leaks, inefficient operation or even water damage. Hiring a professional means the job is done correctly, safely and to code.

    Most softeners last 10 to 15 years. Resin and valves wear out sooner with very hard or chlorinated water.
