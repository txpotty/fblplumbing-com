    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repair in Lodi, CA

    A leaking bypass valve can let hard water slip past the softener without you noticing. We test and rebuild bypass valves and replace cracked fittings. We also inspect the drain line for kinks and proper air gap and the brine overflow line for correct routing. These small fixes head off leaks and backflow problems.

    Maintenance Versus New Softener Costs in Lodi, CA

    A maintenance visit usually costs far less than a new system, which often runs $1,200 to $3,500 installed. Resin rebedding typically costs $300 to $700 and can bring an older tank back to full capacity. Most quality softeners last 10 to 15 years with service. We will tell you when a repair makes sense and when a replacement is the smarter investment.

    Regeneration Settings and Hardness Testing in Lodi, CA

    Many softeners are set incorrectly from day one or never updated when household size changes. We test your raw water hardness and program the valve to match your actual use. Metered systems regenerate based on gallons used, which saves salt compared to time clock models. Correct settings keep water soft without squandering salt or water.

    Valve and Controller Diagnostics in Lodi, CA

    The control valve and electronic controller regulate the entire softening process. Our technicians perform diagnostic checks to confirm these components are operating accurately, adjusting programming as needed. We check for wear and tear on moving parts and lubricate them to prevent future issues, keeping your water softener reliable in Lodi, CA.

    Brine Tank Cleaning and Inspection in Lodi, CA

    Over time, sludge and insoluble materials can accumulate in your brine tank, hindering the softening process. Our maintenance includes thoroughly cleaning the brine tank, inspecting for any cracks or leaks and ensuring the float valve operates correctly. This step is crucial for the efficient regeneration of your water softener in Lodi, CA.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Lodi

    Softener resin can foul with iron, manganese or sediment and lose capacity over time. Chlorine and chloramine in city water also slowly break resin down. We run a resin cleaner through the system and test performance afterward. If the resin is exhausted, we rebed the tank with fresh high-capacity resin, which costs much less than a new system.
