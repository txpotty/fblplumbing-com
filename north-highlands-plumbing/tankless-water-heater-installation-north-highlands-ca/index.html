    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in North Highlands, CA

    Calcium scale is the leading cause of tankless heater failure. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides added protection.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in North Highlands

    Converting from a tank requires more than swapping units. A 40 gallon tank heater uses roughly 40,000 BTU, while a whole-home tankless unit may need up to 199,000 BTU, so the gas line usually needs to be upsized. Venting changes from a metal flue to PVC or stainless, and the unit moves to a wall location. We remove and dispose of your old tank and patch the old flue penetration.

    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in North Highlands, CA
