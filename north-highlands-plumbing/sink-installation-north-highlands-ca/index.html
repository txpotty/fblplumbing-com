    Drop-In Sink Installation in North Highlands, CA

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim covers small flaws in the cutout, so a laminate counter can take a new self-rimming sink with only a check of the hole size and a bead of silicone or putty as the maker specifies. Mounting clips snug it down. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Undermount Sink Installation in North Highlands

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. We set the bowl with the clips or anchors the stone shop provides, run a continuous bead of 100 percent silicone around the rim, and leave it braced while the adhesive cures overnight. Adhesive by itself will not hold it. Plumbing goes on the next day.

    Leak-Proof Sink Connections in North Highlands, CA

    Every joint gets tested before we leave. The bowl gets filled and drained while we watch every nut under the sink with a light and a dry towel. Paper towels catch the smallest drip. Pipe dope goes on threads, not compression fittings.

    Pedestal Sink Mounting in North Highlands

    The pedestal is mostly for looks. The basin itself hangs on a bracket lagged into 2x blocking behind the drywall, and if the old wall has no blocking we open it, add a board between the studs, and patch it back. Anchors in drywall will fail. A loose basin cracks the trap.

    Wall-Mount and ADA Sink Installation in North Highlands, CA

    Wall-hung lavatories need a carrier or heavy blocking. ADA layouts call for a rim at or under 34 inches, open knee space, and insulated pipes below. An offset tailpiece frees up knee room. Lever or sensor faucets finish it off.

    Kitchen Sink Installation in North Highlands

    Kitchen sinks come in four mounts: drop-in, undermount, farmhouse and flush-set. Each one needs its own support, and a cast iron farmhouse sink can weigh well over a hundred pounds before it holds any water, so the cabinet often needs reinforcement first. The cabinet gets checked before install day. Disposal and dishwasher connections are part of the job.

    Kitchen Sink Replacement in North Highlands
