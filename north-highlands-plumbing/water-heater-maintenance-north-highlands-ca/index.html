    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in North Highlands

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion.     The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building dangerous pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats.
