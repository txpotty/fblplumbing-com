    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in North Highlands

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules permit. We size them to avoid too much pressure loss on your system.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in North Highlands

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are commonly required for high-hazard applications in North Highlands. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. FBL Plumbing focuses on precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties.     Frequently Asked Questions About Backflow Preventer Installation in North Highlands

    Residential pressure vacuum breakers usually cost $250 to $700 installed. Double check and RPZ assemblies for larger homes or businesses range from $800 to $3,500 or more depending on size and enclosure needs.

    FBL Plumbing stands behind our work with a full warranty on our backflow preventer installations. This warranty covers both the labor and the installed device, providing you with peace of mind. Specific warranty details will be provided with your quote.

    We install backflow preventers from leading manufacturers known for quality and reliability. Our go-to brands include Watts, Febco, Zurn and Apollo, so you receive a durable, effective device that meets all industry standards.

    Backflow preventers are installed at cross-connection points where non-potable water could enter the potable water supply. Common locations include irrigation system connections, fire sprinkler lines, boiler systems and connections to auxiliary water supplies, often just after the main water meter or at the point of hazard.

    Most residential backflow preventer installations can be finished within 2 to 4 hours. More complex commercial installations or those requiring extensive pipe changes might take a full day. We always aim for efficient service with minimal disruption to your water supply.

    An RPZ, or reduced pressure zone device, provides the highest level of protection and is used for high-hazard applications where contaminants could be toxic. A DCVA, or double check valve assembly, is used for low-hazard applications where contaminants are non-toxic. The choice depends on the risk of contamination at the cross-connection point.

    Backflow is when water flows the wrong way through a pipe and carries contaminants into the clean water supply. It can happen from back siphonage during a pressure drop or back pressure from pumps and other equipment.
