    Sump Pump Types We Replace in North Highlands, CA

    Wayne Sump Pump Replacement in North Highlands

    Wayne makes everything from a thermoplastic quarter-horse pump at the hardware store to cast iron models with vertical switches, and the cheap ones are what we replace most. The usual failure is a burned-out motor or a float that hangs up on the cord. We replace it with the right Wayne or another cast iron unit. Takes about two hours.

    Zoeller Switch and Pump Repair in North Highlands

    Zoeller units with a piggyback float plug the pump cord into the switch cord, and if that piggyback plug fails the pump never gets power. That is the first thing we test. Sometimes a new switch is all you need, and sometimes the motor windings are shot. You will know which before we quote a pump.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in North Highlands

    If your pump runs every 30 seconds during a storm and still loses ground, it is undersized for the water coming in. First we time the pump cycles and measure the pit refill rate. From there it is math. A half-horse or three-quarter-horse pump with a higher gallons-per-minute rating at your lift height may be the answer, sometimes paired with a 2-inch discharge.

    Pedestal Pumps for Shallow Pits in North Highlands, CA

    Older basements sometimes have a narrow, shallow pit poured right into the floor, and a pedestal pump is often the only thing that fits without breaking concrete. We replace the pump and float rod and rerun the PVC up to the check valve. Cheaper than a submersible, too.

    Choosing a Backup Battery in North Highlands

    Battery choice decides how long the backup actually runs. Most backups use a sealed AGM or deep-cycle battery around 75 to 100 amp-hours, which covers several hours of on-and-off pumping. We size it to how often your pump runs on a wet day. Plan on a new battery every three to five years.

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in North Highlands, CA

    Yard drainage pits and window well pumps can sit dry from fall to spring and then seize the first time a big storm fills them. We swap in a corrosion-resistant pump and clear out leaves and silt. Discharge goes away from the foundation. Test them each spring.

    Elevator and Garage Pit Pumps in North Highlands

    Elevator pits and parking garage sumps often require oil-sensing controls that stop the pump from sending hydraulic fluid or motor oil into the storm drain. We replace those pumps and controls to meet code. Two alternating pumps on one controller mean one can fail without flooding the pit. Insurers sometimes ask for proof.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in North Highlands, CA

    Zoeller cast iron pumps, the M53 and M63 in particular, are what we pull off the truck most often because they take heat and hard cycling better than plastic housings. If you have a Zoeller now, we can usually match the discharge outlet and switch style so the swap goes quickly. Pump not keeping up? We can move you up to a half-horse model like the M98.
