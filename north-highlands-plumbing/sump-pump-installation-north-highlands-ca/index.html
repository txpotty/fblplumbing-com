    New Sump Pit Installation in North Highlands

    No pit yet? We saw-cut the slab at the low corner, break out the concrete and dig down through soil, hardpan or rock to set an 18 by 22 inch basin. Washed gravel goes around the basin so water can reach it. The slab gets patched with new concrete. The pump, lid and discharge go in last.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in North Highlands

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi monitors go further and send a phone alert when the pump runs too long, the power drops or the water climbs past a set level. Rentals need these. They can go on a new pump or one that is already working.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in North Highlands

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. We install duplex systems with alternating pumps. Downtime costs money. We plan the install around your business hours.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in North Highlands

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. Because the motor is cooled by the surrounding water, they run quieter and can push more gallons per minute up a tall lift than most pedestal pumps. The pit lid sits flush. We seal the cord grommet and the lid on every install.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in North Highlands, CA

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. The charger keeps the battery topped off. Batteries need replacing every few years. They matter most below finished rooms.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in North Highlands

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. We install exterior basins with pumps for those spots. Slopes toward the house are the usual cause. We keep the outlet away from your foundation and the neighbor line.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in North Highlands, CA

    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options. We install their 250 and 280 series often. Parts are easy to find. We drill a 3/16 inch weep hole in the discharge to stop air lock.
