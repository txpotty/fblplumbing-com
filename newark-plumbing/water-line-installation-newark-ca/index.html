    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Line Installation in Newark

    The cost of water line installation in Newark can vary quite a bit, typically ranging from $1,500 to $6,000 for a main service line. Factors include the length of the line, the material used, the excavation method, trenchless or traditional, and any permits or site restoration. We provide detailed, upfront estimates after an on-site assessment.

    A typical residential water line takes one to two days, plus time for utility locates and inspection. Rocky ground or long runs can add a day or two.

    Some homeowners dig their own trench, but connecting to the meter, sizing the pipe and passing inspection are best done by a licensed plumber. A poor joint underground can leak for months before anyone notices.

    Common signs include persistently low water pressure throughout your home, discolored or rusty water from your taps, frequent leaks in your yard or near the foundation, or unusually high water bills without a clear cause. These often indicate an aging or failing main water line.

    Pipe manufacturers offer long warranties on PEX and HDPE, and copper has a long track record. We also warranty our installation labor and will go over the specific terms with you before work starts.

    For most homes, PEX and copper are generally the best materials. PEX is flexible, corrosion-resistant and handles minor ground shifts well. Copper is very durable, long-lasting and resists bacterial growth. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. The choice usually depends on budget, water chemistry and site conditions.

    FBL Plumbing offers a solid warranty on our water line installations, covering both parts and labor. The specific terms will be outlined in your project agreement, giving you peace of mind about the longevity and quality of our work.
