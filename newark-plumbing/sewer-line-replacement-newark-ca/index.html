    Emergency Sewer Replacement in Newark, CA

    Sewage coming up a floor drain is an emergency. The first visit gets a camera in the line so we know if it has caved in or only needs clearing to buy time.     Spot Sewer Repairs in Newark, CA

    Sometimes one joint is the whole problem. With only one bad section, we excavate a single small pit and splice in new PVC using shielded couplings. Far cheaper than a full run. You see the footage that supports the call.

    Cured-in-Place Sewer Lining in Newark

    Lining builds a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line first, often with a cutter and a jetter, then pull a resin-saturated felt tube into place and inflate it so it cures hard against the host pipe. It needs a pipe that still holds its shape. It is a strong choice under slabs and finished rooms. We say plainly whether your line qualifies.

    After-Hours Sewer Collapse Response in Newark

    Raw sewage on a basement floor is a health problem. At night the tech clears what can be cleared, runs the camera and tells you straight whether to keep using water. Cleanup comes after the pipe is fixed. If drywall or carpet got wet, we can refer a restoration company.

    How Pipe Bursting Works in Newark

    A winch pulls the bursting head. The head is slightly bigger than the old pipe, so it splits clay or cast iron outward into the soil while fused HDPE trails behind it in one continuous piece. Only two pits get dug. With no joints in the new pipe, roots have no way back in.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Replacement in Newark, CA

    Orangeburg is tar-soaked wood fiber. It was installed in a lot of homes in the decades after the war, and over time it softens, blisters and squashes into an oval that catches everything going by. Clay holds up better, except at the joints. When the camera shows trouble in three or more places, a full replacement is usually cheaper than chasing spot repairs.

    Pipe Bursting Under Driveways in Newark, CA

    Concrete is expensive to replace. With pipe bursting, the new line goes in under the driveway or tree from one pit to another, following the old pipe's route. The HDPE is butt-fused on site into one long piece. Most bursts finish in a day. You end up patching two small pits rather than a whole slab.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Newark

    A collapsed run or a long belly usually means we dig. The crew trenches the full length of the lateral, removes the failed pipe and sets new PVC on compacted bedding with steady fall. Sod gets cut and rolled aside first when the lawn allows it. The inspector sees open pipe. We compact the backfill in layers, which keeps a dip from forming along the trench.
