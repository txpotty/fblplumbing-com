    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Newark, CA

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a secure seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can be repaired from kinks with a heat gun. It is our preferred choice for many full repipes.

    PEX for Water Line Replacement in Newark

    When your main water line from the street to your home needs replacement, PEX is an smart option. Its flexibility allows for easier trenchless or minimal-dig installations, reducing disruption to your yard in Newark. PEX main lines handle ground movement and resist corrosion better than rigid pipe. Many older homes here sit on raised foundations with crawlspaces, which make supply and drain repairs easier to reach than slab work.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Newark

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a good option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer dependable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to confirm a proper seal.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Newark, CA

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is known for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Plenty of insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to sell and insure.
