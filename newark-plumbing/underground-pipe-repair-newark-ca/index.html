    Frequently Asked Questions About Underground Pipe Repair in Newark

    Simple buried pipe repairs usually run $500 to $2,500. Sewer replacements cost more. Extensive trenchless sewer replacements can be $5,000 to $15,000 or more, with location, damage and method driving the total. The price comes after we look.

    A minor leak repair can take a few hours. Bigger jobs run a day or more. A full trenchless sewer replacement could take one to three days, with footage and inspection timing as the main variables. The timeline is in the estimate.

    For water lines we use PEX or Type K copper. Both are rated for burial. Sewer and drain work uses Schedule 40 PVC, and trenchless jobs use fused HDPE, all of which resist corrosion and handle ground movement better than the old materials they replace. Local code decides the final pick.

    Acoustic ground microphones pick up the sound of escaping water. Tracer gas helps too. Hydrogen-nitrogen gas pushed into the line rises through the soil at the leak, and electronic locators trace the exact pipe route so we know where to listen. Together they narrow it to a few feet.
