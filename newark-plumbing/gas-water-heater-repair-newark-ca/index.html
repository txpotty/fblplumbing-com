    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Newark

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it detects vapors or overheating. Lint and dust in garages can clog the arrestor and shut down the system. We clean the combustion chamber and replace sensors or thermal switches as required.

    Water Heater Tank Leaks and Pressure Issues in Newark, CA

    While gas water heaters are sturdy, tank leaks or pressure problems can occur. We can often fix small fitting leaks or replace relief valves if pressure is too high. If the tank itself is failing due to rust or old age, a replacement might be the more economical long-term solution. We give you an honest assessment of the repair options for your Newark, CA home.

    Propane Water Heater Repair in Newark

    Rural properties often run water heaters on propane rather than natural gas. Propane units use different orifices and regulator pressures, and incorrect conversions cause sooting and poor performance. We confirm the correct parts are installed and repair pilots, valves and burners. We also check tank regulators for proper pressure.

    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Newark, CA

    The gas control valve controls temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something fails. If the valve itself has failed, we replace it with an exact match or listed universal model. We leak test and confirm temperature control afterward.

    Venting and Draft Repair in Newark, CA

    Atmospheric gas water heaters rely on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home. Disconnected sections, rusted draft hoods and poor slope can let combustion gases spill back. We repair or replace vent sections and verify draft with a test. Proper venting is critical for your family's safety.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Repair in Newark

    The cost of gas water heater repair in Newark, CA can vary widely depending on the issue. Minor repairs like a thermocouple replacement might range from $150 to $350, while more complex issues such as a gas valve replacement could be $400 to $800 or more. We always provide a detailed, upfront estimate once we diagnose the problem.

    Replacing a part like a thermocouple or gas valve typically does not. Replacing the water heater or altering gas piping or venting often requires a permit, which we handle.

    Watch for a lack of hot water, inconsistent water temperature, a pilot light that won't stay lit, unusual noises like rumbling or popping, a rotten egg smell, which can point to a gas leak, or visible leaks around the tank. Any of these warrant a professional inspection.

    If you smell gas, immediately evacuate your home right away, do not turn any lights or appliances on or off, and call your gas company's emergency line and then FBL Plumbing from a safe location. This points to a potentially dangerous gas leak that requires immediate professional attention.

    Regular maintenance, such as annual flushing of the tank to remove sediment, inspecting the anode rod and checking the pressure relief valve, can greatly extend the life of your gas water heater and prevent common issues. Ask about a maintenance plan with FBL Plumbing.

    Most common gas water heater repairs can be completed within 1 to 3 hours. More complex issues, or cases where special parts need to be ordered, could extend the timeline. We aim for efficiency without compromising safety or quality.

    While some minor issues might seem DIY friendly, working with gas appliances carries serious safety risks, including gas leaks and carbon monoxide poisoning. It is always best to call a licensed professional for gas water heater repair to protect safety and proper function, and to stay within local codes.
