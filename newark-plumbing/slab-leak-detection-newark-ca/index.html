    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Newark, CA

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are harder to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know exactly where to listen.

    Water and Sewer Line Slab Leaks in Newark, CA

    Slab leaks can affect both freshwater supply lines and sewer drain pipes. Our detection services are set up to identify both types of leaks, which present different challenges. Sewer line leaks often involve foul odors and slow drainage, while water line leaks can appear as wet spots or high water bills in your Newark, CA home. We have separate methods for each.

    Video Pipe Inspection for Sewer Leaks in Newark, CA

    Common pipe materials prone to slab leaks include older copper pipes vulnerable to corrosion from hard water or soil acidity, and sometimes PEX or CPVC if they were improperly installed or exposed to extreme temperatures.     A water line slab leak involves pressurized fresh water escaping from supply pipes, often resulting in higher water bills, warm spots or the sound of running water. A sewer line slab leak involves wastewater leaking from drain pipes, which usually causes foul odors, slow drains or visible sewage seepage, and is not usually pressurized.

    Warm spots on the floor, the sound of running water with fixtures off, a moving water meter and an unexplained jump in your water bill are common signs. Cracked tile, damp carpet and mildew odors also point to a slab leak. Call us if you notice any of these.
