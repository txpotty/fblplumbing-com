    Kitchen and Floor Drain Cleaning in Newark

    Floor drains in commercial kitchens collect grease, rice, mop water and the odd utensil. They clog on a schedule you can almost set your watch by.     Emergency Commercial Plumbing in Newark

    A burst supply line above a drop ceiling can soak inventory and ceiling tile in under an hour. Shut the main first. Backups get handled the same way. We clear the main, camera it and tell you whether it will happen again.

    Commercial Plumbing Costs and Maintenance Plans in Newark, CA

    Backflow tests often run $75 to $175 per assembly, and kitchen line jetting usually costs $350 to $900 depending on length and buildup. After-hours calls cost more. Putting testing, jetting and flushing on a plan evens out your costs and cuts down on emergency calls, which cost the most. You get the plan in writing first.

    Backflow Prevention Assembly Service in Newark

    A backflow assembly keeps water from your building, including irrigation lines, boiler water and chemical feeds, from flowing back into the public supply. Most providers want a yearly test. We test RPZ, double check and PVB assemblies with calibrated gauges and send the results straight to the water provider. Failed devices usually need a rebuild kit, not a new valve. We carry Watts, Wilkins and Febco kits for that reason.

    Commercial Pipe Repair and Repiping in Newark, CA

    Older commercial buildings often have galvanized supply lines that are rusted nearly shut, or copper that has pinholed along hot water runs. Patching works for a while. Once leaks start showing up in different spots every few months, a sectional repipe in Type L copper or commercial-grade PEX usually costs less than the next three emergency calls. The first step is mapping the risers and branches. Then we phase the work by floor or by tenant so the building stays open.

    Commercial Leak Detection in Newark, CA

    Water bills that jump 30 percent with no change in business usually mean a hidden leak or a stuck fixture. Start with the meter. We shut fixtures off zone by zone and watch the meter, then use acoustic sensors, thermal cameras and tracer gas to find leaks in walls, ceilings and buried service lines. Running toilets count too. One stuck flush valve can waste several thousand gallons a week.

    After-Hours Commercial Plumbing in Newark

    Emergencies at a business cost more the longer they last, so speed matters more than the hourly rate. Water on the floor comes first. The first visit stops the water and gets fixtures working again, and bigger repairs get scheduled when they will not cost overtime or interrupt business. Burst lines, backups and no-hot-water calls get emergency response.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Newark

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage. We read each check valve and the relief valve on a calibrated gauge and record the numbers on the standard test form. Passing devices get tagged. We file the report with your water provider and keep a copy on our side for next year.
