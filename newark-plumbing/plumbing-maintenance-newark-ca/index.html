    Mineral-heavy water shortens water heater life.     Water Heater Maintenance in Newark

    Sediment is the tank's slow killer. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. The tank gets flushed, the anode rod gets checked, the relief valve gets tested and the connections get inspected. Plan on an hour. Less sediment means quicker heating and a longer tank life.

    Getting Ready for the Hard Months in Newark, CA

    Weak spots show up in bad weather. Even in mild areas, wrapping irrigation backflow devices before a frosty night is cheap insurance against a surprise leak.     Softener Upkeep in Newark, CA

    Softeners fail quietly. A softener that quit regenerating still sends water, just hard water, and you only notice when scale appears. Bypass and drain get tested. We measure hardness after.

    Water Softener Service in Newark

    A softener only helps if it regenerates. We check the salt level, break up any salt bridge in the brine tank, clean the brine well and injector, and run a manual regeneration to confirm the valve cycles correctly. Hardness gets tested after. A failed softener sends hard water through the house without warning.

    Repipe Assessments in Newark, CA
