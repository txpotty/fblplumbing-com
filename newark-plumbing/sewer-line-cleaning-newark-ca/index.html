    Lines with roots or past clogs often need cleaning every 12 to 24 months. Newer PVC lines without issues may go years without service.

    Hydro jetting is generally safe for most pipe materials, but it is crucial to have a professional assess your sewer line's condition first.     Usually, standard sewer line cleaning, like snaking or hydro jetting to clear a clog, does not require a permit in Newark.     For most homes in Newark, annual or twice-yearly sewer line cleaning is enough, especially if you have mature trees nearby or an older plumbing system. If you have frequent clogs or a high-usage commercial property, more frequent cleaning might be advisable to prevent major backups.

    In most cases, sewer line cleaning methods like snaking and hydro jetting are non-invasive and do not require digging. We access the line through existing cleanouts.
