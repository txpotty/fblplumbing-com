    Inlet Filter and Air Intake Cleaning in Newark

    Every tankless unit has an inlet water screen that catches grit and scale flakes. Those screens can clog with sand and debris, especially after municipal line work. We pull and clean the screen and inspect the air intake filter, which can load up with dust and dryer lint. Clean filters keep the flow sensor reading correctly and the burner breathing properly.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Flushing in Newark

    Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running mineral-heavy. Hard water can lead to heavy mineral buildup in tankless water heaters. Our residential flushing service in Newark involves circulating a descaling solution through your unit's heat exchanger to remove calcium and magnesium deposits.
