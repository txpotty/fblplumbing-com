    Control Valve and Timer Diagnostics in Newark, CA

    The control valve runs every stage of regeneration, from backwash through brine draw to the final refill. We advance it manually through each cycle and watch for a motor that stalls, a drive gear that skips, a piston that drags or a drain that never shuts off. A worn piston is a common find.

    Regeneration Programming and Hardness Testing in Newark, CA

    Plenty of softeners still run factory settings. Either the installer left the defaults in, or the family got bigger and the settings stayed put. We enter your raw hardness and household size, then set a metered valve to regenerate by gallons used instead of every few days on a timer.

    Resin Cleaning and Regeneration Tuning in Newark, CA

    Iron is the most common resin fouler we see. It coats the beads orange and blocks the exchange sites. We feed a resin cleaner through the brine well, run a regeneration and retest, and if the capacity comes back we shorten the time between regenerations so the iron does not build back up.

    Resin Bed Checks and Regeneration Settings in Newark

    Resin does the actual softening. The beads swap sodium for calcium and magnesium, and once they are loaded up they need brine to recharge. We compare the softened hardness against the gallons used since the last regeneration to tell whether the bed is still at full strength.

    Brine Tank Cleanout and Inspection in Newark, CA

    Brine tanks collect a gray sludge of salt fines and insoluble grit, and it builds up faster with cheap rock salt. The tank gets emptied, scrubbed and rinsed. Then we look for cracks near the overflow elbow and the brine well and refill with fresh salt.

    Yearly Water Softener Tune-Ups in Newark

    A tune-up starts with two hardness readings and ends with a full manual regeneration that we watch from start to finish. In between, we check the salt level, the brine well and the float. It takes about an hour on most homes.
