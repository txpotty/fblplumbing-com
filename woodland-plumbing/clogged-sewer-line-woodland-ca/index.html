    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Woodland, CA

    Understanding the difference between a main sewer line clog and a branch line clog is key for proper diagnosis and repair in Woodland, CA. A main line clog affects all drains in your home, often causing backups in lower-level fixtures. Branch line clogs are typically isolated to a single fixture or area. Our technicians are skilled at finding the exact location of the blockage, whether it is in the main line or a specific branch, for a targeted and efficient repair.

    Main Line Hydro Jetting in Woodland

    Hydro jetting uses water at thousands of psi through a specialized nozzle to cut roots, grease and scale. It cleans the full diameter of the pipe rather than just boring a hole through the clog. We check the pipe condition with a camera first so jetting is only used on lines that can handle it. Many homeowners find that jetting buys them years before another backup.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Woodland, CA

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful method for clearing severe sewer line clogs, especially those caused by grease, scale, or heavy sludge buildup. This process uses highly pressurized water jets to scour the inside of your pipes, not only clearing the blockage but also cleaning the pipe walls. It is an effective preventative maintenance tool and is especially effective for older pipes in Woodland that may have collected years of debris. This technique is safe for most pipe materials.

    Hydro-Jetting for Stubborn Clogs in Woodland

    FBL Plumbing uses powerful hydro-jetting technology to clear even the most stubborn sewer line blockages. This method uses high-pressure water streams to blast away grease, scale, mineral buildup, and tree roots, restoring your pipes to their original diameter. It's a highly effective, non-invasive way to clean your sewer lines thoroughly and prevent future clogs. Unlike snaking, hydro-jetting cleans the entire pipe wall.
