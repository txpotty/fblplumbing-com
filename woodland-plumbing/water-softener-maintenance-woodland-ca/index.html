    Control Valve and Injector Service in Woodland, CA

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a complete regeneration every time.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Woodland

    Softener resin can foul with iron, manganese or sediment and lose capacity over time. Chlorine and chloramine in city water also slowly break resin down. We run a resin cleaner through the system and test performance afterward. If the resin is exhausted, we rebed the tank with fresh high-capacity resin, which costs far less than a new system.

    Resin Bed Analysis and Regeneration in Woodland

    The resin bed is the heart of your water softener, where ion exchange occurs. During maintenance, we check the condition of the resin beads and confirm the regeneration cycles are programmed correctly for your household's water usage. Proper resin bed function is key to steady soft water throughout your home in Woodland.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Regeneration Optimization in Woodland, CA

    The resin beads are responsible for ion exchange, removing hard minerals from your water. Over time, these beads can become fouled with iron or other contaminants, reducing their effectiveness. Our maintenance includes cleaning the resin bed and adjusting the regeneration cycle settings for full softening capacity. We make sure your system is regenerating efficiently for your water quality, providing truly soft water.
