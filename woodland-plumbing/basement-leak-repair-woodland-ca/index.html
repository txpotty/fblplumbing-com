    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Leak Repair in Woodland

    Basement plumbing leak repairs in Woodland typically cost $200 to $1,500, depending on the pipe and how hard it is to reach. Pipe on an open ceiling is the cheap case. Pipe behind finished drywall or under the slab adds the cost of opening and patching, and a cast iron stack replacement can run higher than the range above if it goes through more than one floor.

    A pipe or fitting repair usually takes an hour or two. Injecting a crack is a half-day job. Sump pit installation takes most of a day, and an interior perimeter drain system takes two to five days depending on wall length and how much finished space has to be cleared first.

    Most plumbing leak repairs in a basement are done in one visit. With wall access, plan on two to four hours. Seepage repairs vary more, from half a day for an injected crack to most of a week for a full interior perimeter drain, which includes cutting and removing the slab edge, setting pipe and stone, and pouring new concrete.

    Small repairs usually do not need a permit. Bigger changes often do. Stack replacement and new backwater valves often need one, and we handle the permit and inspection.

    Musty odors and damp carpet are the early ones. Then come peeling paint and white mineral lines. A water bill that climbs or a sump that runs more than usual are worth a call too.

    Workmanship on pipe repairs is warranted for the term on your invoice. Pumps come with the manufacturer's warranty. Seepage repairs carry terms that match the method, and if water returns at the same spot during the warranty period we come back, retest it, and fix it without charging for the labor.

    Only some repairs need excavation. Outside membranes and new footing drains require digging to the footing.
