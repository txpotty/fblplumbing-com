    Supply Line Leak Repair in Woodland

    Water lines that run along basement ceilings and walls can leak at fittings, valves or pinholes. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. Water chemistry affects how fast copper corrodes, and older galvanized lines often rust from the inside. We locate the leak, replace the damaged section with copper or PEX and pressure test the repair. If the line shows widespread wear, we'll tell you whether a partial repipe makes more sense.
