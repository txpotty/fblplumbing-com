    Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Woodland

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. You may see it at the water heater, under sinks or near the main shutoff. We inspect visible lines, check the stamps and trace runs through the attic. You get a clear answer and a written estimate for replacement.

    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Woodland

    When a home with polybutylene pipes sits on a slab foundation, repiping becomes a more complex process. Our team specializes in full home repiping using PEX (cross-linked polyethylene), a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant material. PEX is an strong choice for Northern California homes, as it resists freezing better than rigid pipes and expands to handle temperature changes. We carefully plan the repipe to minimize disruption, often running new lines through attics or existing wall cavities, ensuring a complete and long-lasting upgrade for your entire plumbing system.

    Copper Replacement Option in Woodland
