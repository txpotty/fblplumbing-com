    Copper Replacement for Polybutylene in Woodland, CA

    Some homeowners in Woodland, CA want copper when they take out polybutylene. That works fine. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes, and join it with lead-free solder or press fittings. Copper costs more and takes longer, since each turn needs a fitting, but it tolerates heat and sunlight well and has a long track record.

    Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Woodland

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. Start at the water heater. You may also see it under sinks, near the main shutoff or coming through the garage wall, and we trace runs through the attic to confirm how much of the house it serves.

    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Woodland

    Most poly replacements we do use PEX-A, a flexible tubing that runs long distances with few fittings. On a slab home, we route the new lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, which keeps every supply line out of the concrete. It also tolerates freezing better. Expansion fittings keep the bore full size, which helps pressure at the last fixture on the line.

    Copper Replacement Option in Woodland

    Type L copper appeals to some homeowners for its long history and rigid feel. Joints are soldered or pressed. Copper costs more than PEX and takes a little longer to install, so we help you weigh the cost, your water conditions and how long you plan to stay in the house.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Woodland

    Copper is a solid alternative to PEX for poly replacement. Copper is stiff, heat tolerant and long proven. It costs more. We review your water and any history of copper pinholes nearby before recommending it over PEX.
