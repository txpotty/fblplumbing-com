    Older homes in Woodland, CA often have soft copper or galvanized steel under the slab, and both corrode with age.     Emergency Slab Leak Detection in Woodland

    Supply and Drain Leaks Under the Slab in Woodland, CA

    A supply leak and a drain leak show different signs. Supply leaks are pressurized, so they move the meter, warm the floor on the hot side and run whether or not anyone uses water. Drain leaks only leak when water goes down, and they show up as odors, slow drains or damp floors near bathrooms. Each needs its own test in your Woodland, CA home.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Woodland, CA

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. They are harder to see. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, so we isolate the cold side, map the pipe with a line tracer and listen along the route with a ground microphone.

    Camera Inspection for Under-Slab Drains in Woodland, CA

    Under-slab drains crack as well. A broken cast iron or PVC drain will not move the water meter, but it can cause odors, soggy floors and slow drains, which is why we push a camera through to look for cracks, bellies and pulled joints. We show you the video before discussing any repair in your Woodland home.
