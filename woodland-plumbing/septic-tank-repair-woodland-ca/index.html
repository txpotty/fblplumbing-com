    Septic Tank Leak Repair in Woodland

    A tank that never fills to the outlet, or one that fills slowly with groundwater after rain, has a leak.     Septic Tee Repair in Woodland

    Sanitary tees on the inlet and outlet can crack or get knocked off by the hose on a pump truck. Without the outlet tee, the floating scum layer drifts into the laterals and plugs the gravel. We glue in a new Schedule 40 tee. An effluent filter goes on if the outlet has room.

    House-to-Tank Line Repair in Woodland

    Gurgling drains right after a pump-out point to the line between the house and the tank, not the tank itself. Roots and settling ground cause most sags and breaks in that run.     Repair Versus Replace in Woodland, CA
