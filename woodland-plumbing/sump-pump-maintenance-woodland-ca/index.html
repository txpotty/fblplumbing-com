    Home Sump Pump Maintenance in Woodland

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Woodland

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. A backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or quits. We load test the battery, check the charger and the alarm, and run the backup by itself with the main pump unplugged. Expect a new battery every three to five years.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Woodland, CA

    Backup pumps sit in the pit next to the main pump and run off a 12-volt battery when the power drops or the primary fails. Our plumber load tests the battery instead of just reading its voltage. Next, we cut power to the main pump and watch the backup clear the basin by itself. Alarms get tested too.

    Float Switch Inspection in Woodland, CA

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. Our plumber watches the float rise and drop through a complete cycle. Then we set the on and off points so the pump runs longer, less often.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Woodland

    Strong winter storms can bring days of heavy rain to Northern California, saturating ground and pushing water into crawlspaces and sewer lines. A few weeks ahead of the wet season, we run a hose into the pit until the float trips and the pump starts. We time the drawdown and listen for bearing noise. That leaves time to replace a weak pump before the first big storm, not during it.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Woodland

    The check valve sits on the discharge pipe a foot or so above the pump and keeps the water in the line from falling back into the pit. A failed valve sends that water back down for the pump to move again. Motors wear out faster. We test the valve, look for a weep hole between the pump and the valve, and check every joint up to the wall.

    Pit Cleaning and Liner Checks in Woodland

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, leaves and gravel settle at the bottom of the basin. That debris gets pulled into the intake and jams the impeller. We pump the basin dry, rinse the screen and check the plastic liner for cracks, since a cracked liner lets soil wash in from the sides. Takes about twenty minutes.

    Emergency Sump Pump Service in Woodland

    A pump that runs nonstop, hums without moving water or will not start at all needs a plumber the same day, before the pit overflows. We send a plumber with floats, check valves and spare pumps on the truck. A jammed impeller usually clears in a few minutes. A burned motor means we swap in a new pump on the spot and leave the pit protected.
