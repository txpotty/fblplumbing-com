    Tank and Tankless Water Heater Repair in Woodland

    Tank and tankless heaters fail in different ways. FBL Plumbing repairs both, including the major brands, gas and electric. On a tank we check the thermocouple, gas valve, elements and anode rod. Tankless trouble is usually scale in the heat exchanger or a dirty inlet screen, and a descaling flush fixes more of them than new parts do.

    Slow Drain Repair in Woodland

    Gurgling toilets and a tub that drains slow often trace to a plugged roof vent or a sagging drain line. We clear the line, camera it and repair the damaged section. Common fixes include replacing corroded cast iron, cutting out failed ABS joints, rebuilding a broken trap arm and resetting toilets on a new wax ring and flange. The goal is a drain you stop thinking about.

    Well Pump Repair in Woodland, CA

    No water at the tap on a well system points to a short list of parts. It is usually the pressure switch, the capacitor in the control box, the pump motor or a waterlogged pressure tank. We check voltage and amp draw at the control box before pulling anything out of the well. Pulling a submersible pump is a two-person job.

    Drain Repairs in Woodland

    Slow drains that come back right after a snake usually mean the pipe itself is damaged. Cracked cast iron or a sagging run will hold debris every time. We run a camera to find the spot, then replace that section or hydro-jet the line if buildup is the only problem. Grease is the usual buildup.

    Sewer Line Repair in Woodland, CA

    Roots are the usual cause of a sewer line that backs up on a regular schedule. They slip into a cracked joint, then grow into a mat that snags paper and grease. We camera the line to see the damage and mark the depth on the surface, then choose between an open dig and a trenchless method. Clay pipe usually goes first.

    Water Softener Repair in Woodland, CA

    Rural properties around Woodland often rely on private wells, which may need treatment for iron, sediment, or other minerals. White crust on faucets and spotty glasses are the first signs a softener has stopped working. Most of the time it is a salt bridge or a plugged injector. FBL Plumbing repairs the common brands and tests the water hardness before and after the fix so you can see the number drop. Resin lasts ten to fifteen years.

    Drain and Pipe Repair in Woodland

    Hydro-jetting at around 4,000 psi clears grease and scale that a cable snake only pokes a small hole through, which is why the clog keeps coming back. A broken pipe needs a repair, not more jetting.
