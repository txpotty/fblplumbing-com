    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Woodland, CA

    Polybutylene pipes, installed in many homes between the 1970s and 1990s, are known for premature failure due to chemical reactions with disinfectants in the water supply. If your home in Woodland, CA has polybutylene, a full replacement is essential to prevent major water damage. FBL Plumbing offers specialized polybutylene plumbing replacement, carefully removing the old, brittle pipes and installing a dependable new system.

    Copper Repiping in Woodland, CA

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes. Joints are soldered with lead-free solder or pressed with ProPress fittings. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a bit longer than PEX.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Woodland

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab. This is a good option for newer homes or a first leak. If the home has had multiple leaks, we will explain why a full repipe may save you money.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Woodland

    Kitec, with its orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe and brass fittings, and gray polybutylene pipe are both known for failures. Many homes built in the 1980s through early 2000s contain one or the other. We replace these systems completely so your home is easier to insure and sell later. Visible pipe at the water heater often tells us which type you have.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Woodland, CA

    Many older homes in Woodland, CA still have galvanized steel pipes, which are vulnerable to corrosion, rust, and mineral buildup. This leads to low water pressure, discolored water, and eventual leaks. Our team specializes in the complete removal and replacement of these outdated systems with modern PEX or copper piping. This upgrade not only resolves existing problems but also greatly improves water quality and flow, boosting your home's value and plumbing reliability for decades to come.

    Condo, Townhome and Multi-Family Repipes in Woodland

    Shared walls and HOA rules make repiping attached homes more complex. We coordinate with HOAs, schedule water shutoffs and protect neighboring units. For property managers we offer building-wide repipes done in phases to keep residents at home. Each unit gets clear communication and daily cleanup.

    Drain Line Repiping in Woodland

    Frequently Asked Questions About Repipe Specialists in Woodland

    The cost of a whole-home repipe in Woodland, CA can vary significantly depending on the size of your home, the number of fixtures, and the chosen piping material, such as copper or PEX. Generally, you can expect to pay anywhere from $4,000 to $15,000 or more. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after a thorough assessment of your specific home and needs.

    FBL Plumbing stands behind our repiping work with a comprehensive warranty on both labor and materials. Specific warranty details will be provided with your estimate, but generally, you can expect a multi-year warranty covering defects and leaks. We use only high-quality, warrantied piping materials from trusted brands.

    PEX resists scale and pinhole corrosion and handles expansion well, which makes it a solid choice for hard water. Copper is durable too but costs more and can pinhole in corrosive water.

    We provide a written workmanship warranty, and PEX manufacturers such as Uponor offer long-term warranties when certified installers use their system. We explain the details before work begins.

    We commonly use Uponor PEX-A with ProPEX fittings and Type L copper from domestic mills. We also use Viega ProPress fittings for copper work.

    Repiping is a complex plumbing project that requires specialized tools, knowledge of plumbing codes, and significant experience.
