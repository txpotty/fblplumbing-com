    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Woodland, CA

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Expansion wears them out. The pipe swells and shrinks against rock every time hot water flows, and a recirculation pump keeps that going constantly, thinning the wall. We repair or reroute the line and can set the recirculation pump on a timer.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Woodland

    Cold leaks don't make warm spots. The bill gives them away. They show up as a higher bill, a meter that turns with every faucet off, or a patch of carpet that stays damp, and they soak the soil just as much as hot leaks do. Your Woodland home is no exception. We isolate the cold line and fix it or run a new one.

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Woodland, CA

    Direct repair is often the most practical fix for a single leak. We cut a small square. Once the pipe is exposed, we replace the bad section, test the line and backfill in compacted lifts before patching. The concrete is patched flush with the floor.

    Emergency Slab Leak Repair in Woodland, CA

    A large slab leak can push water up through flooring or into walls quickly. Find your main valve. We help you close the main by phone, and a tech then isolates the leaking line so the rest of the house gets water back.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Woodland

    Old galvanized or thin copper is hard to patch reliably. Rerouting skips it. We abandon the leaking run in place and install new pipe through walls or the attic, so the problem section is gone from service and the water travels through fresh line that you can reach later. Woodland homes with original plumbing see this often.

    After-Hours Slab Leak Calls in Woodland

    A main line splitting under the slab can lift tile and soak drywall fast. Don't wait until morning. The crew caps the broken line, restores water elsewhere and begins repairs right away, keeping soil and flooring damage down. We cover all of Woodland.
