    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Woodland, CA

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. The jet is the bigger bill. Because jetting removes the buildup that causes repeat clogs, many homeowners spend less over a few years than they would on repeated snaking visits for the same line.

    Main Line Jetting in Woodland, CA

    The lateral from your house to the street carries everything, so buildup there causes backups everywhere at once. The whole lateral gets jetted to the tap. Low spots where the pipe has settled hold water and collect sludge, so we spend extra passes there and mark the location on the camera footage in case a repair makes sense later.

    After-Hours Hydrojet Service in Woodland

    Backups at night or on weekends are handled with the same gear as any weekday call. On-call trucks are fully equipped. We can clear and jet a line in one visit instead of returning Monday.

    Commercial and Restaurant Hydrojetting in Woodland

    Hotels, apartment complexes and shopping centers need drains that keep up with heavy use every day. Downtime costs them money. We set up jetting plans for each building's needs, schedule work during slow hours, and keep the camera footage on file so property managers can show tenants or owners the condition of the lines.

    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Woodland

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. Cabling only cuts a narrow channel. Jetting with a spinning nozzle strips the grease from the full circumference so it flushes downstream instead of re-forming a plug at the next fitting.

    Residential Hydrojetting in Woodland

    Most home jetting calls involve a greasy kitchen line or a root-filled sewer. We set up outside at the cleanout whenever we can, so the jetter hose never has to come through your house or across your floors. Some kitchen lines get jetted from under the sink. We lay down floor protection first.

    Storm and Yard Drain Jetting in Woodland, CA

    Yard drains, channel drains and downspout lines fill with sand, silt and leaves between storms. Tree roots seek out sewer laterals during dry spells, which is why heavy rains often reveal damaged lines through sudden backups. A clogged yard drain sends that water back toward the house, so we jet the lines from the outlet end and flush the catch basins before the wet season starts. Pop-up emitters get checked too.
