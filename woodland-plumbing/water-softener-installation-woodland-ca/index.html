    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Woodland

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Soft water reaches every fixture.     Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Woodland

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. It complements a softener. We can handle filter changes.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Woodland

    Testing is what makes the settings right. We test for hardness, iron and chlorine before installing, then program the regeneration frequency and salt dose so you get soft water without wasting salt. Iron adds to the setting. We review the settings with you before we leave.

    Pelican Water Softener Installation in Woodland, CA

    Pelican sells both salt-based softeners and salt-free conditioners, often direct to homeowners. When a homeowner has already bought one, we handle the plumbing, the bypass and the startup flush the manual requires. Skipping the flush causes trouble. We follow it step by step.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Woodland, CA

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. FBL Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. Most effective option.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Woodland

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. Every salt-based install includes a sized resin tank, a brine tank, a bypass and a drain to an air gap. Then we program it. Regeneration time is usually set for 2 a.m.
