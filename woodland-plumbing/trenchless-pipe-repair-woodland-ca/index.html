    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Woodland

    CIPP lining involves cleaning the old pipe, then inserting a flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin. The liner is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light. Once hardened, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works best for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Woodland, CA

    Main water lines can also be replaced with trenchless methods. We can pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through the old path using bursting or a directional boring tool. This avoids digging across lawns, driveways and walkways. The new line is pressure tested before service is restored.

    Commercial Trenchless Pipe Repair in Woodland

    Commercial properties often have sewer and storm lines under parking lots, sidewalks and buildings. Trenchless repair avoids costly concrete demolition and disruption to tenants and customers. We line larger diameter pipes and multiple laterals. We schedule work to minimize impact on business operations.

    Pipe Bursting in Woodland, CA

    Pipe bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. A bursting head is pulled through the old pipe, fracturing it outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind. The new pipe is fused into one continuous length with no joints. Bursting needs an entry and exit pit but avoids digging the full length.

    Sectional Point Repair in Woodland

    When damage is confined to a specific section of pipe, sectional point repair is an ideal trenchless solution. We target only the damaged area, installing a patch or liner to seal the leak or reinforce the pipe. This method is cost-effective and minimizes the scope of work, which suits isolated issues in Woodland without affecting the entire line.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Systems in Woodland

    Lateral and Spot Lining in Woodland

    Not every pipe needs to be lined end to end. When damage is limited to a few joints or a short section, we can install a short sectional liner. This costs less than a full-length liner and is completed quickly. We confirm the rest of the line is sound before recommending a spot liner.
