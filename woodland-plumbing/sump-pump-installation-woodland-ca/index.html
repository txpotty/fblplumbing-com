    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Woodland, CA

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. The charger keeps the battery topped off. Batteries need replacing every few years. They matter most below finished rooms.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Woodland

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. For those jobs we install duplex systems where two pumps alternate, with a control panel and a high water alarm that can tie into the building monitoring. Downtime costs money. Work is scheduled around your operating hours.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Woodland

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi monitors go further and send a phone alert when the pump runs too long, the power drops or the water climbs past a set level. Landlords like them. We add them to new installs and to pumps already in place.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Woodland, CA

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. The backup pump gets its own float and its own check valve, and it runs off a sealed 12-volt battery when the outlet goes dead. Most run five to seven hours on a full charge. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Woodland, CA

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. You can reach the motor and the switch without fishing anything out of the pit, which helps in a basement where the pit is shallow or tight. They are louder than submersibles. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Woodland, CA

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary handles daily water. People with carpet downstairs buy these. We install them in homes where a flood would ruin flooring and drywall.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Woodland

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. Sizing comes from two measurements we take on site, the pit diameter and the vertical lift to the discharge. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Woodland, CA

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. When we replace a pump we also check the basin for silt, look at the check valve flapper and run water through the discharge line to find any clogs. Undersized pumps get upsized. Testing is done with a full pit.
