    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Pittsburg, CA

    Every fabricator handles undermount clips a bit differently. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. Either way, the bowl gets braced. We do not connect the trap until the adhesive has cured.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Pittsburg

    Undermount sinks rely on clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone and brace it while the adhesive sets, which usually takes overnight before the sink can hold a full load of water. Then come the strainers and trap.

    Farmhouse Sink Installation in Pittsburg, CA

    Apron-front sinks rest on a support frame built into the cabinet, not on the countertop.     Undermount Sink Installation in Pittsburg, CA

    Undermounts hang from clips anchored into the underside of the stone, plus a bead of silicone along the rim. We support the bowl from below with a brace while the silicone cures, usually overnight, before loading it with water. Skipping the cure time leads to sagging. Just an eighth of an inch of sag leaves a gap that catches water and food along the edge.

    Granite and Quartz Composite Sinks in Pittsburg

    Composite sinks are heavier than stainless and usually thicker at the drain, so standard strainers sometimes need a longer threaded body. We check the strainer fit first. When the strainer is too short for the sink thickness, we replace it instead of cranking it down and chipping the drain opening.
