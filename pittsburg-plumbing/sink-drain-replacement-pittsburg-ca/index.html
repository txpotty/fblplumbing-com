    Hard-Water Drain Damage Repair in Pittsburg, CA

    Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. Hard water builds scale inside pop-up bodies and on stopper rods, and over time this freezes the mechanism or snaps the pivot rod. We remove scaled parts and install new drains with smoother materials that resist buildup. We can also suggest softening options to protect the new parts.

    Utility Sink Drain Replacement in Pittsburg

    Corroded Drain Replacement in Pittsburg, CA

    Even good-quality water can contribute to corrosion in older metal drain pipes, especially under sinks where cleaning chemicals are often used.     Push-Button and Click-Clack Drain Installation in Pittsburg, CA

    Many homeowners switch to push-button drains to get rid of the lift rod entirely. These drains open and close with a press on the stopper. We confirm your sink has an overflow or choose a non-overflow version, then seal and test it. They are easy to clean and work well with newer faucets.

    Trap Arm and Wall Drain Repair in Pittsburg, CA

    When a leak comes from where the drain enters the wall, the trap arm or the stub may be cracked or corroded. Older homes sometimes have galvanized drain stubs that rust from the inside.     Pop-Up Drain Replacement in Pittsburg

    The lift-rod pop-up is the most common bathroom drain and the one that wears out most. We remove the old body, clean the flange seat, and install a new assembly with a fresh pivot ball and gasket. The lift rod is adjusted so the stopper seals and opens fully. This also fixes leaks from the pivot nut on the back of the drain.

    Replacing a single gasket or slip nut costs less up front, but on an older drain the next part often fails within months.     P-Trap Replacement in Pittsburg

Each trap is sized correctly and aligned so slip joints seal without strain. A new trap is one of the most cost-effective fixes under a sink.
