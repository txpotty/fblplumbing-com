    What Goes Wrong on a Disposal, and How We Fix It in Pittsburg, CA

    Preventive Care and Longevity in Pittsburg, CA

    Keep cold water running before, during and after use, and let it go about 15 seconds after the motor stops so the food clears the trap. Grease poured in with hot water coats the chamber and the pipe, while cold water keeps it solid so the impellers can break it up. Stringy peels cause most jams. Throw out celery, corn husks and artichoke leaves.

    Humming Disposal Repair in Pittsburg

    Humming with no spin usually means something is stuck. Bones, pits, glass and spoons are the usual suspects. With the power off, we free the flywheel using the hex socket underneath, pull the object, and test the motor and reset with water running.

    Noisy Disposal Repair in Pittsburg

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough usually mean trouble inside the unit. A loose mount will rattle. We check the mount first, then the chamber for debris, then listen to the motor with the chamber empty, because a motor that whines with nothing in it has bad bearings.

    InSinkErator and Other Brand Repairs in Pittsburg, CA

    InSinkErator, Waste King and Moen each use different mounts, so a flange leak is fixed with that brand's kit. InSinkErator uses a three-bolt or Quick Lock style ring, while Waste King uses an EZ Mount twist ring that comes off without tools. We stock the common kits. It means most repairs need only one trip.

    Emergency Disposal and Sink Backup Repair in Pittsburg

    A failed disposal can push water back into the sink and dishwasher. With water rising or on the floor, turn off the disposal and dishwasher and call, because running them only adds water to the cabinet. We move quickly on these.

    Commercial Disposal Repair in Pittsburg

    Restaurant disposals run 1 to 5 horsepower and often have a separate control panel and solenoid water valve. When one stops, the dish line backs up fast. We check the controls first. A failed solenoid or a tripped overload in the control panel stops the unit just as completely as a jam, and it is a much faster fix.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Pittsburg

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A constant high whine means something else. A steady whine usually points to failing motor bearings, and the noise keeps building until the motor locks up. Odors usually trace to the splash guard. The guard comes out, the chamber gets cleaned and a torn guard gets replaced.

    Disposal Reset and Electrical Repair in Pittsburg

    When a disposal will not turn on at all, the thermal overload may have tripped or the switch may be bad. The reset, breaker, wall switch and wiring all get tested, along with air switches and the magnetic lids on batch-feed units. Most are simple fixes.
