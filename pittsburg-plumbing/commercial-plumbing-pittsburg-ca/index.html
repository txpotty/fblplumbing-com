    Commercial Sewer Line Repair in Pittsburg, CA

    The building sewer on a commercial property carries everything from every tenant, so a root intrusion or a belly in the line affects the whole property. First we run a camera and locate the damage depth, then we compare a spot dig, a cured-in-place liner and pipe bursting. Trenchless saves parking lots. Lining a 6-inch lateral can often be done in a day without tearing up asphalt or closing an entrance.

    Grease Interceptor and Line Service in Pittsburg, CA

    Grease does not stay in the trap if the trap is full or the baffles are broken. It ends up in your line. We inspect interceptors, replace failed baffles and outlet tees and jet the kitchen lines that feed them, which removes hardened grease that a cable just slides past. A jetted line holds up much better on a busy night.

    Commercial Pipe Repair and Repiping in Pittsburg, CA

    Older commercial buildings often have galvanized supply lines that are rusted nearly shut, or copper that has pinholed along hot water runs. Patching works for a while. Once leaks start showing up in different spots every few months, a sectional repipe in Type L copper or commercial-grade PEX usually costs less than the next three emergency calls. We map the system first. Work gets phased by floor or suite so tenants keep their water most of the day.

    Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Pittsburg

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. We use high-pressure jetting with rotating nozzles that scrub grease off the whole circumference of 4-inch and 6-inch lines, not just the bottom. Jetting keeps the line open for months instead of days. Restaurants, schools and apartment stacks are where we see the biggest difference.

    Commercial Restroom Plumbing in Pittsburg

    A broken flushometer in a busy restroom gets noticed by every customer who walks in. Sloan and Zurn flush valves, sensor faucets, wall-hung toilets and carriers are routine work for us. ADA work is part of it too. For ADA remodels we set fixture heights, add grab bar blocking in the wall and insulate the drain and supply lines under sinks.

    Commercial Water Heater Service in Pittsburg, CA

    Big tank heaters fail in a few common ways: a failed gas valve, a cracked flue baffle, a stuck T and P valve or a tank full of scale. Each sounds different. We check draft and combustion, test water temperature at the far end of the building and flush the tank, then tell you whether repair or replacement makes more sense. Tankless units get a descaling flush. Recirculation pumps on long runs get tested as part of the visit.

    Commercial Restroom Fixture Repair in Pittsburg, CA

    Restroom calls are the most common commercial work we do. Running flush valves, stuck urinals and dripping sensor faucets usually need a diaphragm, a solenoid or fresh batteries. We stock those. Clogged wall-hung toilets sometimes need the carrier checked, because a loose bowl on a carrier can leak behind the wall. Low pressure on one fixture usually points to a clogged supply stop.

    Backflow Prevention Assembly Service in Pittsburg

    A backflow assembly keeps water from your building, including irrigation lines, boiler water and chemical feeds, from flowing back into the public supply. Most providers want a yearly test. We test RPZ, double check and PVB assemblies with calibrated gauges and send the results straight to the water provider. Failed devices usually need a rebuild kit, not a new valve. That is why the truck carries Watts, Wilkins and Febco kits.
