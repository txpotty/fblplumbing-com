    Standard Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Pittsburg

    Our standard gas tank install starts with removing the old heater. The old tank gets drained and removed, then the new one goes in with new connectors, a drip leg and a sloped vent. Testing comes last. Value and reliability are why people choose them.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in Pittsburg, CA

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Because the exhaust comes out cool and wet, they vent through PVC and need a condensate drain, which changes where the heater can go and what the install involves. Energy Star models qualify for some rebates. We install Rheem, Bradford White and AO Smith models.

    Tankless Gas Water Heater Conversion in Pittsburg

    Converting from a tank to tankless is usually more than a swap. A conversion often means a larger gas line, new venting, a condensate drain and service valves. It takes most of a day. Our licensed plumbers handle all of it to code.

    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Pittsburg

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. That sealed setup is a good fit for tight utility rooms and interior closets, since the heater never draws air from the living space. Most run without electricity. The vent goes through a wall with proper clearances.

    Traditional Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Pittsburg

    Tank gas heaters are still the workhorse in most garages and utility rooms, usually 40 or 50 gallons. We connect a new gas flex line with a sediment trap, set the vent connector with a quarter inch per foot of rise and pipe the relief valve to an approved spot. Strapping where required. Your household sets the size.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Installation in Pittsburg, CA

    Rheem gas heaters show up in a lot of local homes, from basic atmospheric tanks to power vent models. FBL Plumbing handles every Rheem gas model. Our install covers gas, flue and the Guardian safety system, including the air intake that keeps it working. Draft is checked last.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Pittsburg, CA

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through PVC or other approved pipe. Without needing a chimney, the vent can exit through a side wall within the allowed length. They need an outlet. Termination clearances follow the manual.
