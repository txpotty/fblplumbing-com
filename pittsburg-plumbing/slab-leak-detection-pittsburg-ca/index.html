    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Pittsburg

    Many newer homes sit on post-tension slabs. These slabs have steel tendons stretched under tens of thousands of pounds of force, and cutting one by mistake is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We scan for tendons with ground-penetrating radar or a rebar locator before opening anything, or recommend a reroute that never touches the concrete.

    Emergency Slab Leak Detection in Pittsburg

    Water bubbling up through a tile joint or a sudden pressure drop tells you the leak got bigger. Shut the main. Our techs in Pittsburg respond quickly, isolate the leaking side so you can get water back on the rest of the house overnight, then return to locate and repair it properly.

    Pressure Testing and Isolation in Pittsburg

    We always start with isolation. Our tech closes the main, caps or valves off the hot and cold sides, and puts a gauge on each, then watches which side loses pressure over a set time. That tells us where to look. It also rules out a toilet or faucet leak masquerading as a slab leak in your Pittsburg, CA home.

    Thermal Scans on Large Floors in Pittsburg, CA

    Big open floor plans hide leaks well. A thermal camera scans an entire living area in a few minutes, showing temperature differences of a degree or less across tile or concrete, so we can rule out half the house before setting up the microphone. It saves an hour on large homes across Pittsburg, CA.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Pittsburg, CA

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. They are harder to see. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, which is why we isolate the cold line, trace its route and listen along it with acoustic gear.

    Direct Access Slab Leak Repair in Pittsburg, CA

    One leak on healthy pipe is a good spot-repair candidate. We cut a small opening at the marked spot, dig down to the pipe, replace the bad section and hold a pressure test. Then we backfill and patch the concrete level, ready for flooring. A precise mark keeps the opening tight.

    Tracer Gas Leak Detection in Pittsburg

    PEX and CPVC do not carry sound well. For those, we drain the line and push in a safe hydrogen and nitrogen tracer gas, which rises through the slab and gets picked up by a sniffer at the floor surface, often through grout joints and hairline cracks. It finds leaks acoustic gear misses in Pittsburg homes.
