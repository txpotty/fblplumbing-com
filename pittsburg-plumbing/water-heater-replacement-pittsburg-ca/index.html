    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Pittsburg, CA

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Pittsburg, CA homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes.     Conventional Tank Water Heater Replacement in Pittsburg

    Standard tank heaters are still in most homes, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment. A leak at the base, loud popping or rusty hot water means it is time, and a like-for-like or resized swap usually takes two to four hours. You shower tonight.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Pittsburg, CA

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters move heat from the surrounding air into the tank, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need space and airflow, often a room of about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they work best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. The space runs cooler.

    Commercial Water Heater Replacement in Pittsburg

    Businesses in Pittsburg put far more hours on a heater than a house, so commercial replacements need more capacity and faster recovery. Restaurants, salons, laundromats and offices each have a different demand curve, and we size the new unit by peak gallons per hour and the temperature health code requires, which can mean a larger tank, a booster or a rack of tankless units. We schedule around your hours.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Pittsburg

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is common, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually has to be upsized, and the old metal vent gets replaced with stainless or PVC run to the maker's specs. We price both options.

    Copper became standard in many mid-century homes here, and decades later pinhole leaks and worn fittings are a common repair call. Attic water heaters take more work to replace than ones in a garage or closet.
