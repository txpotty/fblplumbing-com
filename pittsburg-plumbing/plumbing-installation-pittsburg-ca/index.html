    Natural Gas and Propane Lines in Pittsburg, CA

    Natural gas and propane run at different pressures, and the pipe sizing changes with them. We size every run from the appliance BTU ratings and total pipe length, and use black iron or CSST depending on the path. CSST must be bonded.
