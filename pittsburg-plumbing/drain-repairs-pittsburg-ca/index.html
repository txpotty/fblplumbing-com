    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Pittsburg

    Simple trap or slip-joint repairs usually cost $150 to $350. Replacing a section of drain pipe usually runs $400 to $1,200, and cast iron stack replacement can range from $2,000 to $6,000 depending on access. We provide a written price before we start.

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water to clear out stubborn blockages, grease buildup, mineral deposits and even tree roots from your pipes. It is commonly used for severe clogs or as a preventive measure to thoroughly clean drain lines, leaving them almost like new.

    The duration of drain repairs depends entirely on the issue. A simple clog can often be cleared in an hour or two. More involved repairs, like replacing a section of a main sewer line, might take a full day or even several days, especially if permits or major digging are involved.

    Repeat clogs usually come from grease buildup, hard water scale, poor pipe slope, a missing vent or a damaged pipe that catches debris. A camera inspection shows which cause you are dealing with so it can be fixed permanently.

    Harsh chemical cleaners can soften older ABS glue joints and corrode metal drain parts with repeated use. They also seldom clear grease or scale fully. A mechanical auger or hydro-jet is safer and more effective.

    Most trap and fixture drain repairs take one to two hours. Replacing drain pipe inside a wall or under a floor can take half a day to a full day. We give you a time estimate when we price the job.

    Minor fixture drain repairs typically do not require a permit. Replacing drain stacks, relocating drains or adding new lines often does, depending on the city. We handle permits when they apply.

    Tightening a loose slip nut or replacing a plastic trap is a reasonable DIY job. If the leak is from a cracked tailpiece, a corroded metal drain or inside a wall, a plumber can make a lasting repair and check for hidden damage.
