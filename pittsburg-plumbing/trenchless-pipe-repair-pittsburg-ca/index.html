    CIPP Lining for Clay and Cast Iron in Pittsburg

    Older Pittsburg homes often have clay or cast iron sewer pipe. Both fail with age. Roots get into clay joints and cast iron rusts from inside, and a liner seals over both with jointless pipe that should outlast the original. Only one or two access points are needed.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Pittsburg

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Pittsburg yard.

    Pipe Bursting in Pittsburg, CA

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. It needs two pits. A bursting head is pulled through, fracturing the old pipe outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind, and that HDPE is fused into one continuous length with no joints at all.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Repair Cost in Pittsburg, CA

    Per foot of pipe, trenchless can cost more than open trenching. That's not the whole bill. After adding drive, patio, landscaping and sprinkler repairs to the trench bid, trenchless usually wins on price and speed. We compare both with real numbers for your property.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Pittsburg, CA

    Bursting works when the pipe has collapsed or is too broken to line. It can go up a size. Our crew sets an entry pit and an exit pit, then pulls a bursting head and new HDPE through on a hydraulic winch, which replaces the full run in a day or two for most homes. FBL Plumbing fuses the HDPE into one length.

    Sewer lines that fail suddenly can often be cleared and scoped the same day. Lining comes next. When lining fits, the repair gets booked fast, and until then we keep it flowing and tell you what not to send down the drain.
