    What a Toilet Replacement Covers in Pittsburg, CA

    Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in Pittsburg, CA

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Measure to the closet bolts, not the baseboard. If the new toilet's rough-in does not match, it either sits too far out or will not fit. An offset flange can correct an inch or two without moving the drain.

    Comfort-Height Toilet Replacement in Pittsburg

    Going from a standard bowl to a comfort-height model raises the seat two or three inches, which matters a lot for seniors and anyone recovering from knee or hip surgery. No drain changes are needed. Pair it with a grab bar if you can, and we can anchor that into a stud during the same visit.

    Commercial Toilet Replacement in Pittsburg, CA

    Office and retail restrooms wear out toilets faster than any house, and a replacement there usually means a floor-mount or wall-hung bowl with a flushometer instead of a tank. We schedule around your hours. We match the bowl to the gallons per flush on the valve, since a mismatch leaves waste behind or wastes water.

    ADA and Commercial Toilet Replacement in Pittsburg, CA

    Public restrooms need bowls rated for heavy use, and many need to meet ADA height and clearance rules when a fixture is replaced. Clearances get checked before we order anything. Adding a sensor flush valve during the swap takes little extra time, and they cut down on unflushed bowls.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Pittsburg

    Chair height toilets put the seat about 17 to 19 inches off the floor, roughly two inches higher than a standard bowl, and that makes a real difference for knees and hips. The drain stays the same. We often pair it with an elongated bowl and a soft-close seat, and the swap takes the same couple of hours as any other.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Pittsburg, CA

    Late-night failures happen, usually a tank that splits along a seam or a bowl that cracks at the base and keeps seeping no matter how many towels go down. Turn off the angle stop and the leak stops. We can usually replace it the next morning, and we will tell you if the floor needs to dry before we set the new one.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in Pittsburg

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. Some carriers need new bolts. The new bowl gets matched to the carrier already in the wall whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.

    Water-Saving Toilet Upgrades in Pittsburg, CA

    Older toilets are one of the largest indoor water users in a house, and replacing a 3.5 gallon model with a 1.28 gallon one cuts toilet water use by more than half. Dual-flush toilets add a lighter flush for liquids. Our recommendations lean on high MaP scores so the savings do not come with double flushing.
