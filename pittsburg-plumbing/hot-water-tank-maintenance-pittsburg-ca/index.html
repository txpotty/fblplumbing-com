    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Pittsburg, CA

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can clog the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a steady blue flame and lower gas bills.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Pittsburg, CA

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is essential for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank. Water chemistry varies from one neighborhood to the next, and it changes the rod's lifespan. Our technicians inspect the anode rod for wear and recommend replacement if it's significantly corroded, typically every 3 to 5 years. Replacing a worn anode rod is a cost-effective way to protect your tank from rust and leaks, sparing you a full tank replacement.

    Standard Hot Water Tank Flushing in Pittsburg

    Sediment buildup is a frequent issue in hot water tanks, especially where water hardness runs high. Our standard flushing service removes these mineral deposits, which can reduce efficiency and lead to premature tank failure. We drain the tank completely, flush out all sediment and refill it, giving you clear water flow and improved heating performance. This routine service is crucial for extending the life of your unit.

    Standard Hot Water Tank Tune-Up in Pittsburg

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Pittsburg

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause noisy popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution approved for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Pittsburg

    A standard flush and inspection typically runs $125 to $250 depending on tank size and access. Anode replacement usually adds $75 to $200 in parts and labor, and powered anodes cost more.

    The anode rod, also known as a sacrificial rod, is a metal rod inside your hot water tank designed to corrode before the tank itself. It protects the tank lining from rust and corrosion, which matters most when water carries a lot of minerals, and it lengthens the lifespan of your unit.

    FBL Plumbing services all major brands of hot water tanks, including but not limited to Rheem, Bradford White, A. O. Smith, State, Noritz and Bosch. Our technicians are familiar with a broad range of models and their specific maintenance needs.

    Many manufacturers, including Rheem, A.O. Smith and Bradford White, expect regular maintenance and can reject claims for sediment damage. Keeping service records safeguards your warranty.

    Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. Mineral content adds to sediment buildup in hot water tanks, which reduces efficiency and can damage heating elements or tank linings over time. Regular flushing is especially important in hard water areas.

    Watch for slower recovery, rusty or cloudy hot water, noises, moisture around the base or a relief valve that drips. Any of these means it is time for an inspection visit.

    Most manufacturers and plumbing experts recommend yearly hot water tank maintenance. This is especially true where water is hard, since minerals speed up sediment buildup and anode rod depletion.
