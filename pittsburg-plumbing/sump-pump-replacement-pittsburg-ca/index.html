    Which Sump Pump Fits Your Pittsburg, CA Home

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Pittsburg, CA

    Yard drainage pits and window well pumps can sit dry from fall to spring and then seize the first time a big storm fills them. We swap in a corrosion-resistant pump and clear out leaves and silt. Discharge goes away from the foundation. A bucket test every spring catches problems early.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Pittsburg

    A submersible pump sits at the bottom of the pit, sealed in an oil-filled housing, and it runs quieter than anything else you can put down there. We pull the old unit, scoop the gravel and silt out of the basin, and set the new pump on a flat brick so it does not suck up debris. Then a new check valve goes on. A worn valve lets water fall back and makes the pump run twice as often.

    Choosing a Backup Battery in Pittsburg

    Battery choice decides how long the backup actually runs. A sealed AGM or deep-cycle marine battery in the 75 to 100 amp-hour range will typically handle several hours of intermittent pumping during an outage. We ask how often your pump runs in a storm and size the battery to match. Batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Pittsburg, CA

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. You will hear one kick on from upstairs. The new column gets set plumb and braced, and we set the float rod stops so the pump turns on and off at the right water levels. Narrow pits, under about 12 inches across, are where they make the most sense.

    Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Pittsburg

    Commercial sumps run harder than residential ones, and a restaurant or office basement often has a duplex system with two pumps that alternate on a controller. We replace the pumps and the alternating relay. We can do the work after hours so your tenants or staff are not walking around an open pit.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Pittsburg

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. When the check valve leaks, the water in the pipe drops back into the basin and the pump has to move it twice, which shortens its life. New valve every time. Vertical switches suit tight pits, and tethered floats suit wide ones.

    Pedestal Pumps for Shallow Pits in Pittsburg, CA

    Older basements sometimes have a narrow, shallow pit poured right into the floor, and a pedestal pump is often the only thing that fits without breaking concrete. We replace the pump and float rod and rerun the PVC up to the check valve. They usually cost less than a submersible.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Pittsburg

    If your pump runs every 30 seconds during a storm and still loses ground, it is undersized for the water coming in. We time the cycles and measure how fast the pit refills. From there it is math. A half-horse or three-quarter-horse pump with a higher gallons-per-minute rating at your lift height may be the answer, sometimes paired with a 2-inch discharge.
