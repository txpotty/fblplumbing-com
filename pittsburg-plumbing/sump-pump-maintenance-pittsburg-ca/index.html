    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Pittsburg, CA

    Backup pumps sit in the pit next to the main pump and run off a 12-volt battery when the power drops or the primary fails. Our plumber load tests the battery instead of just reading its voltage. Then we unplug the main pump and let the backup empty the pit on its own. Alarms get tested too.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Service in Pittsburg

    Zoeller pumps like the M53 and M63 use cast iron housings and an integral float switch, and they tend to outlast cheaper plastic units. We still check the casting for cracks and the float for free travel. We carry the common Zoeller parts on the truck.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Pittsburg, CA

    Storms knock out power when the pit fills fastest. A backup pump exists for exactly that hour. We check the battery date, clean the terminals and confirm the charger is actually charging, since a dead charger quietly drains the battery to nothing over a few months.

    Emergency Sump Pump Service in Pittsburg

    A pump that runs nonstop, hums without moving water or will not start at all needs a plumber the same day, before the pit overflows. Our plumber arrives with spare floats, check valves and a replacement pump in the van. A jammed impeller usually clears in a few minutes. A burned motor means we swap in a new pump on the spot and leave the pit protected.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Pittsburg

    The check valve sits on the discharge pipe a foot or so above the pump and keeps the water in the line from falling back into the pit. A failed valve sends that water back down for the pump to move again. Motors wear out faster. Our plumber tests the valve, confirms there is a weep hole below it to prevent air lock, and checks each joint to the wall.

    Check Valve and Discharge Pipe Repair in Pittsburg, CA

    A bad check valve can nearly double how often the pump starts. Every startup puts heat into the motor. Our plumbers replace worn valves with quieter spring-loaded types and check the pipe for cracks and loose couplings out to where it leaves the house. The water should exit well away from the slab or foundation so it does not soak back toward the pit.

    Elevator Pit and Garage Pumps in Pittsburg

    Elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms all collect water that has nowhere to go by gravity, so a pump has to lift it out. In elevator pits, the pump usually needs an oil-sensing control so it will not discharge hydraulic fluid. We test duplex systems and high-water alarms on a set schedule. The property manager gets a copy of every log.

    Annual Sump Pump Inspections in Pittsburg

    Once a year is the minimum. We check the motor, impeller, float and discharge line on every annual visit. After that, we fill the basin and time the drawdown, since a slow pump-out is the first hint of a tired impeller or a restricted line.
