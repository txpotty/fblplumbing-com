    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in Pittsburg, CA

    Switching from a tank is more than a swap. A 40-gallon tank burns about 40,000 BTU, but a whole-home tankless unit may need 150,000 to 199,000, so the meter line nearly always gets bigger. The vent changes as well. We plan both on the quote.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Pittsburg, CA

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Pittsburg, CA homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. Whole-house electric units pull a lot of current.
