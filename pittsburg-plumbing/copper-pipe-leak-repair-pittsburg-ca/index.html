    The cost of copper pipe leak repair in Pittsburg can vary significantly depending on the leak's location, severity and accessibility.     Type K, L and M copper pipes differ mainly in their wall thickness and thus their pressure ratings. Type K has the thickest walls and is used for underground service and main water lines. Type L is common for interior water supply and radiant heating. Type M has the thinnest walls and is typically used for less demanding applications. We are skilled in repairing all types.

    We typically use Type L copper, which has thicker walls than Type M and lasts longer. In some situations we use ProPress fittings or a PEX transition.

    PEX resists the corrosion and scale that cause copper pinholes and is flexible and quick to install. If you have repeated copper leaks, repiping with PEX is often a cost-effective long-term solution.

    We work with all standard types and brands of copper piping found in residential and commercial properties, including Type M, Type L and Type K copper pipes. Our repairs use high-quality copper and fittings from reputable manufacturers like Mueller Streamline and Cerro Flow Products.

    Copper pipes can leak due to several factors, including corrosion from water chemistry when the pH is too acidic or alkaline, aggressive soil conditions, high water pressure, poor installation or physical damage from foundation shifts. Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running mineral-heavy. Pinhole leaks usually result from long-term internal corrosion.

    Common signs of a copper pipe leak include unexplained wet spots on walls, ceilings or floors, a sudden drop in water pressure, the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use, unusually high water bills or visible blue-green stains on pipes or nearby surfaces that point to corrosion.
