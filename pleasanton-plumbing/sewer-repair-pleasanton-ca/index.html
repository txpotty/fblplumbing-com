    Root Intrusion Removal in Pleasanton, CA

    Roots enter through hairline cracks and loose joints, then grow into a mat that catches paper and grease. They come back fast. We clear the line with a root cutting head or a jetter with a chain flail, camera it afterward to see the condition of each joint, and tell you whether sealing or replacing those joints makes more sense than cleaning every spring. Mature trees in Pleasanton yards make this a common call.

    Restaurant and Retail Sewer Repair in Pleasanton

    Restaurants, retail centers and multifamily buildings load a lateral far beyond a single house. Grease is the usual culprit. We jet the line, camera it, and repair the weak section with options that limit how long you are closed, such as overnight work or temporary bypass pumps that keep the building running. We also install two-way cleanouts for future maintenance.

    Sewer Repair for Commercial Properties in Pleasanton, CA

    Commercial laterals run six inches or larger and carry far more flow than a house. Downtime costs money.     Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Pleasanton

    Clay and cast iron crack from settling soil, tree roots and vehicles parked over the line. Dirt falls in through the crack. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded stainless couplings rated for the transition between materials, then test the repair with water and video before closing the hole. Most of these are one-day jobs.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection & Repair in Pleasanton, CA

    Sewer smell outside the house points to a leak. A lush stripe over the line is another clue. We confirm it on camera, look for cracks, open joints or a broken cleanout riser, and mark the location so the repair stays small. A small crack can sometimes be fixed with a short sectional liner, so nothing in the yard gets disturbed. Larger breaks get cut out and replaced.

    Sewer Pipe Replacement Options in Pleasanton, CA

    Replacement makes sense when the camera shows failures every few feet. Patching that line is wasted money. The right pick depends on depth and access.

    Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in Pleasanton, CA

Full replacement is right for Orangeburg or a line broken in several places. You get each option in writing with a number attached.
