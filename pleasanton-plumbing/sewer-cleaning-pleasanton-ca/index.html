    Preventative Sewer Maintenance in Pleasanton

    Roots come back after cutting. Plan on regrowth in 12 to 18 months. Putting a root-prone main on a yearly cleaning schedule keeps the growth small enough that it never stops the line, and it costs far less than an after-hours emergency call.

    Apartment and Strip Center Sewers in Pleasanton, CA

    Scheduled Sewer Cleaning in Pleasanton, CA

    History predicts the next backup. We set a cleaning interval. Homes with past root problems, older cast iron or a lot of kitchen grease do well on a cleaning every 12 to 24 months, timed before the growth or buildup closes the line.

    Dealing with Grease Buildup in Pleasanton, CA

    Grease hardens as it cools. It shrinks the opening a little every week. Kitchen grease coats the sewer line the same way plaque coats an artery, and over a few years the opening can close to the size of a finger, which is when drains start to back up, and jetting is the right fix.
