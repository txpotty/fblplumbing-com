    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Maintenance in Pleasanton

    The cost for sump pump maintenance in Pleasanton, CA typically ranges from $100 to $250, depending on the pump type and its condition. This price usually includes a thorough inspection, cleaning and operational testing. Any necessary repairs or parts replacement would be an additional cost, which we would quote upfront.

    Most local codes do not allow sump discharge into the sanitary sewer. We route discharge to the yard, a dry well or another code-approved location.

    We recommend having your sump pump professionally maintained at least once a year. If your home is in an area prone to heavy rainfall or has a high water table, twice-yearly maintenance might be more worthwhile. Regular checks ensure it's ready for peak seasons.

    The best time is a few weeks before your wettest season starts. Homes that see snow may also want a fall check before winter storms and snowmelt.

    Watch for a pump that runs constantly, hums without moving water, cycles very often or makes grinding noises. Rust, a stuck float or a musty smell around the pit are also warning signs.

    FBL Plumbing services all major brands of sump pumps common in Pleasanton homes, including Zoeller, Wayne, Little Giant, Liberty Pumps and Flotec. Our technicians are trained on the specifics of many models, providing expert care regardless of your pump's manufacturer.

    Watch out for odd noises, such as grinding or rattling, that point to debris or a failing motor. If the pump runs constantly, fails to turn on or gives off a foul odor from the pit, it's definitely time for a professional check. Visible rust or corrosion are also red flags.
