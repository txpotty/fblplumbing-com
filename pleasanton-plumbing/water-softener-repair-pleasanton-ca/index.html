    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Pleasanton

    A softener using far too much salt or water usually has settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units may regenerate on a schedule regardless of use.     No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Pleasanton, CA

    While less common than traditional softeners, no-salt water conditioners also require maintenance and periodic repair. These systems work by conditioning water to prevent scale buildup without removing minerals. If you notice scale returning or reduced water pressure, your no-salt system may need attention. FBL Plumbing is experienced in diagnosing and repairing issues with these unique units in Pleasanton, CA, ensuring they continue to protect your plumbing and appliances effectively.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Pleasanton, CA

    A salt bridge, a crust of salt that forms in the brine tank, can prevent your water softener from regenerating properly. This leads to hard water throughout your home. Our team can safely break up and remove salt bridges, clean the brine tank, and advise on best practices to prevent future occurrences. We also repair leaks or damage to the brine tank itself, common in older systems.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Pleasanton
