    Touchless Faucet Upgrade in Pleasanton

    Swapping a standard faucet for a touchless one takes a little more planning. Touchless models need a dry spot on the cabinet wall for the control box and either batteries or an outlet within reach of the cord. We check that first. We adjust the sensor range at the end.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Replacement in Pleasanton

    Pull-down spray heads take a beating. The hose wears, the spray button sticks, and after a few years the docking magnet or weight stops pulling the head back into place. We replace the faucet as a unit. The new hose weight gets clipped where the head docks cleanly, and both supplies are new braided lines.

    Utility Sink Faucet Replacement in Pleasanton

    Utility faucets in garages and laundry rooms take hard use. People hang buckets on the spout and yank hoses off the threads, which loosens the mounting and cracks the supply connections over time. We use brass-bodied replacements. We mount it tight to the wall or tub deck.

    Commercial Kitchen Faucet Replacement in Pleasanton

    Commercial kitchens are hard on faucets. We replace pre-rinse units and hand sink faucets with commercial-grade models from T&S and Chicago Faucets, which keep parts on the shelf for years. Work is scheduled around service. Each one is set to health and accessibility code.

    Budget-Friendly Faucet Replacement in Pleasanton, CA

    Not every faucet swap needs a designer model. Mid-range Moen, Delta and Pfister faucets with brass bodies and ceramic cartridges often outlast expensive imports built around plastic.     Corroded Faucet Replacement in Pleasanton

    Green crust at the base means the faucet body is corroding. A pitted body will not let a new cartridge seal, so the drip comes back a few weeks after each repair. Replacement is the only fix. Even with fused nuts, we get it out and install a faucet with a ceramic disc cartridge.

    Kitchen Faucet Replacement in Pleasanton

    Kitchen faucets usually fail at the hose first. Pull-down hoses crack at the spot where they flex over the neck, and the leak runs inside the body down into the cabinet where it stays hidden. A hose kit can fix that. When the body or cartridge is worn too, we swap the whole faucet and put new supplies on.
