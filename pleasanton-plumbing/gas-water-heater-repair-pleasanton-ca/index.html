    Pilot Light Repair in Pleasanton

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most common gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and clean or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are finished in about an hour.

    Pilot Light & Burner Issues in Pleasanton

    A common problem with gas water heaters is a pilot light that won't stay lit or a burner that fails to ignite. This can be caused by a faulty thermocouple, a dirty pilot tube or issues with the gas control valve. Our technicians in Pleasanton are adept at diagnosing these components and performing the necessary repairs, whether it is cleaning, adjusting or replacing parts. We ensure proper gas flow and ignition for consistent hot water.

    Venting and Draft Repair in Pleasanton, CA

    Atmospheric gas water heaters depend on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home. Disconnected sections, rusted draft hoods and poor slope can let combustion gases spill back. We repair or replace vent sections and verify draft with a test. Proper venting is essential for your family's safety.

    Restoring Hot Water for Older Homes in Pleasanton

    Many homes in Pleasanton feature older gas water heater models that require specialized knowledge for repair. Our technicians are familiar with the common issues and less common parts associated with these systems. We can troubleshoot and repair older units, often extending their lifespan, rather than pushing for instant replacement. We focus on cost-effective repairs that respect the history of your home.

    Propane Water Heater Repair in Pleasanton

    Rural properties often run water heaters on propane rather than natural gas. Propane units use different orifices and regulator pressures, and improper conversions cause sooting and poor performance. We confirm the correct parts are installed and repair pilots, valves and burners. We also check tank regulators for correct pressure.

    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Pleasanton, CA

    The gas control valve regulates temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something goes wrong. If the valve itself has failed, we replace it with an exact match or listed universal model. We leak test and verify temperature control afterward.

    Pilot Light and Ignition System Repair in Pleasanton

    A frequent culprit for no hot water in gas heaters is a faulty pilot light or ignition system. Our technicians are experienced in diagnosing issues like a dirty pilot tube, thermocouple failure or a malfunctioning igniter.
