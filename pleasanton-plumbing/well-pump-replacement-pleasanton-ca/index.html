    What We Replace on Well Systems in Pleasanton, CA

    Pulling a Submersible Pump in Pleasanton

    Pulling a submersible means lifting the pump and every length of drop pipe out through the pitless adapter, sometimes hundreds of feet of it. We check the torque arrestor and every splice on the way up. Most jobs are done in a day.

    Jet Pump Suction Line Repair in Pleasanton, CA

    Foot valves and suction lines cause most jet pump trouble, not the pump. A pinhole in the suction pipe lets air in, the pump loses prime, and it runs dry until the seal burns up. The suction line gets a pressure test. If the well is too deep for a jet pump, we will say so and quote a submersible.

    Constant Pressure Well Pump Installation in Pleasanton, CA

    Constant pressure systems hold the house at one pressure, say 50 psi, whether one faucet is open or the shower and the irrigation are running together. A variable frequency drive speeds the pump up and slows it down to match demand. Showers stop going weak.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Pleasanton, CA

    No-water calls often end at the control box, not the pump. Three-wire pumps have a control box with a start capacitor and relay, and those parts fail far more often than the motor itself. Those get tested first. Swapping a capacitor costs a fraction of pulling a pump.

    Jet Pump Seal and Motor Replacement in Pleasanton, CA

    Single-stage and two-stage jet pumps wear out at the seal and impeller, and a leaking shaft seal shows up as a puddle under the motor. We replace the pump and prime it properly. After that, we watch the pressure gauge cycle from cut-in to cut-out.

    Sandy Wells and Submersible Pumps in Pleasanton

    Sand kills submersibles early. A well that pumps sand will wear impellers down in a few years, so we check for grit in the old pump and talk about setting the new one higher off the bottom or adding a sand separator. Pump setting depth matters.

    Well Pump Efficiency Upgrades in Pleasanton, CA

    Older 1 HP pumps running against a 60 psi switch use more power than most people expect, especially on a well with a long horizontal run to the house. Right-sizing the pump or adding a variable-speed drive often trims the electric bill. The math does not always work. We run the numbers with you.

    Jet Pump Replacement in Pleasanton, CA

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet. A two-pipe deep-well jet can reach further down at the cost of efficiency. The common failure is losing prime. Most of the time a bad foot valve or a suction leak is letting water drain back, and we check those before condemning the pump.
