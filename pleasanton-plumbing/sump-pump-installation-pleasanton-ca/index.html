    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Pleasanton, CA

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. Expect more motor noise than a submersible. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Pleasanton, CA

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. A smart charger keeps the battery topped off and sounds an alarm if the battery weakens, the primary fails or the water rises past the backup float. Batteries need replacing every few years. Finished basements get the most out of them.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Pleasanton, CA

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. A battery backup pump sits beside the primary and runs on its own float. A good battery carries it through most outages. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Pleasanton, CA

    Price depends mostly on whether a pit already exists. Swapping a pump in an existing pit is the cheapest job, while a new pit means saw cutting, digging, gravel and a concrete patch, which roughly doubles the labor. Battery backups and alarms add to the total. We list each option on a written quote.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Pleasanton, CA

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. We set a basin at the lowest point, run perforated drain pipe to it where needed and seal the vapor barrier around the lid so the space stays dry. Clearance is usually under two feet. The discharge exits through the rim joist and drains away from the house.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Pleasanton

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. Sizing comes from two measurements we take on site, the pit diameter and the vertical lift to the discharge. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Pleasanton, CA

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary runs on house power and handles the everyday inflow, while the battery unit waits above it on a separate float. People with carpet downstairs buy these. We install them in homes where a flood would ruin flooring and drywall.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Pleasanton, CA

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. We install their M53 and M98 models often. Backup and combo units are available too. Pit size and lift decide which model we recommend.
