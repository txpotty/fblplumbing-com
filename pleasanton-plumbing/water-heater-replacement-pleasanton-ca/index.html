    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Pleasanton

    The cost of water heater replacement in Pleasanton, CA typically ranges from $1,200 to $2,500 for a standard tank unit, including installation. Tankless units can range from $3,000 to $5,000 or more, depending on the model and needed plumbing modifications. Factors like unit type, capacity, brand and labor complexity influence the final price.

    Most replacements take two to four hours. Attic installs or relocations can take longer.

    FBL Plumbing installs and services all major brands, including Bradford White, Rheem, AO Smith, Rinnai, Navien and State Water Heaters. We can help you choose the best brand and model that suits your budget and hot water demands.

    Yes, in many areas of Pleasanton and Northern California, a plumbing permit is required for water heater replacement to make sure the installation meets local safety and building codes. FBL Plumbing handles all necessary permitting on your behalf, so you do not have to worry about compliance.

    While it might seem tempting, water heater replacement involves gas lines, high voltage electricity, water pressure and heavy equipment. It is a complex job best handled by licensed professionals to ensure safety, proper installation and compliance with local plumbing codes, preventing potential leaks or hazards.

    A traditional water heater stores and heats a limited amount of water in a tank, whereas a tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit. Tankless units offer endless hot water and can be more energy-efficient, but usually have a higher upfront cost and may require gas line upgrades.

    Flushing a traditional tank water heater once a year is recommended in most homes. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. This helps remove sediment buildup, which can reduce efficiency and shorten the lifespan of the unit. Regular flushing can also prevent scale from impacting heating elements.

    Leaks at the base, rusty water, rumbling noises, inconsistent hot water and an age over 10 years are common signs. A leaking tank should be replaced right away.
