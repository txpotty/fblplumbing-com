    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Pleasanton

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, especially in closed-loop systems. Code here requires seismic strapping on water heaters and water-efficient fixtures, and many local agencies require sewer lateral inspections at sale. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to support proper system operation and prevent early wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Pleasanton. This small component offers significant long-term protection.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Pleasanton, CA

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves. An expansion tank absorbs that extra pressure. We install and pre-charge the tank to match your water pressure. This protects your new water heater and pipes.
