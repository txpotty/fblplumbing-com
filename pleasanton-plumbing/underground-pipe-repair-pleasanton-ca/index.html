    Pipe Bursting Services in Pleasanton, CA

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless technique where a new pipe is pulled through the path of the old, broken pipe, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is very effective for complete pipe replacement, creating a new, durable pipe of the same or larger diameter. It's a solid solution for severely damaged or collapsed sewer and water lines, offering a long-term fix in Pleasanton, CA.

    Irrigation and Yard Line Repair in Pleasanton

    Drip lines and sprinkler supply pipes are often shallow and easy to break during landscaping or by roots. A broken irrigation main can run up a water bill fast in summer. We repair PVC and poly irrigation mains, replace failed backflow connections and fix the supply line to the valve manifold. We can also reroute exposed lines away from trees.

    Underground Sewer Line Repair in Pleasanton, CA

    Buried sewer laterals crack from root intrusion, soil settling and age. We camera the line to find the exact spot, then excavate and replace the damaged section with PVC. Shielded couplings make a watertight connection to the existing pipe. We confirm proper slope and flow before backfilling and tamping the trench.

    Main Water Line Repair in Pleasanton

    Your main water line runs from the meter box to the house and is under constant pressure. When it leaks, you may notice a spinning meter dial with all fixtures off, low pressure or a wet spot that never dries. We locate the leak, expose the pipe and splice in a new section with approved fittings. If the line is old galvanized or polybutylene, we will recommend a full replacement before more breaks occur.

    Underground Pipe Repair Cost Comparison in Pleasanton, CA
