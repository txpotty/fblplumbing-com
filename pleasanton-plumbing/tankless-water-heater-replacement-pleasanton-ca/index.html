    Picking a Tankless for Your Pleasanton, CA Home

    Whole-House Electric Tankless Replacement in Pleasanton

    Whole-house electric tankless performance drops when the incoming water is cold, since the unit has to raise the temperature further at the same amperage. We replace Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart units. Sometimes a heat pump tank serves the house better, and we will say so.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Pleasanton, CA

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Their combi boilers handle both space heat and domestic water. Both are in our wheelhouse.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Pleasanton

    Rinnai units we pull are usually 12 to 18 years old, and the failure tends to be a leaking heat exchanger or a dead control board. A current Rinnai often mounts on the same bracket. Less drywall work that way. We match the vent type or convert to PVC on a condensing model.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Pleasanton

    Cascade controls on a commercial rack decide which unit fires first, and after one unit is replaced they need rebalancing. Skip that and the new unit wears out first. We also set a descaling schedule.

    Gas Line Testing on Tankless Installs in Pleasanton, CA

    Every gas connection on a tankless gets a soap test and a manometer reading at full fire before we leave the house. Static pressure means little if it drops when the burner opens up. We test both.

    Tankless-to-Tankless Replacement in Pleasanton

    Replacing an existing tankless with a new one is usually the simpler job, since the gas line, vent path and water connections are already there. We still check sizing against the new model. Old service valves often get replaced too.

    Emergency Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Pleasanton

    No hot water from a tankless usually shows up as an error code on the controller, and the code tells us half of what we need to know. Send us a photo of the display. A leaking heat exchanger means replacement, usually done same day or next day.

    Recirculation Tankless Upgrades in Pleasanton, CA

    Waiting a minute for hot water at the far bathroom wastes thousands of gallons a year in a long single-story house. Navien A models and Rinnai units with internal pumps can recirculate through a return line or a crossover valve under the farthest sink. The pump runs on a timer. Mornings and evenings, usually.
