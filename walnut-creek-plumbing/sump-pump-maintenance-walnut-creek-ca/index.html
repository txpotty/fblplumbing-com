    Battery Backup System Testing in Walnut Creek, CA

    Storms knock out power when the pit fills fastest. A backup pump exists for exactly that hour. Our plumber reads the battery date, cleans corrosion off the terminals and tests the charger, because a failed charger can leave the battery flat within a season.

    Sump Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Walnut Creek

    Silt and gravel wash into a pit through the drain tile every time it rains, and over a few years that layer can bury the intake screen. Our plumber drains the basin and digs the sediment out by hand. With the pit clean, the float swings without catching. The pump runs cooler too.

    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Walnut Creek

    Submersible pumps sit at the bottom of the pit, which keeps them quiet but hides the problems. We lift the unit out to look at the seals and the cord. A cracked cord usually means a new pump. We also clean the intake screen and check the housing for corrosion, which shows up fast on pumps that sit in standing water for years.

    Annual Sump Pump Inspections in Walnut Creek

    Once a year is the minimum. We check the motor, impeller, float and discharge line on every annual visit. Then we fill the pit and time how long the pump takes to empty it, because a slow drawdown is the earliest sign of a worn impeller or a partly blocked line.

    Emergency Sump Pump Service in Walnut Creek

    A pump that runs nonstop, hums without moving water or will not start at all needs a plumber the same day, before the pit overflows. Our plumber arrives with spare floats, check valves and a replacement pump in the van. Most jammed impellers clear in minutes. Burned-out motors get a new pump installed before we leave, so the pit is protected again that day.

    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Walnut Creek, CA

    Float trouble causes more no-start calls than anything else. Tethered floats can wrap around the discharge pipe or snag on the pit wall. Vertical floats stick when mineral crust builds up on the rod. For narrow basins where a float keeps catching, an electronic switch with no moving float is a good swap that ends the problem.

    Float Switch Inspection in Walnut Creek, CA

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. We watch the float through a full cycle. Then we set the on and off points so the pump runs longer, less often.
