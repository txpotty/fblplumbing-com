    Preventative Sewer Maintenance in Walnut Creek

    Roots come back after cutting. Usually within a year or so. A scheduled cleaning every year or two keeps roots and grease from closing the line and lets you plan around it.

    Dealing with Grease Buildup in Walnut Creek, CA

    Grease hardens as it cools. It shrinks the opening a little every week. Kitchen grease coats the sewer line the same way plaque coats an artery, and over a few years the opening can close to the size of a finger, which is when drains start to back up, and jetting is the right fix.

    Emergency Sewer Backup Cleaning in Walnut Creek

    Tree Root Removal in Walnut Creek, CA Sewers

    Roots get in through tiny gaps. Once inside, they grow fast. We cut the root ball with a saw head sized for the pipe, then flush the leftovers with a jetter when the growth is heavy, and a camera shows where they are getting in.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Walnut Creek

    No cleanout means pulling a toilet. That adds time. A two-way cleanout by the foundation gives us access toward the house and toward the street, so future service is quicker, and the work is done to local code with any required permit.

    Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Walnut Creek, CA

    Grease bonds with mineral scale into a hard crust. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. Flow slows a little each year. Cabling opens a path, while jetting strips the crust off the walls so the line stays open longer, and the method depends on pipe material and condition.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Walnut Creek

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. A cable only makes a path. A jetter sends high-pressure water around the full circumference of the pipe, removing buildup that a cable would leave behind.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Walnut Creek
