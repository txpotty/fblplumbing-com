    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Walnut Creek

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler.     Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Walnut Creek, CA

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. When someone flushes a toilet, the valve cuts hot and cold flow in proportion so the mix stays steady, and a thermostatic valve goes further by holding a set temperature even as the water heater cools. Either one satisfies scald rules. Thermostatic costs more.

    Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in Walnut Creek

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting. We cut out the old valve, sweat or crimp in a single-handle pressure-balance valve, and cover the old handle holes with a remodel plate so the tile stays intact around it. We usually work from behind the wall. Tile around the plate stays intact.

    Delta tub valves are some of the easiest to update. Delta Monitor valves take new cartridges from the front, and the MultiChoice body accepts future trim swaps. Popular Delta cartridges are on the truck. Older Delta valves sometimes need full replacement.
