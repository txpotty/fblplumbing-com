    Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in Walnut Creek

    Stone shops cut the hole, but we set the bowl. Getting an undermount centered under a polished cutout so the reveal is even on all sides takes a helper, a few shims and patience before the clips get tightened. A crooked reveal never goes away. We check it from both ends of the counter.

    Undermount Clips and Cure Time in Walnut Creek

    Undermount bowls need overnight to cure. The silicone bead under the rim reaches handling strength in a few hours but needs about a day before it carries the weight of a full basin of water and a pot leaning on the edge. We brace it and come back. Plumbing goes in on the second visit.
