    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Walnut Creek, CA

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, with the tank hidden behind an access plate.     TOTO, Kohler and American Standard consistently perform well in flush tests and have good parts availability. Gerber and Mansfield are solid budget options. We help you match the brand and model to your needs.

    Installing a new toilet can offer several benefits, including improved water efficiency, reduced water bills, better flushing power, a better look, and increased comfort. Modern toilets are also designed to be more durable and easier to clean.

    A standard toilet installation, assuming no major plumbing modifications are needed, typically takes about 1 to 3 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new rough-ins or dealing with damaged subflooring, may take longer.

    When choosing a new toilet, consider factors like bowl shape (round vs. elongated), height (standard vs. ADA-compliant), flushing system (gravity-fed vs. pressure-assisted), water efficiency (gallons per flush), and overall style to match your bathroom. We can help you sort through these choices.

    A comfort height toilet has a bowl that is taller than standard models, usually around 17 to 19 inches from the floor to the top of the seat. This height is closer to a standard chair, making it more comfortable for many adults, especially those with mobility issues.
