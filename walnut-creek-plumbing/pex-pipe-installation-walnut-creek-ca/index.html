    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Walnut Creek

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. A crimp joint is only as good as the tool, so we check every ring with a go/no-go gauge, and a ring that does not pass gets cut out and redone. It is a five-second check. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.

    Full Home PEX Repiping in Walnut Creek

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside out. After 40 or 50 years the bore is so choked with scale that a half-inch line delivers a trickle, and the water comes out tinted brown after it sits overnight. A full PEX repipe replaces all of it. You get real pressure back, and the discolored water goes away with the old pipe.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Walnut Creek

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool plus a ring slipped over the pipe end. We stretch the pipe and ring, seat the fitting, and within seconds the plastic shrinks back around the barb into a tight seal. No crimp tool to calibrate. The fittings have a wide bore, so flow loss through each joint stays low.
