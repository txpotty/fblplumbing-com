    Standard Tank Tune-Up in Walnut Creek

    A standard tune-up starts with the fuel off and the cold supply closed, then a full drain through the bottom valve until the water runs clear. That alone takes 30 to 40 minutes. We check the heating elements or burner, read the thermostat setting and look at every fitting on top of the tank for green crust or drips.     Gas and Electric Tank Checks in Walnut Creek, CA

    Gas and electric tanks share the same steel shell, but the parts that wear out are different. Gas units get a burner, thermocouple and vent check. Electric units get a meter test on both elements and a look at the wiring at each thermostat, where loose terminals can scorch and melt the insulation. Both get a flush.

    Sediment Flushing in Walnut Creek, CA

    Sediment is mostly calcium carbonate and sand, and it settles in a layer that can reach several inches on a neglected 50-gallon tank. The heat has to push through it to reach the water. We flush with full line pressure, opening and closing the cold supply in bursts to stir the bottom. The drain hose runs to a floor drain or outside, never onto a lawn where hot water can burn the grass.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Walnut Creek, CA

    The anode rod is a sacrificial metal rod that corrodes so the steel tank does not. Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running hard. We break the hex head loose, pull the rod and check how much bare wire shows. Six inches of bare wire means replace it.

    Standard Tank Flushing in Walnut Creek

    Flushing is the one job every tank needs, gas or electric, new or old. Hard water makes it more urgent. We drain, partly refill and drain again until the hose runs clear, sometimes four or five rounds. Then we close the valve and check that it reseals without a drip.

    Commercial Tank Maintenance in Walnut Creek, CA

    Expansion Tank Service in Walnut Creek, CA
