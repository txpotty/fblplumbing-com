    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Walnut Creek, CA

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. Our crews use Uponor PEX-A and full-bore fittings. Every fixture, appliance and the water heater gets new pipe from the main, and most houses are finished in two to four days with water on each evening.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Walnut Creek

    Copper is a solid alternative to PEX for poly replacement. Copper is stiff, heat tolerant and long proven. Plan on a higher price. Some water can pinhole copper over time, so we look at your water and any past leak history before recommending it over PEX.

    Polybutylene Replacement Costs in Walnut Creek, CA

    Replacing polybutylene with PEX in an average home often costs $5,000 to $12,000, depending on size and bathrooms. Copper runs higher. A yard service line typically adds $1,500 to $4,000, and that is still usually less than repairing damage from one burst.

    Insurance and Home Sale Documentation in Walnut Creek

    Some insurers decline or surcharge policies on homes with polybutylene. Lenders may ask as well. After replacement, we provide the permit record, the invoice and photos of the new system, which is the paperwork insurance agents and buyers want when they ask whether the poly is gone.

    Copper Replacement for Polybutylene in Walnut Creek, CA

    Some homeowners in Walnut Creek, CA want copper when they take out polybutylene. That works fine. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes, and join it with lead-free solder or press fittings. Expect a higher price and a slightly longer job with copper, in exchange for good heat and UV tolerance and a long history.

    Fitting Failure and Emergency Repair in Walnut Creek

    Acetal plastic and copper crimp ring fittings are often the first part of a poly system to fail. Attic fittings fail hard. We respond to emergency leaks, make temporary repairs and then schedule a full replacement, because stopping the problem for good means taking the poly system out of service entirely.

    Finding Polybutylene Leaks in Walnut Creek

    A poly leak inside a wall or ceiling can run for days before you see a stain. The meter often tells first. With every fixture off, a spinning leak indicator on the meter means water is going somewhere, and we use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to find it without opening walls at random. Once we find it, a temporary fix buys time to schedule the repipe.

    Full Home Polybutylene Repiping in Walnut Creek

    A full replacement takes every poly supply line out of service, not just the sections that have failed. Leaving some poly keeps the risk. We run new lines from the main shutoff to every sink, toilet, tub, washer, ice maker and water heater, add new quarter-turn stops and test the whole system before patching.
