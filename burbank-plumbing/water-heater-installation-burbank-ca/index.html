    Expansion Tank Installation in Burbank, CA

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves. An expansion tank absorbs that extra pressure. We install and pre-charge the tank to match your water pressure. This protects your new water heater and plumbing.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Burbank

    Some local homes have water heaters in attics or interior closets. These installs require careful planning for access, drain pans and relief line routing. Attic temperatures can swing widely with the seasons, so we take extra steps to protect connections. We install drain pans with proper drain lines to protect ceilings. If relocation makes sense, we can move the heater to a safer spot.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Burbank

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, particularly in closed-loop systems. Code here requires seismic strapping on water heaters and water-efficient fixtures, and many local agencies require sewer lateral inspections at sale. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to ensure proper system operation and prevent early wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Burbank. This small component offers significant long-term protection.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Burbank, CA

    Electric tank water heaters are common in homes without natural gas service. We install models with the correct voltage and breaker size for your electrical panel. These units do not need venting, which allows more flexible placement. We also install a drain pan when the heater is in a closet or attic. Electric models are simple, dependable and quiet.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Burbank

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon tank water heater typically costs $1,600 to $3,500 installed. Tankless installations often cost $3,000 to $6,500 depending on gas line and venting needs.

    The decision to repair or replace often depends on the age of your unit, the cost of the repair and its energy efficiency. If your water heater is over 10 years old, frequently breaks down, or the repair cost is more than 50% of a new unit, replacement is often the more cost-effective choice. Newer models also offer significantly better energy efficiency.

    The correct size water heater depends on the number of people in your household and your peak hot water demand. For tank units, a 40 to 50 gallon tank is common for 2 to 4 people, while larger families might need 60 to 80 gallons. For tankless units, it is about flow rate (GPM) and temperature rise. Our plumbers will assess your needs to recommend the right size.

    New water heaters usually come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and components, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. FBL Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work, ensuring peace of mind. We will discuss specific warranty details for your chosen unit during the consultation.

    Common signs that you might need a new water heater include a lack of hot water, rusty or discolored water coming from the hot tap, banging or rumbling noises from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit and an age of 10 to 15 years or more. If you notice any of these issues, it is wise to have a professional inspection.

    FBL Plumbing installs all major reputable brands of water heaters, including Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz. We work with both gas and electric models, as well as various sizes and efficiencies. Our team can help you choose the best brand and model to suit your hot water demands and budget.

    Most standard water heater installations, especially for replacing a similar tank unit, can be completed within 3 to 4 hours. However, a conversion from a tank to a tankless system, or installations requiring major plumbing or electrical modifications, might take a full day or longer. We will provide an estimated timeline during our first assessment.

    Many jurisdictions require a permit for water heater replacement. We pull permits when required and confirm the install meets code.
