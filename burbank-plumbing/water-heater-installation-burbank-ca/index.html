    Expansion Tank Installation in Burbank, CA

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Burbank

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. A small expansion tank on the cold line absorbs that extra volume, which keeps the relief valve from weeping and saves wear on faucets and fittings.
