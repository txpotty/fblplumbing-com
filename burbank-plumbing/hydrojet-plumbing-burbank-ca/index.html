    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydrojet Plumbing in Burbank

    The cost of hydrojet plumbing in Burbank, CA can range from $300 to $800 or more, depending on the severity and location of the clog, as well as the length and diameter of the pipe. Factors like accessibility, the presence of tree roots, and whether a prior camera inspection is needed will also affect the final price. We provide clear, upfront quotes after an assessment.

    We use professional cart and trailer jetters from manufacturers like Spartan and RIDGID, paired with rotating, penetrating and root-cutting nozzles. Our cameras record video of the line before and after. This equipment lets us handle both homes and commercial buildings.

    The duration of hydrojet plumbing depends on the complexity of the clog and the length of the pipe being cleaned. Most residential hydrojetting jobs in Burbank can be completed within 1 to 3 hours, but more severe or extensive blockages, especially those involving main sewer lines or heavy root intrusion, can take longer.

    Jetting removes the roots inside the pipe, but roots will grow back if the joint or crack remains open. Regular jetting can control them for years. Pipe lining or repair is the permanent fix.

    Snaking involves using an auger to punch a hole through a clog, often leaving behind some debris. Hydrojetting, on the other hand, uses high-pressure water to thoroughly scour the entire inner surface of the pipe, removing buildup and leaving it clean. Hydrojetting provides a much more comprehensive and longer-lasting solution.

    Key signs you might need hydrojet plumbing include often recurring clogs that snaking doesn't fully resolve, multiple slow drains throughout your home, gurgling sounds from drains or toilets, or foul odors coming from your drains. A severe sewer backup is also a clear indicator that hydrojetting is needed to clear the main line.

    It is safe for most cast iron, clay, ABS and PVC lines in reasonable condition. We always inspect with a camera first and lower the pressure or recommend another method if the pipe is cracked or badly corroded. That step protects weak lines.

    For most homes, hydrojetting every year or two is sufficient for preventive maintenance, especially in areas with hard water or older plumbing. Businesses, particularly those in food service, may benefit from more frequent cleanings, such as quarterly or every six months, to prevent major blockages.
