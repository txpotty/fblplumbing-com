    Softener Systems We Replace in Burbank, CA

    Brine Tank Replacement in Burbank

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break up the crust and scoop out the mushy salt underneath. Cracked tanks and stuck safety floats get replaced. Pellets bridge less than rock salt.

    Emergency Softener Replacement in Burbank, CA

    A softener that sticks in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours, and a cracked bypass or tank can flood a garage floor. First step, turn the bypass handle. We can usually replace a leaking or stuck system within a day or two, and same day if we have the size on the truck.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Burbank, CA

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. What they do well is keep scale off pipe walls and heater elements. They need no drain line. We can replace one or switch you to a true softener.

    Softener Loop Installation in Burbank, CA

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. Homes without one get a loop cut in, with shutoffs and a bypass. Outdoor spigots stay on hard water. We run the drain to a standpipe or floor drain through an air gap.

    Clack Water Softener Replacement in Burbank

    Clack WS1 valves are easy to program and easy to rebuild, which is why a lot of water treatment shops have switched to them. A sound tank can keep its resin and get a new valve. Or the whole system.

    Long-Term Softener Costs in Burbank, CA

    Long-term cost on a softener is mostly salt and water. Compared with a timer unit, a metered valve set efficiently often uses a third less salt, which matters over a fifteen-year life. That is how we compare models.

    Whole-Home Softener Placement in Burbank, CA

    Every tap in the house, including the shower and the water heater, gets soft water when the softener sits on the main line ahead of everything. We typically leave the hose bibs on hard water. No sense spending salt on the garden.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Burbank, CA

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. Rebedding with new resin can be cheaper than a new system. It depends on the valve. New resin under a tired valve rarely lasts.
