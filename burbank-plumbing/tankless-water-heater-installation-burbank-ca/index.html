    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Burbank

    Converting from a tank requires more than swapping units. A 40 gallon tank heater uses roughly 40,000 BTU, while a whole-home tankless unit may need up to 199,000 BTU, so the gas line usually needs to be upsized. Venting changes from a metal flue to PVC or stainless, and the unit moves to a wall location. We remove and dispose of your old tank and patch the old flue penetration.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Burbank

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to deliver hot water to distant fixtures. A recirculation pump or dedicated return line keeps hot water close to faucets, cutting wait time and water waste. Some units have built-in pumps with smart scheduling. We design the system to fit your plumbing layout and usage patterns.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Burbank, CA

    Electric tankless water heaters are an good option for homes in Burbank where natural gas is not available or preferred. FBL Plumbing provides expert electrical sizing to make sure your home's panel can safely support a new electric tankless unit, offering consistent hot water without the need for a gas line.

    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in Burbank, CA

    Tankless installation costs more upfront than a standard tank because of the unit price, gas line upgrades and venting changes. Tankless units often last 15 to 20 years with proper descaling, compared with about 8 to 12 years for many tanks in hard water areas.
