    What We Fix on Faucets in Burbank, CA

    Dripping Faucet Solutions in Burbank

    Every drip has a source, and on a faucet that has dripped for years there is usually more than one worn part. Anything worn gets replaced, and the rest gets checked. Each valve type, from compression to ceramic disc, wears in its own way, so the repair is matched to the valve inside yours.

    Faucet Handle Repair and Replacement in Burbank, CA

    Stripped handle splines, cracked handle bodies, worn adapters and loose set screws make a faucet hard to turn or impossible to shut tight. Many brands sell the handle on its own. The replacement handle gets matched to both the stem and the finish, and if the stem itself is stripped we replace it at the same time.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Burbank

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. Hoses split near the quick-connect. We replace hoses, spray heads and weights with brand parts, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.

    Cracked Faucet Body Repair in Burbank

    Cracked faucet bodies, usually from freezing or an old brass casting that finally gave out, cannot be repaired and need a replacement faucet. Shut the stops first. We can usually install a new faucet the same day, and we will check the supply lines and stops while the water is off.

    Tub Spout and Diverter Repair in Burbank, CA

    Water still running from the tub spout while the shower is on means the diverter is worn, either the gate inside the spout or a diverter stem in the valve. There are slip-on and threaded spouts. We measure the pipe stub and bring the right style, and the fix gives you full pressure at the showerhead again.

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Burbank, CA

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base usually means failed O-rings on the spout or cartridge, or a mounting nut that has worked loose under the sink. Check the cabinet floor. New O-rings go on and the mounting nut gets tightened, then reseal the base so water on the counter cannot get under it.

    Emergency Faucet Repair in Burbank

    A faucet that will not shut off, or one with a cracked body spraying under the sink, needs the water off before anything else happens. Turn the two small valves under the sink clockwise. If they will not turn, use the main shutoff for the house. We come fast for these.

    Bathroom Pop-Up Drain Repair in Burbank

    Pop-up drains on bathroom sinks fail at the pivot ball, the clevis strap, the lift rod or the stopper itself, and a slow drain often turns out to be hair wrapped around the stopper. Try lifting out the stopper first. We replace the pop-up assembly in a matching finish when the old one is corroded or the pivot leaks.
