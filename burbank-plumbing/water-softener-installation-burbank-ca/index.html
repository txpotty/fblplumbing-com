    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in Burbank

    The cost of water softener installation in Burbank, CA usually ranges from $1,500 to $4,000, depending on the system type, brand and complexity of the installation. Factors like existing plumbing modifications and the size of your home can influence the final price. We provide transparent, upfront quotes after an on-site assessment.

    Softeners remove hardness minerals using salt, while conditioners change how minerals behave to reduce scale. Softeners are more effective for very hard water.

    Homeowners with a loop can sometimes install a softener, but proper drain air gaps, bypass valves and programming matter. Professional installation helps avoid leaks and weak performance.

    Installing on an existing loop usually takes two to three hours. Adding a new loop can take half a day or more.

    While some homeowners attempt DIY installation, it's generally recommended to hire a professional. Incorrect installation can lead to leaks, inefficient operation or even damage to your plumbing system. Professional installation provides proper sizing, correct connections and adherence to local plumbing codes.

    The warranty typically covers both the water softener unit itself, which varies by manufacturer (often 5 to 10 years on major components), and our installation workmanship, usually for one year.     Most households add salt every one to two months depending on water use and hardness. Metered systems use salt more efficiently.

    A properly sized and installed water softener should not significantly affect your water pressure. If you experience a noticeable drop in pressure after installation, it could point to an issue with the system or installation, which our plumbers can diagnose and resolve.
