    Effluent Pump Repair in Burbank, CA

    Homes with uphill or distant fields often use an effluent pump in a pump tank. Failed pumps, stuck floats or tripped breakers trigger high water alarms. We test the pump, float switches and control panel and replace failed parts. A working alarm gives you warning before sewage backs up.

    Septic Baffle and Tee Repair in Burbank

    Baffles and tees keep scum and solids in the tank so they do not flow out into the leach field. Concrete baffles crumble from hydrogen sulfide gas, and plastic tees can break off. When they fail, solids clog the field fast. We replace damaged baffles with durable PVC tees and add an effluent filter where it makes sense.

    Septic Tank Leak Repair in Burbank

    Frequently Asked Questions About Septic Tank Repair in Burbank

    Septic tank repair costs in Burbank, CA vary widely depending on the issue. Minor repairs like clearing a clog might range from $300 to $700, while major repairs such as drain field replacement can cost $5,000 to $15,000 or more. We provide a detailed estimate after finding the problem.

    For minor repairs like baffle replacement, a permit may not be required. Major repairs that involve excavation, major pipe replacement or any work on the drain field typically require permits from the local health department or building authority in Burbank, CA. FBL Plumbing will help you work through the permitting process and make sure all work is compliant.

    FBL Plumbing services and repairs all major septic tank brands and types, including concrete, fiberglass and plastic tanks and components from manufacturers like Infiltrator Systems, Presby Environmental and Advanced Drainage Systems. Our technicians are experienced with many septic system designs, from conventional gravity systems to aerobic treatment units.

    Common causes of septic tank failure include infrequent pumping, which lets solids accumulate, tree root intrusion into pipes or the tank, excessive water usage overloading the system, driving heavy vehicles over the drain field and using harsh chemicals that kill beneficial bacteria. Dense, clay-rich soils can also add to drain field issues.

    Most additives are not needed for a healthy system. Regular pumping, water conservation and keeping grease and wipes out of drains do far more.

    Key signs include slow drains, gurgling toilets, sewage odors inside or outside your home, unusually lush or soggy spots in your yard near the septic tank or drain field, and sewage backups into your home. If you notice any of these, it is important to call FBL Plumbing promptly to prevent further damage.

    The duration of septic tank repair depends on the complexity of the issue. Simple repairs like a baffle replacement might take a few hours, while more extensive work such as drain field repair or pipe replacement could take one to several days, especially if excavation is involved. Our technicians will provide an expected timeline during the diagnosis.
