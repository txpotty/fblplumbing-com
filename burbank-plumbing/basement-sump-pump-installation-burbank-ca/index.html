    Quality cast iron pumps usually carry three to five years of factory coverage. We back our installation labor separately, and we walk you through the exact terms for the model you pick before we install it.

    Permit rules vary by town, including Burbank. Replacing a pump in the same pit rarely needs one, but a new pit, a new outlet or a new exit through the foundation wall often does, and we sort that out before any concrete gets cut. We handle the paperwork.
