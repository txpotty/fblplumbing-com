    Repiping and Pipe Replacement Choices in Burbank, CA

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Burbank, CA

    Galvanized steel was common before the 1960s. The zinc wears off and the steel rusts inside. Over decades the opening shrinks, pressure drops and the water turns brown after sitting, and the threads where pipes join are often the first to leak. Replacing it is the only fix.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Burbank, CA

    Removing every piece is rarely needed. It is disconnected and capped, while new lines run beside it, which keeps drywall damage low. Only accessible pipe comes out.

    Copper Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Burbank, CA

    Older homes sometimes have copper that is thin from decades of use. Small leaks appear in random spots. We replace it with new Type L copper or PEX, and fixing awkward runs from past remodels. Better flow.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Burbank, CA

    Drain lines fail too. Old cast iron rusts through. We replace cracked cast iron, failing ABS or old galvanized drains with PVC, checking slope and venting so the new drains flow freely. Camera first.

    Commercial Pipe Replacement in Burbank

    Commercial buildings often have larger supply lines and more fixtures. Downtime costs money. We plan pipe replacement in phases whenever the layout allows it, keeping restrooms and kitchens running while we work on other sections. Night work available.

    Whole-Home Re-Piping in Burbank

    A whole-home repipe replaces every supply line from the main shutoff to each fixture, including the water heater connections. Plan on a few days for a typical house. We run new trunk lines through the attic or crawlspace, branch down to fixtures through small wall openings, with service restored each evening when possible. Then patching.

    PEX Repiping in Burbank

    PEX installs quicker. Flexible lines snake through walls with fewer openings, and home-run systems from a manifold let you shut off individual fixtures without killing water to the house. Popular choice.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Burbank

    Polybutylene is gray or blue plastic installed from roughly the late 1970s to mid 1990s. Chlorine breaks it down over time. Failures tend to be sudden splits at fittings rather than slow drips, which can cause insurance headaches. Replace it.
