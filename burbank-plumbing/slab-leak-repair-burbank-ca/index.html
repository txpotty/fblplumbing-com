    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Burbank

    Cold water slab leaks are harder to feel but still waste water and wet the soil. They often show up as a high water bill or a meter that moves with all fixtures off. We isolate the cold line, find the leak and repair or reroute it. Fixing cold leaks quickly helps protect your foundation.

    Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Burbank

    Locating a hidden slab leak takes specialized tools. Our plumbers use advanced electronic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to pinpoint the exact location of the leak under your concrete foundation. This precision detection reduces the need for extensive concrete removal, saving you time and money.     Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Burbank

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution. This involves abandoning the leaking line under the slab and installing a new pipe through the walls or attic. This method skips the problem area entirely, providing a fresh, durable plumbing line. It is a common solution for aging homes in Burbank with original copper or galvanized plumbing.

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Burbank, CA

    In some cases, especially when the leak is under a load-bearing wall or in a hard-to-reach area, tunneling beneath the foundation is the preferred method. This allows our plumbers to reach the damaged pipe from outside your home, avoiding disruption to your interior floors and finishes. Tunneling spares the interior but calls for skilled excavation and shoring to protect structural integrity throughout the repair process in Burbank, CA.

    Direct Slab Penetration Repair in Burbank, CA

    When a slab leak is directly accessible and localized, direct slab penetration is often the most efficient repair method. This involves carefully cutting a small section of your concrete slab to access and repair or replace the damaged pipe section. Our team makes sure the repair is fully compliant with Northern California plumbing codes and the slab is correctly restored, preventing future issues. We take real care to contain dust and debris during this process in your Burbank home.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Burbank, CA
