    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Burbank

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside the heat exchanger, restricting flow and causing overheating. Many units warn of this with scale codes, such as the LC code on Rinnai models.     Ignition Failure Repair in Burbank, CA

    If your tankless unit will not fire, the cause may be a dirty flame rod, a bad igniter, low gas pressure or a blocked intake. We test each component and measure gas pressure at the inlet.     Recirculation Pump Repair in Burbank

    Many tankless systems use internal or external recirculation to deliver hot water more quickly. Pump failures, check valve problems or wrong schedule settings can cause long waits or lukewarm water. We test the pump, check valves and controls and set the schedule to match your routine. A properly tuned system saves both water and gas.

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Burbank

    Tankless heaters use flow sensors to start and thermistors to regulate temperature. A dirty inlet filter or failed sensor causes no hot water, fluctuating temperature or the unit turning off mid-shower. We clean filters, test sensor readings and replace failed parts. Temperature becomes steady again once readings are accurate.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling Service in Burbank

    Hard water can lead to mineral buildup, or scaling, in your tankless water heater's heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and eventually causing breakdowns. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. Our descaling service in Burbank involves flushing the unit with a specialized cleaning solution to dissolve these mineral deposits. This preventative maintenance and repair step is key for extending the lifespan of your tankless heater and keeping it at peak performance.
