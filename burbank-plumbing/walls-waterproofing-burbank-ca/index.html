    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in Burbank, CA

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Burbank, CA

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the waterline and check it a day later. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a clamping drain tightened onto the liner. We flood test the new pan before any tile goes down.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Burbank, CA

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Older neighborhoods in Northern California include prewar and postwar homes with galvanized supply lines and cast iron or clay sewer pipe that are past the end of their life. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. Sealing soft old brick with a rigid coating traps water inside it and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with interior drain tile. Old mortar joints get repointed first.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Burbank, CA

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. We set a lidded basin below slab level, install a cast iron pump sized for the inflow, and add a battery backup or water-powered backup for the night the power goes out during a storm. Check valves matter. Without one, the discharge water runs back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed at least 10 feet from the house.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Burbank, CA

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and tie it into a sump pit so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. It just stops puddling on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and works where a driveway or deck blocks the wall.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Burbank

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, lapped 12 inches at the seams and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and fasten it with termination bar. Cheap fix, big difference.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Burbank

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and often add a dehumidifier to keep relative humidity below 60 percent. Coastal fog and moisture can speed corrosion on exposed fittings, so exterior valves and hose bibs deserve an occasional check. Wood rot slows to a stop. Drier air under the floor also cuts down on the musty smell in the house.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Burbank, CA

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head spraying the wall instead of the plants, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. Then we clean the wall, let it dry and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Walls Waterproofing in Burbank

Access changes the price a lot. Each quote is itemized after we inspect.

    Schluter Kerdi, Custom RedGard and Laticrete Hydro Ban cover most of our showers. Sheet membranes like Kerdi suit tight schedules because there is nothing to cure, while liquids like RedGard and Hydro Ban go on in two coats and need to dry between them. We follow each maker's steps exactly.

    Damp proofing is usually a thin spray-on asphalt coat. It slows soil moisture but cannot hold back water under hydrostatic pressure. Waterproofing uses thicker membranes, drainage boards and footing drains rated to stop liquid water, which is what a basement in wet soil or a high water table needs.

    Crack repairs take a few hours. Interior drain tile usually takes one to three days, and a full exterior dig around the whole house can run three to seven days or more depending on weather and how deep the footing sits. We give you a timeline at the first visit.
