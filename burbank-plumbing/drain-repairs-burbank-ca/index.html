    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Burbank

    Water coming up somewhere other than where you are draining. It is the clearest sign. If the tub fills when the washer drains, or the toilet bubbles when the sink runs, the blockage is past the point where those fixtures join, usually in the main branch or the building drain. We check it on video.

    We install PVC and ABS for most new drain work. Cast iron where needed. Cast iron still makes sense in some multi-story walls where noise matters, and we tie new pipe into existing metal drains with listed transition fittings. Every part meets code.

    Tightening a slip nut or replacing a plastic trap is fair game. A plunger is always worth a try. Skip the caustic cleaners.

    Unclogging a drain does not need one. Bigger changes might. Replacing a stack, relocating a drain or replacing a section of the building drain in Burbank may require a plumbing permit, depending on how much pipe is replaced and how the city defines the work. We pull it when one applies.

    Fixing the cause instead of the clog. Slope matters most. After we correct a sag, replace a rough fitting or add a missing vent, the line stays clear with normal use and a little care about grease. Older lines may still need yearly cleaning.

    Clearing a simple clog takes an hour or two. Pipe work takes longer. Replacing a section of main line or opening a wall to swap a cast iron stack may run a day or longer with access and inspections setting the pace. We give you a timeline with the price.
