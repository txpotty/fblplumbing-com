    Dishwasher Connection With Disposal Installation in Elk Grove

    New disposals come with a knockout plug in the dishwasher inlet. We knock it out with a screwdriver and hammer, fish the plug out of the chamber and connect the hose with a new clamp. Forgetting the plug floods the dishwasher. We also install an air gap or high loop, so dirty sink water cannot flow back into the dishwasher while the disposal is draining.

    Batch-Feed Units for Kitchens Without a Switch in Elk Grove

    These disposals run only with the stopper lid seated, which adds safety for families with kids. They need no wall switch, so they are a good choice for kitchens where adding a switch would mean cutting into finished drywall or tile backsplash. The drain hookup is the usual setup.

    Choosing between Badger and Evolution is where we start based on how you cook, how much noise bothers you and how much cabinet room you have.     Disposal Upgrades in Elk Grove, CA

    Upgrading from an old 1/3 HP unit to something quieter is a common request.     The old unit usually drops free with a turn of the mount ring, once the power is off and the dishwasher hose and discharge tube are disconnected.
