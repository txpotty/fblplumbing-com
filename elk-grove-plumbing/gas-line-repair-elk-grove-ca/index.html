    Gas Line Extensions and Relocations in Elk Grove

    Adding a gas fireplace, moving a laundry room or switching a range from electric to gas usually means extending the gas line. Sizing comes first. We add up the BTU load of every appliance on the system, measure the longest run and pick a pipe size that keeps pressure up at the farthest appliance when everything runs together. After that comes routing, the tie-in and the test.

    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Elk Grove

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. Leaking joints get taken apart, cleaned, resealed with gas-rated compound and tightened, and damaged fittings or valves get replaced. Every repair gets a soap test and a system pressure test before we leave.

    Buried Yard Line Repair and Replacement in Elk Grove, CA

    Many older homes have steel gas lines running from the meter to the house or out to a pool heater. The wrap fails eventually. After the coating breaks down, corrosion starts, and constant moisture from irrigation or poor drainage makes it worse. Utilities get marked first. The new line is polyethylene with tracer wire, or coated steel where it has to be exposed, and we backfill and clean up the yard when the test passes.

    Emergency Gas Line Repair in Elk Grove

    If you smell gas, leave the building, skip the light switches and call your gas utility or 911 from outside. Then call us. After the gas company secures the meter, our plumber finds the leak, repairs it and runs the test the utility needs to see before turning gas back on. Pilots get relit once gas is back. Most emergency repairs are done in a single visit with parts from the truck.

    Gas Permits and Inspections in Elk Grove

    Repairs that change piping, and all new gas lines, usually need a permit and inspection from Elk Grove building officials. We pull the permit, book the inspection and meet the inspector at the gauge so you do not have to. Inspectors often come within the week.     Gas Shut Off by the Utility in Elk Grove

    Gas Leak Detection in Elk Grove
