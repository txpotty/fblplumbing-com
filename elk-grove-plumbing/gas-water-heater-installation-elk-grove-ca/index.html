    Propane Water Heater Installation in Elk Grove

    Propane homes need heaters set up for propane, not natural gas. Different pressure and orifice size mean either a propane model or a factory conversion kit. We work with your supplier. Every joint is leak tested.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in Elk Grove, CA

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Because the exhaust comes out cool and wet, they vent through PVC and need a condensate drain, which changes where the heater can go and what the install involves. Rebates may apply. We install Rheem, Bradford White and AO Smith models.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Installation in Elk Grove, CA

    Rheem gas heaters show up in a lot of local homes, from basic atmospheric tanks to power vent models. FBL Plumbing handles every Rheem gas model. We connect the gas, set the flue and check the Guardian flammable vapor sensor and the combustion air path so the safety features work as designed. Then we test draft.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Elk Grove, CA

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through PVC or other approved pipe. That means the heater can go in a spot with no chimney or vertical flue, and the vent can run sideways out an exterior wall as long as it stays within the length limit. A 120-volt outlet is required. Termination clearances follow the manual.

    Proper Sizing and Venting in Elk Grove, CA

    Sizing and venting decide whether a gas heater runs safe and efficient. We size the heater from your peak use, then match the vent to the manufacturer table and code. Undersized vents spill CO. We test it every time.

    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Elk Grove

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. That sealed setup is a good fit for tight utility rooms and interior closets, since the heater never draws air from the living space. Many need no power. The vent goes through a wall with proper clearances.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Elk Grove

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They are common in older homes and usually the least expensive gas option, but they only work if the vent has the right size, slope and clearance to combustibles. Old vents get checked. Corroded or undersized pipe gets replaced.

    Gas Water Heater Permit and Inspection in Elk Grove

    Gas water heater installs usually need a permit and a final inspection. Plumbing work in Northern California follows the statewide plumbing code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code with state amendments. FBL Plumbing pulls the permit and schedules the inspection in Elk Grove. Inspectors check the vent, clearances, relief line, shutoff and drip leg, so we get those right the first time. Insurers care about it too.
