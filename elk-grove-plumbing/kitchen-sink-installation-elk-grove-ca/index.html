    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Elk Grove

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and a lower gauge means thicker steel. Thin 22 gauge bowls flex and drum when the disposal runs, so we add sound pads or suggest an 18 gauge or heavier sink if you have not bought one yet. Mounting style changes the plumbing. Either way, we hook up the faucet, strainers and disposal.

    Top-mount Sink Installation in Elk Grove

    Drop-in sinks are common in Northern California homes with laminate or tile counters. The rim holds the sink up. We check the cutout, run a bead of silicone under the lip and tighten the mounting clips evenly around the bowl so the rim seats flat. Then the drain and faucet get connected and tested.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Elk Grove

    A double bowl drains both sides through one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. We pick the layout around your disposal and dishwasher, since a disposal on one side changes where the tee can go and how much fall each side gets. We use heavy-wall fittings.

    Stainless Sinks With Sound Pads in Elk Grove

    Cheap stainless bowls are noisy when dishes hit them or the disposal runs. Better sinks come with rubber pads and an undercoating, and if yours does not, we can add a coat of spray undercoating before we set it. It is a small upgrade. After the sink is in place the underside is hard to get at, which is why we do it while the bowl is still sitting out.

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Elk Grove

    Sitting on the counter by their rim, drop-in sinks are a natural fit for tile, laminate and butcher block counters. Usually the fastest upgrade. After checking the cutout, we seal the rim and draw the clips down evenly, and in older homes and rentals it is often a two-hour job.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Elk Grove, CA

    Undermounts hang from clips anchored into the underside of the stone, plus a bead of silicone along the rim. We support the bowl from below with a brace while the silicone cures, usually overnight, before loading it with water. Rushing this causes the classic sag. Just an eighth of an inch of sag leaves a gap that catches water and food along the edge.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Elk Grove, CA

    Stone shops vary on how they mount undermounts. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. Either way, the bowl gets braced. We do not connect the trap until the adhesive has cured.

    Granite and Quartz Composite Sinks in Elk Grove

    Composite sinks are heavier than stainless and usually thicker at the drain, so standard strainers sometimes need a longer threaded body. We check the strainer fit first. When the strainer is too short for the sink thickness, we replace it instead of cranking it down and chipping the drain opening.
