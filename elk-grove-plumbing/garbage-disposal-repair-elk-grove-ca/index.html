    InSinkErator and Other Brand Repairs in Elk Grove, CA

    InSinkErator, Waste King and Moen each use different mounts, so a flange leak is fixed with that brand's kit. InSinkErator uses a three-bolt or Quick Lock style ring, while Waste King uses an EZ Mount twist ring that comes off without tools. We stock the common kits. That keeps most repairs to one visit.

    Noisy Disposal Repair in Elk Grove

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough usually mean trouble inside the unit. A loose mount will rattle. We check the mount, then look for debris, then run the motor empty, since a whine with an empty chamber means the bearings are going.

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Elk Grove

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps lodge between the grind ring and the impeller lugs, and the motor stalls. We shut off power, insert a 1/4 inch hex key into the socket on the bottom of the unit and work it back and forth until the flywheel turns freely. Then the object comes out with pliers. A motor that stalled for a long time may have overheated, so we test it under load before calling the job done.

    Preventive Care and Longevity in Elk Grove, CA

    Run cold water before, during and after grinding, and let it go about 15 seconds after the motor stops so the food clears the trap. Grease poured in with hot water coats the chamber and the pipe, while cold water keeps it solid so the impellers can break it up. Stringy peels cause most jams. Celery, artichoke leaves and corn husks belong in the trash.

    Humming Disposal Repair in Elk Grove

    Humming with no spin usually means something is stuck. Bones, pits, glass and spoons are the usual suspects. We cut power, work the flywheel free with a hex wrench on the bottom of the unit, remove the object and then test the motor and the reset button with water running.

    After-Hours Disposal Service in Elk Grove

    A disposal that quits while you are cooking stops everything, especially if the sink is full of water. Do not try drain chemicals. Drain chemicals can eat at the seals and splash on whoever opens the trap, so leave the water where it is and give us a call.

    Electrical Problems and Resetting in Elk Grove

    No hum at all usually means no power reaching the motor. We start at the red reset button on the bottom, then check the breaker, the wall switch and the outlet or junction box under the sink with a meter. It is wet under a sink. In that damp cabinet, wire nuts corrode and cord connections loosen, so we replace them instead of retightening.

    Grinding Noise Repair in Elk Grove

    A metal-on-metal grind may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. After checking the chamber, snugging the mount and pulling debris, we run it empty to hear if the noise is coming from the motor. Bad bearings mean replacement.
