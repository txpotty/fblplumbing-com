    Frequently Asked Questions About Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Elk Grove

    Accessible copper leak repairs typically cost $250 to $650. Repairs inside finished walls or ceilings usually range from $500 to $1,500 before drywall patching. Slab leak repairs on copper lines often run $1,500 to $4,000.

    Common signs of a copper pipe leak include unexplained wet spots on walls, ceilings or floors, a sudden drop in water pressure, the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use, unusually high water bills or visible blue-green stains on pipes or nearby surfaces that point to corrosion.

    PEX resists the corrosion and scale that cause copper pinholes and is flexible and quick to install. If you have repeated copper leaks, repiping with PEX is usually a cost-effective long-term solution.

    Copper pipe leaks are commonly caused by corrosion, especially from hard or acidic water. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. Other causes include excessive water pressure, improper installation, shifting foundations putting stress on joints and freezing temperatures that can cause pipes to burst. Pinhole leaks are a typical symptom of internal corrosion.

    We work with all standard types and brands of copper piping found in residential and commercial properties, including Type M, Type L and Type K copper pipes. Our repairs use high-quality copper and fittings from reputable manufacturers like Mueller Streamline and Cerro Flow Products.

    Type K, L and M copper pipes differ mainly in their wall thickness and thus their pressure ratings. Type K has the thickest walls and is used for underground service and main water lines. Type L is common for interior water supply and radiant heating. Type M has the thinnest walls and is usually used for less demanding applications. We are skilled in repairing all types.

    For minor, isolated repairs, a permit may not be required. However, if the repair involves replacing a significant section of piping, rerouting lines or working on the main water supply, a plumbing permit from Elk Grove may be necessary to meet local building codes. We will tell you on any permit requirements.
