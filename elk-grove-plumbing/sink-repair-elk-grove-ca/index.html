    Loose or Wobbly Sink Repair in Elk Grove

    A sink that moves when you lean on it is pulling on the drain.     Garbage Disposal Repair in Elk Grove

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Elk Grove, CA

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.
