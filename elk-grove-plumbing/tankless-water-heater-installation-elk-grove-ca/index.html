    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in Elk Grove, CA

    Switching from a tank takes more than unbolting one and hanging the other. A 40-gallon tank burns about 40,000 BTU, but a whole-home tankless unit may need 150,000 to 199,000, so the meter line nearly always gets bigger. Venting changes too. Both are on the estimate.

    Cost-Effective Tankless Conversions in Elk Grove, CA

    A tankless conversion in Elk Grove, CA costs more than a tank swap, mostly because of the gas line upgrade, the new venting and the unit price itself. You earn some of it back each month. With no standby loss and a lifespan that can reach 20 years, many homeowners see the difference on the gas bill within the first few winters. Your quote shows the math.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Elk Grove, CA

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Elk Grove, CA homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. Whole-house electric units pull a lot of current. Some models need two or three 40 to 60 amp double-pole breakers, more than a 100-amp panel can usually spare alongside the range and AC. We check the panel first.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Installation in Elk Grove, CA

    Rinnai has been making tankless heaters for decades, and parts are easy to get. We install both condensing and non-condensing Rinnai models, and the right one depends on where the vent can go and how much you want to spend up front. Non-condensing units vent in stainless. Condensing units vent in PVC and drain condensate.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Installation in Elk Grove

    Sizing a home unit depends on two figures: peak gallons per minute and temperature rise. A shower head flows about 2 gallons per minute and a kitchen faucet a bit less, so a house that runs two showers and a sink together needs roughly 5 to 6 gallons at the rise your inlet water calls for. That narrows the model list fast.

    Residential Tankless System Upgrades in Elk Grove

    Upgrading from a tank in Elk Grove starts with a look at what you have now: tank size, vent type, gas line size and where the unit sits.     Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Elk Grove

    Gas tankless units in Elk Grove are the common pick for whole-house hot water because a single 199,000 BTU burner can feed two showers and a sink at the same time without losing temperature. The catch is gas supply. In most houses, the line from the meter has to be upsized, often from half-inch to three-quarter inch or bigger. We size it and pressure test it before the unit is lit.
