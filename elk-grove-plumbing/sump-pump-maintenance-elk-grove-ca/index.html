    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Elk Grove, CA

    The float switch is the most common reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get stuck.

    Zoeller and Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Elk Grove

    FBL Plumbing is proficient in maintaining all major sump pump brands, including popular models from Zoeller and Wayne.     Battery Backup Pump Maintenance in Elk Grove

    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails. We load test the battery, check the charger and alarm and run the backup pump on its own. Most backup batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Maintenance in Elk Grove

    The cost of sump pump maintenance in Elk Grove, CA typically ranges from $100 to $250, depending on the complexity of your system and any minor repairs needed. This usually includes inspection, cleaning and testing. Major repairs or part replacements would be quoted separately after diagnosis.

    With proper maintenance, a quality sump pump can last 7 to 10 years. Neglecting maintenance can greatly shorten its lifespan, leading to premature failure and costly replacements.

    The best time is a few weeks before your wettest season starts. Homes that see snow may also want a fall check before winter storms and snowmelt.

    Key signs include strange noises like grinding or rattling, the pump running constantly, failing to turn on, visible rust or corrosion, or water pooling in your basement. Don't wait for a flood to contact us.

    Most local codes do not allow sump discharge into the sanitary sewer. We route discharge to the yard, a dry well or another code-approved location.

    If your basement is finished or storms often knock out your power, a backup is a smart choice. We install and maintain battery and water-powered backup systems.

    Watch for a pump that runs constantly, hums without moving water, cycles very often or makes grinding noises. Rust, a stuck float or a musty smell around the pit are also red flag signs.

    If your primary sump pump fails during a power outage and you don't have a working battery backup or water-powered backup system, your basement is at risk of flooding. This underscores the importance of maintaining backup systems.
