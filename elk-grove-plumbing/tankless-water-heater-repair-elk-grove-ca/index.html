    Condensing Tankless Repair in Elk Grove, CA

    Condensing tankless heaters, such as many Navien models, extract extra heat from exhaust and produce condensate. Clogged condensate traps, failed neutralizers and blocked drains can shut the unit down. We clean the trap, replace the neutralizer media and verify proper drainage. PVC venting is also inspected for slope and sagging.

    Tankless Repair vs Replacement Cost in Elk Grove, CA

    Descaling service usually costs $175 to $350, and sensor or igniter repairs run $250 to $600. Heat exchanger replacement can cost $800 to $1,500 or more unless covered by warranty. A new tankless unit installed typically ranges from $3,000 to $5,500. We compare these costs against the age and condition of your unit.

    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Elk Grove

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside the heat exchanger, limiting flow and causing overheating. Many units warn of this with scale codes, such as the LC code on Rinnai models. We connect a circulation pump to the service valves and run a descaling solution through the exchanger. Annual descaling is wise for most homes on hard water.

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Elk Grove

    Tankless heaters use flow sensors to start and thermistors to regulate temperature. A dirty inlet filter or failed sensor causes no hot water, fluctuating temperature or the unit turning off mid-shower. We clean filters, test sensor readings and replace failed parts. Temperature becomes steady again once readings are accurate.
