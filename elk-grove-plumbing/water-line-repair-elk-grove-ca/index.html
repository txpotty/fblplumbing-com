    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Elk Grove

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Copper became standard in many mid-century homes here, and decades later pinhole leaks and corroded fittings are a common repair call. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. We pinpoint the leak with acoustic gear and thermal imaging. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or skip the old under-slab pipe and repipe overhead with PEX. You get a price on every option.

    Water Pressure Restoration in Elk Grove

    Low pressure at every fixture points to the service line or the regulator, not the faucets. We put a gauge on a hose bib first.     PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Elk Grove

    Copper and PEX fail in different places. Copper tends to pinhole where a rock rubs it or where the water chemistry eats the wall from inside. PEX usually fails at a fitting. We cut out the bad section of copper and sweat or press in a new length, and for PEX we use the matching crimp or expansion tool so the joint is made the way the manufacturer intends.

    Main Water Line Repair in Elk Grove

    Your main line is the pipe from the meter to the house, usually 3/4 inch or 1 inch, buried anywhere from a foot to several feet down depending on frost depth. When it fails you tend to see wet ground, a spinning meter and weak pressure at every tap. We start by closing the house shutoff and watching the dial. If it still moves, the leak is buried.
