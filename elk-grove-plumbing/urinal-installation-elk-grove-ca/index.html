    Replacing a urinal on existing lines typically costs $500 to $1,200 including a manual flush valve. New installs with carriers and new drain lines usually range from $1,500 to $3,500, and sensor valves add $250 to $600.

    Flush urinals use a water flush to remove waste, while waterless urinals use a specialized trap system with a sealant liquid to prevent odors without water.     Switching from a 1 gpf urinal to a 0.125 gpf model saves most of the water per flush. In a busy restroom that adds up to tens of thousands of gallons a year.
