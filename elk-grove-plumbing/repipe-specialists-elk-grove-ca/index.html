    Drain Line Repiping in Elk Grove

    While often overlooked, your home's drain lines are just as important as your supply lines. Old cast iron or corroded drain pipes can lead to frequent clogs, slow drainage, and even sewage backups. FBL Plumbing provides complete drain line repiping in Elk Grove, replacing failing waste pipes with new, smooth PVC or ABS lines. This supports efficient waste removal and prevents future drainage problems, protecting your home from foul odors and unsanitary conditions.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Elk Grove

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to three days.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Elk Grove, CA

    Many older homes in Elk Grove, CA still have galvanized steel pipes, which are prone to corrosion, rust, and mineral buildup. This leads to low water pressure, discolored water, and eventual leaks. Our team specializes in the complete removal and replacement of these outdated systems with modern PEX or copper piping. This upgrade not only resolves existing issues but also greatly improves water quality and flow, boosting your home's value and plumbing reliability for decades to come.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Elk Grove

    Kitec, with its orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe and brass fittings, and gray polybutylene pipe are both known for failures. Many homes built in the 1980s through early 2000s contain one or the other. We replace these systems completely so your home is easier to insure and sell. Visible pipe at the water heater often tells us what you have.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Elk Grove

    Copper piping offers excellent durability, corrosion resistance, and a long lifespan, making it a premium choice for many homeowners in Elk Grove. While the upfront cost is higher than PEX, copper provides superior rigidity and resistance to UV light if exposed. Our team meticulously solders each joint, creating a leak-proof and robust plumbing system that can withstand the test of time, including changing temperatures and local water conditions. Copper also inherently inhibits bacterial growth, contributing to cleaner water.
