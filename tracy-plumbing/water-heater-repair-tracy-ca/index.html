    Water Heater Problems We Fix in Tracy, CA

    Pilot Repair in Tracy

    A pilot that lights and then dies when you let go of the knob almost always means a weak thermocouple. Less often it is a dirty flame arrestor screen choking off combustion air. We test the thermocouple output in millivolts. If it reads fine, the gas control valve is next.

    Water Heater Leak Repair in Tracy, CA

    Water on the floor next to the heater needs a closer look before anyone panics. Leaks at the relief valve or a supply fitting are usually cheap to fix. Tank body leaks are different, because a failed inner lining cannot be patched. On a slab floor, a drain pan buys time.

    No Hot Water Diagnostics in Tracy

    Diagnosis starts with fuel. We confirm the gas is on, the breaker is set and the pilot is lit. From there we test the thermostat, element or gas valve one at a time and recommend the cheapest repair that will actually hold up. Simple checks first.

    Gas Burner and Venting Repair in Tracy, CA

    Combustion is the part of a gas heater that matters most for safety. Yellow tips on the flame or soot around the draft hood point to a burner starved for air. We clean the burner and check draft with a mirror or a smoke test. Backdrafting is dangerous.

    Electric Water Heater Element Repair in Tracy

    Lukewarm water or hot water that runs out in five minutes usually means a burned-out element or a stuck thermostat. We check each element with an ohmmeter. Readings tell us whether an element is open or shorted, which explains the breaker trips. Parts for most tanks ride on the truck.

    Tankless Water Heater Repair in Tracy

    Scale is the main enemy of a tankless heater. Mineral buildup on the heat exchanger triggers overheating codes and shutdowns. We descale with a pump and approved solution, clean the inlet filter and check the igniter and flow sensor while the unit is open. All major brands.

    Water Heater Maintenance and Tune-Ups in Tracy, CA

    A yearly tune-up adds years to a tank. We flush the sediment, check the anode rod, test the relief valve and look over the gas or electrical connections. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. Left alone, the sediment hardens until a hose flush cannot break it loose.

    Leaking Water Heater Repair in Tracy, CA

    Not every leak means a dead tank. Relief valve, drain valve and supply line drips are cheap fixes. A leak from the tank body or the bottom seam usually means the inner lining has failed, and that tank needs to be replaced soon. We protect your floors either way.
