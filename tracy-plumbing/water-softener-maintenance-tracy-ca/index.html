    Resin Bed Analysis and Regeneration in Tracy

    The resin bed is the heart of your water softener, where ion exchange occurs. During maintenance, we check the condition of the resin beads and confirm the regeneration cycles are programmed correctly for your household's water usage. Proper resin bed function is key to steady soft water throughout your home in Tracy.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Tracy

    Softener resin can foul with iron, manganese or sediment and lose capacity over time. Chlorine and chloramine in city water also slowly break resin down. We run a resin cleaner through the system and test performance afterward. If the resin is exhausted, we rebed the tank with fresh high-capacity resin, which costs far less than a new system.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Tracy

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, controlling regeneration cycles and water flow. Our maintenance service includes complete diagnostics of the control valve's programming and mechanical components. We check for proper timing, motor function and potential blockages. If issues are found, we can often repair or replace worn parts to restore full function, avoiding the need for a costly full system replacement in Tracy.
