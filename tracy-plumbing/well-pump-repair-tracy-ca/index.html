    Control Box and Surge Repair in Tracy, CA

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. Drought years alternate with very wet years here, so conservation rules and efficient fixtures are a constant part of plumbing decisions. Lightning and power surges damage those parts more than anything else. After testing the capacitor, relay and overload, we replace the bad part and suggest a surge arrestor if the box lacks one. Arrestors are cheap.

    Low Water Pressure Diagnosis in Tracy

    Weak pressure on a well is not always the pump. A clogged sediment filter, a pressure switch set too low or a leak in the yard line can all cause it. In some cases the pump is fine and the well just cannot keep up with demand. We test each one.

    Pressure Tank Problems in Tracy

    Short cycling is the classic pressure tank symptom, with the pump clicking on and off every few seconds while a faucet runs. A waterlogged tank or a ruptured bladder causes most of it. We check the precharge with the tank drained. It should sit about 2 psi below the cut-in pressure, so a 40/60 switch wants roughly 38 psi in the tank.

    Above-Ground Jet Pump Repair in Tracy

    Lost prime is the number one jet pump complaint. Shaft seal leaks and worn impellers come next. We reprime the pump, replace the seal and check the foot valve and suction line for leaks, since one tiny air leak will undo the whole repair. Everything is above ground.

    Jet Pump Repair in Tracy, CA

    Shallow wells and booster systems often use jet pumps. Common problems are loss of prime, a leaking shaft seal and a worn impeller. Our crew in Tracy restores prime, replaces worn parts and checks the suction line for leaks that let air in. Where water levels swing through the year, a falling level can starve the pump and cost it prime.
