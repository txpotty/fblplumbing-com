    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Tracy

    A great pump is useless if the water it moves runs right back toward the foundation. We route rigid PVC discharge pipe out of the basement and extend it at least 10 feet from the house or into a pop-up emitter or dry well. A spring or swing check valve keeps the column of water from dropping back into the pit after every cycle, which protects the motor. Freezing is uncommon along the coast and on valley floors in Northern California, but frosty nights do happen and can crack exposed outdoor pipes. Where lines can freeze, we add a freeze-resistant outlet so ice cannot block the discharge.

    Commercial and Multi-Unit Sump Installations in Tracy

    Office buildings, parking structures and multi-unit properties with below-grade levels usually need duplex sump systems with two pumps and an alternating control panel. The panel switches lead and lag pumps on each cycle so wear stays even, and a high water alarm notifies building staff. We coordinate electrical with your electrician and follow manufacturer specs for basin size and float settings. Property managers get a simple maintenance schedule to keep both pumps exercised.
