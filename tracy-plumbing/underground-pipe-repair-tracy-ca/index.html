    Galvanized and Polybutylene Line Replacement in Tracy

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside until little water gets through. Polybutylene splits instead. If your buried service is either one, patches are usually temporary, which is why we recommend a full swap to PEX or copper threaded along the existing route. You will notice the pressure.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Tracy

    Polybutylene was used in a lot of homes built in the 1980s and early 1990s. It splits suddenly. The gray plastic turns brittle as chlorine in the water attacks it, with failures most common at fittings and tight bends. Patching it just moves the next leak. We use PEX or copper.

    Trenchless Pipe Repair in Tracy

    Trenchless repair replaces or relines buried pipe without opening its full length. Two small pits, usually.     Buried Sewer Lateral Repair in Tracy, CA
