    Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Tracy

    Rain heads pull up to 2.5 gallons per minute each. Add a handheld and a couple of body sprays and you can be running more water than a standard 40-gallon water heater can keep hot for a long shower. We check the heater. Thermostatic valves with volume controls run the outlets.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Tracy

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. The generator goes within the maker's distance limit, and we plumb its supply, drain and steam line to the head. An electrician runs the circuit. A sealed enclosure with a sloped ceiling keeps drips off you.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Tracy

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on. Valve, head, handheld and drain go in first, then the pan is sloped a quarter inch per foot to the drain. Testing follows. We pressure-test the valve and flood the pan for a day before tile.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Tracy, CA

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. The tub comes out, the drain shifts to the pan location and the valve moves up to about 48 inches. Slab homes need concrete work. No more stepping over a tub wall.

    Shower Valve and Fixture Installation in Tracy, CA

    Pressure-balancing valves are the baseline for any new shower. Thermostatic valves hold a set temperature and add volume control, which works well with rain heads and handhelds. Either one protects against scalding. We help you choose based on how many outlets you plan.

    Custom Shower System Installation in Tracy, CA

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. One head works fine off a standard valve, while multiple outlets need a thermostatic valve, volume controls and larger 3/4-inch supply lines. Layout comes first. Then we check whether the water heater can keep up.

    Tub and Shower Combo Installation in Tracy

    Setting a tub is mostly about level and support. Acrylic and steel tubs get mortar or foam underneath, while cast iron rests on a level ledger, which keeps the caulk line intact. The valve goes in next. Spout and head go at the heights you choose, and the overflow gasket gets a water test.

    Custom Shower Enclosure Plumbing in Tracy

    Body sprays and steam units add lines and planning. Every body spray needs a supply line, and the sizing has to be right so the farthest outlet still has good pressure. Pressure varies by house. A gauge reading comes first.
