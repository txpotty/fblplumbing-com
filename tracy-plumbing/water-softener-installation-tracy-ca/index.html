    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Tracy, CA

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. Putting a carbon tank in front of the softener takes out chlorine and keeps it from wearing down the resin. Taste improves fast. We install twin-tank setups or dual-stage units.

    Softener Loop Installation in Tracy

    No loop? A softener loop is a pair of capped stubs on the main line, usually in the garage, that lets a softener be connected without cutting pipe later. We add the loop. Where we can, we keep hose bibs and irrigation on hard water to save salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Tracy

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet, and many can also feed a refrigerator water line or an ice maker. It complements a softener. Filters get changed on schedule.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Tracy

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. Install follows the manufacturer manual for bypass, drain and overflow, then we set hardness and regeneration. Bring your own unit. Programming is part of the job.

    Commercial Water Softener Installation in Tracy, CA

    Restaurants, hotels and laundromats feel hard water in their equipment first. We install bigger tanks or twin alternating units for businesses, which keeps soft water flowing during regeneration. Equipment lasts longer. We schedule around your hours.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Tracy

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. That protects every faucet, shower, water heater and appliance downstream, which is where hard water does most of its damage over the years. We size before we sell. Hardness and household use set the grain capacity.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Tracy, CA

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. They are simple to program, easy to rebuild and parts are stocked almost everywhere, which matters when a seal fails ten years in. We program yours to your water. We confirm the setup before leaving.
