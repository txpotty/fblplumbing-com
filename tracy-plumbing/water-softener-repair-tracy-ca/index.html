    Control Valve Repair in Tracy

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, directing the flow of water and starting regeneration cycles. When it malfunctions, your system can stop softening water or waste salt. FBL Plumbing technicians are experts at diagnosing and repairing common control valve issues, whether mechanical or electronic, ensuring precise timing and efficient operation. We stock common replacement parts to speed your repair in Tracy.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Tracy, CA

    A salt bridge, a crust of salt that forms in the brine tank, can prevent your water softener from regenerating properly. This leads to hard water throughout your home. Our team can safely break up and remove salt bridges, clean the brine tank, and advise on best practices to prevent future occurrences. We also repair leaks or damage to the brine tank itself, common in older systems.

    Water Softener Leak Repair in Tracy, CA

    Leaks in a water softener can range from minor drips around fittings to significant puddles from a cracked tank or faulty valve. These leaks can cause water damage and waste water. Our plumbers promptly pinpoint the source of leaks, whether from seals, O-rings, hoses, or internal components, and perform lasting repairs. We use appropriate sealing compounds and replacement parts for a lasting fix in Tracy, CA.
