    Service Line Replacement Choices in Manteca, CA

    PEX Service Lines for Long Runs in Manteca

    We buy PEX in long coils. That makes it the easy pick when the meter sits out at the road and the house is set far back, because one coil covers the whole run without a joint. Fewer joints underground means fewer places a leak can start, and it is the joints that usually fail first.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Manteca, CA

    Bursting fits old cast iron or galvanized lines that will shatter under the head. Pulling suits softer pipe like poly or copper. Either way the new pipe follows the old path, which matters when the route runs under a patio or a neighbor's fence, and the yard work shrinks to two pits. Rock can stop it.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Manteca

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. Scale and corrosion are not a problem with it. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, connect it with brass or poly fittings that are approved underground, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Upgrades in Manteca, CA

    Street pressure varies a lot from one neighborhood to the next. Code says no more than 80 psi inside the house. If your new line comes in above that, we add a pressure reducing valve right after the main shutoff, set it around 55 to 65 psi, and replace the gate valve with a quarter-turn ball valve. A quarter turn and the water is off.

    Damage Assessment Before Replacement in Manteca

    We start at the meter. If the little dial on the meter is spinning with every fixture off, water is leaving somewhere. From there we listen along the line with an acoustic ground mic, check the house side for a toilet or softener running, and sometimes push a camera into the line when we suspect root damage or a crushed section, so the hole goes in the right spot.
