    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Manteca

    High static pressure shortens the life of the service line and every fixture behind it. Most codes cap it at 80 psi.     A ground microphone picks up the hiss of water escaping a pressurized pipe, even through a couple of feet of dirt. Some leaks barely make a sound.
