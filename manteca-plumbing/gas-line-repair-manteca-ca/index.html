    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Manteca

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. We back out the leaking joint, clean the threads, apply gas-rated thread sealant and reassemble it, or replace the fitting or valve if the threads are damaged. Every repair gets a soap test and a system pressure test before we leave.

    Gas Permits and Inspections in Manteca

    Repairs that change piping, and all new gas lines, usually need a permit and inspection from Manteca building officials. We file the permit, schedule the inspection and are on site when the inspector checks the work and the test gauge. Inspection usually happens within a few days. Once it passes, the utility can restore service and we come back to relight appliances if needed.

    Gas Appliance Connector Repair in Manteca, CA

    Weak flames or a smell near one appliance often point to the connector or shutoff behind it. Older uncoated brass connectors can crack at the flare nut, and they are no longer accepted by most codes or utilities. New coated connectors go in. Each appliance also gets a shutoff you can reach and a sediment trap where required, and we check that the connector length and diameter match the appliance rating.

    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Manteca, CA

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. Pool heaters are the big load, often 250,000 to 400,000 BTU, so a line that was fine for a grill can starve the heater and the house appliances at the same time. Sizing is the first thing we check. Repairs and new runs are tested, buried at code depth and marked with tracer wire.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Manteca

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Expansive clay soils are common across Northern California, and seasonal swelling and shrinking can shift slabs and buried sewer laterals. We replace corroded sections with polyethylene pipe laid with tracer wire, using an approved transition riser where the line comes up out of the ground at the house. Above-ground runs stay in steel. We test before backfilling so any problem joint is fixed while the trench is still open.

    Permits and Code Compliance for Gas Work in Manteca, CA

Inspection covers sizing, approved materials, pipe supports, shutoffs, drip legs and a witnessed or documented pressure test. The permit is on us. Doing it this way keeps your insurance clean and gives you a record of the repair if you sell the house later.
