    Wayne Sump Pump Replacement in Manteca

    Wayne makes everything from a thermoplastic quarter-horse pump at the hardware store to cast iron models with vertical switches, and the cheap ones are what we replace most. Most dead Wayne pumps we see have a cooked motor or a float tangled in the power cord. You get a matching Wayne or a sturdier cast iron model, your call. Figure on a couple of hours.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Manteca

    If your pump runs every 30 seconds during a storm and still loses ground, it is undersized for the water coming in. We time the cycles and measure how fast the pit refills. The rest is simple arithmetic. A half-horse or three-quarter-horse pump with a higher gallons-per-minute rating at your lift height may be the answer, sometimes paired with a 2-inch discharge.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Manteca, CA

    A pit that is filling with the pump silent means you have maybe an hour or two before water crosses the basement floor, depending on how fast it is coming in. Check the breaker and the GFCI outlet first, then lift the lid and see whether the float is stuck. A shop vac or a bucket can hold the level down. We stock the common pumps on the truck, and most emergency swaps are done on the first trip.

    Cast Iron Submersible Pumps in Manteca

    Cast iron sheds motor heat into the water around it far better than a plastic housing does, which is a big part of why a Zoeller M53 or Liberty 250 outlasts a hardware-store pump. Figure on an hour or two for a typical swap. Before we set it, we make sure the float can swing freely without catching the wall.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Manteca

    Tree roots seek out sewer laterals during dry spells, which is why heavy rains often reveal cracked lines through sudden backups. A primary pump on household current does nothing once the breaker panel goes dark. The fix is a second, 12-volt pump on its own float, set a little higher than the main pump and wired to a charger and deep-cycle battery. It also covers a stuck primary switch. A high-water alarm lets you know the backup has taken over.

    Elevator and Garage Pit Pumps in Manteca

    Elevator pits and parking garage sumps often require oil-sensing controls that stop the pump from sending hydraulic fluid or motor oil into the storm drain. Pumps and oil-sensing panels get replaced to code. Two alternating pumps on one controller mean one can fail without flooding the pit. Some insurance carriers ask for test records.

    Zoeller Switch and Pump Repair in Manteca

    Zoeller units with a piggyback float plug the pump cord into the switch cord, and if that piggyback plug fails the pump never gets power. We test that first. Some calls only need a new switch, while others need the whole pump. We tell you which before we sell you anything.

    Pedestal Pump Service in Manteca, CA

    Louder, yes, but a pedestal motor sits high and dry, so in a narrow pit it often outlasts a submersible by several years. Narrow pits favor pedestals. We replace worn units and set the float rod stops for a proper cycle. A submersible is worth a look when the pit is wide enough.
