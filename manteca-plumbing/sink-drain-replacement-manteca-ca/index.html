    Leaking P-Trap Replacement in Manteca

    Traps leak at the joints first. The beveled washers inside the slip nuts flatten and harden over the years, and a trap that has been taken apart several times to clear clogs often has stripped plastic threads or a cracked nut. Trap kits are cheap. Labor is most of the bill.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Replacement in Manteca

    Kitchen drains start failing at the basket strainer. Old strainer putty dries out and the locknut rusts, so water runs down the tailpiece and looks like a trap leak. We replace the strainer and trap together. Disposal sinks get a new flange instead.

    Utility and Basement Sink Drain Replacement in Manteca

    Basement sinks often drain to a pump. If your laundry or utility sink sits below the sewer line, its drain runs to a sink pump or a sewage ejector, and we check the check valve and the discharge line when we replace the trap. Pump trouble is easy to miss. A gurgling pit is a clue.

    Commercial Lavatory Drain Replacement in Manteca

    Public restroom drains take constant use. Offset grid drains and heavy traps go in, with insulated covers where accessibility rules apply. Work can happen after hours. Inspectors get a clean, labeled job.

    Hard-Water Drain Damage Repair in Manteca, CA

    Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe.
