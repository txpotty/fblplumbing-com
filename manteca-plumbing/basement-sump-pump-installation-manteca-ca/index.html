    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Manteca

    Older homes often have narrow pits that will not fit a submersible. A pedestal pump fits where nothing else will, and we install it with rigid discharge, a quality check valve, and a float rod set to your pit depth so it starts before water reaches the top of the liner. Noisy, but they last.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Manteca

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the water. That keeps it dry and easy to reach, which is why they suit narrow pits under 14 inches across, and why they often outlast cheap submersibles in Manteca homes. Expect more noise.

    Rigid PVC carries the water from the pump out through the rim joist and at least 10 feet from the house.
