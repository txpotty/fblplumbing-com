    Water Heater Repair and Installs in Manteca

    A water heater works hardest in winter, when incoming water is coldest.     Slab Leak Location and Repair in Manteca

    Slab leaks hide under concrete until something gives them away. Many older homes here sit on raised foundations with crawlspaces, which make supply and drain repairs more accessible than slab work.     Pressure Regulators and Shutoff Valves in Manteca, CA

    Water pressure over 80 psi wears out hoses and valves early.
