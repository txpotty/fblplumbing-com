    Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in Manteca

    Pinholes form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. Hot water speeds it up. They often appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher, which we fix by replacing that length with heavier pipe. One usually means more are coming.

    Copper Fitting and Valve Repair in Manteca

    Angle stops, shutoff valves and copper fittings under sinks and behind toilets can weep or seize. Scale locks them up. Hard water buildup inside a valve can keep it from closing when you need it most, so we replace failing valves and fittings with quarter-turn ball valves that shut off fully and are easy to turn. We test each one.

    Preventive Copper Pipe Maintenance in Manteca, CA

    Copper gives warning signs before it leaks. Most people miss them.
