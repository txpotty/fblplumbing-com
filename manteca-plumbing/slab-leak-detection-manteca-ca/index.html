    Pressure Testing and Isolation in Manteca

    Pressure isolation is always the first step. Our tech closes the main, caps or valves off the hot and cold sides, and puts a gauge on each, then watches which side loses pressure over a set time.     Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Manteca, CA

    Hot water leaking under the slab warms the floor above it.     Supply and Drain Leaks Under the Slab in Manteca, CA

    A supply leak and a drain leak show different signs. Supply leaks are pressurized, so they move the meter, warm the floor on the hot side and run whether or not anyone uses water. Drain leaks only leak when water goes down, showing up as smells, sluggish drains or wet spots near bathrooms. Each needs its own test in your Manteca, CA home.
