    Shower Valve & Trim Installation in Manteca, CA

    The shower valve is the heart of your shower's operation, controlling water temperature and flow. FBL Plumbing installs all types of shower valves, including pressure-balancing, thermostatic, and diverter valves. We also install new trim kits, including handles, spouts, and showerheads. Proper valve installation prevents scalding and ensures consistent water temperature, a key comfort feature for your home in Manteca.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Manteca, CA

    Converting an old tub to a walk-in shower is one of the most requested bathroom upgrades. We remove the tub, relocate or resize the drain and install a new shower valve at the right height. On slab foundations, the drain may need to be moved, which involves cutting concrete. The result is a safer, more usable shower.

    Shower Valve & Fixture Installation in Manteca, CA

    Beyond the main shower unit, we also install new shower valves, diverters, and various fixtures. This includes pressure-balancing valves to prevent scalding, thermostatic valves for consistent temperature, and different showerhead types like rain showers or handheld units. Our team ensures all connections are watertight and that the fixtures operate smoothly, enhancing your showering experience. We can upgrade outdated fixtures to newer, more efficient models.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Manteca

    Curbless showers have no step, which makes them easier to use and gives a modern look. They require a recessed floor and careful drain placement, often with a linear drain. On a slab, we may need to lower the floor in the shower area. We work closely with your contractor to plan the slope and waterproof layers.

    Standard Shower & Tub Installation in Manteca

    FBL Plumbing handles the complete installation of standard shower and tub combinations. This includes setting the tub, installing the shower valve, connecting the showerhead and spout, and ensuring proper drainage. We work with various materials like fiberglass, acrylic, and cast iron, making sure the unit is level and securely sealed to prevent leaks, a common issue with older installations that were not done correctly. Our process accounts for existing plumbing layouts to minimize wall alterations.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Manteca

    Steam showers need a steam generator, a dedicated water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator, plumb the supply and drain, and connect the steam head. A licensed electrician handles the circuit. Steam showers also need sealed enclosures and angled ceilings.
