    Bladder Pressure Tank Replacement in West Sacramento

    Bladder tanks hold water inside a flexible butyl bag surrounded by pressurized air. When the bag ruptures, water fills the whole tank, there is no air cushion and the pump short cycles. A simple test is pressing the air valve on top, since water spitting out means the bladder has failed. We replace the tank, set the new pre-charge and confirm the pump runs a full cycle. Most bladder tank swaps take about two to three hours.

    Galvanized Steel Pressure Tank Upgrades in West Sacramento

    Many older homes in West Sacramento still have traditional galvanized steel pressure tanks, which can corrode over time and lose their air charge. We offer professional upgrades from these older tanks to more efficient and durable bladder-style tanks. This not only improves water pressure stability but also extends the life of your well pump by reducing short-cycling.

    Pressure Tank Sizing and Upsizing in West Sacramento

    Many older systems have a tank that is too small for the pump, so the motor starts every time someone washes their hands. Tank size is based on drawdown, which is the available water stored between cut-out and cut-in pressure. For example, a 10 gallon per minute pump usually needs at least 10 gallons of drawdown for a one minute run time. That often means an 80 to 119 gallon tank rather than the 20 gallon unit that came with the home. Upsizing is one of the best ways to extend pump life.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in West Sacramento, CA

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop, typically at 30/50 or 40/60 psi. Pitted contacts, insect nests and a clogged sensing nipple are frequent causes of failure in well houses.
