    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in West Sacramento, CA

    The float switch is the most common reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get stuck.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in West Sacramento

    Strong winter storms can bring days of heavy rain to Northern California, saturating ground and pushing water into basements and sewer lines. We test your pump ahead of the rainy season by filling the pit until the float trips. We watch the pump empty the basin, listen for grinding bearings and measure how fast it cycles. If the pump struggles, you still have time to fix it before the first big storm.
