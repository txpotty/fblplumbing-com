    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in West Sacramento

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to four days.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in West Sacramento, CA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and highly durable piping material that's gaining popularity for repiping projects. FBL Plumbing specializes in PEX pipe replacement in West Sacramento, CA, offering a system that resists freezing, corrosion, and scale buildup. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points. PEX also significantly dampens water hammer noise, providing a quieter plumbing system for your home.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Repipe Specialists in West Sacramento

    The cost of a whole-home repipe in West Sacramento varies significantly depending on the size of your home, the number of fixtures, and the chosen material (PEX or copper). Generally, you can expect to pay between $4,000 and $15,000 for a PEX repipe, and $8,000 to $25,000 or more for copper. We provide a detailed, itemized quote after an on-site assessment.

    Common signs you need repiping include persistent low water pressure throughout your home, discolored or rusty-looking water, frequent pipe leaks, visible corrosion on exposed pipes, and a metallic taste or odor in your water. If your home is over 50 years old with original pipes, it's a good idea to have them inspected, especially if they are galvanized or polybutylene.

    Repeated slab leaks, pinhole leaks, low pressure, discolored water or polybutylene, Kitec or galvanized pipe are all signs. Frequent leak repairs are the strongest signal.

    Yes, whole-home repipes require a permit and inspection in most Northern California jurisdictions. We pull the permit and schedule inspections.

    We primarily install quality copper piping from reputable manufacturers like Mueller Streamline and Cerro, known for their durability and purity. For PEX systems, we often use leading brands such as Uponor (Wirsbo Aquapex), Zurn PEX, or Viega PEX, chosen for their proven reliability and performance in residential applications.

    Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running hard. Hard water can lead to mineral buildup, or scale, inside pipes over time. This buildup constricts water flow, reduces pressure, and can speed up corrosion, especially in older metal pipes. Repiping with materials like PEX or properly installed copper, along with considering a water softener, can mitigate these effects and extend the life of your plumbing system.

    We commonly use Uponor PEX-A with ProPEX fittings and Type L copper from domestic mills. We also use Viega ProPress fittings for copper work.

    Copper is a rigid, durable metal with a long lifespan, known for its resistance to bacteria. PEX is a flexible plastic tubing that is more affordable, easier to install, resistant to freezing and corrosion, and very quiet. Both are excellent choices for repiping, and our specialists can help you decide which material is best suited for your home and budget in West Sacramento, CA.
