    Corroded Pipe Replacement in West Sacramento, CA

This restores proper water flow and ensures clean, fresh water throughout your home.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in West Sacramento, CA

    Many older homes in West Sacramento, CA still have galvanized steel pipes, which are vulnerable to rust and corrosion on the inside. This leads to reduced water pressure, discolored water, and eventual leaks. FBL Plumbing provides complete galvanized pipe replacement services, removing these failing pipes and installing new, modern materials. Upgrading from galvanized pipes not only improves your water quality and pressure but also shields your home from potential water damage caused by deteriorating plumbing.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in West Sacramento, CA

    Cast iron drain pipe in older homes can rust through at the bottom, while some ABS from the 1980s was made with defective resin that cracks. Signs include sewer odors, slow drains and stains on ceilings below upstairs bathrooms. We camera the line first, then replace accessible sections with PVC or ABS.     PEX Repiping in West Sacramento

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale buildup and expands slightly if water freezes, which helps in colder homes. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe for stronger flow. Systems can be run as a home-run manifold with a shutoff for each fixture or as a trunk and branch layout. PEX installs faster than copper with far fewer fittings in the walls. It is our most requested repipe material.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair and Replacement in West Sacramento

    Slab leaks, where pipes under your concrete foundation leak, are a serious concern for homeowners in West Sacramento. These leaks can cause foundation damage, mold growth, and wasted water. FBL Plumbing uses modern leak detection technology to pinpoint the exact location of the leak. Depending on the severity and accessibility, we offer targeted pipe repair or rerouting and replacement solutions to permanently resolve the issue, protecting your home's structural integrity.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in West Sacramento, CA

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. They are caused by a mix of water chemistry, high velocity and thin Type M pipe, and once they begin they tend to keep coming back. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes. We help you weigh the cost of both paths.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in West Sacramento

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping has become a common choice for residential pipe replacement due to its flexibility, affordability, and resistance to freezing. Freezing is uncommon along the coast and on valley floors in Northern California, but frosty nights do happen and can crack unprotected outdoor pipes. Unlike rigid pipes, PEX can expand, reducing the risk of burst pipes during unexpected cold snaps.
