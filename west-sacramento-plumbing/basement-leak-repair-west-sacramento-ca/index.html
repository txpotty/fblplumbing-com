    Crack Injection for Basements in West Sacramento

    Injection works on poured concrete walls, not block. We set ports along the crack, seal the face with epoxy paste, and pump in polyurethane or epoxy from the bottom port up until resin shows at the next port. Epoxy adds strength. For a crack that is actively wet, polyurethane works better since it reacts with water.

    Drain and Sewer Pipe Leak Repair in West Sacramento, CA

    Cast iron drain stacks in older homes crack and scale from the inside out. A wet spot at the base of the stack or a crack along the hub is common, and we replace the damaged section with PVC or new cast iron joined with no-hub couplings. Plastic joints can fail too. A separated PVC joint usually means the pipe was not fully seated or not supported.

    Floor Drain and Backwater Valve Repair in West Sacramento

    A floor drain that backs up when the washer drains or during a storm usually points to a blockage downstream or a failed backwater valve. After clearing the line, we run a camera to confirm it is open. In homes where the street sewer backs up in storms, a backwater valve stops sewage from rising through the floor drain. Dry traps smell.

    Interior Drainage Systems in West Sacramento

    Where digging outside is not practical, we handle the water from inside instead. We cut the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and slope it to a new or existing sump pit before patching the floor with fresh concrete. The wall gets a dimpled membrane. Seepage runs down behind it and into the pipe.

    Wall and Slab Seepage Repair in West Sacramento

    Seepage at the wall or the slab joint is usually surface water pooling against the foundation. Expansive clay soils are common across Northern California, and seasonal swelling and shrinking can crack slabs and buried sewer laterals. Before anyone cuts the floor, we check the gutters, downspout extensions and the grade along that wall, because fixing those outside often stops seepage for a fraction of the cost. We always try the cheap fixes first.

    Wall Leak Solutions in West Sacramento, CA

    Damp walls without a crack often point to water soaking through porous block or concrete. A simple foil test tells you whether it is seepage or condensation: tape a square of foil to the wall for a day and see which side gets wet. Condensation needs a dehumidifier. Real seepage calls for better drainage or an interior channel.

    Hydrostatic Pressure Management in West Sacramento, CA

    Water pressing against the outside of a basement wall works into the weakest spot, usually the cove joint where wall meets floor. Strong winter storms can bring days of heavy rain to Northern California, saturating ground and pushing water into crawlspaces and sewer lines. An interior drain channel along that joint relieves the pressure by giving the water a path to the sump. No pressure, no seepage.

    Sump Pump Installation & Repair in West Sacramento

    A sump pump sits in a pit below the slab and kicks on when a float rises. Most failures come from a stuck float switch, a burned-out motor, or a check valve that lets the discharge water fall back into the pit and short-cycles the pump. We test yours with a bucket. A battery backup is worth adding where power drops in storms.
