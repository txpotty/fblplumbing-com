    Burst and Leaking Pipe Repair in West Sacramento

    Pipes burst from freezing, high water pressure, corrosion or a failed fitting. Hard or aggressive water can cause pinhole leaks in copper that suddenly widen into a spray. We shut off water, find the damaged section and replace it with copper or PEX. We also check your water pressure, since pressure above 80 PSI strains the whole system.

    Slab Leak Detection & Repair in West Sacramento, CA

    Slab leaks can cause significant structural damage and high water bills. Many older homes here sit on raised foundations with crawlspaces, which make supply and drain repairs more accessible than slab work. Our emergency team uses acoustic leak detection and thermal imaging to locate the exact spot of the leak under your West Sacramento home's foundation. We then provide options for repair, which may include direct slab penetration, re-routing or epoxy pipe lining, always following local building codes for a lasting solution.

    Emergency Slab Leak Response in West Sacramento, CA

    In homes built on concrete slabs, leaks under the slab can show up as warm spots on the floor, a running meter or the sound of water with everything off. We isolate the hot or cold side so you can keep partial water service. Then we locate the leak with acoustic and thermal equipment. Repair options include direct access through the slab, rerouting the line through walls or attic, or a partial repiping.

    Overflowing Toilets and Fixture Emergencies in West Sacramento

    A toilet that overflows repeatedly or a faucet that will not shut off can flood a bathroom fast. We clear the clog, replace failed fill valves, flappers or cartridges and check the supply stop. If the fixture shutoff is seized, which is common with older multi-turn valves, we replace it with a quarter-turn stop. That gives you dependable control next time.

    Sewer Line Backups in West Sacramento, CA

    Sewer line backups are urgent plumbing emergencies that can lead to hazardous wastewater entering your home. These are often caused by tree root intrusion, pipe corrosion or major blockages. Our team uses camera inspection technology to pinpoint the exact problem in your West Sacramento sewer line and offers prompt solutions, from snaking to trenchless repair, to protect your family's health and property.

    Severe Drain Clog Clearing in West Sacramento, CA
