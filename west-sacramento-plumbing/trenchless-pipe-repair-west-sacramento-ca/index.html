    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in West Sacramento, CA

    Pipe bursting is another trenchless method used for replacing badly damaged pipes. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is excellent for completely replacing a pipe segment with a new, stronger material, such as HDPE. It's often chosen when the existing pipe is collapsed or too damaged for lining. FBL Plumbing uses specialized bursting heads and winches for a smooth and efficient replacement, reducing the overall project time and cost for homeowners in West Sacramento, CA.
