    Toilet Flange Repair During Replacement in West Sacramento

    Pulling the old toilet is the only chance to see the flange, and a cracked plastic ring or a rusted cast iron flange will ruin a new install within a year. We check it every time.     One-Piece Toilet Replacement in West Sacramento, CA

    Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in West Sacramento, CA

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Measure to the closet bolts, not the baseboard.
