    Softener Loop Installation in West Sacramento, CA

    Many homes have a softener loop near the main water line that makes for easy installation. When a home lacks one, we can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. We also route a drain line to a laundry box, floor drain or approved location. A good loop keeps outdoor hose bibs on hard water to save salt. This makes future service easier.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in West Sacramento, CA

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and proven solution for hard water in Northern California. When replacing these units, we carefully disconnect the old brine tank and resin tank, then install the new components. We make sure all connections are leak-free and the drain line is properly routed. We will also provide tips on initial setup and salt refills for lasting soft water.

    Clack Water Softener Replacement in West Sacramento

    Clack valves are a popular choice for their reliability and customizable settings. If you have an older Clack system failing, FBL Plumbing offers professional Clack water softener replacement in West Sacramento. We can replace just the control valve or the entire system, ensuring compatibility with your existing setup and configuring the new unit for peak performance.

    Metered Softener Replacement in West Sacramento

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly used up. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first month.

    Water Softener Failure Diagnosis in West Sacramento, CA

    Before replacing, we check whether your softener has a simple fix like a clogged injector, failed motor or wrong settings. If the valve is obsolete or parts are no longer available, replacement is usually the better value. We test hardness before and after the softener to confirm the problem.     Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in West Sacramento

    A new, properly sized water softener can sometimes improve water pressure if your old unit was undersized or had heavy mineral buildup restricting flow. However, a water softener's main job is to remove hardness minerals, not to boost pressure. If low water pressure is your main concern, we can investigate other causes.

    New water softeners usually come with manufacturer warranties ranging from 1 to 10 years on different components, such as the control valve and resin tank. FBL Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work for your peace of mind. Exact warranty details will be provided with your quote.

    Common signs include a return of hard water symptoms like scale buildup on fixtures, dry skin and hair, dingy laundry or excessive soap use. Other signs are a clear drop in water pressure, leaks from the unit, or a softener over 10 to 15 years old that needs frequent repairs.

    New water softeners usually come with two types of warranties. We back our work with a solid warranty for your peace of mind.

    To extend the life of your new water softener, use the correct type of salt, if applicable, and keep the brine tank filled. Regular maintenance, such as cleaning the brine tank and periodic inspections by a professional, can also help protect its longevity and efficiency.

    In many areas, a plumbing permit is not usually required for a like-for-like water softener replacement, especially if there are no significant changes to the existing plumbing lines.
