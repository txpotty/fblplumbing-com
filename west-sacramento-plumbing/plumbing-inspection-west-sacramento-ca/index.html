    Water Quality & Pipe Material Inspection in West Sacramento, CA

    We inspect your plumbing for issues related to local water quality and common pipe materials. Hard water can lead to mineral buildup, reducing pipe diameter and appliance efficiency. Our inspection helps identify pipes at risk and recommend appropriate solutions.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in West Sacramento, CA

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. We test angle stops and the main shutoff valve, which often seize in hard water. We also check for proper venting and gurgling drains. Small fixes like these are easy to plan for once you know they exist.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in West Sacramento

    We check the water heater age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. We confirm proper venting, gas connections, expansion tanks and drain pans. Softeners and filtration systems get a basic function check and hardness test. Hard water shortens heater life, so these checks matter.

    Water Heater Inspections in West Sacramento

    We carefully inspect all types of water heaters, including tankless, electric, and gas models.     Leak Detection and Slab Screening in West Sacramento

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. We test the meter, check pressure loss and inspect for warm spots, damp baseboards and mildew.
