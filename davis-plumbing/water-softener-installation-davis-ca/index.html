    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in Davis

    Most water softener installations cost $1,500 to $4,000 including equipment. Adding a new softener loop typically adds $500 to $1,500 depending on access.

    Control valves usually carry 5 years of coverage, and tanks often carry 10 years or more. We also warranty our installation work.

    Installing a water softener offers many benefits, including preventing scale buildup in pipes and appliances, extending appliance lifespan, reducing soap and detergent usage and improving the feel of your skin and hair. It also makes cleaning easier and keeps clothes looking brighter.

    We install softeners with Fleck and Clack control valves and other reliable brands. We recommend systems based on performance and serviceability.

    With proper maintenance, a quality water softener system can last between 10 and 15 years, and sometimes even longer. The lifespan depends on the quality of the unit, the hardness of your water and how well it is maintained. Regular servicing can help lengthen its life.

    Salt consumption varies significantly depending on the water hardness, your household's water usage and the efficiency of the softener system. On average, a family of four in a hard water area might use 40 to 80 pounds of salt per month. High-efficiency models typically use less salt.

    Maintenance mainly involves periodically adding salt to salt-based systems, typically every 4 to 8 weeks depending on water usage and hardness. It's also good practice to schedule annual check-ups with a professional plumber to ensure the system is operating efficiently and to address any potential issues.

    In many areas, a plumbing permit is required for significant plumbing alterations like water softener installation to confirm compliance with local codes. FBL Plumbing will advise you on any required permits for your Davis installation and can assist with the application process.
