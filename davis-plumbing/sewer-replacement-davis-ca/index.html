    What Goes Into a Sewer Replacement in Davis, CA

    Emergency Sewer Line Replacement in Davis, CA

    Sewage coming up a floor drain or tub is an emergency. Stop running water in the house right away. We open the cleanout to relieve the backup, run a camera to find the break, and if the line has collapsed we dig to it the same day, putting in a section of new pipe to get you flowing before planning the full replacement.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Davis, CA

    A lot of older houses have no outside cleanout. Every clog then means pulling a toilet. When we replace a sewer line we add a two-way cleanout near the foundation, with a cap at ground level you can find, so future cleaning or camera work is quick and cheap.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Davis

    An open trench gives full access to the pipe from the house to the main. We dig with a mini excavator and hand dig near utility marks. The old pipe comes out, the trench bottom gets a bed of gravel or sand, and new 4-inch PVC goes in at a steady quarter inch of fall per foot, checked with a level before backfill. Then the camera.

    Residential Sewer Replacement in Davis, CA

    Most house laterals run 30 to 100 feet. Some are two feet down, some are eight. We measure both before quoting, check whether the run passes under a driveway, porch or tree, and plan the method around what sits on top of the pipe, so the estimate you sign is the price you pay.

    Sewer Line Repair vs. Full Replacement in Davis

    We look at the whole line before recommending either. A single bad joint can usually be dug and fixed. Breaks every few feet, roots at every joint, or a belly that holds water for twenty feet mean the rest of the pipe is close behind, and in that case a full replacement saves you from paying to dig the same yard twice.

    Clay and Orangeburg Pipe Replacement in Davis, CA

    Clay pipe came in short sections with a joint every two or three feet. That is a lot of joints for roots to find. Orangeburg is worse, since the tar-paper wall flattens slowly under the dirt above it, and both are good candidates for bursting because they break apart easily. Fast pulls.

    Sewer Scope Inspections for Replacement in Davis

    A sewer camera on a push cable goes in through the cleanout. The footage shows each joint, crack and root intrusion. A small transmitter called a sonde sits behind the camera head, so we can find its exact spot and depth from the yard above, which keeps the digging small. Bring questions.

    Commercial Sewer Line Replacement in Davis

    Commercial laterals are often 6-inch pipe and carry grease or heavier flows. There is often a grease trap or interceptor to work around. We schedule the work around your hours, set up temporary bypass pumping when the building needs to stay open, and coordinate with the city on any work at the tap. Weekend starts are common.

    Our crews work on sewer lines across Davis every week. Neighbors usually have the same pipe material, since streets were built together.
