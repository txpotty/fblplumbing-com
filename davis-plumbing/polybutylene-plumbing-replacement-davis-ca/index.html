    Polybutylene Checks for Home Sales in Davis, CA

    Fitting Failure and Emergency Repair in Davis

    Acetal plastic and copper crimp ring fittings are often the first part of a poly system to fail. Attic fittings fail hard. We respond to emergency leaks, make temporary repairs and then schedule a full replacement, because stopping the problem for good means taking the poly system out of service entirely.

    Copper Replacement Option in Davis

    Type L copper appeals to some homeowners for its long history and rigid feel. We solder or press each joint. Copper costs more than PEX and takes a little longer to install, so we help you weigh the cost, your water conditions and how long you plan to stay in the house.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Davis, CA

    PEX is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, resists scale and corrosion and installs faster than copper. Faster install means lower labor cost. We keep PEX out of direct sunlight, away from water heater flues and recessed light housings and support it at the spacing the manufacturer calls for, which protects both the pipe and the warranty.

    Yard Service Line Replacement in Davis, CA

    Many poly-era homes also have a blue or black polybutylene service line running from the water meter to the house. These buried lines can split under the yard, raising the water bill and soaking the soil. We call 811 first. Then we trench the route, install new PEX or copper service pipe and connect it to a new main shutoff at the house.
