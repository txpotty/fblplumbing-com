    Types of Toilet Replacements in Davis, CA

    Water-Saving Toilet Upgrades in Davis, CA

    Older toilets are one of the largest indoor water users in a house, and replacing a 3.5 gallon model with a 1.28 gallon one cuts toilet water use by more than half. Dual-flush toilets add a lighter flush for liquids. Our recommendations lean on high MaP scores so the savings do not come with double flushing.

    Standard Toilet Replacement in Davis

    Two-piece toilets are what most houses have, and after twenty or thirty years the tank bolts corrode, the porcelain gets crazed and the flush never quite clears the bowl. That is when we swap them. The old unit comes off, the flange gets scraped clean and checked for cracks, then set the new one on a fresh wax ring with new closet bolts.

    Toilet Replacement Cost Comparison in Davis, CA

    Three repair calls in a year on the same toilet usually cost more than replacing it, especially when the toilet is old enough to waste water on every flush. We put both numbers side by side. The decision is yours, and we do not push replacement on a toilet that only needs a flapper.

    Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in Davis, CA

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Measure to the closet bolts, not the baseboard. If the new toilet's rough-in does not match, it either sits too far out or will not fit. An offset flange can correct an inch or two without moving the drain.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Davis, CA

    Late-night failures happen, usually a tank that splits along a seam or a bowl that cracks at the base and keeps seeping no matter how many towels go down. Turn off the angle stop and the leak stops. We can usually replace it the next morning, and we will tell you if the floor needs to dry before we set the new one.

    High-Efficiency Toilet (HET) Upgrades in Davis, CA

    High-efficiency toilets carry a WaterSense label and a MaP score, which tells you how many grams of waste the toilet clears in one flush. Look for 800 or higher. Higher numbers mean fewer double flushes and fewer plunger trips, and that is where the real water savings come from.

    One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Davis, CA

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, so there are fewer leak points and fewer crevices to clean. They cost more and weigh more. A one-piece toilet usually takes two people to carry upstairs, and we set it without dragging it across your tile so the floor stays unscratched and the wax ring lands centered on the flange.

    Choosing a Toilet Brand in Davis, CA

    Kohler, American Standard, TOTO, Gerber and Mansfield all make solid toilets, and each line has a few models we steer people away from because of weak flushes or hard-to-find parts. We will tell you which ones. What fits your bathroom comes down to rough-in and bowl shape, and we check both before you buy.
