    Replacing a urinal on existing lines usually costs $500 to $1,200 including a manual flush valve. New installs with carriers and new drain lines usually range from $1,500 to $3,500, and sensor valves add $250 to $600.

    Regular cleaning with appropriate cleaners is essential for both flush and waterless urinals. Waterless urinals also require periodic replacement of their sealant cartridges, typically every 1 to 3 months depending on usage. Proper maintenance prevents odors and clogs and keeps the fixture sanitary.

    We guarantee our installation labor, and fixtures and flush valves carry manufacturer warranties, often several years on flushometer components.

    Switching from a 1 gpf urinal to a 0.125 gpf model saves most of the water per flush. In a busy restroom that adds up to tens of thousands of gallons a year.

    A washdown urinal uses a simple flushing action to rinse the bowl, relying on gravity and the slope of the drain. A siphonic urinal creates a siphonic action during flushing, which pulls waste more forcefully down the drain, offering more effective waste removal and usually better odor control.
