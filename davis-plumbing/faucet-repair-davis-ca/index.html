    Ball Faucet Repair in Davis, CA

    Ball faucets have a single lever over a domed cap, and the drip usually comes from the two small rubber seats and springs that press against the ball. A seats and springs kit fixes most of them. Loose handles happen too. If the handle leaks, snugging the adjusting ring under the cap often fixes it.

    Delta and Moen Faucet Repair in Davis, CA

    Delta and Moen are the faucets we repair most, and both back their cartridges with lifetime warranties for the original owner on many models. We can check whether yours qualifies. Kohler, Pfister and American Standard each use proprietary cartridges, and we stock the common ones for each brand.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Davis

    Sputtering flow, where the water spits and surges, often means air or debris in the line after a water main repair, or a partly clogged cartridge screen. A flush of the lines usually fixes it. Pull-down faucets also have check valves in the hose that can stick, and we clean or replace those.

    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Davis, CA

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. Diverters are cheap parts. Touchless faucets add a solenoid and a battery pack to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.

    Bathroom Faucet Repair in Davis, CA

    Bathroom sinks see less wear than kitchen sinks, but scale still builds up in cartridges, and pop-up drains break at the pivot rod under the sink. Neither one takes long. We match cartridges for widespread, centerset and single-hole faucets, and pop-up assemblies are replaced to fit the finish.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Davis

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. The hose usually splits near the quick-connect fitting. We replace hoses, spray heads and weights with brand parts, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.

    Specialty Faucet Repair in Davis, CA

    Pot fillers, laundry tub faucets and wall-mount utility faucets each have their own valve styles, and many use compression stems or quarter-turn cartridges that hardware stores do not stock. The common stems ride on the truck. For filtered water faucets, we replace the cartridge or the check valve as needed.

    Compression Faucet Repair in Davis

    Two-handle faucets with separate hot and cold handles are often compression faucets, which seal by pressing a rubber washer down onto a brass seat inside the valve. Washers harden and seats get pitted. New washers go in and the seat gets dressed or replaced with a seat wrench, and the packing gets redone if the stem leaks.
