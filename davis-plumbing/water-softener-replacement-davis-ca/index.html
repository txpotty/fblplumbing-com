    Softener Systems We Replace in Davis, CA

    Fleck Valve Programming in Davis, CA

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. The valve needs your hardness in grains, the number of people and the regeneration time set correctly, or it will regenerate too often or not enough. We set it before we leave.

    Softener Loop Installation in Davis, CA

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. We can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. Outdoor spigots stay on hard water. The drain goes to a laundry box or floor drain with an air gap.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Davis

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. Old clock-timer units come out and metered valves go in. You will buy less salt. We set the brine refill to your hardness so the system is not dumping salt down the drain every few days.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Davis

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. Metered valves like the Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 count gallons and regenerate only when the resin is close to spent. Salt bills usually drop in the first month.

    Softener Failure Diagnosis in Davis, CA

    A clogged injector, a dead motor or a wrong setting can make a good softener look finished. Hardness gets tested on both sides of the unit, and the cheap fixes get tried before we talk replacement. Discontinued valves usually mean replacement.

    Long-Term Softener Costs in Davis, CA

    Long-term cost on a softener is mostly salt and water. A metered valve with an efficient brine setting can cut salt use by a third or more compared with an old timer unit, and that adds up over fifteen years. That is how we compare models.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Davis, CA

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in Northern California homes. An old Fleck with discontinued parts usually gets replaced, valve and tank together. Current Fleck valves fit standard tanks.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Davis, CA

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. New resin in the old tank sometimes beats a full replacement on price. The valve's age decides it. New resin under a tired valve rarely lasts.
