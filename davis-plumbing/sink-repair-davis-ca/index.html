    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Davis, CA

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. In kitchens it is cold grease and food, and in bathrooms it is hair knotted on the stopper and glued with soap scum. We clear it with a hand auger. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.

    Office and Restaurant Sink Repair in Davis

    Pop-Up Stopper Repair in Davis, CA

    Stoppers that will not hold water have a linkage problem. The clevis strap slips on the lift rod, the pivot ball loses its seal, or hair wraps the stopper so tightly it cannot seat on the flange. Cleaning fixes half of them. The other half get a new pop-up drain in a matching finish.
