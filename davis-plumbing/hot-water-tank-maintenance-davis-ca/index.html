    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Davis, CA

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can block the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a steady blue flame and lower gas bills.

    Electric Element and Thermostat Service in Davis

    Electric tanks rely on upper and lower heating elements that get crusted with scale in hard water. We test each element with a meter, check the thermostats and look for scorched wires at the terminal block. If an element has failed we replace it with the matching wattage and voltage. Many families who think they need a new heater just need a lower element and a flush.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Davis

    The cost for hot water tank maintenance in Davis, CA usually ranges from $100 to $250, depending on the tank type, age and specific services required like anode rod replacement. We provide a detailed quote before any work starts.

    Regular, professional maintenance can frequently be a condition for keeping your manufacturer's warranty valid. Check your warranty documentation, but generally, proof of annual service can help keep your warranty intact.

    Many homeowners can do a basic drain with a garden hose. The risk is a plastic drain valve that breaks or will not reseal, and DIY flushes seldom include an anode check or relief valve test.

    Neglecting maintenance can lead to inefficient operation, higher energy bills, premature tank failure from corrosion or sediment buildup, and potential safety hazards from a malfunctioning pressure relief valve. Regular service heads off these costly and dangerous issues.

    While some basic steps like draining a small amount of water can be done by a homeowner, professional hot water tank maintenance involves specialized tools and experience to properly inspect electrical or gas components, anode rods and pressure relief valves. It is best left to a professional to ensure safety and code compliance.

    Many manufacturers, including Rheem, A.O. Smith and Bradford White, expect regular maintenance and can reject claims for sediment damage. Keeping service records protects your warranty.

    A typical hot water tank maintenance service usually takes about 1 to 2 hours. This includes draining, flushing, inspection and any minor adjustments or part swaps like an anode rod.
