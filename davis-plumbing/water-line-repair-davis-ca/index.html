    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Davis, CA

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which chokes flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe rarely lasts because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and ends repeat leaks.

    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Davis

    Underground water line leaks are often hard to detect until significant damage has occurred. Common signs include sudden spikes in your water bill, damp spots in your yard, or a clear drop in water pressure. Our team uses acoustic leak detection and ground-penetrating radar to accurately pinpoint these hidden leaks in Davis, reducing the need for extensive excavation. We repair breaks caused by shifting soil, tree root intrusion, or aging pipes, restoring your water service quickly.

    Main Water Line Leak Repair in Davis

    Most main line leaks show up as a soggy strip of yard, a sudden jump in your water bill or a meter dial that moves with every fixture off.
