    Frozen Pipe Thawing in Davis

    When a faucet only trickles or nothing comes out after a cold night, ice is blocking the line somewhere upstream. We open a faucet downstream to relieve pressure, then locate the frozen section with thermal imaging. Copper and galvanized pipes can be thawed with an electric pipe thawing machine that warms the metal from the inside. PEX and CPVC are warmed gently with heat guns or infrared heaters to avoid softening the plastic.

    Pool, Outdoor Equipment and Water Heater Line Freeze Damage in Davis, CA

    Pool equipment lines, outdoor kitchen and ice maker supply tubing and water heater connections in garages are all common freeze points. Thin copper or plastic tubing in exposed spots splits easily in a hard freeze. We repair these lines and can add shutoff and drain valves so they can be winterized quickly. If a water heater connection or valve was damaged, we repair it and check the unit.

    Backflow Preventer and Irrigation Freeze Repair in Davis

    Backflow preventers and irrigation valves sit above ground and are exposed to the coldest air. Frozen backflow assemblies can crack their bodies or bonnets, causing leaks and failed tests. We repair or replace the assembly, insulate it with a fitted cover and suggest draining when deep cold is forecast. Many water providers require a passing backflow test after repairs, and we can set that up.

    Burst Pipe Repair in Davis, CA

    Water expands as it freezes, and that pressure can split copper, crack PVC fittings and pop PEX connections. Leaks often appear only after the ice melts, sometimes hours later. We shut off the water, open the wall or ceiling only where needed and replace the damaged section. Every repair is pressure tested so you know the line is sound.

    Water Heater Line Thawing and Repair in Davis

    The water lines leading to and from your water heater can also freeze, especially if located in an unconditioned space like a garage or utility closet in Davis. Our plumbers are skilled at safely thawing these lines, inspecting for any damage to the water heater unit, and confirming proper function is restored.     Hose Bib and Outdoor Faucet Repair in Davis, CA

    Outdoor faucets freeze quickly, especially with a hose left connected. A split often hides inside the wall and only shows when the faucet is turned on in spring. We replace damaged hose bibs with frost-free models from brands like Woodford and Prier that shut off water inside the warm part of the wall. Insulated covers add more protection on freezing nights.
