    Why Polybutylene Fails in San Jose

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Rural properties around San Jose often rely on private wells, which may need treatment for iron, sediment, or other minerals. Hot water speeds it up. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.

    Full Home Polybutylene Repiping in San Jose

    A full replacement takes every poly supply line out of service, not just the sections that have failed. Leaving some poly keeps the risk. We run new lines from the main shutoff to every sink, toilet, tub, washer, ice maker and water heater, add new quarter-turn stops and test the whole system before patching.

    Copper Replacement for Polybutylene in San Jose, CA

    Some homeowners in San Jose, CA want copper when they take out polybutylene. That works fine. Our copper replacements use thicker Type L pipe with lead-free soldered or pressed joints, not the thinner Type M. Expect a higher price and a slightly longer job with copper, in exchange for good heat and UV tolerance and a long history.

    Full Home Repiping with PEX in San Jose

    Most poly replacements we do use PEX-A, a flexible tubing that runs long distances with few fittings. For slab houses, new PEX-A runs through the attic and drops down interior walls, so none of it sits under the floor. It also tolerates freezing better. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter open, so pressure at the far shower stays strong.
