    Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in San Jose

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. Some carriers need new bolts. The new bowl gets matched to the carrier already in the wall whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.

    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in San Jose, CA

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs. They are noticeably louder than gravity toilets. When we replace one, we pick a vessel brand with parts that are easy to get later, and we check that house pressure is in the range the unit needs.

    Choosing a Toilet Brand in San Jose, CA

    Kohler, American Standard, TOTO, Gerber and Mansfield all make solid toilets, and each line has a few models we steer people away from because of weak flushes or hard-to-find parts. We will tell you which ones. Rough-in and bowl shape decide what fits, and we check both before you buy.

    Gravity-Flush Toilet Replacement in San Jose

    Gravity-flush toilets are the simplest and most forgiving, and a well-made one with a 3 inch flush valve will outflush most of the older toilets it replaces. Replacement parts are on every hardware shelf. If the floor is uneven, we shim it level, then caulk the base and test several full flushes.

    High-Efficiency Toilet (HET) Upgrades in San Jose, CA

    High-efficiency toilets carry a WaterSense label and a MaP score, which tells you how many grams of waste the toilet clears in one flush. Look for 800 or higher. Higher numbers mean fewer double flushes and fewer plunger trips, and that is where the real water savings come from.

    Water-Saving Toilet Upgrades in San Jose, CA

    Older toilets are one of the largest indoor water users in a house, and replacing a 3.5 gallon model with a 1.28 gallon one cuts toilet water use by more than half. Dual-flush toilets add a lighter flush for liquids. Our recommendations lean on high MaP scores so the savings do not come with double flushing.
