    Interior Drainage Systems in San Jose

    Where digging outside is not practical, an interior drain system does the same job from inside. A strip of slab comes out along the walls, perforated pipe goes in on stone, and it all drains to a sump. The wall gets a dimpled membrane. Water then runs behind it into the channel.

    Exterior Waterproofing Solutions in San Jose, CA

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing, cleaning the wall, and applying a membrane with a drainage board over it. It fixes the problem from the outside, at the cost of a torn-up yard. Expect a few days of work. Our crew handles the footing drain and its outlet, and a waterproofing partner applies the membrane.

    Supply Line Leak Repair in San Jose

    Water lines along a basement ceiling can leak at fittings, valves or pinholes. Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running mineral-heavy. We cut out the bad section and replace it with copper or PEX, and if we find more than two or three pinholes on one run, we recommend repiping that run instead of patching it again. Repiping a basement ceiling is usually a one-day job.

    Sump Pump Repair and Installation in San Jose, CA

    Plenty of basements rely on a sump pit to handle runoff and groundwater. Tree roots seek out sewer laterals during dry spells, which is why heavy rains often reveal damaged lines through sudden backups. Pumps from big-box stores often last five to seven years, and the first sign of trouble is usually a pump that runs constantly or hums without moving water. We replace pumps the same day.

    Wall Leak Solutions in San Jose, CA

    Damp walls without a crack often point to water soaking through porous block or concrete. Tape foil to the wall for a full day, and if the room side is wet it is condensation, not a leak. Condensation needs a dehumidifier. Seepage needs drainage outside or a channel inside.
