    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in San Jose

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. Salt sits there unused, and regeneration fails. After breaking up the crust, we clear the sludge and test the float and brine well. Cracked tanks get replaced.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in San Jose, CA

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. A clogged injector leaves the brine tank full of water and the resin never gets recharged. The injector and screen get cleaned or replaced, and the brine line gets checked.

    Control Valve Rebuild in San Jose

    Service, backwash, brine and rinse are the four cycles the control valve directs every time the softener regenerates, usually in the middle of the night. Over the years, seals, spacers and pistons wear down. Leaks at the valve or water running to the drain are common signs. A matching rebuild kit fixes it.
