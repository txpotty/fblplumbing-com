    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in San Jose, CA

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. Every one is listed for potable water. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in San Jose

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A makes tighter bends, can be heat-fixed if it kinks, and its fittings flow better, while PEX-B is cheaper and its crimps hold just as well when each one is gauged. Both last decades. For tight attics we lean toward PEX-A.
