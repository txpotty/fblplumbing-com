    Frequently Asked Questions About PEX Pipe Installation in San Jose

    The cost of PEX pipe installation in San Jose can range from $2,500 to $15,000 or more, depending on the size of your home, accessibility and whether it is a full repipe or a partial replacement. Minor repairs might be a few hundred dollars. We provide a detailed quote after an on-site assessment.

    Whole-home repipes typically require a plumbing permit and inspection. We handle the permit process as part of the project.

    PEX resists scale and corrosion, flexes with temperature changes and has far fewer joints. Copper is very durable but can develop pinhole leaks in some water conditions and costs more to install.

    Yes, PEX piping is certified safe for drinking water. It does not leach chemicals into water and is approved by the major plumbing codes for potable water systems. It is a healthier alternative to older pipes that can corrode and affect water quality.

    PEX can be used for outdoor applications like irrigation lines or outdoor spigots, but it needs to be protected from direct UV light, which can degrade the material over time. When used outdoors, PEX should consistently be buried or otherwise shielded.

    Properly installed PEX is expected to last many decades. Manufacturers often offer 25 year warranties when their pipe and fittings are used together.

    PEX must be protected from sunlight because UV light breaks down it. Outdoors it needs to be buried, sleeved or completely covered.
