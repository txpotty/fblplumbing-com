    Grease Trap Installation Cost in San Jose, CA

    Small under-sink hydromechanical traps generally cost much less to install than large outdoor interceptors. Indoor units are usually a few thousand dollars installed, while in-ground interceptors can run well into five figures with excavation and concrete work. Pricing depends on unit size, location, soil conditions and whether we need to cut and patch floors. We give you a written estimate that includes permits and inspection.     Automatic Grease Removal Units in San Jose

    Automatic grease removal devices skim grease from the water and collect it in a container your staff empties. Brands like Thermaco Big Dipper are popular in kitchens with limited space and frequent cleaning needs. We install these units with the correct power supply and drain connections. They can reduce the need for frequent pump-outs in smaller kitchens. We explain daily maintenance so your staff keeps the unit running well.

    Grease Trap Installation for Schools and Institutions in San Jose

    School cafeterias, hospitals, senior living facilities and churches with kitchens often need commercial interceptors.     In-Ground Gravity Interceptor Installation in San Jose, CA

    Larger restaurants and high-volume kitchens often need an outdoor gravity interceptor. These concrete or polyethylene tanks typically range from several hundred to over 1,500 gallons. We excavate, set the tank on a stable base, connect inlet and outlet piping and install manhole risers. Earthquakes and hillside soil movement are real risks in Northern California, so flexible connections and seismic straps help protect plumbing from ground movement. We plan excavation equipment around the ground conditions we find on site. A properly installed interceptor can go much longer between pump-outs than an indoor trap.

    Under-Sink Grease Trap Setup in San Jose, CA

    Smaller establishments or specific kitchen stations in San Jose, CA may benefit from compact under-sink grease traps. These units are ideal for capturing FOG at the source, preventing it from entering the main plumbing lines. Our team carefully installs these smaller units, ensuring proper sizing, sealing, and connection to your existing plumbing system for reliable waste management and odor control.

    Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in San Jose, CA

    Gravity grease interceptors, or GGI units, are larger, typically located outdoors, and rely on the natural difference in specific gravity between FOG and water. These are common for high-volume commercial kitchens. Our team handles the complex excavation, concrete work, and plumbing connections required for proper GGI installation, maintaining compliance with all local Northern California ordinances for waste management.
