    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in San Jose, CA

    Calcium scale is what kills most tankless units early. Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running hard. Once scale coats the heat exchanger, efficiency drops and error codes start, and over time it can crack the exchanger and void the warranty. A yearly flush prevents it. Service valves make it easy.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in San Jose

    Gas tankless units in San Jose are the common pick for whole-house hot water because a single 199,000 BTU burner can feed two showers and a sink at the same time without losing temperature.
