    T&P Valve and Safety Testing in San Jose, CA

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building unsafe pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets swapped out. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge where installed.

    Attic and Garage Water Heater Maintenance in San Jose

    Many homes have water heaters in garages or attics, where heat, cold and tight access add stress to the unit. Attic units need working drain pans and drain lines to prevent ceiling damage if the tank leaks. We check the pan, drain line, venting and access platform. Garage units should be raised and protected where required for gas appliances. We make sure your setup meets local code.

    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in San Jose, CA

    Gas water heater maintenance involves important safety inspections in San Jose, CA. We check for gas leaks, proper venting of exhaust gases, and the function of the pilot light or igniter. We also clean the burner assembly and inspect the thermocouple. These checks are vital to prevent carbon monoxide exposure and keep your gas-fired appliance running safely.

    Tank Water Heater Flushing in San Jose

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium sediment at the bottom of the tank. This layer insulates the water from the burner or lower element, wasting energy and causing popping sounds. We connect a hose to the drain valve and flush the tank until the water runs clear. For heavy buildup, we may stir and flush in several cycles. A clean tank heats faster and runs more quietly.

    Emergency Water Heater Inspection in San Jose, CA

    If your water heater is acting up and you're unsure why, our emergency inspection service in San Jose, CA can diagnose the problem fast. While not a full maintenance visit, we can pinpoint issues like strange noises, sudden temperature drops, or leaks that point to an immediate need for repair. We'll then provide an straight assessment and options for repair or replacement.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in San Jose

    Tankless units are especially sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. We isolate the unit with its service valves and circulate a descaling solution with a pump for the recommended time. We also clean the inlet water filter and check the unit's error history. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures consistent.

    Maintenance Cost Versus Replacement in San Jose, CA

    A water heater tune-up costs a fraction of a new unit, which can run well over a thousand dollars installed. Regular flushing and anode replacement can help a tank reach the high end of its expected lifespan. Tankless units in particular can last many years with proper descaling. If your heater is near the end of its life or leaking from the tank, we will tell you honestly. We help you weigh repair against replacement with actual numbers.

    Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in San Jose

    Gas water heaters need a clean burner and a proper vent to run safely. We inspect the burner flame, clean the combustion chamber and check the thermocouple or flame sensor. The vent is checked for proper draft and secure connections. Blocked or loose vents can let carbon monoxide into your home. We also check the gas line and shutoff valve for leaks.
