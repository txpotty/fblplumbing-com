    Why Choose FBL Plumbing for Hydro Jetting in San Jose, CA?

    Jetting cleans the pipe, not just the clog. A cable leaves the walls coated. That coating is why snaked lines clog again so fast, and why a jetted line usually holds much longer.

    • Water Only, Nothing but water goes into the pipe, so there are no harsh chemicals to eat at older lines.
    • Fewer Repeat Calls, Clean walls trap less debris, so the clog takes much longer to form again.
    • Full-Wall Clean, Jetting scrubs the entire pipe wall, not just a path through the clog.

    Root Cutting With Hydro Jetting in San Jose

    Specialty nozzles slice fine roots. Root hairs slip past a saw. Jetting removes root hairs left by a cable and exposes each joint where roots get in, which shows up clearly on camera, which helps you weigh lining against a spot repair.

    Cast iron grows rust tubercles. Minerals make it worse. We set the pressure for old iron, strip the buildup with a descaling nozzle and check for weak spots on camera as we go, which is also the standard prep before pipe lining.
