    Tree Root Removal with Hydro Jetting in San Jose

    Tree roots are a common problem in San Jose, CA, especially where mature landscaping grows over older sewer lines. Roots can work into tiny cracks in pipes and grow, causing severe blockages.     Hydro Jetting Before Pipe Lining in San Jose, CA

    Hydro Jetting vs. Snaking Cost Comparison in San Jose, CA

Hydro jetting cleans the entire pipe, preventing future issues and reducing the frequency of service calls, making it a more economical choice for persistent drain problems.
