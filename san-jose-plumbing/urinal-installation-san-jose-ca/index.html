    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in San Jose, CA

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. Tankless flushometers deliver a set volume from a 3/4 inch line and recover instantly for the next user. The volume matches the fixture rating. Wrong settings stain bowls.

    Flushometer Urinal Installation in San Jose

Handle side follows the layout. ADA units need the handle within reach.
