    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in San Jose, CA

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in San Jose

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and usually add a dehumidifier to hold humidity under 60 percent. Inland valleys get hot summers that work water heaters and exterior lines hard, while coastal areas stay mild and damp year round. The joists stop rotting. Drier air under the floor also means less musty air upstairs.

    Basement Wall Sealing in San Jose, CA

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Inland valleys get hot summers that work water heaters and exterior lines hard, while coastal areas stay mild and damp year round. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then seal the face with a masonry waterproofer that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. Most homeowners notice the musty smell is gone within a few weeks. Those weep holes empty into the interior drain if the house has one.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in San Jose, CA

    Rubber washing machine hoses burst. We swap them for braided stainless hoses, put a drain pan under the washer piped to a floor drain where one is available, and install a quarter-turn shutoff or an automatic leak valve on the supply box. Water heaters get a pan too. The wall behind them can take a moisture-resistant board and sealer.

    Commercial Wall Waterproofing in San Jose

    Restaurant kitchens, gym locker rooms and salon wash stations put water on walls all day. We waterproof commercial showers, mop sink walls and restroom wet walls with membranes rated for constant water exposure, and we repair the supply and drain piping behind them if demolition turns up a leak. We can work after hours.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in San Jose

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most reliable fix for a leaking basement, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing to carry the water away from the wall. Expansive clay soils are common across Northern California, and seasonal swelling and shrinking can crack slabs and buried sewer laterals. We backfill after the drain is tested.

    Waterproofing Costs vs. Repeat Water Damage in San Jose, CA

    Paying once beats paying every two years. Waterproofing a shower wall usually lands in the low thousands, while replacing drywall, cabinets and flooring again and again costs far more, and mold remediation on top of that can double the bill. The price for exterior block sealing depends on how much wall there is. We write up each option with its price so you can compare.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in San Jose

    Older neighborhoods in Northern California include prewar and postwar homes with galvanized supply lines and cast iron or clay sewer pipe that are nearing the end of their life. Any wall below grade sits in soil that gets saturated after heavy rain or snowmelt, and that water pushes against the wall with steady hydrostatic pressure. We inject cracks, coat the wall inside or out, and install drain tile where water pools along the footing. A sump goes in if groundwater sits high. Not every basement needs one.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in San Jose, CA

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the level and wait 24 hours. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a two-piece clamping drain set on the liner. We flood test the new pan before any tile goes down.
