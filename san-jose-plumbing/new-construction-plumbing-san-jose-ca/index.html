    Underground and Under-Slab Plumbing in San Jose

    The underground phase sets the drain, waste and vent layout that every fixture in the house will rely on. We trench, bed and slope PVC or ABS drain lines at a minimum quarter inch per foot and protect every pipe that passes through the slab or foundation with sleeves. Earthquakes and hillside soil movement are real risks in Northern California, so flexible connections and seismic straps help protect plumbing from sudden movement. We compact bedding carefully and avoid rigid points that could stress the pipe as the ground moves. Each line is water tested and left visible for the inspector before your concrete contractor pours.
