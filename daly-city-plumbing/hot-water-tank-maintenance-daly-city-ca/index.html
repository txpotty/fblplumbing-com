    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Daly City

    Calcium carbonate drops out of hard water every time the tank heats, and over a year it can harden into chunks the size of a thumbnail. That is what makes the popping noise. When a plain flush will not move them, we use a descaling solution rated for potable systems, let it sit and then flush it all out. A clean bottom puts less heat stress on the seam.

    Electric Element Check in Daly City

    Electric tanks have two elements, usually 4,500 watts at 240 volts, and the lower one does most of the work and takes most of the scale. Each element gets an ohm reading. A failed lower element shows up as hot water that runs out halfway through a shower. We replace it with the matching wattage and voltage.

    Gas Venting Tune-Up in Daly City, CA

    Atmospheric gas tanks vent by draft alone, so the vent connector needs upward slope of at least a quarter inch per foot and tight joints with three screws each. Draft at the hood gets checked first. Lint on the flame arrestor of an FVIR tank can starve the burner and kill the pilot. We vacuum it clean.

    Burner and Pilot Service in Daly City, CA

    On a gas tank, a dirty burner throws a lazy yellow flame and leaves soot on the flue baffle. The burner comes out and gets brushed and vacuumed. A tired thermocouple drops the pilot, so we test it too. The goal is a steady blue flame.

    Rheem and Bradford White Tank Service in Daly City

    Rheem, Bradford White, AO Smith and State make most of the tanks in local garages, and each has quirks a tech should know before opening a drain valve. Some models hide the anode under the top cover. Bradford White often uses a combo anode in the hot outlet nipple. We carry the right sockets for each brand.

    Electric Thermostat Service in Daly City

    Each element on an electric tank has its own thermostat, and the upper one carries a reset button for the high-limit switch. When the reset trips, something caused it. We trace the cause, often a shorted element or a stuck thermostat, before resetting it. Power stays off at the breaker the whole time.

    T&P Valve Replacement in Daly City

    Replacing a relief valve is a short job, but it has to be done right. We match the new valve to the heater's input rating. We drain the tank below the valve opening, thread in the new one with pipe dope rated for potable water, and reconnect a discharge pipe in copper, CPVC or another approved material. Never plug or cap that pipe.

    Anode Upgrades for Odor in Daly City, CA

    A rotten egg smell from the hot side only is almost always sulfate-reducing bacteria reacting with a magnesium anode. A different rod usually solves it. We replace it with an aluminum-zinc rod or a powered anode, then chlorinate the tank to kill the bacteria living in it. Powered anodes never wear out and draw very little electricity.
