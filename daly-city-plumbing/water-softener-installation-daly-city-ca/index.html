    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Daly City

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. Install follows the manufacturer manual for bypass, drain and overflow, then we set hardness and regeneration. We install store-bought units. Programming is part of the job.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Daly City, CA

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. FBL Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. Most effective option.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Daly City

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet, and many can also feed a refrigerator water line or an ice maker. It complements a softener. We can handle filter changes.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Daly City, CA

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. Putting a carbon tank in front of the softener takes out chlorine and keeps it from wearing down the resin. Taste improves fast. We install twin-tank setups or dual-stage units.

    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Daly City, CA

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Modern units meter water use, send salt reminders by app and come in larger capacities for growing families. Salt bills drop. We swap it out in a few hours.

    Commercial Water Softener Installation in Daly City, CA

    Restaurants, hotels and laundromats feel hard water in their equipment first. Commercial softeners we install use larger resin tanks or twin-tank alternating systems so soft water is available around the clock, even during regeneration. Equipment lasts longer. We schedule around your hours.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Daly City

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. That protects every faucet, shower, water heater and appliance downstream, which is where hard water does most of its damage over the years. We size before we sell. Hardness and household use set the grain capacity.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in Daly City

    Most installs cost $1,500 to $4,000 including equipment. Adding a new softener loop usually adds $500 to $1,500, depending on how easy it is to reach the main line.

    On an existing loop, installs usually take two to three hours. A new loop adds a few hours.

    Homes with hard water usually benefit from one. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. Softeners protect water heaters, fixtures and appliances from scale.

    Ten to fifteen years is typical for a quality softener. Unit quality, how hard your water is and how well the system is maintained all change that, and resin can be replaced to extend it. Service helps.

    White scale on faucets, spotty dishes, dry skin and a water heater that struggles are common signs. A water test confirms hardness.

    Control valves usually carry about 5 years, and tanks often 10 or more. We also warranty our installation work.

    With a loop in place, some homeowners can do it, but air gaps, bypass valves and programming matter. A professional install helps avoid leaks and weak softening.

    Softeners remove hardness minerals using salt. Conditioners change how minerals behave to reduce scale, but softeners work better on very hard water.
