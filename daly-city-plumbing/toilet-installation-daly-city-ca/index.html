    Commercial Flushometer Toilets in Daly City

    Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. They recover instantly between flushes. Manual and sensor valves go on both floor-mount and wall-hung bowls, with the control stop adjusted so the flush is full but does not splash. Sensor valves cut down on touch points.

    Low-Flow WaterSense Toilet Installation in Daly City, CA

    WaterSense toilets are capped at 1.28 gallons per flush, and some dual-flush models drop to around 0.8 for liquid waste. That adds up fast. A family of four swapping one old 3.5 gallon toilet can save thousands of gallons a year, and some water providers offer rebates worth checking. Pick a strong MaP rating.

    Wall-Mounted Toilet Installation in Daly City

    Wall-mounted toilets hang off a steel carrier bolted to the floor inside the wall. An access plate hides the tank. Because the carrier carries all the weight, we anchor its feet to the slab or framing first and check it for plumb before the drywall or tile goes up. Plan on a remodel, not a swap.

    Smart Toilet Installation in Daly City, CA

    Smart toilets with heated seats and built-in bidets need two things the old toilet did not: power and a dedicated water connection. Most need a GFCI outlet close enough for the cord. The water connection and mounting are ours, and when an outlet has to be added we schedule a licensed electrician instead of doing that wiring. Then we test every button.

    New Construction Toilet Installation in Daly City

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. The flange should sit on top of the finished floor. Set it too early and the tile setter has to cut around it, which leaves the flange low and forces a thick seal or an extender later on. Each bowl gets a fresh seal.

    Toilet Brands We Install in Daly City

    We install Kohler, TOTO, American Standard, Gerber and most other brands sold at supply houses and home centers. Every brand has its quirks. Some one-piece TOTO models mount with a Unifit adapter, and certain Kohler toilets use a tank-to-bowl gasket that most hardware stores do not carry, so we stock both. We keep those parts on hand.

    ADA-Compliant Toilet Installation in Daly City

    ADA toilets put the seat 17 to 19 inches off the floor, and the centerline sits 16 to 18 inches from the side wall. It is set that way so a grab bar fits. Before anything is ordered we measure the drain, because shifting it to meet the ADA centerline means cutting into the floor. Elongated bowls are standard here.

    Rough-In Toilet Installation in Daly City, CA

    Rough-in is the plumbing that goes in before walls and floors close up. For a toilet that means a 3 inch drain with a vent, plus a cold supply stub. The drain goes 12 inches out from the finished wall line, which means we add the drywall thickness to the framing measurement. Then the inspector sees it open.
