    Copper to PEX Conversions in Daly City

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running mineral-heavy. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more often follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and reduces the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Daly City, CA

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, much like a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when multiple fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to balance cost and performance.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Daly City, CA

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is known for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Plenty of insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to insure and sell.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Daly City

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the different advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, typically using crimp or compression fittings. Our experts in Daly City can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.
