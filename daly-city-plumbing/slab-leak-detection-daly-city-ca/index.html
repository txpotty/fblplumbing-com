    Electronic Leak Detection in Daly City

    For elusive leaks, our electronic leak detection equipment sends a signal through your pipes and measures changes that indicate a breach.     Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Daly City

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat speeds up corrosion and causes expansion and contraction. Signs include warm spots on the floor, a water heater that runs constantly and higher gas or electric bills. Thermal imaging shows the heat pattern spreading through tile or concrete. We confirm with a pressure test and acoustic listening before marking the spot.

    Leak Detection for Older Northern California Homes in Daly City, CA

    Water and Sewer Line Slab Leaks in Daly City, CA

    Slab leaks can affect both freshwater supply lines and sewer drain pipes. Our detection services are equipped to identify both types of leaks, which present different challenges. Sewer line leaks often involve foul odors and slow drainage, while water line leaks can show up as wet spots or high water bills in your Daly City, CA home. We have separate methods for each.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Daly City, CA

    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to see temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak. As water escapes a hot water line, it creates a clear thermal signature on the concrete slab. This technique is particularly useful for hot water line leaks and helps us narrow down the search area significantly without any physical disruption to your property in Daly City.

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Daly City

    Our technicians use highly sensitive acoustic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a leak under your slab. This method involves listening for the faint sound of escaping water, even through concrete. It's particularly effective for pressurized water lines, allowing us to find leaks without unnecessary excavation and minimizing disruption to your Daly City home.
