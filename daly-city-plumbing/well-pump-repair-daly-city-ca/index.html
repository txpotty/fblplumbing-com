    Pressure Switch Replacement in Daly City

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, a clogged sensing tube and ant nests are the usual reasons one fails. After swapping the switch and clearing the tube, we set cut-in and cut-out to match the tank, usually 40 and 60 psi. One of the cheaper well repairs.

    Deep Well Pump Repair in Daly City

    Deep wells get submersible pumps. They push water up, run quietly and last, but motors, impellers and splices still wear out. We pull the pump with the right equipment, test it and replace only what failed. Heat-shrink splice kits and a torque arrestor protect the new work in your Daly City, CA well.

    Pressure Tank Problems in Daly City

    Short cycling is the classic pressure tank symptom, with the pump clicking on and off every few seconds while a faucet runs. A waterlogged tank or a ruptured bladder causes most of it. Precharge gets checked on an empty tank. Set it 2 psi under cut-in, which means about 38 psi for a 40/60 switch.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Daly City, CA

    Electrical faults cause a big share of well calls, from a tripped breaker to a burned contact in the pressure switch. After that, we look at the capacitor and relay in the control box. We lock out the power before testing anything. All wiring and splices meet Northern California electrical code before power goes back on.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Daly City

    Submersible pumps hang inside the well casing on drop pipe, often 100 feet or more below the wellhead. No water, low pressure or a pump that hums without moving water are the usual signs one has failed. Testing starts at the surface. If it has to come up, a pump hoist lifts the pipe safely, and we replace the burned motor, worn impellers or a bad check valve before setting it back.

    Goulds, Grundfos, Franklin Electric and Myers make most of the pumps we pull in this area, and FBL Plumbing services all of them. Older units and newer variable speed systems are both familiar.
