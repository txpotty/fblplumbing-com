    Frequently Asked Questions About Drainage Services in Daly City

    The cost of drainage services in Daly City, CA can range from $150 to $500 for a basic clog removal, but complex issues like main sewer line clearing or hydro-jetting can cost $400 to $800 or more. Factors include the severity of the clog, accessibility, and the tools required. We provide upfront, transparent pricing before any work begins.

    While readily available, chemical drain cleaners are generally not recommended. They often contain harsh chemicals that can corrode pipes over time, especially older ones, and can be dangerous if not handled properly. Professional plumbers use safer, more effective methods.

    Drain clearing comes with a service guarantee, and installations carry a workmanship warranty in writing. Products like NDS channel drains and pipe also have manufacturer warranties. We explain the terms before you approve the job.

    A French drain collects water that soaks into soil and is best along foundations and low areas. A channel drain catches water flowing across hard surfaces like driveways and patios. Many properties benefit from both.

    Watch for slow or gurgling drains, sewer odors, water pooling near the house after storms, soil erosion and cracks in walkways. Standing water in the yard for more than a day is another sign. Call us to have it looked at.

    A typical French drain or downspout system takes one to three days. Larger projects with dry wells or extensive trenching can take longer. We give you a timeline with your estimate.

    Drain snaking uses a flexible auger to physically break through or retrieve a blockage. Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the inside of pipes, removing buildup like grease, scale, and roots, providing a more thorough cleaning. Hydro-jetting is usually more effective for severe or recurring clogs.

    A standard residential drain cleaning for a single fixture typically takes about one to two hours. More complex jobs, like main sewer line clearing or hydro-jetting, can take longer, possibly three to four hours or more depending on the severity of the blockage.
