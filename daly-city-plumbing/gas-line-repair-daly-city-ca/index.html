    Gas Permits and Inspections in Daly City

    Repairs that change piping, and all new gas lines, usually need a permit and inspection from Daly City building officials. We pull the permit, book the inspection and meet the inspector at the gauge so you do not have to. Inspectors often come within the week. Once it passes, the utility can restore service and we come back to relight appliances if needed.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Daly City

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. We replace them with new coated stainless connectors sized for the appliance and add an accessible shutoff valve if the old one is missing or seized. This is a quick fix. Ranges, dryers, water heaters and furnaces all need proper connections, and most also need a sediment trap ahead of the appliance gas valve.

    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Daly City, CA

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. Pool heaters are the big load, often 250,000 to 400,000 BTU, so a line that was fine for a grill can starve the heater and the house appliances at the same time. We size outdoor lines for the full load. Repairs and new runs are tested, buried at code depth and marked with tracer wire.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Daly City

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Many older homes here sit on raised foundations with crawlspaces, which make supply and drain repairs more accessible than slab work. We replace corroded sections with polyethylene pipe laid with tracer wire, using an approved transition riser where the line comes up out of the ground at the house. Exposed sections get coated steel. Everything is tested before the trench is backfilled, so a bad joint gets caught while it is still easy to reach.

    Gas Shut Off by the Utility in Daly City

    When your utility shuts off gas for a leak, you may be without hot water, heat or cooking until a licensed plumber fixes it. Our plumber finds and fixes the leak, then runs the test your gas company requires before unlocking the meter. The truck carries what we need. We do not leave until the gauge holds steady and the system is ready for the utility.

    Emergency Gas Line Repair in Daly City

    If you smell gas, leave the building, skip the light switches and call your gas utility or 911 from outside. Then call us. Once the utility has shut off the meter and made the area safe, we locate the leak, make the repair and run the pressure test needed before service can be restored. We relight pilots when service returns. Most emergency repairs are done in a single visit with parts from the truck.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Daly City, CA

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. We repair buried and above-ground propane lines, replace leaking regulators and fittings and check that each appliance has been converted with the correct orifices for propane. A natural gas burner running on propane will burn rich and soot up. The test procedure is the same as for natural gas.
