    Emergency Trenchless Sewer Repair in Daly City

    Root Intrusion Repair in Daly City, CA

    Roots enter at the joints, where clay bells or cast iron hubs have a small gap or a failed gasket. Once inside, they branch into a mat that fills the pipe. We cut them out with a mechanical root cutter or a hydro jet, then line the pipe so those joints are sealed for good and the line stops needing a cleaning every few months. The roots cannot get back in.

    Commercial Trenchless Sewer Line Work in Daly City, CA

    Restaurants feel a sewer failure fast, because grease and a dish machine put heavy load on a 4- or 6-inch building drain all day. Lining can be scheduled overnight or on a day you are closed. A trench through a parking lot means lost spaces and a blocked entrance for a week or more. Liners skip that.

    Sectional Point Repair in Daly City

    A point repair is a short liner, often 3 to 5 feet long, set over one bad spot. It rides in on an inflatable packer, guided by the camera footage, and the packer inflates to press the resin-soaked sleeve against the pipe wall until it cures. It fixes a single crack or root entry, not a whole failing line. Sound lines with one bad joint are the right candidates.

    Causes of Sewer Line Failure in Daly City, CA

    Shifting soil is one of the main reasons sewer joints separate, crack or drop out of grade over the years. Many older homes here sit on raised foundations with crawlspaces, which make supply and drain repairs easier to reach than slab work. Roots are the other big one, since a hairline gap at a clay joint is enough for a feeder root to get in and then swell into a mat that catches paper. Then age finishes the job. Cast iron scales and thins, and clay bells crack under the weight of soil and traffic.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Daly City, CA

    CIPP stands for cured-in-place pipe. The crew saturates a felt tube with two-part epoxy on a roller table at the truck, invert it into the cleaned line with air pressure, and hold it against the old pipe wall until the resin hardens. Cure time runs a few hours. The finished liner seals cracks and leaking joints in the original pipe.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Daly City, CA

    Cast iron rusts from the inside out, and the bottom of the pipe goes first. Plumbers call that channel rot, a groove that eats through the bottom. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or carbide cutters spinning on a flexible shaft, which brings the wall back to bare metal so a liner can bond to it. Under a slab, lining beats breaking up the floor in two rooms.

    Pipe Bursting in Daly City, CA

    Two pits are standard for a burst, one at the house end near the foundation and one out near the street or the city tap. Before pulling, the crew heat-fuses sections of HDPE into one continuous length on the ground. The winch or hydraulic puller then drags the bursting head and the new pipe through. It goes quickly.
