    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Daly City, CA

    Sewer lines in Daly City, CA can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or misalign. Expansive clay soils are common across Northern California, and seasonal swelling and shrinking can shift slabs and buried sewer laterals. Tree root intrusion is another major culprit, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also add to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Sectional Point Repair in Daly City

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are a good fit on otherwise healthy lines.

    Pipe Bursting in Daly City, CA

    Pipe bursting replaces a badly damaged line by pulling a bursting head through the old pipe. The head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe follows behind it. Bursting is ideal for collapsed or severely offset lines that cannot be lined. It typically requires an entry pit and an exit pit.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Daly City, CA

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is well suited for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a fast and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Northern California homeowners.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Costs in Daly City, CA

    While the upfront cost of trenchless sewer repair in Daly City, CA can sometimes be comparable to or slightly higher than traditional methods, the overall savings are often significant. This is because trenchless repairs eliminate the need for extensive excavation, backfilling and landscape restoration. Homeowners save money on repairing driveways, patios and gardens, making it a more sensible choice in the long run for many.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Daly City

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot get into. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their shape.
