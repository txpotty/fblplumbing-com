    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Daly City

    High static pressure shortens the life of the service line and every fixture behind it. The usual code limit is 80 psi. Old gate valves that will not close get replaced with full-port ball valves. So the shutoff works when you need it.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Daly City, CA

    Galvanized service lines were common in houses built before the 1970s, and a lot of them are still in the ground. Rural properties around Daly City often rely on private wells, which may need treatment for iron, sediment, or other minerals. Scale builds up inside and the opening shrinks. Rusty water at the first tap in the morning is a classic sign, and so is pressure that falls off when the shower and the washer run together. Replacing the full run with copper or PEX clears all of it up. Usually a one-day job.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Daly City

    Buried copper is tough, but it is not forever. Pinholes show up where stones press on the pipe, and acidic soil or high water velocity can thin the wall from the inside.
