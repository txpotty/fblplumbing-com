    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Installation in Daly City

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon gas tank usually costs $1,800 to $4,000 installed. Power vent and tankless gas units often cost more because of the venting and gas line work involved.

    Fuel and burner setup are the difference. Propane runs at a different pressure than natural gas and needs its own orifice. Either fuel is fine.

    Gas usually recovers faster and can cost less to run where gas rates are reasonable. Electric skips the vent and the gas line.

    A standard tank replacement usually takes three to four hours. Vent or gas work adds time.

    We advise against doing it yourself. A mistake in the gas joint, the vent or the water lines can be dangerous or expensive to fix later. Licensed plumbers handle it safely.

    Doing it yourself means working with gas lines, water lines and exhaust venting at once. Mistakes can cause gas leaks, carbon monoxide poisoning or water damage, and many areas require a licensed plumber for the work anyway. Hire a licensed plumber.

    Most gas tanks carry a manufacturer warranty of 6 to 12 years on the tank and parts, with longer coverage on premium models that use thicker steel and better anodes. FBL Plumbing also warranties our labor. We explain both before you buy.

    Flush the tank once a year and check the anode rod every few years. Sediment and a worn anode are the two things that kill gas tanks early. We can do the service.
