    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity.     Drain Line Repiping in Daly City

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Repeat clogs, sewer smells and several slow drains at once often mean the drain pipe itself is failing.     Residential Repiping Plans in Daly City

    Every house is piped a little differently, so a repipe starts with a walk-through.     Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Daly City

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. Our plumbers solder joints with lead-free solder or use press fittings, support the pipe at the spacing the code calls for and pressure test the system before any wall is closed.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Daly City

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. That is often the first sign. New PEX or copper stops the rust, restores pressure and ends leaks at the threads, which is where galvanized fails first. We disconnect and abandon the old pipe where removing it would mean tearing out finished walls.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Daly City, CA

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Because PEX flexes, it absorbs some of the shock that causes water hammer, and long runs with few fittings mean fewer places for a leak to start inside a wall. We keep PEX out of sunlight and away from water heater flues.
