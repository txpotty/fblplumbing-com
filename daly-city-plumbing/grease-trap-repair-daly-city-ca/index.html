    Damaged Baffle Repair in Daly City

    Baffles are the walls inside the trap that slow the water down long enough for grease to float and solids to sink. Once one cracks or falls loose, the trap is just a box that grease passes through. We pump it out and pull the old baffle. Plastic units get a factory replacement part. Steel ones often need a fabricated baffle or a new trap.

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Daly City

    Inside a concrete gravity interceptor the baffle is usually a poured or precast wall with a crossover opening. When that wall spalls, grease slips through to the second compartment. We can rebuild the wall with form work and a sulfate-resistant mix or set a polyethylene baffle panel, depending on the tank. Either way, it needs a full pump-out first.

    Commercial Grease Interceptor Repair in Daly City, CA

    In-ground gravity interceptors run from about 500 gallons to several thousand, usually precast concrete with two compartments. Hydrogen sulfide from the waste eats the concrete above the waterline over time. We pump the tank, inspect the walls and the crossover, and repair spalled concrete with a cementitious patch or an epoxy liner rated for that service. Entry follows confined space rules.

    Grease Trap Leak Repair in Daly City

    Plastic and fiberglass trap bodies do not rust, but they can crack from a dropped lid or a dishwasher dumping water hotter than the trap is rated for. A hairline crack can sometimes be welded. You will see the crack yourself before we suggest a fix.

    Grease Trap Drain Line Repair in Daly City

    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Daly City, CA

    Tees are cheap parts with an outsized job. The inlet tee sends flow down below the grease layer. We replace broken tees in Schedule 40 PVC and set the drop to the depth the code or the manufacturer calls for.
