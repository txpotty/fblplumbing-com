    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Upgrades in Daly City, CA

    Street pressure varies a lot from one neighborhood to the next. Plumbing code caps house pressure at 80 psi.     PEX Water Line Installation in Daly City, CA

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house.     Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Daly City, CA

    Bursting fits old cast iron or galvanized lines that will shatter under the head. Pulling suits softer pipe like poly or copper.
