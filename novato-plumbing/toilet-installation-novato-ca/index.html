    Standard Toilet Installation in Novato

    A standard floor-mount toilet is a two-hour job when the flange is sound. The stop gets shut, the tank drained, and the old bowl lifted out. Once the old wax is scraped off, the flange gets checked for cracks, new stainless bolts go in, and the new bowl is lowered straight onto a fresh seal. The old one leaves with us.

    Toilet Brands We Install in Novato

    We install Kohler, TOTO, American Standard, Gerber and most other brands sold at supply houses and home centers. Every brand has its quirks. Some skirted TOTO models use a Unifit adapter, and certain Kohler tanks take a gasket that hardware stores rarely stock. We keep those parts on hand.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Novato

    A cracked tank or a bowl leaking at the base cannot wait for next week, especially in a house with one bathroom. Standard two-piece toilets ride on our trucks.     Toilet Replacement and Upgrades in Novato, CA

    Swapping an old 3.5 gallon toilet for a modern one is the easiest water savings in the house. Pulling the old one takes around twenty minutes. The rest is flange cleanup and shimming the new bowl level on a floor that has settled with age. We haul the old one away.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Novato, CA

    Wall-hung toilets need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep, or a furred-out chase built in front of an existing 2 by 4 wall. The floor underneath stays open. That makes mopping easier and lets you set the bowl height anywhere within the carrier's range, which is handy for tall households or anyone who wants a comfort-height seat without a tall bowl. Service happens through the flush plate.

    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Novato

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. Below about 20 psi, they flush poorly. We check static pressure at a hose bib before recommending one, and we warn customers about the noise, since the flush is loud enough to hear across the house. They rarely clog.

    Commercial Restroom Toilets in Novato, CA

    Restaurants and offices get heavy-duty fixtures and accessible stalls built to code. Downtime costs a business money. We schedule commercial installs early in the morning or after hours, and we stage the fixtures and valves on site so each stall is down for as short a time as possible.
