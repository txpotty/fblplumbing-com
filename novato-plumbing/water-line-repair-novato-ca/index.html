    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Novato, CA

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Rural properties around Novato often rely on private wells, which may need filtration for iron, sediment, or other minerals. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which chokes flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe seldom holds up because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and ends repeat leaks.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Novato, CA

    A single leak on a newer copper or PEX line is usually a simple spot repair. Repeat leaks, galvanized pipe or a line older than about 40 years usually justify full replacement. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars, while a full replacement costs more but resets the clock for decades.
