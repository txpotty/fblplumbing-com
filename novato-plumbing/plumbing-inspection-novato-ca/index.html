    What a Plumbing Inspection Covers in Novato, CA

    Preventive Maintenance Checks in Novato, CA

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. We flush the water heater, exercise the main shutoff and angle stops, check pressure and look for slow drains and weeping fittings. Plan on an hour or so. Yearly or every-other-year checks stretch heater life and keep valves from seizing.

    Pre-Purchase Inspection With Sewer Video in Novato

    Plumbing problems are some of the most expensive surprises after closing. We check supply pipe, record the sewer, date the water heater and test every fixture. We send the report to your agent too. Buyers often use the findings to request repairs or credits, and the sewer video by itself can head off a very costly surprise.

    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Novato, CA

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. Too much pressure wears parts. Anything over 80 psi cuts the life of hoses, fixtures and heaters, and we note the supply pipe type, from copper and CPVC to poly, Kitec and galvanized.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Novato

    Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running mineral-heavy. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. We look for crusty aerators, scaled showerheads, slow-filling toilets and sediment in the water heater, and we can run a simple hardness test on the spot. You get options, not a pitch. Softeners, filters and scale reducers all help, and we suggest the one that matches the reading we took.

    Plumbing Inspection Costs in Novato, CA

    A basic plumbing inspection usually costs $150 to $300, and adding a sewer camera usually brings it to $250 to $500. Larger homes are priced by scope. Compared to an unexpected sewer replacement, which can run $4,000 to $15,000 or more, an inspection is cheap protection, and we give you the exact price when you book.

    Gas Connection Safety Check in Novato, CA

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance. Old brass connectors, missing shutoffs or drip legs and rust all get noted, and we sweep joints with a gas detector. Leaks get reported immediately. Most of our gas findings are in older homes, often at original appliance connectors.

    Water Heater Inspections in Novato

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. The serial number tells us the age, and we check for rust, look at the vent and test the relief valve. We check tankless units for scale. On gas units, we look at the burner and flame, check for a sediment trap and confirm the vent is drafting so combustion gases go outside.

    Residential Plumbing Inspections in Novato
