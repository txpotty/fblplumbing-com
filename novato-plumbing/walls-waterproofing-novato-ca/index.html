    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Novato

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most reliable fix for a leaking basement, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing so water has somewhere to go. Many older homes here sit on raised foundations with crawlspaces, which make supply and drain repairs easier to reach than slab work. Backfill goes in last.
