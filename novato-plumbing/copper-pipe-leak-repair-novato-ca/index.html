    Copper Pipe Joint Leak Repair in Novato, CA

    Joints leak from poor original soldering, thermal expansion or a house that settles.     Burst Copper Pipe Repair in Novato, CA

    Copper in an exterior wall or attic can split when the water inside freezes. Mountain and foothill homes in Northern California see real winters, so pipes there need insulation, heat tape, or proper draining when a cabin sits empty. Close the main valve. The split often does not leak until the ice thaws, which is why many bursts are found hours after the cold. We cut out the split and solder in new pipe. Then we add insulation.

    Split Copper Line Repair in Novato, CA

    A copper line can split from a freeze, a settling foundation or a hit from a nail. Splits leak hard. Insulation goes on exposed runs.

    Thin-Wall Copper Pinhole Repair in Novato

    Older copper, especially Type M, pinholes more than heavier pipe. Thin walls wear through first. We locate the leak precisely and either solder in a new section or use a press fitting near framing, with new pipe that is heavier than the original. We check the rest of the run.

    Hot Water Copper Line Repair in Novato, CA

    Hot water lines corrode faster than cold lines. Temperature drives it. Recirculation pumps that run around the clock push water through the copper at high velocity and wear it from the inside, which is why we often add a timer to the pump after fixing the leak. Less run time, less wear.
