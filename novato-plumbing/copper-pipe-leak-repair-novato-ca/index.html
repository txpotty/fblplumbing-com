    Corrosion-Related Leak Repair in Novato

    Corrosion is a natural enemy of copper pipes and is made worse by certain water chemistries. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. This can lead to thinning pipe walls and eventual leaks. Our team assesses the extent of corrosion, which may show up as blue-green stains on pipes or fixtures.
