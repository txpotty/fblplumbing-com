    Common Sink Repairs in Novato, CA

    Bathroom Sink Repair in Novato, CA

    Bath sinks have small parts that wear. The pop-up linkage sits in a damp cabinet for years, and the pivot nut where the rod enters the drain is the most common leak we find on a bathroom sink. We check pedestal and wall-hung sinks the same way. Vanity sinks just hide the leak better.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Novato

    Check the slip nuts first. Plastic slip nuts crack when they are overtightened with pliers, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until water seeps out at every joint along the trap. We replace the trap as one kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Faucet Base Leak Repair in Novato

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. It usually means worn O-rings on a swivel spout or a failed base gasket, and on some faucets it means the body has cracked and the whole faucet needs replacing. Every faucet type rebuilds differently. We identify yours before ordering parts.

    Repair or Replace? Sink Costs in Novato, CA

    Repair is cheaper nine times out of ten. Replace the sink only if the bowl is cracked, the glaze is chipped through, or the drain has rusted into the bowl. We quote both ways if it is a close call. Some people repair now and remodel next year.

    Commercial Sink Repair in Novato

    Restaurants and salons cannot wait a week for a sink. Three-compartment sinks and hand sinks have to work for the health inspector, so we schedule around your hours and bring commercial-grade faucets and basket strainers. Grease is the usual enemy. We also check the grease interceptor connection when the drain backs up.

    Kitchen Sink Repair in Novato

    Kitchen sinks take the most abuse in the house. Strainers lose their putty seal, sprayer hoses split inside the cabinet, and disposal flanges vibrate loose over the years. We fix the part that is wet. A cracked composite or porcelain bowl is a replacement, and we will say so.

    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in Novato, CA

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We look past the wall with a longer cable or a camera for a sag or for buildup where the sink line meets the tub. Now and then it is a plugged vent. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.
