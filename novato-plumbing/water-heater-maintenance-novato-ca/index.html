    Tankless Descaling in Novato, CA

    Scale is the tankless unit's main enemy. Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running mineral-heavy.     Anode Rod Checks in Novato

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. It is a long metal rod screwed into the top of the tank, and once it has corroded down to bare wire, the water starts eating the steel tank lining instead. We pull it and look. Replacing the rod is much cheaper than replacing the heater.

    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Novato, CA

    Gas units need a clean burner and a good draft. We check for gas leaks at the valve and connector, watch the flame, clean the burner and confirm the flue pulls exhaust up and out instead of spilling it into the room. Carbon monoxide is the concern. We also test the thermocouple.

    Water Heater Problem Diagnosis in Novato, CA

    Something sounds or feels wrong. Rumbling, short hot water or a wet pan get diagnosed on the spot, with a straight answer on repair or replace. We lay out choices. No pressure either way.
