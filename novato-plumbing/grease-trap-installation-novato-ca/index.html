    Grease that gets past a trap hardens in the sewer lateral like candle wax, and the first sign is usually a floor sink backing up during service. Jetting clears it, but it comes back.     Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in Novato, CA

    Concrete or polyethylene, a buried gravity interceptor is a two-compartment tank with tees on the inlet and outlet that reach down below the grease layer. Precast concrete weighs more and handles a parking lot better than plastic. Poly is easier to set.     New Restaurant Build-Out Grease Traps in Novato

    Replacement and Upgrade Services in Novato, CA

    Steel traps from decades back rust through at the baffle seams, and by then the outlet is usually passing grease straight to the sewer.     Commercial Kitchen Grease Trap Installs in Novato

    Every commercial install starts with a fixture list. We count each sink, floor drain and dish machine that will tie in, then add the gallons per minute each one can dump. That number decides between an indoor unit and an outdoor tank, and where it can go with clean pipe runs to the sewer. Fewer bends, fewer clogs.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Trap Installation in Novato, CA

    Hydromechanical traps use baffles and a vented flow control to pull grease out of water moving at a set rate, usually 20 to 100 gallons per minute. They fit indoors, under a sink or in a floor pit. The flow control is not optional. Skip it and a full sink drain overruns the rating, which sends grease right past the baffles. We look for units certified to PDI G-101 or ASME A112.14.3.

    Homes rarely need a grease trap, though some do. We see them in licensed home kitchens and in houses where the sewer lateral keeps clogging with grease. A small 4 to 7 GPM unit under the kitchen sink catches most of it, and cleaning takes about ten minutes with a spatula and a bucket. Check with your local health office first.
