    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Novato

    Hydro-mechanical grease interceptors, often called HGI units, are compact and designed for indoor use, typically under a sink or on the floor. These units use baffles to slow down wastewater flow, allowing grease to separate and accumulate. FBL Plumbing installs various sizes of HGI units, ensuring proper venting, leveling, and connection to your drainage system in Novato for the best efficiency.

    Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in Novato, CA

    Gravity grease interceptors, or GGI units, are larger, usually located outdoors, and rely on the natural difference in specific gravity between FOG and water. These are common for high-volume commercial kitchens. Our team handles the complex excavation, concrete work, and plumbing connections required for proper GGI installation, maintaining compliance with all local Northern California ordinances for waste management.

    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Novato

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with limited space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid without trouble.
