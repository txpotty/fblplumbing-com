    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Novato

    Residential hydro jetting in Novato usually costs $350 to $800, depending on line length, access and how heavy the buildup is. Commercial jetting is often priced by the hour and may run higher.

    While hydro jetting effectively removes existing tree roots from your sewer line, it does not prevent new roots from growing back. For a long-term solution to recurring root intrusion, we might recommend trenchless sewer repair or pipe replacement, which we can go over during our assessment.

    Hydro jetting is very effective against almost all types of clogs. It can break through solid obstructions like tree roots, flush out heavy grease and food buildup, clear mineral scale, and remove compacted sludge and debris that traditional snaking often leaves behind.

    Drain snaking uses a cable with an auger that punches a hole through a blockage to restore some flow. Hydro jetting, however, uses high-pressure water to completely scour the inside of the pipe, removing all buildup and restoring the pipe to a near-new condition. Hydro jetting offers a more complete and longer-lasting clean.

    FBL Plumbing uses professional-grade hydro jetting equipment from trusted manufacturers like Spartan Tool and MyTana. These brands are known for their reliability, powerful performance, and safety features, so we can deliver the most effective cleaning for your pipes.

    Most residential jobs take 1 to 3 hours including inspection. Long commercial lines or heavy grease can take longer.

    While small, low-pressure drain jetters are available for rent, professional hydro jetting equipment is far more powerful and requires training to operate safely and effectively. Improper use can damage pipes, cause injury, or fail to clear the blockage. For severe clogs, it is always best to rely on experienced professionals like FBL Plumbing.

    For most homes in Novato, hydro jetting every 1 to 3 years can be a good preventative measure, especially if you have recurring drain issues or older pipes. Businesses, particularly restaurants, may benefit from quarterly or twice-yearly hydro jetting to manage grease buildup. We can suggest a schedule based on your needs.
