    Undermount Sink Installation in Novato, CA

    Undermounts hang from clips anchored into the underside of the stone, with silicone run around the rim. A sink that drops even an eighth of an inch opens a gap where water and crumbs collect along the counter edge.

    Apron-Front Sinks in Novato, CA

    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Novato

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and a lower gauge means thicker steel.
