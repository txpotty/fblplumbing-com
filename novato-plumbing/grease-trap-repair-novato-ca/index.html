    Grease Line Clog Repair in Novato, CA

    Grease that hardens in the lines before or after the trap can cause backups even when the trap itself is clean. We use hydro jetting to clear the pipe and remove buildup.     Clogged Grease Trap Repair in Novato

    Grease traps often clog due to excessive accumulation of fats, oils, and grease (FOGs) or food solids. Our technicians use high-pressure hydro-jetting to break down tough blockages and thoroughly clean the internal baffles and lines. We also inspect for underlying issues like damaged pipes or improper disposal practices that contribute to repeat clogs, offering solutions to prevent future blockages. Identifying the exact location and nature of the clog is key for effective repair and long-term performance in your Novato establishment.

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Novato

    Baffles slow incoming water so grease can rise and solids can settle. When baffles crack, corrode or break loose, grease passes straight through the trap. We remove damaged baffles and install new ones made for your trap model. In concrete interceptors, we can repair or rebuild baffle walls. Restoring baffles brings back proper separation and helps you stay compliant.

    Flow Control Device Repair in Novato

    Hydromechanical traps rely on a flow control fitting to keep water moving at the rated speed. If this fitting is missing or clogged, the trap can be overwhelmed and grease escapes. We inspect, clean or replace flow control devices during repair visits. We also check the air intake that allows the device to work properly. Correct flow control is essential for a trap to pass inspection.

    A leaking grease trap can cause significant property damage, foul odors, and potential health code violations. Leaks often stem from corroded tanks, cracked seams, or failing pipe connections. We use leak detection techniques to locate the source and provide targeted repairs, which may involve patching the tank, sealing connections, or replacing damaged sections of the trap or associated plumbing. Addressing leaks quickly is essential to maintain a sanitary environment and avoid costly remediation in Novato, CA.
