    Effective Storm Drain Cleaning Services in Novato, CA

    Downspout and Roof Drain Lines in Novato, CA

    Many homes connect downspouts to buried pipe that carries water away from the foundation. These lines clog with roof granules, leaves and nests, then back up and dump water at the wall. We jet them clear and test with a hose to confirm flow at the outlet. For flat roofs with scuppers, we check that the discharge path is open too. Keeping water away from the slab helps protect against soil movement and cracking.

    Root Removal from Storm Drains in Novato

    Tree roots are a common cause of severe storm drain blockages in Novato, especially in older neighborhoods with mature trees. Roots seek out moisture and can work into drain pipes through tiny cracks or loose joints, growing and eventually completely blocking water flow. FBL Plumbing uses dedicated root-cutting attachments on our drain snakes and hydro jetting equipment to cut through and remove root intrusions. After removal, we can suggest further steps to prevent future root problems.

    Preventative Storm Drain Cleaning in Novato, CA

    Proactive cleaning is key to avoiding major issues with storm drains in Novato, CA. We offer scheduled preventative maintenance to keep your drains clear of leaves, debris, and sediment throughout the year.     Emergency Storm Drain Clearing in Novato

    If water is pooling against your home during a storm, a clogged drain is often the cause. We respond to urgent calls to clear the blockage and get water flowing.     Yard and Area Drain Cleaning in Novato

    Yard drains and area inlets collect runoff from lawns, rock landscaping and patios. Over time the grates fill with leaves and the pipes fill with silt and small gravel. We pull the grates, vacuum the inlet box and jet the pipe toward its outlet. This restores the line so it can handle a full storm. We also check that grates are secure and seated.

    Hydro Jetting for Storm Drains in Novato, CA

    Hydro jetting is a powerful solution for deeply clogged storm drains in Novato, CA. This method uses high-pressure water streams to wash out stubborn obstructions like compacted soil, heavy silt, roots, and grease buildup. It is very effective for thoroughly cleaning the inside of the pipe walls, restoring the drain to its full capacity and preventing a quick return of blockages, a common issue with older drainage systems.
