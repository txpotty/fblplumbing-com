    Softener Not Regenerating in Alameda

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. The usual causes are a dead timer motor, a bad board, a stuck meter or settings wiped by an outage. Each part gets tested. After the repair, the valve is programmed for your household size and hardness so you have soft water by the next cycle.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Alameda

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. We break the bridge, clean out the mushy salt and check the float and brine well. A cracked brine tank gets swapped for a new one.

    Water Softener Repair vs. Replacement in Alameda, CA

    Age and parts availability decide whether a softener is worth repairing. FBL Plumbing gives honest advice on both counts. A sound tank with a rebuildable valve is worth saving, but an obsolete head without parts usually is not. We lay out both options.
