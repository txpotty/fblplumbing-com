    Trenchless Sewer Line Replacement in Alameda, CA

    To minimize disruption, trenchless sewer line replacement replaces pipes without extensive digging. We offer pipe bursting, where a new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward. This is ideal when protecting landscaping or driveways is a priority. The method works well for many homes in Alameda and can often be completed faster than traditional methods, using high-density polyethylene (HDPE) pipe known for its long life.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair & Replacement in Alameda, CA

    Trenchless technology, such as pipe bursting or pipe lining, offers a less invasive alternative to traditional methods. Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one, fracturing the old pipe in place. Pipe lining installs a new, durable resin liner inside the existing pipe. Both methods greatly reduce the need for excavation, preserving your yard and landscaping in Alameda, CA while providing a long-lasting solution.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Alameda, CA

    Traditional excavation is sometimes the best option, especially when the old line has a severe belly or the slope must be corrected. We dig along the line, remove the old pipe and set new PVC on compacted bedding at the correct grade. Rocky or hard-packed soil can slow digging, and we account for that in the estimate. Each joint is glued and checked before backfill. The inspector sees the open trench before we cover it.

    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in Alameda

    Many older homes in Alameda may have sewer lines made from outdated materials like clay or cast iron, which are prone to cracking, corrosion, and root invasion. We offer upgrades to modern, more durable materials such as PVC or HDPE (high-density polyethylene). These materials stand up to corrosion, roots, and shifting soil, giving your sewer system a much longer lifespan.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Alameda

    Pipe bursting replaces the old sewer line by pulling a new pipe through the path of the old one. A bursting head breaks the old pipe outward while fused HDPE pipe follows close behind. This typically needs only an entry pit and an exit pit instead of a long trench. It works well under driveways, mature trees and landscaping. The new pipe is seamless, which leaves no seams for roots to enter.
