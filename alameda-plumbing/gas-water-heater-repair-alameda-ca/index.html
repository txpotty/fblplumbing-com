    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Alameda

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust out through PVC pipe. Failures usually involve the blower motor, pressure switch, hot surface igniter or condensate in the vent. We read the error codes, test each part and repair the failure. Correct vent slope and termination are verified before we leave.

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Alameda

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it detects vapors or overheating. Lint and dust in garages can clog the arrestor and shut down the system. We clean the combustion chamber and replace sensors or thermal switches as required.

    Pilot Light and Burner Issues in Alameda

    A frequent problem with gas water heaters is a pilot light that won't stay lit or a burner that fails to ignite. This can be caused by a faulty thermocouple, a dirty pilot tube or issues with the gas control valve. Our technicians in Alameda are adept at diagnosing these components and performing the needed repairs, whether it is cleaning, adjusting or replacing parts. We ensure proper gas flow and ignition for consistent hot water.

    Venting and Draft Repair in Alameda, CA

    Atmospheric gas water heaters rely on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home. Disconnected sections, rusted draft hoods and poor slope can let combustion gases spill back. We repair or replace vent sections and verify draft with a test. Proper venting is critical for your family's safety.

    Pilot Light Repair in Alameda

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most frequent gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and clean or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are completed in about an hour.

    Gas Water Heater Noise Troubleshooting in Alameda

    Odd noises coming from your gas water heater, such as rumbling, popping or whistling, often indicate sediment buildup in the tank or a burner problem. Hard water can add to sediment accumulation. Our repair service includes flushing the tank and inspecting for sediment, addressing pressure issues or checking other components that might be causing the noise, restoring efficiency and quiet operation.
