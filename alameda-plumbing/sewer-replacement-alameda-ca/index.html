    Clay and Orangeburg Pipe Replacement in Alameda, CA

    Clay pipe joints let roots in, while Orangeburg pipe made from tar paper deforms over time. Both are likely to fail. We replace these lines with modern materials. Camera inspection shows the extent of damage. Replacement ends ongoing root problems.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Alameda

    Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE pipe through the old line while breaking the old pipe apart. It needs only small access pits at each end. This protects landscaping, driveways and patios. It works well for clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe. Most jobs are completed in one day.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Alameda, CA

    A cleanout provides simple access for future cleaning and inspection. Many older homes lack an exterior cleanout. We install one during sewer replacement. This saves time and money on later maintenance. It also helps in emergencies.

    Sewer Pipe Material Upgrades in Alameda, CA

    Many older homes in Alameda, CA still have original sewer lines made of clay, cast iron, or Orangeburg pipe, which are prone to degradation, cracking, and root intrusion over time. When we perform a sewer replacement, we often recommend upgrading to modern, durable materials like PVC (polyvinyl chloride) or ABS (acrylonitrile butadiene styrene). These materials are more flexible, resistant to corrosion and roots, and have a much longer lifespan, providing a significant upgrade to your home's plumbing infrastructure.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement Options in Alameda, CA
