    Annual Sump Pump Check-ups in Alameda

    Regular annual maintenance is crucial for the longevity and reliability of your sump pump. Our technicians perform a thorough inspection, checking the motor, impeller, float switch and discharge lines for any signs of wear or blockage. We also test the system's operation by filling the pit with water to ensure it activates correctly and pumps water away efficiently, protecting your Alameda home from unexpected flooding.

    Commercial and Elevator Pit Pumps in Alameda

    Commercial buildings often have sump pumps in elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms. These pumps may need oil-minder controls or alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. We test duplex systems, alternating controls and high-water alarms on a scheduled basis. Maintenance logs help property managers stay on top of inspections.

    Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Alameda

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, gravel and leaves settle in the pit. That debris can get sucked into the intake screen and jam the impeller. We pump down and scoop out the basin, rinse the intake and inspect the pit liner for cracks. A clean basin helps the pump run cooler and last longer.

    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Alameda, CA

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil around the slab or foundation.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Alameda, CA

    Many Alameda, CA homeowners rely on battery backup sump pumps for protection during power outages. FBL Plumbing provides thorough maintenance for these critical systems, including testing the battery's charge and health, inspecting the backup pump's operation and checking the charging system. This keeps your home protected even when the primary power source fails, a common concern during severe storms.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Check-Ups in Alameda, CA

    For homes equipped with pedestal sump pumps, FBL Plumbing provides dedicated maintenance services in Alameda, CA.
