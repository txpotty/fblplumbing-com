    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Alameda

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the different advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, typically using crimp or compression fittings. Our plumbers in Alameda can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    PEX for Water Line Replacement in Alameda

    When your main water line from the street to your home needs replacement, PEX is an excellent option. PEX main lines handle ground movement and resist corrosion better than rigid pipe. Earthquakes and hillside soil movement are real risks in Northern California, so flexible connections and seismic straps help protect plumbing from ground movement.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Alameda
