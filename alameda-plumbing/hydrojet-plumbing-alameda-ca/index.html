    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Alameda, CA

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. A jet scours the entire pipe wall. Snaking suits a one-time clog, while jetting fixes a line that clogs repeatedly.

    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Alameda

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. Jetting the main clears roots, grease and sludge all the way to the city connection. On a 4-inch line we use a heavier nozzle with more flow, which moves the loosened debris downstream instead of leaving it to settle in a low spot.

    Hard Water Scale Removal in Alameda, CA

    Scale builds slowly inside drain pipe, narrowing it a little more each year. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. A jetter with a scouring nozzle strips that scale from the walls, and on heavily scaled cast iron we may follow up with a descaling chain head to finish the job. The difference shows on camera.

    Root Removal Jetting in Alameda

    Shade trees and shrubs send roots toward small cracks in clay and cast iron pipe. A root nozzle cuts them out. Jetting does not stop roots from returning, because the crack is still there, but it clears the pipe completely and lets us see on camera whether a spot repair or a liner would stop the regrowth.

    Residential Hydrojetting in Alameda

    Most home jetting calls involve a greasy kitchen line or a root-filled sewer. Whenever possible, we work from the outside cleanout and keep the hose out of the house. Some kitchen lines get jetted from under the sink. Floors get protected.

    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in Alameda

    Preventive jetting makes sense when the same line clogs more than once a year. Camera findings set the interval. A line with heavy grease might need jetting every six months, while a sewer with a few root entry points might only need it every 18 to 24 months, and the footage tells us which.

    Water-Only Drain Cleaning in Alameda, CA

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Caustic cleaners can harm old pipe. Jetting removes the buildup physically, and the debris flushes to the sewer or septic tank as normal waste, so there is nothing left in the pipe to eat at joints or harm the bacteria a septic system depends on.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Alameda, CA

    Older cast iron drains corrode and build scale on the inner wall, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. Jetting can remove that buildup. Thin or cracked cast iron gets gentler settings or a lining recommendation instead.

    A local jetting crew has a good idea of the pipe before arriving. Older blocks usually mean clay. Newer neighborhoods have PVC that takes full pressure, while older ones need gentler settings, and knowing that before we arrive means the right nozzles are already on the truck.
