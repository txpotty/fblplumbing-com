    Commercial Restroom Plumbing in Alameda

    A broken flushometer in a busy restroom gets noticed by every customer who walks in. We repair and replace Sloan and Zurn flush valves, sensor faucets, wall-hung toilets and carriers.     Commercial Water Heater Service in Alameda, CA

    Big tank heaters fail in a few common ways: a failed gas valve, a cracked flue baffle, a stuck T and P valve or a tank full of scale. Each sounds different. We check draft and combustion, test water temperature at the far end of the building and flush the tank, then tell you whether repair or replacement makes more sense. Tankless racks get descaled. Recirculation pumps on long runs get tested as part of the visit.

    Commercial Sewer Line Repair in Alameda, CA

    The building sewer on a commercial property carries everything from every tenant, so a root intrusion or a belly in the line affects the whole property. First we run a camera and locate the damage depth, then we compare a spot dig, a cured-in-place liner and pipe bursting. Trenchless saves parking lots. Many 6-inch laterals can be lined in a single day without cutting the parking lot or closing a door.

    Backflow Prevention Assembly Service in Alameda

    A backflow assembly keeps water from your building, including irrigation lines, boiler water and chemical feeds, from flowing back into the public supply. Most providers want a yearly test. Our certified testers check reduced pressure zone assemblies, double check valves and pressure vacuum breakers with calibrated differential gauges, then file the report with your water provider. A failed test usually means new rubber and springs, not a whole new assembly. We carry Watts, Wilkins and Febco kits for that reason.

    Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Alameda

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. We use high-pressure jetting with rotating nozzles that scrub grease off the whole circumference of 4-inch and 6-inch lines, not just the bottom. A jetted line stays open far longer than a cabled one. Restaurants, schools and apartment stacks are where we see the biggest difference.

    Sewer Camera Inspection for Businesses in Alameda

    A camera inspection shows the inside of a drain line on a monitor and records it so you can share it with a landlord or contractor. Guesswork goes away. The camera goes in at a cleanout, we note distances to every defect and then locate each one from the surface with a receiver. The video is yours to keep. That record makes capital planning far easier when the board asks why the sewer needs money.
