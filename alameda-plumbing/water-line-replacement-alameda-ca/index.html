    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Alameda, CA

    Main Water Line Replacement in Alameda

    Your service line runs from the meter or curb stop to the main shutoff inside the house, usually as 3/4-inch or 1-inch pipe. We dig at both ends, pull or cut out the old pipe, and run new line at the depth local code calls for, with fresh connections at the meter and at the shutoff. Most homes are done in a day.

    Trenchless Bores Under Driveways in Alameda

    Directional boring drills a path under the obstacle and pulls the pipe back through it. No old line is needed to follow.     Copper Water Line Installation in Alameda

    Type K copper is the thick-wall grade made for burial. Street pressure does not bother it. We bed it in sand, keep it away from sharp rock, and sleeve it where it passes through a foundation wall, because concrete and copper rubbing together for twenty years eventually wear a hole.     Galvanized Main Line Replacement in Alameda

    Galvanized steel was common in homes built before the 1960s. The zinc coating wears off and the steel rusts from the inside.     PEX Service Lines for Long Runs in Alameda

Fewer joints underground means fewer places a leak can start, and it is the joints that usually fail first.
