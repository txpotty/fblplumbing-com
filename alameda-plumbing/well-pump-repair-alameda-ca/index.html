    Jet Pump System Repair in Alameda, CA

    Jet pumps, typically found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Common problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. FBL Plumbing can reliably troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Alameda operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is crucial for jet pump function.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Alameda, CA

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps prematurely. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank. Correct tank sizing protects the pump and gives steadier pressure at the tap.
