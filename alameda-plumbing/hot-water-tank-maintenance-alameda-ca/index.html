    Complete Hot Water Tank Maintenance Services in Alameda, CA

    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Alameda

    A properly functioning temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank. Our technicians will test this valve to ensure it's not stuck or leaking, preventing dangerous pressure buildup within the tank. We verify it opens and closes correctly, and if it fails, we will recommend immediate replacement to keep your Alameda home safe and compliant with plumbing codes.

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Alameda

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause noisy popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution approved for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Burner and Pilot Light Maintenance in Alameda, CA

    For gas hot water tanks, proper burner and pilot light operation is key for efficient heating.     Anode Rod Inspection & Replacement in Alameda, CA

    The anode rod is your hot water tank's sacrificial defense against corrosion. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. During our maintenance, we pull and inspect the anode rod. If it's badly depleted, we recommend and perform a replacement to extend the life of your tank in Alameda, CA, preventing rust from eating away at the tank lining.

    Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Alameda, CA

    Many homes put the water heater in a garage or attic where temperatures can swing hard. Heat speeds up the wear on plastic drain valves, gas valve seals and wiring insulation. We check drain pans, pan drain lines, platform height for gas units in garages and clearances around the tank. A leak in an attic tank without a working pan can destroy a ceiling, so we confirm that protection is in place.
