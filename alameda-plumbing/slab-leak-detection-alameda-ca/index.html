    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Alameda

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. Our ground microphones hear it.     Slab Leaks in Commercial Buildings in Alameda

    Thermal Scans on Large Floors in Alameda, CA

    Large open floors can hide a leak.     Tracer Gas Leak Detection in Alameda

    Plastic pipe muffles leak noise. For those, we drain the line and push in a safe hydrogen and nitrogen tracer gas, which rises through the slab and gets picked up by a sniffer at the floor surface, often through grout joints and hairline cracks. It catches leaks the microphone cannot in Alameda homes.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Alameda

    Many newer homes sit on post-tension slabs.
