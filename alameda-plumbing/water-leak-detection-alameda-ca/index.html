    Leak Detection for PEX and Copper Pipes in Alameda

    Whether your plumbing system uses modern PEX tubing or traditional copper pipes, FBL Plumbing has the expertise to detect leaks in both materials. PEX, while flexible, can be susceptible to damage from rodents or improper installation, while copper can corrode over time, especially with hard water. Our tools are effective across many pipe materials, allowing accurate detection regardless of your home's plumbing type.

    Water Heater and Softener Leak Detection in Alameda

    Water heaters and softeners can leak at fittings, valves and drain lines. A softener stuck in regeneration can send water down the drain continuously, and a dripping relief valve on a water heater wastes water too. We check these units carefully and repair or replace failed parts. That often explains a mystery bill in homes with water softeners.

    Irrigation Leak Detection in Alameda

    Drip and sprinkler systems run underground across yards and often account for hidden water loss. Cracked tubing, broken emitters and stuck valves can waste large amounts of water. We isolate the irrigation system, test each zone and trace leaking lines. Repairs restore proper watering for your landscape.

    Smart Leak Detection Device Installation in Alameda

    Smart shutoff valves like Moen Flo and Phyn monitor water flow and can shut off water automatically when they detect a leak. Small sensors placed near water heaters, sinks and washers send alerts to your phone. We install and set up these systems so you get warnings before small leaks become big ones. Some insurers offer discounts for these devices.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Alameda

    Leaks in underground water lines, from your meter to your home, can go unnoticed for extended periods, wasting water and causing yard erosion. Signs include oddly green patches in your lawn, persistent wet spots or a constantly running water meter. FBL Plumbing uses ground-penetrating radar and tracer gas detection methods to accurately map out and pinpoint leaks in buried pipes, allowing for targeted excavation and repair rather than digging up your entire yard.

    Advanced Leak Detection Technology in Alameda

    FBL Plumbing invests in modern leak detection technology to provide the most accurate and non-invasive service possible in Alameda. This includes high-resolution thermal cameras for detecting temperature changes caused by hidden moisture, sensitive acoustic sensors that listen for the faint sounds of escaping water, and video pipe inspection cameras for internal pipe diagnostics. These tools allow us to find leaks with minimal disruption to your property.

    Pool Water Leak Detection in Alameda, CA

    Pools lose a lot of water to evaporation in summer heat, so a leak can hide behind normal loss. We use a bucket test to compare evaporation with actual water loss. Then we pressure test pool plumbing and dye test fittings, lights and skimmers. Finding the leak safeguards the pool and the soil around it.

    Slab Leak Detection in Alameda

    Slab leaks are common in homes on shifting soil and moving foundations. These leaks occur in pipes beneath your concrete slab, often indicated by warm spots on the floor, sudden spikes in your water bill or the sound of running water when all fixtures are off. FBL Plumbing uses specialized acoustic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to precisely locate these hidden leaks without destructive guesswork. Early detection is key to preventing major foundation damage.
