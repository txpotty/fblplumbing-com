    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Alameda, CA

    Block and stem walls often show efflorescence, which is the white mineral crust left when water passes through masonry. Irrigation and sprinklers aimed at the house are frequent culprits. We adjust or reroute irrigation, clean the wall and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating. Proper grading and drip line placement help keep the wall dry for good.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Alameda, CA

    A cracked or poorly sloped shower pan often causes wet baseboards and stained ceilings below. We test the pan by plugging the drain and flooding it to verify the leak. Then we rebuild the pan with correct pre-slope, a new liner or bonded membrane and a properly clamped drain. A second flood test confirms the pan is watertight before tile.

    Drainage System Integration in Alameda, CA

    Lasting walls waterproofing often requires a robust drainage system to divert water away from your foundation. This can include interior perimeter drains that channel water to a sump pump, or exterior French drains that collect surface water. Our team designs and installs custom drainage solutions for Alameda, CA properties, so water is managed well and moved away from your home's vulnerable areas.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Alameda

    Newer homes across Northern California mostly use PEX or copper with plastic drains, and many owners are repiping older houses to restore water pressure. Wherever a home has a basement or below-grade walls, heavy rain and snowmelt can push water through foundation walls and cracks. We seal cracks with injection products, apply interior or exterior waterproofing and install drainage where needed. A sump pump may be added if groundwater collects.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Alameda

    Even small cracks in your foundation walls can allow water to seep into your basement or crawl space.     Waterproofing a shower or wall costs much less than repeated drywall, cabinet and flooring repairs. Ignoring moisture also invites mold, which can be expensive to remediate.     Interior Walls Waterproofing in Alameda, CA

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already penetrated the foundation walls. This usually involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and works well where exterior access is limited.
