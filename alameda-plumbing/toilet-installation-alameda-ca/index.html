    Bathroom Remodel Toilet Installation in Alameda, CA

    New tile over old vinyl can raise a bathroom floor by half an inch or more. That leaves the old flange sitting low.     Toilet Replacement and Upgrades in Alameda, CA

    Swapping an old 3.5 gallon toilet for a modern one is the easiest water savings in the house. Pulling the old one takes around twenty minutes. The rest is flange cleanup and shimming the new bowl level on a floor that has settled with age. We haul the old one away.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Alameda, CA

    Wall-hung toilets need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep, or a furred-out chase built in front of an existing 2 by 4 wall. Floors stay clear underneath. Cleaning gets easier, and the bowl height can be set anywhere within the carrier range, useful for a tall family. Service happens through the flush plate.

    Toilet Brands We Install in Alameda

    We install Kohler, TOTO, American Standard, Gerber and most other brands sold at supply houses and home centers. Each one has its own parts quirks. Some skirted TOTO models use a Unifit adapter, and certain Kohler tanks take a gasket that hardware stores rarely stock. We keep those parts on hand.

    Rough-In Toilet Installation in Alameda, CA

    Rough-in is the plumbing that goes in before walls and floors close up. For a toilet that means a 3 inch drain with a vent, plus a cold supply stub. The drain goes 12 inches out from the finished wall line, which means we add the drywall thickness to the framing measurement. Then the inspector sees it open.

    Smart Toilet Installation in Alameda, CA

    Smart toilets with heated seats and built-in bidets need two things the old toilet did not: power and a dedicated water connection. Most require a GFCI outlet within reach of the cord. We run the water side and mount the unit, and if a new outlet is needed we bring in a licensed electrician rather than splicing anything ourselves. Then we test every button.

    Customer-Supplied Toilet Installation in Alameda, CA

    Bought the toilet yourself? Check the rough-in printed on the box first. Before the old toilet comes out, we inspect the new one for shipping cracks, because cracked china goes back to the store, not onto your flange. We bring the seal and stainless bolts.
