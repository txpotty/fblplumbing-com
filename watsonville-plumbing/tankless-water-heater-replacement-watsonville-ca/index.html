    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Watsonville

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE or Rinnai Sensei pass 90 percent efficiency by pulling extra heat out of the exhaust. The vent is plain PVC. The condensate is acidic, so we install a neutralizer cartridge and run the line to an approved drain. Coastal fog and moisture can speed corrosion on exposed fittings, so exterior valves and hose bibs deserve an occasional check. The savings carry through every season because hot water use does.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Watsonville

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. Most need at least a 3/4-inch line. We check meter capacity and pipe length against the manufacturer's sizing chart, then confirm pressure with the unit running. An undersized line causes ignition faults.

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in Watsonville

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. A small under-sink unit needs one 20 or 30 amp circuit and suits a distant bath or break room. Swaps take an hour or two.

    Commercial Tankless Racks in Watsonville, CA

    Restaurants and salons run tankless racks hard. A rack of four units can draw more than 600,000 BTU, so we confirm gas pressure at the far unit with every burner firing. We work after hours when needed.

    Rinnai Condensing Upgrades in Watsonville, CA

    Rinnai Sensei condensing models replace older non-condensing Rinnai units in a lot of homes, but the venting changes from stainless to PVC. The vent swap is part of our job. Gas and water connections usually line up.

    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Watsonville

    A cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board on an older tankless is usually not worth repairing past the ten-year mark. We match the new unit to your gas line, vent type and mounting spot whenever we can. Plan on four to six hours.
