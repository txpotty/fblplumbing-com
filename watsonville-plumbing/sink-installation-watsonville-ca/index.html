    Sink Replacement and Upgrades in Watsonville

    Upgrading the sink is a cheap way to refresh a room. Countertop changes add time. We tell you which one you are looking at.

    Sink Installation Cost Comparison in Watsonville, CA

    Drop-in swaps cost the least. Undermounts cost more for clips and cure time, and vessels add a special drain and a taller faucet. Relocating the drain is the biggest add. We price each piece on the quote.

    Double Vanity Sink Installation in Watsonville

    A second bowl means a second drain and two more supplies. Each bowl drains through its own trap or through a continuous waste to a shared trap, and the second faucet gets new stops. Vent sizing gets checked. Both bowls should drain without gurgling.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Watsonville

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. The bowl hangs on clips with a full bead of silicone, and it stays braced overnight while the adhesive cures. Adhesive by itself will not hold it. Plumbing goes on the next day.

    Pedestal Sink Installation in Watsonville, CA

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. Since the trap and stops are right behind the column, we center the drain and use chrome parts that look finished. The wall needs solid blocking. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.

    Kitchen Sink Replacement in Watsonville

    Pulling the old kitchen sink is half the job. Old basket strainers fuse to the bowl, the disposal has to come off its mounting ring, and the dishwasher drain hose needs to be rerouted to the new disposal or tailpiece with a high loop. After that, the new bowl goes in. We reuse the disposal if it still runs well.

    Faucet and Drain Assembly for New Sinks in Watsonville, CA

    Faucet and drain go on before the sink is set, when possible. Mounting the faucet and the pop-up on a sink that is still on sawhorses takes a fraction of the time and gets a tighter seal than working upside down inside the cabinet. Undermounts are the exception. Those get plumbed from below.
