    Frequently Asked Questions About Clogged Drain Repair in Watsonville

    Clearing a single sink, tub or shower drain typically costs $125 to $300. Harder clogs that need a larger machine or access through a roof vent run $250 to $450. Hydro jetting a heavily scaled or greasy line typically costs $350 to $800.

    Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to clear stubborn blockages and completely clean the inside of your pipes. It's often necessary for severe clogs caused by tree roots, heavy grease buildup, or heavy mineral deposits from hard water, which traditional snaking might not fully clear.

    Enzyme or bacterial cleaners can help reduce organic buildup in kitchen and bathroom drains over time. They are gentler than caustic products and safe for most pipes. They will not clear a full clog but can help head off one.

    Caustic drain cleaners can damage ABS fittings, corrode older metal pipes and sit in the trap if they do not clear the clog. They also create a hazard for anyone working on the drain afterward. Mechanical clearing is safer and more effective.

    You should call a professional if a plunger or simple home remedies don't work, if you experience multiple clogged drains, sewage smells, or water backing up. These symptoms often point to a deeper, more serious issue in your main sewer line that requires expert diagnosis and specialized tools.

    While chemical drain cleaners are widely available, we generally advise against them. They often contain harsh chemicals that can corrode pipes, especially older ones, and can be hazardous to your health and the environment. Professional plumbers use safer, more effective methods.

    Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running mineral-heavy. Hard water leaves mineral scale that narrows pipe walls and gives grease and hair something to cling to. Older homes may also have cast iron pipes that rust and roughen inside. Both conditions make clogs form faster.

    You should call a professional plumber for clogged drain repair if DIY methods like plungers are ineffective, if you experience multiple clogged drains, or if you suspect a main sewer line blockage.
