    Sewer Pipe Material Upgrades in Watsonville, CA

    Code-Compliant Sewer Replacement in Watsonville

    Code sets the minimum slope, the cleanout spacing and the pipe type. Inspectors check all three in an open trench. We set fall at a quarter inch per foot unless the run requires otherwise, place cleanouts at the house and at major direction changes, and only bury pipe rated for it. Paperwork stays on file.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Watsonville, CA

    A lot of older houses have no outside cleanout. Without one, the plumber has to pull a toilet or go through the roof vent. When we replace a sewer line we add a two-way cleanout near the foundation, with a cap at ground level you can find, so future cleaning or camera work is quick and cheap.

    Under-Slab Sewer Replacement in Watsonville

    Sewer pipe under a slab is the hardest kind to reach. There are two options.     Trenchless Sewer Replacement Options in Watsonville, CA

    Bursting and pulling replace the pipe. CIPP lining coats the inside of the existing pipe instead. We use bursting when the old line is broken or undersized, because it leaves you with brand new pipe rather than a liner inside an old one, and lining when the pipe is still round and mostly intact.
