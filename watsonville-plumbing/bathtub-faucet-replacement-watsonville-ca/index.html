    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Watsonville, CA

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. When someone flushes a toilet, the valve cuts hot and cold flow in proportion so the mix stays steady, and a thermostatic valve goes further by holding a set temperature even as the water heater cools. Both meet anti-scald code. Thermostatic costs more.

    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Watsonville

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler. The supply lines come up through the floor under the filler, which means cutting the slab or working from a crawlspace or basement, and the filler needs a mounting bracket anchored to the subfloor. Deck-mount fillers sit on the rim. Tub-only fillers need a temperature limit.

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Watsonville

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. That cartridge wears out and causes drips or a handle that will not reach full hot, and swapping it with a cartridge puller takes about an hour when the valve body is in good shape. Older Moentrol valves take another cartridge. We carry both.

    A tub that will not shut off needs water off now. Check behind the trim plate or an access panel for stops, and if there are none, shut the main and leave the drain open. We send the next open plumber. Broken stems are a common cause.

    Plenty of people replace a working tub faucet for looks.     Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Watsonville, CA

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. A new valve makes sense for discontinued parts, a cracked body or an old faucet with no scald protection. You see both prices. Most people choose repair first.
