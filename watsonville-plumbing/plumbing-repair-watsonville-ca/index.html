    What We Fix in Watsonville, CA Homes

    Water Softener Repair in Watsonville, CA

    Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. White crust on faucets and spotty glasses are the first signs a softener has stopped working. The usual causes are a salt bridge in the brine tank or a plugged injector screen. FBL Plumbing repairs the common brands and tests the water hardness before and after the fix so you can see the number drop. Resin lasts ten to fifteen years.

    Well Pump Repair in Watsonville, CA

    No water at the tap on a well system narrows the list quickly. It is usually the pressure switch, the capacitor in the control box, the pump motor or a waterlogged pressure tank. Voltage and amp readings at the control box tell us whether the pump needs to come up. Pulling a submersible pump is a two-person job.

    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Watsonville, CA

    A warm spot on tile, a meter that spins with every tap off, or a water bill that jumps for no reason. Those are slab leak signs. We pinpoint the leak with acoustic listening gear and a thermal camera before anyone breaks concrete. Options include a spot repair through the slab or a reroute overhead, each priced separately.

    Trenchless Pipe Repair in Watsonville, CA

    Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the path of the old one, and cured-in-place lining builds a new pipe inside the existing pipe. Neither one needs a trench across the driveway. Once restoration is counted, the price is often close to a deep dig. A camera decides.

    Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Watsonville, CA

    Pinhole leaks in copper start from the inside, where water chemistry and high velocity wear through the wall. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. One pinhole usually means others are coming in the same run. The fix is a new section soldered or pressed in, plus a pressure check at a hose bib. Above 80 psi, add a regulator.

    Under-Slab Leak Repair in Watsonville, CA

    Newer homes across Northern California mostly use PEX or copper with plastic drains, and many owners are repiping older houses to improve water pressure. Copper or PEX under the slab can run for decades until a fitting or a rubbed spot gives out. We find the leak with acoustic and thermal equipment, then lay out the repair options side by side with a price for each. Epoxy lining is one choice. A reroute through the attic or walls is often the cleaner one.

    Drain Repairs in Watsonville

    Slow drains that come back right after a snake usually mean the pipe itself is damaged. Cast iron that has cracked or a run that sags will hold debris no matter how often it is cleared. We run a camera to find the spot, then replace that section or hydro-jet the line if buildup is the only problem. Grease is the usual buildup.

    Well Pump Service in Watsonville, CA

    Submersible pumps sit deep in the casing, so diagnosis happens at the surface first. FBL Plumbing checks the pressure switch, the control box capacitor and the tank precharge, then measures amp draw on the motor. High amp draw usually means a worn motor or a seized impeller. Jet pumps are simpler, and a lost prime or a bad foot valve is the usual cause when one stops moving water.
