    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Watsonville

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units regenerate on a schedule no matter how much water you used.     Water Softener Repair vs. Replacement in Watsonville, CA

    Age and parts availability decide whether a softener is worth repairing. FBL Plumbing gives honest advice based on both.     Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Watsonville

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads so they cannot grab hardness. We test the resin, clean it with a resin cleaner or replace it. New resin restores full capacity.

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Chlorine and chloramine in city water slowly break resin down until it turns to mush.
