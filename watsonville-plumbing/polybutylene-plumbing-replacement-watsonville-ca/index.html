    Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Watsonville

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. Check the heater first. It also shows up under sinks, at the main and through garage walls, and we follow it into the attic to see how much of the house it feeds.

    Polybutylene to PEX Transitions in Watsonville, CA

    Sometimes the yard service line coming into the house is not polybutylene, or it was replaced already. When the service line is already sound, we tie new PEX into it at the main with a listed transition fitting and skip the trenching. The service line gets checked. If it is blue or black poly, we will recommend replacing it too.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Watsonville, CA

    PEX is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, resists scale and corrosion and installs faster than copper. Faster install means lower labor cost. We keep PEX out of direct sunlight, away from water heater flues and recessed light housings and support it at the spacing the manufacturer calls for, which protects both the pipe and the warranty.

    Polybutylene Leak Repair in Watsonville

    Repairs on polybutylene are temporary by nature, because the pipe next to the repair is the same age. A repair buys time. We cut out the failed piece and splice in PEX with listed fittings, stopping the leak and giving you time to plan the full job.

    Full Home Polybutylene Repiping in Watsonville

    A full replacement takes every poly supply line out of service, not just the sections that have failed. Leaving some poly keeps the risk. New pipe goes from the main to every fixture and appliance, each one gets a new quarter-turn stop and the system is tested before any wall is closed.

    Permits for Polybutylene Replacement in Watsonville

    A whole-house poly replacement in Watsonville is a repipe, and it usually needs a plumbing permit. The permit is on us. The inspector looks at the new lines with the walls still open, checking supports, nail plates, shutoffs and the pressure test before anything is patched. A permit on file also helps with insurers and buyers.

    Copper Replacement for Polybutylene in Watsonville, CA

    Some homeowners in Watsonville, CA want copper when they take out polybutylene. That works fine. Our copper replacements use thicker Type L pipe with lead-free soldered or pressed joints, not the thinner Type M. Expect a higher price and a slightly longer job with copper, in exchange for good heat and UV tolerance and a long history.

    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Watsonville, CA

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. Our crews use Uponor PEX-A and full-bore fittings. New lines run from the main shutoff to every fixture, water heater and appliance, and most homes are completed in two to four working days with water back on each night.
