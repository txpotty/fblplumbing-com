    How We Repair Basement Leaks in Watsonville, CA

    Water Heater and Appliance Leak Repair in Watsonville, CA

    Water heaters, softeners and washing machines often sit in basements, and their leaks pool on the floor out of sight. A tank leaking at the seam is done. Valve and connector leaks are usually a same-visit fix, and an automatic shutoff sensor is worth adding.

    Floor Drain and Backwater Valve Repair in Watsonville

    A floor drain that backs up during heavy use or a storm usually points to a blockage downstream or a failed backwater valve. After clearing the line, we run a camera to confirm it is open. Where the city sewer surcharges in storms, a backwater valve on the building drain keeps sewage from coming up through the floor drain, and it needs its flapper cleaned once a year. Dry traps smell.

    Crack Injection for Basements in Watsonville

    Injection works on poured concrete walls, not block. We set ports along the crack, seal the face with epoxy paste, and pump in polyurethane or epoxy from the bottom port up until resin shows at the next port. Epoxy bonds the two sides. Polyurethane is better for active leaks because it foams on contact with water.
