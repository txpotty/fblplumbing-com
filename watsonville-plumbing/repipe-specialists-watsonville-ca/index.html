    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Watsonville

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to three days.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Watsonville, CA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and very durable piping material that's gaining popularity for repiping projects. FBL Plumbing specializes in PEX pipe replacement in Watsonville, CA, offering a system that resists freezing, corrosion, and scale buildup. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points. PEX also noticeably dampens water hammer noise, providing a quieter plumbing system for your home.

    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Watsonville

    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Expansive clay soils are common across Northern California, and seasonal swelling and shrinking can shift slabs and buried sewer laterals. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.
