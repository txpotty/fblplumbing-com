    Trenchless Sewer Replacement in Watsonville, CA

    Two holes instead of one long trench. With bursting, a steel head splits the old pipe into the soil and pulls new HDPE into the same path. The other option is a cured-in-place liner.     Pipe Bursting Under Driveways in Watsonville, CA

    Concrete is expensive to replace. With pipe bursting, the new line goes in under the driveway or tree from one pit to another, following the old pipe's route. The HDPE is butt-fused on site into one long piece. Most bursts finish in a day. Restoration is two small holes instead of a sawcut slab.

    Most of our sewer work is single-family homes. Many older homes here sit on raised foundations with crawlspaces, which make supply and drain repairs more accessible than slab work.     How Pipe Bursting Works in Watsonville

    A winch pulls the bursting head. Because the head is a little wider than the old pipe, it cracks it into the surrounding dirt as the new HDPE follows. You get a pit at each end. The fused pipe has no joints, so roots cannot get in again.

    Cured-in-Place Sewer Lining in Watsonville

    Lining builds a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line first, often with a cutter and a jetter, then pull a resin-saturated felt tube into place and inflate it so it cures hard against the host pipe. Collapsed pipe will not work.
