    What Goes Wrong on a Disposal, and How We Fix It in Watsonville, CA

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Watsonville

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps lodge between the grind ring and the impeller lugs, and the motor stalls. We shut off power, insert a 1/4 inch hex key into the socket on the bottom of the unit and work it back and forth until the flywheel turns freely. Then the object comes out with pliers. A motor that stalled for a long time may have overheated, so we test it under load before calling the job done.

    InSinkErator and Other Brand Repairs in Watsonville, CA

    InSinkErator, Waste King and Moen each use different mounts, so a flange leak is fixed with that brand's kit. InSinkErator uses a three-bolt or Quick Lock style ring, while Waste King uses an EZ Mount twist ring that comes off without tools. We stock the common kits. That keeps most repairs to one visit.

    Electrical Issues and Motor Repair in Watsonville

    A disposal that hums but will not grind has power but cannot turn, while one that is silent has no power at all. They are different problems. For a silent unit we test the breaker, reset and switch, and for a humming one we check the flywheel and motor before deciding if it is worth repairing.

    Jammed Disposal Repair in Watsonville

    Jams usually trace back to one hard object, like a peach pit, a chicken bone or a twist tie wrapped around the impeller. We free the flywheel from below with the hex key and pull the object with pliers, never a hand, then run it with water to make sure nothing else is stuck. We press the reset after clearing it. Check the trap arm if it jams again.

    Noisy Disposal Repair in Watsonville

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough usually mean trouble inside the unit. A loose mount will rattle. We check the mount, then look for debris, then run the motor empty, since a whine with an empty chamber means the bearings are going.

    Leak Detection and Repair in Watsonville, CA

    There are four usual leak points on a disposal: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Most leak points can be repaired. When the housing leaks, the unit has to go.

    Cost-Effective Disposal Repair in Watsonville, CA

    Paying half the price of a new disposal for a repair seldom pays off, and we will say so. Many fixes are cheap. A reset, a cleared jam, a new switch or a resealed flange usually costs far less than a new unit and can keep a younger disposal going for years.

    After-Hours Disposal Service in Watsonville

    A disposal that quits while you are cooking stops everything, especially if the sink is full of water. Do not try drain chemicals. Drain chemicals can eat at the seals and splash on whoever opens the trap, so leave the water where it is and give us a call.
