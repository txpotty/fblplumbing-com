    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Replacement in Cupertino

    Most toilet replacements, including a quality standard toilet, labor and disposal, cost $450 to $950. Premium one-piece or smart toilets can raise the total to $1,200 to $2,500. Flange repairs, if needed, typically add $100 to $250.

    The rough-in measurement refers to the distance from the finished wall behind the toilet to the center of the drainpipe (the bolts that secure the toilet). Standard rough-ins are usually 12 inches, but 10-inch and 14-inch options exist. Knowing your rough-in is essential to make sure your new toilet fits correctly in the existing space.

    FBL Plumbing provides a warranty on both our workmanship and the installed toilet fixture. The specific terms vary by toilet brand, but we stand behind our installations to ensure your peace of mind regarding leaks or malfunctions.

    Manufacturers typically warranty parts for 1 to 5 years and porcelain longer. We guarantee our workmanship and will return if a leak is caused by the installation.

    Older toilets can use 3.5 to 7 gallons per flush, while WaterSense toilets use 1.28 gallons or less. For a average household, that can mean thousands of gallons saved each year.

    A standard toilet replacement usually takes our skilled plumbers about 1 to 3 hours to complete. This includes removing the old toilet, preparing the area, and installing the new unit, making sure it is properly sealed and functional.

    Signs of failing internal toilet parts include a toilet that runs constantly, a weak or incomplete flush, a handle that needs jiggling, or visible wear and tear on components like the flapper, fill valve, or flush valve. While some parts can be replaced one at a time, an older toilet with multiple failing components might benefit more from a full replacement.
