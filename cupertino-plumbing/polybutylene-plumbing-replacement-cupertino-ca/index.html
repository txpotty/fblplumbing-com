    Why Polybutylene Fails in Cupertino

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Rural properties around Cupertino often rely on private wells, which may need treatment for iron, sediment, or other minerals. Hot water speeds it up. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.

    Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Cupertino

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. Check the heater first. You may also see it under sinks, near the main shutoff or coming through the garage wall, and we trace runs through the attic to confirm how much of the house it serves.

    Polybutylene Checks for Home Sales in Cupertino, CA
