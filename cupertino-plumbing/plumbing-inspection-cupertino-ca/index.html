    Plumbing Inspection Costs in Cupertino, CA

    A basic plumbing inspection usually costs $150 to $300, and adding a sewer camera usually brings it to $250 to $500. Bigger properties cost more. Compared to an unexpected sewer replacement, which can run $4,000 to $15,000 or more, an inspection is cheap protection, and we give you the exact price when you book.

    Water Heater Inspections in Cupertino

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. The serial number tells us the age, and we check for rust, look at the vent and test the relief valve. We check tankless units for scale. On gas units, we look at the burner and flame, check for a sediment trap and confirm the vent is drafting so combustion gases go outside.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Cupertino

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. We also check for warm floor spots. We test the meter, watch for pressure loss and look for damp baseboards and mildew, and on slab homes we follow up with acoustic and thermal equipment to pinpoint the leak. Catching a slab leak early limits damage to floors and foundations.

    Residential Plumbing Inspections in Cupertino

    A full home inspection covers the system from the water meter to the sewer connection. Our plumber looks at all exposed pipe, runs every faucet and toilet and tests the heater, the main valve and the fixture stops. Pressure gets measured too. We note anything above 80 psi.

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Cupertino, CA

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. We identify copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, polybutylene and Kitec, and we note the known failure patterns for each one. Certain types need a plan. Poly and Kitec fail suddenly and galvanized chokes off with rust, so we flag them with replacement options.

    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspections in Cupertino

    Our whole-home inspections focus on the problems we see most in Northern California houses. Common finds include old pipe, stiff shutoffs, tired heaters, slow drains and rooty or sagging sewers. Each gets tested, not guessed at. Catching a corroded fitting or a partial sewer blockage now keeps it from becoming a ceiling stain or a backup on a holiday weekend.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Cupertino, CA

    Older homes in Cupertino often have cast iron or clay sewer pipe that cracks, scales up or lets roots in at the joints. The camera records every defect along with its distance from the cleanout. We mark it on the ground. With depth and position marked, repair pricing gets accurate, and choosing between a spot fix, a liner or replacement gets easier.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Cupertino

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. We confirm proper venting. Expansion tanks, drain pans and softeners get a look as well, and a quick hardness test tells us whether the softener is actually doing its job or just using salt.
