    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Cupertino, CA

    Submersible units sit down in the pit, sealed, with the water around them cooling the motor. That makes them quieter than a pedestal, and in Cupertino, CA we set them on a couple of bricks so the intake stays above the silt that settles on the bottom of every liner. The cord seal matters.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Cupertino

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the water. The motor stays dry and you can reach it without pulling the pump, which is why they suit narrow pits under 14 inches across, and why they often outlast cheap submersibles in Cupertino homes. Expect more noise.

    New Sump Pit Excavation in Cupertino

    No pit yet? We find the low spot where water collects, saw cut the slab, dig roughly 24 to 30 inches deep, and set a liner surrounded by washed gravel so water can enter while fine soil stays out. We pour new concrete flush with the floor.

    Commercial and Multi-Unit Sump Installations in Cupertino

    Duplex systems with two pumps and an alternating panel are standard for parking garages and multi-unit buildings with below-grade levels in Cupertino.     Pedestal Sump Pumps in Cupertino

    Older homes often have narrow pits that will not fit a submersible. That is where a pedestal pump earns its place, and we install it with rigid discharge, a quality check valve, and a float rod set to your pit depth so it starts before water reaches the top of the liner. Noisy, but they last.
