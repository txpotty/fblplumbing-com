    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Installation in Cupertino

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon gas water heater typically costs $1,800 to $4,000 installed. Power vent and tankless gas units often cost more due to venting and gas line requirements.

    A conventional gas water heater stores and heats a large volume of water in a tank, providing a limited supply. A gas tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit, offering an endless supply but requiring more involved venting and gas line work.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from 6 to 12 years. We also warranty our installation labor.

    Installing a gas water heater involves working with natural gas lines, water lines and exhaust venting, which can be dangerous if not done correctly. Improper installation can lead to gas leaks, carbon monoxide poisoning or water damage. It is highly recommended to hire a licensed professional for safety and code compliance.

    Power vent units use a fan to push exhaust outside, while direct vent units use a sealed system that draws outside air. Both allow flexible placement compared to atmospheric vents.

    Most standard gas water heater installations can be completed within 3 to 5 hours. However, more complex jobs, such as converting to a tankless unit or major gas line modifications, might take a full day.

    We install Rheem, A.O. Smith, Bradford White, Navien and Rinnai. We recommend models based on reliability, efficiency and warranty.

    Choosing the right size depends on your household's peak hot water demand, determined by the number of residents and hot water fixtures. Our technicians will assess your needs during the consultation to recommend the appropriate gallon capacity or flow rate for your Cupertino home.
