    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in Cupertino, CA

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Condensing units vent through PVC and need a condensate line, so placement and install work differ from a standard tank. Rebates may apply. We install Rheem, Bradford White and AO Smith models.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Cupertino, CA

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through PVC or other approved pipe. That means the heater can go in a spot with no chimney or vertical flue, and the vent can run sideways out an exterior wall as long as it stays within the length limit. They need an outlet. We terminate the vent to spec.
