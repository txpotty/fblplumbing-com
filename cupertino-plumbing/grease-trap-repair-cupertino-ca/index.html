    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Cupertino

    Baffles slow incoming water so grease can rise and solids can settle. When baffles crack, corrode or break loose, grease passes straight through the trap. We remove damaged baffles and install new ones made for your trap model. In concrete interceptors, we can repair or rebuild baffle walls. Restoring baffles brings back proper separation and helps you stay compliant.

    Clogged Grease Trap Repair in Cupertino

    Grease traps often clog due to excessive accumulation of fats, oils, and grease (FOGs) or food solids. Our technicians use high-pressure hydro-jetting to break down tough blockages and thoroughly clean the internal baffles and lines. We also inspect for underlying issues like damaged pipes or improper disposal practices that contribute to repeat clogs, offering solutions to prevent future blockages. Identifying the precise location and nature of the clog is crucial for effective repair and long-term performance in your Cupertino establishment.

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Cupertino

    When traditional snaking isn't enough, hydro-jetting is our go-to method for severe grease trap blockages. This powerful technique uses high-pressure water streams to thoroughly scour the interior of pipes and the grease trap itself, removing years of FOG buildup, scale, and debris. It's a highly effective, non-invasive method that not only clears clogs but also cleans the lines, greatly reducing the likelihood of future blockages in your Cupertino business.

    Lid, Gasket and Riser Repair in Cupertino, CA

    Damaged lids and failed gaskets let odors escape and create safety hazards. Outdoor risers can crack from vehicle traffic or ground movement. We replace lids, install new gaskets and repair or replace risers. Traffic-rated lids are installed where trucks or cars drive over the interceptor. These repairs keep odors in and keep people protected.

    Grease Line Clog Repair in Cupertino, CA

    Grease that hardens in the lines before or after the trap can cause backups even when the trap itself is clean. We use hydro jetting to clear the pipe and remove buildup.     Damaged Baffle and Lid Repair in Cupertino, CA

    The internal baffles of a grease trap are crucial for separating FOG from wastewater. If these are damaged or dislodged, the trap becomes inefficient, allowing grease to escape into the sewer system. We repair or replace broken baffles and cracked lids in Cupertino, CA, making sure your trap functions as designed. A secure lid also prevents odors and keeps pests out, maintaining hygiene around the unit.

    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Cupertino, CA

    Tees direct flow into and out of the trap at the correct depth. Missing or broken tees allow floating grease to escape into the sewer line. We replace damaged tees with durable PVC or other approved materials. We also check that they are set at the right depth for your unit.
