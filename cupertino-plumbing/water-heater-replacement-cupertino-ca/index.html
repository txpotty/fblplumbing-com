    Upsizing Your Water Heater in Cupertino

    Running out of hot water usually means the house outgrew the tank. A larger tank or a model with a higher first-hour rating can keep up with more showers back to back, and in some homes a 50-gallon high-recovery gas unit does the job of a 75-gallon standard tank without taking up any more floor space. Recovery rate counts too.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Cupertino, CA

    Gas water heaters recover faster than electric, and in Cupertino, CA homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Every joint gets checked.

    Commercial Water Heater Replacement in Cupertino

    Businesses in Cupertino put far more hours on a heater than a house, so commercial replacements need more capacity and faster recovery. Restaurants, salons and offices all draw differently, so we size by peak gallons per hour and required temperature, which may mean a larger tank, a booster or a tankless rack. We schedule around your hours.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Cupertino, CA

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. The space runs cooler.

    Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith in Cupertino, CA

    We install Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith on most tank jobs. Bradford White is sold only through plumbers and known for durability, Rheem has a wide lineup and good parts support, and AO Smith offers many sizes and efficiency options, so the right pick usually depends on fit, warranty and stock. All three hold up.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Cupertino

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is common, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. Since tankless can pull four times the gas of a tank, the meter line usually gets bigger, and the old vent is swapped for stainless or PVC. You see both numbers.

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Cupertino

    Electric water heaters in Cupertino usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. A bad element can be replaced. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Most swaps take a few hours.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Cupertino

    Older neighborhoods in Northern California include prewar and postwar homes with galvanized supply lines and cast iron or clay sewer pipe that are nearing the end of their life. Attic water heaters take more work to replace than ones in a garage or closet. Getting a 50-gallon tank up a pull-down ladder or through a narrow scuttle takes two people and care, and code calls for a drain pan with a line to the outside or a safe drain, a service platform and a light near the unit.
