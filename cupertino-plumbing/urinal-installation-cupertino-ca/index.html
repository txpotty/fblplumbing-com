    Rough-In Plumbing for Urinals in Cupertino, CA

    A successful urinal installation in Cupertino, CA starts with accurate rough-in plumbing. This involves laying out and connecting the supply lines and drain pipes before the walls are closed.     Urinal Replacement and Upgrades in Cupertino, CA

    Older urinals often use 1 gallon or more per flush and have cracked or stained bowls. We remove the old fixture, inspect the waste outlet and carrier, and install a modern low-flow model.     Urinal Installation Cost Factors in Cupertino, CA

    Costs depend on the fixture type, whether a carrier is needed, and how far the drain and supply must run.
