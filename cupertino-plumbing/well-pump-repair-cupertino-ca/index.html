    Pressure Tank Repair in Cupertino, CA

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. Fast cycling kills pumps young. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank, then confirm the pump runs at least a minute per cycle. Steadier pressure follows.

    Submersible Pump Replacement in Cupertino

    Sand wears impellers down over time, and a pump that once filled the tank in a minute starts taking three. Other common failures are the motor, the splice and the check valve. We test the motor and wire from the surface before deciding whether to pull. New pumps match your well's yield.

    Well Pump Maintenance & Repair Cost in Cupertino, CA

    Maintenance costs far less than an emergency pull. A typical well pump repair in Cupertino runs $400 to $1,500, while a yearly checkup costs a fraction of that. FBL Plumbing prices both up front. We check tank precharge, switch contacts, amp draw and the well cap, which catches most problems early.

    Jet Pump Repair in Cupertino, CA

    Shallow wells and booster systems often use jet pumps. Common problems are loss of prime, a leaking shaft seal and a worn impeller. Our crew in Cupertino restores prime, replaces worn parts and checks the suction line for leaks that let air in. Where water levels swing through the year, a falling level can starve the pump and cost it prime.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Cupertino, CA

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Most failures start with an air leak somewhere in the suction line. FBL Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and watches it reach cut-out pressure. Nonstop running without pressure usually means lost prime or an air leak.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Cupertino

    Bladder tanks hold a cushion of air that lets the pump rest between cycles. A failed bladder lets the tank waterlog, and the pump begins to short cycle. We inspect the bladder, the switch and the tank connections. Proper sizing adds years to a pump.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Cupertino, CA

    Capacitors, relays and corroded terminals inside the control box cause many pump failures. Breaker trips usually come first. Licensed technicians test these parts safely with the power locked out, replace what failed and tighten every connection in the box. Wiring meets Northern California code when the job is done.

    Well Pump Diagnostic Services in Cupertino, CA

    Voltage, amperage, pressure and flow rate tell us most of what is happening down the well without pulling a single length of pipe. High amp draw points to a weak motor. Low flow with normal amp draw points to a plugged intake or a falling water level, which is a different repair. Testing first saves a pull.
