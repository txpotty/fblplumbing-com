    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Maintenance in Cupertino

    A standard softener service visit typically runs $125 to $250. Valve rebuilds typically add $100 to $250 and resin replacement costs $300 to $700.

    Usually, routine maintenance and minor repairs on an existing water softener do not require a plumbing permit in Cupertino. However, if major modifications or a full replacement of the system are needed, a permit might be required. We always ensure compliance with local codes.

    Common signs include a return of hard water symptoms such as mineral spots on dishes, dry skin and hair, or scale buildup on fixtures. You might also notice your softener using more salt than usual, odd noises or the system not regenerating properly. Don't wait for a complete failure.

    You can add salt, break small bridges and run manual regenerations. Valve rebuilds, injector cleaning and resin work are best left to a plumber with the right parts.

    Resin often lasts 10 to 15 years, but chlorinated city water and iron can shorten that. Fine resin beads in your faucet aerators can signal resin breakdown.

    A brine tank full of water typically means a clogged injector, a stuck float or a drain line problem. Our service clears the cause and resets the system.

    A standard water softener maintenance service typically takes about 1 to 2 hours. This can vary depending on the system's condition, age and whether any unforeseen issues are discovered during the inspection.

    For the best performance and longevity, we recommend professional water softener maintenance annually. In areas with extremely hard water, or for older systems, a twice-yearly check-up might be worthwhile to prevent issues before they become serious.
