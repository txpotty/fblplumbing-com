    Control Valve and Timer Diagnostics in Cupertino, CA

    The control valve runs every stage of regeneration, from backwash through brine draw to the final refill. We step it through each cycle by hand and watch for a motor that stalls, a drive gear that skips, a piston that drags or a drain that never shuts off. Worn pistons turn up a lot.

    Softener Upkeep for Restaurants and Shops in Cupertino

    Ice machines, espresso machines, dish machines and boilers all scale up fast on hard water, and equipment warranties often require treated water. We test hardness on every visit and log it for your records. Brine tank cleanouts come around more often on these systems.

    Regeneration Programming and Hardness Testing in Cupertino, CA

    Plenty of softeners still run factory settings. The installer never changed them, or the household grew and nobody updated the valve. We enter your raw hardness and household size, then set a metered valve to regenerate by gallons used rather than on a fixed timer schedule.

    Resin Bed Checks and Regeneration Settings in Cupertino

    Resin does the actual softening. Each bead trades sodium for calcium and magnesium until it runs out of capacity. We compare the softened hardness against the gallons used since the last regeneration to see whether the bed still holds its rated capacity.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Service in Cupertino

    Salt-free conditioners, usually template assisted crystallization units, do not remove hardness. A hardness test still reads high afterward.
