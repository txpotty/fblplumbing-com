    Foundation Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Cupertino, CA

    Direct repairs are often the lowest upfront cost but involve breaking concrete and may need flooring repairs.     Water Line Reroute in Cupertino

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the foundation and runs a new line through the attic or walls. This avoids breaking concrete and removes the risk of future leaks under the slab. We typically use PEX for reroutes because it is flexible and resists corrosion. Drywall openings are kept small and neat for simple patching.

    PEX and PVC Foundation Leak Repair in Cupertino

    Newer homes often feature PEX or PVC plumbing, which, while durable, can still develop foundation leaks due to ground movement or improper installation.
