    Faulty Shower Valve Repair in Santa Rosa

    Problems with your shower's water temperature or pressure often point to a malfunctioning mixing valve or cartridge. These components control the hot and cold water flow. Our technicians are experienced at diagnosing valve issues and can repair or replace them, ensuring you get consistent water temperature and pressure every time you shower. We work with all major valve brands.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Santa Rosa, CA

    A diverter sends water from the tub spout to the showerhead. When it wears out, water keeps running from the spout and shower pressure drops. We replace diverter spouts, three-handle diverter stems and multi-function diverter valves. This brings back full flow to the showerhead.

    Leaky Shower Head Repair in Santa Rosa

    A dripping shower head wastes water and can be incredibly annoying. Our plumbers can promptly identify the cause, whether it is a worn-out washer, O-ring, or a faulty cartridge. We carry common replacement parts on our trucks to ensure a fast fix. We will stop the leak, saving you money on your water bill and preventing potential water damage in your bathroom.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Santa Rosa, CA

    Leaks under the shower pan or behind tile can cause rot, mold and stains on ceilings below. We use moisture meters and flood tests to find failed grout, cracked pans or leaking pipe joints. Then we repair the source, whether that means resealing a drain, replacing a valve or rebuilding a pan. On slab homes, these leaks can damage flooring in nearby rooms.

    Hot Water Pressure Issues in Santa Rosa, CA

    Lack of hot water or weak hot water pressure in your shower can be incredibly frustrating. This can stem from several issues, including a failing water heater, sediment buildup in the pipes, or a problem with the shower mixing valve itself. Our technicians will diagnose whether the issue is with your water heater, a blocked pipe, or a faulty component within the shower. We provide lasting solutions to restore consistent hot water flow and pressure.

    Shower Temperature Problem Repair in Santa Rosa

    If your shower turns cold when someone flushes, the pressure-balance spool may be stuck. Your shower will hold a steady, consistent temperature.
