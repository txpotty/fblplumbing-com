    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Santa Rosa, CA

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. Jetting costs more up front. Fewer repeat visits often make jetting cheaper over a few years.

    Main Line Jetting in Santa Rosa, CA

    The lateral from your house to the street carries everything, so buildup there causes backups everywhere at once. We jet it from the cleanout to the tap. Low spots where the pipe has settled hold water and collect sludge, so we spend extra passes there and mark the location on the camera footage in case a repair makes sense later.

    Residential Hydrojetting in Santa Rosa

    Most home jetting calls involve a greasy kitchen line or a root-filled sewer. Whenever possible, we work from the outside cleanout and keep the hose out of the house. Kitchen lines may need access under the sink. We lay down floor protection first.

    Hydrojetting for Shops and Offices in Santa Rosa, CA

    Retail and office buildings have long horizontal drain runs that collect buildup. Flat runs collect more debris. After-hours jetting keeps equipment out of hallways while tenants are working.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Santa Rosa, CA

    Older cast iron drains rust and scale from the inside, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. Jetting can remove that buildup. Badly corroded cast iron with thin walls or cracks needs gentler settings or no jetting at all, and the camera tells us which before we start, along with whether the pipe is a candidate for lining.

    Storm and Yard Drain Jetting in Santa Rosa, CA

    Yard drains, channel drains and downspout lines fill with sand, silt and leaves between storms. Tree roots seek out sewer laterals during dry spells, which is why heavy rains often reveal cracked lines through sudden backups. We jet yard lines from the outlet back and clean the basins before the wet season. We also check the pop-up emitters.

    Whole-House Drain Jetting in Santa Rosa

    Slow drains in several rooms point to buildup in the main or a branch line. We jet the main from the cleanout and the branches from roof vents or from fixture access points as needed for that layout. Plan on two to three hours.
