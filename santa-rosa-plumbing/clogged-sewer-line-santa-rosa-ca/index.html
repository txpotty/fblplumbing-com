    Sewer Camera Inspection in Santa Rosa

    A sewer camera is a waterproof head on a push rod with a locator transmitter built in. We see what the pipe looks like. Cracks, offsets, sags, roots and heavy scale all show up on the screen, while the locator marks the spot and depth from above. It saves guessing where to dig. You get a copy of the footage.

    Cleaning vs. Repairing a Sewer Line in Santa Rosa, CA

    A sound pipe with a grease or root clog just needs cleaning. Cracked pipe is different. Offset joints, cracks and collapsed sections keep clogging no matter how often they get cleared, and the camera tells you which path makes sense. You see the footage first.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Santa Rosa

    Some older homes have no cleanout at all. Each clog costs more without one. Without one, a plumber has to pull a toilet or climb on the roof to reach the main, and a two-way cleanout by the foundation fixes that. Future clearing gets cheaper and faster.

    Hydro-Jetting for Stubborn Clogs in Santa Rosa

    Some lines get cabled three times a year and still clog. The cable is not the fix. Those lines are usually coated in grease or scale, and the cable only bores a narrow path through the middle that closes up again within weeks. Jetting removes the coating down to the pipe wall. The results hold up far better.

    Main Line Hydro Jetting in Santa Rosa

    A jetter cleans the full diameter of the pipe, not just a hole through the middle. That matters a lot. Water at thousands of psi cuts roots, grease and scale, and the rear-facing spray carries all of it downstream as the nozzle pulls itself along the line. We camera the pipe first so jetting only goes into lines that can take it. Many lines stay clear for years after.
