    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Santa Rosa

    Trap and slip-joint repairs typically cost $150 to $350. Replacing pipe costs more. A section of drain pipe usually runs $400 to $1,200, and cast iron stack replacement can range from $2,000 to $6,000 with drywall, flooring and access driving most of the spread. We write it up before we start.

    Snugging a slip nut or replacing a plastic trap is a fair DIY job. Do not crank it down. If the leak comes from a cracked tailpiece, a corroded metal drain or somewhere inside the wall, a plumber can make a lasting repair and check the cabinet floor and subfloor for hidden damage. Water damage under sinks spreads quietly.

    Repeat clogs come from something in the pipe catching debris. Grease or scale is common. Poor pipe slope, a missing vent or a cracked fitting with a rough edge will snag paper and hair until the line closes off again a few weeks after each cleaning. A camera shows which one you have.

    PVC, ABS, cast iron, galvanized and copper drain all show up in Northern California homes. We repair every one. Joining new plastic to old metal takes a shielded transition coupling sized to the outside diameter of each pipe, and getting that fitting right is what keeps the repair from leaking later. The common sizes ride on the truck.

    Slow drains, gurgling and sewer smell are the big three warnings. So is water under the sink. A drain that clogs again a few weeks after it was cleared, or water spots below a bathroom, point to a pipe problem instead of a simple clog. Small leaks get worse.

    Small fixture drain repairs usually do not. Larger jobs often do. Replacing drain stacks, relocating drains or adding new lines may require one depending on the scope and your local rules. The permit is on us if needed.

    Hydro-jetting scours the pipe with water at several thousand psi. The whole inside gets cleaned. A snake only punches a hole through a clog, while a jetter removes grease, scale and fine roots from the full circumference of the pipe. We camera first on old or brittle pipe.

    A trap swap or a fixture drain fix is usually an hour or two. Main lines take longer. Replacing a section of main line can take a full day or several days, with permits and digging as the main things that stretch it out. We give you the timeline up front.
