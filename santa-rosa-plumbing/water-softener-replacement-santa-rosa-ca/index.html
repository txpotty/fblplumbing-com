    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Santa Rosa

    A standard single tank softener replacement usually costs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Dual tank or premium systems usually run $3,000 to $5,000. Adding a new loop or drain line can raise the cost.

    Requirements vary by city. Simple replacements may not require one, but new plumbing connections may. We check local rules for you.

    A new, properly sized water softener can sometimes improve water pressure if your old unit was undersized or had significant mineral buildup restricting flow.     The right size depends on your household's daily water usage and the hardness of your water in grains per gallon. Our technicians will perform a water test and calculate the correct softening capacity in grains per day for your Santa Rosa home, so the system keeps up.

    In many areas, a plumbing permit is usually not required for a direct like-for-like water softener replacement if no significant changes to the existing plumbing are made.     Most residential water softener replacements can be completed within 3 to 6 hours, assuming no major plumbing changes are required. Our skilled plumbers work efficiently to minimize disruption, restoring soft water to your home on the same day.
