    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Santa Rosa

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Newer homes across Northern California mostly use PEX or copper with plastic drains, and many owners are repiping older houses to restore water pressure. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. We pinpoint the leak with acoustic gear and thermal imaging. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or abandon the old pipe under the floor and run new PEX above it. We price each one for you.

    PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Santa Rosa

    Copper and PEX fail in different places. Copper tends to pinhole where a rock rubs it or where the water chemistry eats the wall from inside. PEX usually fails at a fitting. We cut out the bad section of copper and sweat or press in a new length, and for PEX we use the matching crimp or expansion tool so the joint is made the way the manufacturer intends.
