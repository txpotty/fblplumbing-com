    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Santa Rosa, CA

    Leaks often start at the compression fittings and couplings right next to the meter box. Shifting soil and heavy traffic over the box can work loose those joints over time.     Trenchless Water Line Repair in Santa Rosa

    For some water line repairs, traditional trenching can be avoided. Trenchless repair methods, like pipe bursting or pipe lining, allow us to fix or replace underground pipes with little disruption to your landscaping, driveway, or foundation. This method is often faster and less intrusive than traditional digging, making it a popular choice for homeowners in Santa Rosa looking to preserve their property. We'll assess whether your water line issue is a good candidate for trenchless technology.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Santa Rosa, CA

    A single leak on a newer copper or PEX line is usually a quick spot repair. Repeat leaks, galvanized pipe or a line older than about 40 years usually justify full replacement. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars, while a full replacement costs more but resets the clock for decades. We show you the condition of the pipe we expose so you can make the call with actual information.

    Main Water Line Leak Repair in Santa Rosa

    Most main line leaks show up as a soggy strip of yard, a sudden jump in your water bill or a meter dial that moves with every fixture off. We verify the leak by isolating the house shutoff and watching the meter. Then we use ground microphones to locate the exact spot. A single section is cut out and replaced with repair couplings or a new length of pipe, and the line is pressure tested before backfill.

    If your service line runs under a driveway, pool deck or mature landscaping, trenchless replacement can save a lot of restoration cost.
