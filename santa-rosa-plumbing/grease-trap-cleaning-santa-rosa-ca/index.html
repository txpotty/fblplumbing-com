    A full trap lets grease out the outlet tee. It hardens in the pipe from there. We pump on a schedule that keeps the grease and solids layer well under the 25 percent mark, measured with a sludge judge at every visit rather than guessed from the calendar.

    • System Longevity, Less grease in the line means fewer jetting calls and a longer life for the drain pipe.

    Citywide Coverage

    Our routes cover Santa Rosa and the towns around it on a regular rotation. Outlying kitchens are not an extra trip. Being on a set route keeps scheduled visits on the date we promised, which matters when an inspector expects a pump-out every 30 or 60 days.

    Grease Trap Hydro-Jetting in Santa Rosa, CA

    Some traps have years of grease baked onto the walls and inlet piping. Pumping alone leaves that crust in place. We jet the inside of the empty trap and the line feeding it, which takes the unit back to bare walls and restores the capacity the crust was eating up. Plan on about an extra hour.

    Grease Line Jetting in Santa Rosa

    Grease that slips past a full trap coats the 4-inch line out to the main. It builds up in rings, thickest at the bottom of the pipe. Snaking just bores a small path that closes up in weeks, but a rotating jetter nozzle strips grease off the full wall of the pipe. A camera confirms it afterward.
