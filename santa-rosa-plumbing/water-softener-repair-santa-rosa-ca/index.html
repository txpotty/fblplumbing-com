    Control Valve Rebuild in Santa Rosa

    Service, backwash, brine and rinse are the four cycles the control valve directs every time the softener regenerates, usually in the middle of the night. Over the years, seals, spacers and pistons wear down. Leaks at the valve or water running to the drain are common signs. The valve gets rebuilt with the kit made for it.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Santa Rosa, CA

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. Installing a new quality softener usually runs $1,500 to $3,500.     Resin Bed Replacement in Santa Rosa

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. Older systems on chlorinated city water see it most. FBL Plumbing pulls the old resin and fills the tank with new media. The softener works like new.

    Resin Testing and Replacement in Santa Rosa

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Treated city water slowly breaks resin down into mush. After a capacity test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced, and a good tank and valve with new resin performs like new. Mushy resin can clog the valve.
