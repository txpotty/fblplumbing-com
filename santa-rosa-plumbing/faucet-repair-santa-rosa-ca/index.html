    Ball Faucet Repair in Santa Rosa, CA

    Ball faucets, recognizable by their single lever that moves over a rounded cap, are common in many kitchens. They use a rotating ball joint to control water flow and temperature. If your ball faucet is leaking from the spout or base, or if the handle feels loose, it's likely time for a repair. We can replace the spring-loaded rubber seats and O-rings, which are usually the culprits, restoring smooth operation and preventing drips.

    Faucet Repair Versus Replacement in Santa Rosa, CA

    A cartridge repair often costs well under the price of a new faucet and can add years of life. However, if the faucet body is cracked, parts are discontinued or it has needed several repairs, replacement might be better value. We give you both prices so you can choose. Many brands like Moen and Delta offer lifetime warranties on cartridges, which can reduce your repair cost.

    Kitchen Faucet Repair in Santa Rosa

    Kitchen faucets endure heavy daily use and are prone to specific issues like sprayer malfunctions, clogged aerators, or swivel problems. Our plumbers are adept at repairing all kitchen faucet styles, including pull-down, pull-out, and commercial-style fixtures. We understand the importance of a fully functional kitchen sink for cooking and cleaning. We address everything from leaks around the base to issues with water filtration attachments.

    Compression Faucet Repair in Santa Rosa

    Compression faucets, often found in older Santa Rosa homes, are known for their separate hot and cold handles. When these faucets leak, it's usually due to worn-out rubber washers or packing. Our team is skilled in disassembling these classic fixtures, replacing the necessary seals and washers, and reassembling them to stop leaks effectively. We respect the character of older homes and make sure these repairs are done carefully.

    Outdoor Hose Bib Repair in Santa Rosa

    Outdoor spigots take a beating from sun and weather. Even in mild areas, wrapping irrigation backflow devices before a cold snap is cheap insurance against a surprise leak. We repair leaking packing nuts, replace worn washers and install new frost-free hose bibs when necessary. We also add vacuum breakers to meet backflow prevention requirements. A leaking hose bib can waste a lot of water over a long season of yard use.

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Santa Rosa, CA

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base points to failed O-rings or a loose mounting nut. If left alone, the leak can soak into the cabinet below and damage the sink base. We replace O-rings, tighten mounting hardware and reseal the base. We also check the deck for water damage.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Santa Rosa

    Weak or sputtering flow is usually caused by mineral buildup in the aerator or cartridge. We remove and clean or replace the aerator, flush the lines and check the cartridge for scale blockage. On pull-down faucets, the spray head and check valves can also clog. If flow is low at every fixture, we test whole-house water pressure.
