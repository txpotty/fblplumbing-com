    Bladder Pressure Tank Replacement in Santa Rosa

    Bladder tanks hold water inside a flexible butyl bag surrounded by pressurized air. When the bag ruptures, water fills the whole tank, there is no air cushion and the pump short cycles. A simple test is pressing the air valve on top, since water spitting out means the bladder has failed. We replace the tank, set the new pre-charge and confirm the pump runs a full cycle. Most bladder tank swaps take about two to three hours.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Santa Rosa

    Most well pressure tank replacements cost $900 to $2,200 installed, depending on tank size and whether the switch and tank tee are replaced. Large capacity or multi-tank setups can run $2,500 or more. We give a firm price before starting.

    A new well pressure tank mainly helps maintain consistent water pressure and reduces pump cycling. If your current low pressure is due to a failing or undersized tank, a replacement will definitely improve the consistency and feel of your water flow. However, if the issue is with the well pump itself or a clogged pipe, other solutions might be needed.

    It depends on your pump's flow rate and the drawdown needed for at least a one minute run time. We calculate this on site instead of guessing from the old tank.

    Both bladder and diaphragm tanks use an air cushion to pressurize water, but they differ in how the water and air are separated. Bladder tanks have a replaceable rubber bladder that holds the water, fully isolating it from the air. Diaphragm tanks use a fixed rubber diaphragm. Bladder tanks are generally preferred for their longevity and ease of maintenance.

    On average, a well pressure tank can last between 5 to 10 years, though some high-quality tanks can last longer. Factors like water quality, proper initial installation, and regular maintenance can significantly impact its lifespan.

    If your switch is more than a few years old or the contacts are pitted, replacing it with the tank is low-cost insurance. It also lets us match the switch settings to the new tank.

    A like-for-like tank replacement usually does not require a permit in most counties. Electrical changes or new piping may, and we check local rules for you.

    Most pressure tank replacements take two to four hours. Adding a constant pressure system or multiple tanks can take the better part of a day.
