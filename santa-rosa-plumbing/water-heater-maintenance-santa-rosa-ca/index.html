    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Santa Rosa

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Calcium and magnesium settle on the bottom of the tank and form a layer that traps heat under it, so water boils in pockets beneath the scale and pops every time the burner or lower element fires. The tank drains until the water is clean. Heavy buildup may take several cycles.

    Tune-Up Versus Replacement in Santa Rosa, CA

    A tune-up costs a fraction of a new heater. Maintenance helps a tank reach its full rated life and helps tankless units last much longer. Sometimes replacement makes sense. We give you real numbers for both.

    Tankless Water Heater Service in Santa Rosa, CA

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale inside the heat exchanger chokes flow until the unit throws codes or shuts off mid-shower. A pump circulates descaler through it. We also check the igniter, the fan and the exhaust vent for safe operation.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Santa Rosa

    Some water eats anode rods fast. Water chemistry can wear a rod out in a few years, so we check it every time. Spent rods get replaced. A healthy rod keeps the tank from rusting through.

    Gas Burner and Venting Checks in Santa Rosa

    A blue flame is what we want to see. Poor flames mean dirty burners, so we clean them, test the sensor and check the draft. Loose vents leak CO. We test the gas connection too.

    Electric Element Service in Santa Rosa, CA

    Two elements, two thermostats. A burned-out lower element is the usual cause of running short on hot water. Power gets shut off first. We only replace what failed.

    The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense. We test the valve, confirm it reseats and check that the discharge pipe is routed correctly. We swap bad ones. We check the expansion tank too.
