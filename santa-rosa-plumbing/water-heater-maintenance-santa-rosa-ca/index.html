    Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in Santa Rosa

    Gas water heaters need a clean burner and a proper vent to run safely. We inspect the burner flame, clean the combustion chamber and check the thermocouple or flame sensor. The vent is checked for proper draft and secure connections. Blocked or loose vents can let carbon monoxide into your home. We also check the gas line and shutoff valve for leaks.

    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Santa Rosa, CA

    Gas water heater maintenance involves crucial safety inspections in Santa Rosa, CA. We check for gas leaks, proper venting of exhaust gases, and the function of the pilot light or igniter. We also clean the burner assembly and inspect the thermocouple. These checks are essential to prevent carbon monoxide exposure and keep your gas-fired appliance running safely.

    T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Santa Rosa, CA

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building unsafe pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets replaced. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge where installed.

We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as needed. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water quickly. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Maintenance in Santa Rosa

    The cost of water heater maintenance in Santa Rosa, CA can range from $120 to $250, depending on the type of water heater (tank or tankless) and the services required. This usually includes a full flush, anode rod inspection, and safety checks. More complex issues or parts replacement would be an additional cost.

    Tank water heaters typically last about 8 to 12 years, and tankless units often last longer with regular descaling. Hard water can shorten life without maintenance.

    The anode rod is a metal rod inside your water heater that sacrifices itself to keep the steel tank from corroding. It attracts corrosive elements in the water. Regular inspection and replacement of a depleted anode rod are key for extending the lifespan of your water heater, particularly where water quality varies.

    During a water heater flush, we drain the tank completely and then refill it, letting water flow out through the drain valve until it runs clear. This process removes mineral deposits and sediment that collect at the bottom of the tank, which can hurt efficiency and cause damage.

    We service all major brands including Rheem, A.O. Smith, Bradford White, State, Rinnai, Navien and Noritz.

    Common signs include strange noises like rumbling or popping, rusty or discolored hot water, a rotten egg smell, reduced hot water supply, or a clear drop in water pressure. If you notice any of these, it's time for a professional check.

    Many homeowners can do a basic flush, but older drain valves can break or leak. A pro also tests safety valves, venting and gas lines.

    Sediment buildup consists of mineral deposits, mainly calcium and magnesium, that settle at the bottom of your water heater tank. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe.
