    PEX Pipe Installation in Santa Clara, CA

    PEX bends around corners, shrugs off corrosion and needs far fewer fittings than copper. On a whole-house repipe that means fewer joints hidden behind drywall and fewer places for a slow leak to start rotting a stud. It can also stretch when water freezes. Even in mild areas, wrapping irrigation backflow devices before a frosty night is cheap insurance against a surprise leak. That stretch is not a license to skip insulation, so exterior-wall and crawl-space runs still get wrapped.
