    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Santa Clara

    For more complex or recurring slab leaks, or when the original piping is badly deteriorated, pipe rerouting is an excellent long-term solution. This method involves abandoning the leaky pipe under the slab and installing new plumbing lines through walls or the attic, bypassing the foundation entirely. This removes future slab leak risks from that particular line and is often preferred for older homes in Santa Clara with aging copper or galvanized pipes.
