    Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Santa Clara

    One leak on a twenty-year-old copper line? A repair coupling is often the right call. Three leaks on a galvanized line from the 1950s is different, because the pipe is thin everywhere and the next leak is already forming, so replacing the whole run is cheaper over five years. We tell you which camp you are in and show you the pipe we cut out.

    Water Line Materials We Install in Santa Clara, CA

    Our trucks carry Type K copper, PEX-a and HDPE. Each has a place. Copper suits short runs and high pressure, PEX handles long runs with bends, and HDPE is the usual pick for pipe pulling and directional boring, where we need a tough, fused pipe that can be dragged a long way. All of it is listed for drinking water, meeting NSF 61.

    Burst Main Line Response in Santa Clara, CA

    A burst main is a fast-moving problem. We get the water off, dig to the break, and decide on site whether a clamp or a short section of new pipe will hold until a full replacement can be scheduled, or whether the pipe crumbles when we touch it. You will not be without water overnight if we can help it.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Santa Clara, CA

    Bursting fits old cast iron or galvanized lines that will shatter under the head. Copper and poly are better pulled than burst. Either way the new pipe follows the old path, which matters when the route runs under a patio or a neighbor's fence, and the yard work shrinks to two pits. Rock can stop it.

    Traditional Water Line Excavation in Santa Clara

    Sometimes the open trench wins. If the old pipe has collapsed or turns hard corners, we dig. We cut the sod and set it aside, dig to depth with a mini excavator or by hand near other utilities, lay the new pipe on a sand bed, and backfill in layers with compaction so the trench does not sink after the first good rain. Sod goes back on top.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Santa Clara

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. It does not corrode or build scale. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, use only fittings listed for burial, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    Galvanized Main Line Replacement in Santa Clara

    Galvanized steel was common in homes built before the 1960s. Inside, the pipe rusts once the zinc is gone. After a few decades the opening can be half its original size, and that shows up as weak pressure and orange water, and no amount of cleaning brings it back. It has to come out.

    Emergency Water Line Replacement in Santa Clara

    Water bubbling up through the lawn or the street side of the sidewalk means a break, not a drip. Close the main shutoff at the house first. If that does not stop it, the break is on your side of the meter but before the valve, and we will shut it at the curb stop with a meter key and plan the replacement from there, usually starting the same or next day.
