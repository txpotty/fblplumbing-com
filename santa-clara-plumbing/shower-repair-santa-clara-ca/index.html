    Fixing Showers in Santa Clara, CA

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Santa Clara

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Grit and scale scratch the sealing surfaces, the O-rings flatten and harden, and eventually the valve cannot fully stop the water when the handle is closed. A new cartridge fixes it. The new cartridge goes in with silicone grease, which keeps the handle smooth.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Santa Clara, CA

    Stains on the ceiling below are often the first clue. We use a moisture meter on the walls and a flood test on the pan to tell a leaking valve from a cracked pan, a failed grout line or a loose drain body. The source gets repaired. On slab homes, these leaks can spread into the flooring of the next room before anyone sees them.

    Mixing Valve Repair in Santa Clara

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. A sticking cartridge or spool shows up as temperature that drifts or a handle that has to go much farther than it used to. We replace the cartridge. If the valve is too old for parts, we talk through a replacement valve and what it means for the tile.

    Commercial Shower Repair in Santa Clara, CA

    Gyms and apartment buildings run showers dozens of times a day. We repair commercial pressure-balance and thermostatic valves with heavy-duty cartridges, and we can work early or late so a locker room or a unit is not out of service during peak hours. We stock tamper-resistant heads. Every repair is set to scald-limit code.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Santa Clara, CA

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. It comes down to the hot side, whether that is a sediment-choked heater outlet or a cartridge with scale packed in the hot port. Each side gets tested. If the sinks have good hot pressure and the shower does not, the cartridge is the first suspect.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Santa Clara, CA

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. Because everything is linked, a weak diverter starves the head and a leaky valve drips from the spout and the head alike. We check all of it. The overflow gasket gets a look too, since it is a common hidden leak.

    Emergency Shower Leak Repair in Santa Clara

    Water coming through a ceiling below a bathroom needs fast action. Close the main, set a bucket under the drip and stay clear of any drywall that is bulging, because wet ceilings can drop. Then give us a call. We find the source, stop it and dry out what we can that day.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Santa Clara, CA

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. Usually a new spout fixes it. It is a short job.
