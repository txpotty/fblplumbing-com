    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Santa Clara

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit usually costs $600 to $1,500. Installing a new pit with slab cutting and excavation often runs $1,500 to $4,000. Battery backups usually add $500 to $1,500.

    In many cities, a plumbing permit is typically required for new sump pump installations or major replacements. This confirms the work meets local building and plumbing codes. FBL Plumbing handles all necessary permitting in Santa Clara, CA to make sure your installation is fully compliant.

    Water should discharge away from the foundation to a safe area on your property. Many cities do not allow sump discharge into the sanitary sewer.

    A simple replacement often does not need a permit. New pits, electrical work or discharge connections may require one, and we will let you know.

    Most sump pumps last 7 to 10 years. Pumps that sit idle for long periods may fail sooner, so regular testing is important.

    To keep your new sump pump running efficiently, test it monthly by pouring water into the pit to confirm it activates. Annually, inspect the discharge pipe for obstructions, clean the pit and check the float switch. Regular professional maintenance from FBL Plumbing can extend its lifespan in your Santa Clara home.

    Not every home does, but homes with basements, crawl spaces, sunken patios or lots that collect storm runoff can benefit a lot. A sump pump protects against fast-rising storm flooding.

    A submersible sump pump sits inside the sump pit, making it quieter and more powerful, well suited for heavy use. A pedestal sump pump has its motor above the pit, making it easier to access for maintenance and often more affordable, though it can be louder.
