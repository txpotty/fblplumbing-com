    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Santa Clara

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. Because the motor is cooled by the surrounding water, they run quieter and can push more gallons per minute up a tall lift than most pedestal pumps. Lids close flat over them. Each install gets a sealed lid and a grommet around the cord to keep out radon and odor.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Santa Clara, CA

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. You can reach the motor and the switch without fishing anything out of the pit, which helps in a basement where the pit is shallow or tight. Expect more motor noise than a submersible. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Santa Clara

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. For those jobs we install duplex systems where two pumps alternate, with a control panel and a high water alarm that can tie into the building monitoring. A flooded elevator pit shuts down a building. We plan the install around your business hours.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Santa Clara

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Santa Clara, CA

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. We carry common pumps on the truck. Figure on two hours for a typical swap. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Santa Clara, CA

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. When we replace a pump we also check the basin for silt, look at the check valve flapper and run water through the discharge line to find any clogs. Undersized pumps get upsized. We fill the pit and watch it run.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Santa Clara, CA

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary handles daily water. Finished basements are the usual reason. They are worth it anywhere a few inches of water would mean new drywall.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Santa Clara

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. We install exterior basins with pumps for those spots. Slopes toward the house are the usual cause. We keep the outlet away from your foundation and the neighbor line.
