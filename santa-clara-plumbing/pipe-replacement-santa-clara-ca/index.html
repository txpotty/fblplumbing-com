    What Goes Into a Repipe in Santa Clara, CA

    Whole-Home Re-Piping in Santa Clara

    A whole-home repipe replaces every supply line from the main shutoff to each fixture, including the water heater connections. Plan on a few days for a typical house. We run new trunk lines through the attic or crawlspace, branch down to fixtures through small wall openings, with service restored each evening when possible. Then patching.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Santa Clara, CA

    Copper still has fans. It is durable and heat tolerant. We install Type L copper for most interior supply lines, sweat or press the joints, and support every run with hangers at the spacing code requires, so the new pipe does not rattle or sag. Costs more than PEX.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Santa Clara, CA

    Pinhole leaks start small. A drip, then a stain. They usually mean the copper wall has thinned from corrosion, and when one shows up, more are often on the way along the same lines. Repipe or patch, we explain.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Santa Clara, CA

    Galvanized steel was common before the 1960s. The zinc wears off and the steel rusts inside. Over decades the opening shrinks, pressure drops and the water turns brown after sitting, and the threads where pipes join are often the first to leak. Replacing it is the only fix.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Santa Clara

    Polybutylene is gray or blue plastic installed from roughly the late 1970s to mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside. Failures tend to be sudden splits at fittings rather than slow drips, and some insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Replace it.

    Copper Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Santa Clara, CA

    Older homes sometimes have copper that is thin from decades of use. Small leaks appear in random spots. We replace it with new Type L copper or PEX, matching the layout or improving it where the old routing was awkward. Better flow.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Santa Clara

    PEX bends around corners and comes in long rolls, so a typical repipe needs fewer fittings than copper would on the same layout. Freezing is uncommon along the coast and on valley floors in Northern California, but frosty nights do happen and can crack unprotected outdoor pipes. PEX can stretch a little in a freeze instead of splitting. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings where possible, keeping flow strong at each joint. Faster install.

    Commercial Pipe Replacement in Santa Clara

    Commercial buildings often have larger supply lines and more fixtures. Work has to fit around your hours. We plan pipe replacement in phases whenever the layout allows it, so only part of the building is offline at a time. Night work available.
