    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Santa Clara, CA

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside. These units face sun, rain and temperature swings, which can wear out plastic covers and wiring over time. Mountain and foothill homes in Northern California see real winters, so pipes there need insulation, heat tape, or careful draining when a cabin sits empty. When we replace them, we check the pipe cover, freeze protection settings and the electrical connection. We can add a protective pipe cover kit to shield the water and gas lines. Exterior models are often the easiest swap when the gas line is already sized correctly.

    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Santa Clara

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Santa Clara

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by recovering heat from exhaust gases. They vent through low-cost PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Coastal fog and moisture can speed corrosion on exposed fittings, so exterior valves and hose bibs deserve an occasional check. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth it.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Santa Clara

    The cost for tankless water heater replacement in Santa Clara, CA typically ranges from $3,000 to $6,000, depending on the unit's brand, capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any needed changes to gas lines, electrical or venting. This estimate includes both the new unit and professional installation. We always provide a detailed quote upfront after assessing your needs.

    Most jurisdictions in Northern California require a permit for water heater replacement, especially when gas piping or venting changes. We handle permits where they are required.

    Yes, you can absolutely replace a traditional tank water heater with a tankless unit. This is a common upgrade for homeowners looking for better energy efficiency and endless hot water. However, it often requires changes to gas lines, venting and possibly electrical wiring, making professional installation essential for safety and performance.

    A standard tankless water heater replacement, if you are upgrading from an existing tankless unit, typically takes 4 to 8 hours. If you are converting from a traditional tank water heater, the process can take longer, possibly a full day, due to additional plumbing, gas or electrical changes required for proper installation and venting.

    A direct swap typically takes four to six hours. A tank to tankless conversion with new gas and venting typically takes a full day.

    To extend the life and efficiency of your new tankless water heater, regular maintenance is key, especially in areas with hard water. This typically involves annual flushing to remove mineral buildup, checking the air filter, and inspecting gas and water connections. FBL Plumbing offers maintenance plans to help keep your unit in top condition.

    New tankless water heaters typically come with a manufacturer's warranty on the heat exchanger, usually 10 to 15 years, and on parts, usually 3 to 5 years.     Well-maintained tankless units can last 15 to 20 years. In hard water areas without regular descaling, heat exchangers can fail much sooner. Annual flushing makes the biggest difference.
