    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Santa Clara, CA

    Air switches start the disposal with a button on the sink or counter. The button pushes air through a tube to a control box in the cabinet, which keeps electricity away from the button and avoids a new wall switch. Islands use them often.

    High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in Santa Clara

    A 3/4 or 1 HP unit takes up more room and weighs more than a basic unit, so we measure the cabinet first. Taller units can push the discharge outlet below the wall stub, and when that happens we either lower the drain at the wall or pick a model with a higher outlet so water does not sit in the chamber. Power needs a check too. Bigger motors draw more at startup.

    Batch-Feed Units for Kitchens Without a Switch in Santa Clara

    Batch-feed disposals only run when the stopper lid is in place, which adds safety for families with kids. They need no wall switch, so they are a good choice for kitchens where adding a switch would mean cutting into finished drywall or tile backsplash. Mounting and drains are standard.

    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Santa Clara, CA

    On a double sink, the disposal goes on one bowl and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. The layout keeps both bowls draining and stops the disposal from pushing water into the second bowl, a problem when the tee is too close to the discharge. We use heavy-wall fittings.

    Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in Santa Clara, CA

    A continuous-feed unit needs a switch, which a batch-feed kitchen may not have. That can be a wall switch, an air switch button on the sink deck or a switched outlet under the sink, and the choice depends on how hard it is to run wire to the spot you want. Air switches avoid new wiring in the wall. The drain usually stays the same.

    InSinkErator Disposal Installation in Santa Clara, CA

    InSinkErator uses the Quick Lock mount on most current models, so we tighten the ring with the included wrench. Badger units are the simple single-stage line, while Evolution models add grind stages and insulation that make them noticeably quieter in an open kitchen. Cords are often sold separately. We bring the right cord kit for the model.

    Standard Disposal Installation in Santa Clara

    Most households in Santa Clara do well with a continuous-feed unit between 1/3 and 1/2 horsepower. We remove the old unit if there is one, set a new flange in fresh plumber's putty, hang the disposal on a new mount and connect it to the trap with a new discharge tube and gasket. Wiring and testing come last. A smooth spin and a clean drain tell us it is right.

    High-Capacity Disposal Installation in Santa Clara, CA

    Busy kitchens get the most from a 1 HP unit, like an InSinkErator Evolution or a Waste King Legend model. We check that the circuit can handle the motor and that the discharge path is sized for the heavier flow, since a big unit feeding a narrow or poorly sloped trap arm still clogs. Bigger units are heavier. We support them while mounting.
