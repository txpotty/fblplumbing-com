    Why Polybutylene Fails in Santa Clara

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. Heat makes it worse. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.

    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Santa Clara, CA

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. We use Uponor PEX-A with expansion fittings that do not restrict flow. Every fixture, appliance and the water heater gets new pipe from the main, and most houses are finished in two to four days with water on each evening.
