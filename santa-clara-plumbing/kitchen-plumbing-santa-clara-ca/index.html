    Burst Supply Lines in Santa Clara, CA

    Plastic supply lines with plastic nuts crack more often than braided stainless, especially after years of pressure swings.     Dishwasher and Ice Maker Lines in Santa Clara

    A dishwasher needs a hot supply with its own shutoff, a drain to the disposal or a wye tailpiece, and an air gap on the sink deck or a high loop fastened under the counter, depending on local code. Ice makers need a cold line. We use copper or braided stainless with a quarter-turn valve, not the thin plastic tubing that cracks behind refrigerators.

    New Disposal Installation in Santa Clara

    Adding a disposal where there never was one means a switched outlet or circuit in the cabinet, and that part is electrical work. We handle the plumbing and can line up the electrician.     Disposal Jams and Hums in Santa Clara, CA

    A hum without grinding tells us the motor has power and the plate will not turn. Usually a ten minute fix. We free the plate from below with a hex key, clear the hopper with pliers, never a hand, and reset the overload before running water through it. Silence with no hum points to the switch, the outlet or the breaker instead.

    InSinkErator Installation and Service in Santa Clara

    InSinkErator units use the Quick Lock mount, so swapping one InSinkErator for another is usually fast.
