    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Santa Clara

    Control Valve Repair in Santa Clara

    Water that runs to the drain all day usually means the control valve is stuck between cycles. FBL Plumbing rebuilds stuck valves with the correct seal and spacer kit for your model.     Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Santa Clara, CA

    A crust of salt across the brine tank stops regeneration, and hard water shows up at every tap within a day or two. The bridge gets broken up and the tank cleaned out. Older brine tanks can crack, and we repair or replace those too. Keep the salt about half full.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Santa Clara

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads so they cannot grab hardness. Depending on the test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced. New resin restores full capacity.

    Water Softener Brands We Repair in Santa Clara

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Santa Clara

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. Salt sits there unused, and regeneration fails. We break the bridge, clean out the mushy salt and check the float and brine well. Cracked tanks get replaced.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Santa Clara, CA

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. If scale comes back or pressure drops, the media or the prefilter is usually the problem. FBL Plumbing repairs these units in Santa Clara, CA. Media lasts a few years.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Santa Clara
