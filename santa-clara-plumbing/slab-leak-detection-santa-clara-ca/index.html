    How We Find Slab Leaks in Santa Clara, CA

    Supply and Drain Leaks Under the Slab in Santa Clara, CA

    A supply leak and a drain leak show different signs. Supply leaks are pressurized, so they move the meter, warm the floor on the hot side and run whether or not anyone uses water. Drain leaks only leak when water goes down, and they show up as odors, slow drains or damp floors near bathrooms. Each needs its own test in your Santa Clara, CA home.

    Repiping After Repeat Slab Leaks in Santa Clara, CA

    Two slab leaks usually means more are coming. The rest of the pipe has likely aged the same way, so repiping replaces every supply line with new PEX or copper through the walls and attic, which ends the pattern of new leaks and new floor openings. A full repipe takes about two to four days.

    Emergency Slab Leak Detection in Santa Clara

    Water bubbling up through a tile joint or a sudden pressure drop tells you the leak got bigger. Shut the main. Our techs in Santa Clara respond quickly, isolate the leaking side so you can get water back on the rest of the house overnight, and come back to pinpoint and fix it.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Santa Clara, CA

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. That makes them harder to spot. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, so we isolate the cold side, map the pipe with a line tracer and listen along the route with a ground microphone.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Santa Clara

    A lot of newer houses are built on post-tension slabs. These slabs have steel tendons stretched under tens of thousands of pounds of force, and cutting one by mistake is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We scan for tendons with ground-penetrating radar or a rebar locator before opening anything, or suggest a reroute that skips the slab.

    Pressure Testing and Isolation in Santa Clara

    We always start with isolation. Our tech closes the main, caps or valves off the hot and cold sides, and puts a gauge on each, then watches which one drops over fifteen to thirty minutes. That tells us where to look. It also rules out a toilet or faucet leak masquerading as a slab leak in your Santa Clara, CA home.

    Why Locating First Saves Money in Santa Clara

    Detection usually costs a few hundred dollars. A wrong guess costs more. Breaking up a floor in the wrong place means paying for concrete work, flooring and a second opening, while an accurate mark keeps the repair to one small hole or a clean reroute that never touches the slab in your Santa Clara property.

    Tracer Gas Leak Detection in Santa Clara

    PEX and CPVC do not carry sound well. For those, we drain the line and push in a safe hydrogen and nitrogen tracer gas, which rises through the slab and gets picked up by a sniffer at the floor surface, often through grout joints and hairline cracks. It finds leaks acoustic gear misses in Santa Clara homes.
