    Leaks in Walls and Ceilings in Hanford, CA

    A ceiling stain right below a bathroom usually points to a shower valve, a drain joint or a toilet wax ring. We figure out which.     Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Hanford, CA

    The service line from the meter to the house can leak for months without surfacing, especially in old galvanized pipe. Green grass in a dry spell is a clue. We trace the line with an electronic locator, then use a ground microphone to hear the leak, and on plastic pipe we inject tracer gas and sniff for it at the surface.

    Gas Leak Detection in Hanford

    A faint sulfur smell near the range or furnace needs a professional check. Leave the house if the odor is strong. Our licensed plumbers use a combustible gas detector and soap solution on fittings, appliance connectors and the meter riser, then pressure test the line to find a leak too small to smell, and we repair it to the fuel gas code before turning service back on.

    Preventive Leak Inspections in Hanford, CA
