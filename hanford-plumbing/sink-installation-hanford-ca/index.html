    How We Set New Sinks in Hanford, CA

    Drop-In Sink Installation in Hanford, CA

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim hides small cutout flaws, so laminate tops take a drop-in with just a size check and the right sealant. Clips pull it tight to the counter. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Sink Replacement and Upgrades in Hanford

    Upgrading the sink is a cheap way to refresh a room. Swapping a chipped drop-in for a new one in the same cutout usually takes a morning, and moving up to a deeper single bowl may only need a new trap and a short drain extension. Countertop changes add time. We tell you which one you are looking at.

    Double Vanity Sink Installation in Hanford

    A second bowl means a second drain and two more supplies. We tie both drains into the wall with either two separate traps or a continuous waste to one trap, depending on the spacing, and add a new pair of stops for the second faucet. We check the vent size too. Both bowls should drain without gurgling.

    Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in Hanford

    Stone shops cut the hole, but we set the bowl. An even reveal around a polished cutout takes shims and patience before the clips are tightened. A crooked reveal never goes away. We check it from both ends of the counter.

    Sink Installation Cost Comparison in Hanford, CA

    Drop-in swaps cost the least. Undermounts cost more for clips and cure time, and vessels add a special drain and a taller faucet. Relocating the drain is the biggest add. We price each piece on the quote.

    Kitchen Sink Replacement in Hanford

    Pulling the old kitchen sink is half the job. Strainers are often fused on, the disposal has to come off, and the dishwasher hose needs a fresh high loop. After that, the new bowl goes in. We reuse the disposal if it still runs well.

    Pedestal Sink Mounting in Hanford

    The pedestal is mostly for looks. The basin itself hangs on a bracket lagged into 2x blocking behind the drywall, and if the old wall has no blocking we open it, add a board between the studs, and patch it back. Drywall anchors do not cut it. A loose basin cracks the trap.

    Faucet and Drain Assembly for New Sinks in Hanford, CA

    Faucet and drain go on before the sink is set, when possible. Setting the faucet and drain while the bowl is on sawhorses is faster and seals better than working in the cabinet. Undermounts are the exception. Those get plumbed from below.
