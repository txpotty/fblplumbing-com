    From Spouts to In-Wall Valves in Hanford, CA

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Hanford

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. That cartridge wears out and causes drips or a handle that will not reach full hot, and swapping it with a cartridge puller takes about an hour when the valve body is in good shape. Older Moentrol valves take another cartridge. We carry both.

    Brand Tub Faucet Replacement in Hanford

    Brand matters more on tub valves than anywhere else. Each maker uses its own cartridges and trim threads, so a same-brand trim kit usually bolts on without plumbing changes. Cross-brand trim seldom fits. We find the logo or the casting mark first.

    Budget Tub Faucet Options in Hanford, CA

    Not every tub faucet needs a full valve. A cartridge plus a same-brand trim kit can refresh an old faucet for far less than a new valve. We offer that option first. Three-handle faucets are the exception.

    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Hanford, CA

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We replace Moen and Delta cartridges or old compression stems and seats, and we grease the O-rings with silicone plumber's grease so the handle turns smoothly for years. Old seats need a seat wrench. About an hour, usually.

    Delta Tub Faucet Replacement in Hanford, CA

    Delta tub valves are some of the easiest to update. Many Delta Monitor valves accept a new cartridge and trim from the front, and the MultiChoice universal rough-in lets you switch trim styles later without touching the pipes in the wall. We stock common Delta cartridges. Older Delta valves sometimes need full replacement.

    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Hanford, CA

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. Replacement inside the wall makes sense when parts are discontinued, the valve body is cracked or pitted, or you want anti-scald protection on an old three-handle faucet that has none. We quote both options. Most people choose repair first.

    Emergency Tub Faucet Service in Hanford, CA

    A tub that will not shut off needs water off now. Check behind the trim plate or an access panel for stops, and if there are none, shut the main and leave the drain open. A plumber heads out as soon as one is free. Broken stems are a common cause.

    Wall-mount tub fillers are a popular upgrade. A long-reach filler or a slide-bar handheld changes the tub, and both usually need a new valve or diverter. We plan the rough-in first. The trim goes on last.
