    Tankless Water Heater Descaling Service in Hanford

    Hard water can lead to mineral buildup, or scaling, in your tankless water heater's heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and eventually causing breakdowns. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. Our descaling service in Hanford involves flushing the unit with a dedicated cleaning solution to dissolve these mineral deposits. This preventative maintenance and repair step is crucial for extending the lifespan of your tankless heater and keeping it at peak performance.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Repair in Hanford

    Gas tankless water heaters require correct gas pressure and proper ventilation for safe operation. Issues can range from gas valve malfunctions to carbon monoxide sensor errors. Our licensed technicians in Hanford perform thorough diagnostics on gas lines, igniters and exhaust systems, so your unit is both functional and safe.

    Tankless Repair vs Replacement Cost in Hanford, CA

    Descaling service usually costs $175 to $350, and sensor or igniter repairs run $250 to $600. Heat exchanger replacement can cost $800 to $1,500 or more unless covered by warranty.     Recirculation Pump Repair in Hanford

    Many tankless systems use internal or external recirculation to deliver hot water more quickly. Pump failures, check valve problems or wrong schedule settings can cause long waits or lukewarm water. We test the pump, check valves and controls and set the schedule to match your daily routine. A properly tuned system saves both water and gas.

    Tankless Water Heater Error Code Diagnostics in Hanford, CA

    Modern tankless water heaters display error codes to indicate specific problems, from flow rate issues to combustion errors. Our experts in Hanford, CA are trained to interpret these codes accurately, using specialized diagnostic tools to pinpoint the exact component failure. This accuracy allows us to quickly order the correct parts and perform targeted repairs, saving you time and money. We address codes related to burner failures, fan issues and temperature sensor errors, among others.

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Hanford

    Tankless heaters use flow sensors to start and thermistors to regulate temperature. A dirty inlet filter or failed sensor causes no hot water, fluctuating temperature or the unit turning off mid-shower. We clean filters, test sensor readings and replace failed parts. Temperature becomes steady again once readings are correct.

    Pilot Light and Ignition Repair in Hanford, CA

    For gas-fired tankless water heaters, a malfunctioning ignition system can completely stop hot water production. Our team in Hanford, CA diagnoses issues with igniters, flame sensors and gas valves. We confirm proper gas pressure and ventilation are maintained for safe and efficient operation. Timely repair of these components prevents hazardous gas problems and restores your unit's ability to heat water on demand.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Hanford, CA

    Condensing tankless heaters, such as many Navien models, extract extra heat from exhaust and produce condensate. Clogged condensate traps, failed neutralizers and blocked drains can shut the unit down. We clean the trap, replace the neutralizer media and confirm proper drainage. PVC venting is also checked for slope and sagging.
