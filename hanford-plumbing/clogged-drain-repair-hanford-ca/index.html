    Frequently Asked Questions About Clogged Drain Repair in Hanford

    The cost of clogged drain repair in Hanford, CA can vary widely, usually ranging from $150 to $500 for standard clogs cleared with snaking or augering. More complex issues like main sewer line blockages requiring hydro-jetting or excavation could range from $500 to $2,000 or more. We provide clear, upfront pricing after diagnosis.

    Most single fixture clogs are cleared in 30 to 90 minutes. Lines with heavy buildup or limited access can take longer. We tell you if the job will run beyond the usual time.

    You should call a professional plumber for clogged drain repair if DIY methods like plungers are ineffective, if you experience multiple clogged drains, or if you suspect a main sewer line blockage. Recurring clogs or slow drains also indicate a need for professional diagnosis to prevent bigger issues.

    Most common household clogs, like those in sinks or showers, can be cleared within one to two hours. More severe blockages, such as main sewer line clogs requiring camera inspection and hydro-jetting, might take a few hours to a full day, depending on the complexity.

    Almost never. We access drains through the fixture, trap, cleanout or roof vent. Opening a wall is only needed if a pipe is broken and must be replaced.

    A plunger or a hand-held hair snake can handle many simple tub and sink clogs. If the clog returns, affects more than one fixture or does not respond to a plunger, call a plumber. Forcing a cable can crack old pipes or scratch fixtures.

    Drain snaking uses a flexible auger to physically break through or pull out blockages. Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the inside of pipes, thoroughly removing scale, grease, and roots, providing a much more complete cleaning. Hydro-jetting is often used for tough clogs or preventative maintenance.

    While chemical drain cleaners are readily available, FBL Plumbing generally advises against them for homes in Hanford. They often provide only temporary relief, can damage pipes over time, and pose environmental and health risks. Professional tools like augers or hydro-jetting are safer and more effective.
