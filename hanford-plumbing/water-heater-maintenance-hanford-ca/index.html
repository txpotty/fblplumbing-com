    Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in Hanford

    Gas water heaters need a clean burner and a proper vent to run safely and efficiently. We inspect the burner flame, clean the combustion chamber and check the thermocouple or flame sensor.     Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Hanford

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in Hanford, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's significantly depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing expensive tank leaks.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Hanford, CA

    Tankless water heaters need specific maintenance, mainly a descaling flush to remove mineral deposits that can restrict flow and reduce efficiency.     Tank Water Heater Flushing in Hanford

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium sediment at the bottom of the tank. This layer insulates the water from the burner or lower element, wasting energy and causing popping sounds. We connect a hose to the drain valve and flush the tank until the water runs clean. For heavy buildup, we may stir and flush in several cycles. A clean tank heats faster and runs quieter.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Hanford

    Tankless units are especially sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. We isolate the unit with its service valves and circulate a descaling solution with a pump for the recommended time. We also clean the inlet water filter and check the unit's error history. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures consistent.
