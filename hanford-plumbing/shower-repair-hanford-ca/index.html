    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Hanford

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. If the head drips with the valve closed, water is slipping past the cartridge, and a new head will not change that. The fix is in the valve. Leaks at the arm threads are different, and those get new thread tape and a snug turn.

    Same-Day Shower Leak Repair in Hanford, CA

    Shut off the main first if water is getting where it should not. For a sudden wall leak or a valve that will not close, the main valve near the meter is the first move. Call us next. Our plumber stops the water, fixes it or makes it safe and checks for wet areas.

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Hanford

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. Water that seeps from the arm connection runs back along the pipe into the wall, and you only find out when the paint on the other side starts to bubble. Fresh thread tape fixes it. We can add a handheld or slide bar while we are there.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Hanford, CA

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. Because everything is linked, a weak diverter starves the head and a leaky valve drips from the spout and the head alike. We check all of it. We also check the overflow gasket, a frequent hidden leak.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Hanford, CA

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. With good pressure elsewhere, the problem is in the head, the restrictor or the cartridge, and that is typically a short repair. The head comes off first. Low pressure everywhere sends us to the pressure regulator and main valve instead.

    Slow Shower Drain Repair in Hanford, CA

    Standing water at your ankles is the first sign. Hard water makes it worse, because the scale binds with soap scum and hair into a mat that sticks to the pipe wall and builds out over time. An auger cuts through it. For stubborn buildup in the branch line, we jet the line with water instead of using acid cleaners.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Hanford

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. When it does not, we install a new head with soft rubber nozzles that you can wipe clean with a thumb.
