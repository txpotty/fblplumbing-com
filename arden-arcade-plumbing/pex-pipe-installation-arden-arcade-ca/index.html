    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Arden-Arcade

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A bends tighter, recovers from a kink with a heat gun, and its expansion fittings flow more water, while PEX-B costs less and its crimp joints are just as reliable when they are gauged properly. Either one lasts for decades. For tight attics we lean toward PEX-A.

    Hydronic Heating with PEX in Arden-Arcade

    Radiant floor loops are PEX too, but a different kind you drink from. Heating work calls for oxygen-barrier PEX, coated so oxygen cannot seep through the pipe wall and corrode the boiler, the circulator and the iron parts. Loop length matters as well. Run them too long and the last stretch never warms up.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Arden-Arcade, CA
