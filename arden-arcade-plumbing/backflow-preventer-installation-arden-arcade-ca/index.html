    Frequently Asked Questions About Backflow Preventer Installation in Arden-Arcade

    Residential pressure vacuum breakers typically cost $250 to $700 installed. Double check and RPZ assemblies for larger homes or businesses range from $800 to $3,500 or more depending on size and enclosure needs.

    Backflow preventers are installed at cross-connection points where non-potable water could enter the potable water supply. Common locations include irrigation system connections, fire sprinkler lines, boiler systems and connections to auxiliary water supplies, often just after the main water meter or at the point of hazard.

    Most residential backflow preventer installations can be finished within 2 to 4 hours. More complex commercial installations or those requiring extensive pipe changes might take a full day. We always aim for efficient service with minimal disruption to your water supply.

    FBL Plumbing stands behind our work with a comprehensive warranty on our backflow preventer installations. This warranty covers both the labor and the installed device, providing you with peace of mind. Specific warranty details will be included with your quote.

    Many jurisdictions require a permit for new backflow assemblies, especially on commercial properties. We check local rules and pull the permit when required.

    Most water providers require testable assemblies to be tested at installation and then annually. Some high hazard facilities may be tested more often.

    Constant dripping or discharge from an RPZ relief valve, low pressure downstream and a failed annual test are common signs. Visible corrosion or a cracked body from freezing also means service is needed.
