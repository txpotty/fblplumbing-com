    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Arden-Arcade

    A standard single tank softener replacement usually costs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Dual tank or premium systems usually run $3,000 to $5,000. Adding a new loop or drain line can raise the cost.

    With proper maintenance, most quality water softeners can last between 10 and 15 years, and sometimes even longer. Factors like water hardness, usage volume and the quality of the original installation can affect its lifespan. Regular servicing helps extend the life of your unit.

    Common signs include a return of hard water symptoms like scale buildup on fixtures, dry skin and hair, dingy laundry or excessive soap use. Other signs are a noticeable drop in water pressure, leaks from the unit, or a softener over 10 to 15 years old that needs frequent repairs.

    To extend the life of your new water softener, use the correct type of salt, if applicable, and keep the brine tank filled. Regular maintenance, such as cleaning the brine tank and periodic inspections by a professional, can also help ensure its longevity and efficiency.

    The right size depends on your household's daily water usage and the hardness of your water in grains per gallon. Our technicians will perform a water test and calculate the appropriate softening capacity in grains per day for your Arden-Arcade home, so the system performs well.

    Many systems include 5 to 10 years on the valve and longer coverage on tanks. We also provide a labor warranty.

    Spots on dishes, scale on faucets, dry skin and increased soap use can point to a failing softener. A softener that no longer uses salt or keeps flooding the brine tank may also need replacement.

    Salt-based softeners remove hardness minerals, calcium and magnesium, through ion exchange, truly softening the water. Salt-free conditioners do not remove minerals but change their crystal structure so they do not stick to surfaces. That prevents scale buildup without actually softening the water.
