    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Arden-Arcade

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. We replace timer units with metered valves that regenerate on gallons used. You will buy less salt. We set the brine refill to your hardness so the system is not dumping salt down the drain every few days.

    Urgent Softener Problems in Arden-Arcade

    Brine Tank Replacement in Arden-Arcade

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break them up and clean out the mush at the bottom. Cracked tanks and stuck safety floats get replaced. Pellets bridge less than rock salt.
