    Delta Faucet Replacement in Arden-Arcade, CA

    Delta faucets come in a few cartridge families, and the model number tells you which.     Kitchen Faucet Replacement in Arden-Arcade

    Kitchen faucets usually fail at the hose first. Pull-down hoses crack at the spot where they flex over the neck, and the leak runs inside the body down into the cabinet where it stays hidden. A hose kit can fix that. When the body or cartridge is worn too, we swap the whole faucet and put new supplies on.

    Moen Faucet Replacement in Arden-Arcade

    Moen's 1225 cartridge runs a huge share of single-handle Moen faucets. A stuck 1225 makes the handle stiff and the temperature wander, and after many years in place it can be cemented in by minerals. Sometimes pulling it cracks the body. That is when we replace the faucet.

    Tub and shower trim can be replaced without opening the wall.
