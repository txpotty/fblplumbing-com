    Gas Tankless Water Heater Repair in Arden-Arcade

    Pressure at full fire matters on a gas tankless burner, not just at the moment it lights. We put a manometer on the inlet test port and run every hot tap in the house, because pressure that drops below the manufacturer's minimum under load causes flame loss and cold slugs. Then we check the exhaust. Codes come off when the numbers are right.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling Service in Arden-Arcade

    Scale is calcium and magnesium carbonate that drops out of hard water when it heats, and a tankless exchanger heats water fast through narrow passages. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. Our tech connects a pump to the isolation valves and runs descaler through the exchanger for close to an hour. Then we flush with clean water. The inlet screen gets cleaned too.

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Arden-Arcade

    Tankless heaters start when the flow sensor sees about half a gallon a minute, and they hold temperature off thermistor readings. A dirty inlet screen or a failing sensor shows up as no hot water, temperature swings or a unit that drops out mid-shower. We pull the screen, read sensor values in the service menu and compare thermistor resistance to the chart. Usually under an hour.

    Navien Condensate & Fan Repair in Arden-Arcade

    Navien's E016 is a heat exchanger overheat, and scale or a backed-up condensate line is usually behind it. We also read fan speed from the service menu and compare it to spec, since a fan that cannot reach target RPM will cause flame and exhaust faults on the same unit. Clean trap, clean screen, retest.
