    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Arden-Arcade

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. An old timer head regenerates by the calendar, used or not. After retesting hardness, we reprogram the valve or swap in a metered head that regenerates only when needed. Salt bills drop.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Arden-Arcade, CA

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. Once it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying, and the softener regenerates with plain water that does nothing to the resin. We clean or replace it and check the brine line for air leaks.

    Water Softener Repair vs. Replacement in Arden-Arcade, CA

    Age and parts availability decide whether a softener is worth repairing. FBL Plumbing gives honest advice based on both. A ten-year-old unit with a sound tank and a rebuildable valve is usually worth fixing, while an obsolete head with no parts available is a different conversation. You get a clear breakdown.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Arden-Arcade, CA

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. Installing a new quality softener usually runs $1,500 to $3,500.     Resin Testing and Replacement in Arden-Arcade

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Treated city water slowly breaks resin down into mush. We test resin capacity, then clean it with resin cleaner or replace it, and a solid tank and valve with fresh resin works like new for years. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Arden-Arcade, CA

    A crust of salt across the brine tank stops regeneration, and hard water shows up at every tap within a day or two. The bridge gets broken up and the tank cleaned out. Older brine tanks can crack, and we repair or replace those too. Keep the salt about half full.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Arden-Arcade

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. After the repair, the valve is programmed for your household size and hardness so you have soft water by the next cycle.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Arden-Arcade

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. After breaking up the crust, we clear the sludge and test the float and brine well. Cracked tanks get replaced.
