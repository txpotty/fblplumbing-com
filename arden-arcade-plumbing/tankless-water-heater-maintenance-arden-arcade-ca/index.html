    What Our Tankless Maintenance Covers in Arden-Arcade, CA

    Condensate and Neutralizer Service in Arden-Arcade

    Condensing tankless heaters drip acidic condensate, often around pH 3 to 4, and it needs to pass through a neutralizer before it reaches a drain. The trap gets cleaned and the media checked. Spent media gets replaced. Where the line runs outside, we check that it is supported and protected.

    Inlet Screen and Air Filter Cleaning in Arden-Arcade

    Grit gets in. A small screen at the cold inlet catches sand and scale flakes, especially after a main break. Units with an air intake filter collect dust and lint, which can push the burner into a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.

    Multi-Unit Tankless Checks in Arden-Arcade, CA

    Restaurants and salons often run two or more tankless heaters on a shared manifold, and one weak unit can drag the whole bank down. We check gas pressure with all units firing at once. Each heater gets its own flow and temperature reading. Then we compare them.

    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Arden-Arcade

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. That second exchanger makes acidic condensate. We clear the condensate trap and check the neutralizer media. On A-series models with the built-in recirculation pump, we test the pump and the buffer tank too.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in Arden-Arcade, CA

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. On a Rinnai an LC code means scale, on a Navien E003 means no ignition, and on a Noritz a 90 usually means an air or exhaust problem. We pull the code history before touching anything. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.

    Rinnai Tankless Service in Arden-Arcade

    Rinnai units tell you a lot through the controller. Code 11 means the burner never lit, and code 12 means the flame went out after it did. We clean the Rinnai air intake filter and look over the flame rod for soot or cracks. The LC codes are scale warnings, and they usually clear once the exchanger has been flushed and the unit is reset.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Arden-Arcade

    Picture a bundle of thin tubes with a gas flame under them and cold water racing through at three or four gallons a minute. That is the exchanger. Calcium and magnesium drop out on the hottest tube walls first and narrow the passages. We dissolve it with 45 to 60 minutes of pump-driven descaling solution.

    Annual Tankless Checkup in Arden-Arcade, CA

    The annual checkup is a top-to-bottom pass on the heater. Gas pressure at the inlet gets read with a manometer at full fire and checked against the rating plate. The flue gets a look for obstructions. A written report with every reading goes in your file for next year's comparison.
