    Faucet and Drain Assembly for New Sinks in Arden-Arcade, CA

    Faucet and drain go on before the sink is set, when possible. Setting the faucet and drain while the bowl is on sawhorses is faster and seals better than working in the cabinet. That does not work on undermounts. Those get plumbed from below.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Arden-Arcade, CA

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. Since the rim sits higher, the faucet has to be taller or wall-mounted, and the drain must match whether the bowl has an overflow. No overflow means no slotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Faucet and Disposal Hookup in Arden-Arcade, CA

    New sinks often get a new disposal. The disposal goes on a new flange, the dishwasher knockout gets punched, and the outlet lines up above the trap. Forgetting the knockout plug floods the dishwasher. We run both before leaving.

    Undermount Clips and Cure Time in Arden-Arcade

    Undermount bowls need overnight to cure. Silicone holds in a few hours but needs about a day before it carries a full bowl. The bowl stays braced until our return. Plumbing goes in on the second visit.

    Pedestal Sink Installation in Arden-Arcade, CA

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. Since the trap and stops are right behind the column, we center the drain and use chrome parts that look finished. The wall needs solid blocking. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.

    Pedestal Sink Mounting in Arden-Arcade

    The pedestal is mostly for looks. A wall bracket lagged into blocking carries the weight, and if the wall has none, we open it and add a board. Drywall anchors do not cut it. A loose basin cracks the trap.
