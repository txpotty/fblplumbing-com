    Choosing Drain Material in Fresno, CA

    PVC is the default choice under most sinks. It will not rust and costs less, while thick brass is worth it where the trap shows, like under a pedestal. Some houses use black ABS instead. We match what you have, with a proper transition where they meet.

    Bathroom Drain Upgrade in Fresno, CA

    Old bath drains often carry decades of hair and scum. Replacing the pop-up body and the trap removes the rough inside walls that grab hair, and a smooth new 1-1/4 inch path drains a full bowl in seconds instead of minutes. Repeat clogs drop off. A hair catcher keeps it that way.

    Trap Arm and Wall Drain Repair in Fresno, CA

    Leaks right at the wall point to the trap arm or stub.     Leaking P-Trap Replacement in Fresno

    Traps leak at the joints first. Slip nut washers flatten and harden with age, and a trap opened often for clogs usually has stripped threads. Trap kits are cheap. Labor is most of the bill.

    Corroded Drain Pipe Replacement in Fresno, CA

    Galvanized drain arms rust from the inside. An old steel stub can crumble at the threads, so we cut it back and join new PVC with a shielded coupling. We handle cast iron the same way. Sometimes that means opening the wall.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Replacement in Fresno

    Kitchen drains start failing at the basket strainer. Old strainer putty dries out and the locknut rusts, so water runs down the tailpiece and looks like a trap leak. Strainer and trap get swapped as a pair. Disposal sinks get a new flange instead.

    Code Updates During Drain Replacement in Fresno
