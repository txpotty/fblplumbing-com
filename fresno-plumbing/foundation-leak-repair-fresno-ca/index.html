    Foundation Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Fresno, CA

    Direct repairs are often the lowest upfront cost but involve breaking concrete and may need flooring repairs. Reroutes cost somewhat more but avoid concrete work and future leaks in the abandoned line. A full repipe is the most complete option for homes with multiple leaks or aging pipe. We help you compare total costs, including flooring and drywall repairs.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Repair in Fresno

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs with steel cables under high tension. Cutting into these slabs requires care and scanning to avoid hitting a cable. We use ground-penetrating radar or other scanning methods before opening the floor. When risk is too high, rerouting the plumbing is usually the safer option.

    Drain Line Leak Under Foundation in Fresno, CA

    Leaks in drain lines under the foundation can be harder to detect because they are not under constant pressure. We use camera inspection and hydrostatic testing to find cracks and separated joints. Repairs may involve tunneling, cutting the slab or pipe lining. We walk you through the trade-offs of each method.

    Direct Foundation Leak Repair in Fresno, CA

    When the leak is in an accessible spot and the rest of the pipe is in good condition, direct repair is often the most economical choice. We cut a small opening in the floor, excavate to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The soil is compacted and the concrete is patched to match. Flooring can then be restored by your flooring contractor.

    Water Line Reroute in Fresno

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the foundation and runs a new line through the attic or walls. This skips breaking concrete and removes the risk of future leaks under the slab. We usually use PEX for reroutes because it is flexible and resists corrosion. Drywall openings are kept small and neat for easy patching.

    Basement Wall Sealing and Waterproofing in Fresno, CA

    For homes with basements in Fresno, CA, wall leaks can be a significant concern. We offer comprehensive basement waterproofing services, including interior and exterior sealing. This may involve applying waterproof coatings, injecting cracks with polyurethane or epoxy, or setting up interior drainage systems. These measures prevent water from penetrating porous concrete and masonry, keeping your basement dry and healthy.
