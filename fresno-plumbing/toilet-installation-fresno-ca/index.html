    Toilet Installation Work We Handle in Fresno, CA

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Fresno, CA

    Comfort height bowls stand about two inches taller than standard ones. Consider who uses the bathroom first. Kids and shorter adults may not like their feet off the floor, but people with sore knees or a new hip usually love the extra height. Installation is the same as a standard bowl.

    Standard Toilet Installation in Fresno

    A standard floor-mount toilet is a two-hour job when the flange is sound. We shut off the stop, drain the tank and pull the old bowl. After scraping the old wax off, we check the flange for cracks, set new stainless bolts, and lower the new toilet straight down onto a fresh seal so it compresses evenly. The old one leaves with us.

    ADA-Compliant Toilet Installation in Fresno

    ADA toilets put the seat 17 to 19 inches off the floor, and the centerline sits 16 to 18 inches from the side wall. It is set that way so a grab bar fits. We measure the existing drain before ordering, since moving a drain two inches to hit the ADA centerline means opening the floor. Elongated bowls are standard here.

    Commercial Restroom Toilets in Fresno, CA

    Restaurants and offices get heavy-duty fixtures and accessible stalls built to code. Downtime costs a business money. We schedule commercial installs early in the morning or after hours, and we stage the fixtures and valves on site so each stall is down for as short a time as possible. We work with your GC on inspections.

    New Construction Toilet Installation in Fresno

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. The flange should sit on top of the finished floor. Set it too early and the tile setter has to cut around it, which leaves the flange low and forces a thick seal or an extender later on. Each bowl gets a fresh seal.

    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Fresno

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. They need at least 20 psi to work right. Before suggesting one, we put a gauge on a hose bib, and we mention the noise, because the flush carries through the whole house. They rarely clog.

    Two-Piece Toilet Installation in Fresno

    Two-piece toilets come with the tank and bowl separate, joined by a rubber gasket and two or three bolts. We assemble them on the floor before setting. Tightening the tank bolts evenly, side to side, matters more than how hard they go, because an uneven pull cracks the china or leaves a slow drip at one bolt. It should not wobble.

    Smart Toilet Installation in Fresno, CA

    Smart toilets with heated seats and built-in bidets need two things the old toilet did not: power and a dedicated water connection. Most require a GFCI outlet within reach of the cord. The water connection and mounting are ours, and when an outlet has to be added we schedule a licensed electrician instead of doing that wiring. Then we test every button.
