    Frequently Asked Questions About Drainage Services in Fresno

    Most basic drain clearing in Fresno, CA runs $150 to $500. A main line costs more than a sink. Jetting costs more than cabling, and sewer repairs run from a few thousand for a spot fix to much more for replacement.

    Call when more than one drain is slow, or when a toilet gurgles while the washer drains. Those point to the main line. In the yard, water that stands for a day or more after rain is the cue.

    Roots usually enter through an existing crack or joint, then widen it as they grow. Clay pipe can split over time. A root mass can also push a joint out of line, creating an offset that catches waste, and in bad cases the pipe collapses and has to be dug up or lined from the inside.

    A typical French drain or downspout system takes one to three days. Bigger jobs take longer. Dry wells, long trenches or saw-cut concrete can push it toward a week.

    Most homes never need scheduled drain cleaning. Clean them when they slow down. Homes with root-prone sewers, old cast iron, or a kitchen that sends a lot of grease down the drain do better with a cleaning every 12 to 24 months, timed before the line slows to a crawl.

    We recommend skipping them. They can eat at old pipe. They do not remove scale or roots, and leftover cleaner in the trap is a splash hazard.

    Some do. Busy kitchens benefit from jetting once a year. Sewer laterals with a history of roots need cutting every 12 to 18 months, and older cast iron with heavy scale may need jetting every couple of years to keep it from narrowing back down to a trickle.

    Snaking is the right first step for most single clogs. It costs less and takes less time. Jetting wins for grease, scale and recurring roots since it cleans the whole wall.
