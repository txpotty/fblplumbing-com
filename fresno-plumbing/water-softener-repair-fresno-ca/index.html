    Resin Bed Replacement & Regeneration Issues in Fresno

    Over time, the resin beads inside your water softener can become fouled, damaged, or depleted, reducing their ability to remove hardness minerals. If your system is regenerating but still delivering hard water, the resin bed may be the cause.     Softener Not Regenerating in Fresno

    If hard water symptoms return, the softener may not be regenerating. Causes include a failed timer motor, a bad circuit board, a stuck meter turbine or incorrect settings after a power outage. We test each component, replace the failed part and program the valve for your household size and hardness. You get soft water again after the next cycle.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Fresno, CA

    A salt bridge is a hard crust that forms in the brine tank and keeps salt from dissolving. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. We break up bridges, clean out mushy salt, inspect the float and brine well and repair the safety valve. We also recommend salt types that bridge less in warm or humid spaces.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Fresno, CA

    While less common than traditional softeners, no-salt water conditioners also require maintenance and occasional repair. These systems work by conditioning water to prevent scale buildup without removing minerals. If you notice scale returning or reduced water pressure, your no-salt system may need attention. FBL Plumbing is experienced in diagnosing and repairing issues with these specialized units in Fresno, CA, ensuring they continue to protect your plumbing and appliances effectively.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Fresno

    Over time, the resin beads in your water softener can become fouled or degrade, losing their ability to exchange ions and soften water. This is a frequent issue, especially with older systems or those exposed to high levels of chlorine.
