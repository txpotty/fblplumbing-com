    Picking a Gas Water Heater for Your Fresno, CA Home

    40 and 50 Gallon Tank Replacement in Fresno

    Forty and fifty gallon gas tanks are the workhorse in most Northern California homes, and a leak at the bottom seam means the tank is finished. Newer tanks can run taller than the old one. We measure the closet before ordering. Most of these swaps take two to three hours.

    A.O. Smith Gas Water Heater Service in Fresno

    A.O. Smith makes tanks under its own name and also builds the State line, so parts overlap a lot between the two brands. Sizing and gas hookup are part of the job. We confirm the vent meets their specs.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Replacement in Fresno, CA

    Rheem Performance and Performance Platinum tanks cover most of the range we install, from basic 6-year models to 12-year tanks with better anodes. More warranty years generally buy a heavier anode rod. Choosing is simple.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Fresno, CA

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. A natural gas unit does not go on propane unless the manufacturer allows a conversion. We confirm pressure with a manometer.

    No Hot Water Calls in Fresno

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. Those get tested before anything else. An old tank with a bad valve usually gets replaced.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Upgrades in Fresno, CA

    Condensing gas tanks run around 90 percent efficient or better and vent through PVC, while a standard atmospheric tank sits closer to 60 percent. You will need a condensate drain and a nearby outlet. Payback depends on usage.

    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Fresno

    Closed systems are the reason expansion tanks exist. A pressure regulator or backflow preventer on the main stops heated water from pushing back to the street, so pressure climbs every time the burner runs. Code commonly calls for an expansion tank. We precharge it to your house pressure.

    Bradford White Gas Water Heater Installation in Fresno

    Bradford White tanks are sold only through plumbing wholesalers, not big-box stores, and plenty of Northern California plumbers install them for that reason. Replacement parts are easy to find. Installing to their specs keeps the tank warranty intact.
