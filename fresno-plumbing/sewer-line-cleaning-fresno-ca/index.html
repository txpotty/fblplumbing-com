    Sewer Line Cleaning Cost Compared to Replacement in Fresno, CA

    Cleaning costs a few hundred dollars. Digging costs far more. A sound pipe is worth maintaining, while a collapsed or sagging one may need repair, and the camera tells us which, and you see both prices.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Fresno, CA

    Jetting cleans the pipe wall. A snake punches a hole. A jetter scours the full circumference of the pipe at pressures we set for the material, from PVC to clay.

    Identifying Signs of Sewer Line Clogs in Fresno, CA

    Several drains backing up at once is the main sign. Listen for gurgling. Water coming up through a drain when you flush, a sewage smell in the yard or basement and slow drains throughout the house all point to a sewer line that needs cleaning.
