    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Fresno, CA

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. Jetting cleans the whole pipe. For a single clog in an otherwise clean line, snaking is cheaper and works fine, but for a line that clogs every few months, jetting removes the buildup that keeps catching debris.

    Hydrojetting for Shops and Offices in Fresno, CA

    Retail and office buildings tend to have long drain runs where buildup settles. Flat runs collect more debris. We jet those runs on a schedule that matches the building's use, and we can work after hours so tenants are not dealing with equipment in hallways or restrooms during the day.

    After-Hours Hydrojet Service in Fresno

    Backups at night or on weekends get the same equipment as weekday calls. On-call trucks are fully equipped. That means we can clear and jet a grease-packed restaurant line or a root-filled home sewer in one visit, without waiting until Monday to bring the jetter back for a second trip.

    Same-Day Jetting for Backups in Fresno

    Sewage in a tub or floor drain is a main line blockage. Stop using water first. Our technician opens the line, then jets it to remove the cause, and a camera pass after jetting shows whether the backup came from buildup alone or from a broken or root-filled section that needs repair.

    Commercial Hydrojet Plumbing in Fresno, CA

    Businesses put more through their drains than any house. Apartment buildings, hotels and offices see heavy daily flow. Commercial stacks and mains get jetted outside business hours, with a written report for the manager.

    Water-Only Drain Cleaning in Fresno, CA

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Chemical cleaners can damage pipe. Jetting removes the buildup physically, and the debris flushes to the sewer or septic tank as normal waste, so there is nothing left in the pipe to eat at joints or harm the bacteria a septic system depends on.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydrojetting in Fresno

    Roots find their way into sewer lines through cracked joints, and older clay pipe has a joint every few feet. We use a spinning root-cutter nozzle. The jet then flushes the debris to the main, and the camera marks the joints that let the roots in.
