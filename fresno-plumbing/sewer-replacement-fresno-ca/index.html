    Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Fresno

    For severely damaged or collapsed sewer lines, traditional trenching is often the most reliable solution. This involves excavating a trench along the sewer line's path to allow for direct access and replacement of the entire pipe. While more invasive, it provides a complete overhaul and is often necessary for older homes with clay or cast iron pipes that have reached the end of their lifespan. We ensure careful planning and execution to minimize disruption to your yard.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement Options in Fresno, CA

    When appropriate, trenchless sewer replacement offers a less invasive alternative to traditional digging. Methods like pipe bursting or pipe lining allow us to replace or repair your sewer line with minimal excavation, often only requiring two access points. This can save your lawn, driveway, and landscaping from extensive damage, making it a popular choice for homeowners in Fresno who want to preserve their property's appearance. We'll assess if your sewer line is a good candidate for this advanced technique.

    Tree roots are a common cause of sewer line damage, especially with older, more porous pipe materials like clay or cast iron. Our sewer replacement services specifically address root-damaged pipes, often recommending more resistant materials like PVC or ABS to stop future intrusions. We also advise on tree placement to protect your new sewer system.
