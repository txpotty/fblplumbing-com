    What Our Commercial Plumbers Do in Fresno, CA

    Commercial Leak Detection in Fresno, CA

    Water bills that jump 30 percent with no change in business usually mean a hidden leak or a stuck fixture. Start with the meter. We shut fixtures off zone by zone and watch the meter, then use acoustic sensors, thermal cameras and tracer gas to find leaks in walls, ceilings and buried service lines. Sometimes the culprit is just a running toilet. A single stuck flush valve can burn through thousands of gallons in a week.

    Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Fresno

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. We use high-pressure jetting with rotating nozzles that scrub grease off the whole circumference of 4-inch and 6-inch lines, not just the bottom. Jetting keeps the line open for months instead of days. We see the most benefit in restaurants, schools and multi-family stacks.

    Commercial Water Heaters and Boilers in Fresno, CA

    Gyms, commercial kitchens and apartment buildings burn through hot water in short, heavy peaks. Our work covers high-input tanks, multi-unit tankless racks and boilers feeding indirect storage tanks. Rural properties around Fresno often rely on private wells, which may need filtration for iron, sediment, or other minerals. Mineral scale chokes output quickly. We put the heaters on a descaling schedule and install mixing valves so hand-wash sinks get tempered water at a safe temperature.

    Multi-Family and Property Management Plumbing in Fresno

    Apartment complexes need a plumber who can handle many units and quick turns between tenants. Our work in multi-family buildings includes leak repair, heater swaps, stack clearing and full repipes, with posted notices before any shutoff. Pricing stays consistent by unit.     Backflow Testing and Certification in Fresno

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage. Our tester opens each test cock in order, records the check valve and relief valve readings on a calibrated gauge and compares them against the pass limits printed on the test form. We tag every passing device. We file the report with your water provider and keep a copy on our side for next year.

    Grease Trap Installation in Fresno

    A new restaurant or a kitchen expansion usually needs a grease interceptor sized from the fixtures that drain to it. Under-sink traps work for small kitchens, while larger operations need in-ground interceptors of 500 gallons or more. Sizing is set by code. We size the unit from the equipment schedule, set it at the right grade and vent it properly so it passes inspection the first time.

    Commercial Pipe Repair and Repiping in Fresno, CA

    Older commercial buildings often have galvanized supply lines that are rusted nearly shut, or copper that has pinholed along hot water runs. Patching works for a while. Once leaks start showing up in different spots every few months, a sectional repipe in Type L copper or commercial-grade PEX usually costs less than the next three emergency calls. We map the system first. Then we phase the work by floor or by tenant so the building stays open.

    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Fresno

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Earthquakes and hillside soil movement are real risks in Northern California, so flexible connections and seismic straps help protect plumbing from sudden movement. We confirm it with a meter test and pressure test, then use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to pinpoint the leak within a foot or two. Then we decide. A spot repair through the floor makes sense for some, and an overhead reroute makes more sense for others. We price both so you can compare.
