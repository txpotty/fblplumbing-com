    Inlet Filter and Air Intake Cleaning in Fresno

    Every tankless unit has an inlet water screen that catches grit and scale flakes. Those screens can clog with sand and debris, especially after municipal line work. We pull and clean the screen and inspect the air intake filter, which can load up with dust and dryer lint. Clean filters keep the flow sensor reading accurately and the burner breathing properly.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Flushing in Fresno

    Rural properties around Fresno often rely on private wells, which may need treatment for iron, sediment, or other minerals. Hard water can lead to heavy mineral buildup in tankless water heaters. Our residential flushing service in Fresno involves circulating a descaling solution through your unit's heat exchanger to remove calcium and magnesium deposits. This process restores efficiency, prevents overheating and extends the life of your appliance, so your family gets continuous hot water without interruption.

    Rinnai Tankless Heater Servicing in Fresno

    Rinnai units are popular for their efficiency and compact design.     Noritz Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Fresno, CA

    Noritz tankless heaters are known for their solid build.     Commercial Tankless System Checks in Fresno, CA

    Error Code Diagnostics in Fresno, CA

    Codes like Rinnai LC, Navien E003 or Noritz 90 tell us a lot about what is going wrong. We read the code history, test flame rods, flow sensors and igniters and check gas pressure at the inlet.
