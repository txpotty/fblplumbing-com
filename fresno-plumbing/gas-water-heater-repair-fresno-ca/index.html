    Pilot Light Repair in Fresno

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most common gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and service or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are finished in about an hour.

    While gas water heaters are sturdy, tank leaks or pressure problems can occur. We can often fix small fitting leaks or replace relief valves if pressure is too high.     Gas Water Heater Noise Troubleshooting in Fresno

    Strange noises coming from your gas water heater, such as rumbling, popping or whistling, often indicate sediment buildup in the tank or a burner problem.
