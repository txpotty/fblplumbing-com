    PEX and HDPE Water Line Installation in Fresno, CA

    PEX and HDPE are flexible, which means fewer fittings in the ground and better tolerance for soil movement. HDPE is especially durable for long runs and holds up against cracking in expansive clay. We use approved compression or fusion fittings and add tracer wire so the line can be found with a locator later. PEX must stay out of sunlight before burial, so we store and lay it with care on every job site.

    Upsizing Water Lines for Better Pressure in Fresno, CA

    Many homes built decades ago have a 3/4 inch line that cannot keep up with modern fixture loads, irrigation and pools. If your pressure drops when two showers run or the sprinklers are on, an undersized line may be the cause. We test static and flowing pressure, then calculate whether a larger line will make a real difference. Upsizing is frequently paired with a new pressure reducing valve and main shutoff.

    In most communities the homeowner owns the line from the meter to the home, while the utility owns the meter and the line on the street side. We confirm responsibility with your provider before work begins.

    Common signs include persistently low water pressure throughout your home, discolored or rusty water from your taps, frequent leaks in your yard or near the foundation, or unusually high water bills without a clear cause. These usually indicate an aging or failing main water line.

    Type K copper, PEX and HDPE are all accepted choices. HDPE and PEX flex with soil movement and have fewer joints, while copper offers rigidity and puncture resistance.

    PEX is flexible, less expensive, resistant to freezing and corrosion, and has fewer fittings, which means fewer possible leak points. Copper is rigid, more expensive, has a proven track record, is naturally antimicrobial and can withstand higher temperatures.
