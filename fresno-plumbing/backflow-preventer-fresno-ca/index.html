    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Service in Fresno, CA

    RPZ assemblies protect against high-hazard cross-connections at restaurants, medical offices, car washes and buildings with chemical feed. Two independent checks sit on either side of a relief valve, which opens and discharges water when a check fails. Plan a place for the water. We install, test and rebuild RPZ units from Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.
