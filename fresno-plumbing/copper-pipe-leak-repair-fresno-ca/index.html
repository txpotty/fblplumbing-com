    Copper Slab Leak Repair in Fresno

    Many slab homes have copper water lines running under the concrete. Listen for running water. When those lines leak, you may notice warm spots on the floor, the sound of running water or a meter that turns with every fixture off, and we locate the leak electronically before choosing a spot repair through the slab or a reroute. Both get a price.

    Corrosion-Related Leak Repair in Fresno

    Corrosion thins copper from the inside. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. Look for blue-green spots. On exposed pipe you may see blue-green deposits at fittings, while hidden pipe usually announces itself with a small leak or a stain. We replace the corroded length. Then we talk about the cause.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Fresno

    Pinholes start inside the pipe where the copper wall pits and thins. You see a drip or a stain. They are small and easy to miss behind drywall, which is why we use thermal imaging to find them before we open anything. We replace the pitted length with Type L. Nearby pipe gets checked.

    Under Slab Copper Leak Repair in Fresno, CA

    Copper lines under a slab can wear through where they rub concrete or gravel. Warm floors are a clue. We isolate hot and cold lines with pressure tests and locate the leak with acoustic and thermal equipment, then choose between a small slab cut at the leak and rerouting the line through the walls or attic. We quote each way.

    Copper Leak Behind Walls in Fresno, CA

    Small pinholes can suddenly widen and spray water into a wall or ceiling. Find the main valve. We help you shut off the water over the phone and send a plumber to locate and repair the leak, with a temporary repair first when the permanent fix needs more access. We tell you about wet drywall.

    Thin-Wall Copper Pinhole Repair in Fresno

    Older copper, especially Type M, pinholes more than heavier pipe. Thin walls wear through first. We locate the leak precisely and either solder in a new section or use a press fitting near framing, with new pipe that is heavier than the original. We check the rest of the run.

    Copper Pipe Joint Leak Repair in Fresno, CA

    Joints leak from poor original soldering, thermal expansion or a house that settles. Expansive clay soils are common across Northern California, and seasonal swelling and shrinking can shift slabs and buried sewer laterals. A crusty fitting is the clue. We inspect each joint near the leak for weeping or mineral buildup, then clean, reflux and resolder the joint, or replace the fitting entirely with a new sweat or press fitting. Every joint gets tested.

    Copper Repair vs Repipe Cost in Fresno, CA

    A single copper leak repair is affordable and fast. Repeated leaks change the math. If you have had two or more pinhole leaks in a year, the copper is likely thinning throughout the system, so repiping often beats another round of spot fixes on total cost. You get both numbers.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Fresno

    Accessible copper leak repairs typically cost $250 to $650. Hidden pipe runs higher. Repairs inside finished walls or ceilings usually range from $500 to $1,500 before drywall patching, while slab work on copper tends to land between $1,500 and $4,000. We write it up before we start.

    Keep pressure in range. Test it yearly. Soften hard water, limit recirculation pump run time and get any green crust on fittings looked at early, while homes with older copper should have it checked every few years. Small fixes now save drywall later.

    All standard copper used in homes and businesses. Type M, L and K. Our repairs use copper and fittings from established manufacturers that meet ASTM and NSF standards, with every part listed for drinking water. We skip cheap parts.

    We typically use Type L copper. It is heavier than Type M. Type L lasts longer than the Type M found in many older homes, while press fittings or a PEX transition get used when a torch is a bad idea. Every part is lead-free.

    Insurance often covers sudden water damage. Worn pipe is usually excluded. The cost of repairing old or corroded copper is usually yours, but the resulting damage to the house may be claimable. Check with your agent. We provide documentation.

    The pipe wall wears through from the inside. Water chemistry drives it. Aggressive water, high velocity, excess pressure, poor original soldering and stray electrical current from grounding issues each thin the wall until it pins through. Hot water speeds it up.

    A repair clamp or epoxy can buy time in an emergency. It will not last. A plumber should cut out the damaged section and install proper fittings, particularly when the leak is somewhere you cannot see. Shut the water off first.
