    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Fresno

    A simple clog clearing starts around $150. Main line work costs more. Complex main line repairs can run past $3,000, depending on the drain involved, the extent of the damage and the access. We price it after we look.

    Clearing a simple clog takes an hour or two. Repairs run longer. Replacing a section of main line or opening a wall to swap a cast iron stack can take a full day or more, depending on access and whether a permit inspection is needed. We give you a timeline with the price.

    Hydro-jetting scours the pipe with water at several thousand psi. The whole inside gets cleaned. A snake only punches a hole through a clog, but a jet nozzle strips buildup off the entire pipe wall. Fragile pipe gets checked before jetting.

    Call us if a plunger or a small hand snake has not cleared it. Same goes for repeat clogs. Multiple slow drains, sewer smell or water coming up in a different fixture than the one you are using usually mean the problem is deeper in the line than a hand tool can reach. Those call for professional equipment.

    Age and corrosion lead the list. Soil movement is close behind. Tree roots, temperature swings, heavy loads over buried pipe, and poor original work like unprimed glue joints or missing support straps all cause early failure. It is usually a combination.

    We install PVC and ABS for most new drain work. Some jobs call for cast iron. Cast iron still makes sense in some multi-story walls where noise matters, and we tie new pipe into existing metal drains with listed transition fittings. All materials are code-listed.

    Keep grease out of the kitchen sink. Strainers in the tub help. Flush only toilet paper, run hot water after greasy dishes, and schedule a professional cleaning every year or two if your home is older or has a history of clogs. Scale-prone lines benefit from yearly cleaning.

    A dry trap is the most common cause. Unused fixtures dry out. A cracked drain fitting, a failed wax ring under the toilet or a blocked vent all leak gas too, and a smoke test usually finds the source in minutes. Then we seal it.
