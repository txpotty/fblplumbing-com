    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Fresno, CA

    Strong storms can knock out power right when your pump is needed most. Battery backup pumps take over automatically when the main pump fails or power goes out. We install backup systems with sealed batteries and charging units. Many include alarms that warn you about problems. Backups add an extra layer of protection for finished basements.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Fresno

    Some homes collect water in sunken patios, below-grade driveways or window wells rather than a basement. We install exterior sump basins with pumps that move storm water to a safe discharge point. These systems are helpful for lots that slope toward the house. We route discharge lines so water drains away from the foundation and neighbors. This helps prevent siding damage and foundation problems.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Fresno, CA

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and repair. They are often a good choice for shallow sump pits or areas where space is tight.
