    Sump Basin and Discharge Line Installation in Fresno

    The basin sets the ceiling on what the pump can do. We place it at the lowest point of the floor, bed it in washed stone, and run the discharge line with a slight pitch to the exit point so nothing sits in the pipe in Fresno. Nothing should drain back toward the wall.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Fresno

    Outages and heavy rain tend to show up together. Without house power, a battery backup keeps a second pump going, and a fully charged 75 amp-hour battery can run a typical backup pump for several hours of on-and-off cycling, longer on a light night. We load test the battery.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Fresno

    Rigid PVC carries the water from the pump out through the rim joist and at least 10 feet from the house.
