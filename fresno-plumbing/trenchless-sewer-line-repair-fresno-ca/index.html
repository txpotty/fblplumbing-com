    Sectional Point Repairs in Fresno

    A point repair is a short liner, often 3 to 5 feet long, set over one bad spot. We push it in on a packer, guided by the camera footage, and the packer inflates to press the resin-soaked sleeve against the pipe wall until it cures. One crack, one patch. Sound lines with one bad joint are the right candidates.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Fresno

    Prep is most of the job. We jet the line at high pressure to strip roots, grease and loose scale, then camera it again to measure the exact footage before cutting the liner tube to length. Skip that step and the liner can wrinkle or fail to bond. Lining works best on cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their round shape.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Replacement in Fresno

    Orangeburg is bituminous fiber pipe that was laid in houses mostly from the late 1940s into the early 1970s. It goes oval and blisters, so a liner has nothing round to bond to and bursting is usually the better fix. Clay is different. Clay that still holds its round shape is a good candidate for a liner.

    Sewer Line Video Inspections in Fresno

    We record the full camera run on every job, from the cleanout out to the city main or the septic tank. You get the file. On the footage you can see what we see, roots at a joint at 22 feet and an offset at 38 feet where the pipe dropped. Those footage marks tell us where to line and where a point repair will do.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Fresno, CA

    Cast iron rusts from the inside out, and the bottom of the pipe goes first. That is called channel rot. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or carbide cutters spinning on a flexible shaft, which brings the wall back to bare metal so a liner can bond to it. Under a slab, lining beats breaking up the floor in two rooms.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Pricing in Fresno, CA

    Compare total cost, not just the plumbing line on the invoice. An open-trench replacement can look cheaper on paper, until you add saw-cutting the driveway and pouring new concrete over a trench that may run 50 feet. Most of those extra costs never show up with a liner or a burst. Sod and sprinkler repair add up too.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Fresno

    Bursting can upsize the line. We can pull in 6-inch HDPE behind a head that bursts an old 4-inch clay pipe, which helps on lines that clog because they were undersized from the start or have been added onto with extra bathrooms. The pit at each end is small. No trench in between.
