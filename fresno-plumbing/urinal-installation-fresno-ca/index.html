    Urinal Replacement and Upgrades in Fresno

    Cracked or stained urinals are worth replacing. Cracked china leaks eventually and heavy scale rarely comes clean, so a new bowl on the old carrier is usually best. We check the carrier bolts first. Rusted bolts get replaced.

    Waterless Urinal Upgrades in Fresno

    Switching to waterless is mostly a fixture swap. We remove the flush valve, cap the supply and set the waterless bowl on the same carrier. Some codes want the supply left in place. That allows a future change back.

    American Standard and Kohler Urinal Installation in Fresno

    American Standard and Kohler make the china we install most. Models like the Washbrook and the Bardon come in versions from 0.125 to 1.0 gallons per flush, and each one needs a flush valve matched to that rating or the bowl will either splash or not rinse clean. The spec sheet makes matching easy. We order both together.

    Waterless Urinal Installation in Fresno

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. A cartridge or sealing liquid blocks sewer gas while letting urine through, and it must be replaced on schedule. Your custodial team needs training. We show them how.

    Urinal Rough-In for New Restrooms in Fresno

    Tenant improvements need rough-in before the walls close.     Commercial Urinal Installation in Fresno

    Offices and restaurants want urinals that survive heavy use.     Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Fresno, CA

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. A flushometer from Sloan or Zurn delivers a measured volume from a 3/4 inch supply with no tank, and it recovers instantly for the next user, which matters in a stadium or school restroom. We set the volume to the bowl. Wrong settings stain bowls.
