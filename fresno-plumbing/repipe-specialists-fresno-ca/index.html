    PEX Versus Copper Repipe Pricing in Fresno, CA

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper because the material is cheaper and installation goes faster. Copper costs more.     Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Fresno, CA

    A whole-home PEX repipe for an average house often runs $5,000 to $12,000, with copper costing more. One slab leak with access and repair often costs $1,000 to $4,000. The numbers add up quickly.     Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Fresno, CA

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly. Rust-colored water in the morning, poor pressure upstairs and dripping threaded joints all point to failing galvanized. New PEX or copper takes its place. Owners usually notice stronger flow the first day.

    Drain Line Repiping in Fresno

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Repeat clogs, sewer smells and several slow drains at once often mean the drain pipe itself is failing. We camera the lines first. Failing sections are replaced with PVC or ABS sloped and vented to code, which drains faster and resists buildup much better than the pipe it replaces.

    Drywall Patching and Restoration in Fresno, CA

    Repiping needs small openings in walls and ceilings to reach fixtures and pull pipe. Our crew cuts neat rectangles, covers nearby surfaces and patches each opening after the pressure test. We match the texture. We match knockdown, orange peel or smooth walls, so the patches are ready to prime and paint.
