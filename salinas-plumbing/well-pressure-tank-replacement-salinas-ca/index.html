    Pressure Tank Replacement Choices in Salinas, CA

    Pressure Tanks for Well Houses and Barns in Salinas, CA

    Tanks in outbuildings face heat, cold and damp. Steel rusts fast in a damp well house. We often recommend a composite tank in those spaces, raise it off the floor on a stand, while protecting the switch and pipes from freezing. Simple protection.

    Amtrol Pressure Tank Replacement in Salinas

    Replacing an Amtrol tank with another Amtrol is often straightforward. Fittings tend to line up. Even so, we confirm the model is still the right size for your pump, because the old tank may have been too small all along. Check before swapping.

    Large Capacity Tanks for Ranch and Commercial Wells in Salinas

    Big properties need big drawdown. Livestock and irrigation draw a lot. We install large capacity tanks or banks of tanks on a manifold, sizing them so the pump runs long enough each cycle even under heavy demand. Floor space matters.

    Diaphragm Pressure Tank Replacement in Salinas, CA

    Diaphragm tanks use a flexible sheet clamped inside the shell. Air sits above it, water below. When it fails, symptoms match a bladder tank, with short cycling, pressure swings and water spitting from the air valve. Replacement is the usual answer.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Salinas, CA

    Switch contacts pit and burn over time. A pitted switch can stick on or chatter. When we swap a tank we look at the contacts and the sensing port underneath, since a new tank paired with a tired switch is asking for trouble. Cheap part.

    Cost-Effective Well Pressure Tank Upgrades in Salinas, CA

    Upgrading a size often costs less than people think. Going one size larger adds only a little. A larger tank means fewer pump starts per day, which is the cheapest way to extend the life of your pump, so we often recommend it during a replacement.

    Residential Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Salinas

    Most homes on a well have a tank between 20 and 86 gallons sitting near the pressure switch. We shut off power to the pump first. Then we drain the system, unthread the old tank from the tank tee, set the pre-charge on the new one, and connect it with new fittings and fresh sealant. Usually a morning.

    Well Pressure Tank Leak Repair vs. Replacement in Salinas

    Leaks at the tank tee, a gauge or a fitting can be fixed. Once the shell leaks, it is done. Bladder tanks with a fixed bladder cannot be rebuilt, and a pinhole in the shell means replacement, while a leaking tee or relief valve is a simple part swap we can usually do in under an hour.
