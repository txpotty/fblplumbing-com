    Pressure Tank Sizing and Upsizing in Salinas

    Many older systems have a tank that is too small for the pump, so the motor starts every time someone washes their hands. Tank size is based on drawdown, which is the available water stored between cut-out and cut-in pressure. For example, a 10 gallon per minute pump usually needs at least 10 gallons of drawdown for a one minute run time. That often means an 80 to 119 gallon tank rather than the 20 gallon unit that came with the home. Upsizing is one of the best ways to extend pump life.

    A well pressure tank replacement often involves inspecting and, if necessary, replacing the associated pressure switch and pressure gauge.     A constant pressure system pairs a variable speed drive like the Franklin SubDrive or Grundfos SQE with a smaller pressure tank.     Pressure Switch Replacement in Salinas, CA

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop, typically at 30/50 or 40/60 psi. Pitted contacts, insect nests and a clogged sensing nipple are common causes of failure in well houses. We replace worn switches with Square D or similar models and clean or replace the nipple below it. A low-pressure cutoff switch can also be added to stop the pump if the well runs dry.     When your well pressure tank shows signs of trouble, it is important to determine if a repair is feasible or if full replacement is necessary.
