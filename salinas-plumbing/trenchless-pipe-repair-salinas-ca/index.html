    Perma-Liner Trenchless Systems in Salinas

    Perma-Liner is one of the CIPP systems our crews install. It's built for sewer use. The liners and resins are structural, meaning the cured pipe can stand on its own even if the old pipe around it keeps deteriorating, which is why we trust them for local lines. They handle harsh soil and flow.

    Pipe Bursting in Salinas, CA

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. You'll see an entry and exit pit. A bursting head is pulled through, fracturing the old pipe outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind, and that HDPE is fused into one continuous length with no joints at all.

    Emergency Trenchless Pipe Repair in Salinas, CA

    A collapsed sewer line stops every drain in the house. It can't wait.     Perma-Liner and Picote Solutions in Salinas

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Both matter. Picote milling heads clear roots, scale and old fittings out of the pipe before lining, and after the liner cures, they cut open the lateral connections so every drain in your home flows again. Good prep makes a good liner.

    CIPP Lining for Clay and Cast Iron in Salinas

    Older Salinas homes often have clay or cast iron sewer pipe. Both fail with age. Clay joints let roots in and cast iron scales and rusts through, and a liner seals both problems with a new jointless pipe that often outlasts the original material by decades. Only one or two access points are needed.
