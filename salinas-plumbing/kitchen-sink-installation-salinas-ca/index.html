    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in Salinas, CA: Style by Style

    Farmhouse Sink Plumbing in Salinas, CA

    Fireclay and cast iron farmhouse sinks can weigh well over 100 pounds empty, before a single drop of water goes in. A support frame goes into the cabinet and the sink is leveled before the countertop goes on. Deep bowls need a lower trap. We plan the drain for that depth from the start.

    Farmhouse Sink Installation in Salinas, CA

    Apron-front sinks rest on a support frame built into the cabinet, not on the countertop. We build that frame from 2x4s or a steel bracket, set the sink dead level and leave the right reveal for the stone shop. We plan the drain early. Many farmhouse sinks drain at the back, and the trap has to work around that.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Salinas

    Both bowls of a double sink share one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. The layout depends on your disposal and dishwasher, because the disposal side changes where the tee sits and how much slope each bowl gets. Heavy-wall fittings resist leaks.

    Restaurant Sink Installation in Salinas

    Restaurants, cafes and commissary kitchens in Salinas get their commercial sinks installed by FBL Plumbing. Hand sinks need tempered or hot and cold water.     Undermount Sink Installation in Salinas, CA

    Undermounts hang from clips anchored into the underside of the stone, plus a bead of silicone along the rim. We support the bowl from below with a brace while the silicone cures, typically for a full day, before loading it with water. Skipping the cure time leads to sagging. Just an eighth of an inch of sag leaves a gap that catches water and food along the edge.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Salinas, CA

    Stone shops vary on how they mount undermounts. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. The bowl is braced regardless. The trap does not get connected until the adhesive cures.

    New Construction Sink Rough-In in Salinas, CA

    The rough-in decides where the drain and valves end up long before the cabinets arrive. We usually set the kitchen drain stub around 16 to 20 inches off the floor, then adjust for deep bowls or a disposal so the trap does not end up below the wall outlet. Vents go in now. Having the sink and faucet specs early saves moving pipe later.

    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Salinas

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and the lower the number, the thicker the steel. Thin 22 gauge bowls flex and drum when the disposal runs, so we add sound pads or suggest an 18 gauge or heavier sink if you have not bought one yet. Top-mount and undermount plumb differently. We connect the strainers, faucet and disposal either way.
