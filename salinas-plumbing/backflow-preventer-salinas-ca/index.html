    Testing in Salinas, CA usually costs between $75 and $150 per assembly. The price includes the trip, a calibrated test and sending the report to your provider.     You will usually get a notice from the Salinas water department with a repair deadline. Most of the time we find the failed part, rebuild it and retest during the same trip, then send in the passing report. Do not let it lapse.

    Installation and repair should be left to a licensed plumber who holds tester certification. Installing the wrong device, mounting a PVB too low or burying an RPZ in a pit can each fail inspection and leave the water unprotected. Providers can fine for it.

    New installations and replacements often require a plumbing permit and an initial test. Requirements vary by city and water provider. We pull permits and handle the initial test, so the paperwork is done before the assembly goes into regular service.

    A brass assembly often lasts 10 to 20 years, sometimes longer. The rubber parts inside wear out much sooner, which is why rebuild kits exist and why most failed tests are fixed without replacing the body. Freezes shorten that life.

    Ten to twenty years is common for a well-kept brass assembly. How long it lasts depends on the water, the weather it sits in and the kind of device. Freezing is uncommon along the coast and on valley floors in Northern California, but frosty nights do happen and can crack unprotected outdoor pipes. Yearly testing extends its life.
