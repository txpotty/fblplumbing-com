    Efficient PEX Repiping in Salinas, CA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and very popular choice for repiping projects in Salinas, CA. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also very resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use high-quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a dependable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Salinas

    Kitec, with its orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe and brass fittings, and gray polybutylene pipe are both known for failures. Many homes built in the 1980s through early 2000s contain one or the other. We replace these systems completely so your home is easier to insure and sell. Visible pipe at the water heater often tells us what you have.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Salinas

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to three days.

    Drain Line Repiping in Salinas

    While often overlooked, your home's drain lines are just as important as your supply lines. Old cast iron or corroded drain pipes can lead to frequent clogs, slow drainage, and even sewage backups. FBL Plumbing provides comprehensive drain line repiping in Salinas, replacing failing waste pipes with new, smooth PVC or ABS lines. This ensures efficient waste removal and prevents future drainage problems, protecting your home from foul odors and unsanitary conditions.

    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Salinas

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Expansive clay soils are common across Northern California, and seasonal swelling and shrinking can crack slabs and buried sewer laterals. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.
