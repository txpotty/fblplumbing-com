    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Salinas

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the distinct advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, typically using crimp or compression fittings. Our experts in Salinas can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Salinas

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more often follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and lowers the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Salinas, CA

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, similar to a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when multiple fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to weigh cost and performance.
