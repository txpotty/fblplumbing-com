    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Salinas

    Residential expansion tank installs usually cost $250 to $550 with the tank and labor. Add an attic install, a bigger tank or a new PRV and it can reach $600 to $1,000.

    It looks simple, but the job involves pressurized lines, correct sizing and setting the precharge. Getting the charge wrong shortens the tank's life, and a tank hung on the pipe without support can crack a fitting, so we recommend a licensed plumber for a clean, code-compliant install. Mistakes cause leaks.

    Heated water expands. With a closed system, the extra water has no escape, so pressure climbs every cycle and wears out heater components and relief valves, sometimes causing leaks, while the expansion tank gives that water an air cushion to push against instead. The air side compresses.

    Closed plumbing systems need somewhere to put expanded hot water. A missing tank lets thermal expansion damage the heater and fixtures, leading to leaks and early failure, and plenty of local codes in Northern California require a tank for that reason. The tank itself is small.
