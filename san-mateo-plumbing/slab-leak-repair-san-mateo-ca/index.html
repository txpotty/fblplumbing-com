    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in San Mateo

    Cold water slab leaks are harder to feel but still waste water and wet the soil. They often show up as a high water bill or a meter that moves with all fixtures off. We isolate the cold line, find the leak and repair or reroute it. Fixing cold leaks promptly helps protect your foundation.

    Direct Slab Penetration Repair in San Mateo, CA

    When a slab leak is directly accessible and localized, direct slab penetration is often the most efficient repair method. This involves carefully cutting a small section of your concrete slab to access and repair or replace the damaged pipe section. Our team makes sure the repair is fully compliant with Northern California plumbing codes and the slab is properly restored, preventing future issues. We take great care to contain dust and debris during this process in your San Mateo home.

    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in San Mateo, CA

    If multiple slab leaks point to a systemic issue with your home's original plumbing, a full PEX repipe might be the most cost-effective and durable solution. PEX tubing is flexible, corrosion-resistant, and less prone to leaks caused by foundation shifts, making it a strong choice for many homes. We can replace all the water supply lines in your San Mateo home, providing a brand-new, leak-free plumbing system that will last for decades.

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in San Mateo, CA

    In some cases, especially when the leak is under a load-bearing wall or in a hard-to-reach area, tunneling beneath the foundation is the preferred method. This allows our plumbers to reach the damaged pipe from outside your home, avoiding disruption to your interior floors and finishes. Tunneling spares the interior but calls for skilled excavation and shoring to protect structural integrity throughout the repair process in San Mateo, CA.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in San Mateo

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution. This involves abandoning the leaking line under the slab and installing a new pipe through the walls or attic. This method bypasses the problem area entirely, providing a fresh, durable plumbing line. It is a common solution for aging homes in San Mateo with original copper or galvanized plumbing.

    Slab Leak Repair for PEX and Copper Lines in San Mateo, CA

    Whether your home in San Mateo, CA has modern PEX piping or traditional copper lines, our plumbers are proficient in repairing both. Pinhole leaks are common in older copper due to corrosive soil conditions and water chemistry, while PEX can sometimes suffer from installation issues or rodent damage. We carry a full range of parts and tools for both materials, ensuring a correct and durable repair no matter your pipe type.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in San Mateo, CA

    When the leak is isolated and the rest of the pipe is in good shape, a spot repair through the slab is a practical option. We cut a small opening, excavate to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The trench is compacted and the concrete is patched. Flooring is then restored by your preferred contractor.
