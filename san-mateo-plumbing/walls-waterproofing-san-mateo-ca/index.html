It's an effective way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Walls Waterproofing in San Mateo

    The cost of walls waterproofing in San Mateo, CA can vary widely, typically ranging from $3,000 for interior crack sealing to $15,000 or more for extensive exterior foundation waterproofing with drainage systems. Factors like the size of the area, severity of water damage, chosen methods, and accessibility all influence the final price. We provide detailed, transparent quotes after a thorough inspection.

    Look for bubbling paint, soft drywall, stained baseboards and musty odors. White crust on block or stucco walls is another sign of moisture moving through. Loose tiles or cracked grout in a shower also suggest water is getting behind.

    For extensive walls waterproofing projects involving excavation, structural changes, or major changes to drainage systems, a permit from the city of San Mateo may be required. Simple crack repairs typically do not need a permit. FBL Plumbing will help you determine if a permit is necessary for your project and assist with the application process if needed.

    When professionally installed using high-quality materials, walls waterproofing can last for decades, often 20 to 50 years or even longer. The lifespan depends on the materials used, the quality of installation, and environmental factors like soil movement and water exposure. Modern membranes and sealants are designed for long-term durability.

    Shower remodels that change plumbing or drain locations often require a permit. Simple membrane replacement may not. We check local requirements and obtain permits when needed.

    Interior waterproofing addresses water after it has entered the foundation, usually managing it with sealants and drainage systems inside the basement or crawl space. Exterior waterproofing prevents water from reaching the foundation walls in the first place, usually by excavating and applying membranes or coatings to the outside of the foundation.

    The duration of a walls waterproofing project varies a lot. Smaller jobs like interior crack sealing might take a day or two, while extensive exterior waterproofing with excavation and drainage system installation could take several days to over a week. We'll provide a clear timeline during your consultation.

    Damp proofing is a less durable solution, typically a spray-on asphalt coating that resists soil moisture but isn't designed to withstand hydrostatic pressure. Waterproofing uses stronger membranes, coatings, or drainage systems specifically engineered to prevent liquid water from entering, even under pressure. For basements or crawl spaces in areas with high water tables or heavy rainfall, waterproofing is the right choice.
