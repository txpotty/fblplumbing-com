    Frequently Asked Questions About Bathtub Faucet Replacement in San Mateo

    Replacing a spout or trim kit typically costs $150 to $350. A cartridge or stem swap runs $175 to $400, and a full in-wall valve replacement typically ranges from $450 to $1,200 depending on access and tile.

    To prolong the life of your new faucet, regularly clean it with non-abrasive cleaners to prevent mineral buildup, avoid overtightening handles, and consider installing a whole-home water softener if you have very hard water. Quickly address any small leaks to prevent them from escalating.

    Spout and handle swaps are reasonable DIY projects. In-wall valve replacement involves soldering or crimping and is best done by a plumber.

    Spout and trim swaps take about an hour. Cartridge replacement takes one to two hours, and a full valve replacement may take between three and six hours.

    We install Moen, Delta, Kohler, American Standard, Pfister, Grohe and Hansgrohe. We can also find parts for many older brands.

    There are several types, including single-handle, double-handle, Roman tub fillers, and wall-mounted faucets. Each offers different style and functional benefits. We can help you choose the best type for your specific bathtub and bathroom design.

    A new faucet might improve perceived water pressure if your old faucet was clogged with mineral buildup or had a restricted aerator. However, it won't fix underlying low water pressure issues coming from your home's main water supply or plumbing system.
