    Fixing Showers in San Mateo, CA

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in San Mateo

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Grit and scale scratch the sealing surfaces, the O-rings flatten and harden, and eventually the valve cannot fully stop the water when the handle is closed. A new cartridge fixes it. We use genuine parts and coat the new one with silicone grease so the handle turns smoothly.

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in San Mateo

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck. The spool should shift to keep the mix steady when one side loses pressure, but scale can lock it in one spot. Replacing the cartridge solves it. Water that never gets hot enough may only need the limit stop turned.

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in San Mateo

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. A leak at the arm threads follows the pipe into the wall, and it shows up as bubbling paint in the next room. Fresh thread tape fixes it. We can add a handheld or slide bar while we are there.

    Mixing Valve Repair in San Mateo

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. Over time the cartridge or spool sticks, and you get a shower that will not stay at one temperature or a handle that turns past where it should before the water warms up. We replace the cartridge. Valves with no parts left get a replacement plan and an honest look at the tile.

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in San Mateo

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year. Single-handle showers drip from a worn cartridge or O-rings, while old two-handle valves drip from a washer and a pitted seat. The parts are on the truck. Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister cartridges cover most of the valves we open, so the repair is usually done that same visit.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in San Mateo

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. If the head drips with the valve closed, water is slipping past the cartridge, and a new head will not change that. The fix is in the valve. Leaks at the arm threads are different, and those get new thread tape and a snug turn.

    Emergency Shower Repair in San Mateo, CA

    A shower that will not shut off is an emergency. Shut the main, or use the shower's integral stops if your valve has them behind the trim or an access panel. Next, call us. FBL Plumbing sends a plumber with cartridges on the truck, and we can often get your water back on within the hour.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in San Mateo, CA

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. That points to the head, the flow restrictor or the cartridge, not the main or the water heater, and it is usually something we can fix within an hour. The head comes off first. If every fixture is weak, we check the regulator and the main.
