    Offices and restaurants want urinals that survive heavy use. Each unit goes on a carrier with new gaskets and stainless bolts, paired with a matched Sloan or Zurn valve. Partitions get spaced to code. Most codes want 15 inches from centerline to the side.

    Flushometer Urinal Installation in San Mateo

    Manual flushometers are still the most common setup. Manual valves go on a 3/4 inch line with the control stop tuned and the vacuum breaker at the correct height. Handle side follows the layout. ADA units need the handle within reach.

    Sensor Urinal Installation in San Mateo, CA

    Sensor valves stop missed flushes and double flushes. Battery or hardwired valves get a tuned sensing range and an optional sanitary flush for idle periods. Expect years between battery changes. Hardwired units need an electrician first.
