    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Line Installation in San Mateo

    Most residential water line installations run $1,800 to $5,500 depending on length, soil and material. Long runs through rocky ground, trenchless boring or copper pipe can raise costs to $6,000 to $10,000 or more.

    Most residential main water line installations or replacements in San Mateo can be finished within one to three days. This timeline can vary based on the length of the line, the complexity of the excavation, weather and permit processing times.

    PEX is flexible, less expensive, resistant to freezing and corrosion, and has fewer fittings, which means fewer possible leak points. Copper is rigid, more expensive, has a proven track record, is naturally antimicrobial and can withstand higher temperatures. Both are solid choices, and the best option depends on your budget, property needs and local water conditions in San Mateo.

    Installing a main water line is a complex task that involves excavation, knowledge of plumbing codes, precise connections and permits. It is strongly recommended to hire a licensed professional like FBL Plumbing for water line installation in San Mateo, CA, for safety, compliance and long-term reliability.     FBL Plumbing uses high-quality, industry-leading brands for our water line installations to deliver durability and reliability. For PEX, we often use Uponor, Viega or Zurn systems. For copper, we source from reputable manufacturers that follow ASTM standards. We select materials best suited to your project and site in San Mateo.

    A typical residential water line takes one to two days, plus time for utility locates and inspection. Rocky ground or long runs can add a day or two.

    In most communities the homeowner owns the line from the meter to the home, while the utility owns the meter and the line on the street side. We verify responsibility with your provider before work begins.
