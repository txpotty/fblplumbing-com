    Sewer Repair Methods for San Mateo, CA Homes

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in San Mateo

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. A fully collapsed section is one example. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. Older homes often need this on at least one section. We stage the work so you lose sewer service for a few hours, not days.

    Sewer Repair for Commercial Properties in San Mateo, CA

    Commercial laterals run six inches or larger and carry far more flow than a house. Downtime costs money. For restaurants, strip centers and apartment buildings in San Mateo we schedule repairs after hours where we can, set up temporary bypass pumping when the line has to stay in service, and give managers a clear window to share with tenants. We repair a lot of grease-clogged outlet lines.

    Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in San Mateo, CA

    A spot repair is the cheapest fix when the rest of the line is sound. Lining costs more per foot. It avoids excavation, though, and on long runs under driveways or patios the savings on concrete and landscaping often outweigh the higher price of the liner itself. Full replacement is right for Orangeburg or a line broken in several places. We price all three so you can compare.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in San Mateo

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. A leaking joint invited them in. Warm, wet, nutrient-rich air escaping from a loose joint draws roots toward the line, and once they get in they thicken until they crack the pipe or separate the joint completely. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. A liner or new pipe at that joint is what actually stops regrowth.

    Restaurant and Retail Sewer Repair in San Mateo

    Restaurants, retail centers and multifamily buildings load a lateral far beyond a single house. Grease is the usual culprit. We jet the line, camera it, and repair the weak section with options that limit how long you are closed, including after-hours digs and bypass pumping when the business has to stay open. A two-way cleanout usually goes in at the same time.

    Root Intrusion Repair in San Mateo, CA

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. The joint is the real problem. After clearing the line, we look at each rooted joint on camera and decide between a short sectional liner, a full liner or cutting in new pipe, depending on how many joints are affected and how far apart they sit. One or two joints is a small job. A line rooted end to end is a lining or replacement job.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection & Repair in San Mateo, CA

    Sewer smell outside the house points to a leak. A lush stripe over the line is another clue. We confirm it on camera, look for cracks, open joints or a broken cleanout riser, and mark the location so the repair stays small. A small crack can sometimes be fixed with a short sectional liner, with no digging at all. Larger breaks get cut out and replaced.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in San Mateo, CA

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Two access holes, sometimes one. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one and splits the old pipe outward, while cured-in-place lining pushes a resin-soaked felt tube into the old pipe and hardens it into a new wall. Either way, the driveway and the trees stay put. We tell you which one your pipe can take.
