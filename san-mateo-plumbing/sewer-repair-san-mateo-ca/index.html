    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in San Mateo, CA

    For many sewer line issues, especially those caused by tree roots or pipe corrosion, trenchless repair offers a less invasive solution. This method, which includes pipe bursting or pipe lining, requires minimal excavation, saving your yard and landscaping from extensive disruption. We use specialized equipment to either pull a new pipe through the old one or insert a resin-coated liner that cures in place, creating a new, durable pipe inside the old one. It's an effective and popular choice for preserving property appearance in San Mateo.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Repair in San Mateo

    The cost of sewer repair in San Mateo, CA can vary widely, typically ranging from $300 for a simple clog removal to $6,000 or more for extensive line replacement using trenchless methods. Factors include the extent of damage, pipe material, accessibility and whether excavation is needed. We provide a detailed estimate after a full inspection.

    Most spot repairs are completed in one day, including excavation and backfill. Larger replacements or jobs that need a city right-of-way permit can take two to four days. We give you a timeline before we begin.

    Sewer repair is complex and best left to licensed professionals due to the specialized equipment required, potential health hazards and strict local plumbing codes. Attempting DIY repairs can lead to further damage, costly mistakes and even safety risks. Always talk to a professional plumber.

    A small rental auger can clear a soft clog, but it will not fix a crack, belly or root intrusion. Forcing a cable through a broken pipe can make the damage worse. A camera inspection tells you whether you have a clog or a structural problem.

    Key signs you need sewer repair include multiple slow drains or frequent backups, gurgling noises from toilets or drains, foul sewer odors in or around your home, soggy spots in your yard or an unexplained increase in your water bill. If you notice any of these, it's smart to call for an inspection.

    Standard policies frequently exclude wear, tear and root damage to the sewer line itself, though they may cover resulting water damage inside the home. Some insurers offer a service line endorsement. We provide video and written documentation if you file a claim.

    FBL Plumbing stands behind our work with comprehensive warranties on parts and labor for sewer repair services. The warranty period can vary based on the type of repair and materials used, but we always make sure our customers have lasting peace of mind. Details will be provided with your estimate.
