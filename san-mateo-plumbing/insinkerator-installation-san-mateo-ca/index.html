    Hard Water and Disposals in San Mateo

    Rural properties around San Mateo often rely on private wells, which may need filtration for iron, sediment, or other minerals. Scale builds up on grind rings and drain tailpieces over the years. Scale makes a disposal noisy. It also drains slower, because mineral crust narrows the tailpiece and the trap, so we look inside the old drain parts before we connect anything new to them. If they are crusted badly, we swap them while we are there.

    Upgrading Your InSinkErator in San Mateo, CA

    Moving up from a 1/3 HP builder unit to a 3/4 HP Evolution changes more than the grind. The new unit is taller. That extra height can put the discharge outlet below the trap arm, so we measure the drop before install day and lower the trap or the arm if the numbers do not work. The circuit gets checked for the bigger motor's draw too.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in San Mateo

    An air switch puts a push button on the countertop or sink deck instead of a wall switch. The button pushes a puff of air down a thin tube. A control box under the sink reads that pulse and flips the power, keeping live wiring well away from the water. If there is a spare faucet hole, we use it, otherwise we drill.

    Disposal Installs for New Sinks in San Mateo, CA

    A new sink changes the drain math. Undermount and farmhouse bowls are often deeper than the old drop-in. With the bowl lower, the disposal drops too, and the discharge can land below the old trap arm, which means lowering the stub. Better to sort that out mid-remodel than after the tile is up.

    Commercial Disposer Installation in San Mateo, CA

    Restaurant disposals are a different class of machine. A commercial disposer runs 1 to 5 HP on its own circuit, and many come with a wall control panel and a water solenoid. We set the unit, plumb the water feed and coordinate with the electrician on the circuit. Expect an inspector to check it. Local code may also limit where a disposer can discharge relative to the grease interceptor, so we check that rule before the job starts.

    Standard InSinkErator Installation in San Mateo

    A standard install goes into a sink that already has a drain and power. The old disposal comes down, the flange gets scraped clean and a new one goes in on fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on. We hang the unit, turn the lower ring until it locks and connect the discharge tube to the trap with a new slip-joint washer so nothing weeps. Power comes from a cord kit or the existing hardwire, checked for ground before the breaker goes back on.
