    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to ensure reliable operation for your home in San Mateo, CA.
