    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in San Mateo, CA

    Think of a manifold like an electrical panel, but for water lines.     Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in San Mateo

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in San Mateo, CA

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. All three are code-listed for drinking water. Pipe, fittings and tools come from the same maker on our jobs, which keeps the warranty valid.

    Emergency PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in San Mateo, CA

    PEX rarely fails on its own. When it does, the cause is usually a nail or screw driven through it, a rodent chewing on an attic run, or a fitting that was never crimped all the way. Our trucks stock half-inch to one-inch pipe and fittings. Most emergency PEX repairs in San Mateo, CA are a cut-and-splice job that takes under an hour once the drywall is open.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in San Mateo

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. Since a crimp is only as good as the tool that made it, every ring gets a go/no-go gauge check, and any ring that fails gets cut off and redone. That takes seconds. Skipping it is how leaks start.
