    Thermostat and Gas Valve Repair in Hayward, CA

    If your gas water heater isn't heating water to the correct temperature, or if it is running too hot, the thermostat or gas valve might be to blame.     Gas Valve and Thermostat Repair in Hayward, CA

    Issues with the gas valve or thermostat can lead to uneven water temperatures or a complete lack of hot water. If a part is defective, we will replace it with a quality, manufacturer-approved component so your water heater heats efficiently and safely.

    Gas Water Heater Noise Troubleshooting in Hayward

    Strange noises coming from your gas water heater, such as rumbling, popping or whistling, often indicate sediment buildup in the tank or a burner problem. Hard water can speed up sediment accumulation. Our repair service includes flushing the tank and inspecting for sediment, addressing pressure issues or checking other components that might be causing the noise, improving efficiency and quiet operation.

    Pilot Light Repair in Hayward

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most common gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and service or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are completed in about an hour.

    Gas Water Heater Repair Cost in Hayward, CA

    Thermocouple and pilot repairs usually cost $150 to $350. Gas control valve replacement often runs $350 to $700, and power vent blower repairs can range from $400 to $800. A new gas tank installed typically costs $1,600 to $3,000 depending on size and venting. We help you choose based on tank age and condition.

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Hayward

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it senses vapors or overheating. Lint and dust in garages can clog the arrestor and shut down the system. We clean the combustion chamber and replace sensors or thermal switches as needed.

    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Hayward, CA

    A yellow, lazy or noisy flame signals a dirty burner or poor combustion air. We remove and clean the burner, check the orifice size for natural gas or propane and adjust as needed. A clean blue flame heats more quickly and produces less carbon monoxide. We also check for rust flakes falling from the flue.
