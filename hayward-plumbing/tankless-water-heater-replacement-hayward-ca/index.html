    Tank to Tankless Conversion in Hayward, CA

    Converting from a tank to tankless takes a full day in most homes. The work usually includes a larger gas line, new venting through a wall or roof, and a new spot on the wall for the unit. The old flue gets capped.

    Rinnai Condensing Upgrades in Hayward, CA

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Hayward, CA

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Sun and weather wear on the covers and wiring. Freezing is uncommon along the coast and on valley floors in Northern California, but frosty nights do happen and can crack exposed outdoor pipes. We check the freeze protection settings and the pipe cover kit on every outdoor swap. Often the simplest job we do.

    Gas Line Testing on Tankless Installs in Hayward, CA

    Every gas connection on a tankless gets a soap test and a manometer reading at full fire before we leave the house. Static pressure means little if it drops when the burner opens up. We test both.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Hayward

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. Most need at least a 3/4-inch line. We check meter capacity and pipe length against the manufacturer's sizing chart, then confirm pressure with the unit running. Starved units throw error codes.

    Whole-House Electric Tankless Replacement in Hayward

    Whole-house electric tankless performance drops when the incoming water is cold, since the unit has to raise the temperature further at the same amperage. We replace Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart units. Sometimes a heat pump tank serves the house better, and we will say so.
