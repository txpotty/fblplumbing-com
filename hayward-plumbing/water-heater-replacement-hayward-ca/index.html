    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Hayward

    The cost of water heater replacement in Hayward, CA usually ranges from $1,200 to $2,500 for a standard tank unit, including installation. Tankless units can range from $3,000 to $5,000 or more, depending on the model and necessary plumbing modifications. Factors like unit type, capacity, brand and labor complexity influence the final price.

    In general, if your water heater is over 10 years old, frequently breaks down or has a major issue like a leaking tank, replacement is often more cost-effective in the long run. For newer units with minor, easily fixable issues, repair might be a viable option. Our technicians can provide an honest assessment.

    Many households of two to three people do well with a 40 gallon tank, while larger families often need 50 gallons or more. We size according to your bathroom count and usage.

    We install Bradford White, Rheem and A.O. Smith water heaters. Each offers reliable performance and a range of warranties.

    A traditional water heater stores and heats a limited amount of water in a tank, whereas a tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit. Tankless units offer endless hot water and can be more energy-efficient, but often have a higher upfront cost and may require gas line upgrades.

    Most new water heaters come with a manufacturer's warranty ranging from 6 to 12 years on the tank and components, depending on the model and brand. FBL Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation, so your investment is covered.

    Most replacements take two to four hours. Attic installs or relocations can take longer.

    FBL Plumbing installs and services all major brands, including Bradford White, Rheem, AO Smith, Rinnai, Navien and State Water Heaters. We can help you choose the best brand and model that fits your budget and hot water demands.
