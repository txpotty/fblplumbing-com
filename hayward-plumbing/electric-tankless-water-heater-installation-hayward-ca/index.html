    Sizing comes down to two numbers, flow rate and temperature rise. If your groundwater comes in at 50 degrees and you want 120 at the shower, that is a 70 degree rise, and a 27 kW unit delivers about 2.6 gallons per minute at that rise. Summer water helps. Panel and pipe layout matter as well.

    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless Electric in Hayward

    Switching from tank to tankless electric usually takes panel work and some re-piping. The higher amperage often means new breakers, heavier wire and sometimes a service upgrade, and the water lines get moved to meet the unit on the wall. We manage both trades. The closet opens up.

    Electrical Panel Review and Upgrades in Hayward

    Before any electric tankless install, we look hard at the panel.     Converting from Tank to Electric Tankless in Hayward, CA

    Converting a tank to electric tankless frees floor space and ends standby loss. We remove the old tank, mount the new unit on the wall, reroute the water lines and add isolation valves, while the electrician runs the new circuits to the unit. Most finish in a day. It looks tidier.

    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Hayward

    Hard water builds scale on tankless heating elements, which cuts efficiency and can trigger error codes. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. We recommend a softener or a scale inhibitor ahead of the unit, and every install gets service valves so the heater can be flushed with a descaling solution once a year. It is a short service.
