    Detecting Pipe Damage and Corrosion in Hayward, CA

    Sewer lines can suffer from many forms of damage, including cracks, breaks, corrosion and sagging sections due to shifting soil. Our camera inspections provide a detailed visual assessment of the pipe's interior, identifying these structural issues. We can determine the severity and exact location of damage, helping you decide between spot repair, relining or full pipe replacement. Early detection is essential to preventing catastrophic failures.

    Belly and Offset Detection in Hayward, CA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds water and waste. Offsets happen when pipe sections shift out of alignment, often because of soil movement. Expansive clay soils are common across Northern California, and seasonal swelling and shrinking can shift slabs and buried sewer laterals. The camera shows these defects clearly, and we mark their location for targeted repair.
