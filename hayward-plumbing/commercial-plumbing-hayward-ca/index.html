    Commercial Leak Detection in Hayward, CA

    Water bills that jump 30 percent with no change in business usually mean a hidden leak or a stuck fixture. Start with the meter. We shut fixtures off zone by zone and watch the meter, then use acoustic sensors, thermal cameras and tracer gas to find leaks in walls, ceilings and buried service lines. Sometimes the culprit is just a running toilet. One stuck flush valve can waste several thousand gallons a week.

    Commercial Restroom Fixture Repair in Hayward, CA

    Restroom calls are the most common commercial work we do. A flushometer that runs, a urinal that will not stop, or a sensor faucet that drips are usually fixed with a diaphragm kit, a solenoid or a new battery pack. We stock those. Wall-hung toilets that keep clogging or rocking get a carrier check, since a loose bowl can leak inside the wall. Weak flow at one sink often comes down to a plugged stop valve.

    Backflow Prevention Assembly Service in Hayward

    A backflow assembly keeps water from your building, including irrigation lines, boiler water and chemical feeds, from flowing back into the public supply. Most providers want a yearly test. We test RPZ, double check and PVB assemblies with calibrated gauges and send the results straight to the water provider. Failed devices usually need a rebuild kit, not a new valve. That is why the truck carries Watts, Wilkins and Febco kits.

    Commercial Water Heater Repair and Installation in Hayward, CA

    A commercial water heater is judged by recovery rate, the gallons it can reheat per hour, not by tank size alone. Undersized heaters run cold by mid-shift. We service AO Smith, Rheem, Bradford White and State commercial units, gas and electric, tank and tankless, and we check thermostats, gas valves, flue draft and T and P valves on every call. For a replacement, we size from fixture count and peak demand. That is how a busy kitchen stops running out of hot water during the dinner push.

    Sewer Camera Inspection for Businesses in Hayward

    A camera inspection shows the inside of a drain line on a monitor and records it so you can share it with a landlord or contractor. Guesswork goes away. We push the camera from a cleanout, note the footage at every break, root mass or sag, and use a locator to mark depth and position on the ground above. You get the video file. That footage helps you budget repairs and explain them to owners or a board.

    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Hayward

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Many older homes here sit on raised foundations with crawlspaces, which make supply and drain repairs more accessible than slab work. We confirm it with a meter test and pressure test, then use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to pinpoint the leak within a foot or two. Then we decide. Some leaks are best fixed with a small cut through the slab, while others are cheaper to abandon and reroute overhead. We price both so you can compare.

    Commercial Pipe Repair and Repiping in Hayward, CA

    Older commercial buildings often have galvanized supply lines that are rusted nearly shut, or copper that has pinholed along hot water runs. Patching works for a while. Once leaks start showing up in different spots every few months, a sectional repipe in Type L copper or commercial-grade PEX usually costs less than the next three emergency calls. We map the system first. Then we phase the work by floor or by tenant so the building stays open.
