    Copper piping is a long-standing, reliable choice known for its durability and resistance to corrosion.     Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Hayward, CA

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. They are caused by a mix of water chemistry, high velocity and thin Type M pipe, and once they begin they tend to keep coming back. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes. We help you compare the cost of both paths.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Hayward, CA

    Cast iron drain pipe in older homes can rust through at the bottom, while some ABS from the 1980s was made with defective resin that cracks. Signs include sewer odors, slow drains and stains on ceilings below upstairs bathrooms. We camera the line first, then replace reachable sections with PVC or ABS. Under-slab drains can be tunneled, rerouted or lined depending on location. We explain the options with the camera footage on screen.

    PEX Repiping in Hayward

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale buildup and expands slightly if water freezes, which helps in colder homes. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe for better flow. Systems can be run as a home-run manifold with a shutoff for each fixture or as a trunk and branch layout. PEX installs faster than copper with many fewer fittings in the walls. It is our most requested repipe material.
