    Grease Trap Pumping and Hauling in Hayward

    Trap waste is regulated as a liquid waste, so in most places it has to be hauled by a licensed hauler to an approved receiving facility, not dumped down a manhole or into a dumpster.     Hydro-Jetting for Grease Lines in Hayward, CA

    A cable machine pokes a hole through grease. A jetter takes it off the pipe wall. We run a jetter at roughly 3,000 to 4,000 psi with a rotating nozzle that cuts the grease off the full circumference of the pipe and flushes it downstream. Afterward a camera pass confirms the pipe is clean.

    Grease Trap Inspection and Repair in Hayward

    An empty trap is the only time anyone can see what is wrong with it. We look for cracked baffles and missing outlet tees. A missing outlet tee lets the floating grease cap pour straight into the sewer, and it is one of the most common failures we find on older concrete interceptors. We can often fix small problems on the spot.

    Outdoor Grease Interceptor Pumping in Hayward, CA

    In-ground interceptors need a vacuum truck and usually a pressure wand to break up the cap. We open every compartment and pump each one to the floor. With the tank empty, we inspect the tees, baffle wall, risers and lid seals, since a cracked riser lets dirt and groundwater into the tank. Lids go back on tight.

    Grease Trap Hydro-Jetting in Hayward, CA
