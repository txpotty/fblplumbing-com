    Pressure Switch Replacement in Hayward

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are common causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most budget-friendly well repairs.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Hayward, CA

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. Tree roots seek out sewer laterals during dry spells, which is why heavy rains often reveal cracked lines through sudden backups. Lightning and power surges frequently damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help reduce repeat failures.
