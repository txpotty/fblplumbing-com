    Well Pump Diagnostic Services in Hayward, CA

    Voltage, amperage, pressure and flow rate tell us most of what is happening down the well without pulling a single length of pipe. High amp draw points to a weak motor.     Grundfos Well Pump Repair in Hayward

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have built-in electronics that protect the motor and report faults. Sensor failures and drive errors are the usual problems. FBL Plumbing reads the fault code, checks the wiring and tests the pressure sensor before replacing anything in a Hayward, CA well. Usually the electronics are fine.

    Constant Pressure System Repair in Hayward, CA

    Variable speed drives like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by changing motor speed. When they fault, the drive shows a code that guides diagnosis. We troubleshoot the drive, the pressure sensor and the wiring, then repair or replace the failed part and verify the setpoint. These need a smaller tank.

    Low Water Pressure Repair in Hayward

    Weak pressure on a well is not always the pump. A clogged sediment filter, a pressure switch set too low or a leak in the yard line can all cause it. In some cases the pump is fine and the well just cannot keep up with demand. We test each one.

    Jet Pump Repair in Hayward, CA

    Shallow wells and booster systems often use jet pumps. Lost prime, seal leaks and impeller wear account for most jet pump calls. Our crew in Hayward restores prime, replaces worn parts and checks the suction line for air leaks. That matters most where water levels rise and fall through the year, since a dropping level can starve the pump and cost it prime.

    Control Box & Electrical Repair in Hayward, CA

    Capacitors, relays and corroded terminals inside the control box cause many pump failures. A tripped breaker is often the first sign. With the power locked out, we test and replace the failed parts and tighten every terminal. Wiring meets Northern California code before we restart the pump.

    Deep Well Pump Repair in Hayward

    Deep wells get submersible pumps. They push water up instead of pulling it, and they run quiet for years, but motors, impellers and wire splices still fail eventually. After pulling the pump, we test each part and replace only the failed one. Heat-shrink splice kits and a torque arrestor protect the new work in your Hayward, CA well.
