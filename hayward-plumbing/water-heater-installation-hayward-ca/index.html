    Expansion Tank Installation in Hayward, CA

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves. An expansion tank absorbs that extra pressure. We install and pre-charge the tank to match your water pressure. This protects your new water heater and pipes.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Hayward

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, especially in closed-loop systems. Plumbing work in Northern California follows the state plumbing code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code with state amendments. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to support proper system operation and prevent early wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Hayward. This small component offers significant long-term protection.
