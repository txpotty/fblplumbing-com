    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Hayward, CA

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly. Brown water after the house sits overnight, weak flow at upstairs showers and leaks at threaded joints are the usual signs that galvanized is near the end. New PEX or copper takes its place. Most homeowners notice the pressure difference the first time they turn on the shower.

    Emergency Pipe Failures and Repipes in Hayward

    Sometimes a house reaches the repipe point all at once, with a burst line and two more leaks right behind it. First we stop the leak and get water back on with a temporary fix, then we schedule the repipe without pressure. That gives you time. Insurance may cover the water damage from the burst even if it does not cover the repipe itself, so we document what we find with photos.

    Drain Line Repiping in Hayward

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Slow drains in several fixtures, sewer odors and repeated clogs in the same line are common signs of a failing drain system. We camera the lines first. Failing sections are replaced with PVC or ABS sloped and vented to code, which drains faster and resists buildup much better than the pipe it replaces.

    Slab Leak Repiping in Hayward

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Earthquakes and hillside soil movement are real risks in Northern California, so flexible connections and seismic straps help protect plumbing from ground movement. Rather than jackhammer to reach one leak, we run new lines through the attic and walls and leave the old under-slab pipe out of service. No jackhammer needed. It ends this leak and takes the rest of the slab plumbing out of the picture.

    Commercial Repiping in Hayward, CA

    Commercial buildings in Hayward, CA often have larger pipe, risers that serve several floors and tenants who cannot lose water for a full day.
