    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Hayward, CA

    A single leak on a newer copper or PEX line is usually a quick spot repair. Repeat leaks, galvanized pipe or a line older than about 40 years usually justify full replacement. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars, while a full replacement costs more but resets the clock for decades. We show you the condition of the pipe we expose so you can make the call with actual information.

    Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Hayward, CA

    Many older homes in Hayward, CA still have galvanized steel water lines, which are prone to corrosion, rust, and reduced water flow over time.     Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Hayward

    If your service line runs under a driveway, pool deck or mature landscaping, trenchless replacement can save a lot of restoration cost. We dig small pits at each end and pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through using a pneumatic boring tool or pipe pulling equipment. The process usually takes one day. You keep your pavers and trees, and the new line carries a lengthy manufacturer warranty.
