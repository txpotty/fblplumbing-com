    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Hayward

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Older neighborhoods in Northern California include prewar and postwar homes with galvanized supply lines and cast iron or clay sewer pipe that are nearing the end of their life. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. Acoustic gear and a thermal camera tell us where it is.
