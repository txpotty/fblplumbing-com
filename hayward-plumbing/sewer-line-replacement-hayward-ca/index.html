    Long-Lasting Sewer Line Replacement Services in Hayward, CA

    Traditional Trench Sewer Line Replacement in Hayward

    For significant damage or certain pipe layouts, traditional trenching may be the most effective method. This involves digging a trench to access and replace the damaged sewer line directly. Our team carefully plans the excavation to minimize disruption to your landscape, using the right equipment for clean, precise digging. We make sure of proper grading and backfilling to prevent future issues and restore your property.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Hayward, CA

    Traditional excavation is sometimes the best option, especially when the old line has a severe belly or the slope must be corrected. We dig along the line, remove the old pipe and set new PVC on compacted bedding at the correct grade. Rocky or hard-packed soil can slow digging, and we account for that in the estimate. Each joint is glued and checked before backfill. The inspector sees the open trench before we cover it.

    Sewer Pipe Lining as an Alternative in Hayward

    Cured-in-place pipe lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. After cleaning, a resin-soaked liner is installed and cured in place. This works when the host pipe still holds its shape without major sags or collapse. Lining can be a good option under slabs or finished areas. We tell you honestly if your line is a candidate.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair & Replacement in Hayward, CA

    Trenchless technology, such as pipe bursting or pipe lining, offers a less invasive option to traditional methods. Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one, fracturing the old pipe in place. Pipe lining inserts a new, durable resin liner inside the existing pipe. Both methods significantly reduce the need for excavation, preserving your yard and landscaping in Hayward, CA while providing a long-lasting solution.

    Sewer Line Replacement Cost Assessment in Hayward

    The cost of sewer line replacement varies widely based on the method used (trenchless or traditional), the length and depth of the pipe, the type of material, and site access. Before any work begins, FBL Plumbing provides a clear and transparent cost assessment. We explain all potential expenses, including labor, materials, equipment, and permit fees in Hayward. Our goal is a cost-effective solution without compromising on quality or long-term reliability.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Hayward

    Pipe bursting replaces the old sewer line by pulling a new pipe through the path of the old one. A bursting head breaks the old pipe outward while fused HDPE pipe follows behind. This typically needs only an entry pit and an exit pit instead of a long trench. It works well under driveways, mature trees and landscaping. The new pipe is seamless, which leaves no seams for roots to enter.

    Sewer Line Inspection and Diagnostics in Hayward

    Before any replacement, a thorough video inspection is essential. We use high-resolution cameras to travel through your sewer lines, identifying the exact location and nature of the problem, whether it is cracks, root intrusion, or collapsed sections. This accuracy lets us recommend the most appropriate and cost-effective replacement plan. That way we address the real cause of your sewer issues in Hayward.

    Clay and Orangeburg Line Replacement in Hayward, CA

    Clay pipe joints let roots in, and Orangeburg fiber pipe flattens and blisters with age. Both are near the end of their life in many older properties. When the camera shows multiple failures, replacement is typically the best value. We remove or burst the old line and install modern pipe with a long expected service life. You get a smooth, root-resistant line to the street.
