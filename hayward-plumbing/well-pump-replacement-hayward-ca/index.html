    Low pressure, sputtering faucets, no water and high electric bills can point to pump trouble. Frequent cycling is another warning sign.

    In many areas, a plumbing or well permit is required for well pump replacement to ensure compliance with local building and health codes. FBL Plumbing handles all necessary permitting on your behalf, so your installation is fully compliant and safe.

    Regular maintenance, such as checking your well's water level, keeping the pressure tank air charge correct and fixing any leaks promptly, can extend your pump's life. Avoiding excessive water use and installing a good quality sediment filter can also reduce wear and tear on the pump.
