    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Hayward

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while fracturing the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is highly resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Hayward homes. This method is well suited when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Hayward, CA

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is particularly useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add many years of service.

    Sewer Camera Inspections for Trenchless Repair in Hayward

    Before any trenchless repair, a detailed sewer camera inspection is crucial. Our high-definition cameras travel through your sewer lines, providing real-time video of the pipe's interior. This allows us to precisely diagnose the problem, identify its exact location and determine the most appropriate trenchless method, whether it is pipe bursting or CIPP.     Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Hayward

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot penetrate. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their form.

    Trenchless Drain Pipe Repair in Hayward, CA

    Beyond main sewer lines, trenchless techniques can also be applied to repair damaged drain pipes on your property. Whether it is a cracked kitchen drain or a clogged laundry line, we can often reline or burst smaller diameter pipes without tearing up your walls or floors in Hayward, CA. This preserves your home's interior and minimizes renovation costs.

    Sectional Point Repair in Hayward

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are common on otherwise healthy lines.
