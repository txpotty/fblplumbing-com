    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Antioch

    A single clogged sink or tub in Antioch, CA usually runs $200 to $350. Root-filled mains and jobs that need jetting usually fall in the $400 to $1,000 range. Location changes the price. A clean outdoor cleanout keeps the price down, while pulling a toilet or working through a roof vent adds time. You get the number before we start.

    Snaking runs a spinning cable with a cutting head through the clog. Hydro jetting sends water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scrubs the whole pipe wall as it is pulled back toward the cleanout. Jetting leaves the pipe cleaner. We usually jet recurring grease lines and scaled mains, and snake the one-off clogs.

    Drain snaking, also called augering, uses a flexible cable and cutting head to break up or pull out the clog. Hair, wipes and roots respond well to it. Jetting scours the whole pipe with high-pressure water and strips the grease, scale and sludge a cable slides past. Repeat clogs usually need jetting.

    Keep grease, coffee grounds and wipes out of drains. Hair strainers in tubs and showers help. After using the disposal, run hot water for a bit and scrape food into the trash first to keep the kitchen line clearer. Root-prone lines need cutting every year or two.

    No permit is needed for routine rooting. Installing a new cleanout, digging up a broken section or replacing the sewer lateral usually does require a plumbing permit and an inspection. We pull permits for any repair work we do.

    Watch for very slow drains, gurgling from toilets and sour odors. Water rising in a tub or shower when another fixture drains is a strong sign of a main line clog. That one is urgent. The longer it goes, the more likely the next flush ends with sewage on the bathroom floor.

    Chemical drain openers rarely clear a real clog and can eat at old pipe. Old cast iron and the thin brass traps found in Antioch's older homes suffer the worst of it. They also put our techs at risk. Let us know if you used a chemical, because that water splashes when the trap comes off.

    Clearing a clog in Antioch does not need a permit. Repairs are another matter. A new cleanout, a dig to fix broken pipe or a sewer replacement usually needs a plumbing permit. We handle the permit and the inspection.
