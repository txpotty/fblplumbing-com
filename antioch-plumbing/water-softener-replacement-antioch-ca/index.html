    Metered Softener Replacement in Antioch

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly used up. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first few weeks.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Antioch, CA

    For homeowners in Antioch, CA looking for an alternative to traditional salt-based systems, salt-free water conditioners offer a different approach. These units, sometimes called descalers, condition water to prevent mineral scale without removing the minerals or adding salt. While they do not produce soft feeling water, they reliably protect pipes and appliances from scale. We can replace your existing salt-free system or help you transition from a salt-based unit.

    Replacing Pelican Water Softeners in Antioch

    Pelican Water Systems are popular for their whole-house filtration and softening solutions, including both salt-based and salt-free options. If your Pelican system in Antioch is nearing the end of its lifespan or needs a significant upgrade, our technicians are experienced in their replacement. We make sure your new Pelican system or an equivalent quality brand is installed to factory specifications for reliable performance and warranty validity.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Antioch, CA

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more economical than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes better sense.
