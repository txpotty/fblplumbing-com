    Gas and Electric Water Heater Checks in Antioch, CA

    Whether your home in Antioch, CA uses a gas or electric hot water tank, our maintenance covers the specific needs of each.     Electric Element and Thermostat Service in Antioch

    Electric tanks rely on upper and lower heating elements that get crusted with scale in hard water. We test each element with a meter, check the thermostats and look for scorched wires at the terminal block. If an element has failed we replace it with the matching wattage and voltage. Many families who think they need a new heater just need a lower element and a flush.

    Commercial Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Antioch, CA

    Businesses in Antioch, CA rely closely on consistent hot water for their operations. Our commercial hot water tank maintenance services are tailored to the higher demands of commercial units.     Sediment Flush and Descaling in Antioch

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause noisy popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution approved for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Antioch

    A properly functioning temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank. Our technicians will test this valve to ensure it's not stuck or leaking, preventing dangerous pressure buildup within the tank. We verify it opens and closes correctly, and if it fails, we will recommend immediate replacement to keep your Antioch home safe and compliant with plumbing codes.

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Antioch, CA

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber.     Frequently Asked Questions About Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Antioch

    A standard flush and inspection typically runs $125 to $250 depending on tank size and access. Anode replacement typically adds $75 to $200 in parts and labor, and powered anodes cost more.

    FBL Plumbing services all major brands of hot water tanks, including but not limited to Rheem, Bradford White, A. O. Smith, State, Noritz and Bosch. Our technicians are familiar with a broad range of models and their specific maintenance needs.

    While some basic steps like draining a small amount of water can be done by a homeowner, professional hot water tank maintenance involves specialized tools and experience to properly inspect electrical or gas components, anode rods and pressure relief valves. It is best left to a professional to ensure safety and code compliance.

    FBL Plumbing stands behind our work. While maintenance services usually don't have long-term warranties like new installations, we guarantee our workmanship for a set period. Any parts replaced during maintenance will carry their manufacturer's warranty.

    The anode rod, also known as a sacrificial rod, is a metal rod inside your hot water tank designed to corrode before the tank itself. It protects the tank lining from rust and corrosion, which matters most when water carries a lot of minerals, and it extends the lifespan of your unit.

    That smell typically comes from bacteria reacting with a magnesium anode. We can switch to an aluminum-zinc or powered anode and sanitize the tank to get rid of it.

    Most flushes and inspections take 45 minutes to 90 minutes. Heavily scaled tanks or anode replacements in tight closets can take a little longer.
