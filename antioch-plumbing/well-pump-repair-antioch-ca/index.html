    Well Pump Pressure Tank Issues in Antioch

    The pressure tank works together with your well pump to maintain consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling. A waterlogged tank, a ruptured diaphragm, or a faulty air valve can cause rapid pump cycling, fluctuating water pressure, or even no water at all. Our team accurately diagnoses pressure tank problems and performs necessary repairs or replacements. We make sure your tank is properly sized and charged for optimal system performance in Antioch.

    Diagnosing Low Water Pressure in Antioch

    Low water pressure from a well system can be a stubborn problem, often indicating issues beyond just the pump itself. This could be due to a failing pressure switch, a partially clogged well screen, a leak in the supply line, or even a pump that is no longer meeting demand. Our team conducts thorough diagnostics to pinpoint the exact cause of low pressure in your Antioch home, ensuring a targeted and effective repair solution.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Antioch, CA

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. Tree roots seek out sewer laterals during dry spells, which is why heavy rains often reveal damaged lines through sudden backups. Lightning and power surges frequently damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help prevent repeat failures.

    Control and pressure switch repairs usually cost $150 to $500, while a pressure tank replacement typically runs $600 to $1,500.     Pressure Switch Replacement in Antioch

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are frequent causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most budget-friendly well repairs.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Antioch, CA

    Jet pumps, usually found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Common problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. FBL Plumbing can reliably troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Antioch operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is crucial for jet pump function.
