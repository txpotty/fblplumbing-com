    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Antioch, CA

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is notorious for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Many insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to insure and sell.

    Hydronic Heating with PEX in Antioch

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Antioch, CA

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, much like a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when multiple fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to balance cost and performance.

    PEX for Water Line Replacement in Antioch

    When your main water line from the street to your home needs replacement, PEX is an smart option. PEX main lines handle ground movement and resist corrosion better than rigid pipe. Earthquakes and hillside soil movement are real risks in Northern California, so flexible connections and seismic straps help protect plumbing from sudden movement.
