    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Antioch, CA

    A salt bridge is a hard crust that forms in the brine tank and keeps salt from dissolving. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. We break up bridges, clean out mushy salt, inspect the float and brine well and repair the safety valve. We also recommend salt types that bridge less in warm or humid spaces.

    Control Valve Repair in Antioch

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, directing the flow of water and starting regeneration cycles. When it malfunctions, your system can stop softening water or waste salt. FBL Plumbing technicians are experts at diagnosing and repairing common control valve issues, whether mechanical or electronic, restoring precise timing and efficient operation. We stock common replacement parts to speed up your repair in Antioch.

    Over time, the resin beads in your water softener can become fouled or degrade, losing their ability to exchange ions and soften water.
