    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Maintenance in Antioch

    Standard visits typically cost $125 to $250. A valve rebuild adds roughly $100 to $250. Resin replacement is the big-ticket item at $300 to $700, and it still costs far less than a new softener.

    Once a year works for most homes. Very hard water or a heavily used older unit can justify a visit every six months, especially in a house with a tankless water heater to protect. Between visits, look in the brine tank once a month and watch for spots on glassware.

    At least once a year. Twice a year makes sense for very hard water or a busy household. The resin and injector last longer when grit and sludge get cleared regularly, and the salt dose stays matched to what the house actually uses.

    Evaporated salt pellets are the cleanest option and cause fewer bridges and less sludge than rock salt. Solar salt falls in between. Potassium chloride works if you want to cut sodium, but it softens less per pound, so the salt dose usually has to go up by a quarter or more.
