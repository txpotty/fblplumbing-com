    Frequently Asked Questions About Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Antioch

    Most poly replacements in Antioch fall between $4,000 and $15,000 or more. Size drives the price.     Most full poly repipes take three to five days, including drywall patching. Big houses need more time. Fixture count, attic access and pipe choice set the schedule, and you will usually have water each evening.

    For PEX, we use trusted brands like Uponor, Viega and Zurn. We use Uponor most often. Copper replacements use Type L instead of thin Type M, since the heavier wall lasts longer.
