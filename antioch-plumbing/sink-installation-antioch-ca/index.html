    Each Kind of Sink We Install in Antioch, CA

    Drop-In Sink Installation in Antioch, CA

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim hides small cutout flaws, so laminate tops take a drop-in with just a size check and the right sealant. Mounting clips snug it down. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in Antioch

    Stone shops cut the hole, but we set the bowl. Getting an undermount centered under a polished cutout so the reveal is even on all sides takes a helper, a few shims and patience before the clips get tightened. Uneven reveals look bad forever. We check it from both ends of the counter.

    Undermount Clips and Cure Time in Antioch

    Undermount bowls need overnight to cure. Silicone holds in a few hours but needs about a day before it carries a full bowl. We brace it and come back. Plumbing goes in on the second visit.

    Sink Installation Cost Factors in Antioch, CA

    Labor is the main cost driver. Replacing old galvanized stubs or adding a second sink adds hours, while a same-size drop-in swap with good shutoffs is the cheapest job we do on any given week. We quote labor apart from the sink. You can buy it or we can.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Antioch, CA

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. Since the rim sits higher, the faucet has to be taller or wall-mounted, and the drain must match whether the bowl has an overflow. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Kitchen Sink Installation in Antioch

    Kitchen sinks come in four mounts: drop-in, undermount, farmhouse and flush-set. Each one needs its own support, and a cast iron farmhouse sink can weigh well over a hundred pounds before it holds any water, so the cabinet often needs reinforcement first. The cabinet gets checked before install day. Disposal and dishwasher connections are part of the job.

    Leak-Proof Sink Connections in Antioch, CA

    Every joint gets tested before we leave. The bowl gets filled and drained while we watch every nut under the sink with a light and a dry towel. A towel shows a weep fast. Pipe dope goes on threads, not compression fittings.

    Pedestal Sink Mounting in Antioch

    The pedestal is mostly for looks. A wall bracket lagged into blocking carries the weight, and if the wall has none, we open it and add a board. Drywall anchors do not cut it. A loose basin cracks the trap.
