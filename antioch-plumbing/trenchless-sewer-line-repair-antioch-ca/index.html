    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Costs in Antioch, CA

    Compare total cost, not just the plumbing line on the invoice. A dig job looks cheaper on paper, until you add saw-cutting the driveway and pouring new concrete over a trench that may run 50 feet. Trenchless avoids most of that. Sod and sprinkler repair add up too.

    Cost Comparison of Trenchless vs Traditional in Antioch, CA

    Depth changes the math. A line two feet down in open lawn is often cheaper to dig than to line, but a lateral eight feet deep that runs under the driveway and a porch costs far more to excavate than to burst or line from two pits. We quote both ways. Then you pick.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacements in Antioch

    A bursting head is a steel cone, slightly wider than the old pipe, pulled through the line by a hydraulic unit or a winch cable. It cracks the old pipe outward into the soil while a string of new HDPE pipe follows right behind it. Fused joints, no gaps. This is the fix for collapsed pipe.

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow Trenchless Systems in Antioch

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow both make lining systems we use, each with its own resin chemistry and installation equipment. Both systems are made to meet the ASTM standards written for cured-in-place pipe. The brand matters less than the prep, because a liner bonded to a dirty, scaly pipe wall will not hold the way the manufacturer rates it. So the line gets jetted or descaled to bare wall before any resin goes in.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Cost in Antioch, CA

    Point repairs often cost $1,500 to $3,500, while full lining of a residential lateral often runs $4,000 to $12,000 depending on length and diameter. Pipe bursting typically ranges from $5,000 to $15,000. On a short, shallow run, a traditional dig can come in lower. Once you add back concrete and sod, the gap usually closes, and we price both options side by side so you can see the difference.

    Root Intrusion Fixes in Antioch, CA

    Roots enter at the joints, where clay bells or cast iron hubs have a small gap or a failed gasket. Once inside, they branch into a mat that fills the pipe. We cut them out with a mechanical root cutter or a hydro jet, then line the pipe so those joints are sealed for good and the line stops needing a cleaning every few months. With no joints left, there is nowhere for them to return.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Lining in Antioch, CA

    Cast iron rusts from the inside out, and the bottom of the pipe goes first. That is called channel rot. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or carbide cutters spinning on a flexible shaft, which brings the wall back to bare metal so a liner can bond to it. Under a slab, lining beats breaking up the floor in two rooms.
