    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Antioch, CA

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones corrode from the acid that sewer gas forms and eventually fall off into the tank. With no outlet baffle, sludge flows right out to the leach lines. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Septic Code and Permit Work in Antioch, CA

    New tanks and new fields need a permit from the local health authority in most of the country, along with any change to the system's layout. Our office prepares the site sketch and submits the permit, then book the inspector before anything is covered. Small fixes like a tee usually skip the permit. The inspector's sign-off goes in your file.

    Septic Line Clog Clearing in Antioch

    A slow toilet and a gurgle in the tub tend to mean something is stuck in the line before the tank.     Effluent Pump Replacement in Antioch, CA

    Effluent pumps in a dosing chamber last roughly 8 to 15 years, and they usually quit without much warning at all. Humming with no flow points to a jammed impeller or a failed capacitor. We pull it and test the discharge line before setting a new one. Floats get replaced at the same time.

    Leach Field Restoration in Antioch, CA

    Before we condemn a leach field, we open the distribution box and look at how evenly it splits the flow. A box that has settled or cracked can push all the effluent into a single trench.
