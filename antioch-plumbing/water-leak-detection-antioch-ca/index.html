    Toilets are one of the most common sources of wasted water. A worn flapper or a fill valve that never shuts off can leak quietly around the clock. We use dye tests to find leaking toilets and check faucets, showers and supply lines for drips. These fixes are usually quick and save water right away.

    Main Service Line Leak Detection in Antioch, CA

    The line from the meter to your house carries full pressure and can leak underground for a long time. Wet soil, soft spots or unusually green patches of yard may be the only clues. We trace the line, listen for the leak and mark it for a targeted repair. Replacing old galvanized or polybutylene service lines is often recommended.

    Irrigation Leak Detection in Antioch

    Drip and sprinkler systems run underground across yards and often account for hidden water loss. Cracked tubing, broken emitters and stuck valves can waste large amounts of water.     Hidden Wall & Ceiling Leak Detection in Antioch, CA

    Leaks behind walls or in ceilings can cause significant damage before they become visible, leading to stained drywall, peeling paint or a musty odor. Our technicians use moisture meters and infrared cameras to scan these areas, identifying elevated moisture levels and temperature differences that indicate a hidden leak, minimizing the need for extensive wall demolition.

    Water Heater and Softener Leak Detection in Antioch
