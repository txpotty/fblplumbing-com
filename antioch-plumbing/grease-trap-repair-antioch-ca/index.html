    Grease Trap Drain Line Repair in Antioch

    Sometimes the trap is not the problem. Lines between the sinks and the trap clog first, because that is where hot water cools. We put a camera in from the nearest cleanout and record the run, looking for sags, offset joints, roots in clay sections downstream, or a belly where grease collects every week. Jetting a belly buys time, but only regrading the pipe fixes it.

    Leaking Grease Trap Repair in Antioch, CA

    A wet floor around an under-sink trap usually points to a failed lid gasket or a loose inlet coupling. Older steel bodies rust out along the bottom seams, too. We dry the area, fill the trap and watch where the water shows up. Sometimes it is a $15 gasket. If the seam itself has opened, patching rarely holds and we will tell you so before you pay for it.

    Cost-Effective Grease Trap Solutions in Antioch, CA

    Pricing depends on what failed, so we quote after the trap is pumped and we can see inside it. A gasket and a tee are a small bill.     Residential Grease Trap Repair in Antioch, CA

    Homes rarely need a grease trap, but some have one, usually a small plastic unit under the kitchen sink on a house with a septic system or a home bakery. Most leaks come from the lid or the threaded inlet fitting. We replace the gasket, reset the fittings with fresh thread sealant, and clear any grease plug in the outlet. On most homes this is under an hour of work.

    Grease Trap Repair vs Replacement Cost in Antioch, CA

    Age and material decide most repair versus replace calls. Once a steel unit has rust holes in the body, it is finished. A plastic or fiberglass trap with a broken baffle is usually worth fixing, and a concrete interceptor with sound walls and a bad riser almost always is, since replacing a buried tank means excavation and several days closed. We put both options in writing with a price on each.

    Grease Trap Leak Repair in Antioch

    Plastic and fiberglass trap bodies do not rust, but they can crack from a dropped lid or a dishwasher dumping water hotter than the trap is rated for. A hairline crack can sometimes be welded. Anything through the floor of the tank or along a molded seam we treat as a replacement, because patches there fail under the weight of a full trap. You will see the crack yourself before we suggest a fix.

    Damaged Baffle Repair in Antioch

    Baffles are the walls inside the trap that slow the water down long enough for grease to float and solids to sink. Once one cracks or falls loose, the trap is just a box that grease passes through. We pump it out and pull the old baffle. Plastic units get a factory replacement part. Steel traps with a rusted-out baffle may need a fabricated piece, and sometimes a new trap is the cheaper answer.

    Clogged Grease Trap Repair in Antioch

    Hardened grease in the inlet tee is the usual culprit when a trap backs up. We pump the trap down, then jet the inlet line with a 4,000 PSI unit until water runs free. Solids packed against the baffle come out by hand, with a scoop and a bucket. Then we look for the reason it clogged, such as a dishwasher dumping too much hot water at once or a missing sink strainer upstream. A clog that keeps coming back nearly always has a cause you can see.
