    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Antioch, CA

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. Unlike a pressure-balance valve, which only reacts to pressure changes, a thermostatic valve senses the actual water temperature and adjusts the mix, which matters for kids and older adults who cannot react fast to a hot spike. Many have separate heat and flow controls. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Tub Spout Replacement in Antioch

    Spouts mount two ways. Slip-on spouts clamp to copper with a set screw, and threaded ones screw onto a pipe nipple. The connection gets checked first. Diverter spouts must match too.

    Brand Tub Faucet Replacement in Antioch

    Brand matters more on tub valves than anywhere else. Each maker uses its own cartridges and trim threads, so a same-brand trim kit usually bolts on without plumbing changes. Cross-brand trim seldom fits. We find the logo or the casting mark first.

    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Antioch

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler. The supply lines come up through the floor under the filler, which means cutting the slab or working from a crawlspace or basement, and the filler needs a mounting bracket anchored to the subfloor. Deck-mount fillers sit on the rim. Tub-only fillers need a temperature limit.

    Corroded Tub Faucet Replacement in Antioch, CA

    Rural properties around Antioch often rely on private wells, which may need treatment for iron, sediment, or other minerals. Corrosion shows up as green crust at the handle or a stem you cannot turn by hand. By that point the threads that hold the trim are often too weak to grip a new part, and the valve body itself may be pitted where the cartridge seals against it. A new valve makes more sense.

    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Antioch, CA

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. When someone flushes a toilet, the valve cuts hot and cold flow in proportion so the mix stays steady, and a thermostatic valve goes further by holding a set temperature even as the water heater cools. Either one satisfies scald rules. Thermostatic costs more.

    Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in Antioch

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting. The old valve comes out, a pressure-balance valve goes in, and a remodel plate covers the extra holes. We usually work from behind the wall. Tile around the plate stays intact.
