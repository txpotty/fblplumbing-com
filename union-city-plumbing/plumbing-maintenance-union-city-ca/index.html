Proper softener maintenance extends its life and keeps soft water flowing to your home, preventing expensive scale damage.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Union City

    Regular water heater maintenance is crucial to preventing sediment buildup. Our service includes flushing the tank, inspecting the anode rod for corrosion, checking the temperature and pressure relief valve, and examining all connections and heating elements.     Newer homes across Northern California mostly use PEX or copper with plastic drains, and many owners are repiping older houses to improve water pressure. Our maintenance includes testing the float switch, checking for proper discharge, cleaning the pit of debris, and inspecting the check valve.
