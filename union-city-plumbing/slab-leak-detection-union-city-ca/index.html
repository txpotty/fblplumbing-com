    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Union City, CA

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. They are harder to see. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, so we isolate the cold side, map the pipe with a line tracer and listen along the route with a ground microphone.

    Slab Leaks in Older Homes in Union City, CA

    Older homes in Union City, CA often have soft copper or galvanized steel under the slab, and both corrode with age. Copper fails at bends and where it rubs concrete, while galvanized rusts from the inside until it splits. We know those failure points. It lets us test the most likely spots first.

    Slab Leaks in Commercial Buildings in Union City
