    Control Valve Troubleshooting & Repair in Union City, CA

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, regulating the flow of water and starting regeneration cycles.     Softener Not Regenerating in Union City

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Union City, CA

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. A new quality softener installed typically costs $1,500 to $3,500.     Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Union City

    A frequent issue with salt-based water softeners is a salt bridge forming in the brine tank, preventing the salt from dissolving properly. This leads to inefficient regeneration and hard water. Our technicians are skilled at breaking up these bridges, cleaning the brine tank, and ensuring the float switch and brine well are functioning correctly. We also inspect for any leaks or cracks in the tank itself that could be compromising your system's performance, offering precise repairs or replacements.
