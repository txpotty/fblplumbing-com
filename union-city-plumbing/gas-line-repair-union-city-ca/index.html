    Gas Line Extensions and Relocations in Union City

    Adding a gas fireplace, moving a laundry room or switching a range from electric to gas usually means extending the gas line. Sizing comes first. After that comes routing, the tie-in and the test.

    Gas Appliance Line Installation in Union City, CA

    Interior Black Iron Pipe Repair in Union City

    Threaded black iron pipe can leak at joints after years of vibration or settling. We tighten, reseal or replace fittings and sections with new pipe and gas-rated thread sealant.     Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Union City

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. We back out the leaking joint, clean the threads, apply gas-rated thread sealant and reassemble it, or replace the fitting or valve if the threads are damaged. We soap-test the repaired joint and pressure test the system before leaving.

    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Union City, CA

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas.     Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Union City
