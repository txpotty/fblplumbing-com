    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options. We install their 250 and 280 series often. Parts are easy to find. A small weep hole in the discharge pipe keeps the pump from air locking.

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. We install their M53 and M98 models often. Backup and combo units are available too. We help you pick the model that fits the pit.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Union City, CA

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary handles daily water. People with carpet downstairs buy these. They are worth it anywhere a few inches of water would mean new drywall.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Union City, CA

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. We pull the old pump and inspect the pit. If the old one could not keep up, we size up. We fill the pit and watch it run.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Union City, CA

    Price depends mostly on whether a pit already exists. A swap in an existing pit is the low end. Backups add cost. Your written quote shows each option and its price.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Union City, CA

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. The backup pump gets its own float and its own check valve, and it runs off a sealed 12-volt battery when the outlet goes dead. Most run five to seven hours on a full charge. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Union City

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. Because the motor is cooled by the surrounding water, they run quieter and can push more gallons per minute up a tall lift than most pedestal pumps. The pit lid sits flush. We seal the cord grommet and the lid on every install.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Union City

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. Sizing comes from two measurements we take on site, the pit diameter and the vertical lift to the discharge. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.
