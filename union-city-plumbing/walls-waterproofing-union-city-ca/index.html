    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in Union City, CA

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Union City

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. We find it with a moisture meter, a thermal camera that picks up the cooler wet area, and a pressure test on the supply lines. Then we cut a clean access hole. The bad fitting or section of pipe gets replaced. Wet insulation comes out, and fans dry the cavity before we close it up.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Union City, CA

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Older neighborhoods in Northern California include prewar and postwar homes with galvanized supply lines and cast iron or clay sewer pipe that are nearing the end of their life. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. Sealing soft old brick with a rigid coating traps water inside it and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with a new interior drain. We repoint failed mortar joints before sealing.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Union City, CA

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the waterline and check it a day later. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a clamping drain tightened onto the liner. The new pan gets its own flood test before tile.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Union City, CA

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head spraying the wall instead of the plants, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. After that the wall gets cleaned, dried and coated with a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Union City

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and often add a dehumidifier to keep relative humidity below 60 percent. Wet winters and long dry summers define the plumbing calendar in Northern California, with drainage problems in the rainy season and outdoor leaks in summer. The joists stop rotting. Drier air under the floor also cuts down on the musty smell in the house.

    Drainage System Integration in Union City, CA

    Water has to go somewhere once a wall is sealed. Exterior footing drains, interior drain tile and surface French drains all do the same job at different depths. We match the drain to where the water comes from, which might be a high water table, a yard that slopes toward the house, or a gutter dumping at the corner. We extend downspouts at least four feet from the wall.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Union City

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, overlapped a foot at every seam and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and hold it in place with termination strip. It is a cheap fix that makes a big difference.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Walls Waterproofing in Union City

    Most jobs fall between $3,000 for interior crack sealing and $15,000 or more for exterior excavation with new footing drains. The size of the wall, how wet it gets, the method we pick and how easy the wall is to reach all move the number. Access is a big one. Nothing starts until you have the quote in writing.

    Most systems we install should go 20 to 30 years or longer. Life span comes down to how it was installed, what method was used and what the soil around the house is like. The sump pump will need replacing well before that. Most pumps last seven to ten years.

    Sprinklers, nine times out of ten. Spray heads and drip lines aimed at the house soak the block every day, and soil sloped toward the wall or a short downspout adds to it, so the masonry stays wet and leaves mineral deposits as it dries. Sometimes adjusting a single sprinkler head solves it.

    Damp proofing is usually a thin spray-on asphalt coat. It slows soil moisture but cannot hold back water under hydrostatic pressure. Waterproofing uses thicker membranes, drainage boards and footing drains rated to stop liquid water, which is what a basement in wet soil or a high water table needs.

    Patching one small crack yourself is fine. A full system involves excavation, digging equipment, proper membrane thickness and knowing how water drains around a footing, plus local code on drainage and backfill. Done wrong, it can trap water against the wall and cause more damage.

    It depends on the wall and the site. On exterior foundation work we use liquid polyurethane-modified asphalt, rubberized asphalt sheets, bentonite panels and crystalline coatings, picking the one that suits the soil and the wall material. Inside showers, it is Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban.

    A shower wall job usually takes two to four days from demolition to finished membrane, counting the drying time between coats. Exterior block sealing often wraps up in a day. Larger foundation jobs can run several days, and weather can stretch that.
