    Understanding Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Union City, CA

    While snaking can punch a hole through a clog, hydrojetting in Union City, CA provides a complete pipe cleaning. Snaking is effective for simple, localized clogs, but it often leaves behind residual buildup that can lead to recurring problems. Hydrojetting, by contrast, scours the entire interior of the pipe, removing debris, grease, and scale, offering a more lasting solution to your drainage issues.
