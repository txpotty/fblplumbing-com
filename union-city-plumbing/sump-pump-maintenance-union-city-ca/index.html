    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Union City

    Drought years alternate with very wet years here, so conservation rules and low-flow fixtures are a constant part of plumbing decisions. We test your pump ahead of the rainy season by filling the pit until the float trips. We watch the pump empty the basin, listen for grinding bearings and measure how fast it cycles. If the pump struggles, you still have time to fix it before the first big storm.

    Battery Backup Pump Maintenance in Union City

    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails. We load test the battery, check the charger and alarm and run the backup pump on its own. Most backup batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Union City, CA

    If your sump pump is acting up, making unusual noises or not turning on, our emergency diagnostic service can quickly identify the problem. We use specialized tools to troubleshoot electrical issues, motor failures and float switch malfunctions. Prompt diagnosis and repair are essential to prevent water buildup, especially during unpredictable rainy seasons in Union City, CA.

    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Union City, CA

    The float switch is the most frequent reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get stuck.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Check-Ups in Union City, CA

    For homes equipped with pedestal sump pumps, FBL Plumbing provides specialized maintenance services in Union City, CA. We focus on inspecting the external motor, ensuring proper lubrication and checking the impeller for any obstructions. This type of pump, with its motor above the water, requires specific attention to its shaft and bearings to prevent premature wear and ensure reliable performance during heavy rains.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Union City, CA

    A power outage during a heavy storm can leave your primary sump pump useless. Our maintenance service includes complete testing of your battery backup sump pump system. We check the battery charge, verify the charger's function and confirm the backup pump activates when the main power is interrupted. This dual protection is vital for homes in Union City, CA where power interruptions can coincide with severe weather.

    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Union City, CA

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil around the slab or foundation.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Union City

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to premature wear. We confirm these vital parts are functioning correctly to maintain an effective water removal system for your Union City residence.
