    Frequently Asked Questions About Faucet Replacement in Union City

    Faucet replacement labor typically runs $150 to $350. Including a quality mid-range faucet, most jobs total $300 to $750. Touchless or high-end faucets can bring the total to $800 or more.

    Typically, a permit is not required for a direct faucet replacement unless it involves significant changes to the existing plumbing system or relocating water lines. If there are major changes, we will let you know about any necessary permitting.

    We check your sink's hole count and spacing and recommend matching faucets. Many single-hole faucets include a deck plate for three-hole sinks.

    To extend your faucet's life, routinely clean it with non-abrasive cleaners, avoid over-tightening handles, and address minor drips promptly. Rural properties around Union City often rely on private wells, which may need treatment for iron, sediment, or other minerals. If your water is hard, consider a whole-home water softener to combat mineral buildup.

    We install all major reputable faucet brands, including Delta, Moen, Kohler, American Standard, Pfister, and more. Our plumbers are familiar with the specifications and installation requirements for a wide range of manufacturers, giving you a perfect fit.

    Most faucet replacements take about 1 to 1.5 hours. Seized faucets or shutoff valve replacements can add time.

    Faucets with ceramic disc cartridges from Moen, Delta, Kohler and Grohe tend to last well. Keeping aerators clean and using a water softener also help.

    While some basic faucet replacements can be a DIY project, it is often best to hire a professional. Incorrect installation can lead to leaks, water damage, or even issues with your home's water pressure. Our plumbers make sure of proper sealing and connection.
