    Rooting vs. Pipe Repair in Palo Alto, CA

    Rooting makes sense when the pipe is sound and just clogged. When roots return in months, or the cable hangs on a broken joint every time, repairing that section starts to save money. The camera settles it. Rooting usually runs a few hundred dollars, and lining or replacing a section costs more up front and stops the repeat visits.

    Preventive Drain Maintenance in Palo Alto, CA

    Some lines need cutting on a calendar. A house with a big oak near the sewer might need rooting every 12 to 18 months. FBL Plumbing can put your Palo Alto, CA property on a schedule and call you a couple of weeks before the next cutting is due. Cutting a line before it stops costs less than an after-hours backup and spares you the cleanup.

    Rooter Plus Hydro Jetting in Palo Alto

    A cable cuts roots, and a jetter washes out what is left. The pair clears dense roots and old grease better than either tool alone. The pipe gets filmed before we jet. Restaurants and homes with yearly root clogs are where this combination gets used most.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Palo Alto, CA

    Jetting puts water through a nozzle at 3,000 to 4,000 psi and scrubs the pipe wall clean. Grease, scale and fine roots that a cable rides over come off with the water. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. We camera the line first. Cracked clay or thin cast iron can be hurt by that pressure, so we only jet a line we have seen and trust.

    Kitchen Line Rooter Service in Palo Alto, CA

    Kitchen lines clog slowly, over months, with grease, soap and food scraps that cool and harden on the pipe wall. Access is through the trap arm or a cleanout, using a medium cable and a spiral cutter. Thick grease calls for a follow-up jet. The jet washes the walls clean, which keeps the line open much longer than the cable alone.
