    Cost-Effective Bowl Swaps in Palo Alto, CA

    Swapping only the bowl saves money when the tank is newer, the fill valve and flush valve are in good shape, and the bowl is still made in your color. White is the easiest to match.     Bowl Replacement Versus Full Toilet Replacement in Palo Alto, CA

    Bowl-only swaps make sense when the tank is newer and the bowl is still made, since a matched bowl often costs $100 to $350 while a new basic toilet starts around $200.     Toilet Bowl Replacement for Older Homes in Palo Alto, CA

    Houses built before the 1970s often have a lead closet bend or a cast iron flange caulked with oakum, and those need gentle handling when the bowl comes off. Old cast iron can crack if you pry on it. We avoid prying on the flange and use repair rings or flange adapters made for cast iron, so the drain stays intact.

    Pressure-Assist Toilet Bowl Replacement in Palo Alto

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the tank, and the bowl is matched to that vessel's flush rate, so a bowl-only swap means finding the exact paired model. Many are not sold separately. When the matching bowl is gone, a full toilet replacement is the better route, and we will price both.

    Flange Repair During Bowl Replacement in Palo Alto, CA

    The flange is the ring that anchors the bowl to the drain, and the only time anyone sees it is when the old bowl comes off. That is the moment to check it. We fix a cracked or sunken flange before the new bowl is set, since a new bowl on a bad flange will rock and leak within months.

    Wall-Hung Commercial Bowl Replacement in Palo Alto, CA

    Wall-hung commercial bowls sit on steel carriers inside the wall, and when one cracks we also check that the carrier bolts and the outlet gasket are still sound. Loose carriers crack bowls. We reconnect and test the flushometer before anyone uses the restroom again.

    Model numbers are stamped inside the tank, usually on the back wall, and sometimes on the back of the bowl near the floor. Send us a photo of it. We order matched bowls from supply houses, and most arrive within a day or two unless the model has been discontinued.
