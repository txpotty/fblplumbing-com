    Hydro Jetting vs. Snaking Cost Comparison in Palo Alto, CA

    Snaking costs less up front. Jetting lasts longer. A cable punches through the blockage and leaves residue on the walls, which brings the clog back, while jetting cleans the whole pipe and cuts down the number of service calls over time.

    Hydro Jetting Before Pipe Lining in Palo Alto, CA

    Liners need clean pipe. Dirt ruins the bond. We jet the host pipe to remove roots, scale and debris, then camera it to confirm it is ready, cutting out any heavy obstructions before the liner goes in, since skipping prep can lead to wrinkles or poor bonding.

    Residential Hydro Jetting in Palo Alto

    Household lines collect a mix of buildup. Kitchen grease and bathroom soap scum both pile up. We use nozzles and pressure settings that fit everything from a 1-1/2 inch kitchen line to a 4 inch main, so the jet cleans the pipe without blowing back into a fixture.

    Scale Removal in Cast Iron Pipes in Palo Alto

    Cast iron grows rust tubercles. Scale adds to them. We set the pressure for old iron, strip the buildup with a descaling nozzle and check for weak spots on camera as we go, which is also the standard prep before pipe lining.

    Camera Inspection and Hydro Jetting in Palo Alto, CA
