    Rinnai Tankless Service in Palo Alto

    Rinnai units tell you a lot through the controller. Code 11 means the burner never lit, and code 12 means the flame went out after it did.     Service Valve Kit Installs in Palo Alto, CA

    Plenty of tankless heaters went in without service valves, and without them there is no clean way to hook up a descaling pump. We add a pair of isolation valves with purge ports on the hot and cold lines. Most kits include a relief valve port as well. After that, every flush takes minutes to set up.

    Maintenance for Every Tankless Brand in Palo Alto, CA

    We service Bosch, Rheem, Takagi and Navien heaters around Palo Alto, and each one has its own filter locations and code list in the manual. The manufacturer's steps come first, not a generic checklist. Warranty claims later can hinge on it.

    Home Tankless Flushing in Palo Alto

    Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running mineral-heavy. Inside a tankless heater, that water runs through a small copper or stainless coil while the burner fires, and every minute of hot water leaves a little calcium behind. A pump on the service valves pushes descaling solution through the coil for roughly an hour. Flow comes back. Temperatures stop swinging, and the unit quits shutting down on high-limit.

    Rinnai Air Filter and Flame Rod Care in Palo Alto

    Rinnai cabinets are tight. We wash the air intake filter and check the flame rod for carbon. Recirculating Rinnai models also have a pump setting worth checking, since a wrong schedule wastes gas every hour the pump runs for no reason.

    Condensate and Neutralizer Service in Palo Alto

    Condensing tankless heaters drip acidic condensate, often around pH 3 to 4, and it needs to pass through a neutralizer before it reaches a drain. The trap gets cleaned and the media checked. We replace spent media. Where the line runs outside, we check that it is supported and protected.

    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Palo Alto

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. That second exchanger makes acidic condensate. We clear the condensate trap and check the neutralizer media. On A-series models with the built-in recirculation pump, we test the pump and the buffer tank too.

    Yearly Tankless Inspection in Palo Alto

    A yearly inspection costs less than one service call for a dead heater. Gas connections get a leak test, and we work the pressure relief valve. We pull the air filter and check the igniter. Anything that is wearing out goes on a written list with a plain price, and you decide what gets done now and what can wait until next year.
