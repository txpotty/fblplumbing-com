    Hydro Jetting for Drains in Palo Alto

    Jetting does what a cable cannot. Water at around 3,000 to 4,000 PSI scours the whole pipe wall. Cabling opens a path, but jetting strips the buildup off the walls. It works best on a kitchen or main line that keeps clogging every few months no matter how often it gets cabled.

    Bathroom Drain Cleaning in Palo Alto, CA

    Hair mixed with soap scum makes a rope that wraps around the stopper linkage. Most bath clogs sit within a foot of the drain. After removing the stopper and cleaning the trap, a small cable goes into the branch, and if the vent is plugged and the drain still gurgles, we check that too before we pack up.

    Jetting Versus Cabling in Palo Alto, CA

    Cabling and jetting solve different problems. A cable cuts through a blockage. A jetter scrubs the pipe wall clean and flushes the debris downstream to the city main or septic tank, so we cable first on a hard root mass and jet afterward when the line has years of buildup on it.

    Kitchen Drain Cleaning in Palo Alto

    Grease is the big one in kitchens. Hot grease cools in the line and hardens on the pipe wall. Food scraps and soap stick to that layer and the opening keeps shrinking until the sink holds water, and we clear it with a drum machine that is safe for PVC and old cast iron alike.
