    Yes, we offer trenchless water line replacement in Palo Alto where practical. This method minimizes disruption to your landscaping by replacing the old pipe with specialized equipment, often requiring only two small access points. It is an efficient, less invasive option for many properties.

    For most homes, PEX and copper are usually the best materials. PEX is flexible, corrosion-resistant and handles minor ground shifts well.
