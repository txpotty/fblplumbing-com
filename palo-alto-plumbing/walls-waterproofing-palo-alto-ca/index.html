    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Palo Alto, CA

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Copper became standard in many mid-century homes here, and decades later pinhole leaks and corroded fittings are a common repair call. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. A rigid coating on soft brick traps moisture and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with interior drain tile. Old mortar joints get repointed first.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Palo Alto

    Crack injection takes about two hours per crack. We set injection ports every 8 to 12 inches along the crack, seal the surface with epoxy paste, and pump in resin starting at the lowest port and working up until it pushes out the next port. Polyurethane stays flexible if the wall moves a little, and epoxy bonds the two sides back together. The surface paste gets ground off once it cures.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Palo Alto, CA

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head aimed at the house instead of the bed, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. Then we clean the wall, let it dry and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.

    Cost-Effective Waterproofing Solutions in Palo Alto, CA

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Palo Alto

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most dependable way to stop a basement leak, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing to carry the water away from the wall. Many older homes here sit on raised foundations with crawlspaces, which make supply and drain repairs easier to reach than slab work. We backfill after the drain is tested.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Palo Alto

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Corners, niches and valve holes get extra fabric or preformed pieces.
