    Commercial Grease Interceptor Repair in Palo Alto, CA

    Larger commercial establishments often utilize grease interceptors, which are more substantial than standard grease traps. These systems require specialized knowledge for repair, often involving more complex piping and larger capacity tanks. Our technicians are trained in the repair of all types and sizes of commercial grease interceptors, ensuring your high-volume kitchen in Palo Alto, CA remains compliant and functional, regardless of the system's complexity.

    Grease Line Clog Repair in Palo Alto, CA

    Grease that hardens in the lines before or after the trap can cause backups even when the trap itself is clean. We use hydro jetting to clear the pipe and remove buildup. A camera inspection helps us confirm the line is clear and check for damage. If we find broken or sagging pipe, we can repair the section. This keeps water and waste moving freely.

    Lid, Gasket and Riser Repair in Palo Alto, CA

    Damaged lids and failed gaskets let odors escape and create safety hazards. Outdoor risers can crack from vehicle traffic or ground movement. We replace lids, install new gaskets and repair or replace risers. Traffic-rated lids are installed where trucks or cars drive over the interceptor. These repairs keep odors in and keep people safe.

    Damaged Baffle Repair in Palo Alto

    The baffles inside a grease trap are critical for separating FOGs from wastewater. If these baffles are corroded, broken, or dislodged, the trap's efficiency plummets, allowing grease into the sewer system. Our service includes inspecting the condition of all baffles and repairing or replacing them as needed, often using durable, corrosion-resistant materials. Proper baffle function is key to compliance with wastewater regulations in Palo Alto.

    Flow Control Device Repair in Palo Alto

    Hydromechanical traps rely on a flow control fitting to keep water moving at the rated speed. If this fitting is missing or clogged, the trap can be overwhelmed and grease escapes. We inspect, clean or replace flow control devices during repair visits. We also check the air intake that allows the device to work properly. Correct flow control is necessary for a trap to pass inspection.

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Palo Alto

    Baffles slow incoming water so grease can rise and solids can settle. When baffles crack, corrode or break loose, grease passes straight through the trap. We remove damaged baffles and install new ones made for your trap model. In concrete interceptors, we can repair or rebuild baffle walls. Restoring baffles brings back proper separation and helps you stay up to code.
