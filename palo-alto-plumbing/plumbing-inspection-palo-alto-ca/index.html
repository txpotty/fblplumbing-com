    Rental and Commercial Property Inspections in Palo Alto

    Landlords and property managers use our inspections between tenants or before buying an investment property. Every unit gets its own notes.     Water Heater Inspections in Palo Alto

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. We read the serial number for age, look for rust at the fittings and the base, check the venting and test the T and P relief valve. Tankless units get checked for scale. Gas heaters get a look at the flame, the drip leg and the vent draft, since a poorly venting heater is a safety issue.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Palo Alto

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. We also check for warm floor spots. Meter tests, pressure loss checks and a look for damp baseboards come first, and slab homes get acoustic and thermal follow-up. Catching a slab leak early limits damage to floors and foundations.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Palo Alto, CA

    Older homes in Palo Alto often have cast iron or clay sewer pipe that cracks, scales up or lets roots in at the joints. The camera records every defect along with its distance from the cleanout. Then we locate it. With depth and position marked, repair pricing gets accurate, and choosing between a spot fix, a liner or replacement gets easier.

    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Palo Alto, CA

    Wet winters and long dry summers define the plumbing calendar in Northern California, with drainage problems in the rainy season and irrigation leaks in summer. A seasonal check looks at the parts of the system that weather affects most. Outdoor faucets, exposed pipe, irrigation backflow devices and the heater drain and relief valve all get checked. Insulation gets checked too. Fixing a cracked hose bibb or adding insulation is a small job compared to a burst pipe in the wall behind it.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Palo Alto

    Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. We look for crusty aerators, scaled showerheads, slow-filling toilets and sediment in the water heater, and we can run a simple hardness test on the spot. Then we explain your options. A softener, a filter or a scale-reducing system can extend heater and fixture life, and we tell you which one fits the hardness we measured.

    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Palo Alto, CA

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. Too much pressure wears parts. Pressure above 80 psi shortens the life of fixtures, supply hoses and water heaters, and we also identify the supply pipe material, from copper and CPVC to polybutylene, Kitec and galvanized.

    Leak Detection and Assessment in Palo Alto, CA

    Hidden leaks waste water and quietly damage floors and walls. Start at the meter. We shut everything off, check the meter's leak dial and then follow the leak with acoustic equipment and thermal imaging. Catching it early keeps it small. Expansive clay soils are common across Northern California, and seasonal swelling and shrinking can shift slabs and buried sewer laterals.
