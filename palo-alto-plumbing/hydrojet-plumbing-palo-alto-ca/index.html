    Understanding Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Palo Alto, CA

    While snaking can punch a hole through a clog, hydrojetting in Palo Alto, CA provides a complete pipe cleaning. Snaking is effective for simple, localized clogs, but it often leaves behind residual buildup that can lead to recurring problems. Hydrojetting, by contrast, scours the entire interior of the pipe, removing debris, grease, and scale, offering a more long-term solution to your drainage issues.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Palo Alto, CA

    Many older homes in Palo Alto, CA have cast iron drain pipes, which are prone to corrosion and scaling over time. Our hydrojetting service is carefully calibrated to clean cast iron pipes without causing damage, removing rust, scale, and other mineral deposits that restrict flow. This extends the life of these older pipes and improves drainage noticeably.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Palo Alto, CA

    Snaking a main line usually costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 including camera inspection. Commercial lines and long runs can cost $800 to $2,000 or more. Jetting costs more up front, but it often prevents repeat calls that add up over a year. We help you decide which method suits your line and budget.
