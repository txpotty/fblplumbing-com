    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Palo Alto, CA

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. All three are code-listed for drinking water. Pipe, fittings and tools come from the same maker on our jobs, which keeps the warranty valid.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Palo Alto

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool plus a ring slipped over the pipe end. We stretch the pipe and ring, seat the fitting, and within seconds the plastic shrinks back around the barb into a tight seal. No crimp tool to calibrate. Because the fittings are wide inside, each joint costs very little flow.
