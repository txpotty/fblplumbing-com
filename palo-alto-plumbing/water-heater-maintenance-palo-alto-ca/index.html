    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Maintenance in Palo Alto

    The cost of water heater maintenance in Palo Alto, CA can range from $120 to $250, based on the type of water heater (tank or tankless) and the services required. This usually includes a full flush, anode rod inspection, and safety checks. More complex issues or parts replacement would be an additional cost.

    Many homeowners can do a basic flush, but older drain valves can break or leak. A pro also tests safety valves, venting and gas connections.

    The anode rod is a metal rod inside your water heater that sacrifices itself to keep the steel tank from corroding. It attracts corrosive elements in the water. Regular inspection and replacement of a depleted anode rod are crucial for extending the lifespan of your water heater, particularly where water quality varies.

    While some basic tasks like flushing the tank might seem DIY-friendly, professional maintenance makes sure all critical components, such as the anode rod, pressure relief valve, and heating elements, are properly inspected. Incorrect DIY methods can damage your unit or void warranties.

    During a water heater flush, we drain the tank completely and then refill it, letting water flow out through the drain valve until it runs clear. This process removes mineral deposits and sediment that accumulate at the bottom of the tank, which can hurt efficiency and cause damage.

    Most manufacturers and plumbing experts recommend annual maintenance for your water heater. In homes with harder water, flushing every 6 to 12 months can be helpful to prevent sediment buildup.

    Most tank flushes and inspections take about 45 to 90 minutes. Tankless descaling usually takes 1 to 2 hours.
