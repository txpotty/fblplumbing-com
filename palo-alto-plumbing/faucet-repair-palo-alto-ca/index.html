    Compression Faucet Repair in Palo Alto

    Two-handle faucets with separate hot and cold handles are often compression faucets, which seal by pressing a rubber washer down onto a brass seat inside the valve. Washers harden and seats get pitted. New washers go in and the seat gets dressed or replaced with a seat wrench, and the packing gets redone if the stem leaks.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Palo Alto

    Sputtering flow, where the water spits and surges, often means air or debris in the line after a water main repair, or a partly clogged cartridge screen. A flush of the lines usually fixes it. On pull-down faucets, the check valves in the hose can stick too, and we clean or replace those.

    Emergency Faucet Repair in Palo Alto

    A faucet that will not shut off, or one with a cracked body spraying under the sink, needs the water off before anything else happens. Close the angle stops under the sink. Should those stops be stuck, the house main shutoff is the backup. We come fast for these.

    Low Water Pressure Faucet Fix in Palo Alto, CA

    Weak flow at one faucet usually points to a clogged aerator or a cartridge packed with scale, not a problem with the house pressure.     Cartridge Faucet Repair in Palo Alto, CA

    Ball Faucet Repair in Palo Alto, CA

    Ball faucets have a single lever over a domed cap, and the drip usually comes from the two small rubber seats and springs that press against the ball.     Cracked Faucet Body Repair in Palo Alto
