    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Palo Alto

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. We replace them with new coated stainless connectors sized for the appliance and add an accessible shutoff valve if the old one is missing or seized. This is a quick fix. Furnaces, water heaters, ranges and dryers each need a sound connection, and most need a drip leg before the appliance valve.

    Gas Leak Detection in Palo Alto

    A rotten egg smell, hissing near a line, dead grass in a stripe across the yard or a gas meter that moves with everything off are the common leak signs. Trust your nose. Our plumbers isolate the system section by section, sweep it with an electronic detector and confirm the exact spot with soap solution. Once we find it, you get a written repair price before work starts.

    Gas Appliance Line Installation in Palo Alto, CA

    A new range, dryer or water heater needs a gas line sized for its BTU rating and the length of the run. Undersized lines starve burners. We install the branch line, add an accessible shutoff valve and a sediment trap where the code calls for one, then connect the appliance with a new coated flexible connector. If the existing appliance line is leaking or damaged, we fix it on the same visit.

    Gas Line Extensions and Relocations in Palo Alto

    Adding a gas fireplace, moving a laundry room or switching a range from electric to gas usually means extending the gas line. Sizing comes first. Our plumbers total the BTU load, measure the run and size the pipe so the last appliance on the line still gets full pressure at peak use. After that comes routing, the tie-in and the test.

    Buried Yard Line Repair and Replacement in Palo Alto, CA

    Many older homes have steel gas lines running from the meter to the house or out to a pool heater. The wrap fails eventually. Once the coating cracks, the pipe starts to corrode, and wet soil from sprinklers or a low spot in the yard speeds that up a great deal. We call 811 before digging. We lay polyethylene with tracer wire or coated steel where exposed, then backfill and clean up after the test passes.

    CSST Repair in Palo Alto

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing is a flexible gas pipe with a plastic jacket, often yellow, that runs through attics and walls in newer homes. It can be damaged. Nails from a remodel, rodents chewing the jacket and nearby lightning strikes are the usual causes, and a damaged run has to be cut out and spliced with the manufacturer's own fittings. We check bonding on every CSST job. A bonding clamp and conductor tie the CSST to the home's grounding system, following the maker's instructions and code.

    Gas Permits and Inspections in Palo Alto

    Repairs that change piping, and all new gas lines, usually need a permit and inspection from Palo Alto building officials. We pull the permit, book the inspection and meet the inspector at the gauge so you do not have to. Inspectors often come within the week. Once it passes, the utility can restore service and we come back to relight appliances if needed.

    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Palo Alto, CA

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Vibration and settling loosen threaded joints over time, and pipe in damp garages or outside walls can rust through from the surface. Bad sections get replaced with new pipe. Rusted pipe that runs outside gets painted or replaced with coated steel so it does not come back as the same problem.
