    Water Meter Leak Testing in Palo Alto

    The water meter is the first and simplest leak test. With every fixture and appliance off, a moving leak indicator means water is escaping somewhere. We read the flow rate to estimate how many gallons are being lost. Then we shut off zones one at a time to see which part of the property is losing water.

    Pinhole Leak Detection in Palo Alto, CA

    Pinhole leaks, often found in copper or PEX piping, are small but can cause serious damage over time, especially behind walls or under floors. We use non-invasive moisture meters to detect elevated humidity levels and thermal cameras to identify cold spots that indicate water. Our plumbers are adept at tracing these elusive leaks without tearing apart your entire home. Swift action on pinhole leaks prevents extensive drywall damage, mold growth and compromised structural integrity. Inland valleys get hot summers that work water heaters and exterior lines hard, while coastal areas stay mild and damp year round.

    Hidden Wall & Ceiling Leak Detection in Palo Alto, CA

    Leaks behind walls or in ceilings can cause serious damage before they become visible, leading to stained drywall, peeling paint or a musty odor. These often stem from faulty pipe connections, old galvanized pipes or issues with shower pans and drains. Our technicians use moisture meters and infrared cameras to scan these areas, identifying elevated moisture levels and temperature differences that indicate a hidden leak, minimizing the need for extensive wall demolition.

    Preventative Leak Detection Audits in Palo Alto, CA

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Palo Alto

    Leaks in underground water lines, from your meter to your home, can go unnoticed for extended periods, wasting water and causing yard erosion. Signs include unusually green patches in your lawn, persistent wet spots or a constantly running water meter. FBL Plumbing uses ground-penetrating radar and tracer gas detection methods to accurately map out and pinpoint leaks in buried pipes, allowing for targeted excavation and repair rather than digging up your entire yard.
