    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Turlock, CA

    Rubber washing machine hoses burst. We swap them for braided stainless hoses, put a drain pan under the washer piped to a floor drain where one is available, and install a quarter-turn shutoff or an automatic leak valve on the supply box. Water heaters get a pan too. The wall behind them can take a moisture-resistant board and sealer.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Turlock

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, lapped 12 inches at the seams and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and fasten it with termination bar. Cheap fix, big difference.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Turlock

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most reliable fix for a leaking basement, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing so water has somewhere to go. Expansive clay soils are common across Northern California, and seasonal swelling and shrinking can crack slabs and buried sewer laterals. Backfill goes in last.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Turlock, CA

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and run it to a sump basin so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. The difference is that the water no longer ends up on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and works where a driveway or deck blocks the wall.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Turlock, CA

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Wet winters and long dry summers define the plumbing calendar in Northern California, with drainage problems in the rainy season and outdoor leaks in summer. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. The musty smell usually fades within weeks. The weep holes empty into interior drain tile when there is one.

    Commercial Wall Waterproofing in Turlock

    Restaurant kitchens, gym locker rooms and salon wash stations put water on walls all day.     Foundation Crack Repair in Turlock

    Crack injection takes about two hours per crack. We set injection ports every 8 to 12 inches along the crack, seal the surface with epoxy paste, and pump in resin starting at the lowest port and working up until it pushes out the next port. Polyurethane stays flexible if the wall moves a little, and epoxy bonds the two sides back together. The surface paste gets ground off once it cures.
