    Backflow Enclosures and Freeze Protection in Turlock, CA

    Above-ground assemblies sit in direct sun in summer and can freeze on cold nights. Even in mild areas, wrapping irrigation backflow devices before a frosty night is cheap insurance against a surprise leak. We install insulated enclosures, freeze blankets or heated cabinets based on your location. Enclosures also discourage theft, since brass backflow assemblies are a common target for scrap thieves. We choose enclosures with enough room for testing and repairs.

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Turlock, CA

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Turlock, CA, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a popular and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers expertly install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also check for proper clearance and drainage.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Turlock

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules permit. We size them to avoid too much pressure loss on your system.

    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Turlock, CA

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested easily. We carry trusted brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.
