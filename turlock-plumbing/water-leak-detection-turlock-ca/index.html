    Slab Leak Detection in Turlock

    Slab leaks are common in homes on shifting soil and moving foundations. FBL Plumbing uses specialized acoustic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to precisely locate these hidden leaks without destructive guesswork. Early detection is key to preventing major foundation damage.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Turlock

    Leaks in underground water lines, from your meter to your home, can go unnoticed for extended periods, wasting water and causing yard erosion.
