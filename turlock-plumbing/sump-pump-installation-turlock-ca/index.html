    A submersible sump pump sits entirely inside the sump pit, submerged in water, making it quieter and taking up less space. A pedestal sump pump has its motor mounted on a shaft above the pit, making it easier to reach for maintenance and usually more affordable. Both types are effective, and our plumbers can help you choose the best fit for your Turlock basement.

    Signs you might need a new sump pump include unusual noises during operation, frequent cycling, visible rust or corrosion, or a pump that runs continuously without much water.
