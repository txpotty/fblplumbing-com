    Tankless Water Heater Repair in Turlock

    Scale is the main enemy of a tankless heater. It coats the heat exchanger and trips the unit into an error code. We descale with a pump and approved solution, clean the inlet filter and check the igniter and flow sensor while the unit is open. All major brands.

    Gas Control and Thermocouple Repair in Turlock

    Thermocouples, flame sensors, igniters and gas control valves cause most gas heater failures. Each part gets tested, the burner gets cleaned and the vent gets a draft check. A pilot that lights and then goes out a few seconds later usually points to a weak thermocouple or a clogged flame arrestor screen. Most are done in under two hours.

    Leaking Water Heater Repair in Turlock, CA

    Not every leak means a dead tank. Drips from the relief valve, the drain valve or a supply connection are repairable for a modest cost. A leak from the tank body or the bottom seam usually means the inner lining has failed, and that tank needs to be replaced soon. We protect your floors either way.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Turlock, CA

    The anode rod sacrifices itself so the steel tank does not rust. In hard water it can be eaten away within a few years. After pulling the old rod, we fit a magnesium, aluminum or powered anode to suit your water and any smell. Rotten-egg odor often clears up.

    Electric Water Heater Element Repair in Turlock

    Lukewarm water or hot water that runs out in five minutes usually means a burned-out element or a stuck thermostat. We check each element with an ohmmeter. A shorted element will trip the breaker, while an open one just sits there cold, and the readings tell us which one is which. Common elements and thermostats are stocked.

    Water Heater Leak Repair in Turlock, CA

    Water on the floor next to the heater needs a closer look before anyone panics. Leaks at the relief valve or a supply fitting are usually cheap to fix. Tank body leaks are different, because a failed inner lining cannot be patched. A drain pan helps on a slab.

    Tankless Unit Repair in Turlock

    Navien, Rinnai, Noritz and Rheem tankless units show error codes on the front panel that help us narrow the problem quickly. Scale, a dirty inlet screen, a bad flow sensor or a weak igniter cause most failures. Descaling uses a small pump and approved solution. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe.

    Gas Water Heater Repair in Turlock, CA

    Gas units add a burner, a pilot or igniter and a gas control valve to the list of parts that can fail. The thermocouple and the control valve cause most no-heat calls. We leak test each gas connection after the repair. Draft at the vent gets checked too.
