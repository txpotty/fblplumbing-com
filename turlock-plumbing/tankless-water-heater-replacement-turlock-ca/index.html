    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Turlock

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by recovering heat from exhaust gases. They vent through inexpensive PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Inland valleys get hot summers that work water heaters and outdoor lines hard, while coastal areas stay mild and damp year round. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth the price.
