    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Turlock

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. Over decades it goes oval and blisters. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, and once the camera shows failures along the whole run, patching it stops being worth the money. We replace these lines with PVC. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Turlock

    Clay and cast iron crack from settling soil, tree roots and vehicles parked over the line. The crack lets soil in and sewage out. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded stainless couplings rated for the transition between materials, then test the repair with water and video before closing the hole. Most of these are one-day jobs.
