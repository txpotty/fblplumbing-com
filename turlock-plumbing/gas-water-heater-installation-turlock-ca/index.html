    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Turlock, CA

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through PVC or other approved pipe. Without needing a chimney, the vent can exit through a side wall within the allowed length. They need an outlet. We terminate the vent to spec.

    Propane Water Heater Installation in Turlock

    Propane homes need heaters set up for propane, not natural gas. Propane runs at a different pressure and needs a smaller burner orifice, so we install propane-specific models or convert units with the manufacturer kit where the manual allows it. We call the propane company when needed. We test all fittings.

    Tankless Gas Water Heater Conversion in Turlock

    Converting from a tank to tankless is usually more than a swap. Most conversions need a bigger gas line from the meter, a new vent through the wall or roof, a condensate drain and isolation valves for future descaling. It takes most of a day. We do all of it to code.

    Cost-Effective Gas Water Heater Upgrades in Turlock, CA

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in Turlock, CA

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Condensing units vent through PVC and need a condensate line, so placement and install work differ from a standard tank. Energy Star models qualify for some rebates.
