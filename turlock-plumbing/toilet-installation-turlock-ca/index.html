    Kinds of Toilet Installation in Turlock, CA

    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Turlock

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. Below about 20 psi, they flush poorly. Before suggesting one, we put a gauge on a hose bib, and we mention the noise, because the flush carries through the whole house. They rarely clog.

    Smart Toilet Installation in Turlock, CA

    Smart toilets with heated seats and built-in bidets need two things the old toilet did not: power and a dedicated water connection. Most require a GFCI outlet within reach of the cord. The water connection and mounting are ours, and when an outlet has to be added we schedule a licensed electrician instead of doing that wiring. Then we test every button.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Turlock, CA

    Wall-hung toilets need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep, or a furred-out chase built in front of an existing 2 by 4 wall. Floors stay clear underneath. That makes mopping easier and lets you set the bowl height anywhere within the carrier's range, which is handy for tall households or anyone who wants a comfort-height seat without a tall bowl. Service happens through the flush plate.

    Smart Toilet and Bidet Seat Installation in Turlock

    Bidet seats like the TOTO Washlet and Bio Bidet bolt onto most existing elongated bowls. Water comes from a T-valve at the fill line. Heated seats with a warm-air dryer need a nearby GFCI outlet, and when there is none we bring in an electrician first. Non-electric seats take about thirty minutes.

    Commercial Restroom Toilets in Turlock, CA

    Restaurants and offices get heavy-duty fixtures and accessible stalls built to code. Downtime costs a business money. We schedule commercial installs early in the morning or after hours, and we stage the fixtures and valves on site so each stall is down for as short a time as possible. We work with your GC on inspections.

    Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in Turlock, CA

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. Cast iron flanges rust through at the bolt slots. PVC and ABS flanges crack when someone overtightens the bolts, and a repair ring screwed over the top is often all it takes to restore a solid anchor. Seal goes on last.

    Customer-Supplied Toilet Installation in Turlock, CA

    Bought the toilet yourself? First, look at the rough-in printed on the carton. Before the old toilet comes out, we inspect the new one for shipping cracks, because cracked china goes back to the store, not onto your flange. We bring the seal and stainless bolts.
