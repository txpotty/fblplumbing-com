    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Turlock

    Residential hydro jetting in Turlock typically costs $350 to $800, depending on line length, access and how heavy the buildup is. Commercial jetting is often priced by the hour and may run higher.

    While hydro jetting is very powerful, it is usually safe for most sound plumbing systems, including PVC, cast iron, and concrete pipes.     While hydro jetting effectively removes existing tree roots from your sewer line, it does not prevent new roots from growing back. For a long-term solution to recurring root intrusion, we might recommend trenchless sewer repair or pipe replacement, which we can go over during our assessment.

    No permit is needed for jetting. If the inspection reveals damage that needs repair, permits may be required and we handle them.
