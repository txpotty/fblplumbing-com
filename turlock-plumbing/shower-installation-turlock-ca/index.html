    How We Install Showers in Turlock, CA

    Walk-In Shower Installation in Turlock

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. With no curb, the floor needs a steady quarter-inch-per-foot pitch to the drain and waterproofing that extends past the opening. We set that slope. We add blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare.

    Shower Pan Installation in Turlock

    Shower pans fail quietly. A pan with a pinhole in the liner or a poorly clamped drain will hold water for years while it slowly soaks the subfloor, so we flood-test every tiled pan for 24 hours before tile goes down. Prefab pans get a level check. Either way, the slope runs to the drain.

    Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Turlock, CA

    Accessible showers start with blocking in the wall. Blocking goes in for grab bars, the valve goes where a seated person can reach it and a handheld goes on a slide bar. Thresholds stay low or flat. We follow ADA guidelines when you ask.

    Custom Shower System Installation in Turlock, CA

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. One head works fine off a standard valve, while multiple outlets need a thermostatic valve, volume controls and larger 3/4-inch supply lines. We plan the layout first. The water heater gets checked for capacity too.

    Gym and Locker Room Showers in Turlock

    Gym showers run back to back from open to close. Commercial valves with metered or push-button controls keep run times in check, and the drains are sized for heavy use. ADA stalls get handheld units. We phase the work to keep some showers running.

    Shower Valve and Trim Installation in Turlock, CA

    The valve sits behind the tile for decades. We install pressure-balancing, thermostatic and diverter valves, set to the depth your trim kit needs and plumbed with integral stops when the valve has them. Trim goes on after tile. That timing keeps the trim from getting scratched or caked with thinset while the tile is set.

    Custom Shower Enclosure Plumbing in Turlock

    Body sprays and steam units add lines and planning. Each spray needs its own supply, and the valve, the diverter and the pipe sizing all have to work together so the last spray in the line still gets decent pressure. Older neighborhoods may have lower pressure. A gauge reading comes first.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Turlock, CA

    Prefab units are the fastest route to a new shower. We set the base on mortar, connect the drain, install the valve and trim and make sure the walls are fastened so nothing flexes when someone leans on them. They suit guest baths well. Usually a one-day job.
