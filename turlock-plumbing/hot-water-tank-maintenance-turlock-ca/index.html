    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Turlock

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause loud popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution rated for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Turlock

    The cost for hot water tank maintenance in Turlock, CA typically ranges from $100 to $250, depending on the tank type, age and specific services required like anode rod replacement. We provide a detailed quote before any work starts.

    FBL Plumbing services all major brands of hot water tanks, including but not limited to Rheem, Bradford White, A. O. Smith, State, Noritz and Bosch. Our technicians are familiar with a wide range of models and their specific maintenance needs.

    FBL Plumbing stands behind our work. While maintenance services usually don't have long-term warranties like new installations, we guarantee our workmanship for a specific period. Any parts replaced during maintenance will carry their manufacturer's warranty.

    Watch for slower recovery, rusty or cloudy hot water, noises, moisture around the base or a relief valve that drips. Any of these means it is time for an inspection.

    A typical hot water tank maintenance service usually takes about 1 to 2 hours. This includes draining, flushing, inspection and any minor adjustments or part replacements like an anode rod.

    Many homeowners can do a basic drain with a garden hose. The risk is a plastic drain valve that breaks or will not reseal, and DIY flushes seldom include an anode check or relief valve test.

    In mineral-heavy water a magnesium rod may last three to five years. We measure it at every visit so you replace it before the tank lining starts to rust.

    The anode rod, also known as a sacrificial rod, is a metal rod inside your hot water tank designed to corrode before the tank itself. It protects the tank lining from rust and corrosion, which matters most when water carries a lot of minerals, and it extends the lifespan of your unit.
