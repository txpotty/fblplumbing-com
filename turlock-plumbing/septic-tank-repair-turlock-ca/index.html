    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Turlock, CA

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones get eaten by sulfuric acid from sewer gas and eventually fall off into the tank. With no outlet baffle, sludge flows right out to the leach lines. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Effluent Pump Replacement in Turlock, CA

    Effluent pumps in a dosing chamber last roughly 8 to 15 years, and they usually quit without much warning at all. Humming with no flow points to a jammed impeller or a failed capacitor. We pull it and test the discharge line before setting a new one. Floats get replaced at the same time.

    Drain Field Repair in Turlock, CA

    Green stripes over the trenches and a wet lawn in dry weather are the classic signs of a field in trouble. Most of the time it is biomat, a slimy layer that seals the trench bottom. Compaction and hydraulic overload caused by a leaking fixture are the other usual causes. Testing always comes first. Resting or jetting can bring a field back.

    Septic Line Clog Clearing in Turlock

    A slow toilet and a gurgle in the tub usually point to a clog between the house and the tank. We run a camera down the line first, then clear it with a jetter set up for a 4-inch building sewer. Grease and wipes cause most of these, and the fix after clearing depends on which one we pull out. Roots mean a joint has opened.

    Camera Inspection of Septic Baffles in Turlock, CA

    Some tanks have no riser, so we run a push camera through the small inspection port to see the baffles. That saves digging up the whole lid. If the outlet tee is cracked or missing, we dig at that end only, then install a PVC tee with a filter housing. Concrete lids get a riser while they are open.
