    Simple replacements usually do not need a permit. Grease trap installs and drain relocations often do, and we take care of the permit when required.

    If your garbage disposal is more than 8 to 10 years old, frequently jams, or requires expensive parts for repair, replacement is often the more cost-effective long-term solution. Newer models are also more energy-efficient and less noisy.

    To prevent kitchen drain clogs, avoid pouring grease or oil down the drain, use a drain strainer to catch food particles, and run cold water while operating your garbage disposal. Occasionally flush your drain with hot water and a bit of dish soap. These habits can greatly reduce the risk of blockages in your Berkeley home.

    Persistent kitchen sink clogs are often caused by a buildup of grease, food particles, soap scum, and hair in the drain line. Hard water can also contribute to mineral deposits that narrow pipes. Using chemical drain cleaners can sometimes worsen the problem or damage pipes.

    Watch for slow draining sinks, foul odors coming from the drain, gurgling sounds, standing water in the sink, leaks under the sink, or a garbage disposal that hums but doesn't grind. Any of these could point to a plumbing issue that needs professional attention to prevent further damage.
