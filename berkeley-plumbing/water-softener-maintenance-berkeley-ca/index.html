    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Berkeley

    Softener resin can foul with iron, manganese or sediment and lose capacity over time. Chlorine and chloramine in city water also slowly break resin down. We run a resin cleaner through the system and test performance afterward. If the resin is exhausted, we rebed the tank with fresh high-capacity resin, which costs far less than a new system.

    Control Valve and Injector Service in Berkeley, CA

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a complete regeneration every time.

    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repair in Berkeley, CA

    A leaking bypass valve can let hard water slip past the softener without you noticing. We test and rebuild bypass valves and replace cracked fittings. We also inspect the drain line for kinks and proper air gap and the brine overflow line for correct routing. These small fixes head off leaks and backflow problems.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Berkeley

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, dictating regeneration cycles and water flow. Our maintenance service includes complete diagnostics of the control valve's programming and mechanical components. We check for proper timing, motor function and potential blockages. If issues are found, we can often repair or replace worn parts to restore full function, preventing the need for a costly full system replacement in Berkeley.

    Commercial Softener Maintenance in Berkeley

    Restaurants, laundromats, salons and car washes rely on soft water to protect equipment like dishwashers, ice machines and boilers. Commercial twin-tank systems regenerate often and need closer attention. We set up scheduled visits to test hardness, clean brine tanks and service valves. Consistent maintenance keeps equipment warranties valid and lowers repair costs.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Maintenance in Berkeley

    The cost of water softener maintenance in Berkeley, CA generally ranges from $100 to $250 for a standard service. This can vary based on the system's age, brand and any specific repairs needed. For a precise quote, please contact FBL Plumbing to assess your unit.

    A salt bridge is a hard crust that forms in the brine tank, preventing salt from dissolving into the water. This means the system can't regenerate properly, leading to hard water. Our maintenance service includes breaking up and removing salt bridges, bringing back your softener's function.

    You can add salt, break small bridges and run manual regenerations. Valve rebuilds, injector cleaning and resin work are best left to a plumber with the right parts.

    Resin often lasts 10 to 15 years, but chlorinated city water and iron can shorten that. Fine resin beads in your faucet aerators can point to resin breakdown.

    A standard water softener maintenance service usually takes about 1 to 2 hours. This can vary depending on the system's condition, age and whether any unexpected issues are discovered during the inspection.
