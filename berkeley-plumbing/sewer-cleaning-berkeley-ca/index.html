    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Cleaning in Berkeley

    The cost of sewer cleaning in Berkeley can range from $250 to $700, depending on the severity of the clog, the method used, such as snaking or hydro jetting, and whether a camera inspection is required. More complex issues like heavy root intrusion or main line blockages will be at the higher end of this range.

    Cleaning does not require a permit. Installing a cleanout or repairing the sewer usually does, and we handle that paperwork.

    Multiple slow drains, toilets that gurgle when a sink drains, and water coming up in the tub or shower are common signs. Sewer odors in the yard or a soggy spot near the cleanout can also point to a problem.

    Most standard sewer cleaning jobs in Berkeley can be finished within 1 to 3 hours, depending on the severity and nature of the clog. More complex issues, like extensive root removal or a needed camera inspection, might take longer. We will provide an expected timeline during our initial assessment.

    Homes with no issues may never need it. Lines with known roots or grease buildup often benefit from cleaning every 12 to 24 months.

    For standard sewer cleaning, such as snaking or hydro jetting to clear a blockage, a permit is usually not required in Berkeley. However, if the cleaning uncovers a need for major sewer line repair or replacement, then permits from the local building department would be necessary. We handle all necessary permits for larger jobs.

    Drain cleaning usually refers to clearing clogs in smaller branch lines leading from individual fixtures, like sinks or tubs. Sewer cleaning, on the other hand, addresses blockages in the main sewer line that collects wastewater from all household drains and carries it to the municipal system. Sewer clogs affect all drains, while drain clogs are often isolated.

    Generally, standard home insurance policies do not cover routine sewer cleaning or damage from gradual wear and tear. However, if a sudden, unexpected event causes sewer damage, it might be covered. It is best to check with your insurance provider directly.
