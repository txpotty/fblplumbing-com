    Jetting Equipment We Use in Berkeley, CA

    Different lines need different machines. Small-line jetters for branch drains and larger units up to 4,000 PSI for sewers cover most jobs. Nozzles make the difference. Penetrating heads bore through packed clogs, rear-jet heads scrub walls, and spinning cutters handle roots.

    Jetting Versus Snaking Costs in Berkeley, CA

    Jetting costs more per visit than a cable. Three or four cable calls a year usually cost more than one jetting visit that lasts. For a single hair clog, a cable is fine. We quote both on site.

    Jetting Hard Water Scale in Berkeley

    Galvanized and cast iron drains collect mineral scale. Rough scale catches hair and grease on every flush, so clogs return faster year after year. A scale nozzle helps. Before and after camera footage shows how much pipe wall is left.

    Grease Line Jetting in Berkeley, CA

    Grease turns hard once it cools. The line between the sinks and the interceptor gets packed with cooled grease until floor sinks overflow. We often jet with hot water. Regular jetting keeps the kitchen open and the interceptor working the way it should.

    Commercial Drain Jetting in Berkeley, CA

    Commercial drains carry heavier loads. Restaurants pour grease, food fines and hot water down prep sinks and floor sinks all day, which coats branch lines faster than any home kitchen ever could. Bigger jetters handle this. Most commercial work gets done before opening or after close so nobody loses a service.

    Sewer Line Jetting in Berkeley

    A plugged main backs up the lowest drain first. We run a larger jetter, up to 4,000 PSI at a higher flow, from the outdoor cleanout toward the city main to cut roots and flush out sludge and paper. Root masses take a spinning cutter. We follow with a camera to find the joint the roots came in through.

    Floor Drain and Utility Sink Jetting in Berkeley, CA

    Garage floor drains get forgotten. Sand, paint rinse and dirt settle in the trap and line until a big rinse floods the floor. We jet both. We also check the trap seal or primer to keep sewer gas out.

    Restaurant Grease Line Jetting in Berkeley

    Floor sinks backing up during service shut a kitchen down. Grease solidifies in the line between the dish area and the interceptor, and once it narrows the pipe enough, every pot wash sends dirty water across the floor. Hot water jetting breaks it loose. Clean lines also help with health inspections.
