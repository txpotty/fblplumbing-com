    ADA-Compliant Toilet Installation in Berkeley

    ADA toilets put the seat 17 to 19 inches off the floor, and the centerline sits 16 to 18 inches from the side wall. That spacing leaves room for a grab bar.     Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Berkeley, CA

    Comfort height bowls stand about two inches taller than standard ones. Consider who uses the bathroom first.
