    For extensive walls waterproofing projects involving excavation, structural changes, or major changes to drainage systems, a permit from the city of Berkeley may be required.     The duration of a walls waterproofing project varies significantly.     FBL Plumbing works with trusted manufacturers known for high-quality waterproofing products.     Look for bubbling paint, soft drywall, stained baseboards and musty odors. White crust on block or stucco walls is another sign of moisture moving through. Loose tiles or cracked grout in a shower also suggest water is getting behind.

    A shower wall waterproofing project usually takes two to four days, including drying time between coats. Exterior block sealing can often be completed in a day. Larger foundation jobs may take a few days.

    Interior waterproofing addresses water after it has entered the foundation, typically managing it with sealants and drainage systems inside the basement or crawl space.
