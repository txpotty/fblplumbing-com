    Full service line replacement typically requires a plumbing permit and inspection from your city or county. Small spot repairs often do not. We pull the permit and schedule the inspection when needed.

    Common signs include a wet or unusually green patch of yard, a hissing sound near the meter, low pressure at every fixture and a water bill that climbs without explanation. Dirty or rusty water can also point to a damaged line. If your meter moves with all fixtures off, you likely have a leak.

    Water lines can leak or break due to several factors, including aging pipes (corrosion in galvanized steel or copper), shifting soil, tree root intrusion, high water pressure, ground freezing and thawing cycles, and accidental damage during excavation.     In most communities the water provider owns the meter and the pipe on the street side of it. The homeowner is responsible for the service line from the meter to the house. We check which side the leak is on before quoting you.
