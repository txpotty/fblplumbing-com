    What Goes Into a Well Tank Swap in Berkeley, CA

    Galvanized Steel Pressure Tank Upgrades in Berkeley

    Old galvanized tanks have no bladder at all. Air and water share the same space. Over time the water absorbs the air, the tank waterlogs, and someone has to drain and recharge it every few months. A sealed bladder tank ends that chore.

    Preventative Well Pressure Tank Maintenance in Berkeley, CA

    Once a year, test the pre-charge. Shut off the pump, drain the system to zero, and put a tire gauge on the valve at the top of the tank, topping it up to two psi below the cut-in if it reads low. Water out of the valve means a torn bladder.

    Pressure Tanks for Well Houses and Barns in Berkeley, CA

    Tanks in outbuildings face heat, cold and damp. Steel rusts fast in a damp well house. We often recommend a composite tank in those spaces, raise it off the floor on a stand, and insulate exposed piping and the switch when the building sees freezing weather. Simple protection.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Berkeley, CA

    Switch contacts pit and burn over time. Worn contacts can fail to open or close. When we swap a tank we look at the contacts and the sensing port underneath, since a new tank paired with a tired switch is asking for trouble. Cheap part.

    Same-Day Tank Swaps in Berkeley

    We keep common tank sizes on our trucks. That means a call in the morning can often end with a new tank installed by afternoon, including new tank tee, gauge and relief valve, without waiting on a parts order. Bigger tanks may take a day to order.

    Whole-House Tank Replacement on Older Wells in Berkeley

    Older wells sometimes have a tank, switch and plumbing that were all installed together decades ago. A new tank can reveal tired fittings. We look at the whole tank area, from the check valve to the house shutoff, and give you options for doing the tank alone or the tank plus its fittings. Your call.

    Emergency Pressure Tank Replacement in Berkeley, CA

    If the pump is running nonstop or the taps are dry, call (669) 319-2034. A ruptured tank or a stuck switch can burn out a pump, and we work to get you running before the pump is damaged. Turn off the breaker first.

    Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Berkeley

    Flex-Lite tanks have a composite shell. Rust is not a concern. That makes them a good pick for damp basements, crawlspaces and well houses where steel tanks tend to corrode from the bottom up, and they are lighter to carry into tight spaces. Same plumbing as steel.
