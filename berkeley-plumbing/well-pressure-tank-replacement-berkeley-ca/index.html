    Well Pressure Tank Leak Repair vs. Replacement in Berkeley

    When your well pressure tank shows signs of trouble, it is important to determine if a repair is feasible or if full replacement is necessary. Small air leaks or issues with the air valve might be repairable. However, if the tank itself is corroded, has a ruptured diaphragm, or is waterlogged, replacement is almost always the more cost-effective and reliable option. Our technicians in Berkeley provide an straightforward assessment, explaining the pros and cons of repair versus a complete well pressure tank replacement, so you can make an informed decision.

    Diaphragm Pressure Tank Replacement in Berkeley, CA

    Diaphragm tanks like the Amtrol Well-X-Trol use a fixed membrane that separates air above from water below. They are tough, but the steel shell can rust through at the base after years of condensation in a hot, damp equipment space. We replace corroded diaphragm tanks and check the stand and fittings under them. The new tank is leveled and supported so it does not stress the piping. This restores steady pressure to every faucet.

    Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Berkeley

    Flex-Lite composite pressure tanks are an excellent option for those seeking a lightweight, corrosion-resistant alternative to traditional steel tanks. They are particularly well-suited for homes with aggressive water conditions that might accelerate corrosion in metal tanks. FBL Plumbing expertly replaces old or failing tanks with Flex-Lite models, ensuring a precise fit and proper connection to your existing well system. Their durable, fiberglass-wound construction provides long-lasting, trouble-free operation.

    Galvanized Steel Pressure Tank Upgrades in Berkeley

    Many older homes in Berkeley still have traditional galvanized steel pressure tanks, which can corrode over time and lose their air charge. This not only improves water pressure stability but also extends the life of your well pump by reducing short-cycling.
