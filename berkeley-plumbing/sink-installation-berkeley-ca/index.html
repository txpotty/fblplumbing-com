    Drop-In Sink Installation in Berkeley, CA

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style.     Double Vanity Sink Installation in Berkeley

    A second bowl means a second drain and two more supplies.     Undermount Sink Installation in Berkeley

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. The bowl hangs on clips with a full bead of silicone, and it stays braced overnight while the adhesive cures. Glue alone is not enough. Plumbing goes on the next day.

    Undermount Clips and Cure Time in Berkeley

    Undermount bowls need overnight to cure. The bowl stays braced until our return. Plumbing goes in on the second visit.
