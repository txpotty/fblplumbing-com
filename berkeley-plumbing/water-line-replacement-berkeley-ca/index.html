    Copper Water Line Installation in Berkeley

    Type K copper is the thick-wall grade made for burial. Street pressure does not bother it. We bed it in sand, keep it away from sharp rock, and sleeve it where it passes through a foundation wall, because concrete and copper rubbing together for twenty years will wear through the pipe. It costs more than PEX.

    PEX Water Line Installation in Berkeley, CA

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house. Mountain and foothill homes in Northern California see real winters, so pipes there need insulation, heat tape, or careful draining when a cabin sits empty. A frozen PEX line often survives where copper would crack. We still bury it below the frost depth the local code sets, and keep it covered from sunlight at the meter box, since UV light breaks it down. Short exposure only.

    Type L vs. Type K Copper in Berkeley, CA

    The thickest wall belongs to Type K, the usual choice underground. Type L is lighter and mostly goes indoors. Some codes allow L for burial, but we stick with K for service lines because a thicker wall buys more years against stone contact and soil chemistry. Same fittings, different pipe.

    Commercial Water Line Services in Berkeley, CA

    Commercial services run bigger, often 1-1/2 to 2 inches, and almost always have a backflow assembly that needs testing after any work. We schedule the shutdown around your hours. For a restaurant or an office building that might mean an early morning start, temporary water to the restrooms, and a call to the utility about their side of the meter. Tenants get notice.

    Water Line Upgrades and Code Compliance in Berkeley, CA

    A lot of older houses were fed with a 1/2-inch or 3/4-inch line that was fine for one bathroom. It cannot keep up with two baths and a washer running. When we replace the line we size it to your fixture count, often stepping up to 1-inch pipe, and bring the shutoff, depth and backflow pieces up to what code asks for today. Better pressure upstairs.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Berkeley, CA

    Pipe pulling works like this. We dig a pit at each end of the old line. A cable or bursting head goes through the old pipe and drags new HDPE or PEX back through the same path, breaking up the old line if it has gone brittle, so a driveway, a row of shrubs or a concrete walk can stay where it is.

    Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Berkeley
