    Water Heater Problem Diagnosis in Berkeley, CA

    Something sounds or feels wrong. Rumbling, short hot water or a wet pan get diagnosed on the spot, with a straight answer on repair or replace. We lay out choices. No pressure either way.

    Electric Element Service in Berkeley, CA

    Two elements, two thermostats. A burned-out lower element is the usual cause of running short on hot water. We test with power off. We only replace what failed.

    Tune-Up Versus Replacement in Berkeley, CA

    A tune-up costs a fraction of a new heater. Maintenance helps a tank reach its full rated life and helps tankless units last much longer. Sometimes replacement makes sense. We give you real numbers for both.

    Anode Rod Checks in Berkeley

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. It is a long metal rod screwed into the top of the tank, and once it has corroded down to bare wire, the water starts eating the steel tank lining instead. We pull it and look. Replacing the rod is much cheaper than replacing the heater.

    Descaling Tankless Units in Berkeley

    Error codes often mean scale. Tankless heaters are especially sensitive to hard water, and once scale coats the heat exchanger, flow drops, the unit overheats and the control board shuts it down to protect itself. A pump kit handles it. Yearly descaling is standard advice for hard water.

    Water Heater Leak Checks in Berkeley, CA

    Small drips become floods. We inspect the supply connections, the relief valve outlet, the drain valve and the tank seams for moisture or rust trails, and we check the drain pan and the floor around the unit for signs of a slow leak. Rust stains are a warning. Early repairs protect floors and drywall.

    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Berkeley, CA

    Gas units need a clean burner and a good draft. Leak checks, flame inspection, burner cleaning and a draft test are part of every gas visit. CO is the risk. We also test the thermocouple.

    Yearly Service Plans in Berkeley, CA

    Forgetting is the main reason tanks go unflushed. A yearly plan puts the visit on the calendar, sends a reminder a week ahead, and gives you priority scheduling and repair discounts if the heater ever acts up between visits. No remembering required. Long-term savings add up.
