    Trenchless vs Traditional Repair Cost in Berkeley, CA

    Trenchless repair can cost more per foot of pipe than open trenching. However, once you add the cost of restoring driveways, patios, landscaping and irrigation, trenchless is often the better value. It is also faster and less disruptive. We compare both methods with real numbers for your home.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Berkeley, CA

    Pipe bursting is another trenchless method used for replacing badly damaged pipes. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is excellent for completely replacing a pipe segment with a new, stronger material, such as HDPE. It's often chosen when the existing pipe is collapsed or too damaged for lining. FBL Plumbing uses specialized bursting heads and winches for a smooth and efficient replacement, reducing the overall project time and cost for homeowners in Berkeley, CA.

    Sectional Point Repair in Berkeley

    When damage is confined to a specific section of pipe, sectional point repair is an ideal trenchless solution. We target only the damaged area, installing a patch or liner to seal the leak or reinforce the pipe. This method is cost-effective and limits the scope of work, which suits isolated issues in Berkeley without affecting the entire line.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Berkeley

    CIPP lining involves cleaning the old pipe, then inserting a flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin. The liner is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light. Once hardened, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works best for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Berkeley, CA

    For severely damaged or collapsed pipes, pipe bursting offers a full replacement solution. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, breaking apart the old pipe at the same time. This provides a completely new, durable pipe, often HDPE (high-density polyethylene), which resists corrosion and leaks. It's an effective long-term fix for extensive issues in Berkeley, CA.
