    What Goes Into a Sewer Replacement in Berkeley, CA

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Berkeley, CA

    Trenching lets us fix grade. That is its big advantage. When an old line has a sag that holds water and solids, bursting would just follow the same sag, while a trench lets us lay new pipe at the right pitch. Bellies disappear.

    Foundation Protection during Sewer Replacement in Berkeley, CA

    Earthquakes and hillside soil movement are real risks in Northern California, so flexible connections and seismic straps help protect plumbing from ground movement. A sewer leaking under or beside the house keeps the soil wet in one spot, which can lead to uneven settling of the slab. When we replace a line near the foundation we dig carefully, avoid undermining footings, and compact the backfill in lifts. Slow work near the house.

    Under-Slab Sewer Replacement in Berkeley

    Sewer pipe under a slab is the hardest kind to reach. There are two options. We can cut and remove a strip of floor inside the house and patch it after, or tunnel under the slab from outside so the floors stay intact, and the right choice depends on the length of the run, the flooring, and what sits above the pipe.

    Advanced Diagnostics for Sewer Issues in Berkeley, CA

    Diagnosis comes before price. The camera shows what is wrong, the locator shows where. We sometimes add a hydro-jet cleaning first, because a line full of grease can hide a crack, then camera again so we are looking at the pipe itself. Guesswork costs money.

    Code-Compliant Sewer Replacement in Berkeley

    Code sets the minimum slope, the cleanout spacing and the pipe type. The inspector looks at fall, cleanouts and material before backfill. We set fall at a quarter inch per foot unless the run requires otherwise, place cleanouts at the house and at major direction changes, and only bury pipe rated for it. Paperwork stays on file.

    Commercial Sewer Replacement in Berkeley

    Bigger pipe, more flow, more rules. Commercial laterals usually start at 6 inches. We handle the permit, set up bypass pumping when the building cannot shut down, and arrange traffic control when the work reaches the road, keeping the outage as short as the job allows. Night shifts possible.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Berkeley

    An open trench gives full access to the pipe from the house to the main. A small excavator does most of it, with hand digging near other lines. The old pipe comes out, the trench bottom gets a bed of gravel or sand, and new 4-inch PVC goes in at a steady quarter inch of fall per foot, checked with a level before backfill. Then the camera.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Berkeley

    Cast iron sewer pipe lasts a long time, though not indefinitely. Over decades it scales up inside and the bottom wears through. We replace it with PVC, and where cast iron remains under the house we use a shielded no-hub coupling at the transition. Clean seal.

    Sewer Replacement Near Me in Berkeley, CA

    A local crew can come back for the inspection. On sewer jobs that is a big deal. The trench usually has to stay open until the inspector signs off, so we plan our schedule in Berkeley around the inspection window and keep the hole covered, fenced and marked overnight until it passes.

    Local plumbers know local sewer layouts. Some laterals run out the front, some out the back to an alley. That affects depth, length and which method fits, and in Berkeley we often know it before the truck pulls up. We still camera everything.
