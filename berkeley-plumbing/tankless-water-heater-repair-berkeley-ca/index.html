    Ignition Failure Repair in Berkeley, CA

    No ignition codes have four usual causes: a dirty flame rod, a bad igniter, low gas pressure or a blocked intake. Undersized gas pipe is the one people miss.     Electric Tankless Element Repair in Berkeley, CA

    Electric units fail in their own way. We check each element's resistance, measure voltage under load and check amperage on each leg, then confirm the panel and wire size match the unit's rating.

    No Hot Water Tankless Repair in Berkeley

    No hot water at all usually points to ignition, gas supply or a heat exchanger choked with scale.     Tankless Water Heater Descaling Service in Berkeley

    Scale is calcium and magnesium carbonate that comes out of hard water as it heats up, and a tankless exchanger heats water fast through narrow passages. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. We hook a small pump and hoses to the isolation valves and circulate descaler for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with clean water. The inlet screen gets cleaned too.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Berkeley, CA

    Condensing tankless heaters pull extra heat out of the exhaust and make acidic condensate that has to drain somewhere. That condensate runs through a trap and, where code or the manual calls for it, a neutralizer filled with limestone or marble chips before it reaches the drain. Clogged traps shut the unit down. We clean the trap, refill the neutralizer and check the PVC vent for sag or the wrong slope.
