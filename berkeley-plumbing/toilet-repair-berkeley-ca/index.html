    Persistent toilet clogs are a common nuisance in Berkeley, CA homes, often caused by non-flushable items or excessive toilet paper.     Leaking Toilet Base Repair in Berkeley, CA

    Water around the base typically means the wax ring has failed or the toilet has been rocking on a loose flange.     Fill Valve Replacement in Berkeley

    A noisy, slow or hissing tank often points to a worn fill valve. Mineral scale clogs the small ports inside, which causes long refill times and phantom refills in the night. The job takes about 20 minutes and makes an older toilet much quieter.
