    Identifying Tree Root Intrusion in Berkeley, CA

    Roots on camera look like fine hairs at first. Then they become a mat. Our footage shows where they come in, how many joints are affected and whether the root mass is a light fringe that cleaning will handle or a dense ball that is already cracking the pipe.
