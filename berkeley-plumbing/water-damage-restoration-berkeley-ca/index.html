    Drying and minor drywall repairs usually do not need permits. Replacing water heaters, rerouting pipes or making structural repairs often do. We obtain permits when required.

    For minor water damage, you might be able to stay in your home. However, for extensive damage, especially involving contaminated water or major structural drying, it may be safer and more comfortable to temporarily relocate. We will let you know based on your situation.

    The duration of the water damage restoration process depends largely on the severity of the damage.     To prevent future water damage, regularly inspect plumbing for leaks, maintain your roof and gutters, keep drainage flowing away from your foundation, and consider installing smart water leak detectors.     Water damage can stem from any plumbing brand.     Right after discovering water damage, turn off the water source if possible, and turn off electricity to affected areas.
