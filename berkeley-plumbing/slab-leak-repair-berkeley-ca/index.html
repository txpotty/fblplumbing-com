    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Berkeley, CA

    Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Berkeley

    Locating a hidden slab leak takes specialized tools. Our plumbers use sensitive electronic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to pinpoint the exact location of the leak under your concrete foundation. This precise detection minimizes the need for extensive concrete removal, saving you time and money.     Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Berkeley

    Cold water slab leaks are harder to feel but still waste water and wet the soil. They often show up as a high water bill or a meter that moves with all fixtures off. We isolate the cold line, find the leak and repair or reroute it. Fixing cold leaks quickly helps protect your foundation.

    Sewer Line Slab Leak Repair in Berkeley, CA

    Slab leaks aren't always about clean water pipes, since sewer lines can also leak under your foundation. These leaks pose serious health risks and can cause extensive damage. We are experienced in detecting and repairing sewer line slab leaks, using camera inspections to identify the exact problem area. Our methods ensure proper waste removal and prevent contamination in your Berkeley, CA property, following health and safety codes.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Berkeley

    Rerouting is often the best long-term choice, especially with older copper lines. We abandon the leaking line under the slab and run a new PEX line through the attic and walls. Openings are small and placed where patching is easiest. Reroutes eliminate future leaks in that section of pipe.

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Berkeley, CA

    When a slab leak is localized and accessible, direct repair is often the most efficient solution. This involves carefully jackhammering a small section of your foundation to expose the damaged pipe, then repairing or replacing the damaged section. Our technicians are skilled in careful concrete cutting and restoration, so the repair is effective and the area is professionally restored. We use durable materials designed to hold up to soil movement under the slab.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Berkeley

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution. This involves abandoning the leaking line under the slab and installing a new pipe through the walls or attic. This method skips the problem area entirely, providing a fresh, durable plumbing line. It is a common solution for aging homes in Berkeley with original copper or galvanized plumbing.

    Slab Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Berkeley, CA

    Spot repairs usually have a lower upfront cost but involve breaking concrete and repairing flooring. Reroutes cost a bit more but avoid concrete work and future leaks in that line. Whole-house repipes are the best choice when you have had multiple slab leaks or old copper throughout. We give you clear prices for each option.
