    Diverter Valve Repair and Replacement in Vallejo, CA

    Water running from the spout during a shower means a failing diverter. It is usually the gate in the spout, or the middle stem on three-handle faucets, and both wear out over time. Spout diverters are a quick swap. Middle stems need a seat too.

    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Vallejo, CA

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We swap cartridges or stems and seats, greasing the O-rings so handles turn easily. Old seats need a seat wrench. About an hour, usually.

    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Vallejo

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. Single-handle drips mean worn cartridge seals, and multi-handle drips mean worn washers and seats. New internals end the drip. Cracked bodies get a new valve.

    Corroded Tub Faucet Replacement in Vallejo, CA

    Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. Corrosion shows up as green crust at the handle or a stem you cannot turn by hand. By that point the threads that hold the trim are often too weak to grip a new part, and the valve body itself may be pitted where the cartridge seals against it. Replacement is the better call.

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Vallejo

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. That cartridge wears out and causes drips or a handle that will not reach full hot, and swapping it with a cartridge puller takes about an hour when the valve body is in good shape. Moentrol valves use a different part. We carry both.

    Budget Tub Faucet Options in Vallejo, CA

    Not every tub faucet needs a full valve. A new cartridge and a matching trim kit from the same brand can make a twenty-year-old single-handle faucet work and look nearly new for a fraction of what a valve replacement costs. We offer that option first. Three-handle faucets are the exception.

    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Vallejo, CA

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. A new valve makes sense for discontinued parts, a cracked body or an old faucet with no scald protection. We quote both options. Most people choose repair first.

    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Vallejo

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler. Supplies have to rise through the floor, from a slab cut or a crawlspace, with a bracket anchored below. Deck-mount fillers sit on the rim. Tub-only fillers need a temperature limit.
