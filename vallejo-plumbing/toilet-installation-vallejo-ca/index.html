    Kinds of Toilet Installation in Vallejo, CA

    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Vallejo

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. They need at least 20 psi to work right. We check static pressure at a hose bib before recommending one, and we warn customers about the noise, since the flush is loud enough to hear across the house. They rarely clog.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Vallejo, CA

    Comfort height bowls stand about two inches taller than standard ones. Think about who will use it before you buy. Kids and shorter adults may not like their feet off the floor, but people with sore knees or a new hip usually love the extra height. Installation is the same as a standard bowl.

    Bathroom Remodel Toilet Installation in Vallejo, CA

    New tile over old vinyl can raise a bathroom floor by half an inch or more. Suddenly the old flange is below the floor. We bring the flange up with a stacking extender or replace it outright, and if you are moving the toilet on a slab, that means cutting concrete to reroute the drain and patching it afterward. Moving it on a wood floor is easier.

    Wall-Mounted Toilet Installation in Vallejo

    Wall-mounted toilets hang off a steel carrier bolted to the floor inside the wall. The tank sits behind an access plate. Because the carrier carries all the weight, we anchor its feet to the slab or framing first and check it for plumb before the drywall or tile goes up. Plan on a remodel, not a swap.

    Rough-In Toilet Installation in Vallejo, CA

    Rough-in is the plumbing that goes in before walls and floors close up. On a toilet, that is a vented 3 inch drain and a cold water stub. The drain goes 12 inches out from the finished wall line, which means we add the drywall thickness to the framing measurement. Then the inspector sees it open.

    Two-Piece Toilet Installation in Vallejo

    Two-piece toilets come with the tank and bowl separate, joined by a rubber gasket and two or three bolts. We assemble them on the floor before setting. Tightening the tank bolts evenly, side to side, matters more than how hard they go, because an uneven pull cracks the china or leaves a slow drip at one bolt. It should not wobble.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Vallejo, CA

    Wall-hung toilets need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep, or a furred-out chase built in front of an existing 2 by 4 wall. Floors stay clear underneath. That makes mopping easier and lets you set the bowl height anywhere within the carrier's range, which is handy for tall households or anyone who wants a comfort-height seat without a tall bowl. Service happens through the flush plate.

    Commercial Restroom Toilets in Vallejo, CA

    Restaurants and offices get heavy-duty fixtures and accessible stalls built to code. Downtime costs a business money. Work gets scheduled before opening or after close, with fixtures staged on site, so each stall is out of service only briefly. We work with your GC on inspections.
