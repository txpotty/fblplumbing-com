    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Replacement in Vallejo

    Costs vary by job. A tank water heater replacement usually runs $1,400 to $3,000, a water softener $1,500 to $3,500, a water line $1,800 to $5,500 and a sewer line $4,000 to $15,000 or more.

    Some simple fixture swaps are DIY friendly, but water heaters, well pumps and sewer lines involve gas, electrical and code requirements. A licensed plumber provides safety and compliance.

    We primarily use PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) and copper piping for water supply lines. For sewer and drain lines, we use durable PVC (polyvinyl chloride) or ABS (acrylonitrile butadiene styrene) pipes, based on the application and local codes.

    Consider the age of the equipment, the cost of repair compared with replacement and how often it has needed service. We give you both options with pricing so you can choose.

    We install water heaters from Bradford White, Rheem and A.O. Smith, tankless units from Rinnai and Navien, and quality softeners and well pumps from proven manufacturers.

    While some minor plumbing repairs can be DIY, major replacements like water heaters, sewer lines or repiping should always be handled by licensed professionals. These jobs involve complex systems, potential safety hazards (gas, high water pressure) and strict code compliance. Improper installation can lead to leaks, damage and even severe safety risks, costing more in the long run.

    The timeline depends on the complexity of the job. A water heater replacement typically takes 2 to 4 hours. A major project like a full house repipe or sewer line replacement can take 1 to 5 days. We will provide an estimated schedule during your consultation.

    Climate, freezes and soil type all shape how long plumbing lasts.
