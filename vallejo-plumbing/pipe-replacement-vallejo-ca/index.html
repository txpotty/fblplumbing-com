    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Vallejo

    Gray polybutylene pipe was installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s. It breaks down from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water, and fittings can crack without warning. Some insurers raise premiums or decline coverage on homes that still have it. We remove polybutylene supply lines and replace them with PEX-A or copper. A completed repipe can also help when selling the home.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Vallejo, CA

    Copper piping is a long-standing, reliable choice known for its durability and resistance to corrosion. While it's a premium material, its longevity often makes it a cost-effective solution over time. FBL Plumbing offers expert copper pipe replacement for sections or entire homes in Vallejo, CA. Our plumbers are skilled in the precise soldering and installation techniques required for copper, ensuring a leak-proof and robust plumbing system that holds up year after year.

    PEX Repiping in Vallejo

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale buildup and expands slightly if water freezes, which helps in colder homes. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe for better flow. Systems can be run as a home-run manifold with a shutoff for each fixture or as a trunk and branch layout. PEX installs faster than copper with far fewer fittings in the walls. It is our most requested repipe material.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Vallejo, CA

    Cast iron drain pipe in older homes can rust through at the bottom, while some ABS from the 1980s was made with defective resin that cracks. Signs include sewer odors, slow drains and stains on ceilings below upstairs bathrooms. We camera the line first, then replace reachable sections with PVC or ABS. Under-slab drains can be tunneled, rerouted or lined depending on location. We explain the options with the camera footage in hand.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Vallejo, CA

    Many older homes in Vallejo, CA still have galvanized steel pipes, which are prone to internal corrosion over time. This corrosion chokes water flow, reduces water pressure, and can introduce rust particles into your drinking water. Our pipe replacement service targets these corroded sections, replacing them with new, non-corrosive materials like PEX or copper. This restores proper water flow and ensures clean, fresh water throughout your home.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Vallejo

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping has become a popular choice for residential pipe replacement due to its flexibility, affordability, and resistance to freezing. Even in mild areas, wrapping irrigation backflow devices before a frosty night is cheap insurance against a surprise leak. Unlike rigid pipes, PEX can expand, reducing the risk of burst pipes during unexpected cold snaps. FBL Plumbing specializes in PEX installation, which often allows for less invasive replacement methods compared to traditional materials. This modern solution offers excellent performance and water quality for your home.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Vallejo, CA

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. They are caused by a mix of water chemistry, high velocity and thin Type M pipe, and once they begin they tend to keep appearing. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes. We help you weigh the cost of both paths.

    Whole-Home Repiping in Vallejo

    If your home in Vallejo is older and experiencing frequent leaks, low water pressure throughout, or discolored water from multiple fixtures, a whole-home repiping might be the most cost-effective long-term solution. We can replace all the old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes with modern, durable PEX or copper piping.
