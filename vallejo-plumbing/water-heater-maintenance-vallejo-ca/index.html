    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Vallejo

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in Vallejo, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's heavily depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing expensive tank leaks.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Vallejo, CA

    Tankless water heaters need specialized maintenance, mainly a descaling flush to remove mineral deposits that can restrict flow and reduce efficiency. Our technicians in Vallejo, CA use commercial-grade solutions to clean the heat exchanger, so your unit keeps providing endless hot water efficiently. We also inspect the igniter, fan, and exhaust venting for proper performance and safety.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Vallejo

    Tankless units are especially sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. We isolate the unit with its service valves and circulate a descaling solution with a pump for the recommended time. We also clean the inlet water filter and check the unit's error history. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures steady.

    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Vallejo, CA

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as needed. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water fast. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.

    Water Heater Leak Detection and Prevention in Vallejo, CA

    Leaks are a common issue that can lead to significant water damage if not caught early. Our maintenance service includes a thorough inspection of all water heater connections, pipes, and the tank itself for any signs of leaks or corrosion. Early detection and repair of small leaks can prevent larger, more damaging problems, protecting your home's floors and interior from water damage.
