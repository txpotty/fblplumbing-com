    Isolation Valve Kit Installation in Vallejo, CA

    Some tankless heaters were installed without service valves, which makes proper flushing impossible. We install a set of isolation valves with purge ports on the hot and cold lines so future maintenance takes minutes. The kit also lets you shut off water to the heater without closing the main.     Maintenance Cost Versus Repair Cost in Vallejo, CA

    A tankless descaling visit typically costs far less than replacing a scaled heat exchanger, which can run $600 to $1,500 or more in parts and labor. Some manufacturers deny exchanger claims when scale damage is found. Regular maintenance helps protect a unit that can last 20 years when cared for.     Condensing Unit and Neutralizer Service in Vallejo

    High-efficiency condensing tankless heaters produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized before it goes down a drain. We inspect the condensate trap, line and neutralizer cartridge and replace the media when it is spent. A clogged trap can cause shutdowns or water leaking inside the cabinet. We also check that the line is protected where it runs outdoors.

    Inlet Filter and Air Intake Cleaning in Vallejo

    Every tankless unit has an inlet water screen that catches grit and scale flakes. Those screens can clog with sand and debris, especially after municipal line work. We pull and clean the screen and inspect the air intake filter, which can load up with dust and dryer lint. Clean filters keep the flow sensor reading correctly and the burner breathing properly.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Vallejo

    The heat exchanger in a tankless heater is a coil of small tubes that water races through while the burner fires. Hard water drops calcium and magnesium on those hot surfaces, narrowing the passages over time. We connect a submersible pump and hoses to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with fresh water to rinse the system completely. Restored flow means steadier temperatures and fewer shutdowns.

    Recirculation and Combi System Service in Vallejo, CA

    Many tankless units now include built-in recirculation pumps or buffer tanks to deliver faster hot water. These systems move more water through the exchanger and scale up sooner. We flush the recirculation loop, check pump operation and verify crossover or dedicated return line settings. Correct schedules can reduce wasted energy while still giving you quick hot water.
