    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Vallejo

    Submersible pumps are common in residential wells, designed to operate submerged within the well casing. When these pumps fail, symptoms usually include no water, low water pressure, or strange noises from the well. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like motor burnout, damaged impellers, or faulty check valves that can affect submersible pump performance. We use specialized equipment for safe and efficient pump extraction and reinstallation, ensuring the integrity of your well system.

    Jet Pump Repair in Vallejo, CA

    Jet pumps, usually installed above ground, are often used for shallower wells or as booster pumps. Common issues include loss of prime, seal leaks, or motor wear. We troubleshoot problems like air in the lines, clogged foot valves, or faulty pressure switches. Our team in Vallejo is skilled at restoring prime and replacing worn components to get your jet pump operating smoothly again, a frequent need where water levels rise and fall.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Vallejo, CA

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. Strong winter storms can bring days of heavy rain to Northern California, saturating ground and pushing water into crawlspaces and sewer lines. Lightning and power surges often damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help prevent repeat failures.
