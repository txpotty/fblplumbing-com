    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Vallejo

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause loud popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution rated for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Gas and Electric Water Heater Checks in Vallejo, CA

    Whether your home in Vallejo, CA uses a gas or electric hot water tank, our maintenance covers the particular needs of each. For gas units, we inspect burner assemblies, gas lines and vent systems for safety and efficiency. For electric units, we check heating elements, wiring and thermostats. Our detailed checks confirm all energy sources are operating correctly and safely.

    Standard Hot Water Tank Tune-Up in Vallejo

    FBL Plumbing offers a thorough tune-up for conventional hot water tanks, focusing on critical components. This includes draining the tank to remove sediment buildup, which is crucial wherever water carries minerals, and inspecting the anode rod for signs of corrosion. We also check heating elements, thermostat settings and all pipe connections for leaks. Our goal is to improve your tank's performance and prevent future issues.

    Burner and Pilot Light Maintenance in Vallejo, CA

    For gas hot water tanks, proper burner and pilot light operation is essential for efficient heating. Our service includes cleaning the burner assembly to remove dust and debris that can impede combustion. We also check and adjust the pilot light, making sure it ignites reliably. This attention to detail prevents common issues like intermittent hot water or complete heating failure, which can be stressful for homeowners in Vallejo, CA.

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Vallejo

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous. We lift the lever, confirm it opens and reseats and check the discharge line for proper material and termination. We also check your thermal expansion tank pressure with a gauge, since a waterlogged expansion tank lets pressure spike every heating cycle. High street pressure is common in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.

    Electric Element and Thermostat Service in Vallejo

    Electric tanks rely on upper and lower heating elements that get crusted with scale in hard water. We test each element with a meter, check the thermostats and look for scorched wires at the terminal block. If an element has failed we replace it with the matching wattage and voltage. Many families who think they need a new heater just need a lower element and a flush.

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Vallejo, CA

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can clog the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a steady blue flame and lower gas bills.
