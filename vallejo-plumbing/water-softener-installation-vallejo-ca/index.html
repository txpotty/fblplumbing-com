    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Vallejo

    Salt-based (ion exchange) water softeners are the most common and effective solution for truly softening water. These systems use a resin bed to exchange hard minerals like calcium and magnesium with sodium ions. FBL Plumbing carefully installs various models of salt-based softeners, making sure of proper connection to your main water line, correct drainage setup and accurate programming for regeneration cycles in your Vallejo home.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Vallejo

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Vallejo

    A whole-house water softener system is installed directly on your main water line, treating all the water entering your Vallejo home.     Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Vallejo

    Reverse osmosis systems remove dissolved solids, which can be high in some water supplies. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet. Some systems also connect to refrigerators or ice makers. RO pairs well with a softener for complete water treatment. We change filters on a schedule so the system keeps working well.
