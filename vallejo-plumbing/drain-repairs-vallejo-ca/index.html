    Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Vallejo, CA

    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. The bottom goes first. Rust scale catches debris and hairline cracks let water seep into walls and floors, so we cut out the failing section and replace it with ABS or PVC using shielded transition couplings. If the whole stack is failing, we price it separately.

    Vent Pipe Repair in Vallejo

    Sewer gas leaking into a room can point to a missing vent. Old renovations often skipped it. Bird nests, debris and roofing work can block existing vents, while fixtures that never had a vent can sometimes get an air admittance valve if code allows. Proper venting protects every trap in the house.

    Tub and Shower Drain Repair in Vallejo, CA

    Hair, soap scum and minerals narrow bath drains over time. Drain seals wear out. We replace worn pop-up assemblies, broken tub shoes and leaking shower drain bodies, often working from inside the tub so the ceiling underneath stays intact. When access is needed, we keep the opening small.

    Laundry and Floor Drain Repair in Vallejo
