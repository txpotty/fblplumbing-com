    Disc Faucet Repair in Vallejo

    Ceramic disc faucets are known for their durability and are a common choice in many contemporary Vallejo bathrooms. While generally reliable, they can develop leaks if the ceramic discs become scratched or misaligned, or if the inlet and outlet seals wear out.
