    Upgrading Old Sewer Pipe Materials in Vallejo

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all fail in their own ways. Roots get into clay joints, cast iron rusts out along the invert, and Orangeburg fiber pipe squashes flat over the years. Modern plastic pipe avoids all three problems. Fused HDPE has no joints at all, so roots have nowhere to start.

    Cured-in-Place Sewer Lining in Vallejo

    Lining builds a new pipe inside the old one. After cutting roots and jetting the line, we pull in a resin-filled liner and inflate it until it cures against the old pipe wall. Collapsed pipe will not work. It is a strong choice under slabs and finished rooms. You will get a straight answer on whether yours is a candidate.
