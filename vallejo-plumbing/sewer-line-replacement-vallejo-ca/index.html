    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair & Replacement in Vallejo, CA

    Trenchless technology, such as pipe bursting or pipe lining, offers a less invasive alternative to traditional methods. Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one, fracturing the old pipe in place. Pipe lining inserts a new, durable resin liner inside the existing pipe. Both methods greatly reduce the need for excavation, preserving your yard and landscaping in Vallejo, CA while providing a long-lasting solution.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Vallejo, CA

    Traditional excavation is sometimes the best option, especially when the old line has a severe belly or the slope must be corrected. We dig along the line, remove the old pipe and set new PVC on compacted bedding at the correct grade. Rocky or hard-packed soil can slow digging, and we plan for that in the estimate. Each joint is glued and checked before backfill. The inspector sees the open trench before we cover it.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Replacement in Vallejo, CA

    To minimize disruption, trenchless sewer line replacement replaces pipes without extensive digging.
