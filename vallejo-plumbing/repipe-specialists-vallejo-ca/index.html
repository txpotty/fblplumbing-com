    Slab Leak Repiping in Vallejo

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Expansive clay soils are common across Northern California, and seasonal swelling and shrinking can crack slabs and buried sewer laterals. Rather than jackhammer to reach one leak, we run new lines through the attic and walls and leave the old under-slab pipe out of service. No jackhammer needed. It ends this leak and takes the rest of the slab plumbing out of the picture.

    Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Vallejo, CA

    A whole-home PEX repipe for an average house often runs $5,000 to $12,000, with copper costing more. One slab leak with access and repair often costs $1,000 to $4,000. The numbers add up quickly. By the second or third leak, a repipe usually pays for itself, and we put both options side by side so you can decide.

    Drywall Patching and Restoration in Vallejo, CA

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Vallejo

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. Type L tolerates UV, stays rigid and has decades of field history. It costs more. We sweat joints with lead-free solder or press them, hang the pipe at proper support intervals and test the whole system before patching.

    Commercial Repipe Services in Vallejo

    Older commercial buildings in Vallejo often have galvanized risers, copper that has pinholed or plastic pipe that no longer meets code.     PEX Versus Copper Repipe Pricing in Vallejo, CA

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper because the material is cheaper and installation goes faster. Copper costs more. Copper commonly costs 20 to 40 percent more than PEX, and some owners prefer it anyway for its long history. Our quotes for either include permit, fittings, patching and cleanup.

    Type L Copper Repiping in Vallejo

    Copper has been plumbing homes for generations, and done right it lasts a long time. Our copper repipes use Type L with proper hangers and isolators so the pipe does not wear against framing. Lead-free solder only. Because some water chemistries eat copper, we look at your leak history before recommending it over PEX.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Vallejo

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water and long attic runs. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open the pipe end and let it shrink back over the fitting for a full-bore joint. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. Most homes are repiped with PEX-A in two to three days.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Repipe Specialists in Vallejo

    Most whole-home repipes in Vallejo, CA fall somewhere between $4,000 and $15,000. Bigger homes cost more.     PEX resists scale and pinhole corrosion and handles expansion well, which makes it a strong choice for hard water. Copper lasts but is pricier and can pinhole in corrosive water.

    Both work well, and the best choice depends on your house and your water. PEX is cheaper. PEX bends, resists scale and handles freezes better, and copper is rigid, UV-tolerant and proven but pricier and vulnerable to aggressive water.

    Most families stay home the whole time. Expect daytime shutoffs. Each evening the water comes back on, and we keep floors covered and clean up daily so the house stays livable.
