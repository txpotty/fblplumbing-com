    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Repair in Vallejo

    Most jobs fall between $500 for a small spot repair and about $3,000 for a trenchless section. Replacing the whole line is a bigger number. The written price comes after we see the pipe.

    Trenchless repair fixes the pipe through one or two small access pits. No long trench. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE pipe through the old one while splitting the old pipe apart, and cured-in-place lining inserts a resin-soaked tube that hardens into a new pipe wall inside the old one, which leaves concrete and landscaping alone. We check on camera whether yours does.

    Clay, cast iron, ABS, PVC and Orangeburg all come up in Vallejo and around Northern California. We work on all five. Joining new PVC to old clay or cast iron takes a shielded transition coupling sized to the outside diameter of each pipe, because a mismatched coupling is the most common reason a spot repair fails. The usual couplings ride on the truck.

    For a spot repair, you lose the sewer for a few hours. Bigger jobs take a day or more. On a full replacement we stage the work so the house is reconnected each evening where possible, and we tell you which fixtures are safe to use while the line is open. Moving out is rarely necessary.

    It is a repair done without opening a trench along the whole pipe. Two small pits, usually.     Schedule 40 PVC is our standard for open trench work. It does not rust. For pipe bursting we use fused HDPE, which comes in continuous lengths with no joints for roots to find, while lining jobs use epoxy-saturated tubes built for long service life. Local code decides the final choice.
