    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Service in Vallejo, CA

    The tank looks full but makes no brine. A hard crust has formed and is keeping the salt from dissolving.     Resin Bed Replacement in Vallejo

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. Older systems on chlorinated city water see it most. FBL Plumbing pulls the old resin and fills the tank with new media. The softener works like new.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Vallejo, CA

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. Scale returning or a drop in water pressure usually means the conditioning media is spent or the sediment prefilter is clogged. FBL Plumbing repairs these units in Vallejo, CA. Media lasts a few years.

    Emergency Water Softener Repair in Vallejo

    A softener stuck in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours or flood a utility room. Urgent calls like that get priority.     Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Vallejo, CA

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Its small ports clog with sediment and salt. A clogged injector leaves the brine tank full of water and the resin never gets recharged. We clean or replace it and check the brine line for air leaks.
