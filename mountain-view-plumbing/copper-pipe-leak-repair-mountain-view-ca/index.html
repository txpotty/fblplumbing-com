    Copper Pipe Leak Repair Options for Mountain View, CA Homes

    Corrosion-Related Leak Repair in Mountain View

    Corrosion thins copper from the inside. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. It shows up as green crust. On exposed pipe you may see blue-green deposits at fittings, and inside the wall the first sign is usually a pinhole or a weeping joint that stains the drywall below it. We replace the corroded length. We explain what drove it.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in Mountain View

    Pinholes form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. Hot water speeds it up. They often appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher, which we fix by replacing that length with heavier pipe. More pinholes often follow.

    Copper Repair vs Repipe Cost in Mountain View, CA

    A single copper leak repair is affordable and fast. Repeated leaks change the math. If you have had two or more pinhole leaks in a year, the copper is likely thinning throughout the system, so repiping often beats another round of spot fixes on total cost. You get both numbers.

    Copper Slab Leak Repair in Mountain View

    Many slab homes have copper water lines running under the concrete. Listen for running water. When those lines leak, you may notice warm spots on the floor, the sound of running water or a meter that turns with every fixture off, and we locate the leak electronically before choosing a spot repair through the slab or a reroute. Both get a price.

    Preventive Copper Pipe Inspection in Mountain View, CA

    An inspection catches thin spots before they leak. It takes about an hour. We look at exposed copper for green crust and pitting, test static pressure, check the bond wire at the water heater and main, then scan suspect walls with a thermal camera on homes with a leak history. You get a written summary.

    Burst Copper Pipe Repair in Mountain View, CA

    Copper in an exterior wall or attic can split when the water inside freezes. Mountain and foothill homes in Northern California see real winters, so pipes there need insulation, heat tape, or careful draining when a cabin sits empty. Close the main valve. The split often does not leak until the ice thaws, which is why many bursts are found hours after the cold. We cut out the split and solder in new pipe. Then we add insulation.

    Underground Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Mountain View

    Buried copper service lines can leak for months before anyone notices. Bills tell the story. We locate the leak with acoustic gear and tracer gas, and either spot repair it or swap in a new service line with minimal digging. We test before backfill.
