    Direct Foundation Leak Repair in Mountain View, CA

    When the leak is in an accessible spot and the rest of the pipe is in good condition, direct repair is often the most economical choice. We cut a small opening in the floor, excavate to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The soil is compacted and the concrete is patched to match. Flooring can then be restored by your flooring contractor.

    Slab Leak Detection & Repair in Mountain View

    Slab leaks are known for being difficult to detect, often showing subtle signs like warm spots on the floor or a sudden jump in your water bill. FBL Plumbing uses acoustic and infrared technology to accurately locate these hidden leaks under your concrete foundation. Once found, we offer fitting repair solutions, whether it's a spot repair, re-routing, or pipe bursting, to restore your plumbing system in Mountain View without extensive damage to your property.

    Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Mountain View, CA

    Many older homes in Mountain View, CA have copper water lines beneath their foundations, which can corrode over time due to soil conditions or water chemistry. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. When these copper pipes develop pinhole leaks or cracks, they require specialized repair. Our technicians repair or replace sections of damaged copper piping, ensuring a durable seal that holds up to pressure and ground movement and helps prevent future leaks.

    Foundation Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Mountain View, CA

    Direct repairs are often the lowest upfront cost but involve breaking concrete and may need flooring repairs. We help you compare total costs, including flooring and drywall repairs.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Repair in Mountain View

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs with steel cables under high tension. Cutting into these slabs takes care and scanning to avoid hitting a cable. We use ground-penetrating radar or other scanning methods before opening the floor. When risk is too high, rerouting the plumbing is usually the safer option.
