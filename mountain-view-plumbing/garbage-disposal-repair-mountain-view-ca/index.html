    Electrical Problems and Resetting in Mountain View

    No hum at all usually means no power reaching the motor. We start at the red reset button on the bottom, then check the breaker, the wall switch and the outlet or junction box under the sink with a meter. It is wet under a sink.     Jammed Disposal Repair in Mountain View

    A jam usually comes from one hard object, like a peach pit, a chicken bone or a twist tie wrapped around the impeller. We free the flywheel from below with the hex key and pull the object with pliers, never a hand, then run it with water to make sure nothing else is stuck. We press the reset after clearing it. Repeat jams point us to the trap arm.

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Mountain View, CA

    Leaks show up at the flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube or body. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the internal seal has failed, and in that case replacement is the better option. It is not a repairable part.

    Grinding Noise Repair in Mountain View

    A metal-on-metal grind may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. After checking the chamber, snugging the mount and pulling debris, we run it empty to hear if the noise is coming from the motor. Bad bearings mean replacement.

    Humming Disposal Repair in Mountain View

    Humming with no spin usually means something is stuck. Spoons, glass, bones and pits cause most of them. With the power off, we free the flywheel using the hex socket underneath, pull the object, and test the motor and reset with water running.

    Commercial Disposal Repair in Mountain View

    Restaurant disposals run 1 to 5 horsepower and usually include a control box and a solenoid valve for water. When one stops, the dish line backs up fast. Controls get checked first. A bad solenoid or tripped overload in the panel kills the unit as fully as a jam does, but it gets fixed far faster.

    InSinkErator Disposal Repair in Mountain View, CA

    InSinkErator makes the Badger line and the Evolution series, and we repair both. Badgers are simple single-stage units, while Evolution models have multi-stage grinding and, on some models, a separate stage that can jam on its own. Most repairs use parts from the truck. A failed motor means a new unit.
