    PEX Service Lines for Long Runs in Mountain View

    PEX comes in coils up to several hundred feet. Fewer joints underground means fewer places a leak can start, and it is the joints that usually fail first.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Mountain View, CA

    Bursting fits old cast iron or galvanized lines that will shatter under the head. Pulling suits softer pipe like poly or copper. Either way the new pipe follows the old path, which matters when the route runs under a patio or a neighbor's fence, leaving you with two small holes instead of a long trench. Rock can stop it.

    Galvanized Main Line Replacement in Mountain View

    Galvanized steel was common in homes built before the 1960s. Inside, the pipe rusts once the zinc is gone. After a few decades the opening can be half its original size, and that shows up as weak pressure and orange water, and no amount of cleaning brings it back. It has to come out.

    Water Line Upgrades and Code Compliance in Mountain View, CA

    A lot of older houses were fed with a 1/2-inch or 3/4-inch line that was fine for one bathroom. It cannot keep up with two baths and a washer running. When we replace the line we size it to your fixture count, which often means going to 1-inch, and bring the shutoff, depth and backflow pieces up to what code asks for today. Better pressure upstairs.

    Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Mountain View, CA

    Polybutylene is the gray or blue plastic pipe installed from roughly the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. Chlorine in the water breaks it down from the inside. The failures tend to be sudden splits rather than slow drips, and some insurers will not write a policy on a house that still has it. Worth replacing.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Mountain View

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. Scale and corrosion are not a problem with it. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, connect it with brass or poly fittings that are approved underground, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    Copper Water Line Installation in Mountain View

    Type K copper is the thick-wall grade made for burial. It handles high street pressure without complaint. We bed it in sand, keep it away from sharp rock, and sleeve it where it passes through a foundation wall, because concrete and copper rubbing together for twenty years eventually wear a hole. PEX is cheaper.
