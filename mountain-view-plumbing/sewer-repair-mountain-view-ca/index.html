    Sewer Repair Methods for Mountain View, CA Homes

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Mountain View

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and we always jet a line before lining it. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Mountain View

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. It flattens with age. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, so by the time a camera shows widespread damage, spot repairs are a poor investment. We replace these lines with PVC. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Sewer Line Backups and Blockages in Mountain View

    Water coming up in the tub when the washer drains is a classic main line symptom. The clog is past the branches. We clear it from the nearest cleanout, then run the camera to see what caused it, since a grease clog and a root mass need very different fixes. Older Northern California homes with clay lines often show roots right at the joints. You get the footage either way.

    Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Mountain View, CA

    A plugged vent makes drains gurgle and traps run dry. Then the sewer smell shows up. Bird nests, leaves and ice can block a roof vent, and older galvanized or cast iron vent stacks crack inside the walls where nobody sees them, which is why we look at the vent whenever the lateral checks out fine. Fixes range from a quick clearing to a new section of stack.

    Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in Mountain View, CA

    A spot repair is the cheapest fix when the rest of the line is sound. Lining costs more per foot. It avoids excavation, though, and on long runs under driveways or patios the savings on concrete and landscaping often outweigh the higher price of the liner itself. Full replacement is right for Orangeburg or a line broken in several places. We price all three so you can compare.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Mountain View

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. We record the whole run. Cracks, root intrusion, offset joints and standing water all show up on the screen, and the sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the ground above, which keeps the dig to one small pit. Older homes in Mountain View often hide two or three problems in one line, and the footage shows every one.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Mountain View, CA

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. The old pipe gets jetted clean before anything else. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, which seals cracks and root entry points in a single continuous tube. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. On a four-inch residential line, that rarely matters.
