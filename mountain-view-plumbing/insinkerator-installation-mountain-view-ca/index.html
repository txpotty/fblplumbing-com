    Hard Water and Mineral Considerations in Mountain View

    Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running mineral-heavy. Hard water leaves scale on disposal grind rings, sink flanges and drain tailpieces. Over time that buildup can make a disposal noisy and cause slow drainage. We inspect the drain line for scale and debris before connecting a new unit. If heavy buildup is present, we recommend cleaning or replacing the trap and tailpiece at the same time. Stainless steel grind components on Evolution models resist corrosion better in these conditions.

    Common signs you need a new garbage disposal include frequent clogs, persistent leaks from the unit, unusual loud noises during operation, or a complete failure to turn on. If your unit constantly jams or emits a foul odor even after cleaning, it might be time for a replacement.

    For light use, a Badger 5 or Badger 5XP works well. Families who cook a lot or want a quieter unit should consider an Evolution Compact or Excel. Homes on septic should consider the Evolution Septic Assist.

    In most cases, a simple like-for-like replacement of a garbage disposal does not require a permit in Mountain View.
