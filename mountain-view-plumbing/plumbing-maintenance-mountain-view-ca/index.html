    What a Plumbing Maintenance Visit Covers in Mountain View, CA

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Mountain View

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale builds up inside the heat exchanger, especially with hard water, and the unit starts throwing error codes, running lukewarm or cutting out mid-shower long before it actually wears out. We pump descaler through the service valves. We also clean the inlet screen and check venting and gas supply.

    Softener Upkeep in Mountain View, CA

    Softeners fail quietly. A softener that quit regenerating still sends water, just hard water, and you only notice when scale appears. Bypass and drain get tested. We measure hardness after.

    Gas Line and Pipe Material Checks in Mountain View, CA

    Gas leaks are the one item we never skip. We check visible gas piping and appliance connectors for corrosion and leaks, confirm the shutoff valves turn, and note any old flexible connectors that should be replaced before they crack. Pipe material matters too. Older pipe types get flagged with repipe choices.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Mountain View

    Sediment is the tank's slow killer. Rural properties around Mountain View often rely on private wells, which may need treatment for iron, sediment, or other minerals. We drain and flush the tank, pull and check the anode rod, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, and look over the gas or electric connections for corrosion and wear. Plan on an hour. A clean tank heats faster and lasts years longer.

    Water Softener Service in Mountain View

    A softener only helps if it regenerates. We check the salt level, break up any salt bridge in the brine tank, clean the brine well and injector, and run a manual regeneration to confirm the valve cycles correctly. We test the water afterward. A failed softener sends hard water through the house without warning.

    Gas Connector and Shutoff Checks in Mountain View

    Gas feeds water heaters, furnaces, ranges and dryers. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections with leak detector solution, make sure each appliance shutoff valve turns and replace any old or kinked flexible connectors. Repairs follow local code. If you smell gas, get outside before calling the gas company.
