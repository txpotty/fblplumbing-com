    PEX and HDPE Water Line Installation in Mountain View, CA

    PEX and HDPE are flexible, which means fewer fittings in the ground and better tolerance for soil movement. HDPE is especially durable for long runs and resists cracking in expansive clay. We use approved compression or fusion fittings and add tracer wire so the line can be found with a locator later. PEX must stay out of sunlight before burial, so we store and lay it with care on every job site.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Line Installation in Mountain View

    Most residential water line installations run $1,800 to $5,500 depending on length, soil and material. Long runs through rocky ground, trenchless boring or copper pipe can push costs to $6,000 to $10,000 or more.

    We focus on high-quality, reputable brands for our water line installations. For PEX, we often use Uponor Wirsbo or Viega PureFlow. For copper, we use Type L or Type K copper from trusted manufacturers. Our material selection focuses on durability and performance in your local conditions.

    Some homeowners dig their own trench, but connecting to the meter, sizing the pipe and passing inspection are best handled by a licensed plumber. A poor joint underground can leak for months before anyone notices.

    Depth requirements vary by jurisdiction and climate.     Installing a main water line is a complex task that involves excavation, knowledge of plumbing codes, precise connections and permits.     Trenching does disturb a narrow path, but we backfill, compact and regrade when we finish. If the route crosses concrete or mature landscaping, we can discuss trenchless options.

    PEX is flexible, less expensive, resistant to freezing and corrosion, and has fewer fittings, which means fewer possible leak points.
