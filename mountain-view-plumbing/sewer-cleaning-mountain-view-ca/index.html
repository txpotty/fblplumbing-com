    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Mountain View, CA

    Cleaning cannot fix a broken pipe. It only removes what is inside. When a line keeps backing up, we put the cost of regular cleaning next to the cost of a repair so you can see which makes sense, with the numbers in writing.

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Mountain View, CA

    On a repeat backup, the camera goes in first. We would rather look than guess. The camera feed shows roots, grease, breaks and bellies in real time, and the locator marks the spot on the ground above.

    Dealing with Grease Buildup in Mountain View, CA

    Grease hardens as it cools. Then it narrows the pipe. A greasy main slowly loses diameter until it cannot carry a full washer load or a bathtub draining at once, and jetting is the right fix.

    Root Removal From Sewer Lines in Mountain View

    Trees send roots toward any moisture they can find. A sewer joint is a steady source. We use a sectional cable with a root saw sized to the pipe to cut the mass free and pull it out, then follow with a jetter if fine root hairs are left behind, and a camera shows where roots are entering so you can choose between lining and repair.

    Jetting a Sewer Line in Mountain View, CA

    Grease and scale are jetting work. A cable only scratches them. A jetter nozzle with rear-facing jets pulls itself down the line and blasts the buildup off the pipe wall, restoring close to the full diameter of the pipe and slowing the next clog.

    Emergency Sewer Cleaning in Mountain View

    Sewage on the floor cannot wait. Our phones are answered after hours. A tech arrives with a main line machine, protective gear and a wet vac, ready to clear roots or a collapsed section and stop the backup before it spreads into more rooms.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Mountain View

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. A cable only makes a path. Water at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI strips grease, scale and fine roots off the inside of the line and flushes it all toward the street, which leaves the pipe close to its original diameter.
