    Slab Leaks in Older Homes in Mountain View, CA

    Older homes in Mountain View, CA often have soft copper or galvanized steel under the slab, and both wear out with time.     Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Mountain View, CA

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. That makes them harder to spot. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, which is why we isolate the cold line, trace its route and listen along it with acoustic gear.

    Pressure Testing and Isolation in Mountain View

    We always start with isolation. Our tech closes the main, caps or valves off the hot and cold sides, and puts a gauge on each, then watches which side loses pressure over a set time. The gauge points us to the right line. It also rules out a toilet or faucet leak masquerading as a slab leak in your Mountain View, CA home.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Mountain View, CA

    A leaking hot line heats the concrete over it. A thermal camera shows that warm bloom through tile, vinyl and even thin carpet, often spreading outward from the leak point in a recognizable shape. Cold-side leaks will not show. We use it to narrow the search before the acoustic check.

It catches leaks the microphone cannot in Mountain View homes.
