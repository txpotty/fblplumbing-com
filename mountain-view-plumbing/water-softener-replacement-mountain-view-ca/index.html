    Metered Softener Replacement in Mountain View

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly used up. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first few weeks.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Mountain View

    A standard single tank softener replacement usually costs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Dual tank or premium systems usually run $3,000 to $5,000. Adding a new loop or drain line can raise the cost.

    Requirements vary by city. Simple replacements may not require one, but new plumbing connections may. We check local rules for you.

    Most softeners last 10 to 15 years. Resin and valves wear faster with very hard or chlorinated water.

    FBL Plumbing installs a wide range of trusted water softener brands, including Fleck, Autotrol, Culligan, Pelican and Watts. We select brands known for their reliability, efficiency and long-term performance, so you get a high-quality system that meets your needs.

    We test your hardness in grains per gallon and estimate daily water usage. That tells us the resin capacity needed.

    Spots on dishes, scale on faucets, dry skin and increased soap use can signal a failing softener. A softener that no longer uses salt or keeps flooding the brine tank may also need replacement.

    Many systems include 5 to 10 years on the valve and longer coverage on tanks. We also provide a warranty on our labor.
