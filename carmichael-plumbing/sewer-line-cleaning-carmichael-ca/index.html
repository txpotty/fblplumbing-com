    What Our Carmichael, CA Sewer Line Crews Handle

    Jetting Heavy Buildup in Carmichael

    Some lines need more than a cable. That is jetting work. A jetter cleans the entire pipe wall, which lasts much longer than a snake that only opens a path.

    Roots in Clay and Cast Iron in Carmichael

    Older clay and cast iron invite roots. Cracks let them in. We clear root growth with cutters and jetting and talk about lining or repair to stop the next round.

    Restaurant and Shop Sewer Lines in Carmichael, CA

    Commercial lines clog in their own ways. Grease in kitchens. We set up cleaning to fit the type of business, using larger equipment for bigger lines and heavier waste, working quickly to limit downtime.

    Root-Infested Sewer Line Cleaning in Carmichael, CA

    Roots are the top cause of blockages in established neighborhoods. They grow into a mat. After cutting the mass with a root saw, we jet the line to clear root hairs and leave the pipe clean, then show each entry point on camera.

    Routine Sewer Line Cleaning in Carmichael

    Routine cleaning clears slow buildup. Grease, paper and scale. Our tech runs a cable from the cleanout with a blade sized for the line, then jets the walls if needed, and most older lines do well with cleaning every one to two years.

    Sewer Line Cleaning Cost Compared to Replacement in Carmichael, CA

    Cleaning costs a few hundred dollars. Replacement costs thousands. If the pipe is structurally sound, regular cleaning is often the better value, but if the camera shows a collapse or a long belly, cleaning only buys time, and you see both prices.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Carmichael

    A camera shows the line in real time. Problems become obvious. Our inspection checks for sags, separated joints, roots and corrosion, and we mark each defect on the ground above, with a copy of the video for you.

    Identifying Signs of Sewer Line Clogs in Carmichael, CA

    Several drains backing up at once is the main sign. Listen for gurgling. Water coming up through a drain when you flush, a sewage smell in the yard or basement and slow drains throughout the house all point to a sewer line that needs cleaning.
