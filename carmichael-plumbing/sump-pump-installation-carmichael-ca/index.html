    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Carmichael, CA

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. A battery backup pump sits beside the primary and runs on its own float. A good battery carries it through most outages. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Carmichael

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. They fit pits where two separate pumps would crowd each other. Install time is shorter too. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Carmichael, CA

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. Expect more motor noise than a submersible. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Carmichael, CA

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. When we replace a pump we also check the basin for silt, look at the check valve flapper and run water through the discharge line to find any clogs. Undersized pumps get upsized. We fill the pit and watch it run.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Carmichael, CA

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get.     Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Carmichael
