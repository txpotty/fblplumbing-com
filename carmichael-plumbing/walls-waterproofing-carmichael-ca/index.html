    Waterproofing Costs vs. Repeat Water Damage in Carmichael, CA

    Paying once beats paying every two years.     Drainage System Integration in Carmichael, CA

    Water has to go somewhere once a wall is sealed. Exterior footing drains, interior drain tile and surface French drains all do the same job at different depths. We match the drain to where the water comes from, which might be a high water table, a yard that slopes toward the house, or a downspout emptying right at the foundation. We extend downspouts at least four feet from the wall.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Carmichael

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and often add a dehumidifier to keep relative humidity below 60 percent. Inland valleys get hot summers that work water heaters and outdoor lines hard, while coastal areas stay mild and damp year round. The joists stop rotting. Drier air under the floor also means less musty air upstairs.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Carmichael, CA

    Rubber washing machine hoses burst. We swap them for braided stainless hoses, put a drain pan under the washer with a line to a floor drain where one is available, and install a quarter-turn shutoff or an automatic leak valve on the supply box. Water heaters get a pan too. The wall behind them can take a moisture-resistant board and sealer.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Carmichael, CA

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and run it to a sump basin so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. The difference is that the water no longer ends up on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and works where a driveway or deck blocks the wall.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Carmichael, CA

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head aimed at the house instead of the bed, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. After that the wall gets cleaned, dried and coated with a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Carmichael

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, a membrane over cement board or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. Corners, niches and valve holes get extra fabric or preformed pieces.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Carmichael

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. A horizontal crack often signals soil pressure pushing the wall in, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Most vertical cracks get sealed in one visit.
