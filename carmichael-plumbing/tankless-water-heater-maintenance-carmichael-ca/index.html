    Condensing Unit and Neutralizer Service in Carmichael

    High-efficiency condensing tankless heaters produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized before it goes down a drain. We inspect the condensate trap, line and neutralizer cartridge and replace the media when it is spent. A clogged trap can cause shutdowns or water leaking inside the cabinet. We also check that the line is protected where it runs outdoors.

    Inlet Filter and Air Intake Cleaning in Carmichael

    Every tankless unit has an inlet water screen that catches grit and scale flakes. Those screens can clog with sand and debris, especially after municipal line work. We pull and clean the screen and inspect the air intake filter, which can load up with dust and dryer lint. Clean filters keep the flow sensor reading accurately and the burner breathing properly.

    Rinnai Tankless Heater Servicing in Carmichael

    Rinnai units are popular for their efficiency and compact design. Our Rinnai-specific maintenance includes cleaning the air intake filter, checking the flame sensor and verifying the operation of the bypass valve. We also inspect the combustion chamber and ensure proper gas combustion. Our technicians are familiar with Rinnai's diagnostic codes and can address any issues proactively, improving performance for your Carmichael home.

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Carmichael, CA

    Noritz tankless heaters are known for their robust build. Our descaling service for Noritz units focuses on their intricate heat exchangers, which can be prone to scale buildup from mineral-rich water. We use manufacturer-approved descaling agents and follow precise flushing procedures to avoid damage. We also inspect the fan motor and exhaust system for proper airflow, a common area for attention in Carmichael.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Carmichael

    The heat exchanger in a tankless heater is a coil of small tubes that water races through while the burner fires. Hard water drops calcium and magnesium on those hot surfaces, narrowing the passages over time. We connect a submersible pump and hoses to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with fresh water to rinse the system thoroughly. Restored flow means more stable temperatures and fewer shutdowns.

    Navien Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Carmichael

    Navien tankless water heaters often feature condensing technology, making them highly efficient but also requiring particular maintenance. Our tune-ups include checking the condensate trap for blockages, cleaning the secondary heat exchanger and verifying the operation of the circulator pump. We also confirm a proper air-fuel mixture and calibrate sensors for peak efficiency in Carmichael homes.
