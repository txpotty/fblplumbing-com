    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Carmichael, CA

    One leak on a newer copper or PEX line is a spot repair. Two or more leaks on galvanized is a replacement. Age matters too, and a line past about 40 years is usually near the end no matter the material. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars while a full replacement costs more and resets the clock for decades. We show you the pipe we exposed.

    Commercial Water Line Services in Carmichael, CA

    Besides repairs, FBL Plumbing handles service line upgrades when a commercial tenant needs more flow, for example when a restaurant adds fixtures or a gym adds showers. Bigger meters need bigger pipe. We size the line from the fixture count. Then we coordinate with the water provider on the tap and meter change, set the backflow assembly and get the inspection signed off, so the space can open on schedule.

    PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Carmichael

    Copper and PEX fail in different places. Copper tends to pinhole where a rock rubs it or where the water chemistry eats the wall from inside. PEX usually fails at a fitting. We cut out the bad section of copper and sweat or press in a new length, and for PEX we use the matching crimp or expansion tool so the joint is made the way the manufacturer intends.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Carmichael

    Buried copper is tough, but it is not forever. Pinholes show up where stones press on the pipe, and acidic soil or high water velocity can thin the wall from the inside. We usually cut out two feet. The new piece goes in with soldered or pressed couplings, then we look over the pipe on either side for more pitting before we backfill.

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Carmichael

    Trenchless work makes sense when the line runs under a driveway, patio or a row of mature shrubs. We dig a pit at the meter and another at the house. The new line travels between them without a trench. A pneumatic boring tool or a cable puller drags HDPE or PEX through the soil, or through the old pipe if it is a straight run, and most jobs wrap up in a single day.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Carmichael

    High static pressure shortens the life of the service line and every fixture behind it. The usual code limit is 80 psi. We gauge the pressure at a hose bib, then swap a failed regulator for a new Watts or Zurn Wilkins valve when it reads too high. Old gate valves that will not close get replaced with full-port ball valves. So the shutoff works when you need it.

    Main Water Line Repair in Carmichael

    Your main line is the pipe from the meter to the house, usually 3/4 inch or 1 inch, buried anywhere from a foot to several feet down depending on frost depth. When it fails you tend to see wet ground, a spinning meter and weak pressure at every tap. We start by closing the house shutoff and watching the dial. If it still moves, the leak is buried. Then a ground microphone narrows the spot, so the crew opens one small pit rather than trenching the whole run.

    Emergency Water Line Break Repair in Carmichael, CA

    A full break can flood the yard and leave the house dry at the same time. We shut it down first. The crew pumps the hole, finds the break and decides whether a coupling will do or whether a section needs replacing, setting up a temporary supply to the house if the job will take a while. Same-day repairs are the norm. We photograph the damage for your insurance or HOA.
