    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Carmichael

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater.     Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Carmichael, CA

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. FBL Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. They work best on very hard water.

    Water Softener Replacement in Carmichael, CA

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. We remove the old unit and install a new metered model, then check the loop and drain line while everything is apart. Salt use drops. You will notice soft water the same day.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in Carmichael

    Every one to two months is typical, depending on water use and hardness. A metered valve stretches each bag.

    Adding salt every 4 to 8 weeks is most of the work. A yearly checkup with a plumber catches worn seals, salt bridges in the brine tank and clogged injectors before they cause hard water. Do not let the salt run out.

    Three to six hours covers most standard installs. Bigger plumbing changes can stretch it to a day, with the water off as little as we can manage.
