    Granite and Quartz Composite Sinks in Carmichael

    Composite sinks are heavier than stainless and usually thicker at the drain, so standard strainers sometimes need a longer threaded body. We check the strainer fit first.     Stainless Sinks With Sound Pads in Carmichael

    Budget stainless sinks ring like a drum when dishes hit them or the disposal runs.     Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Carmichael

    A double bowl drains both sides through one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. The layout depends on your disposal and dishwasher, because the disposal side changes where the tee sits and how much slope each bowl gets. We use heavy-wall fittings.
