    Thermostat & Gas Valve Repair in Carmichael, CA

    If your gas water heater isn't heating water to the correct temperature, or if it is overheating, the thermostat or gas valve might be to blame.     Pilot Light Repair in Carmichael

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most common gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and clean or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are finished in about an hour.

    Gas Valve and Thermostat Repair in Carmichael, CA

    Issues with the gas valve or thermostat can lead to uneven water temperatures or a complete lack of hot water.     Burner Cleaning and Repair in Carmichael, CA

    A yellow, lazy or noisy flame signals a dirty burner or poor combustion air. We remove and clean the burner, check the orifice size for natural gas or propane and adjust as needed. A clean blue flame heats faster and produces less carbon monoxide. We also check for rust flakes dropping from the flue.

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Carmichael

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it detects vapors or overheating. Lint and dust in garages can clog the arrestor and trip the system. We clean the combustion chamber and replace sensors or thermal switches as needed.

    Tank Leaks and Pressure Problems in Carmichael

    Even gas water heaters can develop tank leaks, often due to corrosion from hard water or high water pressure.
