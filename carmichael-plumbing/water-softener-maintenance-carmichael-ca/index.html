    Control Valve and Injector Service in Carmichael, CA

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a full regeneration every time.

    Commercial Softener Maintenance in Carmichael

    Restaurants, laundromats, salons and car washes rely on soft water to protect equipment like dishwashers, ice machines and boilers. Commercial twin-tank systems regenerate often and need closer attention. We set up scheduled visits to test hardness, clean brine tanks and service valves. Consistent maintenance keeps equipment warranties valid and lowers repair costs.

    Brine Tank Cleaning and Salt Bridge Removal in Carmichael, CA

    The brine tank is crucial for salt dissolution and regeneration. Over time, salt residue and other impurities can accumulate, forming a 'salt bridge' that prevents the system from properly regenerating. Our maintenance service includes thorough cleaning of the brine tank and removal of any salt bridges, so your system can draw brine effectively for full softening. This is a frequent issue we address in Carmichael homes.

    Regeneration Settings and Hardness Testing in Carmichael, CA

    Many softeners are set incorrectly from day one or never updated when household size changes. We test your raw water hardness and program the valve to match your actual use. Metered systems regenerate based on gallons used, which saves salt compared to time clock models. Correct settings keep water soft without wasting salt or water.

    Water Softener Brand Servicing in Carmichael

    FBL Plumbing has broad experience maintaining all major water softener brands, including Culligan, Kenmore, Whirlpool, Fleck, Kinetico and more. Our technicians are familiar with the specific components and maintenance requirements of each manufacturer. Whether you have an older model or a brand-new high-efficiency unit, you can trust us to provide expert maintenance that keeps your system operating smoothly in Carmichael.

    Valve and Controller Diagnostics in Carmichael, CA

    The control valve and electronic controller regulate the entire softening process. Our technicians perform diagnostic checks to confirm these components are operating accurately, adjusting programming as needed. We check for wear and tear on moving parts and lubricate them to prevent future issues, keeping your water softener reliable in Carmichael, CA.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Maintenance in Carmichael

    Template assisted crystallization and other salt-free conditioners do not remove hardness but change how it behaves. They still need media replacement every few years and sediment prefilter changes much more often. We inspect the media tank, replace prefilters and check bypass valves. We also explain plainly how these systems differ from a true softener.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Carmichael

    Softener resin can foul with iron, manganese or sediment and lose capacity over time. Chlorine and chloramine in city water also slowly break resin down. We run a resin cleaner through the system and test performance afterward. If the resin is exhausted, we rebed the tank with fresh high-capacity resin, which costs far less than a new system.
