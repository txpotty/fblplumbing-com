    What a Toilet Replacement Covers in Modesto, CA

    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Modesto, CA

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs. They are noticeably louder than gravity toilets. When we replace one, we pick a vessel brand with parts that are easy to get later, and we check that house pressure is in the range the unit needs.

    Comfort-Height Toilet Replacement in Modesto

    Going from a standard bowl to a comfort-height model raises the seat two or three inches, which matters a lot for seniors and anyone recovering from knee or hip surgery. No drain changes are needed. Pair it with a grab bar if you can, and we can anchor that into a stud during the same visit.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Modesto

    Chair height toilets put the seat about 17 to 19 inches off the floor, roughly two inches higher than a standard bowl, and that makes a real difference for knees and hips. No plumbing changes are needed. Many people pair it with an elongated bowl and a soft-close seat, and the swap takes the same couple of hours as any other.

    Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in Modesto, CA

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Measure to the closet bolts, not the baseboard. If the new toilet's rough-in does not match, it either sits too far out or will not fit. Offset flanges can fix a small mismatch.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Modesto, CA

    Toilets made before 1994 often use 3.5 gallons a flush, and some from the 1970s use five or more. A WaterSense model uses 1.28 gallons or less, and the better ones still clear the bowl in one try because the trapway and flush valve are larger than they used to be. Savings show up fast. Check with your water provider about rebates.

    Gravity-Flush Toilet Replacement in Modesto

    Gravity-flush toilets are the simplest and most forgiving, and a well-made one with a 3 inch flush valve will outflush most of the older toilets it replaces. Parts are easy to find. If the floor is uneven, we shim it level, then caulk the base and test several full flushes.

    One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Modesto, CA

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, so there are fewer leak points and fewer crevices to clean. They cost more and weigh more. A one-piece toilet usually takes two people to carry upstairs, and we set it without dragging it across your tile so the floor stays unscratched and the wax ring lands centered on the flange.

    Commercial Flush Valve Upgrades in Modesto

    Manual flush valves on older commercial toilets leak at the handle and stick open, and swapping to a sensor valve during a replacement improves hygiene without changing the rough-in. Battery and hardwired models are both available. Work can happen after hours, and we check that the new fixtures meet accessibility requirements.
