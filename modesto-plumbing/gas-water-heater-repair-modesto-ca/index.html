    Tank Leaks and Pressure Problems in Modesto, CA

    Water on the floor near the heater does not always mean the tank is gone. A lot of the time it is the drain valve, the T&P valve or a dripping supply fitting.     Pilot Light Repair in Modesto

    The thermocouple is the first thing we test when a pilot will not stay lit. A millivolt meter tells us in about a minute whether it is producing enough current. If it reads fine, the next suspects are a clogged pilot orifice, a cracked piezo wire or a thermal cutoff that tripped because the combustion chamber overheated from blocked air. Most of these jobs run about an hour.

    Bradford White & Rheem Gas Water Heater Repair in Modesto, CA

    Bradford White and Rheem tanks make up a big share of what we repair in Modesto, CA. Most Rheem gas units run a White-Rodgers or Honeywell valve with a blinking status LED. Bradford White uses its own sealed combustion design with a lint screen at the base and a thermal switch, and it needs a specific kit when the pilot assembly goes bad. Both kits ride on the truck. Using factory parts keeps your tank warranty valid.

    Propane Water Heater Repair in Modesto

    Propane and natural gas heaters look the same but burn at different pressures. Propane units need a matching orifice and a control set near 11 inches of water column. Natural gas runs closer to 7 inches, so a heater converted badly, or a regulator that drifted, gives you soot, a yellow flame and a pilot that keeps going out for no obvious reason. A manometer reading tells us the supply pressure. Wrong parts get swapped for the right ones.

    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Modesto

    Power vent heaters push exhaust through PVC with a blower on top of the tank. The usual failures are the blower motor, the pressure switch and the hot surface igniter. Condensate pooling in a vent with the wrong slope can also trip the pressure switch, so we check the pipe run all the way to the termination before we sign off on a part. The control's error code is our starting point. Every suspect part then gets tested with a meter.

    All Major Gas Water Heater Brands Serviced in Modesto

    Every brand label we see in garages and closets uses one of a handful of gas control designs. Rheem and Ruud share most parts, and State shares a lot with A.O. Smith.     Pilot Light & Burner Issues in Modesto

    A pilot that lights, holds for ten seconds and then goes out is almost always a thermocouple or a weak magnet in the gas valve. A pilot that will not light at all is more often a clogged orifice or a dead igniter. Main burner problems show up another way. The pilot stays lit but the main flame never comes on, which points to the thermostat side of the valve, a tripped thermal cutoff or a flame arrestor so clogged with lint that the burner starves for air. We test each piece in that order.
