    Recirculation Pump Repair in Modesto

    Recirculation cuts the wait for hot water at a far bathroom from a minute or more to a few seconds. A dead pump or a stuck check valve means you get long waits again or lukewarm water in the cold line. We test the pump, look for a thermal bypass valve under the farthest sink, and set the schedule so it runs around your mornings and evenings. Two settings, mostly.

    Rinnai Error Code Repair in Modesto

    Rinnai owners usually call about a code 10, which points to an air supply or exhaust blockage. Bird nests and dryer lint at the terminal come first, then pull the front cover to inspect the intake screen and the combustion fan. Vent runs longer than the manual allows can trigger it too. We measure.

    No Hot Water Tankless Repair in Modesto

    No hot water at all usually points to ignition, gas supply or a heat exchanger choked with scale. The service menu's error history is where we start, then test the igniter, flame rod and gas pressure in that order, because a weak flame signal and low pressure can look identical on the display. We look at the vent run as well. Common igniters and flame rods ride on the truck.

    Navien Condensate and Fan Repair in Modesto

    Navien's E016 is a heat exchanger overheat, and scale or a backed-up condensate line is usually behind it. We also read fan speed from the service menu and compare it to spec, since a fan that cannot reach target RPM will cause flame and exhaust faults on the same unit. Clean trap, clean screen, retest.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Repair in Modesto

    Restaurants and laundromats in Modesto often run two to six tankless units on a common manifold. One unit down means the others work harder and scale faster, so we check the cascade controller, the isolation valves on each heater and the water quality coming in. Downtime costs money. We schedule around service hours when we can.

    Electric Tankless Element Repair in Modesto, CA

    Electric units fail in their own way. Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart models use two to four heating elements in separate chambers, and one burned element means weak heat at high flow, while a failed flow switch means no heat at all. We check each element's resistance, read voltage under load and clamp amperage on each leg, then confirm the panel and wire size match the unit's rating.

    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Modesto

    Rinnai shows an LC code and Navien posts a scale warning when the exchanger runs hot from mineral buildup. Resetting the code without a flush only buys a few days, because the scale is still in there. We connect a circulation pump to the service valves, circulate descaling solution through the exchanger for close to an hour, then flush and clean the inlet screen. Yearly is right for most hard-water homes.
