    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Modesto

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while fracturing the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is very resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Modesto homes. This method is well suited when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.
