    Urinal Rough-In for New Restrooms in Modesto

    Tenant improvements need rough-in before the walls close. We space urinals 30 inches apart and 15 inches from side walls, then set carriers and run all the lines. We stay in touch with the GC daily. Rough inspection comes first.

    Sloan Flush Valve Installation in Modesto, CA

    Sloan Royal valves are on most commercial urinals we see. We install them with the correct inside parts kit for the bowl's rated volume, set the control stop so the flush is strong without splashing, and keep rebuild kits on the truck for later service. Sensor versions mount the same way. Battery models need no wiring.

    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Modesto

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. ADA units need a rim at or below 17 inches, a 13-1/2 inch projection and 30 by 48 inches of clear floor. Flush controls must be reachable. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Waterless Urinal Installation in Modesto

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. They use a cartridge or a layer of sealing liquid that lets urine pass while blocking sewer gas, and the cartridge has to be swapped on schedule or the restroom starts to smell. Your custodial team needs training. We show them how.

    Old Urinal Removal and Replacement in Modesto

    Old urinals come off harder than they went on. Corroded hanger bolts and a flush valve seized to the spud mean we come prepared to cut, and we protect the wall tile while we do it so the new fixture does not sit against broken edges. New gaskets always go in. The flange gets cleaned to bare metal.

    Rough-In Plumbing for Urinals in Modesto, CA

    Rough-in decides whether the finish goes smoothly. The carrier, 2 inch waste, 3/4 inch supply and vent all go in to the spec sheet before drywall. The rough gets inspected before cover. Then the walls close.

    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Modesto, CA

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. A flushometer from Sloan or Zurn delivers a measured volume from a 3/4 inch supply with no tank, and it recovers instantly for the next user, which matters in a stadium or school restroom. The volume matches the fixture rating. Wrong settings stain bowls.

    American Standard and Kohler Urinal Installation in Modesto

    American Standard and Kohler make the china we install most. Washbrook and Bardon models come in several flush ratings, and the valve has to match the bowl. The spec sheet makes matching easy. We order both together.
