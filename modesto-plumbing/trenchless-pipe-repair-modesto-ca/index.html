    Trenchless repair can cost more per foot of pipe than open trenching. However, once you add the cost of restoring driveways, patios, landscaping and irrigation, trenchless is often the better value. It is also faster and less disruptive. We compare both methods with real numbers for your home.

    Pipe Bursting in Modesto, CA

    Pipe bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. A bursting head is pulled through the old pipe, fracturing it outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind. The new pipe is fused into one continuous length with no joints. Bursting requires an entry and exit pit but avoids digging the full length.

    Lateral and Spot Lining in Modesto

    Not every pipe needs to be lined end to end. When damage is limited to a few joints or a short section, we can install a short sectional liner. This costs less than a full-length liner and is completed quickly. We verify the rest of the line is sound before recommending a spot liner.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Modesto

    CIPP lining is a proven trenchless method where we insert a resin-saturated felt tube into the existing damaged pipe. The liner is then cured in place using heat or air, creating a new, seamless pipe within the old one. This method is excellent for repairing cracks, breaks, and root intrusion without digging up your yard in Modesto.

    CIPP lining involves cleaning the old pipe, then inserting a flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin. The liner is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light.
