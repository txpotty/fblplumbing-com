    PEX Water Line Replacement in Modesto

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. It does not corrode or build scale.     Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Modesto, CA

    Bursting fits old cast iron or galvanized lines that will shatter under the head. Copper and poly are better pulled than burst.     Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Modesto, CA

    Polybutylene is the gray or blue plastic pipe installed from roughly the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. Chlorine in the water breaks it down from the inside. The failures tend to be sudden splits rather than slow drips, and some home insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Worth replacing.

    PEX Water Line Installation in Modesto, CA

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house. Freezing is uncommon along the coast and on valley floors in Northern California, but frosty nights do happen and can crack unprotected outdoor pipes. A frozen PEX line often survives where copper would crack. We still bury it below the frost depth the local code sets, and keep it covered from sunlight at the meter box, since UV light breaks it down. Short exposure only.
