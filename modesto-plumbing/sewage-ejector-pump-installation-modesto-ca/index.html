    What Goes Into an Ejector Install in Modesto, CA

    Grinder Pump Installation in Modesto

    Grinder pumps chop waste into slurry before pumping it, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. They are the right choice when the sewer is far away, uphill or under pressure, like on a pressure sewer system where the pump feeds a shared force main in the street. Grinders run pricier. For a basement bath near the drain, a standard ejector usually works.

    Liberty Pumps Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Modesto, CA

    Liberty builds single and dual pump packages that arrive with the basin, pump, lid and fittings matched. Matched packages speed up the install, and Liberty's float design is less likely to snag on the basin wall, a common reason older pumps fail to start. Stuck floats are common.

    Emergency Ejector Pump Replacement in Modesto, CA

    A dead ejector pump means the basement fixtures stop working until it is fixed. Stop using those fixtures, including the washer if it drains to the pit, then call (669) 319-2034 and we will try to get a plumber and a replacement pump to you that day. Most swaps take a few hours.

    Upflush and Macerating Toilet Systems in Modesto

    Macerating systems like Saniflo let you add a toilet and sink without breaking the concrete floor. It sits behind the toilet, grinds waste and pumps it through a small line, often three-quarter or one inch, to a drain above, which suits a small half bath. They need a vent too.

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Modesto

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps look alike in a catalog but do very different jobs. Sump pumps push groundwater out to the yard, ejectors push sewage to the sewer, and only the ejector pit must be sealed and vented against gas. Never mix the two.

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Modesto, CA

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars produce gray water that still needs to be lifted to the sewer. A smaller laundry tray pump or a compact ejector can handle it, sized for the 15 or more gallons a washer dumps at once, with a lint filter so fibers do not wrap the impeller. Lint kills small pumps.

    Emergency Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Modesto

Shut off water to the basement fixtures, keep everyone from flushing below grade, and call us, since most emergency pump replacements are done in a few hours once the plumber is on site. Trucks stock popular models.

    Commercial Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Modesto, CA

    Businesses in Modesto, CA with restrooms, kitchens or break rooms below grade need ejector systems that keep up with heavier, steadier use than a house. Commercial setups often run duplex pumps with an alternating panel, so one pump can fail and the other keeps the restrooms open. Downtime costs money. Each pump can carry the load alone.
