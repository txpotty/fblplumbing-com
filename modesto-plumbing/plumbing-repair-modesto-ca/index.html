    What We Fix in Modesto, CA Homes

    Under-Slab Line Repair in Modesto, CA

    Older neighborhoods in Northern California include prewar and postwar homes with galvanized supply lines and cast iron or clay sewer pipe that are past the end of their life. Copper or PEX under the slab can run for decades until a fitting or a rubbed spot gives out. Our leak tech pins down the spot with acoustic and thermal gear, and you get each repair option priced next to the others. Epoxy lining is one choice. Rerouting through the attic is often cleaner.

    Sewer Pipe Repair in Modesto, CA

    Clay and Orangeburg laterals crack and separate as the ground shifts, and roots find those gaps within a season or two. A camera and locator mark exactly where and how deep the break sits. Repair options run from a traditional dig to pipe bursting or a cured-in-place liner. We camera it again before backfilling.

    Water Softener Repair in Modesto, CA

    Rural properties around Modesto often rely on private wells, which may need treatment for iron, sediment, or other minerals. White crust on faucets and spotty glasses are the first signs a softener has stopped working. Most of the time it is a salt bridge or a plugged injector. FBL Plumbing repairs the common brands and tests the water hardness before and after the fix so you know it is working again. Resin lasts ten to fifteen years.

    Sewer Line Repair in Modesto, CA

    Roots are the usual cause of a sewer line that backs up every few months. They slip into a cracked joint, then grow into a mat that snags paper and grease. We camera the line to see the damage and mark the depth on the surface, then choose between an open dig and a trenchless method. Clay pipe usually goes first.

    Slab Leak Repair in Modesto, CA

    A warm spot on tile, a meter that spins with every tap off, or a water bill that jumps for no reason. Those are slab leak signs. We pinpoint the leak with acoustic listening gear and a thermal camera before anyone breaks concrete. Options include a spot repair through the slab or a reroute overhead, each priced separately.

    Water Heater Parts Repair in Modesto

    Rumbling or popping from a tank means sediment on the bottom. A rotten-egg smell in the hot water usually means the anode rod is reacting with the water. We replace thermocouples, thermostats, elements, gas valves, anode rods and relief valves from truck stock, which covers the large majority of tank repairs we see in a year. Rust at the seams is different. That tank is done, and we will say so.

    Drain and Pipe Repair in Modesto

    Hydro-jetting at around 4,000 psi clears grease and scale that a cable snake only pokes a small hole through, which is why the clog keeps coming back. FBL Plumbing follows every jetting job with a camera so you can see whether the pipe is sound. Cracks and offset joints get repaired by section in copper, PEX, PVC or ABS. A broken pipe needs a repair, not more jetting.
