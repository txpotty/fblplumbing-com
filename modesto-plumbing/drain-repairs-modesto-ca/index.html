    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Modesto

    Simple trap or slip-joint repairs usually cost $150 to $350. Replacing a section of drain pipe usually runs $400 to $1,200, and cast iron stack replacement can range from $2,000 to $6,000 depending on access. We provide a written price before we start.

    While chemical drain cleaners are widely available, we usually advise against using them. They often contain harsh chemicals that can damage your pipes, especially older ones, and are typically only a temporary fix. For effective and safe clog removal, professional methods are always the better choice.

    Minor drain repairs, such as clearing a simple clog, usually do not require a permit in Modesto. However, significant repairs involving altering or replacing major sections of your plumbing system, especially main sewer lines, may require permits. FBL Plumbing handles all necessary permit applications for these projects.

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water to clear out stubborn blockages, grease buildup, mineral deposits and even tree roots from your pipes. It is often used for severe clogs or as a preventive measure to thoroughly clean drain lines, leaving them almost like new.

    Harsh chemical cleaners can soften older ABS glue joints and corrode metal drain parts with repeated use. They also rarely clear grease or scale fully. A mechanical auger or hydro-jet is safer and more effective.

    Drain cleaning removes a blockage from an otherwise sound pipe. Drain repair fixes the pipe itself, such as a crack, bad slope, broken fitting or missing vent. Many recurring clogs need repair, not more snaking.

    To prevent future drain problems, avoid pouring grease down sinks, use drain screens to catch hair and food particles and be mindful of what goes down your toilets. Regular professional drain cleaning every one to two years can also help head off clogs and extend the life of your plumbing system.
