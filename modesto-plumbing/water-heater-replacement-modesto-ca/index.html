    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Modesto

    The cost of water heater replacement in Modesto, CA usually ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the type of unit (gas, electric, tankless or hybrid), its capacity and any needed plumbing modifications. Our team will provide a detailed, upfront quote after assessing your needs and existing setup.

    Tank water heaters often last about 8 to 12 years, and hard water and sediment buildup can shorten that. Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running mineral-heavy. Annual flushing and anode rod checks can help lengthen that life.

    Common signs include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, a metallic taste in hot water, strange noises like rumbling or popping from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit or a water heater that is over 10 to 12 years old. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a licensed plumber.

    Manufacturer tank warranties usually range from 6 to 12 years depending on the model. We also warranty our installation labor.

    Most replacements take two to four hours. Attic installs or relocations can take more time.

    FBL Plumbing installs and services all major brands, including Bradford White, Rheem, AO Smith, Rinnai, Navien and State Water Heaters. We can help you choose the best brand and model that suits your budget and hot water demands.

    Most standard tank-style water heater replacements can be completed in 3 to 5 hours, assuming no major complications or plumbing reconfigurations. Tankless or hybrid installations might take a bit longer, often 5 to 8 hours, due to more complex venting or gas line changes.

    While it might seem tempting, water heater replacement involves gas lines, high voltage electricity, water pressure and heavy equipment. It is a complex job best left to licensed professionals to ensure safety, proper installation and compliance with local plumbing codes, preventing potential leaks or hazards.
