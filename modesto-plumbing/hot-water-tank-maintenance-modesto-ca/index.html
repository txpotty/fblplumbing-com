    Relief Valve Testing in Modesto

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Modesto

    Electric Element Check in Modesto

    Electric tanks have two elements, usually 4,500 watts at 240 volts, and the lower one does most of the work and takes most of the scale. We test each one with a meter.     Electric Thermostat Service in Modesto

    Each element on an electric tank has its own thermostat, and the upper one carries a reset button for the high-limit switch. A tripped reset is a symptom. We trace the cause, often a shorted element or a stuck thermostat, before resetting it. Power stays off at the breaker the whole time.

    Anode Upgrades for Odor in Modesto, CA
