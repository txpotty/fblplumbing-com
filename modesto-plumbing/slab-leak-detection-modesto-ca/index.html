    Pressure Testing and Isolation in Modesto

    Pressure isolation is always the first step. Our tech closes the main, caps or valves off the hot and cold sides, and puts a gauge on each, then watches which one drops over fifteen to thirty minutes. That tells us where to look. It also rules out a toilet or faucet leak masquerading as a slab leak in your Modesto, CA home.

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Modesto

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. Our ground microphones hear it. We move the pickup across the floor in a grid over the traced pipe route, finding where the sound is loudest and confirming it from another position, which works best on copper and less well on PEX.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Modesto, CA

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. That makes them harder to spot. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, which is why we isolate the cold line, trace its route and listen along it with acoustic gear.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Modesto, CA

    Hot water leaking under the slab warms the floor above it. A thermal camera shows that warm bloom through tile, vinyl and even thin carpet, often spreading outward from the leak point in a recognizable shape. Cold-side leaks will not show. We use it to narrow the search before the acoustic check.

    Supply and Drain Leaks Under the Slab in Modesto, CA

    A supply leak and a drain leak show different signs. Supply leaks are pressurized, so they move the meter, warm the floor on the hot side and run whether or not anyone uses water. Drain leaks only leak when water goes down, showing up as smells, sluggish drains or wet spots near bathrooms. We test both kinds in your Modesto, CA home.
