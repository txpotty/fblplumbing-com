    What a Plumbing Inspection Covers in Modesto, CA

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Modesto

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. Warm spots are another clue. Meter tests, pressure loss checks and a look for damp baseboards come first, and slab homes get acoustic and thermal follow-up. Early slab leak finds save floors and foundations.

    Preventive Maintenance Checks in Modesto, CA

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. The visit covers flushing the heater, working the shutoffs, reading pressure and checking for slow drains and small leaks. It takes about an hour. Doing this every year or two adds years to a water heater and keeps the shutoffs working for the day you really need them.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Modesto, CA

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. We run a high-resolution camera from a cleanout to the city connection or septic tank. The video shows offsets, bellies, roots and breaks along with their depth and location, and you keep a copy of the recording for your records or for a contractor.

    Pre-Purchase Plumbing Report in Modesto

    Before buying a home in Modesto, a plumber's inspection can keep you from inheriting a sewer replacement. Our report lists every finding with a photo, a plain description and a repair range, so your agent can use it in negotiations right away. Most buyers get it the same day. It fits tight closing schedules.

    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Modesto, CA

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. High pressure ages fixtures fast. Anything over 80 psi cuts the life of hoses, fixtures and heaters, and we note the supply pipe type, from copper and CPVC to poly, Kitec and galvanized.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Modesto

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. Venting and gas connections get checked. We also look at the expansion tank, drain pan and softener, and a hardness test shows whether the softener is working or just eating salt.

    Pre-Purchase Plumbing Inspections in Modesto

    A house can pass a general inspection and still have a sewer full of roots. Our report tells buyers what pipe the house has, what the sewer looks like and how old the heater is, while there is still time to act. That leaves room to negotiate. Old galvanized, a damaged sewer or an aging heater can all become a repair request or a closing credit.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspections in Modesto, CA

    A sewer camera shows the inside of the main drain without digging. We push the camera in from a cleanout, watch for cracks, roots, offsets and low spots and record the whole run to the street or septic tank. A locator marks the depth. Earthquakes and hillside soil movement are real risks in Northern California, so flexible connections and seismic straps help protect plumbing from sudden movement.
