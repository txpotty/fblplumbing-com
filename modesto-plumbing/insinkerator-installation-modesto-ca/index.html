    Careful InSinkErator Installation in Modesto, CA

    Standard InSinkErator Installation in Modesto

    A standard install replaces the unit under an existing sink. The old disposal comes down, the flange gets scraped clean and a new one goes in on fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on. The unit hangs on the ring, locks with a quarter turn, and the discharge tube meets the trap on a new slip-joint washer. Power comes from a cord kit or the existing hardwire, checked for ground before the breaker goes back on.

    Commercial Disposer Installation in Modesto, CA

    Restaurant disposals are a different class of machine. A commercial disposer runs 1 to 5 HP on its own circuit, and many come with a wall control panel and a water solenoid. We set the unit, plumb the water feed and coordinate with the electrician on the circuit. Expect an inspector to check it. Local code may also limit where a disposer can discharge relative to the grease interceptor, so we confirm that before the job starts.

    Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Modesto

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. Flip the switch and it runs until you flip it off. Batch feed only runs with its stopper turned in place, so nobody can reach into a running unit, which some families with small kids prefer and which needs no wall switch at all. Each type gets wired its own way.

    First-Time Disposal Installation in Modesto

    Kitchens that never had a disposal need some rework. First, the sink gets a disposal flange. Then the drain gets reworked, and a switched outlet goes in, with an electrician if there is no power in the cabinet yet. Drain slope to the stack gets checked as well.

    Upgrading Your InSinkErator in Modesto, CA

    Moving up from a 1/3 HP builder unit to a 3/4 HP Evolution changes more than the grind. Expect the new unit to sit lower in the cabinet. That extra height can put the discharge outlet below the trap arm, so we measure the drop before install day and lower the trap or the arm if the numbers do not work. We also confirm the circuit can carry the larger motor.

    Evolution Upgrade Installs in Modesto, CA

    Quiet is the main thing people notice after an Evolution install. Insulation around the motor drops the noise a lot. What they notice later is fewer clogs downstream, because the multi-stage grind breaks fibrous scraps into much smaller pieces before they reach the trap and the branch line. We check for vibration with the unit running.

    Evolution Series Installation in Modesto

    Evolution units grind in two or three stages and carry extra sound insulation around the motor housing, which is why they sit taller and weigh more than a Badger. Clearance under the bowl matters here. Before install day we measure the drop from sink to trap arm so the unit fits. Tight cabinets suit the Compact, while the Excel wants the most space.
