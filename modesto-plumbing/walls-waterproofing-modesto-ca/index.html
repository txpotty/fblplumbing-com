    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in Modesto, CA

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Modesto, CA

    Rubber washing machine hoses burst. We swap them for braided stainless hoses, put a drain pan under the washer piped to a floor drain where one is available, and install a quarter-turn shutoff or an automatic leak valve on the supply box. Water heaters get a pan too. The wall behind them can take a moisture-resistant board and sealer.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Modesto

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, lapped 12 inches at the seams and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and fasten it with termination bar. It is a cheap fix that makes a big difference.

    Commercial Wall Waterproofing in Modesto

    Restaurant kitchens, gym locker rooms and salon wash stations put water on walls all day. We waterproof commercial showers, mop sink walls and restroom wet walls with membranes rated for constant water exposure, and we repair the supply and drain piping behind them when we find a leak during demo. We can work after hours.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Modesto

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, a membrane over cement board or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. We reinforce corners, niches and valve penetrations with fabric or preformed pieces.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Modesto

    Crack injection takes about two hours per crack. We set injection ports every 8 to 12 inches along the crack, seal the surface with epoxy paste, and pump in resin starting at the lowest port and working up until it pushes out the next port. Polyurethane flexes a bit if the wall shifts, and epoxy bonds the two sides back together. The surface paste gets ground off once it cures.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Modesto

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. We find it with a moisture meter, a thermal camera that shows the cool wet spot, and a pressure test on the supply lines. Then we cut a clean access hole. The bad fitting or section of pipe gets replaced. We pull wet insulation and run fans until the cavity reads dry.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Modesto, CA

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head aimed at the house instead of the bed, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. After that the wall gets cleaned, dried and coated with a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Modesto

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and usually add a dehumidifier to hold humidity under 60 percent. Coastal fog and moisture can speed corrosion on exposed fittings, so outdoor valves and hose bibs deserve an occasional check. Wood rot slows to a stop. Drier air under the floor also means less musty air upstairs.
