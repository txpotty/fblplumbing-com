    Septic Baffle and Tee Repair in Modesto

    Baffles and tees keep scum and solids in the tank so they do not flow out into the leach field. Concrete baffles crumble from hydrogen sulfide gas, and plastic tees can break off. When they fail, solids clog the field fast. We replace damaged baffles with durable PVC tees and add an effluent filter where appropriate.

    Emergency Septic Repair in Modesto

    Sewage backing up into tubs or floor drains is a health hazard that needs prompt attention. We respond to emergency septic calls, find the blockage and restore flow.     Septic Tank Alarm Troubleshooting in Modesto

    Many modern septic systems, especially those with pumps or advanced treatment units, include alarms to indicate high water levels or system malfunctions. If your septic alarm is sounding in Modesto, it is a clear sign of trouble. FBL Plumbing technicians can accurately troubleshoot the cause of the alarm, whether it is a float switch issue, pump failure or a clogged line, and perform the needed repairs to silence the alarm and restore system balance.

    Homes with uphill or distant fields often use an effluent pump in a pump tank. Failed pumps, stuck floats or tripped breakers trigger high water alarms. We test the pump, float switches and control panel and replace failed parts. A working alarm gives you warning before sewage backs up.
