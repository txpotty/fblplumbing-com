    What We Fix on Faucets in Napa, CA

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Napa

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. The hose usually splits near the quick-connect fitting. Brand-matched hoses, heads and weights go in, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.

    Cracked Faucet Body Repair in Napa

    Cracked faucet bodies, usually from freezing or an old brass casting that finally gave out, cannot be repaired and need a replacement faucet. Turn off the stops under the sink. A new faucet can usually go in on the same visit, and we will check the supply lines and stops while the water is off.

    Delta and Moen Faucet Repair in Napa, CA

    Delta and Moen are the faucets we repair most, and both back their cartridges with lifetime warranties for the original owner on many models. We can check whether yours qualifies. Kohler, Pfister and American Standard each use proprietary cartridges, and we stock the common ones for each brand.

    Bathroom Pop-Up Drain Repair in Napa

    Pop-up drains on bathroom sinks fail at the pivot ball, the clevis strap, the lift rod or the stopper itself, and a slow drain often turns out to be hair wrapped around the stopper. Pull the stopper first. We replace the pop-up assembly in a matching finish when the old one is corroded or the pivot leaks.

    Commercial Faucet Repair in Napa, CA

    Restaurant and office faucets cycle hundreds of times a day, so cartridges, handle springs, metering valves and sensor solenoids wear out far faster than they do in a house. Commercial cartridges are on the truck. We set metering faucets to run long enough for a full rinse without wasting water, and we can work after hours.

    Low Water Pressure Faucet Fix in Napa, CA

    Weak flow at one faucet usually points to a clogged aerator or a cartridge packed with scale, not a problem with the house pressure. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. The aerator comes off with a key and gets cleaned or replaced, then check the cartridge screens. Low flow everywhere is a different problem.

    Specialty Faucet Repair in Napa, CA

    Pot fillers, laundry tub faucets and wall-mount utility faucets each have their own valve styles, and many use compression stems or quarter-turn cartridges that hardware stores do not stock. The common stems ride on the truck. For filtered water faucets, we replace the cartridge or the check valve as needed.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Napa

    One drip a second adds up to more than 3,000 gallons a year, roughly the water used in 180 showers. That waste lands on your water bill. We find the worn washer, O-ring or cartridge and replace it, then check the cabinet below for swelling or stains from the slow leak.
