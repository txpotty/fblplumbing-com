    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in Napa, CA

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. A Noritz tune-up starts at the exhaust, where we look for soot and blocked terminations. Then we check the fan and the combustion air path. Last, we check the temperature setting and run a few fixtures at once, which shows whether the unit holds temperature between back-to-back showers.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in Napa, CA

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. On a Rinnai an LC code means scale, on a Navien E003 means no ignition, and on a Noritz a 90 usually means an air or exhaust problem. We pull the code history before touching anything. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.

    Noritz Descaling in Napa, CA

    Noritz exchangers scale up fast on hard water. We use a descaler that the manufacturer approves and keep the cycle inside the time limit in the manual, because too strong a mix can etch the metal. The fan motor and exhaust path get checked while the pump runs.

    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in Napa

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing. The pump pulls solution from a bucket, through the cold port, out the hot port and back again. After the cycle we rinse with clean water and pull the inlet screen. Then we check for leaks.

    Same-Day Tankless Help in Napa, CA

    Service Valve Kit Installs in Napa, CA

    Plenty of tankless heaters went in without service valves, and without them there is no clean way to hook up a descaling pump.
