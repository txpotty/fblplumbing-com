    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Napa, CA

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. Even a small drip wastes water and can stain a garage floor or rot a cabinet. O-rings and fittings get replaced and pressure tested. A cracked mineral tank usually means replacement.

    Control Valve Rebuild in Napa

    Service, backwash, brine and rinse are the four cycles the control valve directs every time the softener regenerates, usually in the middle of the night. Over the years, seals, spacers and pistons wear down. Common signs are leaks at the head or constant drain flow. The valve gets rebuilt with the kit made for it.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Napa, CA

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. A new quality softener installed typically costs $1,500 to $3,500. With a good tank and valve, repair is the better value. If the valve is obsolete and parts are scarce, replacing the head or the whole unit may make more sense than chasing used parts online.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Napa, CA

    A crust of salt across the brine tank stops regeneration, and hard water shows up at every tap within a day or two. We break up and remove the bridge and clean the tank. Older brine tanks can crack, and we repair or replace those too. Fill the tank halfway.

    Hard Water Symptoms and Diagnostics in Napa

    Scale on faucets means trouble. FBL Plumbing checks hardness at the tap, inspects every component and gives you a repair plan for your Napa home. A hardness test strip tells the story in seconds, and we run one before the repair and again after so you can see the drop in grains per gallon.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Napa

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. An old timer head regenerates by the calendar, used or not. After retesting hardness, we reprogram the valve or swap in a metered head that regenerates only when needed. Salt bills drop.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Napa

We break the bridge, clean out the mushy salt and check the float and brine well. A cracked brine tank gets swapped for a new one.
