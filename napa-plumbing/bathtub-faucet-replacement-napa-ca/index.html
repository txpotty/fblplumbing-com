    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Napa, CA

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. When someone flushes a toilet, the valve cuts hot and cold flow in proportion so the mix stays steady, and a thermostatic valve goes further by holding a set temperature even as the water heater cools. Both meet anti-scald code. Thermostatic costs more.

    Tub Faucets in Older Homes in Napa

    Older homes hide odd tub plumbing. Expect rusted galvanized supplies, valves with no stops, and steel spout nipples that snap. We bring transition fittings. Replacing a short run of galvanized is common.

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Napa, CA

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. Unlike a pressure-balance valve, which only reacts to pressure changes, a thermostatic valve senses the actual water temperature and adjusts the mix, which matters for kids and older adults who cannot react fast to a hot spike. Volume and temperature often get separate handles. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Napa

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently. A single-handle valve often takes new trim and a cartridge from the front, while a three-handle valve usually has to be cut out of the wall and replaced with a modern pressure-balance body. We check before quoting. Two-handle models fall in between.

    Replace or Keep Repairing in Napa

    Repeated repairs add up.     Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Napa

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler. The supply lines come up through the floor under the filler, which means cutting the slab or working from a crawlspace or basement, and the filler needs a mounting bracket anchored to the subfloor. Deck-mount fillers sit on the rim. Tub-only fillers need a temperature limit.
