    What Our Gas Leak Repair Visits Cover in Napa, CA

    Gas Meter to House Line Repair in Napa, CA

    The run from the utility meter to the house shutoff is the homeowner's pipe in most service areas, so the repair falls to a plumber and not the gas company. That stretch sees more weather than any other pipe in the system. Risers rust at grade. We replace the riser or the buried run, pressure test it and leave the meter connection ready for the utility to open the meter. Expect a half day for most riser swap.

    Red Tag Gas Repairs in Napa

    A red tag on your meter or appliance means the utility found a leak or unsafe condition and will not restore gas until a licensed contractor fixes it. We read the tag first, since it lists what the utility found. Then we test everything. Once the repair is done, we perform the required test and send the documentation to the utility or the inspector. Relights are scheduled with the utility, usually within a day or two.

    Residential Gas Leak Detection in Napa

    A gas leak in a house rarely sits where the smell is strongest, because gas drifts along joists and collects in closets and basements. The first step is a pressure gauge on the piping, which tells us whether the system is actually losing gas. Then the sniffer comes out. Our combustible gas detector reads in parts per million, so we can follow the reading fitting by fitting until it peaks. Bubble solution on the suspect joint gives the final answer.

    Propane Gas Leak Repair in Napa, CA

    Propane behaves differently from natural gas because it is heavier than air, so a leak settles into basements and crawlspaces instead of rising. We check both regulators, the one at the tank and the one at the house. Low spots get sniffed first. If the supplier owns the tank, they handle the tank side and we handle the piping from there to the appliances. Rural properties with underground tanks get a line test too.

    Commercial Gas Leak Repair in Napa

    Commercial gas piping often runs at 2 psi or higher with regulators at each appliance bank, so a leak test there is not the same as a house test. Regulator vents and lockup pressure get checked along with every joint. Rooftop units leak too. Where downtime costs you money, we isolate only the affected branch and keep the rest of the building running. Staff get a short walkthrough on the shutoff locations.

    Corrugated Stainless Steel Tubing Repair in Napa, CA

    CSST is the yellow-jacketed flexible tubing used in many newer homes, and it can be damaged by a nearby lightning strike if it was never bonded. The bond is a heavy copper wire tying the gas pipe to the house ground. Plenty of older installs lack it. Repairs use the same brand's fittings as the existing tubing, since manufacturers do not allow mixing parts. A splice that skips that rule fails the inspection.

    Gas Line Repair & Replacement in Napa, CA

    Black iron pipe with threaded fittings is still the most common interior gas pipe, and the leak is nearly always at a thread, not in the middle of a length. The joint gets broken, cleaned and remade with new pipe dope rated for gas. Cracked fittings get replaced. Where a length has rusted badly, usually near a damp crawlspace vent, we cut back to sound pipe and splice in new. Every remade joint gets a detector check and a gauge test.

    Gas Leak Detection in Napa

    Some leaks announce themselves with a hiss you can hear across the room, and others lose a few cubic feet a day and only show up on the bill. Slow leaks are what the test gauge is for. We isolate, then sniff. Testing happens with the appliance valves shut and again with them open, since some leaks hide past a valve. Finding the exact joint keeps the repair small.
