    Tree Root Removal in Napa, CA Sewers

    Tree roots are a common culprit of sewer line blockages, especially in Napa, CA neighborhoods where mature trees are prevalent. Roots seek out moisture and can infiltrate even tiny cracks in sewer pipes, growing and eventually blocking the entire line. FBL Plumbing uses dedicated root cutting tools attached to our drain augers and high-pressure hydro jetting to remove these invasive roots, restoring flow and preventing further damage. We can also explain long-term solutions to protect your pipes.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Napa

    Some homes have no accessible cleanout, which forces techs to pull a toilet or go through a roof vent.     Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Napa, CA

    Grease from kitchens cools and coats the inside of sewer pipes, and minerals in the water bond with it to form a stubborn crust. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. Over years the pipe opening shrinks until flow slows to a trickle. A cable can punch a hole through, but jetting cleans the walls back to full size. We choose the method based on pipe material and condition. Cleaning before the line fully closes spares you money and stress.

    The main sewer line is essential for carrying wastewater away from your home. Blockages in this line can cause widespread backups affecting all fixtures.     Root Removal From Sewer Lines in Napa

    Trees and shrubs send roots toward any moisture they can find, and a sewer joint is a steady source during dry months. Thirsty yard trees and shrubs are common culprits in local yards.     Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to thoroughly clean the inside of your sewer pipes, removing grease, sludge, and roots. It is used for stubborn blockages, preventative maintenance, or when traditional snaking is not sufficient to restore full pipe diameter.
