    Water Heater Leak Detection and Prevention in Napa, CA

    Leaks are a frequent issue that can lead to significant water damage if not caught early. Our maintenance service includes a thorough inspection of all water heater connections, pipes, and the tank itself for any signs of leaks or corrosion.     Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Napa

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part in your water heater designed to corrode instead of the tank itself. Depending on your water chemistry, these rods can wear down faster than expected. During maintenance, we inspect the anode rod's condition and recommend replacement if necessary. A healthy anode rod is key for preventing rust and extending the lifespan of your water heater tank, saving you from a costly full replacement.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Napa

    Tankless units are especially sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. We isolate the unit with its service valves and circulate a descaling solution with a pump for the recommended time. We also clean the inlet water filter and check the unit's error history. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures consistent.
