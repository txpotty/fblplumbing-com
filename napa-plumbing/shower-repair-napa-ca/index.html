    Fixing Showers in Napa, CA

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Napa

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year. In a single-handle shower the cause is almost always the cartridge or its O-rings, and on an old two-handle valve it is the stem washer and the seat. We carry the common parts. Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister cartridges cover most of the valves we open, so the repair is usually done that same visit.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Napa

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. Water that keeps coming out after the handle is off has to be getting past a worn cartridge or seat, so replacing the showerhead alone will not stop it. We fix the valve. Leaks at the arm threads are different, and those get new thread tape and a snug turn.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Napa, CA

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. A worn spout gate or a tired diverter stem lets water keep running from the spout, and the head pressure drops off. We replace the spout or stem. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Napa, CA

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. We pull the strainer and run a hand auger through the trap, and if the clog sits past the trap in the branch line, we switch to a drum machine with a smaller cable. Chemical cleaners rarely reach it. A hair catcher over the strainer keeps the next one from forming.

    Slow Shower Drain Repair in Napa, CA

    Standing water at your ankles is the first sign. Minerals bind soap scum and hair into a tough mat that clings to the pipe walls and gets thicker with every shower. We cut it out with an auger. Heavy buildup further down gets cleared with a water jetter instead of harsh chemicals.

    Same-Day Shower Leak Repair in Napa, CA

    Shut off the main first if water is getting where it should not. For a sudden wall leak or a valve that will not close, the main valve near the meter is the first move. Then call us. Our plumber stops the water, fixes it or makes it safe and checks for wet areas.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Napa, CA

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. Cleaning often works. A clogged cartridge gets replaced, then we test the flow.

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Napa

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck. That spool is supposed to slide back and forth to keep the hot and cold mix even when pressure drops on one side, and scale can freeze it in place. A new cartridge fixes it. If you cannot get the water hot enough, the anti-scald limit stop may just need adjusting.
