    Choosing between Badger and Evolution is where we start based on how you cook, how much noise bothers you and how much cabinet room you have.     Batch-Feed Units for Kitchens Without a Switch in Napa

    These disposals run only with the stopper lid seated, which adds safety for families with kids. Because they need no wall switch, they suit kitchens where a new switch would mean cutting drywall or a tile backsplash. The drain hookup is the usual setup.

    First-Time Disposal Installation in Napa

    If your kitchen has never had a disposal, we start by checking the sink flange, drain height and electrical supply.     Batch-Feed Disposal Installation in Napa, CA

    A batch-feed disposal will not start until the lid is in, and the lid holds a magnet that closes a switch inside the unit. No wall switch is needed, which makes them handy in kitchens where running a new switch would mean opening finished walls. You grind one load at a time. Parents of young kids often think that is worth it.

    Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in Napa, CA

    Going from batch-feed to continuous-feed means adding a way to switch it on. That can be a wall switch, an air switch button on the sink deck or a switched outlet under the sink, and the choice depends on how hard it is to run wire to the spot you want. Air switches avoid new wiring in the wall. The plumbing side is usually a straight swap.
