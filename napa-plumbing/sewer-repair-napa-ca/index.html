    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Napa

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. We record the whole run.     Sewer Line Backups and Blockages in Napa

    Water coming up in the tub when the washer drains is a classic main line symptom. The blockage sits downstream of every fixture.     Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Napa, CA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Waste collects in the dip. Expansive clay soils are common across Northern California, and seasonal swelling and shrinking can crack slabs and buried sewer laterals. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and set it back to a quarter inch of fall per foot, which is the typical code minimum for a four-inch line. A shallow belly sometimes just needs regular cleaning.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Napa

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and it is the standard prep step before any pipe lining job. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.

    After-Hours Sewer Repair in Napa

    Main lines like to fail on holidays. Our phones stay on. If it is structural, we schedule the dig for the earliest safe window. Daylight digs are safer around buried utilities.

    Commercial Sewer Repair in Napa

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Napa
