    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Napa

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most reliable method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Expansive clay soils are common across Northern California, and seasonal swelling and shrinking can shift slabs and buried sewer laterals.     Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Napa

    Many homes in Napa have crawl spaces, which are highly susceptible to moisture issues. Installing a heavy-duty vapor barrier on crawl space walls and floors is a key waterproofing strategy. This thick polyethylene sheeting stops ground moisture from evaporating into the crawl space, protecting subflooring and joists and improving indoor air quality. It's an effective way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.
