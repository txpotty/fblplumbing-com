    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Napa, CA

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a secure seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can be repaired from kinks with a heat gun. It is our preferred choice for many full repipes.

    Hydronic Heating with PEX in Napa

    PEX is also a leading choice for hydronic heating systems, including radiant floor heating. Its ability to withstand high temperatures and resist corrosion makes it perfect for circulating hot water. If you are considering radiant heat for a specific area of your home or business in Napa, PEX is the pipe to use.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Napa, CA

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, much like a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when several fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to balance cost and performance.
