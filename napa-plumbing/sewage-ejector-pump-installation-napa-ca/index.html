    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Napa

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. Sump pumps push groundwater out to the yard, ejectors push sewage to the sewer, and only the ejector pit must be sealed and vented against gas. Never mix the two.

    Residential ejector systems in Napa serve basement bathrooms, lower-level suites and any fixture that sits too low to drain by gravity to the sewer.     Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Napa, CA

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes may need two-pump setups with an alternating control panel. Redundancy matters here.
