    Basement plumbing leak repairs in Napa typically cost $200 to $1,500, depending on the pipe and how hard it is to reach. Exposed pipe on an unfinished ceiling is cheap.     Most supply and drain repairs are finished in a few hours. Sump installs take most of a day. Stack and slab repairs run one to two days because surfaces get opened and patched.

    Yes, every repair carries a written workmanship warranty. Pumps and parts also carry the maker's warranty.     For sump pumps we mostly install Zoeller and Liberty, with Wayne for some battery backups. Cast iron housings outlast plastic ones. For crack injection we use two-part polyurethane or epoxy kits from established commercial suppliers, and for interior drains we use perforated pipe with a dimpled wall membrane from the same manufacturer so the pieces fit together.

    Most plumbing leak repairs in a basement are done in one visit. Expect two to four hours with drywall access. Seepage work ranges from half a day for one crack to several days for a perimeter drain.

    Simple pipe repairs and crack injection usually do not need a permit. Bigger jobs often do. Replacing a drain stack, installing a new sump with a discharge line, adding a backwater valve, or excavating near the foundation may require a permit, and we pull it when it is needed.

    Look for damp spots on walls or floors, a musty smell, and white chalky deposits on concrete. That white crust is mineral left after water dries. A rising water bill or rust at the water heater base points toward a plumbing leak. Smell often comes first.
