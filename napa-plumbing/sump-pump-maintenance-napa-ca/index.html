    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Maintenance in Napa

    A standard sump pump inspection and cleaning typically runs $125 to $250. Replacing a float switch or check valve usually adds $75 to $250 depending on parts.

    A standard sump pump maintenance appointment typically takes about 30 to 60 minutes. This time allows for a complete inspection, cleaning and testing of all components. If minor repairs are needed, the visit might run a little longer.

    Routine maintenance usually does not require a permit. However, if a significant repair involves altering the discharge line or replacing the entire unit, especially if electrical work is involved, a local permit might be necessary. We handle all permit requirements for you.

    Not every home does, but homes with basements, crawlspaces, below-grade garages or low-lying lots often rely on them. Strong winter storms can bring days of heavy rain to Northern California, saturating ground and pushing water into crawlspaces and sewer lines.

    The best time is a few weeks before your wettest season starts. Homes that see snow may also want a fall check before winter storms and snowmelt.

    If your primary sump pump fails during a power outage and you don't have a working battery backup or water-powered backup system, your basement is at risk of flooding. This underscores the importance of maintaining backup systems.

    The check valve is essential because it prevents water that has been pumped out from flowing back into the sump pit after the pump shuts off.
