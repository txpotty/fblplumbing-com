    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Folsom, CA

    Submersible sump pumps are designed to sit directly in the sump pit, submerged in water, which makes them quieter and less intrusive. They are powerful and effective for basements that take on a lot of water. Our team in Folsom, CA will install your submersible pump with precision, ensuring proper sealing, discharge piping and electrical connections for quiet, reliable operation, which matters most in finished basements.

    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Folsom

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a solid fit for narrow pits in older homes. They usually last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we usually recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod set to your pit depth.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Folsom

    The cost of basement sump pump installation in Folsom usually ranges from $800 to $2,000, depending on the type of pump, the complexity of the installation and whether a new sump pit needs to be dug. Battery backup systems or long discharge line runs can raise the final price. We provide a detailed, upfront estimate after an on-site assessment.

    Swapping a pump in an existing pit typically takes two to three hours. A new pit with slab cutting and concrete patching usually takes most of a day.

    Discharge should go to your yard, a dry well or an approved drainage area at least 10 feet from the foundation. Connecting a sump pump to the sanitary sewer is usually not allowed by local codes.

    Most residential basements need a 1/3 or 1/2 horsepower pump, but the right answer depends on how fast water enters the pit and how high it must be lifted. We measure both before recommending a model.

    A submersible sump pump sits entirely within the sump pit, making it quieter and less obtrusive. A pedestal sump pump has its motor above the pit, making it easier to access for maintenance and usually more affordable. Both types are effective for removing water from your basement.

    Replacing a pump generally does not need a permit, but new pits, new discharge routes or new electrical circuits may. We check local requirements and pull permits when they apply.

    We install a wide range of well-built, reliable sump pump brands, including Zoeller, Wayne, Little Giant and Liberty Pumps. We can help you pick the best brand and model for your budget and water management needs in your Folsom home, considering horsepower and features.

    FBL Plumbing offers a comprehensive warranty on our basement sump pump installations, covering both parts and labor. The specific warranty period will be detailed in your service agreement. We stand behind our work and the quality of the products we install in your Folsom home.
