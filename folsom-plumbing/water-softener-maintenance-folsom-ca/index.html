    Expert Water Softener Maintenance Services in Folsom, CA

    Control Valve and Injector Service in Folsom, CA

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a full regeneration every time.

    Regeneration Settings and Hardness Testing in Folsom, CA

    Many softeners are set incorrectly from day one or never updated when household size changes. We test your raw water hardness and program the valve to match your actual use. Metered systems regenerate based on gallons used, which saves salt compared to time clock models. Correct settings keep water soft without squandering salt or water.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Maintenance in Folsom

    Template assisted crystallization and other salt-free conditioners do not remove hardness but change how it behaves. They still need media replacement every few years and sediment prefilter changes much more often. We inspect the media tank, replace prefilters and check bypass valves. We also explain plainly how these systems differ from a true softener.

    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repair in Folsom, CA

    A leaking bypass valve can let hard water slip past the softener without you noticing. We test and rebuild bypass valves and replace cracked fittings. We also inspect the drain line for kinks and proper air gap and the brine overflow line for correct routing. These small fixes head off leaks and backflow problems.

    Preventative Maintenance for All Brands in Folsom, CA

    Whether you have a Fleck, Culligan, Kenmore, GE or EcoPure system, our technicians are trained to service all major water softener brands. Our preventive maintenance protocols are adapted to the specific requirements of each manufacturer, so your unit receives the best possible care, regardless of its make or model in Folsom, CA.

    Brine Tank Cleaning and Salt Bridge Removal in Folsom, CA

    The brine tank is essential for salt dissolution and regeneration. Over time, salt residue and other impurities can build up, forming a 'salt bridge' that prevents the system from properly regenerating. Our maintenance service includes thorough cleaning of the brine tank and removal of any salt bridges, so your system can draw brine effectively for full softening. This is a common issue we address in Folsom homes.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Folsom

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, controlling regeneration cycles and water flow. Our maintenance service includes complete diagnostics of the control valve's programming and mechanical components. We check for proper timing, motor function and potential blockages. If issues are found, we can often repair or replace worn parts to restore full function, avoiding the need for a costly full system replacement in Folsom.

    Maintenance Versus New Softener Costs in Folsom, CA

    A maintenance visit usually costs far less than a new system, which often runs $1,200 to $3,500 installed. Resin rebedding typically costs $300 to $700 and can bring an older tank back to full capacity. Most quality softeners last 10 to 15 years with service. We will tell you when a repair makes sense and when a replacement is the better investment.
