    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Folsom

    Salt-based (ion exchange) water softeners are the most common and effective solution for fully softening water. These systems use a resin bed to exchange hard minerals like calcium and magnesium with sodium ions. FBL Plumbing expertly installs many models of salt-based softeners, ensuring proper connection to your main water line, correct drainage setup and accurate programming for regeneration cycles in your Folsom home.

    Softener Loop Installation in Folsom

    A softener loop is the plumbing connection that routes your main water line through a softener. Many newer homes include a loop, but older homes often do not. We cut into the main line, install the loop and add a bypass valve. We keep outdoor hose bibs and irrigation on hard water when possible to save salt. A proper loop makes future service simple.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Folsom

    For homeowners looking for an alternative to traditional softeners, we also offer salt-free water conditioner installation. These systems condition the water to prevent scale buildup without removing beneficial minerals or adding salt. While not true softeners, they are an excellent option for scale prevention and are popular in many Folsom households.
