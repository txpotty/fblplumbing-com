    Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Folsom

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. Start at the water heater. You may also see it under sinks, near the main shutoff or coming through the garage wall, and we trace runs through the attic to confirm how much of the house it serves.

    Fitting Failure and Emergency Repair in Folsom

    Acetal plastic and copper crimp ring fittings are often the first part of a poly system to fail. Attic fittings fail hard. After an emergency repair we schedule the full replacement, since removing the poly system is the only lasting fix.

    Polybutylene Leak Repair in Folsom

    Repairs on polybutylene are temporary by nature, because the pipe next to the repair is the same age. We still make them. We cut out the failed piece and splice in PEX with listed fittings, stopping the leak and giving you time to plan the full job.

    Why Polybutylene Fails in Folsom

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. The inner wall of the pipe and fittings slowly turns brittle, and small cracks grow until the pipe splits. Rural properties around Folsom often rely on private wells, which may need treatment for iron, sediment, or other minerals. Hot water speeds it up. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.
