    Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Folsom

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. You may see it at the water heater, under sinks or near the main shutoff. We inspect visible lines, check the stamps and trace runs through the attic. You get a clear answer and a written estimate for replacement.

    Fitting Failure and Emergency Repair in Folsom

    Acetal plastic and copper crimp ring fittings are often the first part of a poly system to fail. A burst fitting in an attic or wall can cause major damage in minutes. We respond to emergency leaks, make temporary repairs and then schedule a full replacement. Stopping the problem for good is the only permanent fix.

    Polybutylene Leak Detection and Repair in Folsom

    Before a full replacement, or if you suspect polybutylene problems, our team offers modern leak detection services. We can pinpoint the exact location of leaks, often hidden behind walls or under floors. While repairs on polybutylene are temporary, identifying the source helps in planning a complete replacement strategy for your Folsom property.

    Understanding Polybutylene Failure in Folsom

    Polybutylene's built-in weakness stems from its chemical reaction with oxidants like chlorine, commonly used in municipal water treatment. Over time, this reaction causes the pipes and their plastic fittings to become brittle, leading to hairline cracks and eventual rupture. Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running mineral-heavy. Hard water can also add stress to pipe that is already weakened. Understanding this failure mechanism is crucial for diagnosing issues and recommending the most effective and permanent replacement strategy for your home.

    Insurance and Home Sale Documentation in Folsom

    Some insurers decline or surcharge policies on homes with polybutylene. Buyers and lenders may also ask about it during inspections. After replacement we provide permit records, invoices and photos showing the new system. That paperwork helps with insurance renewals and home sales.

    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Folsom, CA

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. We use Uponor PEX-A with expansion fittings that do not restrict flow. New lines run from the main shutoff to every fixture, water heater and appliance. Most homes are completed in two to four working days.
