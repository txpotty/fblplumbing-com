    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Maintenance in Folsom

    The cost for sump pump maintenance in Folsom, CA typically ranges from $100 to $250, depending on the pump type and its condition. This price generally includes a thorough inspection, cleaning and operational testing. Any necessary repairs or parts replacement would be an additional cost, which we would quote upfront.

    With proper maintenance, a quality sump pump can last 7 to 10 years. Neglecting maintenance can greatly shorten its lifespan, leading to premature failure and costly replacements.

    Most visits take 45 minutes to an hour. Replacing a check valve or float on the same visit adds a little time.

    Watch for a pump that runs constantly, hums without moving water, cycles very often or makes grinding noises. Rust, a stuck float or a musty smell around the pit are also warning signs.

    A standard sump pump maintenance appointment usually takes about 30 to 60 minutes. This time allows for a complete inspection, cleaning and testing of all components. If minor repairs are needed, the visit might run slightly longer.

    The check valve is crucial because it prevents water that has been pumped out from flowing back into the sump pit after the pump shuts off. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short cycle, leading to premature wear and higher energy use. Our maintenance includes checking its function.

    Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running hard. Where groundwater is hard, mineral deposits can build up on the pump's impeller and in the pit, reducing efficiency and possibly causing clogs or damage over time. Regular maintenance helps reduce these effects.
