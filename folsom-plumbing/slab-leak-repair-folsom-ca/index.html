    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Folsom

    For more complex or recurring slab leaks, or when the original piping is severely deteriorated, pipe rerouting is an effective long-term solution. This method involves abandoning the leaky pipe under the slab and installing new plumbing lines through walls or the attic, bypassing the foundation entirely. This removes future slab leak risks from that particular line and is often preferred for older homes in Folsom with aging copper or galvanized pipes.

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Folsom, CA

    In some cases, especially when the leak is under a load-bearing wall or in a hard-to-reach area, tunneling beneath the foundation is the preferred method. This lets our plumbers reach the damaged pipe from outside your home, avoiding disruption to your interior floors and finishes. Tunneling spares the interior but calls for skilled excavation and shoring to protect structural integrity throughout the repair process in Folsom, CA.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Folsom
