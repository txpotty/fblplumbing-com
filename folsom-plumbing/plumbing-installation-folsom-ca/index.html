    A water heater or softener install usually takes a few hours. Gas line runs and water line replacements can take a day, while whole-home repipes may take several days.

    A sump pump is designed to remove clear groundwater that collects in a sump pit, typically in a basement or crawl space, to prevent flooding.     PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible plastic tubing used for water supply lines. It's popular for installations in Folsom because it's corrosion-resistant, less prone to bursting from freezing than copper, and often quicker to install. Its flexibility allows for fewer fittings, reducing potential leak points and making it a dependable choice for Northern California homes.

    In Folsom, most backflow preventers are required to be tested annually by a certified technician to confirm they are functioning correctly. This is essential for maintaining the safety of your public water supply. FBL Plumbing offers convenient annual testing and certification services.
