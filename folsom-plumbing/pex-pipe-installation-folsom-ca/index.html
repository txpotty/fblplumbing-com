    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Folsom, CA

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is notorious for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Many insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to sell and insure.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in Folsom, CA

    PEX repipes typically cost less than copper repipes because the material is less expensive and installs faster. The total depends on the number of bathrooms, the home's size and how accessible the attic and walls are. Drywall patching and texture matching are sometimes included or quoted separately. We give you a detailed estimate so you can compare PEX-A, PEX-B and copper options side by side.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Folsom

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a good option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer reliable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to confirm a proper seal.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Folsom, CA

    FBL Plumbing is experienced with installing all types of PEX piping, including PEX-A, PEX-B and PEX-C, each with distinct properties for specific applications. We help you choose the best PEX type for your home's needs and budget in Folsom, CA.     PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Folsom, CA

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a tight seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can recover from kinks with a heat gun. It is our favored choice for many full repipes.

    Whole-Home PEX Repiping in Folsom

    If your home has old, corroded or leaking pipes made of galvanized steel or polybutylene, a whole-home PEX repipe is an excellent solution. We remove the outdated piping and install a complete PEX system, improving water pressure and water quality and eliminating costly leaks. This complete service leaves your entire plumbing system modern, efficient and built to last for decades in your Folsom home.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Folsom, CA

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, similar to a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when multiple fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to weigh cost and performance.
