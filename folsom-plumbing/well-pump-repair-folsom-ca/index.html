    Pressure Switch Replacement in Folsom

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, a clogged sensing tube and ant nests are the usual reasons one fails. After swapping the switch and clearing the tube, we set cut-in and cut-out to match the tank, usually 40 and 60 psi. One of the cheaper well repairs.

    Control Box and Surge Repair in Folsom, CA

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. Tree roots seek out sewer laterals during dry spells, which is why heavy rains often reveal cracked lines through sudden backups. Surges from lightning are the usual cause of control box failures. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection, replace what failed and recommend a surge arrestor at the box if one is missing. Arrestors are cheap.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Folsom, CA

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps early. The air charge gets checked and the bladder inspected, then the tank is recharged or replaced so the pump runs a full minute per cycle. Steadier pressure follows.
