    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Folsom, CA

    Many older homes in Folsom, CA still have galvanized steel pipes, which are vulnerable to corrosion, rust, and mineral buildup. This leads to low water pressure, discolored water, and eventual leaks. Our team specializes in the complete removal and replacement of these outdated systems with modern PEX or copper piping. This upgrade not only resolves existing issues but also greatly improves water quality and flow, boosting your home's value and plumbing reliability for decades to come.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Folsom, CA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and very durable piping material that's gaining popularity for repiping projects. FBL Plumbing specializes in PEX pipe replacement in Folsom, CA, offering a system that resists freezing, corrosion, and scale buildup. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points. PEX also noticeably dampens water hammer noise, providing a quieter plumbing system for your home.

    Copper Repiping in Folsom, CA

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes. Joints are soldered with lead-free solder or pressed with ProPress fittings. Copper repipes typically cost more and take a bit longer than PEX.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Folsom

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab. This is a good option for newer homes or a first-time leak. If the home has had multiple leaks, we will explain why a full repipe may save you money.

    Emergency Repipe and Leak Repair in Folsom

    While a full repipe is a planned project, sometimes a major pipe burst or multiple severe leaks can call for immediate action. Our repipe specialists in Folsom are equipped to handle emergency situations, providing temporary fixes and assessing the need for a more complete repiping solution.     Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Folsom, CA

    A whole-home PEX repipe for an average home often runs $5,000 to $12,000, with copper usually costing more. A single slab leak repair can run $1,000 to $4,000 including access and restoration.
