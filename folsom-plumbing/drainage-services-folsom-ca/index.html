    Downspout and Roof Drain Solutions in Folsom

    Gutters and scuppers concentrate a lot of roof water into a few points, usually right next to the foundation.     Main Line and Sewer Drainage in Folsom, CA

    When several fixtures back up at once, the problem is most often in the main line to the city sewer or septic.     Kitchen, Bath and Laundry Drain Clearing in Folsom

    Slow sinks, gurgling tubs and overflowing laundry standpipes usually come from grease, hair, soap scum and mineral scale. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. We clear lines with the right size cable for each fixture and avoid harsh chemicals that damage pipes. If clogs keep coming back, we camera the line to find out why.

    French Drain Installation in Folsom

    A French drain is a gravel-filled trench with perforated pipe that collects water and carries it away from problem areas. It works well along foundations, behind retaining walls and in low spots that hold water after heavy rain. We wrap the gravel in filter fabric so fine soil does not clog the system. The drain is sloped to an outlet, dry well or permitted discharge point.
