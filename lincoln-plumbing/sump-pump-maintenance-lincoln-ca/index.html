    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Lincoln, CA

    The float switch is the most common reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get stuck.

    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Lincoln, CA

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil around the slab or foundation.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Maintenance in Lincoln

    FBL Plumbing is experienced in servicing Zoeller sump pumps, a widely trusted brand known for its durability. We stock common replacement parts for efficient repairs, keeping your Zoeller pump in peak condition.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Maintenance in Lincoln

    A standard sump pump inspection and cleaning usually runs $125 to $250. Replacing a float switch or check valve typically adds $75 to $250 depending on parts.

    The check valve is crucial because it prevents water that has been pumped out from flowing back into the sump pit after the pump shuts off. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short cycle, leading to premature wear and higher energy use. Our maintenance includes checking its function.

    We recommend having your sump pump professionally maintained at least once a year. If your home is in an area prone to heavy rainfall or has a high water table, twice-yearly maintenance might be more worthwhile. Regular checks confirm it's ready for peak seasons.

    Watch out for unusual noises, such as grinding or rattling, that point to debris or a failing motor. If the pump runs constantly, fails to turn on or gives off a foul odor from the pit, it's clearly time for a professional check. Visible rust or corrosion are also red flags.

    Our technicians are experienced with all major sump pump brands, including Zoeller, Wayne, Little Giant, Basement Watchdog, Liberty Pumps and more. We carry common replacement parts for most popular models.

    Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. Where groundwater is hard, mineral deposits can build up on the pump's impeller and in the pit, reducing efficiency and potentially causing clogs or damage over time. Regular maintenance helps reduce these effects.

    The best time is a few weeks before your wettest season begins. Homes that see snow may also want a fall check before winter storms and snowmelt.

    While you can perform basic checks like making sure the pump is plugged in and the discharge pipe isn't blocked, professional maintenance goes much deeper.
