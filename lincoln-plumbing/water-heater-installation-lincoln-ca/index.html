    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Lincoln

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. Each gas swap gets a new connector, a drip leg, a draft test at the hood and a leak check with a detector at every fitting. Carbon monoxide is the risk. All work follows local code.

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Lincoln

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it.     Gas Water Heater Installation in Lincoln, CA

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. We install the gas line, the sediment trap and the vent connector, then check draft at the hood with a match or a smoke pencil to confirm exhaust is going up and out. Leak checks follow. Expansion tanks go in where code requires.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Lincoln, CA

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. They need roughly 700 cubic feet of air around them and a condensate drain, so a warm garage or a large utility room usually works better than a closet. Rebates may apply. We will tell you if your space works.

    Water Heater Installation Cost in Lincoln, CA

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Lincoln

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Each one gets a new gas connector, a sediment trap and a shutoff valve, plus a vent connector pitched up toward the flue at a quarter inch per foot. Then a draft test. Gas suits busy households.

    Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in Lincoln
