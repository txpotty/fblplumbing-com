    What a Plumbing Checkup Covers in Lincoln, CA

    Sump Pump Testing in Lincoln, CA

    Basements flood when the pump fails during a storm. Tree roots seek out sewer laterals during dry spells, which is why heavy rains often reveal cracked lines through sudden backups. We pour water into the pit to make sure the float lifts and the pump kicks on, check the discharge line for clogs or freezing risk, and inspect the check valve so water does not drain back in. Backup pumps get tested too.

    Water Softener Service in Lincoln

    A softener only helps if it regenerates. Salt level, brine tank, injector and a test regeneration all get checked. Hardness gets tested after. A softener that quietly stopped working lets scale back into every pipe and appliance.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Lincoln

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale builds up inside the heat exchanger, especially with hard water, and the unit starts throwing error codes, running lukewarm or cutting out mid-shower long before it actually wears out. A small pump circulates descaler. The inlet filter, venting and gas line get checked too.

    Drain Cleaning as Maintenance in Lincoln

    Slow drains rarely fix themselves. A yearly camera look and a cleaning on known problem lines, especially near big trees, keeps grease and roots from building to the point where the main backs up during a holiday. Jetting helps on grease. Root-prone lines go on a schedule.

    Commercial Plumbing Maintenance in Lincoln, CA

    Businesses cannot close for a plumbing failure. Restaurants, offices and retail spaces in Lincoln run bigger heaters, more restrooms and grease lines that wear faster and cost more when they fail mid-day. Scheduled visits prevent that. Off-hours visits are available.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Lincoln

    Sediment is the tank's slow killer. Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running mineral-heavy. We drain and flush the tank, pull and check the anode rod, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, and look over the gas or electric connections for corrosion and wear. Flushing takes about an hour. A clean tank heats faster and lasts years longer.

    Repipe Assessments in Lincoln, CA

    Polybutylene and old galvanized pipe leak more. During maintenance we identify your supply pipe material, look for pinhole leaks, rust stains and pressure drops, and tell you honestly whether repiping is something to plan for now or years from now. PEX and copper are the options. Repiping can be done in stages.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Lincoln

    Backflow devices protect the drinking water. Many water providers require yearly testing on irrigation and commercial backflow preventers, and our certified testers check each check valve and relief valve and file the results with your utility. Failed devices get rebuilt. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where needed. Testing on time avoids fines.
