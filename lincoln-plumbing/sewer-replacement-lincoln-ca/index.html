    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Lincoln

    Cast iron sewer pipe can corrode from the inside, creating rough surfaces and holes. Scale buildup catches debris and causes clogs. We replace cast iron with PVC or HDPE. This restores flow and ends corrosion. Many homes built before 1975 have cast iron lines.

    Under-Slab Sewer Replacement in Lincoln

    Many homes are built on slab foundations with drain lines underneath. When these lines fail, replacement requires tunneling or rerouting. We evaluate options to minimize impact on your home. Rerouting through walls or attic space is sometimes possible for branch lines. We explain the cost and disruption of each approach.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Lincoln, CA

    A cleanout provides easy access for future cleaning and inspection. Many older homes lack an exterior cleanout. We install one during sewer replacement. This saves time and money on future maintenance. It also helps in emergencies.

    Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in Lincoln, CA

    Tree roots are a leading cause of sewer line damage, especially with older, more porous pipe materials like clay or cast iron. Roots can penetrate tiny cracks, grow inside the pipe, and eventually cause blockages or even burst the line. Our sewer replacement services specifically address root-damaged pipes, often recommending more resistant materials like PVC or ABS to prevent future intrusions. We also advise on tree placement to protect your new sewer system.
