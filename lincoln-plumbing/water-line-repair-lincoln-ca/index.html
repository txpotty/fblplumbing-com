    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Lincoln

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off quickly in an emergency.

    Water Pressure Restoration in Lincoln

    Low water pressure can be a frustrating symptom of an underlying water line problem, such as blockages, leaks, or a failing pressure regulator. In Lincoln, if your water pressure has dropped suddenly, it's important to have your main water line inspected. We diagnose the cause of low pressure, whether it's mineral buildup restricting flow or a hidden leak, and perform the necessary repairs or replacements to restore strong, consistent water pressure throughout your home.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Lincoln

    If your service line runs under a driveway, pool deck or mature landscaping, trenchless replacement can save a lot of restoration cost. We dig small pits at each end and pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through using a pneumatic boring tool or pipe pulling equipment. The process usually takes one day. You keep your pavers and trees, and the new line carries a lengthy manufacturer warranty.

    Main Water Line Leak Repair in Lincoln

    Most main line leaks show up as a soggy strip of yard, a sudden jump in your water bill or a meter dial that moves with every fixture off. We confirm the leak by isolating the house shutoff and watching the meter. Then we use ground microphones to find the exact spot. A single section is cut out and replaced with repair couplings or a new length of pipe, and the line is pressure tested before backfill.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Lincoln

    Slab leaks, where water lines running beneath your home's concrete foundation leak, are a especially challenging issue. Newer homes across Northern California mostly use PEX or copper with plastic drains, and many owners are repiping older houses to improve water pressure. These leaks can undermine your foundation, leading to major structural damage. We use specialized listening devices and camera inspections to locate slab leaks precisely and offer repair options including direct slab penetration, re-routing, or pipe lining. Addressing a slab leak promptly in Lincoln is crucial to prevent extensive, costly foundation damage.
