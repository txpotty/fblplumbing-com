    Toilet Replacement and Upgrades in Lincoln, CA

    Swapping an old 3.5 gallon toilet for a modern one is the easiest water savings in the house. The old unit comes out in about twenty minutes. The rest is flange cleanup and shimming the new bowl level on a floor that has settled with age. We haul the old one away.

    Rough-In Toilet Installation in Lincoln, CA

    Rough-in is the plumbing that goes in before walls and floors close up. For a toilet that means a 3 inch drain with a vent, plus a cold supply stub. We set the drain 12 inches from the framed wall plus the thickness of drywall, so the finished rough-in lands right where the fixture expects it. Then the inspector sees it open.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Lincoln, CA

    Comfort height bowls stand about two inches taller than standard ones. Think about who will use it before you buy. Shorter adults and kids sometimes find the taller seat leaves their feet dangling, while anyone with bad knees or a hip replacement usually finds it much easier to sit and stand. Installation is the same as a standard bowl.

    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Lincoln

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. Below about 20 psi, they flush poorly. We check static pressure at a hose bib before recommending one, and we warn customers about the noise, since the flush is loud enough to hear across the house. They rarely clog.

    Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in Lincoln, CA

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. Cast iron flanges rust through at the bolt slots. PVC and ABS flanges crack when someone overtightens the bolts, and a repair ring screwed over the top is often all it takes to restore a solid anchor. Seal goes on last.

    ADA-Compliant Toilet Installation in Lincoln

    ADA toilets put the seat 17 to 19 inches off the floor, and the centerline sits 16 to 18 inches from the side wall. It is set that way so a grab bar fits. We measure the existing drain before ordering, since moving a drain two inches to hit the ADA centerline means opening the floor. Elongated bowls are standard here.

    Commercial Flushometer Toilets in Lincoln

    Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. There is no tank to refill. Manual and sensor valves go on both floor-mount and wall-hung bowls, with the control stop adjusted so the flush is full but does not splash. Sensor valves cut down on touch points.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in Lincoln

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. We follow the maker's frame height chart exactly. A frame set wrong puts the bowl too low or too high, and once tile is up there is no adjusting it. Measure twice here.
