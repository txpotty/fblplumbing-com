    Utility and Basement Sink Drain Replacement in Lincoln

    Basement sinks often drain to a pump. Sinks below the sewer line drain through a pump, so we check its valve and discharge line during the job. Pump trouble is easy to miss. A gurgling pit is a clue.

    Push-Button Drain Installation in Lincoln, CA

    Push-button drains get rid of the lift rod. A press on the cap opens and closes them, and a slotted body only goes in sinks with an overflow. They clean easier too. Hair still collects, so pull the cap now and then.

    Utility Sink Drain Replacement in Lincoln

    Laundry tubs see lint and paint water. Over time that wears the thin plastic drain and trap on a utility sink until it cracks, and lint from the washer standpipe often builds up in the trap so badly that it never drains at full speed again. A 1-1/2 inch trap with a cleanout plug goes in. A lint trap on the washer hose helps.

    Pop-Up Drain Replacement in Lincoln

    Lift-rod pop-ups are the most common bath drain. The old body comes out, the seat gets cleaned, and the new flange goes in sealed with a clevis set for easy lifting. We match the faucet finish. Overflow-style drains are standard.

    Code Updates During Drain Replacement in Lincoln

    Old drains sometimes break current rules. S-traps and drum traps were common long ago, and replacement brings them up to a vented P-trap. That can mean an air admittance valve. Local rules decide.

    Choosing Drain Material in Lincoln, CA

    PVC is the default choice under most sinks. It does not rust, it stands up to drain cleaners, and it costs less than brass, while polished 17-gauge brass makes sense under a pedestal sink or any trap you can see. Some houses use black ABS instead. We match what you have, with a proper transition where they meet.

    Bathroom Sink Drain Replacement in Lincoln, CA

    Bathroom drains wear out at the pop-up. Patching one piece rarely works. The new drain matches your faucet finish.
