    Repiping for Hard Water in Lincoln, CA

    Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running mineral-heavy. Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe, and over years it narrows the bore and speeds up corrosion in metal lines. Pressure suffers. For homes with hard water, we usually recommend PEX-A, which resists scale and does not corrode, and we can add a softener loop or a whole-house filter during the repipe. Adding the loop now costs less than later.

    Copper Repiping in Lincoln, CA

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. Our copper work uses thicker Type L pipe, joined with lead-free solder or ProPress fittings, rather than the Type M found in many older houses. Copper takes a bit longer. Most copper jobs run a day longer than PEX, since each turn needs its own fitting.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Lincoln, CA

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. It fails without warning. The pipe gets brittle from the inside, and fittings crack, so a home that has had one polybutylene leak is usually at risk everywhere else in the system. Full replacement is the fix. Many insurers ask about it.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Lincoln

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab, which costs far less than a repipe and avoids breaking the floor. This works well for a first leak. After multiple leaks, we will show you why a full repipe may cost less over the next ten years.

    Slab Leak Repiping in Lincoln

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Expansive clay soils are common across Northern California, and seasonal swelling and shrinking can crack slabs and buried sewer laterals. Rather than jackhammer to reach one leak, we run new lines through the attic and walls and leave the old under-slab pipe out of service. No jackhammer needed. That stops the current leak and removes the chance of the next one under the same floor.

    PEX Versus Copper Repipe Pricing in Lincoln, CA

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper because the material is cheaper and installation goes faster. Copper costs more. For a typical house, copper often runs 20 to 40 percent above PEX, though some buyers and homeowners like the look and feel of metal pipe and are willing to pay for it. Both prices include permits, fittings, patching and cleanup.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Lincoln

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water and long attic runs. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open the pipe end and let it shrink back over the fitting for a full-bore joint. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. Two or three days covers most houses.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Lincoln

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. Clogged aerators are a clue. New PEX or copper stops the rust, restores pressure and ends leaks at the threads, which is where galvanized fails first. Old pipe stays in walls when pulling it would wreck finished surfaces.
