    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Lincoln, CA

    Calcium scale is the leading cause of tankless heater failure. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides added protection.
