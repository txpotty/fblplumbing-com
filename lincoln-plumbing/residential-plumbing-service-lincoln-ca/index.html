    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Lincoln

    Slab leaks hide under concrete until something gives them away. Many older homes here sit on raised foundations with crawlspaces, which make supply and drain repairs more accessible than slab work. We locate them with acoustic listening gear and a thermal camera, so the only concrete we break is the small patch right over the leak, not half the kitchen floor. Then you pick the fix. Some leaks get a spot repair, others a reroute overhead that skips the slab entirely.

    Faucet, Toilet and Shower Valve Repair in Lincoln, CA

    Hard water chews through faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. Shower valves get rebuilt, cartridges replaced and tub spout leaks sealed, and if a fixture is too corroded to save, we install the replacement and haul the old one away so it is not sitting in your garage. A slow drip still adds up on the bill.

    Water Heater Repair and Replacement in Lincoln, CA

    Thermocouples, gas valves, elements and relief valves are the common water heater repairs. Our techs work on Rheem, Bradford White, Navien and Rinnai heaters, and when a tank is leaking from the shell we replace it with proper venting, an expansion tank and a drain pan, usually the same day. Old tanks get hauled off.

    Sump Pumps and Basement Drainage in Lincoln

    A sump pump only gets tested when it rains hard. We install and replace cast iron submersible pumps, check float switches and replace worn check valves, and we can add a battery backup so the pit keeps draining when the power goes out in a storm. Pour a bucket in to test it.

    Faucet and Fixture Repair in Lincoln

    Most dripping faucets need a cartridge, not a new faucet. We carry cartridges for Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister, and when you do want new fixtures we install them, connect new braided supply lines, and check the angle stops under the sink while we are there. Old angle stops often break when turned.

    Water Softener and Filtration Service in Lincoln

    Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running hard. Scale shows up first as white crust on faucet aerators and a rumbling water heater. We put in and maintain whole-house softeners and carbon filters, including Kinetico and Pelican units, sized from a hardness test we run at your kitchen sink, not a guess. We program regeneration to your household's use.
