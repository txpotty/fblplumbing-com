    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Replacement in Lincoln

    Costs vary by job. A tank water heater replacement typically runs $1,400 to $3,000, a water softener $1,500 to $3,500, a water line $1,800 to $5,500 and a sewer line $4,000 to $15,000 or more.

    Many sewer line replacements take one to three days depending on length, depth and method. Trenchless options can shorten the timeline.

    Replacing old pipes with PEX or copper offers numerous benefits. PEX is flexible, corrosion-resistant and less prone to bursting in freezes, while copper is durable and long-lasting. Both improve water quality, eliminate leaks from old pipes and can significantly boost water pressure and flow throughout your Lincoln home.

    We install water heaters from Bradford White, Rheem and A.O. Smith, tankless units from Rinnai and Navien, and quality softeners and well pumps from trusted manufacturers.

    Key signs include frequent, recurring leaks in the same area, discolored water, very low water pressure throughout the house, heavy corrosion visible on pipes or a major component like a water heater being over 10 to 15 years old and failing. If repair costs approach the cost of replacement, replacement is often the better long-term investment.

    The timeline depends on the complexity of the job. A water heater replacement typically takes 2 to 4 hours. A major project like a full house repipe or sewer line replacement can take 1 to 5 days. We will provide an estimated schedule during your consultation.

    While some minor plumbing repairs can be DIY, major replacements like water heaters, sewer lines or repiping should always be handled by licensed professionals.
