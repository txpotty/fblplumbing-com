    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Device Installation in Lincoln, CA

    For high-hazard applications in Lincoln, CA where toxic substances could contaminate the water supply, a reduced pressure zone, or RPZ, device is usually required. RPZ backflow preventers offer the highest level of protection, using a relief valve to discharge water if the pressure drops. FBL Plumbing specializes in RPZ installation, making sure all components are correctly sized, positioned and set up according to state and local plumbing codes. Proper installation is critical for effective operation.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Lincoln

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a common backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Lincoln, DCVAs are often installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers precisely install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.

    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Lincoln, CA

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested easily. We carry proven brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Backflow Preventer Installation in Lincoln

    The cost for backflow preventer installation in Lincoln, CA typically ranges from $400 to $1,500, depending on the type of device, its size and the complexity of the installation. A basic residential PVB for an irrigation system is generally on the lower end, while a commercial-grade RPZ assembly costs more because of material and labor requirements.

    We install backflow preventers from leading manufacturers known for quality and reliability. Our preferred brands include Watts, Febco, Zurn and Apollo, so you receive a durable, effective device that meets all industry standards.

    FBL Plumbing stands behind our work with a comprehensive warranty on our backflow preventer installations. This warranty covers both the labor and the installed device, providing you with peace of mind. Specific warranty details will be provided with your quote.

    Backflow is when water flows the wrong way through a pipe and carries contaminants into the clean water supply. It can happen from back siphonage during a pressure drop or back pressure from pumps and other equipment.

    Most residential backflow preventer installations can be finished within 2 to 4 hours. More complex commercial installations or those requiring extensive pipe changes might take a full day. We always aim for prompt service with minimal disruption to your water supply.

    Backflow preventers are installed at cross-connection points where non-potable water could enter the potable water supply. Common locations include irrigation system connections, fire sprinkler lines, boiler systems and connections to auxiliary water supplies, often just after the main water meter or at the point of hazard.

    In Lincoln, most backflow preventers must be tested once a year by a certified backflow tester to confirm they are working correctly and providing adequate protection. This is a separate service from installation but is essential for ongoing compliance and safety.

    Most water providers require testable assemblies to be tested at installation and then every year. Some high hazard facilities may be tested more often.
