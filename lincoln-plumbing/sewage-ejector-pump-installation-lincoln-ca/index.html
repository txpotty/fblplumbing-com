    While both sump pumps and sewage ejector pumps remove water, their applications are distinct. FBL Plumbing provides expert guidance and installation for both in Lincoln. A sump pump handles groundwater and storm runoff, typically at a basement's lowest point to prevent flooding. A sewage ejector pump, however, is designed for wastewater containing solids from toilets, sinks and showers in below-grade plumbing.
