    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Lincoln

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. Sump pumps push groundwater out to the yard, ejectors push sewage to the sewer, and only the ejector pit must be sealed and vented against gas. They are not interchangeable.

    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Lincoln, CA

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes often need duplex systems with an alternating control panel. The panel switches the lead pump each cycle so wear is shared, and if the lead pump fails or the flow is heavy, the second pump starts automatically and the panel trips an alarm so someone knows to call for service. Two pumps, one alarm.
