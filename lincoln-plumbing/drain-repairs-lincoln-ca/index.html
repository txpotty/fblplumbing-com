    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Lincoln

    Trap and slip-joint repairs typically cost $150 to $350. Pipe sections run higher. A section of drain pipe usually runs $400 to $1,200, and cast iron stack replacement can range from $2,000 to $6,000 with drywall, flooring and access driving most of the spread. We write it up before we start.

    Repeat clogs come from something in the pipe catching debris. Grease or scale is common. Poor pipe slope, a missing vent or a cracked fitting with a rough edge will snag paper and hair until the line closes off again a few weeks after each cleaning. A camera shows which one you have.

    Hydro-jetting scours the pipe with water at several thousand psi. It cleans wall to wall. A snake only punches a hole through a clog, but a jet nozzle strips buildup off the entire pipe wall. We camera first on old or brittle pipe.

    Small fixture drain repairs usually do not. Larger jobs often do. Replacing drain stacks, relocating drains or adding new lines may require one depending on the scope and your local rules. We handle permits when they apply.

    Keep grease out of the kitchen sink. Strainers in the tub help. Flush only toilet paper, run hot water after greasy dishes, and schedule a professional cleaning every year or two if your home is older or has a history of clogs. Hard water homes may need it more often.

    We install PVC and ABS for most new drain work. Cast iron where needed. Cast iron still makes sense in some multi-story walls where noise matters, and we tie new pipe into existing metal drains with listed transition fittings. Every part meets code.
