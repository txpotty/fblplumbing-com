    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Lincoln

    The tank tee is the manifold that connects the tank, switch, gauge, relief valve and drain. Galvanized tees corrode and restrict flow, especially with sandy or mineral-heavy water. We replace them with brass or stainless steel tank tees that hold up much longer. A new liquid-filled gauge gives accurate readings, and a fresh relief valve protects against a stuck switch. These small parts make a big difference in how well the system holds up.

    Preventative Well Pressure Tank Maintenance in Lincoln, CA

    While our primary service is well pressure tank replacement, FBL Plumbing also emphasizes the importance of preventative maintenance for your well system in Lincoln, CA. Regular checks of your pressure tank's air charge and overall condition can help identify potential issues before they lead to a complete failure. During a maintenance visit, we can top off the air charge, inspect for early signs of corrosion or leaks, and advise on steps to maximize the lifespan of your current tank, potentially delaying the need for replacement.

    Bladder Pressure Tank Replacement in Lincoln

    Bladder tanks hold water inside a flexible butyl bag surrounded by pressurized air. When the bag ruptures, water fills the whole tank, there is no air cushion and the pump short cycles. A quick test is pressing the air valve on top, since water spitting out means the bladder has failed. We replace the tank, set the new pre-charge and verify the pump runs a full cycle. Most bladder tank swaps take about two to three hours.

    Bladder-Style Pressure Tank Replacement in Lincoln, CA

    Most modern well pressure tanks are bladder-style, separating air and water for better efficiency. When the bladder fails, you'll experience symptoms like short-cycling of the well pump. FBL Plumbing carefully replaces these bladder-style tanks across Lincoln, CA, ensuring proper pre-charge pressure and system integration for long-lasting performance.
