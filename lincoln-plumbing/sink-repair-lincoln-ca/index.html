    Fixing Loose or Wobbly Sinks in Lincoln

    Over time, sinks can become loose or wobbly, especially if they are undermounted or pedestal styles. This can be due to failing caulk, loose mounting clips, or even issues with the countertop. Our plumbers in Lincoln can re-secure your sink, making sure it's stable and properly sealed to prevent water infiltration and further damage to your cabinetry or flooring.

    Supply Line and Shutoff Valve Repair in Lincoln, CA

    Braided supply lines and angle stops are rated for years, not decades, and the rubber inside breaks down in warm cabinets. A failing line can burst without warning and flood a bathroom. We replace worn lines with stainless braided connectors and swap stuck multi-turn valves for quarter-turn stops. It is a small job that heads off a very expensive one.

    Kitchen Sink Repair in Lincoln

    Kitchen sinks endure heavy use, leading to common issues like garbage disposal clogs, leaky basket strainers, or worn-out sprayer hoses. FBL Plumbing specializes in diagnosing these specific problems, whether it's a cracked basin or a faulty P-trap. We can repair or replace components from brands like Moen, Delta, and Kohler, making sure your kitchen sink is fully functional and ready for daily demands. Our repairs are built to last, handling the tough demands of cooking and cleaning.

    Slow Drain and Clog Clearing in Lincoln

    Bathroom sinks clog with hair, soap scum and toothpaste mixed with mineral scale. We pull the stopper, clean the overflow channel, and clear the trap and branch line with a hand auger instead of harsh chemicals. If the clog sits past the wall, we check whether a shared line with the tub or shower is the actual problem. You end up with a sink that drains at full speed.

    Loose or Cracked Basin Repair in Lincoln

    Undermount sinks are held by clips and epoxy that can let go, leaving a gap and a leak along the counter. Vessel sinks can rock on a worn mounting ring, and older porcelain basins can crack around the drain. We reattach, reseal or replace the basin as needed and recaulk with a mildew-resistant silicone. We will tell you plainly when a crack means replacement is the better value.

    Overflow and Drain Body Leak Repair in Lincoln, CA

    Water that appears only when the sink is full often comes from the overflow gasket or the drain flange putty. We pull the drain, clean the old putty or sealant, and reset the flange with fresh plumber's putty or silicone based on the basin material. Then we fill the bowl past the overflow and check for drips. This kind of leak is easy to miss and easy to fix once found.

    Sink Water Pressure Issues in Lincoln, CA

    Low water pressure at your sink can be annoying and indicate underlying plumbing problems such as mineral buildup in the aerator, faulty faucet cartridges, or even larger issues within your home's water lines. Our technicians in Lincoln, CA can correctly diagnose the cause of low pressure and implement the necessary repairs to restore full water flow to your sink.

    Pop-Up Drain and Stopper Repair in Lincoln, CA

    When the stopper will not close, will not open or lets water slowly escape, the pop-up linkage is usually to blame. The pivot rod nut can leak, the clevis strap can slip, and hair wraps around the stopper body. We clean or replace the assembly and adjust it so the lift rod works freely. If the drain body itself is corroded, we replace it with a matching finish.
