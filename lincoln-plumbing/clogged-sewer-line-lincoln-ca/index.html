    Cleaning vs. Repairing a Sewer Line in Lincoln, CA

    If the camera shows a sound pipe with a grease or root clog, cleaning is usually all you need. Cracked, offset or collapsed pipe will keep clogging no matter how often it is cleared.     Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Lincoln, CA

    Understanding the difference between a main sewer line clog and a branch line clog is vital for proper diagnosis and repair in Lincoln, CA. A main line clog affects all drains in your home, often causing backups in lower-level fixtures. Branch line clogs are typically isolated to a single fixture or area. Our technicians are skilled at finding the exact location of the blockage, whether it is in the main line or a specific branch, for a targeted and efficient repair.
