    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Visalia, CA

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. We pull the tub, move the drain from the end of the tub to where the shower pan needs it and raise the valve to shower height, usually around 48 inches off the floor. On a slab, we cut concrete. No more stepping over a tub wall.

    Emergency Shower System Replacement in Visalia, CA

    Water under a failed pan can rot a wood subfloor in a season. When a pan is cracked or a valve is leaking into the framing, we pull the damaged shower, check the joists and subfloor and install a new unit as quickly as the framing allows. Dry framing first. Then the new shower goes in.

    Walk-In Shower Installation in Visalia

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. With no curb, the floor needs a steady quarter-inch-per-foot pitch to the drain and waterproofing that extends past the opening. We set that slope. Grab bar blocking goes in while the walls are open, so bars can be added later without hunting for studs.

    Custom Shower System Installation in Visalia, CA

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. A standard pressure-balance valve can feed one head, but rain heads, body jets and handhelds need a thermostatic valve with separate volume controls and supply lines sized up to 3/4 inch. We plan the layout first. The water heater gets checked for capacity too.

    Commercial Shower Installation in Visalia, CA

    Locker rooms need showers that take hundreds of uses a day. Commercial installs get tamper-resistant trim and heads, plus drains sized for heavy simultaneous use. We work around your business. A master mixing valve can protect the whole room from scalding.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Visalia

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. The generator goes within the maker's distance limit, and we plumb its supply, drain and steam line to the head. The circuit is an electrician's job. The enclosure needs a sealed door and a sloped ceiling.

    Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Visalia

    Rain heads pull up to 2.5 gallons per minute each. Add a handheld and a couple of body sprays and you can be running more water than a standard 40-gallon water heater can keep hot for a long shower. The water heater gets sized up. Thermostatic valves with volume controls run the outlets.
