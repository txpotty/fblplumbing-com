    Annual Tankless Water Heater Inspection in Visalia

    An annual inspection of your tankless water heater in Visalia is a proactive measure against costly repairs.     Inlet Filter and Air Intake Cleaning in Visalia

    Every tankless unit has an inlet water screen that catches grit and scale flakes. Those screens can clog with sand and debris, especially after municipal line work. We pull and clean the screen and inspect the air intake filter, which can load up with dust and dryer lint. Clean filters keep the flow sensor reading accurately and the burner breathing properly.

    Navien Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Visalia

    Navien tankless water heaters often feature condensing technology, making them highly efficient but also requiring specific maintenance. Our tune-ups include checking the condensate trap for blockages, cleaning the secondary heat exchanger and verifying the operation of the circulator pump. We also confirm a proper air-fuel mixture and calibrate sensors for peak efficiency in Visalia homes.
