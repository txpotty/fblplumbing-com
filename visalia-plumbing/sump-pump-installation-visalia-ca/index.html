    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Visalia, CA

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. The backup pump gets its own float and its own check valve, and it runs off a sealed 12-volt battery when the outlet goes dead. A good battery carries it through most outages. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Visalia, CA

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. We install their M53 and M98 models often. They make matching backup units. Pit size and lift decide which model we recommend.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Visalia

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. For those jobs we install duplex systems where two pumps alternate, with a control panel and a high water alarm that can tie into the building monitoring. Downtime costs money. We plan the install around your business hours.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Visalia

    No pit yet? We cut one at the low corner. Washed gravel goes around the basin so water can reach it. Then we patch the slab. The pump, lid and discharge go in last.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Visalia, CA

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary runs on house power and handles the everyday inflow, while the battery unit waits above it on a separate float. Finished basements are the usual reason. We install them in homes where a flood would ruin flooring and drywall.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Visalia

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. Lids close flat over them. Each install gets a sealed lid and a grommet around the cord to keep out radon and odor.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Visalia, CA

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. We pull the old pump and inspect the pit. If the old one could not keep up, we size up. Testing is done with a full pit.

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. We install exterior basins with pumps for those spots. Slopes toward the house are the usual cause. We keep the outlet away from your foundation and the neighbor line.

    Three to five hours covers most standard jobs. New pits take longer. You will know the timeline before we start.

    Have it cleaned and inspected once a year, ideally before the rainy season. We check the float, clean the pit and test the discharge. It catches problems early.

    Manufacturer warranties usually run three to five years, longer on premium models. We also back our workmanship.
