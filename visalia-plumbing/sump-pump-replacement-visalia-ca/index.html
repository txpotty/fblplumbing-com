    Sizing a Submersible Sump Pump in Visalia

    Horsepower matters less than people think. For most houses a 1/3 HP pump is enough, provided the water only has to climb 10 to 15 feet to get out. We check the flow chart against your actual lift before we pick a size. Oversize it and it short cycles.

    Pedestal Pumps for Shallow Pits in Visalia, CA

    Older basements sometimes have a narrow, shallow pit poured right into the floor, and a pedestal pump is often the only thing that fits without breaking concrete. We swap the pump, replace the float rod and stops, and run new 1.5-inch PVC to the check valve. Cheaper than a submersible, too.

    Cast Iron Submersible Pumps in Visalia

    Cast iron sheds motor heat into the water around it far better than a plastic housing does, which is a big part of why a Zoeller M53 or Liberty 250 outlasts a hardware-store pump. Most swaps take one to two hours. Before we set it, we make sure the float can swing freely without catching the wall.

    Choosing a Backup Battery in Visalia

    Battery choice decides how long the backup actually runs. A sealed AGM or deep-cycle marine battery in the 75 to 100 amp-hour range will typically handle several hours of intermittent pumping during an outage. We size it to how often your pump runs on a wet day. Batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Visalia

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. When the check valve leaks, the water in the pipe drops back into the basin and the pump has to move it twice, which shortens its life. Every pump we install gets a new check valve. In a tight pit we use a vertical switch, and in a wide pit a tethered float.

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Visalia, CA

    Yard drainage pits and window well pumps can sit dry from fall to spring and then seize the first time a big storm fills them. We swap in a corrosion-resistant pump and clear out leaves and silt. We route the outlet away from the house. Test them each spring.

    Storm-Night Sump Pump Replacement in Visalia, CA

    Most emergency sump calls come in at night, during the heaviest rain of the year, when the pump has been running nonstop for hours. Those calls go to the top of the schedule. Our tech shows up with a pump and valve on the truck, and in most cases water is leaving the pit within an hour. After that, we look at why the old one quit.

    Pedestal Pump Service in Visalia, CA

    Louder, yes, but a pedestal motor sits high and dry, so in a narrow pit it often outlasts a submersible by several years. Narrow pits favor pedestals. Worn units get replaced, and we reset the float rod stops so the cycle is right. If your pit is wide enough, a submersible may serve you better.
