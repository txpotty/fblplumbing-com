    Backflow Preventer Testing in Visalia

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers call for annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. Proper softener maintenance prolongs its life and keeps soft water flowing to your home, preventing costly scale damage.
