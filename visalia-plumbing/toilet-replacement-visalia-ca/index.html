    Standard Toilet Replacement in Visalia

    Two-piece toilets are what most houses have, and after twenty or thirty years the tank bolts corrode, the porcelain gets crazed and the flush never quite clears the bowl. That is when we swap them. We pull the old toilet, scrape the flange and check it for cracks, then set the new one on a fresh wax ring with new closet bolts.

    Comfort-Height Toilet Replacement in Visalia

    Going from a standard bowl to a comfort-height model raises the seat two or three inches, which matters a lot for seniors and anyone recovering from knee or hip surgery. No drain changes are needed. Pair it with a grab bar if you can, and we can anchor that into a stud during the same visit.

    Rental and Multi-Unit Toilet Replacement in Visalia, CA

    Landlords with several units do better standardizing on one toilet model with common parts, because the next flapper or fill valve is then the same part in every unit. We can replace several toilets in one visit. Tenants keep a working bathroom. Entry gets coordinated with each tenant directly, so you do not have to be there.

    Cracked Toilet Replacement in Visalia

    Porcelain cracks below the waterline are not safely repairable, and epoxy fixes on a tank under constant water pressure rarely last more than a few months. It needs to be replaced. We pull the cracked toilet and check the floor for water damage before setting the new one, since a slow crack leak often soaks the subfloor first.

    What a Standard Swap Includes in Visalia

    The swap itself runs about two hours: water off, tank drained, old toilet out, flange scraped and inspected, new toilet set on a fresh seal, and a braided stainless supply line connected. Then the leveling. We caulk the base except for a small gap at the back so any future leak shows up instead of hiding under the floor.

    Cracked Bowl or Tank Replacement in Visalia

    Sometimes only the tank is cracked, and if the model is still in production, a matching tank costs less than a whole new toilet. First we find the model number inside the tank. A cracked bowl always means a full replacement, since the bowl carries the seal to the floor and cannot be patched.

    Toilet Flange Repair During Replacement in Visalia

    Pulling the old toilet is the only chance to see the flange, and a cracked plastic ring or a rusted cast iron flange will ruin a new install within a year. It gets checked on every replacement. A split ring gets a stainless repair ring, while a flange that sits below the finished floor gets a spacer or extender so the seal compresses properly.

    One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Visalia, CA

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, so there are fewer leak points and fewer crevices to clean. They are heavier and pricier than two-piece models. Getting one up a staircase takes two people, and we set it without dragging it across your tile so the floor stays unscratched and the wax ring lands centered on the flange.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Replacement in Visalia

    Most replacements with a quality standard toilet, labor and disposal cost $450 to $950, and that covers the wax ring, bolts and a new supply line too. Premium one-piece or smart toilets can bring the total to $1,200 to $2,500. Flange repairs usually add $100 to $250.

    Our short list starts with TOTO Drake, American Standard Champion, Kohler Highline and Gerber Viper, all of which flush well and use parts you can find at most supply houses. For a tighter budget, Mansfield holds up well. The model gets matched to your rough-in and space.

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall behind the toilet to the center of the drain, which you can find by measuring to the closet bolts. Twelve inches is standard. Some older houses have a 10 or 14 inch rough-in instead, and a toilet made for the wrong one either sticks out or will not fit.

    A pro install costs more up front, but it comes with a flange check, the right closet bolts and a warranty if the seal leaks later. A bad DIY seal can rot a subfloor. Fixing that costs much more than paying for the install.

    Our workmanship is guaranteed, and the toilet carries its own manufacturer warranty, which differs by brand and usually covers the china longer than the tank parts. If a leak traces back to our install, we return and fix it. Hold on to the invoice and the model number.

    About two hours for a standard swap. That covers removal, flange inspection, setting the new toilet and testing it, with the old fixture loaded on the truck at the end. A bad flange adds thirty to sixty minutes.

    Handy homeowners can do it, but the trouble starts when the old toilet comes off and the flange turns out to be cracked or sitting below the new floor. Most DIY leaks start there. Cranking the closet bolts too tight is the other common error, and that one cracks a new bowl.

    It is doable for handy homeowners, but old toilets are heavy and flange problems are common once the bowl comes off. A pro install avoids cracked china and slow leaks.
