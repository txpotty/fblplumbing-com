    Standard tank heaters remain the most common type, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment.     Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Visalia, CA

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need space and airflow, often a room of about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they work best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. The space runs cooler.

    Upsizing Your Water Heater in Visalia

    Running out of hot water often means the family has outgrown it. A larger tank or a model with a higher first-hour rating can keep up with more showers back to back, and in some homes a 50-gallon high-recovery gas unit does the job of a 75-gallon standard tank without taking up any more floor space. Bigger is not always needed.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Visalia, CA

    Gas water heaters recover faster than electric, and in Visalia, CA homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. Gas gets shut off and capped, the old tank drained and removed, and the new one set with a fresh connector, sediment trap and properly sloped vent. Then we leak test.
