    Same-Day Disposal Installation in Visalia, CA

    If your old disposal quits with a sink full of water, we can usually install a new one the same day. We carry 1/2 and 3/4 HP units, mounts and cord kits on the truck, so there is no supply house run before your sink drains. Most swaps take about an hour.

    High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in Visalia

    A 3/4 or 1 HP unit takes up more room and weighs more than a basic unit, so we measure the cabinet first. Taller units can push the discharge outlet below the wall stub, and when that happens we either lower the drain at the wall or pick a model with a higher outlet so water does not sit in the chamber. We also check the circuit. Bigger motors draw more at startup.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Visalia, CA

    Air switches start the disposal with a button on the sink or counter. Pressing the button sends a pulse of air through a small tube to a control box under the sink, so there is no electricity at the button and no need to drill for a wall switch. They are common on islands.

    Dishwasher Connection With Disposal Installation in Visalia

    Every new disposal has a plug sealing the dishwasher inlet. We knock it out with a screwdriver and hammer, fish the plug out of the chamber and connect the hose with a new clamp. A plug left in backs up the dishwasher. An air gap or high loop goes in too, which keeps sink water from running back into the dishwasher.

    Batch-Feed Disposal Setup in Visalia

    On a batch-feed unit, the lid is the switch. We install the unit, then test the lid in every position to make sure the motor only runs when the lid is fully seated and stops the moment it is lifted out. It is the safety feature. Families with kids and anyone nervous about a hand near the hopper tend to choose these in Visalia homes.

    Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in Visalia, CA

    Going from batch-feed to continuous-feed means adding a way to switch it on. That can be a wall switch, an air switch button on the sink deck or a switched outlet under the sink, and the choice depends on how hard it is to run wire to the spot you want. Air switches avoid new wiring in the wall. The drain usually stays the same.

    First-Time Disposal Installation in Visalia

    If your kitchen has never had a disposal, we start by checking the sink flange, drain height and electrical supply. The mount goes on, the unit gets hung and the discharge tube is connected to a new trap, with the wall stub lowered if it sits too high. Power comes from a licensed electrician when needed.

    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Visalia, CA

    On a double sink, the disposal goes on one bowl and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. The layout keeps both bowls draining and stops the disposal from pushing water into the second bowl, a problem when the tee is too close to the discharge. Heavy-wall fittings last longer.
