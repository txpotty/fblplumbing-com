    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Replacement in Visalia

    The cost for garbage disposal replacement in Visalia, CA typically ranges from $200 to $600. This includes the new unit itself, which can vary from $100 for a basic 1/3 HP model to over $300 for a high-end 1 HP unit, plus labor costs. Factors like the disposal's horsepower, brand, and any necessary plumbing modifications can affect the final price.

    Key signs you need a new garbage disposal include persistent leaks from the unit's housing, a humming noise without grinding, frequent clogs despite proper usage, foul odors that won't go away, or if the unit simply won't turn on. These symptoms usually indicate internal motor or component failure.

    New garbage disposals typically come with manufacturer warranties ranging from 1 to 10 years, depending on the model and brand. FBL Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation services for your peace of mind.

    If your current disposal jams often or struggles with food waste, a 3/4 or 1 HP model can help.

    While it's possible for a handy homeowner to replace a garbage disposal, it involves working with both electricity and plumbing, which can be hazardous if not done correctly. Improper installation can lead to leaks, electrical shorts, and voided warranties. For safety and peace of mind, hiring a professional plumber is recommended.

    Most disposals last 8 to 12 years. Hard water, heavy use and grease can shorten that life.

    For light use, a 1/3 HP or 1/2 HP unit is usually enough. For regular use and tough food scraps, a 3/4 HP or 1 HP unit is recommended. Our plumbers can help you weigh your needs and choose the best fit for your Visalia home.

    In most cases, a simple like-for-like garbage disposal replacement in Visalia does not require a permit. However, if significant plumbing modifications or electrical work beyond a simple swap are needed, a permit might be required. We will let you know if this is the case.
