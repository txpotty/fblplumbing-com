    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Visalia

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while breaking apart the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is highly resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Visalia homes. This method is well suited when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Visalia, CA

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is excellent for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a fast and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Northern California homeowners.
