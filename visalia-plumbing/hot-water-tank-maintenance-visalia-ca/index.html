    Routine hot water tank maintenance, such as flushing and anode rod replacement, generally does not require a permit in Visalia, CA. However, if major repairs or a full replacement are needed, a permit may be required to confirm compliance with local building codes.

    In mineral-heavy water a magnesium rod may last three to five years. We measure it at every visit so you replace it before the tank lining starts to rust.

    Once a year is the minimum for most homes, and more often where water is hard. Homes without a softener or with heavy use may do better with a flush every six months.

    Many homeowners can do a basic drain with a garden hose. The risk is a plastic drain valve that breaks or will not reseal, and DIY flushes rarely include an anode check or relief valve test.
