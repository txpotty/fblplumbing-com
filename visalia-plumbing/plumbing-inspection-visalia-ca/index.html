    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Inspection in Visalia

    Standard plumbing inspections usually cost $150 to $300. With the sewer camera, most totals land between $250 and $500.

    Our technicians inspect all major water heater brands, including Rheem, AO Smith and Bradford White. Tankless is included. Rinnai and Noritz tankless units and most household fixture brands are routine, so your equipment brand will not be a problem.

    We check water supply lines, drain lines, fixtures, the water heater, sump pump, garbage disposal and exposed gas lines. Drainage gets tested. Our plumber also checks for leaks, corrosion and vent issues, measures pressure and writes up the condition of every component.

    No permit is needed for an inspection. Permits apply to the work. If the inspection leads to repairs or replacements that need a permit, we will say so up front and handle it if we do the job.

    Every inspection comes with a written report, photos and sewer video when included. Reports are ready the same day or the next business day.

    A standard home inspection takes about one and a half to three hours. Sewer video adds time. Larger homes, long sewer lines or a hidden leak that needs tracing can push it longer, and we will tell you the expected time when you book so you can plan around it.

    Our plumber explains each issue, how serious it is and what the repair options are. The choice is yours. We can quote any repairs on the spot or later, and you can have us do the work, use another plumber or hand the report to the seller during negotiations.

    Watch for a water bill that jumps for no reason, low pressure, slow drains or brown water. Noisy drains are a clue. Damp spots on walls or floors, a musty smell under a sink or a warm patch of tile can all point to a hidden leak that an inspection will find before it does more damage.
