    Common signs that you might need a new sewage ejector pump include the pump running constantly, not turning on at all, making unusual grinding or loud noises, or foul odors or sewage backing up into your basement fixtures.     A sump pump is designed to remove groundwater from a basement or crawl space, preventing flooding.     With proper installation and regular maintenance, a high-quality sewage ejector pump can last 7 to 15 years. Factors such as usage frequency, the type of waste it handles and water quality can influence its lifespan. Annual inspections can help extend its longevity.
