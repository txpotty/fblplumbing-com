    Kinds of Toilet Installation in Visalia, CA

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Visalia, CA

    High-efficiency toilets flush on 1.28 gallons or less, against 3.5 gallons or more for toilets from before the early 1990s. Check for the WaterSense label. We steer customers toward models with a MaP score of 800 grams or higher, because a cheap low-flow bowl that needs two flushes saves nothing. Dual-flush models add a lighter half flush.

    Commercial Flushometer Toilets in Visalia

    Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. There is no tank to refill. Manual and sensor valves go on both floor-mount and wall-hung bowls, with the control stop adjusted so the flush is full but does not splash. Sensor valves cut down on touch points.

    Standard Toilet Installation in Visalia

    A standard floor-mount toilet is a two-hour job when the flange is sound. The stop gets shut, the tank drained, and the old bowl lifted out. After scraping the old wax off, we check the flange for cracks, set new stainless bolts, and lower the new toilet straight down onto a fresh seal so it compresses evenly. The old one leaves with us.

    Commercial Toilet Installation in Visalia, CA

    Commercial restrooms take a beating, so we install vitreous china bowls with flushometer valves rather than tank toilets. Flushometers want a 1 inch supply line and steady pressure. We confirm the building line can deliver the valve's rated flow, often 20 to 25 gallons a minute, before any fixture goes up. Fixture counts follow code.

    Toilet Replacement and Upgrades in Visalia, CA

    Swapping an old 3.5 gallon toilet for a modern one is the easiest water savings in the house. Pulling the old one takes around twenty minutes. The rest is flange cleanup and shimming the new bowl level on a floor that has settled with age. We haul the old one away.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Visalia, CA

    Wall-hung toilets need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep, or a furred-out chase built in front of an existing 2 by 4 wall. The floor underneath stays open. Cleaning gets easier, and the bowl height can be set anywhere within the carrier range, useful for a tall family. Service happens through the flush plate.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in Visalia

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. The frame height chart from the maker gets followed to the letter. A frame set wrong puts the bowl too low or too high, and once tile is up there is no adjusting it. Measure twice here.

    Toilet Brands We Install in Visalia

    We install Kohler, TOTO, American Standard, Gerber and most other brands sold at supply houses and home centers. Each one has its own parts quirks. Some one-piece TOTO models mount with a Unifit adapter, and certain Kohler toilets use a tank-to-bowl gasket that most hardware stores do not carry, so we stock both. We keep those parts on hand.
