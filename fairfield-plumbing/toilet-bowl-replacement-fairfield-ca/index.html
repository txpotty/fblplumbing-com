    Flange Repair During Bowl Replacement in Fairfield, CA

    The flange is the ring that anchors the bowl to the drain, and the only time anyone sees it is when the old bowl comes off. That is the moment to check it. We fix a cracked or sunken flange before the new bowl is set, since a new bowl on a bad flange will rock and leak within months.

    Cost-Effective Bowl Swaps in Fairfield, CA

    Swapping only the bowl saves money when the tank is newer, the fill valve and flush valve are in good shape, and the bowl is still made in your color. White bowls are the easiest to find. Older colors that are no longer made are where this usually falls apart, and then a full toilet costs about the same.

    Toilet Bowl Replacement for Remodels in Fairfield

    Remodels are a good time to change the bowl shape or height, as long as the rough-in stays the same and the new footprint covers the old flooring cutout.     Toilet Bowl Replacement for Older Homes in Fairfield, CA

    Houses built before the 1970s often have a lead closet bend or a cast iron flange caulked with oakum, and those need gentle handling when the bowl comes off. Old cast iron can crack if you pry on it. We avoid prying on the flange and use repair rings or flange adapters made for cast iron, so the drain stays intact.

    Brands We Match in Fairfield

    American Standard, Kohler, TOTO and Gerber sell bowls separately for many two-piece models, but the tank bolts, gasket style and bolt spacing have to match exactly. Close is not good enough. The pairing gets confirmed from the model number before we order, which avoids a second trip.

    Chipped or Stained Bowl Replacement in Fairfield, CA

    Deep chips in the glaze collect bacteria, and hard water rings that have etched the surface will not come out with any cleaner. Replacing the bowl solves both problems.     Pressure-Assist Toilet Bowl Replacement in Fairfield

Many are not sold separately. When the matching bowl is gone, a full toilet replacement is the better route, and we will price both.
