    Leaking P-Trap Replacement in Fairfield

    Traps leak at the joints first. Slip nut washers flatten and harden with age, and a trap opened often for clogs usually has stripped threads. A new trap kit costs little. Labor is most of the bill.

    Pop-Up Drain Assembly Replacement in Fairfield, CA

    A stopper that drops on its own has a worn pivot. When the pivot ball wears and the nut stops sealing, the stopper drops or sticks and the nut drips. A new pop-up fixes all of it. Takes about an hour.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Replacement in Fairfield

    Kitchen drains start failing at the basket strainer. Old strainer putty dries out and the locknut rusts, so water runs down the tailpiece and looks like a trap leak. Strainer and trap get swapped as a pair. Disposal sinks get a new flange instead.

    P-Trap Replacement in Fairfield

    Thin chrome traps are the ones we replace most. They rust from the inside, the plating flakes off, and a slip nut that will not loosen often twists the whole trap apart, so we cut it out and install heavy-wall PVC or polished brass that matches the room. The new trap gets a full flow test. Total time is often under an hour.

    Hard-Water Drain Damage Repair in Fairfield, CA

    Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. Scale grows inside pop-up bodies and on stopper rods until the linkage seizes. Forcing a scaled lift rod usually snaps the pivot and leaves the stopper stuck, with a narrowed tailpiece slowing the drain. We replace the whole scaled assembly. Smooth PVC sheds buildup better.

    Push-Button Drain Installation in Fairfield, CA

    Push-button drains get rid of the lift rod. They open and close with a press on the cap, and we check that the sink has an overflow before choosing a slotted body, since a slotted drain in a bowl without an overflow leaks out the slots. They clean easier too. Hair still collects, so pull the cap now and then.
