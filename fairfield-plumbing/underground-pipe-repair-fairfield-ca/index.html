    Frequently Asked Questions About Underground Pipe Repair in Fairfield

    The cost of underground pipe repair in Fairfield, CA varies greatly depending on the pipe's location, the extent of the damage and the repair method used. Simple repairs might range from $500 to $2,500, while extensive trenchless sewer line replacements could be $5,000 to $15,000 or more. We provide a detailed estimate after a full inspection.

    Whether an underground pipe leak is covered by insurance depends on your homeowner's policy and the cause of the damage. Typically, sudden and accidental damage may be covered, but issues from normal wear and tear or neglect are generally not. It's best to review your policy or contact your insurance provider directly for clarification.

    Trenchless pipe repair offers several key advantages, including minimal disruption to your yard, landscaping and concrete, which can save on restoration costs. It's usually faster than traditional excavation and less invasive, preserving the look and value of your property. This method is also durable, frequently extending the lifespan of your pipes.

    In most cities, the homeowner owns and maintains the water line from the meter to the house. The utility is responsible for the meter and the line on the street side. We can help confirm which side the leak is on.

    We use acoustic listening devices that pick up the sound of escaping water, tracer gas that rises through soil at the leak point and electronic locators that trace the pipe path. Together these narrow the leak to a small area.

    Ignoring an underground pipe leak can lead to serious problems, including costly water damage to your foundation, landscaping erosion, mold growth, higher water bills and potential contamination of your water supply. It's important to address these issues promptly.

    Common signs of a broken underground pipe include unexplained wet spots or lush patches in your yard, a sudden drop in water pressure, unusual odors such as sulfur or sewage, gurgling sounds from drains or toilets or an unexpected increase in your water bill. If you notice any of these, it's smart to call for a professional inspection promptly.
