    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Fairfield

    The heat exchanger in a tankless heater is a coil of small tubes that water races through while the burner fires. Hard water drops calcium and magnesium on those hot surfaces, narrowing the passages over time. We connect a submersible pump and hoses to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with fresh water to rinse the system thoroughly. Restored flow means more stable temperatures and fewer shutdowns.

    Maintenance Cost Versus Repair Cost in Fairfield, CA

    A tankless descaling visit typically costs far less than replacing a scaled heat exchanger, which can run $600 to $1,500 or more in parts and labor. Some manufacturers deny exchanger claims when scale damage is found. Regular maintenance helps protect a unit that can last 20 years when cared for.     Navien Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Fairfield

    Navien tankless water heaters often feature condensing technology, making them highly efficient but also requiring particular maintenance. Our tune-ups include checking the condensate trap for blockages, cleaning the secondary heat exchanger and verifying the operation of the circulator pump. We also ensure a proper air-fuel mixture and calibrate sensors for peak efficiency in Fairfield homes.

    Condensing Unit and Neutralizer Service in Fairfield

    High-efficiency condensing tankless heaters produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized before it goes down a drain. We inspect the condensate trap, line and neutralizer cartridge and replace the media when it is spent. A clogged trap can cause shutdowns or water leaking inside the cabinet. We also check that the line is protected where it runs outdoors.
