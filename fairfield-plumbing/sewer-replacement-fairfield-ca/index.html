    Sewer Line Replacement Methods in Fairfield, CA

    Emergency Sewer Line Replacement in Fairfield, CA

    Sewage coming up a floor drain or tub is an emergency. Stop using sinks, toilets and the washer. We open the cleanout to relieve the backup, run a camera to find the break, and if the line has collapsed we dig to it the same day, putting in a section of new pipe to get you flowing before planning the full replacement.

    Under-Slab Sewer Replacement in Fairfield

    Sewer pipe under a slab is the hardest kind to reach. We have two ways in. We can cut and remove a strip of floor inside the house and patch it after, or tunnel under the slab from outside so the floors stay intact, and the right choice depends on the length of the run, the flooring, and what sits above the pipe.

    Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Fairfield, CA

    Pipe bursting needs two pits. One sits at the house, the other at the far end of the run. A cone-shaped head is pulled through the old line, breaking it outward, while fused HDPE pipe follows right behind it, so the new line takes the same path without a trench. Driveways stay put.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Fairfield

    An open trench gives full access to the pipe from the house to the main. We dig with a mini excavator and hand dig near utility marks. The old pipe comes out, the trench bottom gets a bed of gravel or sand, and new 4-inch PVC goes in at a steady quarter inch of fall per foot, confirmed with a level on every length. Then the camera.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Fairfield

    Bursting works best on clay, cast iron and Orangeburg. A hydraulic rig at the receiving pit pulls the bursting head through the old line, splitting it into the soil, while a continuous length of fused HDPE follows behind which leaves a single joint-free pipe in the ground. Roots cannot get into a joint that does not exist.

    Sewer Line Repair vs. Full Replacement in Fairfield

    We look at the whole line before recommending either. A single bad joint can usually be dug and fixed. Breaks every few feet, roots at every joint, or a belly that holds water for twenty feet tell us the line is failing everywhere, and in that case a full replacement saves you from paying to dig the same yard twice.

    Commercial Sewer Line Replacement in Fairfield

    Commercial laterals are often 6-inch pipe and carry grease or heavier flows. Restaurants usually have a grease interceptor in the line too. We schedule the work around your hours, set up temporary bypass pumping when the building needs to stay open, and coordinate with the city on any work at the tap. Weekend starts are common.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Fairfield, CA

    A lot of older houses have no outside cleanout. Every clog then means pulling a toilet. When we replace a sewer line we add a two-way cleanout near the foundation, with a cap at ground level you can find, so future cleaning or camera work is quick and cheap.
