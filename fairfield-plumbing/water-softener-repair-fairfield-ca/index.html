    A salt bridge, a crust of salt that forms in the brine tank, can prevent your water softener from regenerating properly. This leads to hard water throughout your home. We also repair leaks or damage to the brine tank itself, frequent in older systems.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Fairfield, CA

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. A new quality softener installed typically costs $1,500 to $3,500.     Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Fairfield

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Fairfield, CA

    A salt bridge is a hard crust that forms in the brine tank and keeps salt from dissolving. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. We break up bridges, clean out mushy salt, inspect the float and brine well and repair the safety valve. We also recommend salt types that bridge less in warm or humid spaces.
