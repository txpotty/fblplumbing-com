    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Upgrades in Fairfield, CA

    Street pressure varies a lot from one neighborhood to the next. Code says no more than 80 psi inside the house.     Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Fairfield, CA

    Pipe pulling works like this. We dig a pit at each end of the old line. A cable or bursting head goes through the old pipe and drags new HDPE or PEX back through the same path, splitting the old pipe if it is brittle, so a driveway, a row of shrubs or a concrete walk can stay where it is.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Fairfield

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. Scale and corrosion are not a problem with it. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, use only fittings listed for burial, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    Damage Assessment Before Replacement in Fairfield

    Water Line Materials We Install in Fairfield, CA

    Our trucks carry Type K copper, PEX-a and HDPE. Each has a place. Copper suits short runs and high pressure, PEX handles long runs with bends, and HDPE is the usual pick for pipe pulling and directional boring, where we need a tough, fused pipe that can be dragged a long way. All of it is listed for drinking water, carrying the NSF 61 listing.

    PEX Water Line Installation in Fairfield, CA

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house.
