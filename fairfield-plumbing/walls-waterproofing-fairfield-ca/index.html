    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Fairfield

    Many homes in Fairfield have crawl spaces, which are very prone to moisture issues. Installing a heavy-duty vapor barrier on crawl space walls and floors is a key waterproofing strategy. This thick polyethylene sheeting stops ground moisture from evaporating into the crawl space, protecting subflooring and joists and improving indoor air quality. It's an effective way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Fairfield

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall. We remove failing tile, install cement board or foam board and apply a membrane such as Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban. Seams, corners and niches get extra attention before new tile is set.

    The cost of walls waterproofing in Fairfield, CA can vary a great deal based on the method used, the extent of the damage, and the size of your foundation. Basic crack sealing might range from $300 to $800 per crack, while a full exterior foundation waterproofing project could be $8,000 to $20,000 or more. Interior drainage systems with a sump pump usually fall between $4,000 and $12,000. We provide clear, itemized quotes after a thorough inspection.

    Absolutely. By preventing water intrusion through your walls, waterproofing greatly reduces the moisture and humidity levels in your basement or crawl space. This creates an environment less conducive to mold and mildew growth, improving air quality and protecting your belongings from moisture damage.

    Interior waterproofing addresses water after it has entered the foundation, typically managing it with sealants and drainage systems inside the basement or crawl space. Exterior waterproofing prevents water from reaching the foundation walls in the first place, usually by excavating and applying membranes or coatings to the outside of the foundation.

    The duration of a walls waterproofing project varies depending on its scope. Minor crack repairs might take a few hours, while an interior drainage system could take 1 to 3 days. A full exterior foundation waterproofing project, involving excavation around the entire home, could take 3 to 7 days or more. We provide a clear timeline during our first consultation.

    We use a range of high-performance waterproofing membranes and coatings depending on the application and foundation type. These can include liquid asphalt-modified polyurethane, rubberized asphalt membranes, bentonite clay systems, and crystalline coatings. Brands often include well-known names known for their durability and effectiveness in preventing water penetration.

    Waterproofing prevents the moisture mold needs to grow. Existing mold should be removed and the area dried before sealing. We remove affected materials when needed and recommend remediation for larger growth.

    Walls waterproofing is a primary step in preventing future mold growth by eliminating the moisture source. However, it does not remove existing mold. If mold is already present, it will need to be professionally remediated before or along with the waterproofing process. Once the area is dry and mold-free, waterproofing will help keep it that way.
