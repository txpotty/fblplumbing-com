    Grease Trap Repair vs Replacement Cost in Fairfield, CA

    Age and material decide most repair versus replace calls. A 25-year-old steel trap with rust holes is done.     Commercial Grease Trap Repair in Fairfield

    Restaurants, school cafeterias, hospital kitchens and bakeries all push more grease than a small trap can hold. Undersized traps fail in predictable spots.     Damaged Baffle and Lid Repair in Fairfield, CA

    Lids crack when someone sets a stockpot on them or a dolly rolls across a floor-mounted unit. A cracked lid lets odor out and lets mop water in.     Damaged Baffle Repair in Fairfield

    Baffles are the walls inside the trap that slow the water down long enough for grease to float and solids to sink. Once one cracks or falls loose, the trap is just a box that grease passes through. We pump it out and pull the old baffle. Plastic units get a factory replacement part. Steel ones often need a fabricated baffle or a new trap.

    Grease Trap Odor Control & Repair in Fairfield

    A sewer smell near the trap means gas is getting out somewhere it should not. The usual suspects are a torn lid gasket or a floor drain trap that has dried out. We check venting on the fixture side too, because a sink with no vent can siphon its own trap seal and let sewer gas back into the kitchen. Pumping the trap helps for a week, but it will not fix a gasket that no longer seals.

    Flow Control Device Repair in Fairfield

    Hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, like 20 or 50 gallons per minute, and the flow control fitting is what holds the water to that rate. Take it out and a three-compartment sink dump blows grease right past the baffles. Inspectors look for it. The air intake gets cleared too, because the fitting needs air to work.
