    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Fairfield

    PEX-B is stiffer, costs less per foot, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. A crimp joint is only as good as the tool, so we check every ring with a go/no-go gauge, and a ring that does not pass gets cut out and redone. That takes seconds. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Fairfield, CA

    Opening a wall for a kitchen remodel is the cheapest time to swap out the old pipe. A far-end bathroom may need a three-quarter inch feed to hold pressure.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Fairfield

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and it really depends on the house. PEX-A makes tighter bends, can be heat-fixed if it kinks, and its fittings flow better, while PEX-B is cheaper and its crimps hold just as well when each one is gauged. Either one lasts for decades. In a cramped attic, we usually pick PEX-A.
