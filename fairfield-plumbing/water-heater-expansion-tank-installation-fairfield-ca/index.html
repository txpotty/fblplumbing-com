    Pressure Reducing Valve and Expansion Tank Pairing in Fairfield

    Many homes see high incoming pressure, so pressure reducing valves are widespread. The PRV protects your fixtures but also turns your plumbing into a closed system, which makes an expansion tank necessary. We test the PRV output and replace it if it is creeping or failing. Pairing a healthy PRV with a correctly charged expansion tank gives you stable pressure throughout the house.

    Expansion Tank Installation Cost Comparison in Fairfield, CA

    Expansion Tank Replacement in Fairfield, CA

    Expansion tanks wear out over time as the internal bladder or diaphragm fails and the tank fills with water. A waterlogged tank feels heavy, and tapping it produces a dull sound all the way up instead of a hollow top. Pressing the air valve and getting water instead of air is a clear sign it has failed. We replace failed tanks with a correctly sized new unit and check the precharge before installation.
