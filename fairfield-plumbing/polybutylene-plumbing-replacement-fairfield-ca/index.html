    Finding Polybutylene Leaks in Fairfield

    A poly leak inside a wall or ceiling can run for days before you see a stain. The meter often tells first. With every fixture off, a spinning leak indicator on the meter means water is going somewhere, and we use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to find it without opening walls at random. Once we find it, a temporary fix buys time to schedule the repipe.

    Polybutylene Leak Repair in Fairfield

    Repairs on polybutylene are temporary by nature, because the pipe next to the repair is the same age. A repair buys time. When a poly line leaks, we cut out the bad section and splice in PEX with approved transition fittings, which stops the leak and gives you time to schedule a full replacement on your own terms.

    Copper Replacement Option in Fairfield

    Type L copper appeals to some homeowners for its long history and rigid feel. Joints are soldered or pressed. Copper costs more than PEX and takes a little longer to install, so we help you weigh the cost, your water conditions and how long you plan to stay in the house.

    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Fairfield

    Most poly replacements we do use PEX-A, a flexible tubing that runs long distances with few fittings. On a slab home, we route the new lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, which keeps every supply line out of the concrete. It also tolerates freezing better. Expansion fittings keep the bore full size, which helps pressure at the last fixture on the line.

    Copper Replacement for Polybutylene in Fairfield, CA

    Some homeowners in Fairfield, CA want copper when they take out polybutylene. That works fine. Our copper replacements use thicker Type L pipe with lead-free soldered or pressed joints, not the thinner Type M. Copper costs more and takes longer, since each turn needs a fitting, but it tolerates heat and sunlight well and has a long track record.

    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Fairfield, CA

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. We use Uponor PEX-A with expansion fittings that do not restrict flow. New lines run from the main shutoff to every fixture, water heater and appliance, and most homes are completed in two to four working days with water back on each night.

    Affordable Polybutylene Solutions in Fairfield, CA

    Poly replacement is a big expense, so we give you options instead of a single take-it-or-leave-it number. PEX-A is the budget pick. Interior and yard service work can be priced separately, letting you do the inside now and the yard line later.

    Fitting Failure and Emergency Repair in Fairfield

    Acetal plastic and copper crimp ring fittings are often the first part of a poly system to fail. Attic fittings fail hard. After an emergency repair we schedule the full replacement, since removing the poly system is the only lasting fix.
