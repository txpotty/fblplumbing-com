    Gas and Electric Water Heater Checks in Fairfield, CA

    Whether your home in Fairfield, CA uses a gas or electric hot water tank, our maintenance covers the specific needs of each. For gas units, we inspect burner assemblies, gas lines and vent systems for safety and efficiency. For electric units, we check heating elements, wiring and thermostats. Our detailed checks ensure all energy sources are operating correctly and safely.

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Fairfield, CA

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can clog the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a steady blue flame and lower gas bills.

    Electric Element and Thermostat Service in Fairfield

    Electric tanks rely on upper and lower heating elements that get crusted with scale in hard water. We test each element with a meter, check the thermostats and look for scorched wires at the terminal block. If an element has failed we replace it with the matching wattage and voltage. Many families who think they need a new heater just need a lower element and a flush.

    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Fairfield

    A properly working temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank. Our technicians will test this valve to confirm it's not stuck or leaking, preventing dangerous pressure buildup within the tank. We verify it opens and closes correctly, and if it fails, we will recommend immediate replacement to keep your Fairfield home safe and compliant with plumbing codes.

    Burner and Pilot Light Maintenance in Fairfield, CA

    For gas hot water tanks, proper burner and pilot light operation is key for efficient heating. Our service includes cleaning the burner assembly to remove dust and debris that can impede combustion. We also check and adjust the pilot light, making sure it ignites reliably. This attention to detail prevents common issues like intermittent hot water or complete heating failure, which can be stressful for homeowners in Fairfield, CA.

    Electric Hot Water Tank Element Check in Fairfield

    Electric hot water tanks rely on heating elements to warm the water. During maintenance, our plumbers inspect these elements for signs of wear, corrosion or burnout. We also check the thermostat settings and wiring connections to confirm they are functioning correctly and safely. This preventative check can identify failing elements before they stop heating altogether, avoiding unexpected cold showers for your family in Fairfield.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Fairfield, CA

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is essential for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank. Water chemistry varies from one neighborhood to the next, and it affects the rod's lifespan. Our technicians inspect the anode rod for wear and recommend replacement if it's significantly corroded, typically every 3 to 5 years. Replacing a worn anode rod is a cost-effective way to protect your tank from rust and leaks, sparing you a full tank replacement.
