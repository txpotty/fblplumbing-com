The goal is a drain you stop thinking about.

    Water Leak Repair in Fairfield

    Hidden leaks sit inside walls, under sink cabinets and in the buried yard line that runs between the meter and the house. A thermal camera narrows it down.     Well Pump Repair in Fairfield, CA

    No water at the tap on a well system points to a short list of parts. It is usually the pressure switch, the capacitor in the control box, the pump motor or a waterlogged pressure tank. Voltage and amp readings at the control box tell us whether the pump needs to come up. Pulling a submersible pump is a two-person job.

    Gas Pipe Repair in Fairfield, CA

    Leave the house first if you smell gas, and call the gas utility from outside. Once the utility clears it, we handle the repair. We pressure test the system, track the leak with an electronic detector and soap solution, and replace failed flex connectors, shutoff valves or corroded steel pipe. Nothing is relit until the test holds.

    Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Fairfield, CA

    Pinhole leaks in copper begin inside the pipe, where water chemistry and high velocity wear through the wall. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. One pinhole usually means others are coming in the same run. We solder in a new section or use a press fitting, and we check the house pressure while we are there. Above 80 psi, add a regulator.

    Sewer Line Repair in Fairfield, CA

    Roots are the usual cause of a sewer line that backs up every few months. They slip into a cracked joint, then grow into a mat that snags paper and grease. We camera the line to see the damage and mark the depth on the surface, then choose between an open dig and a trenchless method. Clay pipe usually goes first.

    Water Heater Valve and Element Repair in Fairfield

    Rumbling or popping from a tank means sediment on the bottom. A rotten-egg smell in the hot water usually means the anode rod is reacting with the water. We replace thermocouples, thermostats, elements, gas valves, anode rods and relief valves from truck stock, which covers the large majority of tank repairs we see in a year. Rust at the seams is different. That tank is done, and we will say so.
