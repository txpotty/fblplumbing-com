    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Concord

    Gray polybutylene pipe was installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s. It breaks down from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water, and fittings can crack with no warning. Some insurers raise premiums or decline coverage on homes that still have it. We remove polybutylene supply lines and replace them with PEX-A or copper. A completed repipe can also help when selling the home.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Concord, CA

    Copper piping is a long-standing, proven choice known for its durability and resistance to corrosion. While it's a premium material, its longevity often makes it a cost-effective solution over time. FBL Plumbing offers expert copper pipe replacement for sections or entire homes in Concord, CA. Our plumbers are skilled in the careful soldering and installation techniques required for copper, ensuring a leak-proof and robust plumbing system that holds up season after season.

    Slab Leak Pipe Replacement in Concord

    Slab leaks, common in homes built on concrete foundations, can be destructive and difficult to detect. FBL Plumbing uses modern leak detection technology to locate the exact source, then expertly repairs or reroutes the compromised pipe, often using durable PEX to bypass the slab entirely, avoiding future issues in the same spot.

    PEX Repiping in Concord

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale buildup and expands slightly if water freezes, which helps in colder homes. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe for better flow. Systems can be run as a home-run manifold with a shutoff for each fixture or as a trunk and branch layout. PEX installs faster than copper with far fewer fittings in the walls. It is our most requested repipe material.
