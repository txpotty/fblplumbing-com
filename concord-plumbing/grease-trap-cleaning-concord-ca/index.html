    Why Choose FBL Plumbing for Grease Trap Cleaning in Concord, CA?

    A grease trap works by slowing the water down long enough for fat to float and food to sink. Past about 25 percent full, the separation stops working. The grease then slips past the outlet into the lateral, cools into a hard white crust, and a cable machine only bores a narrow hole through it.

    • Odor Control, A pumped trap stops the rotten-egg odor that decaying grease gives off.

    Under-Sink Grease Trap Maintenance in Concord, CA

    Under-sink hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, usually 10 to 50 gallons per minute, and they hold far less than people expect. Busy kitchens need them cleaned every week or two. We take the lid off, remove grease and solids, scrape the baffles, check the flow control fitting on the inlet, and replace the lid gasket if it is flattened or torn.     Outdoor Grease Interceptor Pumping in Concord, CA
