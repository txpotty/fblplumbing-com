    Reliable Backflow Preventer Installation Services in Concord, CA

    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Concord

    Landscape irrigation is the most common reason homeowners need a backflow device. Fertilizer, herbicides and stagnant water in sprinkler and drip lines can be siphoned into your drinking water if pressure drops. We install pressure vacuum breakers or double check assemblies based on local requirements and the layout of your system. Above-ground installs are set at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head when a PVB is used.

    Pool and Spa Fill Backflow Protection in Concord, CA

    An auto-fill line or hose connection on a pool or spa creates a direct path for chlorinated water to reach the supply. We install the correct device on pool fill lines and check that hose bibbs have vacuum breakers. This simple step protects your household water and keeps you in line with local code. We can add the work when a pool is being built or remodeled.

    Commercial Backflow Preventer Installation in Concord

    Restaurants, salons, dental offices and light industrial buildings often need multiple backflow devices on soda machines, boilers, dishwashers and process water. We survey the building, identify each cross-connection and install containment and isolation assemblies as required. Work is scheduled after hours or on weekends when needed to keep from shutting down your business. You receive documentation for each device for your records and your water provider.

    Backflow Preventer Brands We Install in Concord

    FBL Plumbing works with leading backflow preventer brands known for reliability and durability. We commonly install devices from manufacturers such as Watts, Wilkins by Zurn, Apollo and Conbraco. Our experience with these brands means you receive a high-quality, long-lasting solution built to withstand the water conditions and pressures in Concord plumbing systems.     Backflow Testing and Certification in Concord

    Most water providers require testable backflow assemblies to be certified at installation and then once a year. Our certified testers check each valve's opening point, relief valve operation and differential pressure. If a device fails, we can repair or rebuild it on the spot with genuine parts. We submit passing results so you avoid compliance letters or service interruption.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Concord

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules allow. We size them to avoid excessive pressure loss on your system.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Device Installation in Concord, CA

    For high-hazard applications in Concord, CA where toxic substances could contaminate the water supply, a reduced pressure zone, or RPZ, device is often required. RPZ backflow preventers offer the highest level of protection, using a relief valve to discharge water if the pressure drops. FBL Plumbing specializes in RPZ installation, making sure all components are correctly sized, positioned and set up according to state and local plumbing codes. Proper installation is critical for effective operation.
