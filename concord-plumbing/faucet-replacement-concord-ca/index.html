    Corroded Faucet Replacement in Concord

    Green crust at the base means the faucet body is corroding. Once the brass under the finish is pitted, cartridges stop sealing against it, and every repair drips again within a few weeks.     Moen Faucet Replacement in Concord

    Moen's 1225 cartridge runs a huge share of single-handle Moen faucets. When it sticks, the handle gets stiff and the temperature drifts, and a cartridge that has been in place for fifteen years can be welded in by scale. Sometimes pulling it cracks the body. That is when we replace the faucet.

    Touchless Faucet Upgrade in Concord
