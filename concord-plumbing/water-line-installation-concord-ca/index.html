    PEX and HDPE Water Line Installation in Concord, CA

    HDPE and PEX both flex, so there are fewer fittings in the ground and better tolerance for soil movement. For long runs, HDPE is the tougher of the two, and we join it with approved compression or heat fusion fittings and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find the line years from now in Concord, CA. We keep PEX out of the sun.

    Main Water Service Line Installation in Concord

    The main service line carries every gallon your house uses, from the meter to the main shutoff. We size it for your fixture count and the distance, then trench to the depth local code sets, lay it on clean bedding and connect it to the meter and to your house shutoff with fittings approved for burial. Then we pressure test it.

    Water Line Replacement for Older Homes in Concord

    Copper became standard in many mid-century homes here, and decades later pinhole leaks and worn fittings are a common repair call. Galvanized steel and early copper in older houses tend to corrode, pit and lose pressure as the scale builds up. A new run of PEX or copper takes its place, and we check the water at the kitchen tap before and after so you can see the difference in flow on the same faucet in your Concord home. Rusty water usually clears up.

    Type K Copper Water Line Installation in Concord

    Type K copper is the thick-walled grade made for burial. It handles high pressure and resists rock punctures, but it costs more than plastic and can corrode in very aggressive soil, so we bed it in clean sand and add protective sleeving anywhere it passes concrete or crosses another utility in Concord. Some codes or owners prefer metal.

    Water Line Pressure Testing and Code Compliance in Concord, CA

    Every new line gets a hydrostatic pressure test before the trench is closed. The line is capped and pumped above normal working pressure, then watch the gauge for any drop, because a leaking fitting is easy to fix with the pipe exposed and very hard to find once it is buried in Concord, CA. A steady needle means a tight line.

    Trenchless Water Line Installation in Concord

    Pipe pulling replaces the old line by dragging new HDPE through the path it already took. Two small pits get dug, one at each end, attach the new pipe to a cable, and pull it through while a bursting head splits the old pipe out of the way, which leaves your lawn in Concord mostly intact. Rock and some soils rule it out.
