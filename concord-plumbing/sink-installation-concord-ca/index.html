    Each Kind of Sink We Install in Concord, CA

    Pedestal Sink Installation in Concord, CA

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. The supply stops and the trap sit right behind the column, so we center the drain stub, use chrome or polished brass trap parts, and set the stops tight to the wall where the pedestal can hide them. Blocking in the wall is a must. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.

    Kitchen Sink Installation in Concord

    Kitchen sinks come in four mounts: drop-in, undermount, farmhouse and flush-set. Each one needs its own support, and a cast iron farmhouse sink can weigh well over a hundred pounds before it holds any water, so the cabinet often needs reinforcement first. We check the cabinet before the sink arrives. Disposal and dishwasher connections are part of the job.

    Bathroom Sink Installation in Concord, CA

    Bathroom sinks are lighter but fussier. Faucet holes must match the faucet, single hole, 4 inch centerset or 8 inch widespread, and the overflow must suit the drain. The spec sheet gets read first. Mismatches get caught before the counter is cut.

    Pedestal Sink Mounting in Concord

    The pedestal is mostly for looks. A wall bracket lagged into blocking carries the weight, and if the wall has none, we open it and add a board. Anchors in drywall will fail. A loose basin cracks the trap.

    Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in Concord

    Stone shops cut the hole, but we set the bowl. Getting an undermount centered under a polished cutout so the reveal is even on all sides takes a helper, a few shims and patience before the clips get tightened. Uneven reveals look bad forever. We check it from both ends of the counter.

    Sink Installation Cost Factors in Concord, CA

    Labor is the main cost driver. Moving the drain or adding a second bowl adds hours, and a straight drop-in swap is the cheapest. We quote labor apart from the sink. You can buy it or we can.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Concord, CA

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. Since the rim sits higher, the faucet has to be taller or wall-mounted, and the drain must match whether the bowl has an overflow. No overflow means no slotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Faucet and Drain Assembly for New Sinks in Concord, CA

    Faucet and drain go on before the sink is set, when possible. Mounting the faucet and the pop-up on a sink that is still on sawhorses takes a fraction of the time and gets a tighter seal than working upside down inside the cabinet. Undermounts are the exception. Those get plumbed from below.
