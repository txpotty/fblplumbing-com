    Rooting uses a spinning cable with cutting blades to break up clogs and roots. Jetting works another way. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scrub the pipe walls clean. Tough lines often get both, rooting to open the path and jetting to clean out what remains.

    Chemical drain openers rarely clear a real clog and can eat at old pipe. Old cast iron and the thin brass traps found in Concord's older homes take the most damage. They also make our job riskier. Standing water full of caustic chemicals splashes when we open the trap, so tell us if you poured anything in before we arrived.

    We run RIDGID, Spartan and Gorlitz augers, plus sewer cameras and jetters from the same tier of commercial makers. These machines take daily abuse and keep working. Brand matters less than setup. Even a top machine can hurt a pipe with the wrong head, so picking the cutter gets as much attention as the machine.

    Drain snaking, also called augering, uses a flexible cable and cutting head to break up or pull out the clog. Hair, wipes and roots respond well to it. Jetting scours the whole pipe with high-pressure water and strips the grease, scale and sludge a cable slides past. Repeat clogs usually need jetting.

    Routine drain cleaning in Concord, CA does not usually need a permit. Digging up the yard, replacing the sewer or making a major repair usually requires one. We handle that paperwork. If the rooter work turns up a problem that needs a permit, we tell you before any repair gets scheduled.
