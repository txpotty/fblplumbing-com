    Gas Water Heater Efficiency Ratings in Concord

    Efficiency ratings on tank heaters are listed as UEF, uniform energy factor, and a higher number means less gas per gallon heated. A standard tank sits around 0.60 to 0.65. Condensing models top 0.80, and some qualify for utility rebates where those are offered.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Concord, CA

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. We never swap a natural gas tank onto propane without the right conversion. We confirm pressure with a manometer.

    Commercial Gas Tank Replacement in Concord

    Laundromats and multi-unit buildings work commercial tanks so hard that replacement comes on a predictable schedule rather than as a surprise. Installs get scheduled during your slow hours. Venting and gas work may be extra.

    No Hot Water Calls in Concord

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. Those get tested before anything else. On a tank past ten years, a new heater usually beats a new valve.

    Bradford White Gas Water Heater Installation in Concord

    Bradford White tanks are sold only through plumbing wholesalers, not big-box stores, and plenty of Northern California plumbers install them for that reason. Replacement parts are easy to find. Installing to their specs keeps the tank warranty intact.

    Large Capacity 75 Gallon Gas Heaters in Concord, CA

    Big families with soaking tubs often outgrow a 50 gallon tank and need a 75 gallon heater or a high-recovery 50 at around 65,000 to 75,000 BTU. A bigger burner needs more gas. We check the meter and pipe size first, because a starved burner never reaches its rated recovery.

    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Concord

    Blower motors and pressure switches are the weak points on power vent tanks. A second blower failure on an older tank is usually the point where a new heater makes more sense. A dedicated outlet gets confirmed first.

    Choosing Between Gas Tank Brands in Concord, CA

    Warranty length on a tank mostly tracks anode size and burner design, so a 12-year tank is a different build from a 6-year one. Self-cleaning dip tubes help on hard water. Powered anodes last longest. Your plans for the house help decide it.
