    Common Sink Repairs in Concord, CA

    Bathroom Sink Repair in Concord, CA

    Bath sinks have small parts that wear. The pop-up linkage sits in a damp cabinet for years, and the pivot nut where the rod enters the drain is the most common leak we find on a bathroom sink. Pedestal and wall-hung sinks get the same checks. Vanity sinks just hide the leak better.

    Kitchen Sink Repair in Concord

    Kitchen sinks take the most abuse in the house. Strainers lose their putty seal, sprayer hoses split inside the cabinet, and disposal flanges vibrate loose over the years. We fix the part that is wet. A cracked composite or porcelain bowl is a replacement, and we will say so.

    Emergency Sink Repair in Concord, CA

    Shut the angle stops first. Turn both oval handles under the sink clockwise until they stop, and if they spin freely or keep weeping, go to the main shutoff and close it before calling us. We send active leaks to the front of the line. The plumber brings stops and supply lines to stop it on the first visit.

    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in Concord, CA

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We look past the wall with a longer cable or a camera for a sag or for buildup where the sink line meets the tub. Now and then it is a plugged vent. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Concord

    Check the slip nuts first. Plastic slip nuts crack when they are overtightened with pliers, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until water seeps out at every joint along the trap. The whole trap gets replaced as a kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Loose or Wobbly Sink Repair in Concord

    A sink that moves when you lean on it is pulling on the drain. Undermount bowls hang from clips epoxied to the stone, and pedestal sinks rely on a wall bracket that may have pulled out of old drywall over the years. The clips get reset and the rim resealed. Pedestals get blocking behind the wall when needed.

    Overflow and Drain Flange Leak Repair in Concord, CA

    Some leaks only show up when the bowl is full. That points to the overflow gasket or the putty under the drain flange, since neither one sees water during a quick hand wash but both get soaked when you fill the sink. We pull the drain and reseal it. Marble and some granite get silicone, because putty can stain them.

    Pop-Up Stopper Repair in Concord, CA

    Stoppers that will not hold water have a linkage problem. The clevis strap slips on the lift rod, the pivot ball loses its seal, or hair wraps the stopper so tightly it cannot seat on the flange. Half the time, a cleaning does it. The other half get a new pop-up drain in a matching finish.
