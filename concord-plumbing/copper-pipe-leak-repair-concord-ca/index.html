    Pinhole Leak Repair in Concord

    Pinholes start inside the pipe where the copper wall pits and thins. You see a drip or a stain.     Copper Fitting and Valve Repair in Concord

    Angle stops, shutoff valves and copper fittings under sinks and behind toilets can weep or seize. Scale locks them up.     Hot Water Copper Line Repair in Concord, CA

    Hot water lines corrode faster than cold lines. Heat speeds it up. Recirculation pumps that run around the clock push water through the copper at high velocity and wear it from the inside, which is why we often add a timer to the pump after fixing the leak. It slows the next pinhole.
