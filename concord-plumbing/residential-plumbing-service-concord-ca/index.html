    Slab Leak Location and Repair in Concord

    Slab leaks hide under concrete until something gives them away. Our techs find them with ground microphones and thermal imaging, so the only concrete we break is the small patch right over the leak, not half the kitchen floor. Then you pick the fix. Options include a spot repair through the slab or a reroute through the attic.
