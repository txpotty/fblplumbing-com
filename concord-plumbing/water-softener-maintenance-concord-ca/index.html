    Resin Bed Checks and Regeneration Settings in Concord

    Resin does the actual softening. Each bead trades sodium for calcium and magnesium until it runs out of capacity. We compare the softened hardness against the gallons used since the last regeneration to tell whether the bed is still at full strength.

    Yearly Maintenance Plans in Concord, CA

    Plans take the remembering off your plate. We call ahead of each yearly visit, and a valve failure between visits moves you to the front of the line. Repairs found during a plan visit come at a lower rate than a regular call.

    Resin Rebedding in Concord

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water slowly swell and soften resin beads, and iron or sediment can coat them until capacity drops off. We try a resin cleaner first. If the bed is spent, we drain the tank, pull the old resin and refill it with fresh high-capacity resin, which runs far less than a new softener.

    Softener Maintenance Plans and Pricing in Concord, CA

    The plan is one flat yearly fee. It covers the service visit and both hardness tests, and any parts we install during plan visits are billed at a lower rate. Catching one stuck float or clogged injector early usually covers the fee.

    Full-System Softener Inspections in Concord

    An inspection covers more than the two tanks. The inlet and outlet piping, bypass, drain line and brine overflow all get traced and checked. Green crust on copper fittings, a drip at the bypass or a drain line jammed down into a standpipe all go on the written report.

    Regeneration Programming and Hardness Testing in Concord, CA

    Plenty of softeners still run factory settings. The installer never changed them, or the household grew and nobody updated the valve. We enter your raw hardness and household size, then set a metered valve to regenerate by gallons used rather than on a fixed timer schedule.

    Softener Upkeep for Restaurants and Shops in Concord

    Ice machines, espresso machines, dish machines and boilers all scale up fast on hard water, and equipment warranties often require treated water. We test hardness on every visit and log it for your records. Brine tank cleanouts come around more often on these systems.
