    Service Valve Kit Installs in Gilroy, CA

    Plenty of tankless heaters went in without service valves, and without them there is no clean way to hook up a descaling pump. We add a pair of isolation valves with purge ports on the hot and cold lines. The kit usually has a relief valve port too. After that, every flush takes minutes to set up.

    What Tankless Maintenance Saves in Gilroy

    Scale on an exchanger acts like insulation. A unit with a thick layer burns noticeably more gas for the same shower than a clean one, and the extra cost adds up month after month. You get the maintenance price beside the likely repair cost, and the decision is yours.

    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in Gilroy

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing. Solution goes from a bucket into the cold port, out the hot port and back to the bucket. Once the cycle ends, we rinse with clean water and pull the inlet screen. Then we check for leaks.

    Yearly Tankless Inspection in Gilroy

    A yearly inspection costs less than one service call for a dead heater. We soap-test the gas connections and try the pressure relief valve. The air filter comes out and the igniter gets a look. Anything that is wearing out goes on a written list with a plain price, and you decide what gets done now and what can wait until next year.

    Rinnai Air Filter and Flame Rod Care in Gilroy

    Rinnai cabinets are tight. We wash the air intake filter and check the flame rod for carbon. On recirculating models we also check the pump schedule, because a wrong setting burns gas for nothing.

    Tankless Banks for Restaurants and Apartments in Gilroy

    Cascade systems link several tankless units to one controller that fires them in turn. They see heavy use, and scale builds faster than at home. We descale each heater in the bank, check the cascade controller and confirm the load is shared evenly across every unit. Quarterly service is common.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Gilroy

    Picture a bundle of thin tubes with a gas flame under them and cold water racing through at three or four gallons a minute. That is the exchanger. Minerals settle on the hottest tube walls first, and the passages get narrower. We dissolve it with 45 to 60 minutes of pump-driven descaling solution.

    Rinnai Tankless Service in Gilroy

    Rinnai units tell you a lot through the controller. Code 11 means the burner never lit, and code 12 means the flame went out after it did. On a Rinnai visit we clean the air intake filter and check the flame rod for soot or cracks. The LC codes are scale warnings, and they usually clear once the exchanger has been flushed and the unit is reset.
