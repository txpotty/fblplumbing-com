    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Gilroy

    Tree roots seek out sewer laterals during dry spells, which is why heavy rains often reveal damaged lines through sudden backups. A few weeks ahead of the wet season, we run a hose into the pit until the float trips and the pump starts. Then we time how fast it pumps out and listen to the bearings. A struggling pump can then be replaced on a calm day instead of in the middle of a storm.

    Emergency Sump Pump Service in Gilroy

    A pump that runs nonstop, hums without moving water or will not start at all needs a plumber the same day, before the pit overflows. Our plumber arrives with spare floats, check valves and a replacement pump in the van. A jammed impeller usually clears in a few minutes. A burned motor means we swap in a new pump on the spot and leave the pit protected.

    Sump Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Gilroy

    Silt and gravel wash into a pit through the drain tile every time it rains, and over a few years that layer can bury the intake screen. Our plumber drains the basin and digs the sediment out by hand. With the pit clean, the float swings without catching. The pump runs cooler too.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Gilroy, CA

    Storms knock out power when the pit fills fastest. That is the moment a backup pump earns its keep. Our plumber reads the battery date, cleans corrosion off the terminals and tests the charger, because a failed charger can leave the battery flat within a season.

    Float Switch Checks in Gilroy, CA

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. Our plumber watches the float rise and drop through a complete cycle. Then we set the on and off points so the pump runs longer, less often.

    Commercial Building Sump Pumps in Gilroy, CA

    Parking garages, mechanical rooms and loading docks often drain to large pits with pumps rated at a horsepower or more. Those units need more frequent checks than a house pump. Our crew tests the controls, the alarm panel and each pump in a duplex station, and records amp draw every visit so a slow decline gets caught. Downtime costs money.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Gilroy

    The check valve sits on the discharge pipe a foot or so above the pump and keeps the water in the line from falling back into the pit. When it fails, the pump pumps the same water twice. Motors wear out faster. Our plumber tests the valve, confirms there is a weep hole below it to prevent air lock, and checks each joint to the wall.
