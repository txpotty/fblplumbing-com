    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Gilroy, CA

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base points to failed O-rings or a loose mounting nut. If left alone, the leak can soak into the cabinet below and damage the sink base. We replace O-rings, tighten mounting hardware and reseal the base. We also check the deck for water stains.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Gilroy

    Ceramic disc faucets are known for their durability and are a common choice in many contemporary Gilroy bathrooms. While generally reliable, they can develop leaks if the ceramic discs become scratched or misaligned, or if the inlet and outlet seals wear out. Our plumbers have the expertise to carefully disassemble these precise mechanisms, clean or replace the ceramic discs, and reseal the faucet to stop leaks and restore full performance.

    Dripping Faucet Solutions in Gilroy

    That constant drip, drip, drip isn't just annoying, it's a sign of a deeper problem. Most dripping faucets are caused by internal component wear, like a deteriorated rubber washer or a cracked cartridge. Our team provides precise solutions, replacing the specific parts that are failing. We work with all types of faucets, including compression, cartridge, disc, and ball varieties, ensuring a quiet and drip-free operation once again.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Gilroy, CA

    Ball faucets, recognizable by their single lever that moves over a rounded cap, are common in many kitchens. They use a rotating ball joint to control water flow and temperature. If your ball faucet is leaking from the spout or base, or if the handle feels loose, it's likely time for a repair. We can replace the spring-loaded rubber seats and O-rings, which are usually the culprits, restoring smooth operation and preventing drips.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Gilroy

    Weak or sputtering flow is often caused by mineral buildup in the aerator or cartridge. We remove and clean or replace the aerator, flush the lines and check the cartridge for scale blockage. On pull-down faucets, the spray head and check valves can also clog. If flow is low at every fixture, we test whole-house water pressure.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Gilroy

    A dripping spout usually means a worn cartridge, washer or valve seat. Hard water speeds up this wear by leaving scale that scratches seals and keeps them from closing tight. We disassemble the faucet, replace the worn parts and clean the valve body. Fixing a drip right away keeps a small leak from building up on your water bill.
