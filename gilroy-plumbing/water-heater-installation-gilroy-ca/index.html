    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Gilroy

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. Each gas swap gets a new connector, a drip leg, a draft test at the hood and a leak check with a detector at every fitting. Venting is what keeps CO out. We follow code on all of it.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Gilroy, CA

    Water on the garage floor and no hot water usually means the tank has rusted through. Shut the cold supply valve on top of the tank and turn the gas knob to pilot or flip the breaker, then call us. Common tanks ride on our trucks. Most emergency swaps finish the same day.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Gilroy, CA

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. When the water heats and expands with nowhere to go, pressure climbs and stresses the relief valve, the toilet fill valves and every supply line in the house. The tank soaks up that extra volume. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.

    Water Heater Installation Cost in Gilroy, CA

    Up front, a tank heater costs less than tankless. Tankless jobs cost more mostly because of gas line and vent work, while attic installs, moves and code updates add labor to any type. Your quote lists permits. You also get an estimate of yearly energy cost for each option.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Gilroy, CA

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. A hybrid needs open air space, often around 700 cubic feet, plus a condensate line to a drain, which rules out most small closets. Some qualify for tax credits. We will tell you if your space works.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Gilroy, CA

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Most gas tankless units need a 3/4 inch gas line and their own PVC or stainless vent, which is different from what a tank uses. They cost more to install. We check gas capacity before quoting.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Gilroy, CA

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match the voltage and breaker size to your panel, and because electric tanks need no vent, they can go in a closet, an attic or a laundry room where a gas unit could not. A drain pan goes under closet installs. Quiet and simple.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Gilroy, CA

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. Before hooking up the new tank we check that the breaker and wire size match the elements, since a mismatch is a common cause of tripped breakers and scorched terminals. No venting needed. That makes placement easier.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Gilroy

    Water heater installation in Gilroy, CA runs about $1,200 to $4,000 or more. Type, capacity and fuel set most of the price, and any gas line, venting or electrical upgrades add to it. Tankless costs more. We quote it in writing after a look.

    Most jurisdictions require a permit for replacement. We pull permits when required and make sure the install meets code.

    Tank water heaters last about 8 to 12 years, though hard water can shorten that. Regular flushing and anode rod checks help.

    A like-for-like tank swap usually takes three to four hours. Converting from tank to tankless, or any job with major gas, vent or electrical changes, can take a full day or more. We give you a timeline first.

    Tank and parts coverage usually runs 6 to 12 years. Our labor has its own warranty.

    For two to four people, a 40 or 50 gallon tank usually does the job. Larger families may need a 75 gallon tank or a tankless unit sized by flow rate.

    An expansion tank absorbs the extra pressure created as water heats. Homes with a closed plumbing system are often required by code to have one, since it protects the heater and every pipe in the house.
