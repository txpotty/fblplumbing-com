    High-Efficiency Toilet (HET) Upgrades in Gilroy, CA

    High-efficiency toilets carry a WaterSense label and a MaP score, which tells you how many grams of waste the toilet clears in one flush. Look for a score of at least 800. Higher numbers mean fewer double flushes and fewer plunger trips, and that is where the real water savings come from.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in Gilroy

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. Some carriers need new bolts. The new bowl gets matched to the carrier already in the wall whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.

    Toilet Flange Repair During Replacement in Gilroy

    Pulling the old toilet is the only chance to see the flange, and a cracked plastic ring or a rusted cast iron flange will ruin a new install within a year. We check it every time. A split ring gets a stainless repair ring, while a flange that sits below the finished floor gets a spacer or extender so the seal compresses properly.

    Cracked Bowl or Tank Replacement in Gilroy

    Sometimes only the tank is cracked, and if the model is still in production, a matching tank costs less than a whole new toilet. First we find the model number inside the tank. A cracked bowl always means a full replacement, since the bowl carries the seal to the floor and cannot be patched.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Gilroy

    Chair height toilets put the seat about 17 to 19 inches off the floor, roughly two inches higher than a standard bowl, and that makes a real difference for knees and hips. No plumbing changes are needed. We often pair it with an elongated bowl and a soft-close seat, and the swap takes the same couple of hours as any other.

    Water-Saving Toilet Upgrades in Gilroy, CA

    Older toilets are one of the largest indoor water users in a house, and replacing a 3.5 gallon model with a 1.28 gallon one cuts toilet water use by more than half. Dual-flush models go lower for liquids. We recommend models with high MaP scores so the savings do not come with double flushing.

    Cracked Toilet Replacement in Gilroy

    Porcelain cracks below the waterline are not safely repairable, and epoxy fixes on a tank under constant water pressure rarely last more than a few months. It needs to be replaced. We pull the cracked toilet and check the floor for water damage before setting the new one, since a slow crack leak often soaks the subfloor first.

A replacement can usually go in first thing the next morning, and we will tell you if the floor needs to dry before we set the new one.
