    What a Plumbing Inspection Covers in Gilroy, CA

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Gilroy

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. We confirm proper venting. We also look at the expansion tank, drain pan and softener, and a hardness test shows whether the softener is working or just eating salt.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Gilroy

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. Our checks include the relief valve and its discharge line, the expansion tank and the drain pan. Gas units get a CO check. A heater that backdrafts can spill combustion gases into the house, so we test the draft at the vent hood and note any problems right away.

    Preventive Maintenance Checks in Gilroy, CA

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. The visit covers flushing the heater, working the shutoffs, reading pressure and checking for slow drains and small leaks. It takes about an hour. Yearly or every-other-year checks stretch heater life and keep valves from seizing.

    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Gilroy, CA

    Coastal fog and moisture can speed corrosion on exposed fittings, so outdoor valves and hose bibs deserve an occasional check. A seasonal check looks at the parts of the system that weather affects most. We look at hose bibbs, exposed pipe in garages and crawlspaces, irrigation backflow devices and the water heater's relief valve and drain. We look at pipe insulation. Fixing a cracked hose bibb or adding insulation is a small job compared to a burst pipe in the wall behind it.

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Gilroy, CA

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. We identify copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, polybutylene and Kitec, and we note the known failure patterns for each one. Certain types need a plan. Poly and Kitec fail suddenly and galvanized chokes off with rust, so we flag them with replacement options.

    Water Heater Inspections in Gilroy

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. The serial number tells us the age, and we check for rust, look at the vent and test the relief valve. Tankless units get checked for scale. On gas units, we look at the burner and flame, check for a sediment trap and confirm the vent is drafting so combustion gases go outside.

    Leak Detection and Assessment in Gilroy, CA

    Hidden leaks waste water and quietly damage floors and walls. Start at the meter. We shut everything off, check the meter's leak dial and then follow the leak with acoustic equipment and thermal imaging. Early detection limits the damage.
