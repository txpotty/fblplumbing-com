    Tunneling for Foundation Leaks in Gilroy, CA

    Tunneling means digging under the slab from outside the house. No floor gets cut. From a pit next to the foundation, the crew tunnels by hand to the pipe, a good choice when the leak is under cabinets or expensive tile. FBL Plumbing backfills and compacts the tunnel when the Gilroy, CA repair is done.

    Commercial Foundation Leak Services in Gilroy

    Shops and offices in Gilroy have longer runs and more fixtures on the slab. More pipe means more ground to cover. Locating and testing can happen after closing or on a weekend, and the concrete cutting gets booked into your slowest hours. FBL Plumbing documents the line layout for your facilities file.

    Drain Line Leak Under Foundation in Gilroy, CA

    Drain leaks under a slab are harder to find. Drains carry no pressure. We plug the line and fill it with water to see if the level drops, then run a sewer camera through it to spot the cracked hub, offset joint or root intrusion that is letting water out. Fixes include tunneling, slab cuts or a pipe liner, and we lay out the trade-offs.

    Water Line Reroute in Gilroy

    A reroute leaves the old pipe under the slab and runs new line through the attic or walls. The floor stays whole. We usually run PEX because it bends around framing and does not corrode, and the drywall openings are cut small, often just a few squares at fixture walls, so patching goes fast.

    Crawl Space Foundation Leak Repair in Gilroy, CA

    Crawl spaces hide leaks from supply lines, drains and AC condensate lines. Sagging, wet insulation is a common clue. Standing water under a house rots sills and joists and grows mold on the subfloor, so we repair the source and look at the vapor barrier while we are down there. A sump pump may make sense in a Gilroy, CA crawl space that floods from outside.

    Commercial Foundation Leak Repair in Gilroy

    Restaurants, offices and retail spaces in Gilroy get slab leaks too, and FBL Plumbing handles them. Every closed hour costs you.     PEX and PVC Foundation Leak Repair in Gilroy
