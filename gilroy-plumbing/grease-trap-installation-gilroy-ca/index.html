    Emergency Grease Trap Services in Gilroy, CA

    A grease trap backing up into the floor sinks at the start of Friday dinner service means a closed kitchen and a health inspector who may not let you reopen until it is fixed. We send a tech with a jetter and a camera to find out whether the trap or the line on either side is the problem. Often it is the outlet. A failed trap can often get a stopgap unit that night and a proper replacement later in the week.

    Commercial Grease Trap Installation in Gilroy

    A commercial kitchen with a three-compartment sink, a pre-rinse station and two floor sinks can push 50 gallons a minute of greasy water at the dinner rush. We size the trap to that peak flow, not the average. Undersized traps are the usual failure. The install covers setting the unit, piping the inlet and outlet, and adding the vented flow control the maker specifies.

    Zurn Grease Trap Installation in Gilroy

    Zurn's indoor grease interceptors cover the common sizes from small under-sink units up to 100 GPM, and we set enough of them to know where the gaskets give trouble. The lid gasket is the usual leak point after a few years. We keep spares on the truck. Most Zurn units come with a flow control fitting, and we mount it on the inlet side at the height the instructions call for.

    Commercial Kitchen Grease Trap Installs in Gilroy

    Every commercial install starts with a fixture list. The tech walks the kitchen and writes down every fixture that drains to the trap and how fast each one empties. That number decides between an indoor unit and an outdoor tank, and where it can go with clean pipe runs to the sewer. Fewer bends, fewer clogs.

    Restaurant Grease Trap Compliance in Gilroy, CA

    Most sewer authorities run a FOG program with an inspector who opens your trap and checks your pump-out manifests against the schedule they set. Fail that and you can face fines or a surcharge on your sewer bill. We install to pass. We add a sampling port if they require it and size to the authority's own formula.

    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Installation in Gilroy

    Outdoor gravity interceptors run from 750 gallons to well past 2,000, and setting one means an excavator on site and a traffic-rated lid if it sits in the drive lane. We confirm the H-20 load rating before the tank ships. Most go in over two to four days. The inlet invert has to sit a couple of inches above the outlet, and we shoot both with a laser level before backfill.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Gilroy

    Inside a hydromechanical interceptor, baffles do the separating while a flow control fitting with an air intake holds incoming water to the rated flow. We pipe the air intake the way the manufacturer and the code both require. Get that wrong and the trap siphons. We also set the unit level within a quarter inch, since a tilted trap short-circuits the baffles and loses capacity.

    Automatic Grease Removal Units in Gilroy

    An automatic grease removal unit heats the trapped grease and skims it into a container on a timer, so staff empty a bucket instead of scraping a tank. The purchase price is higher than a passive trap. They also need a dedicated electrical circuit, which we coordinate with your electrician before the unit arrives. Fry-heavy kitchens like them most.
