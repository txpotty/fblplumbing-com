    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Gilroy, CA

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more cost effective than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes more sense.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Gilroy

    The cost of water softener replacement in Gilroy, CA can vary widely, typically ranging from $1,500 to $4,000. This depends on the type and size of the new system, labor costs and any needed plumbing changes. We provide transparent, upfront pricing after assessing your needs.

    The right size depends on your household's daily water usage and the hardness of your water in grains per gallon. Our technicians will perform a water test and calculate the correct softening capacity in grains per day for your Gilroy home, so the system performs well.

    New water softeners usually come with manufacturer warranties ranging from 1 to 10 years on different components, such as the control valve and resin tank. FBL Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work for your peace of mind. Exact warranty details will be provided with your quote.

    Salt-based softeners remove hardness minerals, calcium and magnesium, through ion exchange, effectively softening the water. Salt-free conditioners do not remove minerals but alter their crystal structure so they do not stick to surfaces. That prevents scale buildup without actually softening the water.

    FBL Plumbing installs a wide range of reputable water softener brands, including Fleck, Autotrol, Culligan, Pelican and Watts. We select brands known for their reliability, efficiency and long-term performance, so you get a high-quality system that meets your needs.

    To extend the life of your new water softener, use the correct type of salt, if applicable, and keep the brine tank filled. Regular maintenance, such as cleaning the brine tank and periodic inspections by a professional, can also help ensure its longevity and efficiency.

    Most softener replacements take two to four hours. New loops or drain lines can take a bit longer.
