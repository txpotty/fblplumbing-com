    Tank Leaks & Anode Rods in Gilroy

    Corrosion starts at the anode rod and works its way into the steel once the rod is spent. Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running hard. The tech pulls the anode, checks what metal remains and swaps it if bare core wire shows. A fresh anode, a drained tank and a relief valve that seats properly can add years to a heater in a Gilroy home that has not started leaking from the shell. Water from the shell seam is another story. That tank needs replacing.

    Venting and Draft Repair in Gilroy, CA

    Atmospheric tanks rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue with an upward slope of at least a quarter inch per foot. A loose, rusted or sagging section lets exhaust spill back into the house. We fix the vent. Each joint gets three screws, the connector gets proper clearance from wood and drywall, and we run the heater for several minutes with a smoke test at the draft hood to watch the exhaust go up. We do not leave with the heater spilling.

    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Gilroy

    Power vent heaters push exhaust through PVC with a blower on top of the tank. When one quits, it is usually the blower motor, the pressure switch or the hot surface igniter. Condensate pooling in a vent with the wrong slope can also trip the pressure switch, so we check the pipe run all the way to the termination before we sign off on a part. We read the error code first. Every suspect part then gets tested with a meter.

    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Gilroy, CA

    Honeywell and White-Rodgers valves report faults with a blinking LED, and the flash count matches a chart on the label. We read the code, then test the thermopile and sensors before blaming the valve. Sometimes the valve really is bad. When the control itself is the problem, we replace it with an exact-match valve or a listed universal model, set the temperature and leak test every joint on the gas train before relighting. We leave the old valve with you if you ask.

    Pilot Light Repair in Gilroy

    The thermocouple is the first thing we test when a pilot will not stay lit. With a millivolt meter, it takes about a minute to see if it is putting out enough. If it reads fine, the next suspects are a clogged pilot orifice, a cracked piezo wire or a thermal cutoff that tripped because the combustion chamber overheated from blocked air. Figure on roughly an hour for most pilot repairs.

    All Major Gas Water Heater Brands Serviced in Gilroy

    Every brand label we see in garages and closets uses one of a handful of gas control designs. Rheem and Ruud share most parts, and State shares a lot with A.O. Smith. Knowing which family a heater belongs to lets the tech bring the right valve, thermocouple or igniter on the first trip instead of ordering parts and coming back two days later. A photo of the rating plate helps us prep.

    Gas Valve and Thermostat Repair in Gilroy, CA

    Lukewarm showers and a tank that never catches up often trace back to the gas control. The tech checks the dial, the immersion sensor and the outlet pressure with a manometer. On most residential tanks the thermostat lives inside the gas valve body, so a bad sensor or a stuck magnet usually means replacing the whole control rather than one small part. That swap takes about an hour. The new valve gets a leak test at every joint.

    Gas Water Heater Noise Diagnosis in Gilroy

    Rumbling or popping from the bottom of the tank is water boiling under a layer of sediment. Hard water and years without a flush both make it worse. A high whistle is different. A relief valve or a half-closed supply valve is the usual cause.
