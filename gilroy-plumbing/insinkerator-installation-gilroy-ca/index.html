    Careful InSinkErator Installation in Gilroy, CA

    Standard InSinkErator Installation in Gilroy

    A standard install goes into a sink that already has a drain and power. The old disposal comes down, the flange gets scraped clean and a new one goes in on fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on. We hang the unit, turn the lower ring until it locks and connect the discharge tube to the trap with a new slip-joint washer so nothing weeps. We connect power through a cord kit or the old hardwire and confirm the ground first.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Gilroy

    An air switch puts a push button on the countertop or sink deck instead of a wall switch. The button pushes a puff of air down a thin tube. A control box under the sink reads that pulse and flips the power, keeping live wiring well away from the water. If there is a spare faucet hole, we use it, otherwise we drill.

    Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Gilroy

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. Flip the switch and it runs until you flip it off. A batch feed unit needs its stopper locked in to run, keeping hands out of the chamber, and it needs no wall switch. Each type gets wired its own way.

    Garbage Disposal Replacement in Gilroy

    If your current disposal hums, leaks from the bottom or trips its reset button every week, replacement usually beats repair. Water from the bottom of the housing means the inner seal failed. No one repairs that seal. We take down the old one, keep the dishwasher hose capped and set the new unit to the existing drain, trimming the tailpiece if the height changed.

    Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Gilroy

    Nearly every InSinkErator has a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. The plug gets knocked loose and pulled out of the grind chamber. Then the dishwasher hose goes on with a proper clamp, routed through an air gap or up into a high loop under the counter so sink water cannot siphon back into the dishwasher tub. Both appliances get run together.

    Same-Day Disposal Replacement in Gilroy, CA

    A dead disposal stops the whole kitchen. Dishes pile up. Our trucks carry the common Badger and Evolution models along with cord kits, flanges and slip-joint fittings, so a same-day replacement is usually possible if you call in the morning. Evening calls often go first thing next day.

    Disposal Installs for New Sinks in Gilroy, CA

    A new sink changes the drain math. Undermount and farmhouse bowls are often deeper than the old drop-in. A deeper bowl pushes the disposal lower, and if the trap arm in the wall was set for a shallow sink, the discharge can end up below it, so we sometimes lower the wall stub. We plan that during the remodel, not after.

    Septic-Friendly Disposal Installs in Gilroy, CA

    Homes on septic can run a disposal, but food solids add load to the tank. Homes on septic should pump the tank on a regular schedule and keep heavy vehicles and new structures off the drain field. InSinkErator's Septic Assist feeds an enzyme solution into the chamber during each run to start breaking down waste before the tank. The enzyme cartridge gets mounted, and we show you the refill.
