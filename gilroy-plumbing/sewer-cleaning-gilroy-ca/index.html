    For the most stubborn sewer line clogs, hydro jetting is the top choice.     Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Gilroy, CA

    Grease from kitchens cools and coats the inside of sewer pipes, and minerals in the water bond with it to form a stubborn crust. A cable can punch a hole through, but jetting cleans the walls back to full size. We choose the method based on pipe material and condition.
