    Sump Pump Types We Replace in Gilroy, CA

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Gilroy, CA

    Yard drainage pits and window well pumps can sit dry from fall to spring and then seize the first time a big storm fills them. We swap in a corrosion-resistant pump and clear out leaves and silt. Discharge goes away from the foundation. A bucket test every spring catches problems early.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Gilroy, CA

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. They are louder, no question. The new column gets set plumb and braced, and we set the float rod stops so the pump turns on and off at the right water levels. They fit pits as narrow as 10 inches.

    Pedestal Pump Service in Gilroy, CA

    Louder, yes, but a pedestal motor sits high and dry, so in a narrow pit it often outlasts a submersible by several years. Narrow pits favor pedestals. We replace worn units and set the float rod stops for a proper cycle. A submersible is worth a look when the pit is wide enough.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Gilroy

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. When the check valve leaks, the water in the pipe drops back into the basin and the pump has to move it twice, which shortens its life. New valve every time. In a tight pit we use a vertical switch, and in a wide pit a tethered float.

    Cast Iron Submersible Pumps in Gilroy

    Cast iron sheds motor heat into the water around it far better than a plastic housing does, which is a big part of why a Zoeller M53 or Liberty 250 outlasts a hardware-store pump. Most swaps take one to two hours. We also check that the float has room to travel without touching the basin wall.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Gilroy

    Drought years alternate with very wet years here, so conservation rules and low-flow fixtures are a constant part of plumbing decisions. A standard pump just sits there once the power drops. The fix is a second, 12-volt pump on its own float, set a little higher than the main pump and wired to a charger and deep-cycle battery. It also covers a stuck primary switch. The alarm will tell you which one is running.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Gilroy, CA

    A pit that is filling with the pump silent means you have maybe an hour or two before water crosses the basement floor, depending on how fast it is coming in. Look at the breaker and the GFCI outlet, then pull the lid and check the float for a hang-up. A shop vac or a bucket can hold the level down. Our trucks carry common sizes, so most emergency calls end with a new pump running the same visit.

    Sump Basin and Pit Replacement in Gilroy, CA

    Old clay tile or 18-inch plastic basins crack and fill with silt, and a pump in a basin that small cycles every few minutes during a heavy rain. The fix is to cut the slab, drop in a bigger poly basin with a bolted lid, and patch the concrete. More volume in the pit means fewer starts per hour. Fewer starts, longer motor life.
