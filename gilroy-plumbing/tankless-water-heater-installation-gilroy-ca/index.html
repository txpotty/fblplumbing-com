    Tankless Water Heater Relocation in Gilroy

    Moving the heater is common on a tankless job because the old tank spot is not always the best place.     Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Gilroy, CA

    Calcium scale is the most common reason tankless heaters fail. Rural properties around Gilroy often rely on private wells, which may need filtration for iron, sediment, or other minerals. Scale coats the heat exchanger, cuts efficiency and triggers error codes, and left alone it can crack the exchanger and end the warranty claim before it starts. Flush it yearly.
