    Professional Toilet Repair Services in Gilroy, CA

    Running Toilet Repair in Gilroy

    A running toilet is not just annoying, it can waste hundreds of gallons of water a day, leading to much higher water bills. This common issue is usually caused by a faulty flapper, a misaligned fill valve, or an improperly adjusted float. Our plumbers promptly identify the exact cause, whether it's mineral buildup from hard water affecting the flapper seal or a worn-out component, and replace the necessary parts to stop the continuous flow and restore efficiency.

    Leaking Toilet Base Repair in Gilroy, CA

    Water around the base usually means the wax ring has failed or the toilet has been rocking on a loose flange. On slab foundations, the flange often sits on concrete and can crack when bolts are overtightened. We pull the toilet, inspect the flange, install a repair ring or new flange if needed and reset the bowl on a new seal. We also check for soft flooring so you know if any water damage needs attention.

    Leaking Toilet Repair in Gilroy

    Toilet leaks can cause serious water damage to your bathroom floor, subfloor, and even the ceiling below. Leaks can start from the base of the toilet, the tank, or the supply line. Common culprits include a worn-out wax ring, loose bolts, or cracked porcelain. We carefully inspect all potential leak points, replacing seals, tightening connections, or recommending bowl replacement if the porcelain is damaged, preventing further water intrusion.

    Fill Valve Replacement in Gilroy

    A noisy, slow or hissing tank usually points to a worn fill valve. Mineral scale clogs the small ports inside, which causes long refill times and phantom refills in the night. We replace old ballcock valves with modern adjustable fill valves from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the water level to the manufacturer mark. The job takes about 20 minutes and makes an older toilet much quieter.

    Clogged Toilet Clearing in Gilroy, CA

    Stubborn toilet clogs are a frequent problem that can bring your bathroom to a halt. While some can be cleared with a plunger, deeper blockages may indicate issues further down the drain line or even in the main sewer line. Our technicians use professional-grade augers and sometimes even camera inspections to locate and remove obstructions effectively. We handle everything from foreign objects to excessive toilet paper, ensuring your toilet drains freely again.

    Toilet Tank and Bowl Crack Repair in Gilroy

    Cracks in a toilet tank or bowl in Gilroy can lead to serious water leakage and require immediate attention. While small hairline cracks might be temporarily sealed, larger or structural cracks often necessitate replacement of the affected component. Our technicians assess the severity of the crack and advise on the most cost-effective and durable solution. We can perform careful tank or bowl replacements, ensuring a watertight seal and proper function, preventing further water damage to your flooring or subfloor.

    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Gilroy, CA

    A hairline crack in the tank or a bulging braided supply line can let go without warning and flood a bathroom. We inspect the tank, tank-to-bowl bolts and gasket, and the supply line on every visit. If the porcelain is cracked we will price a replacement tank or new toilet, and we upgrade old plastic or vinyl supply lines to braided stainless.
