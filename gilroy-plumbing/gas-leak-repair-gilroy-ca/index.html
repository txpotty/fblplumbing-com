    Appliance Gas Line Repair in Gilroy, CA

    Gas leaks can occur at connections to appliances like water heaters, furnaces, stoves or clothes dryers. These often involve worn flex lines, loose fittings or failed shut-off valves. Our technicians are experienced at identifying and repairing leaks at these critical points. We can replace old or damaged appliance connectors, tighten fittings and make sure all gas appliances in your Gilroy, CA home are properly and securely connected to the gas supply.

    Gas Meter to House Line Repair in Gilroy, CA

    The gas line running from your utility's meter to your home's main shut-off valve is a frequent area for leaks. This line can be exposed to environmental factors that speed up wear. FBL Plumbing specializes in repairing or replacing these critical segments.     Propane Gas Leak Repair in Gilroy, CA

    Rural homes often run on propane from an above-ground or buried tank. Leaks may occur at regulators, tank connections or underground lines. Because propane is heavier than air, leaks can collect in low areas. We test the full system, repair the leak and verify correct regulator pressures.

    Corrugated Stainless Steel Tubing Repair in Gilroy, CA

    Many homes in Gilroy, CA use corrugated stainless steel tubing, or CSST, for gas lines due to its flexibility and ease of installation. While efficient, CSST can be vulnerable to damage from lightning strikes or improper installation. Our plumbers are trained in the exact repair and bonding requirements for CSST, so any leaks in this type of piping are addressed safely and according to manufacturer and code specifications.

    Outdoor Gas Line Repair in Gilroy

    Interior Gas Pipe Repair in Gilroy, CA
