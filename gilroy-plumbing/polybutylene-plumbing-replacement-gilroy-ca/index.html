    A poly leak inside a wall or ceiling can run for days before you see a stain. The meter often tells first.     Polybutylene Leak Repair in Gilroy

    Repairs on polybutylene are temporary by nature, because the pipe next to the repair is the same age. A repair buys time. When a poly line leaks, we cut out the bad section and splice in PEX with approved transition fittings, which stops the leak and gives you time to schedule a full replacement on your own terms.

    Emergency Polybutylene Pipe Repair in Gilroy, CA

    Copper Replacement for Polybutylene in Gilroy, CA

    Some homeowners in Gilroy, CA want copper when they take out polybutylene. That works fine. Our copper replacements use thicker Type L pipe with lead-free soldered or pressed joints, not the thinner Type M. Copper costs more and takes longer, since each turn needs a fitting, but it tolerates heat and sunlight well and has a long track record.

    Full Home Polybutylene Repiping in Gilroy

    A full replacement takes every poly supply line out of service, not just the sections that have failed. Leaving some poly keeps the risk. New pipe goes from the main to every fixture and appliance, each one gets a new quarter-turn stop and the system is tested before any wall is closed.

    Fitting Failure and Emergency Repair in Gilroy

    Acetal plastic and copper crimp ring fittings are often the first part of a poly system to fail. A burst fitting in an attic or wall can cause major damage in minutes. We respond to emergency leaks, make temporary repairs and then schedule a full replacement, because stopping the problem for good means taking the poly system out of service entirely.

    Partial Polybutylene Replacement in Gilroy, CA

    Full replacement is the right long-term fix, but it is not always possible right away. Budget and timing matter. Replacing the riskiest parts first, near the heater and in the attic, lowers the odds of a burst while you plan the rest. You will know exactly what remains.
