    Wall Waterproofing Work We Do in Gilroy, CA

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Gilroy

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most reliable fix for a leaking basement, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing so water has somewhere to go. Expansive clay soils are common across Northern California, and seasonal swelling and shrinking can shift slabs and buried sewer laterals. We backfill after the drain is tested.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Gilroy

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, lapped 12 inches at the seams and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and hold it in place with termination strip. It is a cheap fix that makes a big difference.

    Cost-Effective Waterproofing Solutions in Gilroy, CA

    Not every wet wall needs a $15,000 excavation. Sometimes the fix is a downspout extension and regrading the soil so it slopes away from the house, a drop of about six inches in the first ten feet, and that costs a few hundred dollars. We start with the cheapest fix that will actually work. You get the options in writing, side by side.

    Commercial Walls Waterproofing in Gilroy

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and mechanical rooms in Gilroy take on water the same way houses do, just with more at stake. A wet electrical room can shut a business down. We schedule around operating hours, work with property managers on access, and follow the waterproofing specs written into the building plans for below-grade walls. We keep downtime short.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Gilroy

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, cement board with a membrane or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. Corners, niches and valve holes get extra fabric or preformed pieces.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Gilroy, CA

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and run it to a sump basin so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. It just stops puddling on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and makes sense when a patio or driveway sits against the foundation.

    Waterproofing Costs vs. Repeat Water Damage in Gilroy, CA

    Paying once beats paying every two years. Waterproofing a shower wall usually lands in the low thousands, while replacing drywall, cabinets and flooring again and again costs far more, and mold remediation on top of that can double the bill. The price for exterior block sealing depends on how much wall there is. Your quote lists each option with its price.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Gilroy

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane foam, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. They can mean the wall is bowing from soil pressure, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Vertical cracks are usually a one-visit job.
