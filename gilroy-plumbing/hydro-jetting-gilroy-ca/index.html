    High-Pressure Water Jetting for Drain Cleaning in Gilroy

    High-pressure water jetting, or hydro jetting, is a non-invasive method that uses highly pressurized water to clear and clean pipes. Our equipment in Gilroy can generate water pressures up to 4,000 PSI, powerful enough to break apart stubborn blockages and scour pipe walls clean without chemicals. This method is effective for many pipe materials, from PVC to cast iron, and is an environmentally friendly choice for deep drain cleaning.

    Hydro Jetting Before Pipe Lining in Gilroy, CA

    Cured-in-place pipe lining needs a clean surface to adhere and seal correctly. We jet the host pipe to remove roots, scale and debris, then camera it to confirm it is ready. Any heavy obstructions are cut out before the liner is installed. Skipping this step can lead to wrinkles or poor bonding. Proper prep is part of why lined pipe can last many years.

    Hydro Jetting for Hard Water Scale in Gilroy

    Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running mineral-heavy. Even moderate mineral content can lead to scale buildup in pipes over many years. This scale can restrict flow and catch other debris, leading to clogs. Hydro jetting is highly effective at breaking down and flushing out these hard mineral deposits, restoring the full diameter of your pipes. This is a better method compared to chemical treatments for scale removal.

    Scale Removal in Cast Iron Pipes in Gilroy

    Cast iron drains in older homes build up rust tubercles and mineral scale, and hard water speeds that process. Jetting with the right nozzle can strip much of this buildup and restore flow. We keep pressure in a safe range for aged iron and watch the camera for thin spots. This cleaning is also the standard prep step before pipe lining. Clean pipe walls help a liner bond correctly.

    Hydro Jetting vs. Snaking Cost Comparison in Gilroy, CA

    While snaking might be cheaper upfront for simple clogs, hydro jetting offers a more complete and long-lasting solution, often saving money in the long run for Gilroy, CA residents. Snaking usually punches a hole through a blockage, leaving residue behind, which can lead to recurring clogs. Hydro jetting cleans the entire pipe, preventing future issues and reducing the frequency of service calls, making it a more cost-effective choice for persistent drain problems.
