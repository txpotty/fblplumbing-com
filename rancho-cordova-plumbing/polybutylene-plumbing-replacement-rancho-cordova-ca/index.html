    Frequently Asked Questions About Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Rancho Cordova

    PEX replacements usually run $4,000 to $10,000, and copper often runs $8,000 to $15,000 or more. Access changes the price. House size, fixture count and how easy it is to reach the attic or crawlspace are the main factors, and we give you a detailed written quote after looking at the house in person.

    We commonly use Uponor PEX-A and Type L copper with Viega ProPress or soldered fittings. We recommend materials based on your home and water conditions.

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water break down the plastic from the inside, making it brittle. Fittings and hot water lines often fail first.

    Gray or blue plastic pipe stamped PB2110 is the main sign. Leaks at fittings are another. Poly often looks fine right up until it fails, so if your home was built between about 1978 and 1995 and has this pipe, replacement is recommended even without a leak yet.

    Every poly replacement we do comes with a written warranty on materials and labor. Manufacturer coverage adds more. Both warranties come in writing with the photos and permit record, all in one place for later.

    Most full poly repipes take three to five days, including drywall patching. Larger homes take longer. Fixture count, attic access and pipe choice set the schedule, and you will usually have water each evening.

    There will be some noise and a few small openings in walls. We limit the mess. Our crews cover furniture, protect floors and clean up thoroughly each day, and we work through the house in sections so most rooms stay usable while we are there.

    For PEX, we use trusted brands like Uponor, Viega and Zurn. We use Uponor most often. Copper replacements use Type L instead of thin Type M, since the heavier wall lasts longer.
