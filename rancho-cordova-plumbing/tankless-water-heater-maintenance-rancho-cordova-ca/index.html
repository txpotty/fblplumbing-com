    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Rancho Cordova

    Picture a bundle of thin tubes with a gas flame under them and cold water racing through at three or four gallons a minute. That is the exchanger. Calcium and magnesium drop out on the hottest tube walls first and narrow the passages. We dissolve it with 45 to 60 minutes of pump-driven descaling solution.

    Yearly Tankless Inspection in Rancho Cordova

    Annual Tankless Checkup in Rancho Cordova, CA

    The annual checkup is a top-to-bottom pass on the heater. We read gas pressure at the inlet with a manometer while the burner is at full fire and compare it to the rating plate.     Home Tankless Flushing in Rancho Cordova

    Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. A pump on the service valves pushes descaling solution through the coil for roughly an hour. The flow returns. The temperature holds steady and the high-limit shutdowns stop.
