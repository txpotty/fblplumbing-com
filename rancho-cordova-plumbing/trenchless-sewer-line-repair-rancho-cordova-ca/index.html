    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Cost in Rancho Cordova, CA

    Point repairs often cost $1,500 to $3,500, while full lining of a residential lateral commonly runs $4,000 to $12,000. Pipe bursting typically ranges from $5,000 to $15,000.     Pipe Bursting in Rancho Cordova, CA

    Two pits are standard for a burst, one at the house end near the foundation and one out near the street or the city tap. Before pulling, the crew heat-fuses sections of HDPE into one continuous length on the ground. The winch or hydraulic puller then drags the bursting head and the new pipe through. It goes quickly.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Prep and Lining in Rancho Cordova

    Prep is most of the job. We jet the line to remove roots and grease, then camera it again to measure the exact footage before cutting the liner tube to length. A poor prep job means a wrinkled liner. Lining works best on cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their round shape.

    Causes of Sewer Line Failure in Rancho Cordova, CA

    Shifting soil is one of the main reasons sewer joints separate, crack or drop out of grade over the years. Expansive clay soils are common across Northern California, and seasonal swelling and shrinking can shift slabs and buried sewer laterals. Roots are the other big one, since a hairline gap at a clay joint is enough for a feeder root to get in and then swell into a mat that catches paper. Then age finishes the job. Cast iron scales and thins, and clay bells crack under the weight of soil and traffic.

    Commercial Trenchless Sewer Line Work in Rancho Cordova, CA

    Restaurants feel a sewer failure fast, because grease and a dish machine put heavy load on a 4- or 6-inch building drain all day. We can line at night. A trench through a parking lot means lost spaces and a blocked entrance for a week or more. A liner installed from a cleanout skips all of it.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Upgrades in Rancho Cordova

    Bursting can upsize the line. Each end only needs a pit around three by four feet. No trench in between.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Lining in Rancho Cordova, CA

    Cast iron rusts from the inside out, and the bottom of the pipe goes first. That is called channel rot. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or carbide cutters spinning on a flexible shaft, which brings the wall back to bare metal so a liner can bond to it. Under a slab, that beats jackhammering.
