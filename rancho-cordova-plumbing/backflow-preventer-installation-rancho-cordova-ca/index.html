    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Rancho Cordova

    Landscape irrigation is the most common reason homeowners need a backflow device. Fertilizer, herbicides and stagnant water in sprinkler and drip lines can be siphoned into your drinking water if pressure drops. We install pressure vacuum breakers or double check assemblies based on local requirements and the design of your system. Above-ground installs are set at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head when a PVB is used.

    Residential Backflow Preventer Installation in Rancho Cordova

    For homeowners in Rancho Cordova, protecting your family's drinking water is the top priority.     Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Rancho Cordova

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules permit. We size them to avoid excessive pressure loss on your system.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Rancho Cordova

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are often required for high-hazard applications in Rancho Cordova. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. FBL Plumbing specializes in precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with top brands like Watts and Febco.

    Backflow Enclosures and Freeze Protection in Rancho Cordova, CA

    Above-ground assemblies sit in direct sun in summer and can freeze on cold nights. Freezing is uncommon along the coast and on valley floors in Northern California, but frosty nights do happen and can crack exposed outdoor pipes. We install insulated enclosures, freeze blankets or heated cabinets based on your location. Enclosures also discourage theft, since brass backflow assemblies are a common target for scrap thieves. We choose enclosures with enough room for testing and service.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Rancho Cordova

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a common backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Rancho Cordova, DCVAs are frequently installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers precisely install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.

    Most municipalities require annual testing of backflow preventers by a licensed, certified backflow tester. This verifies the device is working correctly and continues to protect your potable water supply from contamination. We can also assist with scheduling these annual tests.

    Many jurisdictions require a permit for new backflow assemblies, especially on commercial properties. We check local rules and pull the permit when required.

    Signs of a failing backflow preventer include discolored or foul-smelling water, a noticeable drop in water pressure, visible leaks around the device or water discharging from the relief valve on an RPZ. If you notice any of these, contact us for an inspection in Rancho Cordova.
