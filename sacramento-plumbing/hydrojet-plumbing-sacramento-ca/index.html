    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydrojet Plumbing in Sacramento

    The cost of hydrojet plumbing in Sacramento, CA can range from $300 to $800 or more, depending on the severity and location of the clog, as well as the length and diameter of the pipe. Factors like accessibility, the presence of tree roots, and whether a prior camera inspection is needed will also affect the final price. We provide transparent, upfront quotes after an assessment.

    Jetting removes the roots inside the pipe, but roots will grow back if the joint or crack remains open. Regular jetting can control them for years. Pipe lining or repair is the permanent fix.

    Repeat clogs, slow drains throughout the house, gurgling sounds and sewer odors all suggest buildup in the line. Kitchens that back up every few months are a telltale sign of grease. A camera inspection confirms whether jetting is the right fix.

    FBL Plumbing utilizes professional hydrojetting equipment from reputable manufacturers known for their power and reliability, such as Spartan Tool and MyTana. These professional-grade machines ensure we can deliver high quality, effective drain cleaning services for our clients in Sacramento.

    Hydrojetting is usually safe for most modern plumbing pipes, including PVC, ABS, and cast iron, when performed by a skilled professional. However, it may not be suitable for severely corroded, cracked, or improperly installed pipes. We always perform a pre-inspection to confirm pipe integrity.

    For most homes, hydrojetting every year or two is enough for preventive maintenance, especially in areas with hard water or older plumbing. Businesses, particularly those in food service, may benefit from more frequent cleanings, such as quarterly or every six months, to prevent major blockages.

    Rental jetters exist, but high pressure can injure you or damage a weak pipe if used incorrectly. Without a camera you also cannot tell whether the line is clean or broken. Professional jetting is safer and usually more effective.

    While hydrojetting provides a thorough clean, some underlying issues like severely damaged or collapsed pipes might cause clogs to return. If a clog reappears shortly after our hydrojet service, we will investigate further, often with another camera inspection, to identify any structural problems that might require repair or replacement, and discuss warranty options.
