    Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in Sacramento, CA

    Gravity grease interceptors, or GGI units, are larger, typically located outdoors, and rely on the natural difference in specific gravity between FOG and water. These are common for high-volume commercial kitchens. Our team handles the complex excavation, concrete work, and plumbing connections required for proper GGI installation, ensuring compliance with all local Northern California ordinances for waste management.

    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Sacramento

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with limited space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid easily.

    Grease Trap Installation for Schools and Institutions in Sacramento

    School cafeterias, hospitals, senior living facilities and churches with kitchens often need commercial interceptors. These sites usually have large dish machines and steam kettles that push high flow. We size interceptors to handle peak lunch or dinner loads without bypassing grease. Our crews schedule work around school breaks or off hours. We also provide documentation for facility managers and inspectors.

    Food Truck Commissary and Ghost Kitchen Traps in Sacramento, CA

    Commissary kitchens and ghost kitchens often have several prep stations and heavy dishwashing loads. We design grease trap installs that handle high flow from multiple sinks and dish machines. Each unit is sized for the combined discharge, not just one fixture. We also install cleanouts and sample ports where the city requires them. This keeps shared kitchens compliant for each tenant.

    New Restaurant Build-Out Grease Traps in Sacramento

    If you are opening a new restaurant, the grease trap is often a key part of plan review. We work with your architect or contractor to size the unit and show it on plumbing plans. We then install it during rough-in so it is ready for inspection. Getting the trap right early avoids expensive changes after the slab is poured. We also coordinate with the city's pretreatment staff when they require a review.

    In-Ground Gravity Interceptor Installation in Sacramento, CA

    Larger restaurants and high-volume kitchens often need an outdoor gravity interceptor. These concrete or polyethylene tanks usually range from several hundred to over 1,500 gallons. We excavate, set the tank on a stable base, connect inlet and outlet piping and install manhole risers. Expansive clay soils are common across Northern California, and seasonal swelling and shrinking can crack slabs and buried sewer laterals. We plan excavation equipment around the ground conditions we find on site. A properly installed interceptor can go far longer between pump-outs than an indoor trap.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Trap Installation in Sacramento, CA

    Hydro-mechanical grease traps, also known as automatic grease removal units, offer an active solution for FOG management in Sacramento, CA. These units often include internal baffles and flow control mechanisms to enhance separation efficiency. We provide expert installation of various hydro-mechanical models, ensuring all electrical and plumbing connections are correctly made for reliable performance and automated FOG removal.
