    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Sacramento

    Expect $300 to $800 in Sacramento. The clog drives the cost. A longer or wider pipe costs more to jet, and so does a line packed with roots or a long commercial run, with a written estimate before work.

    Typically 1 to 3 hours. Inspection time is included. Most residential jobs finish within three hours, but heavy commercial lines and severe blockages can run longer.

    Frequent clogs in the same drain. Slow drains everywhere too. Odors, gurgling and multiple slow drains usually point to buildup that needs more than a cable.

    Sound PVC, cast iron and concrete handle it fine. Damaged pipe does not. Severely corroded, cracked or fragile pipe can be damaged further, which is why a camera inspection before jetting is part of every job we do.

    Snaking punches a hole. Jetting cleans the pipe. A cable with an auger opens a path through the blockage, while high-pressure water scours the inside of the pipe and restores it to near-new condition, which lasts much longer.

    Small rental jetters exist. They are not the same. Professional jetters are far more powerful and take training to run safely, and misuse can damage pipe, injure the user or simply fail to clear the blockage.

    Buildup responds best. Grease, scale and sludge. Jetting also handles hair, soap and root hairs, because it cleans the full circumference of the pipe.
