    Main Water Line Leak Detection in Sacramento

    The service line between your meter and house can leak underground for months, especially in older galvanized or copper lines. Wet spots in the yard, unusually green grass or a sinking area near the front of the house are clues. We use line tracers to map the pipe and ground microphones to hear the leak. That keeps excavation to a small, focused hole.

    Hidden Wall and Ceiling Leak Detection in Sacramento

    Leaks inside walls and ceilings often show up as stains, bubbling paint, soft drywall or a musty smell. We use thermal cameras to spot temperature differences caused by water, then confirm with moisture meters. Acoustic tools help when the leak is on a pressurized line. Pinpointing the source lets us cut a small, neat access opening.

    Pool and Spa Leak Detection in Sacramento

    Summer heat and wind can evaporate a lot of pool water, so it can be hard to tell normal loss from a leak. A bucket test compares evaporation to actual water loss. We then pressure test plumbing lines and use dye testing around lights, skimmers and fittings. Finding the leak protects the pool shell and the soil around it.

    Fixture and Appliance Leak Detection in Sacramento, CA

    Running toilets, worn faucet cartridges and leaking water heater connections waste more water than most people realize. A silent toilet leak can lose hundreds of gallons a day. We check flappers, fill valves, supply lines, softeners and water heater fittings. These repairs are usually quick and inexpensive.
