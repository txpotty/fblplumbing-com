    What Our Commercial Plumbers Do in Sacramento, CA

    Commercial Sewer Line Repair in Sacramento, CA

    The building sewer on a commercial property carries everything from every tenant, so a root intrusion or a belly in the line affects the whole property. First we run a camera and locate the damage depth, then we compare a spot dig, a cured-in-place liner and pipe bursting. Trenchless saves parking lots. Lining a 6-inch lateral can often be done in a day without tearing up asphalt or closing an entrance.

    Kitchen and Floor Drain Cleaning in Sacramento

    Floor drains in commercial kitchens collect grease, rice, mop water and the odd utensil. Most kitchens see the same drain back up every few months. A sectional machine opens the line, and a jetter cleans grease off the walls of the 2-inch and 3-inch branches so it stays open longer. Trap primers get checked too.

    Commercial Restroom Plumbing in Sacramento

    A broken flushometer in a busy restroom gets noticed by every customer who walks in. We repair and replace Sloan and Zurn flush valves, sensor faucets, wall-hung toilets and carriers. ADA work is part of it too. For ADA remodels we set fixture heights, add grab bar blocking in the wall and insulate the drain and supply lines under sinks.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Sacramento

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage. Our tester opens each test cock in order, records the check valve and relief valve readings on a calibrated gauge and compares them against the pass limits printed on the test form. Passing devices get tagged. We file the report with your water provider and keep a copy on our side for next year.

    Commercial Plumbing Costs and Maintenance Plans in Sacramento, CA

    Backflow tests often run $75 to $175 per assembly, and kitchen line jetting usually costs $350 to $900 depending on length and buildup. After-hours calls cost more. A maintenance plan spreads those costs across the year and cuts the number of emergency visits, which are always the most expensive kind of plumbing work for a business. Every plan comes as a written proposal.

    Sewer Camera Inspection for Businesses in Sacramento

    A camera inspection shows the inside of a drain line on a monitor and records it so you can share it with a landlord or contractor. Guesswork goes away. The camera goes in at a cleanout, we note distances to every defect and then locate each one from the surface with a receiver. The video is yours to keep. That footage helps you budget repairs and explain them to owners or a board.

    Grease Interceptor and Line Service in Sacramento, CA

    Grease does not stay in the trap if the trap is full or the baffles are broken. It ends up in your line. We inspect interceptors, replace failed baffles and outlet tees and jet the kitchen lines that feed them, which removes hardened grease that a cable just slides past. A jetted line holds up much better on a busy night.

    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Sacramento

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Expansive clay soils are common across Northern California, and seasonal swelling and shrinking can crack slabs and buried sewer laterals. We confirm it with a meter test and pressure test, then use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to pinpoint the leak within a foot or two. Then we decide. Some leaks are best fixed with a small cut through the slab, while others are cheaper to abandon and reroute overhead. You get a price on each option.
