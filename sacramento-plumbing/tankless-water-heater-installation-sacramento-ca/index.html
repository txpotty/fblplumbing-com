    Why Choose FBL Plumbing for Tankless Water Heater Installation in Sacramento, CA?

    Tankless heaters cost more up front than tanks, so the install has to be right. An undersized unit that short-cycles or a vent that ices up will keep throwing error codes, and you pay for it every month. Sizing is math, not a guess.

    • No Tank to Leak, There is no 50-gallon tank waiting to rust out on the floor.
    • Gas Done Right, Gas lines are sized by load and length and pressure tested.
    • Scale Protection, Service valves plus an inhibitor keep scale off the heat exchanger.

    Citywide Coverage

    From older homes with tanks tucked in closets to newer builds with the heater in the attic, we go tankless all over Sacramento. Closet units may vent through a lined chimney, and attic units need a pan, a platform and a condensate line that drains by gravity. The plan comes first.

    Experienced Team

    Our team uses the code tables to size gas piping, not by rule of thumb. Run length, fitting count and all the other gas appliances in the home decide how big the tankless line must be to run at full fire without starving the furnace. Low gas means error codes.

    Safety-First Approach

    Condensing tankless units drain slightly acidic water, so we route it to an approved drain and usually add a neutralizer. Left untreated, condensate can wear on cast iron drains and concrete, and some codes require neutralizing, so nearly every condensing unit we install gets one. Refills are cheap.

    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in Sacramento, CA

    Switching from a tank is more than a swap. A typical 40-gallon gas tank uses around 40,000 BTU, while a whole-home tankless unit may need 150,000 to 199,000, so the gas line from the meter almost always gets upsized. The vent changes as well. Both are on the estimate.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Sacramento, CA

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Sacramento, CA homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. A whole-home electric unit draws heavy amperage. Some models need two or three 40 to 60 amp double-pole breakers, more than a 100-amp panel can usually spare alongside the range and AC. We check the panel first.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Sacramento

    Conversions in Sacramento usually take a full day. The old tank gets drained and hauled off, the gas line is upsized and tested, a new vent goes out through a wall or roof, and the unit is mounted, plumbed with isolation valves and fired. You shower that night. Inspection follows within days.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Installation in Sacramento

    Commercial hot water loads in Sacramento run longer and harder than a house. Units share a header manifold. Service one, keep the others going.

    Tankless Water Heater Relocation in Sacramento

    Moving the heater is common on a tankless job since the tank's location may not suit the new unit. Mounting near the gas meter on a garage or outside wall shortens the gas line and venting, though it can add distance to the bathrooms. We weigh both. Long hot runs may call for recirculation.
