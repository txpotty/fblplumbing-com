    Sump Pump Installation & Repair in Sacramento

    A sump pump lives in a pit cut below floor level and kicks on when a float rises. Most failures come from a stuck float switch, a burned-out motor, or a check valve that lets the discharge water fall back into the pit and short-cycles the pump. We test yours with a bucket. Add a battery backup if storms knock out power.

    Clogged Drain Tile Repair in Sacramento

    Footing drains clog with silt and roots over the years, and once they stop flowing the water table at the wall rises. A camera run from the sump inlet shows us where the tile is plugged. A jetter usually opens it. If the old clay tile has collapsed, it gets replaced with new perforated pipe.

    Crack Injection for Basements in Sacramento

    Injection works on poured concrete walls, not block. Polyurethane is better for active leaks because it foams on contact with water.
