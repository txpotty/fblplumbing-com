    PEX Pipe Installation in Sacramento, CA

    PEX is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, resists scale and corrosion and installs faster than copper. Faster install means lower labor cost. We keep PEX out of direct sunlight, away from water heater flues and recessed light housings and support it at the spacing the manufacturer calls for, which protects both the pipe and the warranty.

    Copper Replacement for Polybutylene in Sacramento, CA

    Some homeowners in Sacramento, CA want copper when they take out polybutylene. That works fine. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes, and join it with lead-free solder or press fittings. Expect a higher price and a slightly longer job with copper, in exchange for good heat and UV tolerance and a long history.

    Partial Polybutylene Replacement in Sacramento, CA

    Full replacement is the right long-term fix, but it is not always possible right away. Budget and timing matter. Replacing the riskiest parts first, near the heater and in the attic, lowers the odds of a burst while you plan the rest. You will know exactly what remains.

    Finding Polybutylene Leaks in Sacramento

    A poly leak inside a wall or ceiling can run for days before you see a stain. Watch the water meter. With every fixture off, a spinning leak indicator on the meter means water is going somewhere, and we use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to find it without opening walls at random. Finding it lets us make a temporary repair and plan the full replacement.

    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Sacramento, CA

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. We use Uponor PEX-A with expansion fittings that do not restrict flow. Every fixture, appliance and the water heater gets new pipe from the main, and most houses are finished in two to four days with water on each evening.

    Full Home Polybutylene Repiping in Sacramento

    A full replacement takes every poly supply line out of service, not just the sections that have failed. Partial jobs leave risk behind. New pipe goes from the main to every fixture and appliance, each one gets a new quarter-turn stop and the system is tested before any wall is closed.

    Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Sacramento

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. Check the heater first. It also shows up under sinks, at the main and through garage walls, and we follow it into the attic to see how much of the house it feeds.

    Polybutylene to PEX Transitions in Sacramento, CA

    Sometimes the yard service line coming into the house is not polybutylene, or it was replaced already. When the service line is already sound, we tie new PEX into it at the main with a listed transition fitting and skip the trenching. We check the service line first. Blue or black poly service pipe should come out as well.
