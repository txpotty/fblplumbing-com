    PEX Pipe Installation in Sacramento, CA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a popular choice for polybutylene replacement due to its flexibility, durability, and resistance to corrosion and freezing.     Copper Repiping Solutions in Sacramento, CA

    For homeowners in Sacramento, CA who prefer traditional materials, we also offer full polybutylene pipe replacement with copper.     Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Sacramento

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. You may see it at the water heater, under sinks or near the main shutoff. We inspect visible lines, check the stamps and trace runs through the attic. You get a clear answer and a written estimate for replacement.

    Polybutylene to PEX Transition in Sacramento, CA

    Transitioning from polybutylene to PEX is a popular and cost-effective solution for many homeowners in Sacramento, CA. We use specialized transition fittings and techniques to cleanly connect your existing main water line to the new PEX system. This ensures a watertight and code-compliant connection, providing a complete upgrade to a modern, durable plumbing material throughout your home's interior. This transition offers the benefits of PEX without the need for extensive excavation or disruption to your main service line.
