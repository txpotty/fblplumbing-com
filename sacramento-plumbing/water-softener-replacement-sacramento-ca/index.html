    Picking the Right Softener for Sacramento, CA Homes

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Sacramento

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. We replace timer units with metered valves that regenerate on gallons used. You will buy less salt. We set the brine refill to your hardness so the system is not dumping salt down the drain every few days.

    Whole-Home Softener Placement in Sacramento, CA

    Every tap in the house, including the shower and the water heater, gets soft water when the softener sits on the main line ahead of everything. We typically leave the hose bibs on hard water. No sense spending salt on the garden.

    Clack Water Softener Replacement in Sacramento

    Clack WS1 valves are easy to program and easy to rebuild, which is why a lot of water treatment shops have switched to them. We can replace just the valve on a good tank. Or the whole system.

    Salt-Free Alternatives in Sacramento, CA

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. In those cases a TAC conditioner works, though we are upfront that it stops scale and does not make water feel soft. Low-sodium diets are another reason.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Sacramento

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break them up and clean out the mush at the bottom. Cracked tanks and stuck safety floats get replaced. Pellets bridge less than rock salt.

    Urgent Softener Problems in Sacramento

    Not every softener failure is urgent. Water on the floor from the valve or an overflowing brine tank does count, and those calls get moved up. Turn the bypass handle and wait for us.

    Commercial Water Softener Replacement in Sacramento

    Restaurants and laundromats run softeners hard, and a commercial unit often needs a 1.5-inch or 2-inch valve and twin tanks to keep up with flow. Peak flow drives the sizing. Installs happen before opening or after close.

    Softener Loop Installation in Sacramento, CA

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. Homes without one get a loop cut in, with shutoffs and a bypass. Outdoor spigots stay on hard water. We run the drain to a standpipe or floor drain through an air gap.

    Softener Plumbers Near You in Sacramento, CA

    Most softener jobs around Sacramento are finished in an afternoon. We carry common 32,000 and 48,000-grain systems, so a failed unit can often be swapped the same day you call. Bigger systems take a day to order.

    Local water varies. City water and well water a few miles apart can test very differently, and iron or chlorine changes the setup. The system matches your test, not a guess.
