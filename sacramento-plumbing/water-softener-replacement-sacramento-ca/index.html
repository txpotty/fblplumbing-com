    Traditional Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Sacramento

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and effective solution for hard water in Sacramento. These systems use ion exchange to remove calcium and magnesium minerals, which are notorious for causing scale buildup. We replace old, inefficient salt-based units with modern, high-efficiency models that use less salt and water. Our plumbers ensure proper brine tank installation and regeneration settings for strong performance.

    Clack Water Softener Replacement in Sacramento

    Clack valves are a popular choice for their reliability and adjustable settings. If you have an older Clack system failing, FBL Plumbing offers professional Clack water softener replacement in Sacramento. We can replace just the control valve or the entire system, confirming compatibility with your existing setup and configuring the new unit for peak performance.

    Fleck Water Softener Replacement in Sacramento, CA

    Fleck is another leading brand known for long-lasting water softener components. When your Fleck system needs replacing in Sacramento, CA, our plumbers are proficient with these units. We will carefully remove the old Fleck valve and tanks, install the new equipment and program it to your water hardness and usage patterns. That restores efficient water softening.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Sacramento

    Cracked brine tanks, salt bridges and failing float assemblies cause softeners to stop regenerating properly. We replace brine tanks and safety floats to restore consistent brine draw. We also clean out mushy salt buildup that clogs the tank. A new brine tank is often part of a full system replacement. We recommend the best salt type for your system.
