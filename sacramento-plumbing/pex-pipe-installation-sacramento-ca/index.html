    Copper to PEX Conversions in Sacramento

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more often follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and reduces the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.

    Hydronic Heating with PEX in Sacramento

    PEX is also a premier choice for hydronic heating systems, including radiant floor heating. Its ability to withstand high temperatures and resist corrosion makes it well suited for circulating hot water. If you are considering radiant heat for a specific area of your home or business in Sacramento, PEX is the pipe to use.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Sacramento, CA

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is known for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Plenty of insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to insure and sell.

    Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Sacramento

    Kitchen and bathroom remodels are a great time to replace old supply lines with PEX.
