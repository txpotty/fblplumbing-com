    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Sacramento

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall. We remove failing tile, install cement board or foam board and apply a membrane such as Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban. Seams, corners and niches get extra reinforcement before new tile is set.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Sacramento

    Even small cracks in your foundation walls can allow water to seep into your basement or crawl space. Our walls waterproofing service includes foundation crack repair using proven epoxy or polyurethane injection techniques. These materials fill and seal cracks, preventing future water entry and often strengthening the foundation. Addressing cracks early in Sacramento homes is crucial to avoid larger, more costly structural issues.

    Commercial Wall Waterproofing in Sacramento

    Restaurants, gyms, salons and offices often have restrooms, locker rooms and kitchens with constant water exposure. We waterproof commercial showers, mop sink walls and restroom wet walls with durable membranes. Work can be scheduled after hours to reduce downtime.     Basement Wall Sealing in Sacramento, CA

    Basement wall sealing is a critical part of walls waterproofing. Coastal fog and moisture can speed corrosion on exposed fittings, so exterior valves and hose bibs deserve an occasional check. We apply high-quality sealants to porous concrete and masonry walls to prevent moisture from wicking through and causing dampness, mold, or musty odors. This service can be performed alone or as part of a larger waterproofing system, offering a drier, healthier environment in your Sacramento, CA basement.

    Drainage System Integration in Sacramento, CA

    Lasting walls waterproofing often requires a robust drainage system to divert water away from your foundation. This can include interior perimeter drains that channel water to a sump pump, or exterior French drains that collect surface water. Our team designs and installs custom drainage solutions for Sacramento, CA properties, so water is managed well and moved away from your home's vulnerable areas.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Sacramento

    A slow drip from a shower valve, supply stub or drain fitting can soak a wall for months before you notice. We find the leak with moisture meters, thermal imaging and pressure tests. The damaged section of pipe or valve is replaced, and wet drywall is cut out and dried. Once the cavity is dry, we restore the wall and seal any wet-area surfaces.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Sacramento, CA

    Sump pumps are a key part of many interior waterproofing systems, particularly in homes that see heavy rainfall. We install high-capacity sump pumps designed to handle large volumes of water, keeping your basement dry even during flash floods. Our services include primary sump pump installation, battery backup sump pump systems for power outages, and routine maintenance or repair of existing units. We make sure the discharge line is routed properly so water does not circulate back toward the foundation.

    Older homes, especially those built before modern waterproofing standards, are particularly prone to water intrusion. We specialize in assessing and upgrading waterproofing systems for these properties, often combining exterior excavation with interior drainage solutions. We respect the existing structure while installing modern, effective waterproofing, with attention to the challenges of older brick and stone foundations.
