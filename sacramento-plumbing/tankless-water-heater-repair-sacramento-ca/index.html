    Electric Tankless Water Heater Repair in Sacramento, CA

    Whole-house electric tankless units pull a lot of current, often 100 to 150 amps on two or three dedicated breakers. When a breaker trips under load, the cause can be a shorted element or a loose, scorched lug, and both get tested with a meter before anything is replaced. We check element resistance and supply voltage. Scorched wire gets cut back.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Repair in Sacramento

    Navien condensing units use a secondary stainless exchanger to recover heat from the flue gas. That makes condensate, and a plugged trap or a full neutralizer will shut the heater down with a drain or flue error. We clear the trap, check the PVC vent pitch and test the circulation pump on NPE-A models with built-in recirculation. Then we fire it at low and high flow.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnostics in Sacramento, CA

    Every tankless brand flashes its own codes, and the same number can mean different things on two units. A Rinnai 11 is no ignition, a Navien E003 is ignition failure, and a Noritz 11 is also an ignition fault, yet the checks behind each one are not the same. We read the code, pull the history and test the part tied to it. Guessing gets expensive.

    Tankless Flush and Scale Removal in Sacramento

    Lukewarm water at high flow and a unit that runs loud are two early signs of scale. Well-done installs usually include isolation valves with purge ports for this, and if yours does not, we can add them during the visit so future flushing takes less time. White vinegar will do in a pinch. Commercial descaler works faster.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Repair in Sacramento, CA

    Rinnai units are common, and most of their trouble starts with scale. The LC code is the unit's own warning that the exchanger needs a flush. We also see dirty flame rods behind a code 11 or 12 and tired combustion fans behind a code 61, and each part gets tested against Rinnai's published values. Parts come from the factory list.

    Tankless Repair vs Replacement Cost in Sacramento, CA

    Descaling usually runs $175 to $350, while a sensor or igniter repair tends to run $250 to $600. A heat exchanger out of warranty can cost $800 to $1,500 in parts and labor. Replacement is a bigger number. Installed, a new gas tankless typically runs $3,000 to $5,500, so on a unit past 15 years with a failed exchanger we usually lean toward replacing.

    Igniter and Flame Rod Repair in Sacramento, CA

    Gas tankless heaters light with a hot surface or spark igniter, then prove flame with a flame rod. A rod coated in oxide reads low on a microamp meter and the board shuts the gas valve after a few seconds. We clean or replace the rod, look for a cracked igniter or a lazy spark, and confirm the gas valve opens on call. Five-minute fix sometimes.
