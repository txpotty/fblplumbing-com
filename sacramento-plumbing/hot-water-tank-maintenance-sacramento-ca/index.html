    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Sacramento, CA

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is essential for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank. Water chemistry varies from one neighborhood to the next, and it changes the rod's lifespan. Our technicians inspect the anode rod for wear and recommend replacement if it's significantly corroded, typically every 3 to 5 years. Replacing a worn anode rod is a cost-effective way to protect your tank from rust and leaks, sparing you a full tank replacement.

    Standard Hot Water Tank Flushing in Sacramento

    Sediment buildup is a common issue in hot water tanks, especially where water hardness runs high. Our standard flushing service removes these mineral deposits, which can reduce efficiency and lead to premature tank failure. We drain the tank completely, flush out all sediment and refill it, ensuring clear water flow and improved heating performance. This routine service is key for extending the life of your unit.

    Electric Element and Thermostat Service in Sacramento

    Electric tanks rely on upper and lower heating elements that get crusted with scale in hard water. We test each element with a meter, check the thermostats and look for scorched wires at the terminal block. If an element has failed we replace it with the matching wattage and voltage. Plenty of families who think they need a new heater just need a lower element and a flush.

    Emergency Hot Water Tank Troubleshooting in Sacramento

    Even with regular maintenance, now and then unexpected issues arise. FBL Plumbing offers emergency troubleshooting for hot water tank problems in Sacramento. Whether it's a sudden lack of hot water, strange noises or a leak, our technicians are equipped to diagnose the problem quickly and provide immediate solutions. We understand the urgency of a broken hot water tank and strive to restore your comfort quickly and efficiently.

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Sacramento

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause loud popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution approved for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Sacramento

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous. We lift the lever, confirm it opens and reseats and check the discharge line for proper material and termination. We also check your thermal expansion tank pressure with a gauge, since a waterlogged expansion tank lets pressure jump every heating cycle. High street pressure is common in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.
