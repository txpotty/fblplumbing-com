    Water Heater Inspections in Sacramento

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. The serial number tells us the age, and we check for rust, look at the vent and test the relief valve. We check tankless units for scale. On gas units, we look at the burner and flame, check for a sediment trap and confirm the vent is drafting so combustion gases go outside.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspections in Sacramento, CA

    A sewer camera shows the inside of the main drain without digging. We push the camera in from a cleanout, watch for cracks, roots, offsets and low spots and record the whole run to the street or septic tank. A locator marks the depth. Many older homes here sit on raised foundations with crawlspaces, which make supply and drain repairs more accessible than slab work.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Sacramento, CA

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. Shutoffs get tested too.
