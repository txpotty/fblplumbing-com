    Pool and Spa Heater Gas Lines in Sacramento

    Pool heaters are among the largest gas appliances in a home, often rated at 250,000 to 400,000 BTU. They usually sit at the equipment pad far from the meter, so line size and length are crucial. We may recommend a larger main line or a meter upgrade from your gas utility. A properly sized line lets the heater reach its rated output and heat the water efficiently.

    Gas Water Heater and Tankless Line Upgrades in Sacramento, CA

    Tankless water heaters often need up to 199,000 BTU, far more than the 40,000 BTU a typical tank heater uses. Many existing 1/2 inch lines cannot supply that demand, which causes error codes and lukewarm water. We calculate the full house load and upsize the line to 3/4 inch or larger as needed. This upgrade is one of the most critical steps in a successful tankless conversion.

    CSST Gas Line Installation in Sacramento, CA

Our team is certified in CSST installation, including the bonding and grounding code requires. CSST is a common choice for new gas lines or extensions because it routes easily and tolerates building movement, making it a viable option for many Sacramento, CA homes.
