    What Goes Into a Sewer Lateral Replacement in Sacramento, CA

    Sewer Permits and Code in Sacramento, CA

    Sewer replacement nearly always needs a permit. We file it, schedule the inspection and meet the inspector on site, so you never have to take a morning off to stand in the yard. Code covers pipe size, fall, bedding and cleanout placement. Unpermitted sewer work can show up on a home inspection when you sell.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Sacramento

    Cast iron rots from the inside out. Sewer gas and moisture eat away the bottom of the pipe first, so the camera often shows a channel worn through along the invert even when the top still looks solid. Scale snags paper and grease. We swap the outdoor section for plastic pipe. For cast iron under a slab, we talk through lining versus rerouting.

    Commercial Sewer Lateral Work in Sacramento, CA

    Apartment buildings, offices and restaurants in Sacramento, CA need lateral work too. Bigger flow and heavier grease mean careful sizing, extra cleanouts for jetting and attention to the grease interceptor tie-in. Some jobs need traffic control. Tenant notices and work windows get planned ahead.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Sacramento

    No replacement quote without video. The camera goes in at the cleanout and travels out to the main, recording every crack, offset and root ball as it goes. A transmitter in the head lets us paint the spot and depth above ground. That one step decides between a spot repair and a full run.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Replacement in Sacramento, CA

    Orangeburg is tar-soaked wood fiber. Many mid-century houses got it, and by now most of it has blistered and flattened enough to snag waste. Clay is tougher but leaks roots. Several bad spots on one run usually make full replacement the better buy.

    Spot Sewer Repairs in Sacramento, CA

    Sometimes one joint is the whole problem. With only one bad section, we excavate a single small pit and splice in new PVC using shielded couplings. That costs much less. You see the footage that supports the call.

    Emergency Sewer Replacement in Sacramento, CA

    Sewage coming up a floor drain is an emergency. We answer the call, get a camera down the line, and tell you within the first visit whether the pipe has collapsed or just needs to be cleared for now. If it has collapsed, we start permit and locate requests that day. FBL Plumbing can often run a temporary bypass. With a bypass in place, the house can use a toilet and a sink until the new pipe goes in.

    Upgrading Old Sewer Pipe Materials in Sacramento

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all fail in their own ways. Roots get into clay joints, cast iron rusts out along the invert, and Orangeburg fiber pipe squashes flat over the years. Modern plastic pipe avoids all three problems. Fused HDPE has no joints at all, so roots have nowhere to start.
