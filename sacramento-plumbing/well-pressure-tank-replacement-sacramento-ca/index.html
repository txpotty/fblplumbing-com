    Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Sacramento

    Flex-Lite composite pressure tanks are an good option for those seeking a lightweight, corrosion-resistant alternative to traditional steel tanks. They are especially well-suited for homes with aggressive water conditions that might accelerate corrosion in metal tanks. FBL Plumbing carefully replaces old or failing tanks with Flex-Lite models, ensuring a precise fit and proper connection to your existing well system. Their durable, fiberglass-wound construction provides long-lasting, trouble-free operation.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Sacramento, CA

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop, typically at 30/50 or 40/60 psi. Pitted contacts, insect nests and a clogged sensing nipple are common causes of failure in well houses. We replace worn switches with Square D or similar models and clean or replace the nipple below it. A low-pressure cutoff switch can also be added to stop the pump if the well runs dry. We set the switch to suit your new tank's pre-charge.

    Bladder Pressure Tank Replacement in Sacramento

    Bladder tanks hold water inside a flexible butyl bag surrounded by pressurized air. When the bag ruptures, water fills the whole tank, there is no air cushion and the pump short cycles. A simple test is pressing the air valve on top, since water spitting out means the bladder has failed. We replace the tank, set the new pre-charge and verify the pump runs a full cycle. Most bladder tank swaps take about two to three hours.

    Galvanized Steel Pressure Tank Upgrades in Sacramento

    Many older homes in Sacramento still have traditional galvanized steel pressure tanks, which can corrode over time and lose their air charge. We offer professional upgrades from these older tanks to more modern and durable bladder-style tanks. This not only improves water pressure stability but also extends the life of your well pump by reducing short-cycling.

    Diaphragm Pressure Tank Replacement in Sacramento, CA

    Diaphragm tanks like the Amtrol Well-X-Trol use a fixed membrane that separates air above from water below. They are tough, but the steel shell can rust through at the base after years of condensation in a hot, damp equipment space. We replace corroded diaphragm tanks and check the stand and fittings under them. The new tank is leveled and supported so it does not stress the piping. This restores steady pressure to every faucet.

    Amtrol Pressure Tank Replacement in Sacramento

    Amtrol is another leading manufacturer of well pressure tanks, offering a range of models suitable for different well system needs. If your existing Amtrol tank is failing, FBL Plumbing provides precise replacement services in Sacramento. We ensure that the new Amtrol tank is correctly integrated into your plumbing system, calibrated for optimal pressure, and tested for leak-free operation.

    Constant Pressure System Conversion in Sacramento, CA

    A constant pressure system pairs a variable speed drive like the Franklin SubDrive or Grundfos SQE with a smaller pressure tank. The pump speeds up or slows down to hold a set pressure, so showers do not fade when a sprinkler kicks on. These systems cost more, often $2,500 to $5,000, but they eliminate pressure swings. They also cut down on hard starts on the motor. We explain whether your well and pump are good candidates.
