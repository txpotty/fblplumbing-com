    What Our Sacramento, CA Drain Techs Clean

    Bathroom Drain Cleaning in Sacramento, CA

    Hair mixed with soap scum makes a rope that wraps around the stopper linkage. Most bath clogs sit within a foot of the drain. We pull the stopper, clear the trap and run a small cable into the branch line, and if the vent is plugged and the drain still gurgles, we check that too before we pack up.

    Kitchen Drain Cleaning in Sacramento

    Grease is the big one in kitchens. Hot grease cools in the line and hardens on the pipe wall. Bits of food and soap catch on that coating until the 1-1/2 inch line is down to the size of a pencil, and we clear it with a drum machine that is safe for PVC and old cast iron alike.

    Bathroom Sink, Tub and Shower Drains in Sacramento, CA

    Toothpaste and soap scum glue hair into a stringy plug. The pop-up linkage is usually the snag point. We pull the stopper, clean the trap and send a small cable down the branch line toward the stack, and if the stopper keeps catching hair we can adjust it or swap it for one with a simpler design. Usually under an hour.

    Laundry and Floor Drain Cleaning in Sacramento, CA

    Washers push lint and softener residue into the standpipe. Then the standpipe overflows. Our tech cables the standpipe and its branch, and checks the trap and vent while there, and garage floor drains full of grit and gravel get cleared and flushed so they can take a full washer discharge without backing up.

    Clogged Toilet Repair in Sacramento, CA

    Toilets clog at the trapway, a tight bend inside the porcelain. A closet auger reaches it without scratching the bowl. When a cleared toilet still backs up into the shower, the real blockage is farther down the line, and we move to a bigger machine at that point. Kids' toys come out too.

    Commercial Drain Cleaning in Sacramento, CA

    Restaurants, salons and offices load drains harder than homes do. A busy shop puts a year of household waste down a line in a month. We bring heavier sectional machines and jetters that handle 4 and 6 inch lines, and we can schedule work before opening or after close so you do not lose a lunch rush to a plumber in the kitchen.

    Restaurant and Shop Drains in Sacramento, CA

    Commercial kitchens fight fats, oils and grease every day. The grease interceptor helps, but lines still coat. A regular jetting schedule, set by how much frying your kitchen does, keeps the line open, and we leave a service report you can show the health inspector or the landlord.

    Main Line Drain Cleaning in Sacramento

    Toilets bubbling while the washer drains? The main line is the likely spot. A blocked main affects every drain in the house, and the lowest fixture shows it first, and the usual causes are roots, a crushed section of old pipe or a heap of wipes caught on a bad joint, which we find with a camera and clear with a sectional cable or a jetter.
