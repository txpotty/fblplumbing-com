We provide a detailed estimate after our initial assessment.

    Drywall soaked by clean water can sometimes be dried in place if addressed quickly. Drywall that has been wet for a long time or exposed to contaminated water usually must be replaced. We test moisture levels to decide.

    For minor water damage, you might be able to stay in your home. However, for extensive damage, especially involving contaminated water or significant structural drying, it may be safer and more comfortable to temporarily relocate. We will let you know based on your situation.

    While you can handle minor leaks or spills, professional water damage restoration is the safer choice for anything beyond surface moisture. Professionals have professional equipment to confirm complete drying and prevent mold, and they can safely handle contaminated water.

    We use a range of professional-grade drying equipment, including high-capacity air movers, industrial dehumidifiers such as low-grain refrigerant and desiccant models, and professional moisture meters. These tools allow us to quickly and thoroughly dry affected structures and materials, so all hidden moisture is addressed. Brands like Dri-Eaz, Abatement Technologies, and Concrobium are common in our work.

    Mold prevention is a key part of our process. We achieve this through rapid water extraction, thorough drying with air movers and dehumidifiers, and applying antimicrobial treatments to affected areas. Our moisture monitoring confirms all hidden moisture is removed before mold can take hold.
