    Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in Sacramento, CA

    Going from batch-feed to continuous-feed means adding a way to switch it on. That can be a wall switch, an air switch button on the sink deck or a switched outlet under the sink, and the choice depends on how hard it is to run wire to the spot you want. An air button skips the wall wiring. The drain usually stays the same.

    First-Time Disposal Installation in Sacramento

    For kitchens that have never had a disposal, we start by checking the sink flange, drain height and electrical supply.     Same-Day Disposal Installation in Sacramento, CA

    Standard Disposal Installation in Sacramento
