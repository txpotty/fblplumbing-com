    Pilot Light Repair in Redding

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most common gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and service or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are finished in about an hour.

    Water Heater Tank Leaks and Pressure Issues in Redding, CA

    While gas water heaters are robust, tank leaks or pressure problems can occur. We can often fix small fitting leaks or replace relief valves if pressure is too high. If the tank itself is compromised due to rust or old age, a replacement might be the more economical long-term solution. We give you an honest assessment of the repair options for your Redding, CA home.

    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Redding

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust out through PVC pipe. Failures usually involve the blower motor, pressure switch, hot surface igniter or condensate in the vent. We read the error codes, test each part and repair the fault. Correct vent slope and termination are checked before we leave.

    Pilot Light and Ignition System Repair in Redding

    A frequent culprit for no hot water in gas heaters is a faulty pilot light or ignition system. Our technicians are experienced in diagnosing issues like a dirty pilot tube, thermocouple failure or a bad igniter. We carry common replacement parts on our service vehicles to make for a quick fix and restore your hot water flow promptly.

    Burner Assembly and Venting System Repair in Redding

    A malfunctioning burner assembly can result in poor heating or unusual noises. We carefully inspect the burner for blockages, corrosion or wear. We also check the venting system for proper draft and obstructions, which is essential for safe operation and preventing carbon monoxide buildup in your Redding home. Proper venting is a key safety consideration.
