    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Redding, CA

    Braided supply lines last years, but the plastic nut on the toilet end can crack and a bulge in the braid means the inner hose is about to burst. We check every supply line on every visit. Bulges get replaced on the spot. Old vinyl or solid plastic lines get swapped for braided stainless with metal nuts.

    Sensor and Flushometer Repair in Redding

    Sensor flush valves fail in their own ways, from dead batteries and dirty sensor eyes to solenoids that stick open and keep a bowl running. Before replacing parts, we test the sensor and the solenoid. Batteries are the cheap fix. Ghost flushing on a sensor valve usually means the eye needs adjusting or cleaning.

    Older Toilet Brands We Service in Redding, CA

    Older toilets from Eljer, Briggs, Crane and Universal Rundle still turn up in homes built before the 1990s, and their flush valves often take parts nobody stocks anymore. We carry adapters for some of them. For the rest, we will let you know whether parts can still be found or if replacement is the honest answer.

    Leaky Toilet Repair in Redding

    Food coloring in the tank is the cheapest test we run. Should the bowl turn color within fifteen minutes without a flush, water is leaking past the flapper or the flush valve seat, and the toilet is quietly refilling itself all day and night. A new flapper fixes most of these.

    Toilet Leaking Through the Ceiling in Redding, CA

    Water coming through the ceiling under an upstairs bathroom usually traces back to a failed wax ring or a cracked supply nut, and the drywall below holds the evidence. Leave that toilet alone until we look. The toilet comes up, the flange gets checked, and the bowl goes back on a new seal, then we show you how far the water traveled so you can decide about the ceiling.

    Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in Redding

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the china that compresses air with incoming water pressure, and the cartridge inside that vessel eventually wears out. You will not find most of these parts at a hardware store. We carry Flushmate cartridges and dual-flush seals for the common TOTO and American Standard models, and we check house pressure, since these units need steady line pressure to flush properly. Low line pressure gives you a weak, half-hearted flush.

    Clogged Toilet Solutions in Redding, CA

    Paper towels, wipes, dental floss and toys cause most of the repeat clogs we clear, and none of them break down the way toilet paper does. The closet auger usually hooks them and pulls them out. If the toilet clogs again within a week, the problem is further down the line. We check the branch drain and the main next.

    Commercial Toilet Repair in Redding

    Office and retail restrooms take more flushes in a day than a house sees in a month, so the parts wear faster and fail harder. Most commercial toilets use flushometer valves instead of tanks, and those need diaphragm kits and vacuum breakers from time to time. We carry Sloan and Zurn kits. Work can be scheduled before opening or after close.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Repair in Redding

    Expect $120 to $250 for a simple flapper or handle job and $200 to $500 for a base reset or a clog that goes past the trap into the drain line. Flange repairs add to that. We quote a flat price once we see the toilet, before any work begins.

    Flapper and fill valve jobs take twenty to forty-five minutes. Pulling the toilet and resetting it on a new wax ring takes about an hour, and a flange repair adds another thirty to sixty minutes depending on what is under the floor. Deep clogs take longer.

    A failed wax ring is the usual cause, often helped along by a toilet that rocks or a flange that has cracked or rusted through. Condensation can fool you, though. In humid weather, water beads on the tank and drips down to the floor, which looks like a base leak but is not one.

    Most of it comes from a worn flapper or a scaled flush valve seat, and the rest from a fill valve set so high that water spills into the overflow tube. A short chain can also hold the flapper open. With the lid off, you can usually spot it within a minute.

    Repeat clogs usually come from wipes, toys, too much paper per flush, or an early low-flow toilet that never moved waste well. Sometimes the cause is deeper, like a sagging drain, a crushed pipe, roots in the sewer or a blocked vent that keeps the bowl from draining with full force. Cameras settle the question.

    Calcium and magnesium in hard water crust onto the flush valve seat, the fill valve ports and the rim jets under the bowl lip. That leads to slow refills and weak flushes over time. Descaling helps. Parts with fewer small ports also hold up better in hard water.

    Every repair FBL Plumbing does in Redding, CA carries a workmanship guarantee, and the parts carry their own manufacturer coverage on top of that. You will see the warranty terms in writing on the invoice. Hold on to that invoice in case you need it.

    Constant running, a weak flush, water at the base and a bowl that rocks are the big four. A whistle or hiss from the tank usually means the fill valve is wearing out, and a hairline crack in the porcelain should be looked at before it spreads. Do not wait on cracks.
