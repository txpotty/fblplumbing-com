    Pressure Switch and Gauge Integration in Redding, CA

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Switch and tank are a team. A switch set at 40/60 needs a tank pre-charged to 38 psi, and a gauge that reads correctly so you can see what the system is doing, so we replace a worn gauge rather than trusting a sticky needle. Small parts, big role.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Redding

    The tank tee is the brass manifold that connects tank, switch, gauge, drain and relief valve. Threads on old tees weep. The relief valve should open before the system reaches a dangerous pressure, and a corroded valve may not open when needed. Whole set, one visit.

    Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Redding

    Flex-Lite tanks have a composite shell. Rust is not a concern. That makes them a good pick for damp basements, crawlspaces and well houses where steel tanks tend to corrode from the bottom up, plus they are easier to move into cramped utility rooms. Same plumbing as steel.

    Commercial wells often run several tanks in parallel. Multi-tank setups are common. We check each tank's pre-charge separately, since one bad tank in a bank can still cause short cycling, while planning the work so the building keeps water. Larger tanks need two people.
