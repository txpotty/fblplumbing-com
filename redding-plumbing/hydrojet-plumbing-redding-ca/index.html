    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in Redding

    Preventive jetting pays off for lines that clog more than once a year. We set the interval from camera findings. Grease-heavy lines may need six-month service, while root-prone sewers can often go 18 to 24 months.

    Water-Only Drain Cleaning in Redding, CA

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Caustic cleaners can harm old pipe. Jetting removes the buildup physically, and the debris flushes to the sewer or septic tank as normal waste, so there is nothing left in the pipe to eat at joints or harm the bacteria a septic system depends on.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Redding, CA

    Older cast iron drains corrode and build scale on the inner wall, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. Jetting can remove that buildup. Thin or cracked cast iron gets gentler settings or a lining recommendation instead.

    After-Hours Hydrojet Service in Redding

    Sewer Camera Inspection Before Hydrojetting in Redding

    Jetting a broken pipe can make the break worse. The camera always goes in first. Footage shows the material and joint condition, which sets our nozzle and pressure.

    Residential Hydrojetting in Redding
