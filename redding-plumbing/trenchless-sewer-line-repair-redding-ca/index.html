    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Redding, CA

    Shifting soil is one of the main reasons sewer joints separate, crack or drop out of grade over the years. Expansive clay soils are common across Northern California, and seasonal swelling and shrinking can crack slabs and buried sewer laterals. Roots are the other big one, since a hairline gap at a clay joint is enough for a feeder root to get in and then swell into a mat that catches paper. Then age finishes the job. Cast iron scales and thins, and clay bells crack under load.
