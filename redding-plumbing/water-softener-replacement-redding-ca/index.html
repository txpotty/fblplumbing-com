    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Redding

    A standard single tank softener replacement typically costs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Dual tank or premium systems usually run $3,000 to $5,000. Adding a new loop or drain line can add to the cost.

    Requirements vary by city. Simple replacements may not require one, but new plumbing connections may. We check local rules for you.

    Salt-based softeners remove hardness minerals, calcium and magnesium, through ion exchange, effectively softening the water. Salt-free conditioners do not remove minerals but change their crystal structure so they do not stick to surfaces. That prevents scale buildup without actually softening the water.

    Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. Hard water contains dissolved minerals like calcium and magnesium. These minerals can lead to scale buildup in pipes, lower efficiency of water heaters and appliances, spots on dishes, and dry skin and hair. A working water softener is the best defense for combating these issues in Redding homes.

    Many systems include 5 to 10 years on the valve and longer coverage on tanks. We also provide a labor warranty.

    The right size depends on your household's daily water usage and the hardness of your water in grains per gallon. Our technicians will perform a water test and calculate the correct softening capacity in grains per day for your Redding home, so the system keeps up.

    In many areas, a plumbing permit is usually not required for a direct like-for-like water softener replacement if no significant changes to the existing plumbing are made. However, if new water lines or drain lines are being installed or relocated, a permit may be necessary. We will advise you on any permitting requirements.

    Most softener replacements take two to four hours. New loops or drain lines can take longer.
