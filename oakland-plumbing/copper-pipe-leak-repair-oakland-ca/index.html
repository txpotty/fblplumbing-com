    Solder Joint Leak Repair in Oakland, CA

    Older solder joints can fail from poor original work, thermal expansion or vibration. Crusty fittings are a clue.     Corrosion-Related Leak Repair in Oakland

    Corrosion thins copper from the inside. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. It shows up as green crust. On exposed pipe you may see blue-green deposits at fittings, and inside the wall the first sign is usually a pinhole or a weeping joint that stains the drywall below it. We replace the corroded length. Then we talk about the cause.

    Preventive Copper Pipe Inspection in Oakland, CA

    An inspection catches thin spots before they leak. It takes about an hour.     Hot Water Copper Line Repair in Oakland, CA

    Hot water lines corrode faster than cold lines. Temperature drives it. Recirculation pumps that run around the clock push water through the copper at high velocity and wear it from the inside, which is why we often add a timer to the pump after fixing the leak. It slows the next pinhole.

    Burst Copper Pipe Repair in Oakland, CA

    Copper in an exterior wall or attic can split when the water inside freezes. Freezing is uncommon along the coast and on valley floors in Northern California, but frosty nights do happen and can crack unprotected outdoor pipes. Close the main valve. The split often does not leak until the ice thaws, so a pipe that froze overnight may start spraying the next afternoon once the house warms up. We cut out the split and solder in new pipe. Then we add insulation.
