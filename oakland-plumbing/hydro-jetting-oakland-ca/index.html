    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Oakland

    Residential hydro jetting in Oakland typically costs $350 to $800, depending on line length, access and how heavy the buildup is. Commercial jetting is often priced by the hour and may run higher.

    While hydro jetting is very powerful, it is generally safe for most sound plumbing systems, including PVC, cast iron, and concrete pipes. However, it is not recommended for severely damaged, corroded, or fragile pipes, which is why a pre-jetting camera inspection by FBL Plumbing is crucial to assess pipe integrity and prevent further damage.

    A standard residential hydro jetting service in Oakland typically takes between 1 and 3 hours. This includes the initial camera inspection, the jetting process itself, and a final inspection. More extensive commercial jobs or severe main line blockages might need more time.

    Snaking uses a rotating cable to break through a clog. Jetting uses pressurized water to clean the entire pipe wall and flush debris away.

    Generally, a permit is not required for standard hydro jetting aimed at clearing blockages in existing lines. However, if the hydro jetting is part of a larger repair or replacement project that alters the plumbing system, a permit from the local building department may be necessary. We will tell you if a permit is needed for your situation.

    Hydro jetting is very effective against almost all types of clogs. It can break through solid obstructions like tree roots, flush out heavy grease and food buildup, clear mineral scale, and remove compacted sludge and debris that traditional snaking often misses.

    Drain snaking uses a cable with an auger that punches a hole through a blockage to restore some flow. Hydro jetting, however, uses high-pressure water to completely scour the inside of the pipe, removing all buildup and restoring the pipe to a near-new condition. Hydro jetting offers a more thorough and longer-lasting clean.

    Key signs you might need hydro jetting include regularly recurring clogs in the same drain, multiple slow drains throughout your home, persistent foul odors coming from drains, or gurgling sounds from toilets or drains. These often point to a significant buildup or blockage that needs more than just a snake.
