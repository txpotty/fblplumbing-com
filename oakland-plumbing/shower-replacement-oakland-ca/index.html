    Replacing Showers Damaged by Hard Water in Oakland, CA

    Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running mineral-heavy. Scale shows up first as a weak showerhead and a stiff handle. Over enough years, it can lock a cartridge in place, pit a valve body and etch glass doors past the point where cleaning helps, and that is when replacement makes sense.
