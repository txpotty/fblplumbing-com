    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Oakland, CA

    Calcium scale is what kills most tankless units early. Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running hard. Scale coats the heat exchanger, cuts efficiency and triggers error codes, and left alone it can crack the exchanger and end the warranty claim before it starts. A yearly flush prevents it. Service valves make it easy.
