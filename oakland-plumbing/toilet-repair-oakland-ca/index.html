    Leaking Toilet Repair in Oakland

    Water on the floor can come from four places: the wax ring, the tank-to-bowl gasket, the supply line or sweat running off a cold tank. Each one needs a different fix. We dry everything and watch where the water shows up first, which takes about ten minutes and saves pulling a toilet that did not need it. If the porcelain itself is cracked, we price a new bowl rather than trying to seal it.

    Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in Oakland

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the china that compresses air with incoming water pressure, and the cartridge inside that vessel eventually wears out. Hardware stores rarely stock those parts. We carry Flushmate cartridges and dual-flush seals for the common TOTO and American Standard models, and we check house pressure, since these units need steady line pressure to flush properly. Low line pressure gives you a weak, half-hearted flush.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Repair in Oakland

    Restaurant restrooms get the hardest use we see, and wall-hung bowls there can loosen on their carriers until the seal leaks behind the wall. The fix is to tighten or rebuild the carrier, then reseal the bowl. That one needs care. A loose wall-hung bowl can crack the china if it is ignored.

    Commercial Toilet Repair in Oakland

    Office and retail restrooms take more flushes in a day than a house sees in a month, so the parts wear faster and fail harder. Commercial bowls usually run on flushometer valves rather than tanks, and those need diaphragm kits and vacuum breakers from time to time. We carry Sloan and Zurn kits. If downtime is a problem, we can come in before you open or after you lock up.

    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Oakland, CA

    Braided supply lines last years, but the plastic nut on the toilet end can crack and a bulge in the braid means the inner hose is about to burst. We check every supply line on every visit. Bulges get replaced on the spot. If you still have a vinyl or solid plastic line, we change it to braided stainless before it lets go.

    Sensor and Flushometer Repair in Oakland

    Sensor flush valves fail in their own ways, from dead batteries and dirty sensor eyes to solenoids that stick open and keep a bowl running. We test the sensor before we replace anything. Batteries are the cheap fix. If the valve flushes on its own, the sensor range or a dirty lens is usually to blame.
