    We recommend having your sewage ejector pump inspected annually, especially if it is in a high-use area. Regular inspections can identify potential issues early, extend the life of your pump and prevent unexpected failures and costly backups. This is especially important for older units.

    Yes, in most cases a plumbing permit from the City of Oakland is required for sewage ejector pump installation. This ensures the work complies with local building and plumbing codes. FBL Plumbing will help you with securing the necessary permits and make sure all work passes inspection.

    A standard sewage ejector pump installation usually takes one to two days.     A well-maintained sewage ejector pump typically has a lifespan of 7 to 10 years, though some higher-quality units can last longer.
