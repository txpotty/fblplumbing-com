    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Oakland

    Tree roots, corrosion and soil movement wreck most sewer lines. Many older homes here sit on raised foundations with crawlspaces, which make supply and drain repairs easier to reach than slab work. Small cracks get a sectional liner. Longer damage gets a full-length liner, and a fully collapsed line gets burst and replaced, all without trenching across the yard or cutting the driveway at your home.

    Lateral and Spot Lining in Oakland

    Not every pipe needs lining end to end. A few bad joints need a short fix. A sectional liner is cheaper and quicker for a short stretch of damage, though we camera the rest of the line first to be sure it is sound.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Systems in Oakland

    Perma-Liner is one of the CIPP systems our crews install. It's built for sewer use. The liners and resins are structural, meaning the cured pipe can stand on its own even if the old pipe around it keeps deteriorating, which is why we trust them for local lines. They hold up in tough conditions.

    Sewer Lateral Lining in Oakland

    Laterals are the most common candidates for trenchless repair. Clay and Orangeburg fail often. Over time, clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe crack, sag or fill with roots, so we jet, scope and then pick lining or bursting. The new line restores full flow and resists roots.

    Perma-Liner and Picote Solutions in Oakland

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Prep is half the job. Picote milling heads clear roots, scale and old fittings out of the pipe before lining, and after the liner cures, they cut open the lateral connections so every drain in your home flows again. A clean pipe holds a liner best.

    Pipe Bursting in Oakland, CA

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. It needs two pits. A bursting head is pulled through, fracturing the old pipe outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind, and that HDPE is fused into one continuous length with no joints at all.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Oakland

    The old pipe gets cleaned first. Then the liner goes in. We inflate an epoxy-soaked liner against the wall and cure it with heat or UV, leaving a smooth pipe with no joints. Lining works best for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Commercial properties have sewer and storm lines under parking lots, sidewalks and buildings. Tearing it out costs plenty. We skip the demolition and the tenant disruption, lining larger pipe and multiple laterals on a schedule that fits the business.
