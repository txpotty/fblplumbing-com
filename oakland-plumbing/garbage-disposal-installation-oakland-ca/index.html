    We help you pick between the Badger and Evolution lines based on how you cook, how much noise bothers you and how much cabinet room you have.     Dishwasher Connection With Disposal Installation in Oakland

    New disposals come with a knockout plug in the dishwasher inlet. We knock it out with a screwdriver and hammer, fish the plug out of the chamber and connect the hose with a new clamp.
