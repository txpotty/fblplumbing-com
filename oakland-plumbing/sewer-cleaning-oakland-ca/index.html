    Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Oakland, CA

    Grease bonds with mineral scale into a hard crust. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe.     Dealing with Grease Buildup in Oakland, CA

    Grease hardens as it cools. Then it narrows the pipe. A greasy main slowly loses diameter until it cannot carry a full washer load or a bathtub draining at once, and jetting is the right fix.
