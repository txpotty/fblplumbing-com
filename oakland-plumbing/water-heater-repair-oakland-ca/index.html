    Tankless Descaling in Oakland

    Navien, Rinnai, Noritz and Rheem tankless units show error codes on the front panel that help us narrow the problem quickly. Scale, a dirty inlet screen, a bad flow sensor or a weak igniter cause most failures. Descaling uses a small pump and approved solution.     Tankless Water Heater Repair in Oakland

    Scale is the main enemy of a tankless heater. Mineral buildup on the heat exchanger triggers overheating codes and shutdowns. We descale with a pump and approved solution, clean the inlet filter and check the igniter and flow sensor while the unit is open. All major brands.

    Electric Water Heater Element Repair in Oakland

    Lukewarm water or hot water that runs out in five minutes usually means a burned-out element or a stuck thermostat. We check each element with an ohmmeter. Readings tell us whether an element is open or shorted, which explains the breaker trips. Common elements and thermostats are stocked.

    Electric Tank Repair in Oakland, CA

    Four parts can fail on an electric tank, two elements and two thermostats. Hard water piles scale on the lower element until it burns out. After testing resistance and voltage, we replace what failed, reset the high limit and watch it heat up. A breaker that keeps tripping gets traced first.
