    Service Line Replacement Options in Oakland, CA

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Oakland, CA

    Bursting fits old cast iron or galvanized lines that will shatter under the head. Pulling suits softer pipe like poly or copper. Either way the new pipe follows the old path, which matters when the route runs under a patio or a neighbor's fence, and the yard work shrinks to two pits. Rock can stop it.

    PEX Service Lines for Long Runs in Oakland

    PEX comes in coils up to several hundred feet. That makes it the easy pick when the meter sits out at the road and the house is set far back, because one coil covers the whole run without a joint. Fewer joints underground means fewer places a leak can start, and it is the joints that usually fail first.

    Commercial Water Line Services in Oakland, CA

    Commercial services run bigger, often 1-1/2 to 2 inches, and almost always have a backflow assembly that needs testing after any work. We schedule the shutdown around your hours. For a restaurant or an office building that might mean an early morning start, temporary water to the restrooms, and a call to the utility about their side of the meter. Tenants get notice.

    Emergency Water Line Replacement in Oakland

    Water bubbling up through the lawn or the street side of the sidewalk means a break, not a drip. First step, shut off the main valve. If that does not stop it, the break is on your side of the meter but before the valve, and we will close the curb stop with a meter key and plan the replacement from there, usually starting the same or next day.

    Damage Assessment Before Replacement in Oakland

    We start at the meter. With every tap closed, the leak indicator should sit still. From there we listen along the line with an acoustic ground mic, check the house side for a toilet or softener running, and sometimes push a camera into the line when we suspect root damage or a crushed section, so the hole goes in the right spot.

    Trenchless Bores Under Driveways in Oakland

    Directional boring drills a path under the obstacle and pulls the pipe back through it. No old line is needed to follow. We use it when the new line has to take a different route, say around a new addition or under a driveway poured after the house was built, and the launch and exit pits stay small. Heavy rock is the exception.

    Water Line Materials We Install in Oakland, CA

    Our trucks carry Type K copper, PEX-a and HDPE. Each one fits a different job. Copper suits short runs and high pressure, PEX handles long runs with bends, and HDPE is the usual pick for trenchless pulls and bores, where we need a tough, fused pipe that can be dragged a long way. All of it is listed for drinking water, carrying the NSF 61 listing.

    Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Oakland

    One leak on a twenty-year-old copper line? We will usually just fix that spot. Three leaks on a galvanized line from the 1950s is different, because the pipe is thin everywhere and the next leak is already forming, so replacing the whole run is cheaper over five years. We tell you which camp you are in and show you the pipe we cut out.
