    Leaky Pipe Repair in Oakland, CA

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Oakland

    Older homes were plumbed with galvanized steel, which rusts from the inside out. Flow drops slowly. Every time a fitting is disturbed, the next one tends to leak, which makes full replacement of the affected run the smarter spend. Transitions get dielectric fittings.

    Frozen and Burst Line Repair in Oakland

    Pipes in exterior walls, attics and crawlspaces freeze first. Frozen water cracks it. The split usually does not leak until the ice thaws, so a pipe that froze overnight may start spraying the next afternoon once the house warms up. We repair the split and add insulation. Heat tape is an option on problem runs.

    Underground Pipe Repair in Oakland

    Buried pipe takes stress from settling soil, roots and vehicles. You rarely see them. We locate underground leaks with acoustic equipment and tracer gas, and either spot repair at the leak or replace the line by pulling new pipe through. Every repair gets a pressure test.

    Business Pipe Repair in Oakland, CA

    Pipe trouble in a commercial building can close a business for the day. We keep you open. Our plumbers handle restroom groups, kitchen supply lines, mechanical room piping and larger diameter mains, scheduling the repair overnight or early morning when needed. Documentation is available.

    PEX Pipe Repair and Installation in Oakland

    PEX is flexible and handles freezing better than rigid pipe. It still fails. Most PEX leaks happen at a crimp ring that was not fully seated, an early brass fitting that dezincified or a section damaged by sunlight in an attic, and we cut out the bad fitting and replace it with a crimp, clamp or expansion connection rated for the pipe. Usually a quick fix.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Oakland

    Galvanized steel and old copper both corrode with age. Brown water is a clue. Low pressure, discolored water and leaks at threaded joints tell us the pipe is closing off from the inside, so we swap the failing pipe for PEX or copper with proper fittings between metals. Pressure comes right back.
