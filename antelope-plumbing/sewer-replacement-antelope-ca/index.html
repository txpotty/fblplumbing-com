    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Antelope

    Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE pipe through the old line while breaking the old pipe apart. It requires only small access pits at each end. This protects landscaping, driveways and patios. It works well for clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe. Most jobs are completed in one day.

    Emergency Sewer Replacement in Antelope, CA

    A collapsed sewer line can cause sewage backups into your home. Call (669) 319-2034 for emergency service. We inspect, locate the break and plan repairs quickly. Temporary measures may restore flow until replacement. Quick response limits damage.

    Sewer Pipe Material Upgrades in Antelope, CA

    Many older homes in Antelope, CA still have original sewer lines made of clay, cast iron, or Orangeburg pipe, which are prone to degradation, cracking, and root intrusion over time. When we perform a sewer replacement, we often suggest upgrading to modern, durable materials like PVC (polyvinyl chloride) or ABS (acrylonitrile butadiene styrene). These materials are more flexible, resistant to corrosion and roots, and have a much longer lifespan, providing a major upgrade to your home's plumbing infrastructure.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Antelope

    For severely damaged or collapsed sewer lines, traditional trenching is often the most reliable solution. This involves excavating a trench along the sewer line's path to allow for direct access and replacement of the entire pipe. While more invasive, it provides a complete overhaul and is often required for older homes with clay or cast iron pipes that have reached the end of their lifespan. We ensure thorough planning and execution to minimize disruption to your yard.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Antelope, CA

    Some lines have severe sagging, wrong slope or are too shallow for trenchless methods. In these cases, open-trench replacement is the right option. We excavate, remove the old pipe and install new PVC with proper bedding. We restore the trench after inspection. This method allows full correction of slope issues.
