    Scheduled Jetting for Repeat Clogs in Antelope, CA

    Recurring clogs follow a pattern. A schedule breaks it. Jetting once or twice a year keeps mineral deposits, grease and sludge from building up again, which avoids emergency blockages and extends the life of the plumbing system on properties with a history of trouble.

    Hydro Jetting Cost Compared to Snaking in Antelope, CA

    Jetting costs more on the first visit. It usually lasts longer. Several cable visits a year can easily cost more than one jetting job that keeps the line clear for a year or more, though a simple soft clog may only need a cable.

    Preventive Maintenance Jetting in Antelope

    Repeat clogs suggest a maintenance plan. It keeps the line clear. Homes usually need jetting every year or two, while busy kitchens may need it quarterly to stay ahead of grease, and each visit includes a camera check to catch new problems early.

    Hydro Jetting vs. Snaking Cost Comparison in Antelope, CA

    Snaking costs less up front. Jetting usually holds up better. A cable punches through the blockage and leaves residue on the walls, which brings the clog back, while jetting cleans the whole pipe and cuts down the number of service calls over time.

    High-Pressure Water Jetting for Drain Cleaning in Antelope

    Our jetters run up to 4,000 PSI. It is enough to split hardened grease. Water at that pressure scours the pipe wall clean without chemicals, and it works on PVC, cast iron and clay when the pressure is set for the material and the condition of the pipe.

    Camera Inspection and Hydro Jetting in Antelope, CA

    The camera goes in before the jetter. We do it every time. An inspection locates the blockage, shows what it is made of and tells us the condition of the pipe, so we can confirm jetting is right and choose the correct pressure and nozzle.
