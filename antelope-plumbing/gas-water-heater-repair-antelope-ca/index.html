    All Major Gas Water Heater Brands Repaired in Antelope

    Every brand label we see in garages and closets uses one of a handful of gas control designs. Rheem and Ruud share most parts, and State shares a lot with A.O. Smith.     Pilot Light and Ignition Repair in Antelope

    No hot water and a dark pilot window usually point to the ignition side of the heater. The tech pulls the pilot assembly and checks the thermocouple tip, the orifice and the igniter wire. A thermocouple that tests weak on the millivolt meter will not hold the gas valve's magnet open, so the pilot drops out a minute after you relight it by the label. Most trucks carry the common pilot kits.

    Repairing Older Gas Water Heaters in Antelope

    Older tanks with standing pilots and no vapor sensor are still out there in Antelope, mostly in basements and utility closets. Their parts are simple, and a universal thermocouple fits nearly all of them. We can keep a pre-2003 heater running when the tank is sound, but we also check the draft hood, the flue connector and the gas cock, since those fittings on an old install are often original and corroded. Some old setups lack a sediment trap. We add one during the repair.

    Pilot Light Repair in Antelope

    The thermocouple is the first thing we test when a pilot will not stay lit. A millivolt meter tells us in about a minute whether it is producing enough current. If it reads fine, the next suspects are a clogged pilot orifice, a cracked piezo wire or a thermal cutoff that tripped because the combustion chamber overheated from blocked air. Figure on roughly an hour for most pilot repairs.

    Gas Water Heater Repair Cost in Antelope, CA

    Pilot Light & Burner Issues in Antelope

    A pilot that lights, holds for ten seconds and then goes out is almost always a thermocouple or a weak magnet in the gas valve. No flame at all usually means a plugged pilot orifice or a bad piezo. Main burner problems show up another way. The pilot stays lit but the main flame never comes on, which points to the thermostat side of the valve, a tripped thermal cutoff or a flame arrestor so clogged with lint that the burner starves for air. We test each piece in that order.

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Antelope

    Since about 2003, most residential gas tanks have been built with a flammable vapor ignition resistant combustion chamber. Air enters through a flame arrestor screen at the base that also keeps flame inside. When lint, dust or pet hair coats that screen, the burner starves, the chamber overheats and a thermal switch shuts the whole heater down, sometimes several times a week. We clean the screen and the chamber. A tripped thermal switch gets replaced.

    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Antelope, CA

    Yellow flame tips, soot on the burner door or a roaring sound at startup all mean the burner is not getting clean air. The burner comes out, the ports get brushed and the orifice gets cleared. We also confirm the orifice is drilled for the fuel you have, because a natural gas orifice on a propane heater, or the reverse, will soot up the flue and push carbon monoxide levels well past normal. You want a steady blue flame. Rust flakes on the burner send us to check the flue baffle next.
