    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Antelope, CA

    Tankless water heaters need specialized maintenance, mainly a descaling flush to remove mineral deposits that can restrict flow and reduce efficiency. Our technicians in Antelope, CA use commercial-grade solutions to clean the heat exchanger, so your unit keeps providing on-demand hot water efficiently.     Tank Water Heater Flushing in Antelope

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium sediment at the bottom of the tank. This layer insulates the water from the burner or lower element, wasting energy and causing popping sounds. We connect a hose to the drain valve and flush the tank until the water runs clear. For heavy buildup, we may stir and flush in several cycles. A clean tank heats faster and runs quieter.

    Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in Antelope

    Gas water heaters need a clean burner and a proper vent to run safely and efficiently. We inspect the burner flame, clean the combustion chamber and check the thermocouple or flame sensor. The vent is checked for proper draft and secure connections. Blocked or loose vents can let carbon monoxide into your home. We also check the gas line and shutoff valve for any leaks.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Antelope

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part in your water heater designed to corrode instead of the tank itself. Depending on your water chemistry, these rods can wear down faster than expected. During maintenance, we inspect the anode rod's condition and recommend replacement if necessary. A healthy anode rod is crucial for preventing rust and extending the lifespan of your water heater tank, saving you from a costly full replacement.

    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Antelope
