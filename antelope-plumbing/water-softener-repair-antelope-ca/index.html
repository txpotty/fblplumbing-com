    Resin Testing and Replacement in Antelope

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Treated city water slowly breaks resin down into mush. We test resin capacity, then clean it with resin cleaner or replace it, and a solid tank and valve with fresh resin works like new for years. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Control Valve Troubleshooting & Repair in Antelope, CA

    Stuck pistons, worn seals and failed drive motors cause most control valve trouble, and the symptoms are hard water, constant regeneration or a leak at the head. We diagnose the valve on site. Our plumbers carry seal kits and pistons for Fleck, Clack and Autotrol heads, which covers a big share of the softeners installed in homes around here.

    Water Softener Repair vs. Replacement in Antelope, CA

    Age and parts availability decide whether a softener is worth repairing. FBL Plumbing gives honest advice based on both. A ten-year-old unit with a sound tank and a rebuildable valve is usually worth fixing, while an obsolete head with no parts available is a different conversation. We lay out both options.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Antelope, CA

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. If scale comes back or pressure drops, the media or the prefilter is usually the problem. FBL Plumbing repairs these units in Antelope, CA. Media lasts a few years.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Antelope

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. An old timer head regenerates by the calendar, used or not. After retesting hardness, we reprogram the valve or swap in a metered head that regenerates only when needed. Salt bills drop.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Antelope
