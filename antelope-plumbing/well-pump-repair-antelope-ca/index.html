    Well Pump Problems We Fix in Antelope, CA

    Well Pump Diagnostic Services in Antelope, CA

    Voltage, amperage, pressure and flow rate tell us most of what is happening down the well without pulling a single length of pipe. A weak motor draws high amps. A clogged intake or a dropping water level shows up as low flow with normal amps, and that changes the repair entirely. Testing first saves a pull.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Antelope

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, a clogged sensing tube and ant nests are the usual reasons one fails. After swapping the switch and clearing the tube, we set cut-in and cut-out to match the tank, usually 40 and 60 psi. This is among the least expensive well repairs.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Antelope, CA

    Capacitors, relays and corroded terminals inside the control box cause many pump failures. Breaker trips usually come first. Licensed technicians test these parts safely with the power locked out, replace what failed and tighten every connection in the box. Wiring meets Northern California code when the job is done.

    Grundfos Well Pump Repair in Antelope

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have built-in electronics that protect the motor and report faults. Sensor failures and drive errors are the usual problems. FBL Plumbing reads the fault code, checks the wiring and tests the pressure sensor before replacing anything in a Antelope, CA well. Electronics fail less often than people think.

    Low Water Pressure Diagnosis in Antelope

    Weak pressure on a well is not always the pump. A clogged sediment filter, a pressure switch set too low or a leak in the yard line can all cause it. In some cases the pump is fine and the well just cannot keep up with demand. We test each one.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Antelope, CA

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Air leaks on the suction side cause most failures. FBL Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and confirms it builds pressure. Nonstop running without pressure usually means lost prime or an air leak.

    Control Box and Surge Repair in Antelope, CA

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. Strong winter storms can bring days of heavy rain to Northern California, saturating ground and pushing water into basements and sewer lines. Surges from lightning are the usual cause of control box failures. After testing the capacitor, relay and overload, we replace the bad part and suggest a surge arrestor if the box lacks one. An arrestor costs little.

    Well Pump Repair vs Replacement Cost in Antelope, CA

    Switch and control repairs often cost $150 to $500, while a pressure tank replacement typically runs $600 to $1,500. Pulling and replacing a submersible pump can range from about $1,800 to $5,000 or more, depending on depth, horsepower and wire size. An older pump that already needs pulling is often worth replacing on the same trip. We lay out both options.
