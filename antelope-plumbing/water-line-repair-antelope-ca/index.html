    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Antelope, CA

    A single leak on a newer copper or PEX line is usually a quick spot repair. Repeat leaks, galvanized pipe or a line older than about 40 years usually justify full replacement. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars, while a full replacement costs more but resets the clock for decades.     Water Line Repair at the Meter in Antelope, CA

    Leaks often start at the compression fittings and couplings right next to the meter box. Shifting soil and heavy traffic over the box can loosen those joints over time.     Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Antelope

    Slab leaks, where water lines running beneath your home's concrete foundation leak, are a especially challenging issue. Older neighborhoods in Northern California include prewar and postwar homes with galvanized supply lines and cast iron or clay sewer pipe that are past the end of their life. We use specialized listening devices and camera inspections to pinpoint slab leaks precisely and offer repair options including direct slab penetration, re-routing, or pipe lining.     Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Antelope, CA

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Rural properties around Antelope often rely on private wells, which may need treatment for iron, sediment, or other minerals. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which chokes flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe rarely lasts because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and stops repeat leaks.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Antelope

We dig small pits at each end and pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through using a pneumatic boring tool or pipe pulling equipment. The process typically takes one day. You keep your pavers and trees, and the new line carries a long manufacturer warranty.
