    Not every well failure means a bad pump. Failed capacitors, relays or pressure switches can stop the system. We test these components first and replace them if needed. This can save you the cost of pulling the pump. We explain what we find before making recommendations.

    Drop Pipe and Wire Replacement in Antelope

    Old galvanized drop pipe can corrode and leak, causing pressure loss. Damaged wire can cause motor failure. When we pull the pump, we inspect both. We replace them with long-lasting materials like schedule 120 PVC or poly pipe as appropriate. This helps the new pump last for years.
