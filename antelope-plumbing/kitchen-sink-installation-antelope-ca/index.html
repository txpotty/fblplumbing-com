    Disposal and Dishwasher Hookup with Sink Installation in Antelope, CA

    Most new kitchen sinks get paired with a disposal and a dishwasher. We mount the disposal, run the dishwasher drain through an air gap or high loop and install a dedicated hot supply with its own shutoff. One visit costs less than three. We test it all the way you will use it.

    Sink and Disposal Installed Together in Antelope, CA

    Adding a disposal costs least when the sink is already out. The disposal mounts to the new flange, the trap is set to its outlet height and the dishwasher plug gets knocked out for the hose. You also need power. We check for a switched outlet in the cabinet.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Antelope

    An undermount depends on its clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. The bowl is mounted, the rim sealed with silicone and the sink braced while the adhesive sets, normally overnight before it can take a full bowl of water. Strainers and traps go in after.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Antelope, CA

    Every fabricator handles undermount clips a bit differently. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. The bowl is braced regardless. The trap does not get connected until the adhesive cures.

    Farmhouse Sink Plumbing in Antelope, CA

    Fireclay and cast iron farmhouse sinks can weigh well over 100 pounds empty, before a single drop of water goes in. We build a support frame inside the cabinet and set the sink level before the counter is installed. A deep bowl means a lower trap. The drain gets planned for that depth from day one.

    A sink with a crack through the bowl or a strainer that has rusted out cannot be patched for long. We stock the most common double-bowl stainless sizes, which makes same-day replacement common, and we replace the strainers and trap at the same time. We swap the old stops as well.
