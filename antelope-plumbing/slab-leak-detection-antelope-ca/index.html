    Frequently Asked Questions About Slab Leak Detection in Antelope

    The cost for professional slab leak detection in Antelope, CA usually ranges from $250 to $700, depending on the complexity of the leak and the time required for diagnosis. This initial detection fee is separate from any repair costs, which can vary a lot based on the leak's location and required pipe repair.

    While the detection process itself typically doesn't require a permit, any subsequent repair work involving the excavation of the slab or significant plumbing alterations will likely require a plumbing permit from the local building department. FBL Plumbing takes care of all necessary permits for repair work.

    Many policies cover the cost to access the leak and repair resulting water damage, though the pipe itself may not be covered. Coverage varies by insurer. We provide photos and a written report for your claim.

    Slab leaks can cause significant damage including foundation cracks, soil erosion beneath the slab, mold and mildew growth in wall cavities or under flooring, damage to flooring materials and increased humidity levels. Over time, it can lead to structural instability and extensive, costly repairs.

    Many homeowner insurance policies cover the cost of water damage from a sudden or accidental slab leak, and sometimes the cost of accessing the leak for repair. However, coverage varies a lot, so it's essential to check your specific policy with your insurance provider.

    A water line slab leak involves pressurized fresh water escaping from supply pipes, often resulting in higher water bills, warm spots or the sound of running water. A sewer line slab leak involves wastewater leaking from drain pipes, which usually causes foul odors, slow drains or visible sewage seepage, and is not usually pressurized.

    Once the slab leak is accurately detected, our technician will provide you with a detailed report and discuss the best repair options. These options may include trenchless repair, re-routing the affected pipe or traditional slab excavation and repair. We will then provide a clear estimate for the repair work.

    Direct repair works well for a single leak on otherwise healthy pipe in an accessible spot. Rerouting is often better under tile or hardwood and avoids future leaks on that line. We help you compare both.
