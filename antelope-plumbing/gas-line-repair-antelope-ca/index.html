    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Antelope

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. With every appliance off, a moving test dial on the meter tells us gas is escaping somewhere, and then we isolate branches to narrow it down. A combustible gas detector narrows it further. Soap solution at the suspect fitting confirms it with bubbles, and then we repair the joint, replace the section or swap the connector before testing again.

    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Antelope

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. We back out the leaking joint, clean the threads, apply gas-rated thread sealant and reassemble it, or replace the fitting or valve if the threads are damaged. We soap-test the repaired joint and pressure test the system before leaving.

    Cost of Gas Line Repair in Antelope, CA

    Gas line repair prices in Antelope, CA depend on where the leak is and how much pipe has to change. Tightening or replacing a single fitting or connector is usually a few hundred dollars.     Propane Gas Line Service in Antelope, CA

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. Our work covers propane yard lines, regulator replacement and fitting repairs, plus a check that every appliance has its propane orifices installed. Wrong orifices make a burner run dirty and unsafe. We test the system the same way we test natural gas piping.

    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Antelope, CA

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Vibration and settling loosen threaded joints over time, and pipe in damp garages or outside walls can rust through from the surface. Bad sections get replaced with new pipe. Rusted pipe that runs outside gets painted or replaced with coated steel so it does not come back as the same problem.
