    Sectional Point Repair in Antelope

    Older Antelope homes often have clay or cast iron sewer pipe. They wear out. Roots get into clay joints and cast iron rusts from inside, and a liner seals over both with jointless pipe that should outlast the original. Only one or two access points are needed.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Antelope

    Tree roots, corrosion and soil movement wreck most sewer lines.     Pipe Bursting in Antelope, CA

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. You'll see an entry and exit pit. The head fractures the old pipe as it travels, with new HDPE trailing behind, fused into one length with no joints.
