    Most sump pumps last 7 to 10 years. Pumps that sit idle for long periods may fail sooner, so periodic testing is important.

    A submersible sump pump sits entirely inside the sump pit, submerged in water, making it quieter and taking up less space. A pedestal sump pump has its motor mounted on a shaft above the pit, making it easier to access for maintenance and typically more affordable. Both types are effective, and our plumbers can help you choose the best fit for your Antelope basement.

    Strange noises, constant running, failure to turn on and visible rust are common signs. Testing your pump before the rainy season helps catch problems early.
