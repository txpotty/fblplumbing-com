    Storm and Yard Drain Jetting in Antelope, CA

    Yard drains, channel drains and downspout lines fill with sand, silt and leaves between storms. Strong winter storms can bring days of heavy rain to Northern California, saturating ground and pushing water into basements and sewer lines.     Chemical-Free Drain Cleaning in Antelope, CA

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Caustic cleaners can harm old pipe. Jetting removes the buildup physically, and the debris flushes to the sewer or septic tank as normal waste, so there is nothing left in the pipe to eat at joints or harm the bacteria a septic system depends on.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydrojetting in Antelope

    Roots find their way into sewer lines through cracked joints, and older clay pipe has a joint every few feet. We use a spinning root-cutter nozzle. The jet then flushes the debris to the main, and the camera marks the joints that let the roots in.

    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Antelope

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. Jetting the main clears roots, grease and sludge all the way to the city connection. On a 4-inch line we use a heavier nozzle with more flow, which moves the loosened debris downstream instead of leaving it to settle in a low spot.

    Residential Hydrojetting in Antelope

    Most home jetting calls involve a greasy kitchen line or a root-filled sewer. We set up outside at the cleanout whenever we can, so the jetter hose never has to come through your house or across your floors. Some kitchen lines get jetted from under the sink. We lay down floor protection first.

    Whole-House Drain Jetting in Antelope

    Slow drains in several rooms usually mean buildup in a main or branch line. We jet the main from the cleanout and the branches from roof vents or from fixture access points as needed for that layout. Most homes take two to three hours.
