    Type L Copper Repiping in Chico

    Copper has been plumbing homes for generations, and done right it lasts a long time. Our copper repipes use Type L with proper hangers and isolators so the pipe does not wear against framing. All joints use lead-free solder. Copper can pinhole in aggressive water, so we ask about your water and any past leaks before recommending it over PEX.

    Slab Leak Repiping in Chico

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Many older homes here sit on raised foundations with crawlspaces, which make supply and drain repairs easier to reach than slab work. Instead of breaking concrete to patch one pipe, we reroute new supply lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, then abandon the old lines in the slab. No jackhammer needed. That stops the current leak and removes the chance of the next one under the same floor.

    Drain Line Repiping in Chico

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Slow drains in several fixtures, sewer odors and repeated clogs in the same line are common signs of a failing drain system. We camera the lines first. New PVC or ABS, sloped and vented properly, replaces the bad sections and drains better than the old pipe.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Chico

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. It holds up well, does not mind sunlight in an exposed run and has a long track record behind it. It costs more. Our plumbers solder joints with lead-free solder or use press fittings, support the pipe at the spacing the code calls for and pressure test the system before any wall is closed.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Chico, CA

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Because PEX flexes, it absorbs some of the shock that causes water hammer, and long runs with few fittings mean fewer places for a leak to start inside a wall. We keep PEX out of sunlight and away from water heater flues.

    Copper Repiping in Chico, CA

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. Our copper work uses thicker Type L pipe, joined with lead-free solder or ProPress fittings, rather than the Type M found in many older houses. Expect a slightly longer job. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a day longer than PEX, mostly because every turn needs a fitting.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Chico

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. That is often the first sign. Replacing galvanized with PEX or copper ends the rust, brings pressure back and stops the leaks that start at the threaded joints where galvanized is thinnest. We disconnect and abandon the old pipe where removing it would mean tearing out finished walls.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Chico, CA

    Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Weak flow and rusty water are common. We remove or abandon the galvanized lines and replace them with PEX or copper, and your water pressure and water quality usually improve the same day the new lines go into service.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Repipe Specialists in Chico

    Most PEX repipes for Northern California homes cost $5,000 to $12,000, depending on size, bathrooms and access. Copper repipes usually run 20 to 40 percent more.

    Most families stay home the whole time. Water is off during work hours. We restore water each evening, cover floors and furniture near the work and clean up before we leave, so the house stays livable while we work through it one room at a time.

    Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. Hard water deposits calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe over time. Flow drops as the bore narrows. Scale also speeds corrosion in metal lines, which is why PEX-A plus a softener or filter added during a repipe can protect the new system and your fixtures.

    Most homes are repiped in two to four days, plus a day for drywall patching. You will have water most nights.

    We commonly use Uponor PEX-A with ProPEX fittings and Type L copper from domestic mills. We also use Viega ProPress fittings for copper work.

    Both work well, and the best choice depends on your house and your water. PEX costs less. PEX bends, resists scale and handles freezes better, and copper is rigid, UV-tolerant and proven but pricier and vulnerable to aggressive water.
