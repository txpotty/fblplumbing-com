    Root Intrusion Removal in Chico, CA

    Roots enter through hairline cracks and loose joints, then grow into a mat that catches paper and grease. They come back fast.     Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Chico, CA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Paper and solids settle there. Many older homes here sit on raised foundations with crawlspaces, which make supply and drain repairs easier to reach than slab work. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and set it back to a quarter inch of fall per foot, which is the typical code minimum for a four-inch line. Not every sag needs digging, and we will tell you which yours is.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Chico

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. We record the whole run. Cracks, root intrusion, offset joints and standing water all show up on the screen, and the sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the ground above, which keeps the dig to one small pit. Older homes in Chico often hide two or three problems in one line, and the video catches all of them.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Chico

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. Over decades it goes oval and blisters. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, so by the time a camera shows widespread damage, spot repairs are a poor investment. We replace these lines with PVC. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Sewer Repair for Commercial Properties in Chico, CA

    Commercial laterals run six inches or larger and carry far more flow than a house. A closed kitchen loses revenue by the hour. For restaurants, strip centers and apartment buildings in Chico we schedule repairs after hours where we can, set up temporary bypass pumping when the line has to stay in service, and give managers a clear window to share with tenants. We repair a lot of grease-clogged outlet lines.

    Pipe Burst & Collapse Repair in Chico, CA

    A collapsed sewer pipe stops all flow at the point of failure. The whole house loses its drains. Collapses usually come from corroded cast iron, crushed clay under a driveway or tar-paper pipe that has finally flattened, and we locate the break and its depth before we start digging. We dig at the mark and replace the broken length with PVC. Lining cannot fix a full collapse.
