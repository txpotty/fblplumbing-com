    Clay or older cast iron lines with trees nearby usually need rooting every 12 to 24 months. Newer PVC lines may go many years without it. A camera look can set the timing. How quickly roots return after the last cut tells us if yearly or every-other-year service fits your line.

    Routine drain cleaning in Chico, CA does not usually need a permit. Digging up the yard, replacing the sewer or making a major repair usually requires one. We handle that paperwork. You will know about any permit before repair work is scheduled.

    Most single-drain clogs take 1 to 3 hours from arrival to flow test. Main lines with heavy roots, or jobs that need a jetting pass, can stretch to half a day or more. Access is the wild card. A buried or missing cleanout means pulling a toilet first, which adds about an hour.
