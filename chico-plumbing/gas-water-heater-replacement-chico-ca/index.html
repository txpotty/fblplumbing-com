    Flue pipes on older atmospheric heaters rust at the joints and lose their upward slope, and a loose connector can spill exhaust into the closet. Bad vent sections and draft hoods get replaced. We check draft with a match or mirror at the hood.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Replacement in Chico

    Atmospheric vent heaters last 10 to 12 years on average, and the end usually shows up as rusty hot water or a slow drip from the bottom.     No Hot Water Calls in Chico

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. Those get tested before anything else. An old tank with a bad valve usually gets replaced.

    Choosing Between Gas Tank Brands in Chico, CA

    Warranty length on a tank mostly tracks anode size and burner design, so a 12-year tank is a different build from a 6-year one. Self-cleaning dip tubes help on hard water. Powered anodes outlast standard rods. Your plans for the house help decide it.

    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Chico

    Blower motors and pressure switches are the weak points on power vent tanks. A second blower failure on an older tank is usually the point where a new heater makes more sense. We confirm a dedicated outlet first.

    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Chico

    Closed systems are the reason expansion tanks exist. With a check or regulator on the main, expanding hot water has nowhere to go and the pressure spikes each heating cycle. Many codes require a thermal expansion tank there. We precharge it to your house pressure.

    Going tankless usually means a bigger gas line, since a whole-house tankless unit can draw 199,000 BTU against the 40,000 a tank burner uses. Most homes need the line upsized from the meter.
