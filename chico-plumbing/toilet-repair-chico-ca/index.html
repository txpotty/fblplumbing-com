    Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Chico, CA

    If your toilet needs two flushes or clogs often, the problem may be scale in the rim jets, a partial blockage in the trapway or a flush valve that does not open fully. We auger the trap, check the vent if gurgling is present and clean the jets with a descaling treatment. Some low-flow toilets from the 1990s simply flush poorly, and we will tell you honestly if replacement is the better fix.

    Leaking Toilet Repair in Chico

    Toilet leaks can cause significant water damage to your bathroom floor, subfloor, and even the ceiling below. Leaks can originate from the base of the toilet, the tank, or the supply line. Common culprits include a worn-out wax ring, loose bolts, or cracked porcelain. We thoroughly inspect all potential leak points, replacing seals, tightening connections, or recommending bowl replacement if the porcelain is damaged, preventing further water intrusion.

    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Chico, CA

    A hairline crack in the tank or a bulging braided supply line can let go without warning and flood a bathroom. We inspect the tank, tank-to-bowl bolts and gasket, and the supply line on every visit. If the porcelain is cracked we will price a replacement tank or new toilet, and we upgrade old plastic or vinyl supply lines to braided stainless.

    Toilet Tank and Bowl Crack Repair in Chico

    Cracks in a toilet tank or bowl in Chico can lead to serious water leakage and require immediate attention. While small hairline cracks might be temporarily sealed, larger or structural cracks often require replacement of the affected component. Our technicians assess the severity of the crack and advise on the most cost-effective and durable solution. We can perform careful tank or bowl replacements, ensuring a watertight seal and proper function, preventing further water damage to your flooring or subfloor.

    Fill Valve Replacement in Chico

    A noisy, slow or hissing tank often points to a worn fill valve. Mineral scale clogs the small ports inside, which causes long refill times and phantom refills in the night. We replace old ballcock valves with modern adjustable fill valves from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the water level to the manufacturer mark. The job takes about 20 minutes and makes an older toilet much quieter.

    Clogged Toilet Clearing in Chico, CA

    Stubborn toilet clogs are a frequent problem that can bring your bathroom to a halt. While some can be cleared with a plunger, deeper blockages may indicate issues further down the drain line or even in the main sewer line. Our technicians use professional-grade augers and sometimes even camera inspections to locate and remove obstructions effectively. We handle everything from foreign objects to excessive toilet paper, making sure your toilet drains freely again.
