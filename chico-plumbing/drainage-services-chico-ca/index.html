    French Drain Installation in Chico

    A French drain is a gravel-filled trench with perforated pipe that collects water and carries it away from problem areas. It works well along foundations, behind retaining walls and in low spots that hold water after heavy rain. We wrap the gravel in filter fabric so fine soil does not clog the system. The drain is sloped to an outlet, dry well or approved discharge point.

    Channel and Trench Drains in Chico, CA

    Driveways and patios that slope toward the house can send storm runoff straight into a garage or under a door. A channel drain with a grate catches that sheet flow before it reaches the building. We use durable polymer or concrete channels from brands like NDS and set them flush with the surface. The channel ties into solid pipe that carries the water to a proper outlet.

    Outdoor & Storm Drain Cleaning in Chico

    Outdoor drains, including storm drains and area drains, are essential for preventing water accumulation around your property, especially during heavy rainfall. We clear debris, leaves, and sediment from these systems to ensure proper water runoff and protect your foundation from potential water damage. Regular cleaning can prevent costly landscape and structural issues.

    Preventative Drain Maintenance in Chico, CA

    The best way to avoid expensive drain emergencies is through preventive maintenance. FBL Plumbing offers scheduled drain cleaning and inspection services to keep your pipes clear and identify potential problems before they get worse. This forward-looking approach is particularly helpful for homes with older plumbing or properties with mature trees near the sewer line.

    Dry Well Installation in Chico, CA

    Where there is no place to discharge water on the surface, a dry well lets it soak into deeper soil. Dense clay or hardpan layers can block absorption, so we test the soil and dig through hardpan when needed. Some jurisdictions have rules on dry well registration and placement, and we follow them. A properly sized dry well with a sediment chamber can handle back-to-back heavy storms.

    Emergency Drain Clog Removal in Chico, CA

    A sudden, severe drain clog can be an emergency, leading to water damage and health hazards. FBL Plumbing provides fast emergency drainage services to address urgent blockages. Our technicians are available to promptly diagnose and resolve your critical drain problems, minimizing disruption and protecting your property.

    Main Line and Sewer Drainage in Chico, CA

    When several fixtures back up at once, the problem is usually in the main line to the city sewer or septic. Tree roots from mature shade trees and shrubs find their way into older clay and cast iron laterals. We clear the main through a cleanout and inspect it with a camera so you can see breaks, bellies or root intrusion. Repair options include spot repairs, lining or replacement.
