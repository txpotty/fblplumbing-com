    Perma-Liner and NuFlow Trenchless Systems in Chico

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow both make lining systems we use, each with its own resin chemistry and installation equipment. The brand matters less than the prep, because a liner bonded to a dirty, scaly pipe wall will not hold the way the manufacturer rates it. So the line gets jetted or descaled to bare wall before any resin goes in.

    Pipe Bursting in Chico, CA

    Two pits are standard for a burst, one at the house end near the foundation and one out near the street or the city tap. Before pulling, the crew heat-fuses sections of HDPE into one continuous length on the ground. The winch or hydraulic puller then drags the bursting head and the new pipe through. A 60-foot pull often takes under an hour.
