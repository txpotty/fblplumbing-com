    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Chico, CA

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. Lining can add many years of service.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Chico

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for long trenching. It is ideal for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Chico that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Chico, CA

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is excellent for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a fast and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Northern California homeowners.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Chico, CA

    Tree and shrub roots go after moisture in sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense masses that cause recurring backups. We cut roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro jet, then line the pipe to seal the joints. That breaks the cycle of cleaning the line every few months.
