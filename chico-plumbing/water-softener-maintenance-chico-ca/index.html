    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repairs in Chico, CA

    Hardness Testing at the Tap in Chico

    We test with a titration kit, counting drops until the sample changes color, because test strips are too rough for setting a valve. Raw water gets tested from an unsoftened hose bib or with the valve on bypass. The softened sample comes from a kitchen faucet.

    Yearly Water Softener Tune-Ups in Chico

    A tune-up starts with two hardness readings and ends with a full manual regeneration that we watch from start to finish. In between, we check the salt level, the brine well and the float. It takes about an hour on most homes.

    Commercial Twin-Tank Maintenance in Chico

    Salons, car washes, hotels and restaurants put far more gallons through a softener than any house does, and a hard-water day can mean spotted glassware or a scaled ice machine.     Resin Rebedding in Chico

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water slowly swell and soften resin beads, and iron or sediment can coat them until capacity drops off. We try a resin cleaner first. If the bed is spent, we empty out the old resin and refill the tank with new high-capacity media, which runs far less than a new softener.

    Repair or Replace a Softener in Chico, CA

    New softener systems often run $1,200 to $3,500 installed, while a resin rebed usually lands between $300 and $700. If the tank and valve are in good shape, keeping them makes sense. We recommend a new unit when the valve is obsolete and parts are gone, or when the tank itself is cracked.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Service in Chico

Plan on new media every three to five years, and the sediment prefilter ahead of it needs changing every few months, which is the part most owners skip.
