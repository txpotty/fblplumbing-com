    Which Sump Pump Suits Your Chico, CA Home

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Chico

    Basement bathrooms below the sewer line drain into a sealed, vented basin, and the ejector pump in that basin has to pass 2-inch solids. It is a different pump entirely. The ejector and check valve get replaced, then we reseal the lid and check the vent connection. A bad seal shows up as sewer odor.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Chico, CA

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. They are louder, no question. We set the new column plumb, secure it so vibration does not walk it across the basin, and adjust the float rod stops for the cycle you want. Narrow pits, under about 12 inches across, are where they make the most sense.

    Sizing a Submersible Sump Pump in Chico

    Horsepower matters less than people think. A third-horse pump moves plenty of water for most homes, as long as the vertical lift to the discharge is under about 15 feet. We check the flow chart against your actual lift before we pick a size. A pump that is too big empties the pit too fast and wears itself out.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Chico, CA

    Pit filling, pump not running? Call (669) 319-2034 and we will send someone. Keep the lid off, unplug anything sitting on the wet floor, and use a wet vac to hold the water below the rim until we get there. We carry common pumps, so the swap usually happens on the spot. We will also figure out what killed the old pump.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Chico

    Tree roots seek out sewer laterals during dry spells, which is why heavy rains often reveal damaged lines through sudden backups. A primary pump on household current does nothing once the breaker panel goes dark. We add a 12-volt backup pump on its own float, mounted a few inches above the primary, with a deep-cycle battery and a charger that tests itself. It also covers a stuck primary switch. The alarm will tell you which one is running.

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Chico, CA

    Yard drainage pits and window well pumps can sit dry from fall to spring and then seize the first time a big storm fills them. We clean the debris out and install a pump built to handle grit. We route the outlet away from the house. Test them each spring.

    Choosing a Backup Battery in Chico

    Battery choice decides how long the backup actually runs. Most backups use a sealed AGM or deep-cycle battery around 75 to 100 amp-hours, which covers several hours of on-and-off pumping. We size it to how often your pump runs on a wet day. Plan on a new battery every three to five years.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Chico

    A submersible pump sits at the bottom of the pit, sealed in an oil-filled housing, and it runs quieter than anything else you can put down there. We pull the old unit, scoop the gravel and silt out of the basin, and set the new pump on a flat brick so it does not suck up debris. Then a new check valve goes on. Skip that part and the pump short cycles.
