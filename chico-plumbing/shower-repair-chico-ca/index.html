    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Chico

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. If the head drips with the valve closed, water is slipping past the cartridge, and a new head will not change that. The fix is in the valve. Drips from the arm threads just need fresh tape and a snug turn.

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Chico

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water leaking behind the trim slips into the wall cavity at the valve opening and soaks the drywall while the tile face stays dry. The trim comes off first. It is usually a loose bonnet nut or a worn O-ring.

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Chico
