    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Chico

    Most residential expansion tank installations cost $250 to $550 including the tank and labor. Attic installs, larger tanks or a new pressure reducing valve can bring the total to $600 to $1,000.

    While some homeowners attempt DIY installation, it is highly recommended to have a professional plumber install your expansion tank. Proper sizing, placement, connection and adherence to local plumbing codes are crucial for safety and effective operation. Incorrect installation can lead to leaks or system damage.

    While it might seem like a simple task, installing a water heater expansion tank involves working with pressurized water lines and getting the sizing and code compliance right. It is best to hire a licensed plumber for a safe, correct installation that avoids leaks or damage to your system.

    A dripping temperature and pressure relief valve often means thermal expansion is pushing pressure too high. A failed or missing expansion tank is the most common cause.

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve, check valve or backflow preventer on the main, you probably have a closed system that needs one. Many local codes require it whenever a water heater is installed on a closed system.

    Most expansion tanks last 5 to 10 years. Hard water and high pressure can shorten that life, so we check them during water heater service.

    Most standard water heater expansion tank installations can be finished within 1 to 2 hours. More complex installations, or those requiring significant pipe changes, might take a bit longer. Our plumbers work efficiently to minimize disruption to your home.

    A waterlogged tank feels heavy and sounds solid when tapped near the top. Water coming out of the air valve when pressed is a clear sign the bladder has failed.
