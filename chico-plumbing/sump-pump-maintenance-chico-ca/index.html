    Zoeller and Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Chico

    Brand matters less than people think, but parts do not cross over cleanly between Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty and Little Giant. Our plumber reads the model number off the label and orders the matching float or seal kit. A submersible Zoeller and a pedestal Wayne get two different checklists. We know both.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Chico, CA

    Backup pumps sit in the pit next to the main pump and run off a 12-volt battery when the power drops or the primary fails. We check the battery with a load tester, not just a voltmeter. Next, we cut power to the main pump and watch the backup clear the basin by itself. Alarms get tested too.

    Check Valve and Discharge Pipe Repair in Chico, CA

    A bad check valve can nearly double how often the pump starts. Each start heats the motor windings. Our plumbers replace worn valves with quieter spring-loaded types and check the pipe for cracks and loose couplings out to where it leaves the house. Discharge needs to land far enough from the slab or footings that it does not seep right back into the pit.

    Float Switch Inspection in Chico, CA

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. Our plumber watches the float rise and drop through a complete cycle. Then we set the on and off points so the pump runs longer, less often.

    Commercial Building Sump Pumps in Chico, CA

    Parking garages, mechanical rooms and loading docks often drain to large pits with pumps rated at a horsepower or more. Those units need more frequent checks than a house pump. We test the controls, the alarm panel and both pumps in a duplex setup, then log the amp draw on each one so trends show up over time. Downtime costs money.

    Submersible Sump Pump Maintenance in Chico

    Submersible pumps sit at the bottom of the pit, which keeps them quiet but hides the problems. We lift the unit out to look at the seals and the cord. A cracked cord usually means a new pump. The intake screen gets cleaned, and we look over the housing for corrosion, since pumps sitting in standing water for years rust from the bottom up.

    Home Sump Pump Maintenance in Chico

Elevator pit pumps often need oil-detecting controls so hydraulic fluid never gets pumped out with the water. Duplex systems and high-water alarms get tested on a fixed schedule.
