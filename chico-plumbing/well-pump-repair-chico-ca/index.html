    Jet Pump Repair in Chico, CA

    Shallow wells and booster systems often use jet pumps. Common problems are loss of prime, a leaking shaft seal and a worn impeller. Our crew in Chico restores prime, replaces worn parts and checks the suction line for leaks that let air in. Where water levels swing through the year, a falling level can starve the pump and cost it prime.

    Control Box and Surge Repair in Chico, CA

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. Strong winter storms can bring days of heavy rain to Northern California, saturating ground and pushing water into crawlspaces and sewer lines. Surges from lightning are the usual cause of control box failures. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection, replace what failed and recommend a surge arrestor at the box if one is missing. Arrestors are cheap.

    Grundfos Well Pump Repair in Chico

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have built-in electronics that protect the motor and report faults. Sensor failures and drive errors are the usual problems. FBL Plumbing reads the fault code, checks the wiring and tests the pressure sensor before replacing anything in a Chico, CA well. Electronics fail less often than people think.

    Low Water Pressure Repair in Chico

    Weak pressure on a well is not always the pump. A clogged sediment filter, a pressure switch set too low or a leak in the yard line can all cause it. In some cases the pump is fine and the well just cannot keep up with demand. We test each one.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Chico

    Bladder tanks hold a cushion of air that lets the pump rest between cycles. A failed bladder lets the tank waterlog, and the pump begins to short cycle. The bladder, the switch and the tank fittings all get checked. A properly sized tank can add years to pump life.

    No-Water Same-Day Repair in Chico

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Chico

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Most switches fail from pitted contacts, a plugged sensing tube or insects in the housing. After swapping the switch and clearing the tube, we set cut-in and cut-out to match the tank, usually 40 and 60 psi. This is among the least expensive well repairs.

    Deep Well Pump Repair in Chico

    Deep wells get submersible pumps. They push water up instead of pulling it, and they run quiet for years, but motors, impellers and wire splices still fail eventually. We pull the pump with the right equipment, test it and replace only what failed. Heat-shrink splice kits and a torque arrestor protect the new work in your Chico, CA well.
