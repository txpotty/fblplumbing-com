    Disposal Jams and Hums in Chico, CA

    Humming with no grinding means the motor is getting power but the plate is stuck. That is often ten minutes. We free the plate from below with a hex key, clear the hopper with pliers, never a hand, and reset the overload before running water through it. Silence with no hum points to the switch, the outlet or the breaker instead.

    New Disposal Installation in Chico

    Adding a disposal where there never was one means a switched outlet or circuit in the cabinet, and that part is electrical work. We plumb it and can coordinate the wiring.     Grease Trap Cleaning in Chico

    A grease trap that smells sour is normally overdue for a pump-out. We remove the lid, scrape the baffles, pull the floating grease and settled solids, and check the gasket so the lid seals again instead of venting into the kitchen. We log every visit.     Burst Supply Lines in Chico, CA

    Supply lines with plastic coupling nuts split more often than braided stainless, especially after years of pressure swings.     Kitchen Faucet Repair and Replacement in Chico

    A dripping single-handle faucet almost always has a worn cartridge, and Moen, Delta and Kohler all sell the part by model. We bring common cartridges.     Disposal Repair and Replacement in Chico, CA

    A disposal that hums but will not spin usually has something wedged in it.
