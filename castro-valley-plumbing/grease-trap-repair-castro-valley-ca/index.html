    Grease Interceptor Clog Removal in Castro Valley

    Grease does not stay liquid for long once it cools in a pipe. Within a few feet of the sink it hardens like candle wax along the top of the pipe.     Baffle Repair and Replacement in Castro Valley

    Inside a concrete gravity interceptor the baffle is usually a poured or precast wall with a crossover opening. A crumbling wall lets grease slip into the second compartment and out the outlet. We can rebuild the wall with form work and a sulfate-resistant mix or set a polyethylene baffle panel, depending on the tank. Both require a full pump-out and a dry tank.

    Damaged Baffle Repair in Castro Valley

Steel traps with a rusted-out baffle may need a fabricated piece, and sometimes a new trap is the cheaper answer.
