    Salt-Free Conditioner Maintenance in Castro Valley

    Template assisted crystallization and other salt-free conditioners do not remove hardness but change how it behaves. They still need media replacement every few years and sediment prefilter changes much more often. We inspect the media tank, replace prefilters and check bypass valves. We also explain honestly how these systems differ from a true softener.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Castro Valley

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, dictating regeneration cycles and water flow. Our maintenance service includes complete diagnostics of the control valve's programming and mechanical components. We check for proper timing, motor function and potential blockages. If issues are found, we can often repair or replace worn parts to restore full function, preventing the need for a costly full system replacement in Castro Valley.

    Commercial Softener Maintenance in Castro Valley

    Restaurants, laundromats, salons and car washes rely on soft water to protect equipment like dishwashers, ice machines and boilers.     Maintenance Versus New Softener Costs in Castro Valley, CA

    A maintenance visit typically costs far less than a new system, which often runs $1,200 to $3,500 installed. Resin rebedding typically costs $300 to $700 and can bring an older tank back to full capacity. Most quality softeners last 10 to 15 years with service. We will tell you when a repair makes sense and when a replacement is the better investment.

    Brine Tank Cleaning and Inspection in Castro Valley, CA

    Over time, sludge and insoluble materials can build up in your brine tank, hindering the softening process. Our maintenance includes thoroughly cleaning the brine tank, inspecting for any cracks or leaks and ensuring the float valve operates correctly. This step is essential for the efficient regeneration of your water softener in Castro Valley, CA.

    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repair in Castro Valley, CA

    A leaking bypass valve can let hard water slip past the softener without you noticing. We test and rebuild bypass valves and replace cracked fittings. We also inspect the drain line for kinks and proper air gap and the brine overflow line for correct routing. These small fixes head off leaks and backflow problems.

    Control Valve and Injector Service in Castro Valley, CA

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a complete regeneration every time.
