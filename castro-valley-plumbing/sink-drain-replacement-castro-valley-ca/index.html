    Pop-Up Drain Assembly Replacement in Castro Valley, CA

    A stopper that drops on its own has a worn pivot. The plastic ball on the pivot rod wears flat, the nut that holds it loses its seal, and the rod no longer holds the stopper up, so the sink either will not drain or will not hold water. One new pop-up fixes every part of that. Takes about an hour.

    Leaking P-Trap Replacement in Castro Valley

    Traps leak at the joints first. Slip nut washers flatten and harden with age, and a trap opened often for clogs usually has stripped threads. A new trap kit costs little. Labor is most of the bill.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Replacement in Castro Valley

    Kitchen drains start failing at the basket strainer. Old strainer putty dries out and the locknut rusts, so water runs down the tailpiece and looks like a trap leak. Strainer and trap get swapped as a pair. Disposal sinks get a new flange instead.

    Corroded Drain Pipe Replacement in Castro Valley, CA

    Galvanized drain arms rust from the inside. An old steel stub can crumble at the threads, so we cut it back and join new PVC with a shielded coupling. We handle cast iron the same way. Sometimes that means opening the wall.

    Vessel and Grid Drain Replacement in Castro Valley

    Vessel sinks use a different drain. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted grid or umbrella drain, or water leaks out the slots. We see the wrong drain often. Swapping it takes under an hour.

    Drain Replacement vs Repair Costs in Castro Valley, CA

    A single washer is cheaper today. On a drain more than fifteen years old, though, the next joint often fails within months, and paying for two or three service calls costs more than replacing the whole assembly in one visit. You see both options in writing. Your call.

    P-Trap Replacement in Castro Valley

    Thin chrome traps are the ones we replace most. They rust from the inside, the plating flakes off, and a slip nut that will not loosen often twists the whole trap apart, so we cut it out and install heavy-wall PVC or polished brass that matches the room. Then it gets a full flow test. Total time is often under an hour.
