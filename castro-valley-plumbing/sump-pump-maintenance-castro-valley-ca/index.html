    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Castro Valley

    Drought years alternate with very wet years here, so conservation rules and low-flow fixtures are a constant part of plumbing decisions.     Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Castro Valley, CA

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil around the slab or foundation.

    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Castro Valley, CA

    The float switch is a key component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to ensure reliable operation for your home in Castro Valley, CA.

    Annual Sump Pump Check-ups in Castro Valley

    Regular annual maintenance is crucial for the longevity and reliability of your sump pump.
