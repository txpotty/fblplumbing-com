    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Castro Valley

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. Pairs well with a softener. Filters get changed on schedule.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Castro Valley

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. Install follows the manufacturer manual for bypass, drain and overflow, then we set hardness and regeneration. We install store-bought units. We set it up for you.

    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Castro Valley, CA

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Newer valves meter actual water use, some connect to Wi-Fi for salt alerts and higher-capacity tanks regenerate less often for larger households. Salt bills drop. We swap it out in a few hours.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Castro Valley

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. Every whole-house install gets a bypass valve, and we add a pressure reducing valve if incoming pressure is too high. Soft water reaches every fixture. We can skip the hose bibs.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Castro Valley

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. Compared with an older timer-based unit, a modern metered softener can use roughly half the salt and noticeably less regeneration water each month. The savings add up. We install several models.

    Pelican Water Softener Installation in Castro Valley, CA

    Pelican sells both salt-based softeners and salt-free conditioners, often direct to homeowners. When a homeowner has already bought one, we handle the plumbing, the bypass and the startup flush the manual requires. Skipping the flush causes trouble. We follow it step by step.
