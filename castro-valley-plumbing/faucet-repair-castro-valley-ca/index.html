    Disc Faucet Repair in Castro Valley

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. Inlet seals cost very little. A scratched disc means a new cartridge, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.

    Faucet Handle Repair and Replacement in Castro Valley, CA

    Stripped handle splines, cracked handle bodies, worn adapters and loose set screws make a faucet hard to turn or impossible to shut tight. Handles are often sold separately. The replacement handle gets matched to both the stem and the finish, and if the stem itself is stripped we replace it at the same time.

    Delta and Moen Faucet Repair in Castro Valley, CA

    Delta and Moen are the faucets we repair most, and both back their cartridges with lifetime warranties for the original owner on many models. We can check whether yours qualifies.     Leaky Faucet Repair in Castro Valley

    Most drips come from the part that seals the water inside the faucet, which is a rubber washer on older two-handle faucets and a cartridge or ceramic disc on newer ones. We figure out which type you have first. It matters. With the right part on the truck, most drips are fixed in one visit.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Castro Valley

    Sputtering flow, where the water spits and surges, often means air or debris in the line after a water main repair, or a partly clogged cartridge screen. A flush of the lines usually fixes it. Pull-down faucets also have check valves in the hose that can stick, and we clean or replace those.

    Cracked Faucet Body Repair in Castro Valley

    Cracked faucet bodies, usually from freezing or an old brass casting that finally gave out, cannot be repaired and need a replacement faucet. Shut the stops first. We can usually install a new faucet the same day, and we will check the supply lines and stops while the water is off.

    Dripping Faucet Solutions in Castro Valley

    Every drip has a source, and on a faucet that has dripped for years there is usually more than one worn part. We replace what failed and check the rest. Compression, cartridge, ball and disc faucets all fail differently, so the repair is matched to the valve inside yours.

    Bathroom Pop-Up Drain Repair in Castro Valley

    Pop-up drains on bathroom sinks fail at the pivot ball, the clevis strap, the lift rod or the stopper itself, and a slow drain often turns out to be hair wrapped around the stopper. Try lifting out the stopper first. A new pop-up assembly goes in, matched to your faucet finish when the old one is corroded or the pivot leaks.
