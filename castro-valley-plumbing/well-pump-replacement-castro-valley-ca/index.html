    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Castro Valley

    Well pump replacement usually costs $1,800 to $4,500 depending on well depth and pump size. Deep wells or constant pressure systems can cost $4,000 to $8,000.

    Manufacturer warranties typically range from 1 to 5 years. We also provide a labor warranty.

    Most well pumps last 8 to 15 years. Sediment, hard water and frequent cycling can cut lifespan.

    We install Grundfos, Goulds and Franklin Electric pumps. These brands are reliable and widely supported.

    FBL Plumbing installs and services a wide range of trusted well pump brands known for their durability and performance. These commonly include Grundfos, Franklin Electric, Goulds Pumps and Sta-Rite. We can recommend the best brand and model to suit your well's needs.

    Permit requirements vary by county. We follow local requirements for all well work.

    It is often a good idea to consider replacing your pressure tank at the same time as your well pump, especially if both are of similar age. A new pressure tank can improve the efficiency and lifespan of your new pump by reducing cycling. It also saves on labor costs to do both jobs at once.

    The lifespan of a well pump varies greatly depending on usage, water quality and pump type, but most last between 10 and 20 years. Regular maintenance and proper sizing can extend its life, but eventually wear and tear will require replacement.
