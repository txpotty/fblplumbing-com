    Liberty Pumps Installation in Castro Valley, CA

    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options. We install their 250 and 280 series often. Replacement switches are easy to get. A small weep hole in the discharge pipe keeps the pump from air locking.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Castro Valley

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. Because the motor is cooled by the surrounding water, they run quieter and can push more gallons per minute up a tall lift than most pedestal pumps. The pit lid sits flush. Each install gets a sealed lid and a grommet around the cord to keep out radon and odor.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Castro Valley, CA

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. You can reach the motor and the switch without fishing anything out of the pit, which helps in a basement where the pit is shallow or tight. They are louder than submersibles. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Castro Valley

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Castro Valley, CA

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. We pull the old pump and inspect the pit. If the old one could not keep up, we size up. We fill the pit and watch it run.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Castro Valley, CA

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. We carry common pumps on the truck. Figure on two hours for a typical swap. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Castro Valley

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi monitors go further and send a phone alert when the pump runs too long, the power drops or the water climbs past a set level. Landlords like them. They can go on a new pump or one that is already working.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Castro Valley, CA

    Price depends mostly on whether a pit already exists. A swap in an existing pit is the low end. Backups add cost. Your written quote shows each option and its price.
