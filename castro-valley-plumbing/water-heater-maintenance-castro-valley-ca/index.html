    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Castro Valley

    Commercial Tank and Tankless Service in Castro Valley, CA

    Tankless Water Heater Service in Castro Valley, CA

    Tankless units need descaling. Mineral scale coats the inside of the heat exchanger, restricting flow and making the unit work harder, and eventually it starts throwing error codes or cutting out in the middle of a shower. We flush it with descaler. Igniter, fan and exhaust venting get checked too.

    Water Heater Leak Checks in Castro Valley, CA

    Small drips become floods. We inspect the supply connections, the relief valve outlet, the drain valve and the tank seams for moisture or rust trails, and we check the drain pan and the floor around the unit for signs of a slow leak. Rust trails mean trouble. Catching it early saves the floor.

    Thermostat, Element and Burner Checks in Castro Valley

    Wrong temperature means a part is off. Electric units get thermostat and element tests, and gas units get burner, pilot and control checks. Coastal fog and moisture can speed corrosion on exposed fittings, so exterior valves and hose bibs deserve an occasional check. Faulty parts waste energy. You run out sooner.

    Anode Rod Checks in Castro Valley

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. Once the rod is eaten down to its core wire, the water goes after the tank itself. It comes out for inspection. Replacing the rod is much cheaper than replacing the heater.
