    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Castro Valley

    Most standard gas or electric tank replacements cost $1,400 to $3,000 including the heater, labor and typical code upgrades. Attic installs, larger tanks or relocations can raise the cost to $3,000 to $4,500.

    Manufacturer tank warranties typically range from 6 to 12 years depending on the model. We also warranty our installation labor.

    Upgrading provides more reliable hot water, improved energy efficiency and lower utility bills, better performance and a reduced risk of leaks or breakdowns. Newer models often have helpful features and longer warranties, improving comfort and safety in your home.

    Flushing a traditional tank water heater annually is recommended in most homes. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. This helps remove sediment buildup, which can reduce efficiency and shorten the lifespan of the unit. Regular flushing can also prevent scale from damaging heating elements.

    Choosing the right size depends on the number of people in your household, their hot water usage habits and peak demand times. Our technicians will calculate the appropriate GPM (gallons per minute) for tankless or gallon capacity for tank models so you never run out of hot water, especially in multi-bathroom Castro Valley homes.

    Tankless offers endless hot water and a longer lifespan but costs more upfront. We can compare both options around your household and budget.

    New water heaters usually come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and parts, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. FBL Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation, giving you added peace of mind. Specific warranty details vary by brand and model.

    We install Bradford White, Rheem and A.O. Smith water heaters. Each offers dependable performance and a range of warranties.
