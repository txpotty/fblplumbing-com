    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Castro Valley, CA

    Gas water heaters recover faster than electric, and in Castro Valley, CA homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Every joint gets checked.

    Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith in Castro Valley, CA

    We install Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith on most tank jobs.
