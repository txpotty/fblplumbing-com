    Causes of Sewer Line Failure in Castro Valley, CA

    Shifting soil is one of the main reasons sewer joints separate, crack or drop out of grade over the years. Earthquakes and hillside soil movement are real risks in Northern California, so flexible connections and seismic straps help protect plumbing from sudden movement. Roots are the other big one, since a hairline gap at a clay joint is enough for a feeder root to get in and then swell into a mat that catches paper. Age does the rest. Cast iron scales and thins, and clay bells crack under load.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Castro Valley, CA

    CIPP stands for cured-in-place pipe.
