    Frequently Asked Questions About Backflow Preventer Installation in Castro Valley

    The cost for backflow preventer installation in Castro Valley, CA can range from approximately $300 to $1,500, depending on the type of device required, such as a DCVA or an RPZ, the complexity of the installation and any changes needed to existing plumbing.
