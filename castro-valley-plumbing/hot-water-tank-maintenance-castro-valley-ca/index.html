    Electric Element Check in Castro Valley

    Electric tanks have two elements, usually 4,500 watts at 240 volts, and the lower one does most of the work and takes most of the scale. We test each one with a meter. A failed lower element shows up as hot water that runs out halfway through a shower. We replace it with the matching wattage and voltage.

    Relief Valve Testing in Castro Valley

    Temperature and pressure relief valves on residential tanks are typically rated to open at 150 psi or 210 degrees, and they can stick shut after years of mineral buildup. Our tech lifts the lever, lets water flow, then watches it close. A valve that drips afterward gets replaced. We also confirm the discharge pipe runs full size to within six inches of the floor or to an approved drain, which is what plumbing codes ask for.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Castro Valley, CA

    Water chemistry changes from one street to the next, and so does anode life. Three to five years is typical. A softened water supply can chew through a magnesium rod in two. A new rod costs a fraction of a tank and often adds years.

    Rheem and Bradford White Tank Service in Castro Valley

    Rheem, Bradford White, AO Smith and State make most of the tanks in local garages, and each has quirks a tech should know before opening a drain valve. Some models hide the anode under the top cover. Bradford White often uses a combo anode in the hot outlet nipple. Our trucks carry sockets for each brand.

    Expansion Tank Service in Castro Valley, CA

    A thermal expansion tank is the small steel tank on the cold line above the heater, and it gives heated water somewhere to go when a check valve or pressure reducer closes the system. Our tech checks the air charge with a tire gauge. The precharge needs to match static pressure in the house. A waterlogged expansion tank sounds solid when you knock on it, and it lets pressure spike every time the burner fires.

    Standard Tank Flushing in Castro Valley

    Flushing is the one job every tank needs, gas or electric, new or old. Hard water makes it more urgent. We drain, partly refill and drain again until the hose runs clear, sometimes four or five rounds. Then we close the valve and check that it reseals without a drip.

    Burner and Pilot Service in Castro Valley, CA

    On a gas tank, a dirty burner throws a lazy yellow flame and leaves soot on the flue baffle. The burner comes out and gets brushed and vacuumed. We also check the thermocouple or flame sensor, since a weak one lets the pilot go out every few days and leaves you resetting it on your knees. The goal is a steady blue flame.

    Electric Thermostat Service in Castro Valley

    Each element on an electric tank has its own thermostat, and the upper one carries a reset button for the high-limit switch. When the reset trips, something caused it. We find out why it tripped, which usually comes down to a shorted element or a thermostat stuck closed, before we press it back in. Power stays off at the breaker the whole time.
