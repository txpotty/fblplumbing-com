    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Castro Valley

    Double bowl sinks need a continuous waste or end outlet layout to drain both sides through one trap. We plan the layout around your disposal and dishwasher connections. Proper slope keeps both sides draining well. We use heavy-wall fittings that resist leaks.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Castro Valley

    Undermount sinks need strong clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone, and support it while the adhesive sets. Then we install strainers, traps and supply lines. A proper undermount install helps prevent gaps and leaks along the counter edge.

    Workstation Sink Installation in Castro Valley, CA

    Workstation sinks include ledges for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks. They are usually deep, single-bowl designs with offset drains. We position the drain to fit your cabinet and disposal and confirm the faucet reach. These sinks are popular in modern kitchen remodels.

    Under-mount Sink Installation in Castro Valley, CA

    Under-mount sinks provide a sleek, seamless look, making countertop cleaning easier. Their installation is more complex than top-mount sinks, requiring precise measurements and strong adhesive or clips for secure attachment to the underside of the counter. FBL Plumbing excels in under-mount sink installation, ensuring a strong bond and a fully sealed edge to prevent water infiltration. We ensure the cutout is smooth and the sink is level for optimal drainage.
