    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Castro Valley, CA

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. They are harder to see. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, so we isolate the cold side, map the pipe with a line tracer and listen along the route with a ground microphone.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Castro Valley

    The hot side fails more than the cold. Heat speeds up corrosion, and the pipe expands and contracts every time someone showers, wearing against the gravel and concrete until a pinhole opens. Warm floors and a water heater that runs nonstop are the signs. Energy bills often rise.

    Direct Access Slab Leak Repair in Castro Valley, CA

    A single leak on sound pipe can be fixed in place. We cut a small opening at the marked spot, dig down to the pipe, repair the section with approved fittings and pressure test it. Then we backfill and patch the concrete level, ready for flooring. A precise mark keeps the opening tight.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Castro Valley

    A reroute abandons the leaking line under the floor. New pipe runs through the walls or attic instead, which avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than opening a finished floor. We usually use PEX because it handles heat and resists corrosion. Tile and hardwood floors make this the favorite.
