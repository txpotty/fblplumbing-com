    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Castro Valley, CA

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are more difficult to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know where to listen.

    Emergency Slab Leak Services in Castro Valley, CA

    A sudden, severe slab leak can cause fast damage to your property. FBL Plumbing offers emergency slab leak detection services across Castro Valley, CA when you need immediate assistance. Our rapid response team is equipped to handle urgent situations, limiting damage and providing timely solutions to protect your home or business from extensive water harm.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Castro Valley

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat speeds up corrosion and causes expansion and contraction. Signs include warm spots on the floor, a water heater that runs nonstop and higher gas or electric bills. Thermal imaging shows the heat pattern spreading through tile or concrete. We confirm with a pressure test and acoustic listening before flagging the spot.

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Castro Valley

    Our technicians use highly sensitive acoustic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a leak under your slab. This method involves listening for the subtle sound of escaping water, even through concrete. It's particularly effective for pressurized water lines, allowing us to find leaks without unnecessary excavation and minimizing disruption to your Castro Valley home.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Castro Valley

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs that use steel cables under high tension. Cutting into one without care is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We locate the cables with scanning equipment before any repair access is opened. This lets us repair the leak without risk or recommend a reroute that avoids the slab entirely.

    Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in Castro Valley, CA

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic. It is a bigger project, but it ends the cycle of repeat slab leaks. We can typically complete a full repipe in two to four days.
