    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Replacement in Castro Valley

    A standard disposal replacement with the unit typically costs $275 to $650. Premium models like InSinkErator Evolution usually run $500 to $1,000 installed.

    A continuous feed disposal operates when you flip a wall switch, allowing you to add food waste while it's running. A batch feed disposal only operates when a special stopper is inserted into the drain, making it usually safer as it prevents accidental activation while hands are near the opening.

    New garbage disposals usually come with manufacturer warranties ranging from 1 to 10 years, depending on the model and brand. FBL Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation services for your peace of mind.

    If your current disposal jams often or struggles with food waste, a 3/4 or 1 HP model can make a real difference.

    Common signs include frequent clogs, loud grinding noises without processing food, persistent leaks from the unit's housing, or if the disposal simply stops turning on. If you notice these issues, it's probably time for a replacement.

    Most disposals last 8 to 12 years. Hard water, heavy use and grease can shorten that life.

    Key signs you need a new garbage disposal include persistent leaks from the unit's housing, a humming noise without grinding, frequent clogs despite proper usage, foul odors that won't go away, or if the unit simply won't turn on. These symptoms usually indicate internal motor or component failure.

    In most cases, a direct replacement of a garbage disposal with a similar unit does not require a plumbing permit in Castro Valley, CA.
