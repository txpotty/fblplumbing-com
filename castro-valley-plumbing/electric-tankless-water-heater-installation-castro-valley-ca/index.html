    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Castro Valley

    Hard water can cause scale buildup in tankless heat exchangers and heating elements. Scale reduces efficiency and can trigger error codes. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. We recommend a water softener or scale inhibitor to protect your unit, and we install service valves for periodic descaling. Regular maintenance helps extend the life of your tankless heater.
