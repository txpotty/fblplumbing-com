    New Construction Toilet Installation in Castro Valley

    In new homes and additions, we set toilets after the finished floor is in so the flange sits at the right height. We verify the drain is correctly vented and the rough-in matches the fixture you selected. Each toilet gets a new wax or wax-free seal, a stainless supply line and a quarter-turn angle stop.     Bathroom Remodel Toilet Installation in Castro Valley, CA

    Remodels often raise the floor with new tile, which can leave the old flange too low. We use flange extenders or replace the flange so the seal compresses properly. If you are moving the toilet, we can relocate the drain, which on a slab foundation requires cutting and patching concrete. We plan timing so the toilet goes in after tile and grout have cured.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Castro Valley, CA

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, with the tank hidden behind an access plate. They free up floor space and make cleaning simple, but they need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep or a furred-out chase. We install Geberit, Grohe and Kohler carrier systems and set the bowl height to your preference. This work is easiest during a remodel when the wall is open.

    Rough-In Toilet Installation in Castro Valley, CA
