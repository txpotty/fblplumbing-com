    If your shower turns cold when someone flushes, the pressure-balance spool may be stuck. Your shower will hold a steady, consistent temperature.

    Showerhead and Arm Repair in Dublin

    Dripping showerheads, leaking arm connections and broken handheld hoses are frequent problems. We clean or replace showerheads, reseal shower arms and install new handheld hoses and holders.     Frequently Asked Questions About Shower Repair in Dublin

    Most shower repairs, like cartridge replacement or diverter repair, cost $150 to $375. Valve replacements that require opening the wall typically run $450 to $1,200. Shower pan repairs can cost more depending on the damage.

    Leaks behind the wall are often due to a faulty shower valve, loose pipe connections, or cracks in the shower pan or tile grout. These leaks can be serious, leading to mold growth and structural damage. Our team uses professional detection equipment to pinpoint the leak source and repair it with minimal wall disruption.

    Most common shower repairs, like fixing a leaky faucet or clearing a drain clog, can be completed within 1 to 3 hours. More complex issues, such as replacing a shower valve that requires wall access, might take half a day or longer. We aim for efficient service to minimize disruption.

    A stuck or worn pressure-balance spool often causes temperature swings when other fixtures are used. Replacing the cartridge or valve typically solves it.

    Common signs include dripping shower heads, inconsistent water temperature, low water pressure, strange noises coming from the pipes, foul odors from the drain, or standing water in the shower pan. Any of these symptoms warrant a professional inspection.

    Cleaning or replacing a showerhead is an simple DIY task. Valve cartridge replacements and leak repairs behind tile are best handled by a plumber to avoid damage.

    A dripping shower usually has a worn cartridge or seals in the valve. Hard water speeds up wear, so drips are common in many homes.

    Low shower water pressure can be caused by several factors, including mineral buildup in the showerhead, a faulty pressure balancing valve, sediment in your water heater, or even a leak in your water supply lines. Our plumbers can diagnose the exact cause and recommend the appropriate repair.
