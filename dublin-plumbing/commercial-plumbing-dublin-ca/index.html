    What Our Commercial Plumbers Do in Dublin, CA

    Commercial Restroom Fixture Repair in Dublin, CA

    Restroom calls are the most common commercial work we do. A flushometer that runs, a urinal that will not stop, or a sensor faucet that drips are usually fixed with a diaphragm kit, a solenoid or a new battery pack. We stock those. Wall-hung toilets that keep clogging or rocking get a carrier check, since a loose bowl can leak inside the wall. Low pressure on one fixture usually points to a clogged supply stop.

    Commercial Water Heater Repair and Installation in Dublin, CA

    A commercial water heater is judged by recovery rate, the gallons it can reheat per hour, not by tank size alone. Undersized heaters run cold by mid-shift. Our techs work on AO Smith, Rheem, Bradford White and State commercial heaters and check flue draft, gas valves and the T and P valve each visit. For a replacement, we size from fixture count and peak demand. That is how a busy kitchen stops running out of hot water during the dinner push.

    Multi-Family and Property Management Plumbing in Dublin

    Apartment complexes need a plumber who can handle many units and quick turns between tenants. Our work in multi-family buildings includes leak repair, heater swaps, stack clearing and full repipes, with posted notices before any shutoff. Pricing stays consistent by unit. Each invoice shows the unit number so it can be charged to the right ledger.

    Commercial Water Heater Service in Dublin, CA

    Big tank heaters fail in a few common ways: a failed gas valve, a cracked flue baffle, a stuck T and P valve or a tank full of scale. Each sounds different. We check draft and combustion, test water temperature at the far end of the building and flush the tank, then tell you whether repair or replacement makes more sense. Tankless units get a descaling flush. Recirculation pumps on long runs get tested as part of the visit.

    Commercial Fixture and Faucet Repair in Dublin, CA

    Commercial faucets and toilets are built heavy, but they still wear. Cartridges leak. Sensor and metering faucets, wall-hung toilets, mop sinks and compartment sinks are routine for us, with Sloan, Chicago Faucets, Zurn and T and S Brass parts on hand. We can also swap in lower-flow aerators that still rinse hands well. Most fixture calls are done in under two hours.

    Commercial Plumbing Costs and Maintenance Plans in Dublin, CA

    Backflow tests often run $75 to $175 per assembly, and kitchen line jetting usually costs $350 to $900 depending on length and buildup. After-hours calls cost more. Putting testing, jetting and flushing on a plan evens out your costs and cuts down on emergency calls, which cost the most. You get the plan in writing first.

    Kitchen and Floor Drain Cleaning in Dublin

    Floor drains in commercial kitchens collect grease, rice, mop water and the odd utensil. Most kitchens see the same drain back up every few months. We open the line with a sectional cable, then jet the 2-inch and 3-inch branches so the grease does not just close back up in a week. We also check trap primers.

    Backflow Assembly Testing and Rebuilds in Dublin

    Most water providers require annual tests on commercial backflow assemblies, and many send late notices that come with fines. Test early. Our testers handle RPZ, double check and PVB devices with annually calibrated gauges and submit results the way your provider wants them. Failed devices get rebuilt on the spot when we have the kit.
