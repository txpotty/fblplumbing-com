    What a Tank Tune-Up Covers in Dublin, CA

    Electric Thermostat Service in Dublin

    Each element on an electric tank has its own thermostat, and the upper one carries a reset button for the high-limit switch. When the reset trips, something caused it. We find out why it tripped, which usually comes down to a shorted element or a thermostat stuck closed, before we press it back in. Power stays off at the breaker the whole time.

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Dublin

    Street pressure over 80 psi is hard on every fixture in the house, and plumbing codes generally call for a pressure reducing valve above that number. We check static pressure at a hose bib. Then we lift the relief valve lever. If the valve opens and reseats cleanly and the expansion tank holds its charge, the tank is safe from pressure spikes.

    Commercial Tank Maintenance in Dublin, CA

    Restaurants, salons, laundromats and small offices run tanks much harder than a household does, often 75 to 100 gallons cycling all day. Scale piles up much faster than at home. We usually set commercial tanks on a quarterly or twice-yearly flush, with a dated log taped to the jacket that your insurance inspector can read. Downtime gets scheduled outside business hours.

    Sediment Flushing in Dublin, CA

    Sediment is mostly calcium carbonate and sand, and it settles in a layer that can reach several inches on a neglected 50-gallon tank. That layer insulates water from the heat. We flush with full line pressure, opening and closing the cold supply in bursts to stir the bottom. The drain hose runs to a floor drain or outside, never onto a lawn where hot water can burn the grass.

    Emergency Tank Troubleshooting in Dublin

    No hot water at all is usually a pilot, a gas valve, a tripped high-limit or a dead element. Those are fixable. Water on the floor is a different story, and the first question we ask is whether it is coming from a fitting on top or from under the jacket. Leaks from under the jacket mean the tank itself has failed.

    Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Dublin, CA

    Garage and attic tanks live through bigger temperature swings than closet units, which wears out plastic drain valves and wiring insulation faster. Attic tanks need a working pan. We check that the pan drain line runs to a safe spot and is not cracked or plugged, because one slow leak over a bedroom can bring down a ceiling. Gas tanks in garages need their ignition source at least 18 inches off the floor unless they are FVIR models.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Dublin, CA

    Water chemistry changes from one street to the next, and so does anode life. Three to five years is typical. A softened water supply can chew through a magnesium rod in two. A new rod costs a fraction of a tank and often adds years.

    Gas Venting Tune-Up in Dublin, CA

    Atmospheric gas tanks vent by draft alone, so the vent connector needs upward slope of at least a quarter inch per foot and tight joints with three screws each. Draft at the hood gets checked first. Lint on the flame arrestor of an FVIR tank can starve the burner and kill the pilot. We vacuum it clean.
