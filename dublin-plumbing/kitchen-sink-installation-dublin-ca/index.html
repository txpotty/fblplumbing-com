    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Dublin

    Undermount sinks need strong clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone, and support it while the adhesive sets. Then we install strainers, traps and supply lines. A proper undermount install prevents gaps and leaks along the counter edge.

    Under-mount Sink Installation in Dublin, CA

    Under-mount sinks provide a sleek, seamless look, making countertop cleaning easier. Their installation is more complex than top-mount sinks, requiring precise measurements and strong adhesive or clips for secure attachment to the underside of the counter. FBL Plumbing excels in under-mount sink installation, ensuring a robust bond and a perfectly sealed edge to prevent water infiltration. We confirm the cutout is smooth and the sink is level for optimal drainage.

    Top-mount Sink Installation in Dublin

    Top-mount, or drop-in, sinks are a common and straightforward option for many Northern California homes. While simpler to install, proper sealing and alignment are still important to prevent water damage and ensure stability. Our plumbers in Dublin efficiently install top-mount sinks, applying durable sealant around the rim to create a watertight barrier. We make sure the sink is securely fastened and all plumbing connections, including the faucet and drain, are properly tightened and tested for leaks.

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Dublin

    Drop-in sinks rest on the countertop with a rim, making them a good choice for laminate and tile. We check the cutout size, seal the rim, and tighten mounting clips evenly. This is often the fastest and most affordable kitchen sink upgrade. It also works well in rentals and older homes.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Dublin

    Double bowl sinks need a continuous waste or end outlet layout to drain both sides through one trap. We plan the layout around your disposal and dishwasher connections. Proper slope keeps both sides draining freely. We use heavy-wall fittings that resist leaks.
