    New Sump Pit Installation in Dublin

    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can create one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, hardpan or rock and set a durable basin.     Primary Sump Pump Installation in Dublin

    A primary sump pump is your first defense against basement flooding. We install sturdy, electrically powered pumps designed to automatically activate when water levels rise in the sump pit.     Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Dublin

    Submersible sump pumps sit directly inside the sump pit, running quietly and taking up less space. They are usually more powerful and durable, making them a popular choice for many homeowners.     Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Dublin, CA

    Severe storms can knock out power right when your pump is needed most. Battery backup pumps take over automatically when the main pump fails or power goes out. We install backup systems with sealed batteries and charging units. Many include alarms that warn you about problems. Backups add an extra layer of protection for finished basements.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Dublin, CA

    Sump pumps often sit idle for long stretches between storms, which can lead to stuck floats and seized motors. We replace old pumps with new models and inspect the pit, check valve and discharge line. Upgrading to a higher capacity pump may help if your old one could not keep up. We test the new pump by filling the pit with water. Replacement before storm season is a smart move.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Dublin, CA

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and repair. They are often a good choice for shallow sump pits or areas where space is tight. Our team is experienced in installing these pumps, ensuring proper pit depth and discharge line setup for optimal performance in Dublin, CA.
