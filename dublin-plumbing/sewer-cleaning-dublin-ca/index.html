    Jetting a Sewer Line in Dublin, CA

    Grease and scale are jetting work. Cables leave them behind. The rear jets drive the hose forward while the spray scours the wall, so the line ends up much closer to its original size.

    Dealing with Grease Buildup in Dublin, CA

    Grease hardens as it cools. It shrinks the opening a little every week. Kitchen grease coats the sewer line the same way plaque coats an artery, and over a few years the opening can close to the size of a finger, which is when drains start to back up, and jetting is the right fix.

    Shop and Restaurant Sewers in Dublin

    Busy buildings clog more often. More use means more buildup. We bring larger jetters for 4 and 6 inch commercial lines and can work before opening or after closing, so the cleaning does not shut down a lunch rush or a full day of business.
