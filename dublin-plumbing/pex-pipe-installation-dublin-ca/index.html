    What Goes Into a PEX Job in Dublin, CA

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Dublin, CA

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. All three are code-listed for drinking water. Pipe, fittings and tools come from the same maker on our jobs, which keeps the warranty valid.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Dublin, CA

    Gray polybutylene went into millions of homes from the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. Chlorinated water breaks the pipe and its plastic fittings down from within, which means it can look perfectly fine until a fitting suddenly fails. Many insurers ask whether you have it. Replacing it with PEX can make the house easier to insure and sell.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Dublin, CA

    With PEX-A, the tech slips a ring over the pipe end and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting goes in while the plastic is still expanded, and the material's memory pulls it back down around the barb, sealing harder over the first minute. In the cold, it takes longer to shrink back. We let joints set before testing.

    Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Dublin

    Rough-in heights matter more than people think. A shower valve set two inches too low or a vanity stub that misses the cabinet back means opening the wall again after the tile is up, which is expensive and slow. We confirm heights with your contractor first. Then we pressure test with the walls open.

    Whole-Home PEX Repiping in Dublin

    Low pressure at every faucet, across the whole house, usually points at the pipe and not the fixtures. In older homes it is rust-choked galvanized or polybutylene with failing fittings, and swapping a faucet cartridge will not help. A whole-home repipe will. We replace every supply line from the main valve to each angle stop and test the system before closing anything.

    Emergency PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Dublin, CA

    PEX rarely fails on its own. When it does, the cause is usually a nail or screw driven through it, a rodent chewing on an attic run, or a fitting that was never crimped all the way. Our trucks stock half-inch to one-inch pipe and fittings. Most emergency PEX repairs in Dublin, CA are a cut-and-splice job that takes under an hour once the drywall is open.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Dublin

    PEX-B is stiffer, costs less per foot, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. Since a crimp is only as good as the tool that made it, every ring gets a go/no-go gauge check, and any ring that fails gets cut off and redone. That takes seconds. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in Dublin, CA

    Price on a repipe comes down to a few things: bathroom count, square footage, how many stories, and how easy the attic and walls are to work in. PEX usually beats copper on price because the pipe costs less and a crew finishes in two to four days, not a full week. Drywall patching may be separate. Ask and we will price all three, PEX-A, PEX-B and copper, side by side.
