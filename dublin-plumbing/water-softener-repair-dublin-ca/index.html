    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Dublin, CA

    Control Valve Rebuild in Dublin

    Service, backwash, brine and rinse are the four cycles the control valve directs every time the softener regenerates, usually in the middle of the night. Over the years, seals, spacers and pistons wear down. Leaks at the valve or water running to the drain are common signs. A matching rebuild kit fixes it.

    Water Softener Leak Repair in Dublin, CA

    Drips around fittings waste water. Our plumbers find the source, whether seals, O-rings, hoses or internal parts. We replace the failed part with silicone-lubricated O-rings rated for potable water in your Dublin, CA home and test it under line pressure.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Dublin

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. An old timer head regenerates by the calendar, used or not. We retest hardness and reprogram the valve, or upgrade the head to a metered control that only regenerates when the resin is actually close to exhausted. Salt bills drop.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Dublin

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads so they cannot grab hardness. We test the resin, clean it with a resin cleaner or replace it. Fresh resin brings capacity back.

    Water Softener Leak Detection and Repair in Dublin, CA

    Cracked tanks, loose fittings and failed O-rings are behind most softener leaks. A small drip still wastes water and damages flooring. Once we find the source, from the bypass to the drain connection, the failed seal or fitting gets replaced. We test it under pressure.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Dublin, CA

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Its small ports clog with sediment and salt. A clogged injector leaves the brine tank full of water and the resin never gets recharged. We clean or replace it and check the brine line for air leaks.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Dublin, CA

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. Even a small drip wastes water and can stain a garage floor or rot a cabinet. O-rings and fittings get replaced and pressure tested. Mineral tank cracks cannot be fixed.
