    Water Line Installation and Replacement in Dublin

    Corroded galvanized and brittle polybutylene are the two pipes we replace most. New lines go in as PEX or Type L copper, with crimp or expansion fittings on the plastic and solder on the copper. Many older homes here sit on raised foundations with crawlspaces, which make supply and drain repairs easier to reach than slab work. Buried pipe gets sand bedding and proper depth, so rocks and settling do not cut into it later. Above 80 psi, we add a pressure reducing valve.

    Sump Pump and Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Dublin

    Basements and crawl spaces that take on water need a sump pump, and fixtures below the sewer line need an ejector. One moves groundwater, the other moves sewage, and they are not interchangeable. We size before we sell. The pump gets matched to the fill rate, wired to a dedicated circuit and piped out through a check valve.

    Whole-House Water Softener Installation in Dublin

    Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running hard. Softener sizing comes down to hardness times daily gallons, which tells us how many grains the resin has to remove between regenerations. Undersize it and it regenerates all the time, burning through salt. The drain line gets a proper air gap. Then we program the regeneration schedule to your water and run a test cycle.

    Water Heater Replacement in Dublin

    Most tank heaters give out between 8 and 12 years, sooner with heavy sediment. Our stock covers 40 and 50 gallon tanks and 75 gallon units for larger families, and each install includes new connectors, a code relief line and a vent check. We also check whether you need an expansion tank. One visit covers most installs.

    Expansion Tank and Sewage Ejector Installs in Dublin, CA

    A closed water system has nowhere for heated water to expand, so pressure climbs every single time the heater cycles. An expansion tank absorbs that swing and keeps the relief valve from dripping. A PRV usually means you need one. Fixtures below the sewer line get an ejector system with a sealed basin, a check valve and a vent.

    Sump Pump Installation in Dublin, CA

    A sump pump that fails during a storm usually failed months earlier: a stuck float, a seized impeller, a dead check valve or a cord unplugged for the shop vac. We dig the pit to the right depth, set a primary pump sized to the inflow and add a battery backup if you want one. Where the water exits matters most. We run the discharge line far enough from the foundation that the water does not cycle straight back into the pit.

    PEX Pipe Installation and Repiping in Dublin

    Pinhole leaks in copper keep coming back once the pipe walls start to thin. A PEX repipe ends that cycle. We run either a manifold with home runs to each fixture, which balances pressure, or a trunk-and-branch layout when the walls make home runs impractical. We cut small, clean access holes and patch them, or hand off to your drywall contractor if you prefer.

    PEX Repiping in Dublin

    Copper repipes cost more and take longer, which is why most whole-house repipes today use PEX-A or PEX-B tubing with brass or poly fittings. It runs quieter than copper, with less water hammer banging in the walls. It also handles a freeze better. Freezing is uncommon along the coast and on valley floors in Northern California, but frosty nights do happen and can crack exposed outdoor pipes. Fittings are matched to the tubing manufacturer.
