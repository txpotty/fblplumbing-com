    Ejector Pump Replacement in Dublin

    Ejector pumps usually last 7 to 10 years, depending on how often they cycle and what gets flushed. Watch for constant running, a hum or grind, repeated alarms and odor at the pit, since any of them means trouble is close. The float usually goes first.

    Ejector Pump Installation Cost Comparison in Dublin, CA

    Replacing a pump in an existing basin is the least expensive option, often done in a few hours. A full new system with basin, floor excavation, roof vent, electrical and alarm costs more, but it is usually the only way to add a below-sewer bath besides a macerator. We price both options.

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Dublin, CA

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars produce gray water that still needs to be lifted to the sewer. A compact gray water pump handles it, sized for the 15 or more gallons a washer drops at once, with a lint filter to keep fibers off the impeller. Filters save impellers.

    Residential Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Dublin

    Residential ejector systems in Dublin serve basement bathrooms, lower-level suites and any fixture that sits too low to drain by gravity to the sewer. The setup is a sealed basin, a pump, a float switch, a check valve and a vent. Simple parts, exact install. Install mistakes cause more trouble than hardware, so the vent and lid seal get extra attention.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin Installation in Dublin, CA

    The basin holds everything, and a 30-gallon size is common for a single basement bathroom. Bigger basins let the pump run longer, fewer times a day, which is easier on the motor and switch, so we size up when the pit serves a laundry or several fixtures. Every lid gets sealed.

    Upflush and Macerating Toilet Systems in Dublin

    Macerating systems like Saniflo add a bath fixture or two without breaking the concrete floor. The unit sits behind the toilet, grinds the waste and pumps it through a small discharge line, often three-quarter inch or one inch, up to the nearest drain, which makes it a good fit for a small basement half bath. They need a vent too.

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Dublin

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. Sump pumps push groundwater out to the yard, ejectors push sewage to the sewer, and only the ejector pit must be sealed and vented against gas. They are not interchangeable.
