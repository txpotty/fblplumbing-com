    Video Camera Drain Inspection in Dublin

    A camera on a push rod shows the inside of the pipe in real time. On a recurring clog, it is often the quickest way to stop guessing. Roots at a joint, a lost toy, a cracked hub or a low spot holding water all show up clearly on screen. You can watch the screen alongside the tech.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Dublin, CA

    Jetting puts water through a nozzle at 3,000 to 4,000 psi and scrubs the pipe wall clean. It strips grease, scale and root hair that a cable slides past. Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running hard. The pipe gets a camera check first. Pressure that high can damage cracked clay or thin cast iron, so we jet only after we have looked at the pipe.

    Kitchen Line Rooter Service in Dublin, CA
