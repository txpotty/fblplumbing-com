    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Dublin

    Tankless units need descaling. Hard water scales the heat exchanger until the unit runs lukewarm or shuts off mid-shower. A small pump circulates descaler. The inlet filter, venting and gas line get checked too.

    Sump Pump Testing in Dublin, CA

    Basements flood when the pump fails during a storm. Drought years alternate with very wet years here, so conservation rules and efficient fixtures are a constant part of plumbing decisions. We pour water into the pit to make sure the float lifts and the pump kicks on, check the discharge line for clogs or freezing risk, and inspect the check valve so water does not drain back in. Battery backups get load tested.

    Hot Water Tank Service in Dublin, CA

    Gas and electric tanks need different checks. Gas tanks get a burner, flue and leak check, and electric tanks get element and thermostat tests. Sediment comes out either way. Scale on the tank floor wastes energy and shortens tank life.

    Softener Cleaning and Testing in Dublin

    Hard water scale builds in pipes and appliances fast. Brine, injector, resin and regeneration all get checked, and we test the water at a tap. Fouled resin can be cleaned. Older resin may need replacing.

    Slab Leak and Hidden Leak Checks in Dublin

    Copper became standard in many mid-century homes here, and decades later pinhole leaks and corroded fittings are a common repair call. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried lines until they leak out of sight for months. Warm floors are a clue. A spinning meter is another. Acoustic and thermal gear pinpoint it.

    Popping or rumbling means sediment. Minerals settle on the tank bottom, trap heat and cause the popping you hear when it fires. Flushing clears it. Tankless units get a descaling flush through the service valves with a pump and descaler. Regular service helps keep the warranty valid.
