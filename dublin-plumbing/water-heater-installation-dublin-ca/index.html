    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Dublin

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, particularly in closed-loop systems. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to support proper system operation and prevent premature wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Dublin.     Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Dublin

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon tank water heater usually costs $1,600 to $3,500 installed. Tankless installations often cost $3,000 to $6,500 depending on gas line and venting needs.

    FBL Plumbing installs all major trusted brands of water heaters, including Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz. We work with both gas and electric models, as well as various sizes and efficiencies. Our team can help you choose the best brand and model to suit your hot water demands and budget.

    Most households of two to four people do well with a 40 or 50 gallon tank. Larger families may need a 75 gallon tank or a tankless unit sized by flow rate.

    Many tank water heaters last 8 to 12 years, though hard water can shorten that. Regular flushing and anode rod checks help extend life.

    Common signs that you might need a new water heater include a lack of hot water, rusty or discolored water coming from the hot tap, banging or rumbling noises from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit and an age of 10 to 15 years or more. If you notice any of these issues, it is best to have a professional inspection.

    The decision to repair or replace often depends on the age of your unit, the cost of the repair and its energy efficiency. If your water heater is over 10 years old, frequently breaks down, or the repair cost is more than 50% of a new unit, replacement is often the more economical choice. Newer models also offer significantly better energy efficiency.

    Rusty water, leaks around the base, rumbling noises and running out of hot water quickly are common signs. If your heater is over 10 years old, replacement is often the smart move.

    New water heaters usually come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and components, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. FBL Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work, giving you peace of mind. We will discuss specific warranty details for your chosen unit during the consultation.
