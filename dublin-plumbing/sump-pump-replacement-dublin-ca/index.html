    Sump Pump Types We Replace in Dublin, CA

    Light Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Dublin, CA

    Light commercial work, think retail basements and small office buildings, usually means a 1/2 HP or larger cast iron pump with a 2-inch discharge and a panel alarm. We match the old pump or step it up. Sewage ejectors and grinder pumps we quote separately.

    Wayne Sump Pump Swaps in Dublin

    Wayne makes everything from a thermoplastic quarter-horse pump at the hardware store to cast iron models with vertical switches, and the cheap ones are what we replace most. Most dead Wayne pumps we see have a cooked motor or a float tangled in the power cord. You get a matching Wayne or a sturdier cast iron model, your call. Takes about two hours.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Dublin, CA

    A pit that is filling with the pump silent means you have maybe an hour or two before water crosses the basement floor, depending on how fast it is coming in. Look at the breaker and the GFCI outlet, then pull the lid and check the float for a hang-up. A wet vac buys time. Our trucks carry common sizes, so most emergency calls end with a new pump running the same visit.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Dublin, CA

    Zoeller cast iron pumps, the M53 and M63 in particular, are what we pull off the truck most often because they take heat and hard cycling better than plastic housings. An existing Zoeller can usually be swapped for a current model with the same 1.5-inch outlet and float style. Pump not keeping up? We can move you up to a half-horse model like the M98.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Dublin

    Strong winter storms can bring days of heavy rain to Northern California, saturating ground and pushing water into crawlspaces and sewer lines. A standard pump just sits there once the power drops. We add a 12-volt backup pump on its own float, mounted a few inches above the primary, with a deep-cycle battery and a charger that tests itself. It also covers a stuck primary switch. The alarm will tell you which one is running.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Dublin, CA

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. They are louder, no question. We set the new column plumb, secure it so vibration does not walk it across the basin, and adjust the float rod stops for the cycle you want. They fit pits as narrow as 10 inches.

    Pedestal Pumps for Shallow Pits in Dublin, CA

    Older basements sometimes have a narrow, shallow pit poured right into the floor, and a pedestal pump is often the only thing that fits without breaking concrete. We replace the pump and float rod and rerun the PVC up to the check valve. Cheaper than a submersible, too.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Dublin, CA

    Combination units put a primary AC pump and a DC backup on one discharge manifold, so you get two pumps and two floats in a single assembly. They suit pits too tight for two separate pumps. We replace the whole assembly or just the failed side.
