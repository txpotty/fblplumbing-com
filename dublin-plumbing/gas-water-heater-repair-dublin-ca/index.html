    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Dublin, CA

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Dublin

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it senses vapors or overheating.
