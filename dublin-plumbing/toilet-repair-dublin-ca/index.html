    Toilet Leaking Through the Ceiling in Dublin, CA

    Water coming through the ceiling under an upstairs bathroom usually traces back to a failed wax ring or a cracked supply nut, and the drywall below holds the evidence.     Wall-Hung Toilet Repair in Dublin

    Restaurant restrooms get the hardest use we see, and wall-hung bowls there can loosen on their carriers until the seal leaks behind the wall. The fix is to tighten or rebuild the carrier, then reseal the bowl. That one needs care. A loose wall-hung bowl can crack the china if it is ignored.

    After-Hours Toilet Repair in Dublin, CA

    Shutting off the angle stop buys you time, but an old multi-turn stop valve can crumble or leak at the stem when it has not been moved in fifteen years. Do not force it.     Clogged Toilet Clearing in Dublin, CA

    Most clogs sit in the trapway, the curved channel cast into the porcelain, and a closet auger with a rubber-sleeved head clears them without scratching the bowl. Plungers miss some.     Toilet Flush Repair in Dublin, CA

    Two or three tries to get a full flush points to a flapper closing too early, a chain hooked in the wrong hole, scale in the rim jets, or a cracked overflow tube letting water bypass the bowl. You can usually see which one with the lid off. We watch a full cycle with the lid off and replace the flush valve if the overflow tube is cracked, since it cannot be patched.

    Sensor and Flushometer Repair in Dublin

    Sensor flush valves fail in their own ways, from dead batteries and dirty sensor eyes to solenoids that stick open and keep a bowl running. Before replacing parts, we test the sensor and the solenoid. Batteries are the cheap fix. If the valve flushes on its own, the sensor range or a dirty lens is usually to blame.

    Running Toilet Repair in Dublin
