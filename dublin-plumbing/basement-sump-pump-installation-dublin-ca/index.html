    Commercial and Multi-Unit Sump Installations in Dublin

    Office buildings, parking structures and multi-unit properties with below-grade levels usually need duplex sump systems with two pumps and an alternating control panel. The panel switches lead and lag pumps on each cycle so wear stays even, and a high water alarm alerts building staff. We coordinate electrical with your electrician and follow manufacturer specs for basin size and float settings. Property managers get a clear maintenance schedule to keep both pumps exercised.

    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Dublin

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a good fit for narrow pits in older homes. They usually last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we usually recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod adjusted to your pit depth.

    Sump Pump Basin and Discharge Line Installation in Dublin

    A complete sump pump system needs a properly installed basin and discharge line. We handle the excavation and placement of the sump basin, setting it at the lowest point of your basement floor so it collects water effectively. We also install the discharge line, making sure it is correctly sloped, sealed and routed away from your foundation to a suitable drainage area, so water does not find its way back into your Dublin property.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Dublin, CA

    Power outages are common, especially during severe weather, and they leave your primary sump pump useless. We offer and install battery backup sump pump systems that switch on automatically if the power goes out, providing continuous protection for your basement in Dublin, CA. Drought years alternate with very wet years here, so conservation rules and efficient fixtures are a constant part of plumbing decisions. These systems include a deep cycle battery and a secondary pump, so your basement stays dry even when the electricity fails.

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, designed to handle everyday water removal.
