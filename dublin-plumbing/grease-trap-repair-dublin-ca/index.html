    Damaged Baffle Repair in Dublin

    Baffles are the walls inside the trap that slow the water down long enough for grease to float and solids to sink. Once one cracks or falls loose, the trap is just a box that grease passes through. We pump it out and pull the old baffle. On plastic units we order the factory part for that model number. Steel traps with a rusted-out baffle may need a fabricated piece, and sometimes a new trap is the cheaper answer.

    Commercial Grease Trap Repair in Dublin

    Restaurants, school cafeterias, hospital kitchens and bakeries all push more grease than a small trap can hold. We see undersized traps fail in the same few places every time. The inlet tee clogs, the outlet tee gets coated, and grease starts showing up in the sewer manhole down the street, which is usually how the city finds out before the owner does. We fix the failed parts and tell you plainly if the trap is too small for the kitchen.

    Grease Trap Repair vs Replacement Cost in Dublin, CA

    Age and material decide most repair versus replace calls. A 25-year-old steel trap with rust holes is done. A plastic or fiberglass trap with a broken baffle is usually worth fixing, and a concrete interceptor with sound walls and a bad riser almost always is, since replacing a buried tank means excavation and several days closed. You get repair and replacement priced side by side, in writing.

    Grease Line Clog Repair in Dublin, CA

    Lines downstream of the trap should stay clean if the trap is working. Grease past the outlet is a red flag. We jet the downstream run to the property line or the sewer tap, then camera it to confirm the pipe is clear and to document any cracked or sagging sections for a repair quote. If we only clear the line and skip the trap, it clogs again.

    Commercial Grease Interceptor Repair in Dublin, CA

    In-ground gravity interceptors run from about 500 gallons to several thousand, usually precast concrete with two compartments. Hydrogen sulfide from the waste eats the concrete above the waterline over time. We pump the tank, inspect the walls and the crossover, and repair spalled concrete with a cementitious patch or an epoxy liner rated for that service. Nobody goes in without a gas monitor, a tripod and a second person topside.

    Grease Trap Drain Line Repair in Dublin

    Sometimes the trap is not the problem. Lines between the sinks and the trap clog first, because that is where hot water cools. We put a camera in from the nearest cleanout and record the run, looking for sags, offset joints, roots in clay sections downstream, or a belly where grease collects every week. Jetting a belly buys time, but only regrading the pipe fixes it.

    Clogged Grease Trap Repair in Dublin

    Hardened grease in the inlet tee is the usual culprit when a trap backs up. The fix starts with pumping the trap down and jetting the inlet line until it flows again. Solids packed against the baffle come out by hand, with a scoop and a bucket. Then we look for the reason it clogged, such as a dishwasher dumping too much hot water at once or a missing sink strainer upstream. A clog that keeps coming back nearly always has a cause you can see.
