    What We Fix Under the Sink in Dublin, CA

    Cracked or Loose Basin Repair in Dublin

    Undermount clips let go more often than people think. When the epoxy under the counter fails, the bowl drops a quarter inch, the caulk line splits, and water from the counter runs straight down into the cabinet. New clips go in and the bowl gets pulled tight. Hairline cracks in porcelain near the drain mean replacement.

    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in Dublin, CA

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We look past the wall with a longer cable or a camera for a sag or for buildup where the sink line meets the tub. Now and then it is a plugged vent. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Dublin, CA

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. After five to ten years the core can split without warning, and old multi-turn stops often seize open. Lines and stops get replaced together. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Kitchen Sink Repair in Dublin

    Kitchen sinks take the most abuse in the house. Basket strainers loosen and leak around the putty, pull-down sprayer hoses split inside the cabinet, and the disposal flange works loose from years of vibration. The wet part is the one we fix. A cracked composite or porcelain bowl is a replacement, and we will say so.

    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Dublin, CA

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. In kitchens it is cold grease and food, and in bathrooms it is hair knotted on the stopper and glued with soap scum. A hand auger does most of the work. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.

    Office and Restaurant Sink Repair in Dublin

    Office breakroom sinks get abused in ways a home sink never sees. Coffee grounds and paper towels go down the drain all day, and the trap behind the cabinet door may not get looked at until it overflows onto the carpet. A strainer basket goes in after we clear it. For restaurants, we keep three-bowl sinks running between the lunch and dinner rush.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Dublin, CA

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Code calls for a trap seal between 2 and 4 inches, and a trap that leaks, sags or was cut down by a past owner can lose that seal and let the smell in. Bad traps get a new matched kit. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.

    Low Water Pressure at the Sink in Dublin, CA

    Unscrew the aerator before anything else. Most single-faucet pressure problems are a packed aerator screen, and after that we look at the cartridge and the angle stop for debris. Whole-house low pressure is another call. We test the pressure regulator in that case.

    Sink Repair Near Me in Dublin, CA

    A local plumber knows the houses. The older parts of town have galvanized stubs and chrome traps, and the newer parts have PEX with plastic traps, so we bring parts for both. That cuts out a supply-house run.

    Some sink problems look local but are not. A gurgling sink can mean a blocked roof vent, and a sink that backs up when the washer drains points to a clog in the shared line further down. We check before we quote.
