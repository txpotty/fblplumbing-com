    Slab Leak Repair for PEX and Copper Lines in Yuba City, CA

    Copper and PEX fail in different ways under a slab. Copper tends to pinhole. Copper wears through at rub points or from water chemistry, and PEX usually leaks at a poorly crimped fitting or where a rodent got to it. Trucks in Yuba City, CA stock fittings for each type.

    Slab Leak Detection in Yuba City

    The first test is the water meter. We shut off everything first. If the meter still turns, we close the water heater inlet and pressure test hot and cold separately, then walk the losing line with a ground mic until the hiss is loudest. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor.

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Yuba City, CA

    Leaks under a load-bearing wall or a kitchen island are hard to reach from inside. Tunneling solves that. From a pit by the foundation, the crew hand-digs under the slab, shoring as they go, and your floors and cabinets never get touched. Many Yuba City, CA homes are good candidates.

    Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Yuba City
