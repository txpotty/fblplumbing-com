    Why Polybutylene Fails in Yuba City

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. Heat makes it worse. Hot lines and fittings near the water heater fail more than anything else.
