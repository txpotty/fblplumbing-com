    Water-Only Drain Cleaning in Yuba City, CA

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Chemical cleaners can damage pipe. Jetting removes the buildup physically, and the debris flushes to the sewer or septic tank as normal waste, so there is nothing left in the pipe to eat at joints or harm the bacteria a septic system depends on.

    Hard Water Scale Removal in Yuba City, CA

    Scale builds slowly inside drain pipe, narrowing it a little more each year. Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running mineral-heavy. A jetter with a scouring nozzle strips that scale from the walls, and on heavily scaled cast iron we may follow up with a descaling chain head to finish the job. The difference shows on camera.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydrojetting in Yuba City

    Roots find their way into sewer lines through cracked joints, and older clay pipe has a joint every few feet. A root-cutting nozzle spins through the mass. The jet then flushes the debris to the main, and the camera marks the joints that let the roots in.

    Sewer Lateral Jetting in Yuba City, CA

    The lateral from your house to the street carries everything, so buildup there causes backups everywhere at once. We jet it from the cleanout to the tap. Low spots where the pipe has settled hold water and collect sludge, so we spend extra passes there and mark the location on the camera footage in case a repair makes sense later.

    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Yuba City

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. A cable cuts a channel through it. A spinning nozzle removes the grease all around the pipe so it flushes away cleanly.
