    Gas Appliance Connector Repair in Yuba City, CA

    Weak flames or a smell near one appliance often point to the connector or shutoff behind it. Older uncoated brass connectors can crack at the flare nut, and they are no longer accepted by most codes or utilities. We replace them with coated stainless connectors. We also add a reachable shutoff and a drip leg where needed, and confirm the new connector is rated for that appliance's BTU input.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Yuba City

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Earthquakes and hillside soil movement are real risks in Northern California, so flexible connections and seismic straps help protect plumbing from sudden movement. Rusted buried steel gets replaced with polyethylene and tracer wire, and we use a listed transition riser where it rises to the house. Exposed sections get coated steel.     Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Yuba City

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. We shut off every appliance and watch the meter's test dial, then close branch valves one at a time to find which section is leaking. An electronic detector does the rest. A spray of soap solution on the fitting shows bubbles at the leak, and we fix that joint or section and retest the whole system.

    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Yuba City, CA

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. New and repaired lines get tested, buried to the required depth and laid with tracer wire.

    If you smell gas, leave the building, skip the light switches and call your gas utility or 911 from outside. Then call us.     Propane Gas Line Service in Yuba City, CA

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. Our work covers propane yard lines, regulator replacement and fitting repairs, plus a check that every appliance has its propane orifices installed. Wrong orifices make a burner run dirty and unsafe. We test the system the same way we test natural gas piping.

    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Yuba City

We soap-test the repaired joint and pressure test the system before leaving.
