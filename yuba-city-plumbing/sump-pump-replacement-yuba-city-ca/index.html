    Choosing a Backup Battery in Yuba City

    Battery choice decides how long the backup actually runs. A sealed AGM or deep-cycle marine battery in the 75 to 100 amp-hour range will typically handle several hours of intermittent pumping during an outage.     Pedestal Pump Service in Yuba City, CA

    Louder, yes, but a pedestal motor sits high and dry, so in a narrow pit it often outlasts a submersible by several years. Narrow pits favor pedestals. We replace worn units and set the float rod stops for a proper cycle. A submersible is worth a look when the pit is wide enough.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Yuba City, CA

    Zoeller cast iron pumps, the M53 and M63 in particular, are what we pull off the truck most often because they take heat and hard cycling better than plastic housings. An existing Zoeller can usually be swapped for a current model with the same 1.5-inch outlet and float style. Pump not keeping up? The M98 steps up to half horsepower.

    Sump Basin and Pit Replacement in Yuba City, CA

    Old clay tile or 18-inch plastic basins crack and fill with silt, and a pump in a basin that small cycles every few minutes during a heavy rain. We break out the concrete, set a larger poly basin with a sealed lid, and pour the floor back. A bigger basin means longer pump cycles. Fewer starts mean the motor lasts longer.

    Sizing a Submersible Sump Pump in Yuba City

    Horsepower matters less than people think. A third-horse pump moves plenty of water for most homes, as long as the vertical lift to the discharge is under about 15 feet. Before we choose, we read the pump curve at your real discharge height. Oversize it and it short cycles.

    Zoeller Switch and Pump Repair in Yuba City

    Zoeller units with a piggyback float plug the pump cord into the switch cord, and if that piggyback plug fails the pump never gets power. We test that first. Sometimes a new switch is all you need, and sometimes the motor windings are shot. We tell you which before we sell you anything.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Yuba City

    Tree roots seek out sewer laterals during dry spells, which is why heavy rains often reveal damaged lines through sudden backups. A primary pump on household current does nothing once the breaker panel goes dark. We add a 12-volt backup pump on its own float, mounted a few inches above the primary, with a deep-cycle battery and a charger that tests itself. It also covers a stuck primary switch. A high-water alarm lets you know the backup has taken over.

    Light commercial work, think retail basements and small office buildings, usually means a 1/2 HP or larger cast iron pump with a 2-inch discharge and a panel alarm. We replace like for like or upsize. Sewage ejectors and grinder pumps we quote separately.
