    Pressure Switch Replacement in Yuba City

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, a clogged sensing tube and ant nests are the usual reasons one fails. After swapping the switch and clearing the tube, we set cut-in and cut-out to match the tank, usually 40 and 60 psi. One of the cheaper well repairs.

    Control Box and Surge Repair in Yuba City, CA

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. Drought years alternate with very wet years here, so conservation rules and efficient fixtures are a constant part of plumbing decisions. Surges from lightning are the usual cause of control box failures. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection, replace what failed and recommend a surge arrestor at the box if one is missing. Arrestors are cheap.

    Above-Ground Jet Pump Repair in Yuba City

    Lost prime is the number one jet pump complaint. Shaft seal leaks and worn impellers come next. The pump gets reprimed and resealed, and the foot valve and suction line get checked, because a single air leak will undo the work. Everything is above ground.

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. Fast cycling kills pumps young. The air charge gets checked and the bladder inspected, then the tank is recharged or replaced so the pump runs a full minute per cycle. Steadier pressure follows.
