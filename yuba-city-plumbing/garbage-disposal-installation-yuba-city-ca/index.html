    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Installation in Yuba City

    The cost for garbage disposal installation in Yuba City, CA typically ranges from $200 to $500, not including the unit itself.     Most installs take one to two hours. Adding electrical or reworking drains can take longer.

    In most cases, a direct replacement of an existing garbage disposal in Yuba City does not require a permit. However, new installations that involve significant changes to plumbing or electrical wiring may require one to ensure compliance with local building codes. FBL Plumbing can advise you on permit requirements.

    A 1/3 or 1/2 HP disposal suits light use, while 3/4 to 1 HP units are better for families and frequent cooking.

    For light use or single-person households, a 1/3 or 1/2 horsepower unit might suffice. For average families, a 3/4 horsepower unit is a good balance of power and cost. Large families or those who cook frequently might benefit from a 1 horsepower disposal to handle more food waste effectively without jamming.

    Continuous feed disposals operate with a wall switch and can run while you're adding food waste. Batch feed disposals operate only when a special stopper is inserted, making them a popular choice for safety reasons, especially in homes with young children. We install both types, based on your preference.

    If your sink area in Yuba City lacks a dedicated electrical outlet for a garbage disposal, a licensed electrician can install one safely and to code. This is a common requirement for new installations in older homes and makes sure your new unit operates correctly and safely.

    To maintain your new garbage disposal, always run cold water before, during, and after grinding food waste. Avoid putting hard items like bones, fibrous vegetables, or grease down the drain. Now and then, grind citrus peels or ice cubes to help clean the blades and freshen the unit.
