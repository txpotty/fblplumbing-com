    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in San Francisco

    Salt-based (ion exchange) water softeners are the most common and effective solution for truly softening water. These systems use a resin bed to exchange hard minerals like calcium and magnesium with sodium ions. FBL Plumbing expertly installs many models of salt-based softeners, ensuring proper connection to your main water line, correct drainage setup and accurate programming for regeneration cycles in your San Francisco home.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in San Francisco

    For homeowners looking for an alternative to traditional softeners, we also offer salt-free water conditioner installation. These systems condition the water to prevent scale buildup without removing beneficial minerals or adding salt. While not true softeners, they are an excellent option for scale prevention and are popular in many San Francisco households.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in San Francisco
