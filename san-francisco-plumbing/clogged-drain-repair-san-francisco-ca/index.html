    Frequently Asked Questions About Clogged Drain Repair in San Francisco

    The cost of clogged drain repair in San Francisco, CA can range from $150 to $500 for a standard clog, depending on severity and location. More complex issues like main line clogs requiring hydro-jetting or camera inspection might range from $400 to $1000 or more. We provide transparent, upfront pricing after a thorough diagnosis.

    Common signs of a clogged drain include slow drainage, gurgling sounds from drains or toilets, foul odors coming from your pipes, and water backing up into sinks, tubs, or showers. If multiple drains are affected, it often points to a main line issue.

    Yes. We offer evening and weekend drain service for urgent clogs. Call and we will give you the next available arrival time and any after-hours rate up front.

    Drain snaking uses a flexible auger to physically break through or pull out blockages. Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the inside of pipes, effectively removing scale, grease, and roots, providing a much more complete cleaning. Hydro-jetting is often used for tough clogs or preventative maintenance.

    A plunger or a hand-held hair snake can handle many simple tub and sink clogs. If the clog returns, affects more than one fixture or does not respond to a plunger, call a plumber. Forcing a cable can crack old pipes or scratch fixtures.

    To prevent future clogs, avoid pouring grease down kitchen drains, use drain screens to catch hair and food particles, and flush only toilet paper. Regular professional drain maintenance, especially in areas with hard water, can also help limit mineral buildup.

    Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running hard. Hard water leaves mineral scale that narrows pipe walls and gives grease and hair something to grab. Older homes may also have cast iron pipes that rust and roughen inside. Both conditions make clogs form faster.

    While chemical drain cleaners are readily available, we generally advise against them. They often contain harsh chemicals that can corrode pipes, especially older ones, and can be harmful to your health and the environment. Professional plumbers use safer, more effective methods.
