    What Fails on a Disposal, and How We Fix It in San Francisco, CA

    Grinding Noise Repair in San Francisco

    A metal-on-metal grind may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. After checking the chamber, snugging the mount and pulling debris, we run it empty to hear if the noise is coming from the motor. Failing bearings are not worth fixing.

    Emergency Disposal Repair in San Francisco, CA

    Water on the floor beats every other problem. Turn the disposal off, stop the dishwasher if it is running, and close the angle stops under the sink so the faucet cannot add more water to a backed up bowl. Then call us. We can usually stop the leak the same day and either repair the unit or quote a replacement before we leave.

    Electrical Issues and Motor Repair in San Francisco

    A disposal that hums but will not grind has power but cannot turn, while one that is silent has no power at all. They are different problems. For a silent unit we test the breaker, reset and switch, and for a humming one we check the flywheel and motor before deciding if it is worth repairing.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in San Francisco

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A steady whine is different. A steady whine usually points to failing motor bearings, and the noise keeps building until the motor locks up. Most smells start at the splash guard. We pull it, clean the chamber walls and replace the guard if it is torn.

    Humming Disposal Repair in San Francisco

    Humming with no spin almost always means a jam. Bones, pits, glass and spoons are the usual suspects. With the power off, we free the flywheel using the hex socket underneath, pull the object, and test the motor and reset with water running.

    Noisy Disposal Repair in San Francisco

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough all point to something inside. Loose mounting rings rattle too. We check the mount first, then the chamber for debris, then listen to the motor with the chamber empty, because a motor that whines with nothing in it has bad bearings.

    InSinkErator and Other Brand Repairs in San Francisco, CA

    Every brand, from InSinkErator to Waste King and Moen, mounts differently, so a flange leak is fixed with that brand's kit. InSinkErator uses a three-bolt or Quick Lock style ring, while Waste King uses an EZ Mount twist ring that comes off without tools. We stock the common kits. It means most repairs need only one trip.
