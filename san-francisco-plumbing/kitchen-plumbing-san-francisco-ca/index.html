    Grease Trap Installation and Cleaning in San Francisco

    Grease traps are sized by flow, and a three-compartment sink typically calls for a trap rated at 20 gallons per minute or more.     New Disposal Installation in San Francisco

    Adding a disposal where there never was one means a new outlet or a switched circuit under the sink, and that part is electrical work. We plumb it and can coordinate the wiring.     Disposal Repair and Replacement in San Francisco, CA

    A disposal that hums but will not spin usually has something wedged in it.
