    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in San Francisco

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, especially in closed-loop systems. Plumbing work in Northern California follows the state plumbing code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code with state amendments. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to ensure proper system operation and prevent early wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in San Francisco. This small component offers real long-term protection.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in San Francisco

    The cost of water heater installation in San Francisco, CA typically ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the type of water heater (tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any necessary plumbing or electrical modifications. We provide a written, upfront quote after assessing your particular needs.

    To improve efficiency, ensure your new water heater is properly sized for your home, consider a tankless model for on-demand heating, insulate hot water pipes and set the thermostat to 120°F (49°C) to prevent scalding and cut energy consumption.

    New water heaters usually come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and components, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. FBL Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work, giving you peace of mind. We will discuss exact warranty details for your chosen unit during the consultation.

    The correct size water heater depends on the number of people in your household and your peak hot water demand. For tank units, a 40 to 50 gallon tank is common for 2 to 4 people, while larger families might need 60 to 80 gallons. For tankless units, it is about flow rate (GPM) and temperature rise. Our plumbers will assess your needs to recommend the right size.

    Rusty water, leaks around the base, rumbling noises and running out of hot water quickly are common signs. If your heater is over 10 years old, replacement is often the smart move.

    Many jurisdictions require a permit for water heater replacement. We pull permits when required and make sure the install meets code.

    Most standard water heater installations, particularly for replacing a similar tank unit, can be completed within 3 to 4 hours. However, a conversion from a tank to a tankless system, or installations requiring significant plumbing or electrical modifications, might take a full day or longer. We will provide an estimated timeline during our initial assessment.

    The average lifespan of a traditional tank water heater is about 10 to 15 years, while tankless water heaters can last 20 years or more with proper maintenance. Factors like local water quality, maintenance frequency and usage habits can all influence the actual lifespan of your unit. Regular flushing can help extend its life.
