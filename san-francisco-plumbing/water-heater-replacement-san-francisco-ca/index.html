    Upsizing Your Water Heater in San Francisco

    Running out of hot water often means the family has outgrown it.     Hybrid Water Heater Installation in San Francisco, CA

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need space and airflow, often a room of about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they work best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.

    Water Heater Replacement Cost Comparison in San Francisco, CA

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in San Francisco, CA

    Gas water heaters recover faster than electric, and in San Francisco, CA homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Every joint gets checked.

    Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith in San Francisco, CA
