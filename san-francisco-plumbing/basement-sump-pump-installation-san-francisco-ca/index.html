    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in San Francisco

    Pedestal sump pumps are an good choice for narrow or shallow sump pits, as their motor sits above the water level. This design makes them easier to maintain and can sometimes offer a longer lifespan because the motor stays out of the water. FBL Plumbing can properly install pedestal sump pumps in your San Francisco home, with the right sizing and setup for effective water removal without crowding your pit.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in San Francisco, CA

    Submersible sump pumps sit directly in the sump pit, submerged in water, which makes them quieter and less intrusive. They are powerful and effective for basements that take on a lot of water. Our team in San Francisco, CA will install your submersible pump with precision, making sure of proper sealing, discharge piping and electrical connections for quiet, reliable operation, which matters most in finished basements.
