    Why Choose FBL Plumbing for Grease Trap Cleaning in San Francisco, CA?

    A grease trap works by slowing the water down long enough for fat to float and food to sink. Past a quarter full, that stops. Grease rides straight through the outlet and coats the sewer lateral, where it cools into a hard white crust that a snake just punches a hole through.

    • Prevent Blockages, Regular pumping keeps floor sinks and floor drains flowing during the dinner rush.
    • Extended Lifespan, Less hydrogen sulfide in the tank means slower corrosion of concrete and baffles.
    • Odor Control, A pumped trap stops the rotten-egg odor that decaying grease gives off.

    Citywide Coverage

    School cafeterias and church kitchens around San Francisco usually have small traps that get forgotten for a year at a time. Those go on a schedule as well. A once-a-quarter pump-out is often enough for a kitchen that runs lunch five days a week and sits idle in summer.

    Experienced Team

    Techs ask the kitchen staff questions, because the cook knows more about what goes down the drain than anyone. Staff habits move the numbers. Plates scraped into the trash before the dish machine can add weeks between pump-outs, and the techs will mention it when they see a chance.

    Safety-First Approach

    An open interceptor hatch in a parking lot is a serious fall hazard. We set cones before lifting it. The lid stays off only as long as the hose is in the tank, and before we leave we check that it is seated flat and, on locking lids, bolted down.

    Odor Control and Trap Inspections in San Francisco

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Lines in San Francisco, CA

    A cable machine pokes a hole through grease. A jetter takes it off the pipe wall. We run a jetter at roughly 3,000 to 4,000 psi with a rotating nozzle that cuts the grease off the full circumference of the pipe and flushes it downstream. A camera pass afterward shows whether the line is really clean.

    Preventive Maintenance Programs in San Francisco, CA

    A maintenance plan is just a pumping interval plus someone who tracks it. We keep the calendar so your manager does not have to. If the sludge judge shows the trap hitting 20 percent between visits, we pull the next date in, and if it reads 10 percent, we push it out and save you a pump-out. Adjustments happen at every visit.
