    Copper to PEX Conversions in San Francisco

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more usually follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and reduces the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in San Francisco, CA

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, similar to a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when several fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to weigh cost and performance.
