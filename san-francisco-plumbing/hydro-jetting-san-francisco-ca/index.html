    Residential Sewer Line Jetting in San Francisco

    Main sewer lines in homes are usually 3 or 4 inches and can collect years of grease, soap, mineral scale and roots.     Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in San Francisco

    Residential hydro jetting in San Francisco typically costs $350 to $800, depending on line length, access and how heavy the buildup is. Commercial jetting is often priced by the hour and may run higher.

    Repeat clogs in the same line, slow drains after snaking, sewer odors and known grease or root problems are good reasons to consider jetting.

    Key signs you might need hydro jetting include regularly recurring clogs in the same drain, multiple slow drains throughout your home, persistent foul odors coming from drains, or gurgling sounds from toilets or drains. These often indicate a significant buildup or blockage that needs more than just a snake.

    Most residential jobs take 1 to 3 hours including inspection. Long commercial lines or heavy grease can take longer.

    While small, low-pressure drain jetters are available for rent, professional hydro jetting equipment is significantly more powerful and requires training to operate safely and effectively. Improper use can damage pipes, cause injury, or fail to clear the blockage. For severe clogs, it is always best to call experienced professionals like FBL Plumbing.

    Hydro jetting is highly effective against a wide range of stubborn clogs that traditional snaking cannot fully clear. This includes heavy grease buildup, mineral scale, sludge, soap scum, hair clogs, and even invasive tree roots that have gotten into your sewer lines. It is designed to scour the entire pipe surface.
