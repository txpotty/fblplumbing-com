    Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in San Francisco, CA

    A whole-home PEX repipe for an average home often runs $5,000 to $12,000, with copper usually costing more. A single slab leak repair can run $1,000 to $4,000 including access and restoration. After two or three leaks, a repipe often pays for itself. We give you a side-by-side comparison so you can make the call.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in San Francisco

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to four days.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in San Francisco, CA

    Polybutylene pipes, installed in many homes between the 1970s and 1990s, are known for premature failure due to chemical reactions with disinfectants in the water supply. If your home in San Francisco, CA has polybutylene, a full replacement is essential to prevent major water damage. FBL Plumbing offers specialized polybutylene plumbing replacement, carefully removing the old, brittle pipes and installing a reliable new system.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in San Francisco, CA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and highly durable piping material that's gaining popularity for repiping projects. FBL Plumbing specializes in PEX pipe replacement in San Francisco, CA, offering a system that resists freezing, corrosion, and scale buildup. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points. PEX also noticeably dampens water hammer noise, providing a quieter plumbing system for your home.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in San Francisco

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab. This is a good option for newer homes or a first-time leak. If the home has had multiple leaks, we will explain why a full repipe may save you money.

    Copper Repiping in San Francisco, CA

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes. Joints are soldered with lead-free solder or pressed with ProPress fittings. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a bit longer than PEX.

    Drywall Patching and Restoration in San Francisco, CA

    Repiping requires small openings in walls and ceilings to reach fixtures. Our crews cut clean openings, protect surrounding areas and patch every hole when the plumbing is done. We match common textures like knockdown and orange peel. Your walls are ready for paint when we leave.
