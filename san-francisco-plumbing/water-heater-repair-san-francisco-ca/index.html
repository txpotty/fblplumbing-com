    Repair vs Replace Cost Comparison in San Francisco, CA

    A typical repair costs $150 to $600, while a new standard tank installed often runs $1,400 to $2,800 depending on size, fuel and code updates. If your heater is under eight years old and the tank is sound, repair is generally the better value. Past ten years, or with a leaking tank, replacement usually saves money over time.     Tankless Water Heater Repair in San Francisco

    Tankless water heaters, while efficient, can develop issues like mineral buildup, igniter failures, or flow sensor problems. Our plumbers are trained on the intricate systems of tankless units, performing precise diagnostics and repairs to restore your on-demand hot water in San Francisco.     Sediment Flush and Noise Repair in San Francisco

    Popping, rumbling or crackling sounds usually mean a thick layer of sediment is trapping water under it and overheating the tank bottom. We drain and flush the tank, break up scale with repeated fills and check the drain valve for blockage. Flushing restores capacity, lowers energy use and helps extend tank life. We often recommend yearly flushes for homes on hard water.

    Leaking Water Heater Repair in San Francisco, CA

    Not every leak means a dead tank. Drips from the relief valve, drain valve or supply connections are repairable for a reasonable cost. A leak from the tank body or the bottom seam, however, usually means the inner lining has failed. We pinpoint the source, protect your floors and give you a straight repair or replace recommendation.

    Gas Water Heater Repair in San Francisco, CA

    Gas water heaters require specialized knowledge due to their gas lines and pilot lights. We address issues like thermocouple failures, gas valve problems, or burner assembly malfunctions. Our team ensures all gas connections are secure and leak-free, providing safe and effective repairs in San Francisco, CA.

    Anode Rod Replacement in San Francisco, CA

    The anode rod sacrifices itself so the steel tank does not rust. In hard water it can be eaten away within a handful of years. We pull and inspect the rod, then install a magnesium, aluminum or powered anode depending on your water and any odor problems. A rotten egg smell from hot water often goes away with the right anode choice.

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Repair in San Francisco

    Most homes still rely on traditional tank water heaters. We frequently repair issues like faulty thermostats, sediment buildup, anode rod corrosion, and pressure relief valve malfunctions. Our team can diagnose whether a simple part replacement or a more extensive flush and repair is needed to restore your hot water efficiently.

    Water Heater Leak Detection and Repair in San Francisco, CA

    A leaking water heater can cause serious damage to your home's flooring and foundation, and moisture problems get worse the longer a leak runs. We use specialized tools to pinpoint the source of leaks, whether it's from a faulty T&P valve, a loose fitting, or a compromised tank, and provide durable repair solutions to prevent further damage in San Francisco, CA.
