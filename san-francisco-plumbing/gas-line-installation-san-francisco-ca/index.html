    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Line Installation in San Francisco

    The cost for gas line installation in San Francisco typically ranges from $500 to $2,500, depending on the length of the run, the pipe material, black iron or CSST, and the complexity of the installation. A simple hookup for a single appliance is usually on the lower end, while long runs or multiple connections cost more. We provide transparent, upfront pricing after an on-site assessment.

    A rotten egg or sulfur smell, a hissing sound near a line or appliance, and dead vegetation over a buried line are red flags. If you smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility immediately.

    Gas line installation is extremely dangerous and should only be performed by licensed professionals. Improper installation can lead to gas leaks, explosions, fires and carbon monoxide poisoning. It also voids warranties and often fails local inspections, so it is never a DIY project.

    We use proven, industry-standard brands for gas line materials and fittings for durability and safety. This includes manufacturers like Gastite and OmegaFlex for CSST, and high-quality black iron pipe and fittings from reputable suppliers. All materials are chosen to meet or exceed local plumbing codes.

    The most critical sign of a gas line issue is the smell of rotten eggs, which indicates a gas leak. Other signs include a hissing sound near a gas line, visible damage to a pipe or dead vegetation over an underground gas line. If you suspect a gas leak, leave the building immediately and call your gas provider and then us.

    CSST bonding connects the gas piping to the home's electrical grounding system to reduce damage from lightning strikes. It is a code and manufacturer requirement we follow on every job.

    Yes, gas line installation almost always requires a permit and inspection. We handle the permit and inspection scheduling on your behalf.
