    Frequently Asked Questions About Underground Pipe Repair in San Francisco

    The cost of underground pipe repair in San Francisco, CA varies a lot depending on the pipe's location, the extent of the damage and the repair method used. Simple repairs might range from $500 to $2,500, while extensive trenchless sewer line replacements could be $5,000 to $15,000 or more. We provide a detailed estimate after a full inspection.

    Small repairs often do not need a permit, but full water service or sewer line replacement usually does. Work in the public right-of-way always requires coordination with the city. We handle permits when needed.

    For water lines we usually use PEX or Type K copper, both approved for direct burial. For sewer lines we use Schedule 40 PVC. The right choice depends on the existing pipe and local code.

    We use acoustic listening devices that pick up the sound of escaping water, tracer gas that rises through soil at the leak point and electronic locators that trace the pipe path. Together these pin the leak to a small area.

    Trenchless pipe repair offers several key advantages, including minimal disruption to your yard, landscaping and concrete, which can save on restoration costs. It's often faster than traditional excavation and less invasive, preserving the look and value of your property. This method is also durable, often extending the lifespan of your pipes.

    Many water utilities offer a one-time leak adjustment if you repair the leak quickly and provide proof. We give you an invoice and repair documentation to support your request.
