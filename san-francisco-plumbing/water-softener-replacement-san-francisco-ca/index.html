    Picking the Right Softener for San Francisco, CA Water

    Emergency Softener Replacement in San Francisco, CA

    A softener that sticks in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours, and a cracked bypass or tank can flood a garage floor. First step, turn the bypass handle. We can usually replace a leaking or stuck system within a day or two, and same day if we have the size on the truck.

    Salt-Based Softener Swaps in San Francisco, CA

    Swapping a salt-based softener in Northern California homes means disconnecting the old resin and brine tanks, cutting in new fittings if the loop is corroded, and setting the new valve. Every joint gets checked for leaks under pressure. We set an air gap on the drain line.

    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in San Francisco

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. The tank goes on the main line downstream of the filter, with no power or drain needed. We do not sell clamp-on magnet devices.

    Salt-Free Alternatives in San Francisco, CA

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. In those cases a TAC conditioner works, though we are upfront that it stops scale and does not make water feel soft. Sodium concerns are another reason.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in San Francisco, CA

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in Northern California homes. An old Fleck with discontinued parts usually gets replaced, valve and tank together. Newer Fleck heads drop right in.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in San Francisco, CA

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. Rebedding with new resin can be cheaper than a new system. The valve's age decides it. New resin under a tired valve rarely lasts.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in San Francisco

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. Old clock-timer units come out and metered valves go in. Salt use usually drops. The brine setting gets matched to your hardness so the unit is not wasting salt with every cycle.

    Long-Term Softener Costs in San Francisco, CA

    Long-term cost on a softener is mostly salt and water. Compared with a timer unit, a metered valve set efficiently often uses a third less salt, which matters over a fifteen-year life. We compare brands on that basis.
