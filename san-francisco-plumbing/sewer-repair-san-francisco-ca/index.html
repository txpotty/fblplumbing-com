    Hydro-jetting is a powerful cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to clear stubborn blockages, grease buildup and even tree roots from sewer lines.     Root Intrusion Repair in San Francisco, CA

    Tree and shrub roots go straight for the moisture and nutrients leaking from old sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense mats that catch paper and waste.     Yes, weather can impact sewer lines. Inland valleys get hot summers that work water heaters and exterior lines hard, while coastal areas stay mild and damp year round.     For sewer line replacements, we mainly use durable, code-compliant materials such as PVC for its longevity and resistance to corrosion, or HDPE for trenchless applications. We select the best material based on your needs and local soil conditions.

    The duration of a sewer repair depends heavily on the scope of work. Simple spot repairs or hydro-jetting might take a few hours. Full sewer line replacements involving excavation can take anywhere from two to five days, depending on the length and complexity of the line.

    Roots usually enter through existing gaps at joints, then expand as they grow. Over time they can crack clay pipe, separate joints and create a full blockage. Cutting roots helps short-term, but sealing or replacing the joint is the lasting fix.
