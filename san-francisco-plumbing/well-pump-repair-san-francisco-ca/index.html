    Pressure Switch Replacement in San Francisco

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are common causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most budget-friendly well repairs.

    Well Pump Maintenance & Repair Cost Comparison in San Francisco, CA

    Jet Pump System Repair in San Francisco, CA

    Jet pumps, usually found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Typical problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. FBL Plumbing can effectively troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in San Francisco operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is essential for jet pump function.

    Well Pump Pressure Tank Issues in San Francisco

    The pressure tank works together with your well pump to maintain consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling. A waterlogged tank, a ruptured diaphragm, or a faulty air valve can cause rapid pump cycling, fluctuating water pressure, or even no water at all. Our team carefully diagnoses pressure tank problems and performs necessary repairs or replacements. We ensure your tank is properly sized and charged for optimal system performance in San Francisco.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in San Francisco, CA

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. Strong winter storms can bring days of heavy rain to Northern California, saturating ground and pushing water into basements and sewer lines. Lightning and power surges frequently damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help prevent repeat failures.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in San Francisco

    Submersible pumps are common in residential wells, designed to operate submerged within the well casing. When these pumps fail, symptoms usually include no water, low water pressure, or strange noises from the well. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like motor burnout, damaged impellers, or faulty check valves that can affect submersible pump performance. We use dedicated equipment for safe and efficient pump extraction and reinstallation, ensuring the integrity of your well system.
