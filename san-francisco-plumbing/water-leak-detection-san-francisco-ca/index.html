    Pool Water Leak Detection in San Francisco, CA

    Pools lose a lot of water to evaporation in summer heat, so a leak can hide behind normal loss. We use a bucket test to compare evaporation with actual water loss. Then we pressure test pool plumbing and dye test fittings, lights and skimmers. Finding the leak safeguards the pool and the soil around it.

    Hidden Wall & Ceiling Leak Detection in San Francisco, CA

    Leaks behind walls or in ceilings can cause serious damage before they become visible, leading to stained drywall, peeling paint or a musty odor. These often stem from faulty pipe connections, old galvanized pipes or issues with shower pans and drains. Our technicians use moisture meters and infrared cameras to scan these areas, identifying elevated moisture levels and temperature differences that point to a hidden leak, minimizing the need for extensive wall demolition.

    Preventative Leak Inspections in San Francisco, CA

    Beyond emergency detection, FBL Plumbing offers preventative leak inspections to identify potential problems before they become major issues. This service is particularly valuable for older homes in San Francisco with aging plumbing systems. We conduct thorough checks of visible and accessible pipes, connections and fixtures, looking for early signs of corrosion or wear. A proactive approach to leak detection can save homeowners real money and hassle in the long run, extending the life of your plumbing system in Northern California.

    Toilet and Fixture Leak Detection in San Francisco, CA

    Toilets are one of the most common sources of wasted water. A worn flapper or a fill valve that never shuts off can leak quietly around the clock. We use dye tests to find leaking toilets and check faucets, showers and supply lines for drips. These fixes are typically quick and save water right away.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in San Francisco

    Leaks in underground water lines, from your meter to your home, can go unnoticed for extended periods, wasting water and causing yard erosion. Signs include oddly green patches in your lawn, persistent wet spots or a constantly running water meter. FBL Plumbing employs ground-penetrating radar and tracer gas detection methods to accurately map out and pinpoint leaks in buried pipes, allowing for targeted excavation and repair rather than digging up your entire yard.

    Pinhole Leak Detection in San Francisco, CA

    Pinhole leaks, often found in copper or PEX piping, are small but can cause significant damage over time, especially behind walls or under floors. We use non-invasive moisture meters to detect elevated humidity levels and thermal cameras to identify cold spots that indicate water. Our plumbers are adept at tracing these elusive leaks without tearing apart your entire home. Swift action on pinhole leaks prevents extensive drywall damage, mold growth and compromised structural integrity. Inland valleys get hot summers that work water heaters and outdoor lines hard, while coastal areas stay mild and damp year round.
