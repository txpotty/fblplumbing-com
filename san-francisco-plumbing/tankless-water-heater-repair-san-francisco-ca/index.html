    Dependable Tankless Water Heater Repair Services in San Francisco, CA

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in San Francisco

    Tankless heaters use flow sensors to start and thermistors to regulate temperature. A dirty inlet filter or failed sensor causes no hot water, fluctuating temperature or the unit turning off mid-shower. We clean filters, test sensor readings and replace faulty parts. Temperature becomes steady again once readings are correct.

    Tankless Water Heater Error Code Diagnostics in San Francisco, CA

    Modern tankless water heaters display error codes to indicate specific problems, from flow rate issues to combustion errors. Our experts in San Francisco, CA are trained to interpret these codes accurately, using specialized diagnostic tools to pinpoint the exact component failure. This accuracy allows us to quickly order the correct parts and perform targeted repairs, saving you time and money. We address codes related to burner failures, fan issues and temperature sensor errors, among others.

    Pilot Light and Ignition Repair in San Francisco, CA

    For gas-fired tankless water heaters, a faulty ignition system can completely stop hot water production. Our team in San Francisco, CA diagnoses issues with igniters, flame sensors and gas valves. We confirm proper gas pressure and ventilation are maintained for safe and efficient operation. Timely repair of these components prevents hazardous gas problems and restores your unit's ability to heat water on demand.

    Tankless Repair vs Replacement Cost in San Francisco, CA

    Descaling service typically costs $175 to $350, and sensor or igniter repairs run $250 to $600. Heat exchanger replacement can cost $800 to $1,500 or more unless covered by warranty. A new tankless unit installed usually ranges from $3,000 to $5,500. We compare these costs against the age and condition of your unit.

    Recirculation Pump Repair in San Francisco

    Many tankless systems use internal or external recirculation to deliver hot water faster. Pump failures, check valve problems or wrong schedule settings can cause long waits or lukewarm water. We test the pump, check valves and controls and set the schedule to match your routine. A properly tuned system saves both water and gas.

    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in San Francisco

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside the heat exchanger, limiting flow and causing overheating. Many units warn of this with scale codes, such as the LC code on Rinnai models. We connect a circulation pump to the service valves and run a descaling solution through the exchanger. Annual descaling is wise for most homes on hard water.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in San Francisco, CA

    Condensing tankless heaters, such as many Navien models, extract extra heat from exhaust and produce condensate. Clogged condensate traps, failed neutralizers and blocked drains can shut the unit down. We clean the trap, replace the neutralizer media and verify proper drainage. PVC venting is also checked for slope and sagging.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Repair in San Francisco

    Navien units are known for their advanced condensing technology but can sometimes develop issues with their circulation pump or exhaust systems. FBL Plumbing provides dedicated Navien tankless water heater repair in San Francisco, addressing common problems like ignition failures or condensate drain blockages. Our team is familiar with Navien's parts and troubleshooting protocols, ensuring a precise and effective repair that maintains your unit's high efficiency.
