    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in San Francisco, CA

    Sewer lines in San Francisco, CA can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or separate. Earthquakes and hillside soil movement are real risks in Northern California, so flexible connections and seismic straps help protect plumbing from ground movement. Tree root intrusion is another major cause, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also add to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.
