    Tankless Water Heater Service in San Francisco, CA

    Tankless units need descaling. Mineral scale coats the inside of the heat exchanger, restricting flow and making the unit work harder, and eventually it starts throwing error codes or cutting out in the middle of a shower. A pump circulates descaler through it. We also check the igniter, the fan and the exhaust vent for safe operation.

    Descaling Tankless Units in San Francisco

    Error codes often mean scale. Tankless heaters are especially sensitive to hard water, and once scale coats the heat exchanger, flow drops, the unit overheats and the control board shuts it down to protect itself. A pump kit handles it. Yearly descaling is standard advice for hard water.

    Relief Valve and Safety Testing in San Francisco, CA

    The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense. We test the valve, confirm it reseats and check that the discharge pipe is routed correctly. Leaky valves get replaced. We check the expansion tank too.

    Maintenance for Every Brand in San Francisco

    Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White, Noritz, Rinnai and more. Each brand has its own anode type, drain valve style and descaling procedure, and we follow the manufacturer's guidelines so the service holds up if you ever need to file a warranty claim. Model details matter. The truck carries common parts.

    Anode Rod Checks in San Francisco

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. It is a long metal rod screwed into the top of the tank, and once it has corroded down to bare wire, the water starts eating the steel tank lining instead. It comes out for inspection. A new anode costs far less than a new tank.

    Attic, Closet and Garage Units in San Francisco

    Location changes the risks. Attic heaters need pans and drain lines, and garage gas units often need elevation or protection. We check the pan and drain. Access platforms and venting get looked at too.

    Commercial Water Heater Service in San Francisco

    Businesses cannot run without hot water. Restaurants, salons and gyms in San Francisco lean on large tanks or banks of tankless units, and a failure during business hours means closing a kitchen or sending customers home. We schedule around your hours. Big tanks get the full checklist.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in San Francisco

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment piles up around the lower element, it runs hotter than it should, and eventually it burns out, which is why you suddenly get only a few minutes of hot water. We test both elements. Thermostats and wiring get checked with the power off.
