    Gas Connector and Shutoff Checks in San Francisco

    Gas feeds water heaters, furnaces, ranges and dryers. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections with leak detector solution, make sure each appliance shutoff valve turns and replace any old or kinked flexible connectors. Repairs follow local code. If you ever smell gas, leave the house and call your gas utility from outside.

    Softener Upkeep in San Francisco, CA

    Softeners fail quietly. A unit that stopped regenerating or ran out of salt keeps sending water to the house, it just is not soft anymore, and you will not notice until scale shows up on the faucets and the water heater starts popping. Bypass and drain get tested. Then we test the treated water.

    Water Softener Service in San Francisco

    A softener only helps if it regenerates. Salt level, brine tank, injector and a test regeneration all get checked. We test the water afterward. A failed softener sends hard water through the house without warning.

    Scheduled Service for Businesses in San Francisco

    Commercial plumbing takes a beating. We service large water heaters, restroom fixtures, backflow preventers and kitchen drain lines on a set schedule, so failures are caught on a quiet Tuesday morning instead of a busy Friday night. Health inspectors notice. You get records for every visit.

    Hot Water Tank Service in San Francisco, CA

    Gas and electric tanks need different checks. On gas units we look at the burner flame, the flue draft and the gas connection for leaks, and on electric units we test the elements and the thermostats and check the wiring for heat damage. Sediment comes out either way. Scale on the tank floor wastes energy and shortens tank life.

    Drain Cleaning as Maintenance in San Francisco

    Slow drains rarely fix themselves. A yearly camera look and a cleaning on known problem lines, especially near big trees, keeps grease and roots from building to the point where the main backs up during a holiday. We jet greasy lines. Rooted lines get regular cutting.

    Repipe Assessments in San Francisco, CA

    Polybutylene and old galvanized pipe leak more. We identify the pipe type, look for signs of failure and tell you whether repiping is urgent or can wait. PEX and copper are the options. Work can be phased.

    Sewer and Drain Camera Inspection in San Francisco, CA

    A camera shows what the sewer looks like inside. We look for roots, bellies, cracks and grease buildup in the main and branch drains, which matters most in older homes with clay or cast iron lines that have been in the ground for decades. The recording is yours. If cleaning or repair makes sense, we explain the choices.
