    New Construction Water Service Lines in San Francisco

    New homes, guest houses and additions need a properly sized service line from the meter to the building. We review the plans, confirm meter size with your water provider and choose a line diameter that matches the fixture load. Most homes need a 3/4 inch or 1 inch line, while larger homes with pools and big irrigation systems often benefit from 1 1/4 inch. We coordinate the rough-in timing with your builder so the line is ready for inspection.

    Main Water Line Replacement in San Francisco

    Over time, main water lines can corrode, crack or leak because of shifting soil, tree root intrusion or age. FBL Plumbing specializes in replacing old, failing main water lines, often upgrading from galvanized steel or polybutylene to more durable PEX or copper. We handle the excavation, pipe laying and connection to your home and the public water supply, with minimal disruption and lasting performance. Our team in San Francisco is familiar with local soil types and infrastructure.

    PEX and HDPE Water Line Installation in San Francisco, CA

    PEX and HDPE are flexible, which means fewer fittings in the ground and better tolerance for soil movement. HDPE is especially durable for long runs and resists cracking in expansive clay. We use approved compression or fusion fittings and add tracer wire so the line can be found with a locator later. PEX must stay out of sunlight before burial, so we store and lay it carefully on every job site.

    Water Line Replacement for Older Homes in San Francisco

    Copper became standard in many mid-century homes here, and decades later pinhole leaks and worn fittings are a common repair call. Many older homes still have galvanized steel or aging copper pipes that are prone to corrosion, leaks and reduced water pressure. FBL Plumbing provides professional water line replacement in San Francisco, upgrading your system to modern, durable materials like PEX or new copper. This boosts water quality and pressure and prevents costly future repairs caused by failing pipes.

    Upsizing Water Lines for Better Pressure in San Francisco, CA

    Many homes built decades ago have a 3/4 inch line that cannot keep up with modern fixture loads, irrigation and pools. If your pressure drops when two showers run or the sprinklers are on, an undersized line may be the cause. We test static and flowing pressure, then calculate whether a larger line will make a real difference. Upsizing is often paired with a new pressure reducing valve and main shutoff.
