    Baffle Repair and Replacement in San Francisco

    Baffles slow incoming water so grease can rise and solids can settle. When baffles crack, corrode or break loose, grease passes straight through the trap. We remove damaged baffles and install new ones made for your trap model. In concrete interceptors, we can repair or rebuild baffle walls. Restoring baffles brings back proper separation and helps you stay up to code.

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in San Francisco

    When traditional snaking isn't enough, hydro-jetting is our go-to method for severe grease trap blockages. This powerful technique uses high-pressure water streams to fully scour the interior of pipes and the grease trap itself, removing years of FOG buildup, scale, and debris. It's a very effective, non-invasive method that not only clears clogs but also cleans the lines, greatly reducing the likelihood of future blockages in your San Francisco business.

    Damaged Baffle Repair in San Francisco

    The baffles inside a grease trap are essential for separating FOGs from wastewater. If these baffles are corroded, broken, or dislodged, the trap's efficiency drops sharply, allowing grease into the sewer system. Our service includes inspecting the condition of all baffles and repairing or replacing them as needed, often using durable, corrosion-resistant materials. Proper baffle function is key to compliance with wastewater regulations in San Francisco.

    Commercial Grease Interceptor Repair in San Francisco, CA

    Larger commercial establishments often utilize grease interceptors, which are more substantial than standard grease traps.     Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in San Francisco, CA

    Tees direct flow into and out of the trap at the correct depth. Missing or broken tees allow floating grease to escape into the sewer line. We replace damaged tees with durable PVC or other approved materials. We also check that they are set at the right depth for your unit. This is one of the most common and necessary grease trap repairs.
