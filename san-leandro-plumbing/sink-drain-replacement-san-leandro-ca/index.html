    Kitchen Sink Drain Replacement in San Leandro

    Kitchen drains start failing at the basket strainer. Old strainer putty dries out and the locknut rusts, so water runs down the tailpiece and looks like a trap leak. Strainer and trap get swapped as a pair. Disposal sinks get a new flange instead.

    Bathroom Sink Drain Replacement in San Leandro, CA

    Bathroom drains wear out at the pop-up. The pivot nut packing leaks, the drain body corrodes around the overflow holes, and the stopper stops sealing, so a sink that will not hold water usually needs a whole new pop-up assembly. Half-fixes do not hold here. The new drain matches your faucet finish.

    Bathroom Drain Upgrade in San Leandro, CA

    Old bath drains often carry decades of hair and scum. Replacing the pop-up body and the trap removes the rough inside walls that grab hair, and a smooth new 1-1/4 inch path drains a full bowl in seconds instead of minutes. Clogs come back slower. A hair catcher keeps it that way.

    Vessel and Grid Drain Replacement in San Leandro

    Vessel sinks use a different drain. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted grid or umbrella drain, or water leaks out the slots. Mismatched drains are common. Swapping it takes under an hour.

    Corroded Drain Replacement in San Leandro, CA

    Drain cleaners speed up corrosion. Caustic products sit in the bottom of a metal trap between uses and eat at the chrome plating and the thin brass underneath, which is why so many old traps we pull have pinholes right at the bottom of the bend. PVC does not have that problem. We recommend it for most cabinets.

    Utility and Basement Sink Drain Replacement in San Leandro

    Basement sinks often drain to a pump. Sinks below the sewer line drain through a pump, so we check its valve and discharge line during the job. Pumps fail quietly. A gurgling pit is a clue.

    Kitchen Drain and Disposal Tie-In in San Leandro

    Disposals change the kitchen drain. The outlet on the disposal has to sit above the trap inlet, the dishwasher hose needs a high loop or an air gap, and the trap has to line up without the slip joints pulling sideways. We set them all at once. Then the disposal runs with water while we watch.
