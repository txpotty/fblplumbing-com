    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in San Leandro, CA: The Details

    Top-mount Sink Installation in San Leandro

    Drop-in sinks are common in Northern California homes with laminate or tile counters. The rim holds the sink up. We check the cutout, run a bead of silicone under the lip and tighten the mounting clips evenly around the bowl so the rim seats flat. Next we connect and test the faucet and drain.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in San Leandro

    Both bowls of a double sink share one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. We pick the layout around your disposal and dishwasher, since a disposal on one side changes where the tee can go and how much fall each side gets. We use heavy-wall fittings.

    Granite and Quartz Composite Sinks in San Leandro

    Composite sinks are heavier than stainless and usually thicker at the drain, so standard strainers sometimes need a longer threaded body. Strainer fit gets checked first. When the strainer is too short for the sink thickness, we replace it instead of cranking it down and chipping the drain opening.

    Commercial Kitchen Sink Installation in San Leandro, CA

    Three-compartment sinks, prep sinks and hand sinks carry separate health code requirements. Prep sinks usually need an indirect drain to a floor sink with an air gap, while the three-compartment sink often runs through the grease trap. We confirm the rules first. Work is scheduled before or after service so the kitchen does not close.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in San Leandro, CA

    Stone shops vary on how they mount undermounts. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. The bowl is braced regardless. We do not connect the trap until the adhesive has cured.

    Drop-In Sink Replacement in San Leandro

    Removing an old drop-in begins by slicing through the old caulk and backing off the clips from below. Old clips are often rusted solid, so we bring a reciprocating saw and cut them off rather than prying on the counter. Laminate edges chip easily. Your new rim has to fully cover the old hole.

    Kitchen Sink Installation Cost Factors in San Leandro, CA

    A drop-in swap costs less than an undermount on stone. Adding a disposal, moving the drain, lowering a wall stub or replacing old galvanized stubs all add labor, and we list each one separately on the quote. Plumbing is priced apart from the counter.

    Disposal and Dishwasher Hookup with Sink Installation in San Leandro, CA

    Most new kitchen sinks get paired with a disposal and a dishwasher. We mount the disposal, run the dishwasher drain through an air gap or high loop and install a dedicated hot supply with its own shutoff. Doing it all at once saves money. Every connection is tested under real use.
