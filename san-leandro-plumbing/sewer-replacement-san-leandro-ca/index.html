    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in San Leandro, CA

    Some lines have severe sagging, wrong slope or are too shallow for trenchless methods. In these cases, open-trench replacement is the best option. We excavate, remove the old pipe and install new PVC with proper bedding. We restore the trench once it passes inspection. This method allows full correction of slope problems.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in San Leandro

    Cast iron sewer pipe can corrode from the inside, creating rough surfaces and holes. Scale buildup catches debris and causes clogs. We replace cast iron with PVC or HDPE. This restores flow and eliminates corrosion. Many homes built before 1975 have cast iron lines.

    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in San Leandro

    Many older homes in San Leandro still have original clay or cast iron sewer lines, which are prone to root intrusion, corrosion, and cracking. FBL Plumbing can upgrade your system to modern PVC (polyvinyl chloride) piping. PVC is durable, resistant to root penetration, and has a smooth interior that reduces the likelihood of blockages, providing a long-lasting solution for your home's drainage.

    Clay and Orangeburg Pipe Replacement in San Leandro, CA

    Clay pipe joints let roots in, while Orangeburg pipe made from tar paper deforms over time. Both are prone to failure. We replace these lines with modern materials. Camera inspection shows the full extent of damage. Replacement ends recurring root problems.

    Sewer Line Repair vs. Full Replacement in San Leandro

    Deciding between repair and full replacement depends on the extent and nature of the damage. Minor cracks or isolated clogs might be resolved with targeted repairs, but widespread corrosion, multiple breaks, or a collapsed pipe often necessitates a full replacement. Our technicians in San Leandro will conduct a thorough camera inspection to accurately diagnose the problem and recommend the most cost-effective and long-term solution. We provide clear explanations and upfront pricing for both options.
