    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in San Leandro

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. Compared with an older timer-based unit, a modern metered softener can use roughly half the salt and noticeably less regeneration water each month. That pays back over time. Several models are available.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in San Leandro

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. Instead of removing hardness, they convert it into a form that resists sticking, which reduces scale without salt or brine waste. Maintenance is light. They work best on moderate water.

    Softener Loop Installation in San Leandro

    No loop? A softener loop is a pair of capped stubs on the main line, usually in the garage, that lets a softener be connected without cutting pipe later. We add the loop. Outdoor taps can stay on hard water, which saves salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in San Leandro

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet, and many can also feed a refrigerator water line or an ice maker. Pairs well with a softener. Filters get changed on schedule.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in San Leandro

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. That protects every faucet, shower, water heater and appliance downstream, which is where hard water does most of its damage over the years. We size before we sell. Hardness and household use set the grain capacity.

    Water Softener Installation Cost in San Leandro, CA

    Cost depends on the system type, its capacity and whether a loop already exists. An existing loop keeps the price down, and adding a loop adds labor and materials. Filters add cost. We put equipment, labor and setup on the quote.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in San Leandro, CA

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. Putting a carbon tank in front of the softener takes out chlorine and keeps it from wearing down the resin. Taste improves fast. Setups can be two tanks or one dual-stage tank.

    Water Softener Replacement Services in San Leandro, CA
