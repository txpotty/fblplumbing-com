    Uponor PEX System Installation in San Leandro

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool plus a ring slipped over the pipe end.     PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in San Leandro

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A bends tighter, recovers from a kink with a heat gun, and its expansion fittings flow more water, while PEX-B costs less and its crimp joints are just as reliable when they are gauged properly. Both last decades. In a cramped attic, we usually pick PEX-A.

    Leak Repair with PEX in San Leandro, CA

    A single bad section is not always a reason to repipe. We can cut out a split or corroded stretch of copper, transition to PEX with a listed fitting, and have the water back on in an hour or two.     Copper to PEX Conversions in San Leandro

    Pinhole leaks in copper tend to come in clusters. Aggressive water, fast flow and soil contact under slabs can pit copper from the inside, and the first few pinholes are typically followed by more. Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running hard. Converting to PEX stops the cycle. Where new PEX meets existing copper stubs or valves, we use listed transition fittings.

    Full Home PEX Repiping in San Leandro

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside out. After 40 or 50 years the bore is so choked with scale that a half-inch line delivers a trickle, and the water comes out tinted brown after it sits overnight. We replace every foot of it with PEX. Pressure comes back at the fixtures and the rust stops.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in San Leandro, CA

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. All three are code-listed for drinking water. Pipe, fittings and tools come from the same maker on our jobs, which keeps the warranty valid.
