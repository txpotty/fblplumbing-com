    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in San Leandro

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. A cable only punches a hole. We camera first to make sure the pipe can take the pressure.

    Restaurant and Retail Sewer Repair in San Leandro

    Restaurants, retail centers and multifamily buildings load a lateral far beyond a single house. Grease and wipes cause most of it.
