    What Our San Leandro, CA Drain Techs Clean

    Kitchen Drain Cleaning in San Leandro

    Grease is the big one in kitchens. It goes down warm and sets up like candle wax in the pipe. Food scraps and soap stick to that layer and the opening keeps shrinking until the sink holds water, and we clear it with a drum machine that is safe for PVC and old cast iron alike.

    Tub and Shower Drain Cleaning in San Leandro, CA

    Showers clog slowly, then all at once. Hair builds a mat under the strainer. For tubs, the cable goes in through the overflow plate rather than the drain, which protects the tub surface, and we clear the trap and the branch beyond it, then run the shower for a few minutes to confirm it drains at full speed with no gurgle.

    Hydro Jetting for Drains in San Leandro

    Jetting does what a cable cannot. Water at around 3,000 to 4,000 PSI scours the whole pipe wall. A cable punches a hole, while a jetter cleans the pipe back to near its original diameter. It works best on a kitchen or main line that keeps clogging every few months no matter how often it gets cabled.

    Jetting Versus Cabling in San Leandro, CA

    Cabling and jetting solve different problems. A sectional cable is built to chop and pull a clog out. Jetting strips grease and scale off the walls and carries it out of the line, so we cable first on a hard root mass and jet afterward when the line has years of buildup on it.

    Commercial Drain Cleaning in San Leandro, CA

    Restaurants, salons and offices load drains harder than homes do. A busy shop puts a year of household waste down a line in a month. Our commercial setup includes larger cable and jetting equipment for 4 to 6 inch pipe, and we can schedule work before opening or after close so you do not lose a lunch rush to a plumber in the kitchen.
