    Picking a Gas Water Heater for Your San Leandro, CA Home

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Replacement in San Leandro

    Atmospheric vent heaters last 10 to 12 years on average, and the end usually shows up as rusty hot water or a slow drip from the bottom. We disconnect the old unit and reconnect the new one to the existing gas line and flue. Combustion air matters. We confirm a closet install has louvers or ducted air before we light the burner.

    A.O. Smith Gas Water Heater Service in San Leandro

    A.O. Smith makes tanks under its own name and also builds the State line, so parts overlap a lot between the two brands. We handle the sizing and the gas connection. We confirm the vent meets their specs.

    No Hot Water Calls in San Leandro

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. Those get tested before anything else. On a tank past ten years, a new heater usually beats a new valve.

    Gas Water Heater Efficiency Ratings in San Leandro

    Efficiency ratings on tank heaters are listed as UEF, uniform energy factor, and a higher number means less gas per gallon heated. Basic tanks land near 0.60 UEF. Condensing models top 0.80, and some qualify for utility rebates where those are offered.

    Atmospheric Vent Upgrades in San Leandro, CA

    Flue pipes on older atmospheric heaters rust at the joints and lose their upward slope, and a loose connector can spill exhaust into the closet. Bad vent sections and draft hoods get replaced. We check draft with a match or mirror at the hood.

    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in San Leandro

    Blower motors and pressure switches are the weak points on power vent tanks. When one fails twice on a tank that is already 8 or 9 years old, replacing the whole heater is usually cheaper over time. We confirm a dedicated outlet first.

    Large Capacity 75 Gallon Gas Heaters in San Leandro, CA

    Big families with soaking tubs often outgrow a 50 gallon tank and need a 75 gallon heater or a high-recovery 50 at around 65,000 to 75,000 BTU. A bigger burner needs more gas. We check the meter and pipe size first, because a starved burner never reaches its rated recovery.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in San Leandro, CA

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. A natural gas unit does not go on propane unless the manufacturer allows a conversion. Pressure gets checked with a manometer.
