    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Installation in San Leandro

    The cost of grease trap installation in San Leandro, CA varies significantly based on the size, type, and complexity of the system.     The primary purpose of a grease trap is to intercept fats, oils, and greases (FOG) from commercial kitchen wastewater before they enter the public sewer system. This prevents pipe blockages, protects municipal infrastructure, and helps businesses comply with environmental regulations.

    The cleaning frequency for a new grease trap depends on its size, the volume of FOG produced by your kitchen, and local regulations in San Leandro. Typically, grease traps require cleaning every 1 to 3 months. Regular cleaning is essential to maintain efficiency and prevent backups.

    Signs you might need a new grease trap include frequent drain clogs, slow drainage in multiple fixtures, persistent foul odors coming from drains, or if your current unit is old, corroded, or no longer meeting local code requirements. An inspection by a professional plumber can confirm the need for replacement.

    FBL Plumbing provides a warranty on our workmanship for all grease trap installations. Additionally, the grease trap unit itself will typically come with a manufacturer's warranty. We will provide you with all warranty details at the time of installation, ensuring peace of mind.

    The timeline for grease trap installation depends on the project's scope. A simple under-sink unit might take a few hours.
