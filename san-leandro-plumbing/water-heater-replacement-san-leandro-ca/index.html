    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in San Leandro

    The cost of water heater replacement in San Leandro, CA typically ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the type of unit (gas, electric, tankless or hybrid), its capacity and any necessary plumbing modifications. Our team will provide a detailed, upfront quote after assessing your needs and existing setup.

    Most standard tank-style water heater replacements can be completed in 3 to 5 hours, assuming no major complications or plumbing reconfigurations. Tankless or hybrid installations might take a bit longer, often 5 to 8 hours, due to more complex venting or gas line changes.

    New water heaters typically come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and parts, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. FBL Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation, giving you added peace of mind. Specific warranty details vary by brand and unit.

    Common signs include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, a metallic taste in hot water, strange noises like rumbling or popping from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit or a water heater that is over 10 to 12 years old. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a licensed plumber.

    A traditional water heater stores and heats a limited amount of water in a tank, whereas a tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit. Tankless units offer endless hot water and can be more energy-efficient, but usually have a higher upfront cost and may require gas line upgrades.

    FBL Plumbing installs and services all major brands, including Bradford White, Rheem, AO Smith, Rinnai, Navien and State Water Heaters. We can help you choose the best brand and model that fits your budget and hot water demands.

    Upgrading provides more reliable hot water, improved energy efficiency and lower utility bills, better performance and a reduced risk of leaks or breakdowns. Newer models often have advanced features and longer warranties, improving comfort and safety in your home.

    Tank water heaters often last about 8 to 12 years, and hard water and sediment buildup can shorten that. Rural properties around San Leandro often rely on private wells, which may need treatment for iron, sediment, or other minerals. Annual flushing and anode rod checks can help lengthen that life.
