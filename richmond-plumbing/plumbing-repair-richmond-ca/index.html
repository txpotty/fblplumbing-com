    Sewer Line Repair in Richmond, CA

    Roots are the usual cause of a sewer line that backs up on a regular schedule. They find a cracked joint, grow inside and catch paper and grease. We camera the line to see the damage and mark the depth on the surface, then choose between an open dig and a trenchless method. Clay pipe usually goes first.

    Gas Leak Repair in Richmond

    Rotten-egg odor near the range or a hiss at the meter means get out of the house first. Phone the gas company from the yard, then call us to fix it.     Well Pump Service in Richmond, CA

    Submersible pumps sit deep in the casing, so diagnosis happens at the surface first. FBL Plumbing checks the pressure switch, the control box capacitor and the tank precharge, then measures amp draw on the motor. High amp draw usually means a worn motor or a seized impeller. Jet pumps are simpler, and a lost prime or a bad foot valve is the usual cause when one stops moving water.

    Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Richmond, CA

    Pinhole leaks in copper begin inside the pipe, where water chemistry and high velocity wear through the wall. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. One pinhole usually means others are coming in the same run. We solder in a new section or use a press fitting, and we check the house pressure while we are there. Above 80 psi, add a regulator.

    Drain and Pipe Repair in Richmond

    Hydro-jetting at around 4,000 psi clears grease and scale that a cable snake only pokes a small hole through, which is why the clog keeps coming back. FBL Plumbing runs a camera after every jetting so you can see whether the pipe is sound. Cracks and offset joints get repaired by section in copper, PEX, PVC or ABS. A broken pipe needs a repair, not more jetting.

    Tank and Tankless Water Heater Repair in Richmond

    Tank and tankless heaters fail in different ways. FBL Plumbing repairs both, including the major brands, gas and electric. Tank calls usually come down to the thermocouple, the gas valve, an element or the anode. Tankless trouble is usually scale in the heat exchanger or a dirty inlet screen, and a descaling flush fixes more of them than new parts do.

    Water Heater Valve and Element Repair in Richmond

    Rumbling or popping from a tank means sediment on the bottom. A rotten-egg smell in the hot water usually means the anode rod is reacting with the water. We replace thermocouples, thermostats, elements, gas valves, anode rods and relief valves from truck stock, which covers the large majority of tank repairs we see in a year. A tank leaking at the seam is a different story. No part fixes that, and we tell you plainly.

    Drain Line Repair in Richmond

    Slow drains that come back a week after snaking usually mean the pipe itself is damaged. Cracked cast iron or a sagging run will hold debris every time. We run a camera to find the spot, then replace that section or hydro-jet the line if buildup is the only problem. Grease is the usual buildup.
