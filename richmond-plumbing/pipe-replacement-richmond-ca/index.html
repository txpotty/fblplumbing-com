    PEX Pipe Replacement in Richmond

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping has become a popular choice for residential pipe replacement due to its flexibility, affordability, and resistance to freezing. Freezing is uncommon along the coast and on valley floors in Northern California, but frosty nights do happen and can crack exposed outdoor pipes. Unlike rigid pipes, PEX can expand, reducing the risk of burst pipes during unexpected cold snaps. FBL Plumbing specializes in PEX installation, which often allows for less invasive replacement methods compared to traditional materials. This modern solution offers strong performance and water quality for your home.

    PEX Repiping in Richmond

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale buildup and expands slightly if water freezes, which helps in colder homes. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe for better flow. Systems can be run as a home-run manifold with a shutoff for each fixture or as a trunk and branch layout. PEX installs faster than copper with many fewer fittings in the walls. It is our most popular repipe material.
