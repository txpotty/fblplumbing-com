    Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Richmond, CA

    Galvanized steel service lines rust from the inside out. Once one spot leaks, the pipe on either side is usually just as thin.     PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Richmond

    Copper and PEX fail in different places. Copper tends to pinhole where a rock rubs it or where the water chemistry eats the wall from inside. PEX usually fails at a fitting. We cut out the bad section of copper and sweat or press in a new length, while PEX gets a fresh crimp ring or expansion fitting with the right tool for the brand.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Richmond

    Buried copper is tough, but it is not forever. Rocks pressing against the pipe wear pinholes over time, and acidic soil or high water velocity can thin the wall from the inside. A two-foot section is typical. The new piece goes in with soldered or pressed couplings, and we check the exposed pipe on both sides for green crust or soft spots.
