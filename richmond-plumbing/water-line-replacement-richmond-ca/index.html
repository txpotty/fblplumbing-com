    Service Line Replacement Choices in Richmond, CA

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Richmond

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. It does not corrode or build scale. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, use only fittings listed for burial, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    Traditional Water Line Excavation in Richmond

    Sometimes the open trench wins. A collapsed line, a route with sharp bends, or solid rock can rule out trenchless. We cut the sod and set it aside, dig to depth with a mini excavator or by hand near other utilities, set the pipe on clean bedding, and backfill in layers with compaction so the trench does not sink after the first good rain. Sod goes back on top.

    Commercial Water Line Replacement in Richmond

    Larger buildings bring larger pipe, deeper trenches and more inspection steps. We handle the backflow test and certification paperwork. Trench shoring comes into play below five feet, and we plan the work so the building is without water for hours, not days, often running a temporary supply while the new line is tied in. Night work is possible.

    Commercial Water Line Services in Richmond, CA

    Commercial services run bigger, often 1-1/2 to 2 inches, and almost always have a backflow assembly that needs testing after any work. We schedule the shutdown around your hours. For a restaurant or an office building that might mean an early morning start, temporary water to the restrooms, and a call to the utility about their side of the meter. Tenants get notice.

    Copper Service Line Installation in Richmond, CA

    Copper is still the choice for some homeowners. It has a long track record. We flare or use brass compression fittings at the ends, keep the run in one piece between them, and pack sand around it so rocks do not touch the pipe. Where it goes through a concrete wall, it gets a plastic sleeve and a seal to keep water and bugs out.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Richmond, CA

    Bursting fits old cast iron or galvanized lines that will shatter under the head. Pulling suits softer pipe like poly or copper. Either way the new pipe follows the old path, which matters when the route runs under a patio or a neighbor's fence, and the yard work shrinks to two pits. Rock can stop it.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Richmond, CA

    Pipe pulling works like this. Two pits get dug, one at each end. A cable or bursting head goes through the old pipe and drags new HDPE or PEX back through the same path, splitting the old pipe if it is brittle, so a driveway, a row of shrubs or a concrete walk can stay where it is.

    Trenchless Bores Under Driveways in Richmond

    Directional boring drills a path under the obstacle and pulls the pipe back through it. It does not need the old pipe as a guide. We use it when the new line has to take a different route, say around a new addition or under a driveway poured after the house was built, and the launch and exit pits stay small. Heavy rock is the exception.
