    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Richmond, CA

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a tight seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can recover from kinks with a heat gun. It is our preferred choice for many full repipes.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Richmond

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more usually follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and reduces the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.
