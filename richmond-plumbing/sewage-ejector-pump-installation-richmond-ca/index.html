    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Richmond

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so the toilet, shower and sink drain to a sealed basin instead of flowing by gravity. We cut the slab, trench the drain lines to the basin, set it, run a two-inch vent up through the house to the roof and pipe the discharge to the building drain with a check valve and shutoff. Then we patch the concrete.

    New Construction Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Richmond, CA

    On a new build, we set the basin ahead of the slab pour. That is the cheapest time, since there is no concrete to cut, and we stub the drains, the vent and the discharge to the plans so the rough-in inspection goes smoothly. It keeps the pour on schedule.

    Zoeller Ejector Pump Installation in Richmond, CA

    Zoeller has made ejector pumps for a very long time, and its cast iron models are the ones we see still running in older basements.     Liberty Pumps Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Richmond, CA

    Liberty builds single and dual pump packages that arrive with the basin, pump, lid and fittings matched. Switch failures cause many calls.

    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in Richmond

    A high water alarm should be on any ejector basin, since a pump failure can lead to a sewage backup quickly. We install audible alarms with their own power source, or Wi-Fi alarms that text your phone, so you hear about a stuck float while the basin is still filling and not after waste has reached the basement floor. Strong winter storms can bring days of heavy rain to Northern California, saturating ground and pushing water into crawlspaces and sewer lines. Battery backup is an option.

    Residential Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Richmond

    Residential ejector systems in Richmond serve basement bathrooms, lower-level suites and any fixture that sits too low to drain by gravity to the sewer.
