    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Replacement in Richmond

    Costs vary by job. A tank water heater replacement typically runs $1,400 to $3,000, a water softener $1,500 to $3,500, a water line $1,800 to $5,500 and a sewer line $4,000 to $15,000 or more.

    Climate, freezes and soil type all shape how long plumbing lasts. Coastal fog and moisture can speed corrosion on exposed fittings, so outdoor valves and hose bibs deserve an occasional check. Freeze-thaw cycles can damage pipes, and corrosive or shifting soils can accelerate pipe degradation. Earthquakes and hillside soil movement are real risks in Northern California, so flexible connections and seismic straps help protect plumbing from sudden movement. Our replacement solutions are chosen to withstand these local conditions for lasting durability.

    We mainly use PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) and copper piping for water supply lines. For sewer and drain lines, we use durable PVC (polyvinyl chloride) or ABS (acrylonitrile butadiene styrene) pipes, depending on the application and local codes.

    Key signs include frequent, recurring leaks in the same area, discolored water, very low water pressure throughout the house, heavy corrosion visible on pipes or a major component like a water heater being over 10 to 15 years old and failing. If repair costs approach the cost of replacement, replacement is often the better long-term investment.

    Replacing old pipes with PEX or copper offers many benefits. PEX is flexible, corrosion-resistant and less prone to bursting in freezes, while copper is durable and long-lasting. Both improve water quality, eliminate leaks from old pipes and can significantly boost water pressure and flow throughout your Richmond home.

    Most tank water heater replacements take two to four hours. Code upgrades or relocations can take longer.

    If you have isolated leaks on newer pipes, a repair might be enough. However, if you have older galvanized, polybutylene or lead pipes, or if leaks are widespread and frequent, replacement is usually recommended. Our plumbers can conduct an inspection, often with camera technology for sewer lines, to provide an accurate assessment and recommendation.
