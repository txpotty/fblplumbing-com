    Root Removal From Sewer Lines in Richmond

    Trees send roots toward any moisture they can find. A sewer joint is a steady source. Our root saw cuts the mass loose, and heavy growth gets a jetter pass to clean out the fine hairs, and a camera shows where roots are entering so you can choose between lining and repair.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Richmond

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. A cable only makes a path. Water at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI strips grease, scale and fine roots off the inside of the line and flushes it all toward the street, which leaves the pipe close to its original diameter.

    Video Inspection Before Cleaning in Richmond, CA

    Some lines need a look first. Fragile pipe gets the camera before the cutter. Seeing the pipe first lets us pick the cheapest method that will work, and avoid damaging a line that is already failing.

    Clogged Main Sewer Line Solutions in Richmond, CA

    Several drains backing up at once is a main line problem. Every drain shares that one pipe. We clear it with a sectional cable or a jetter, then camera the line to see whether the cause was roots, debris or a structural problem like a broken joint or an offset section.

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Richmond, CA
