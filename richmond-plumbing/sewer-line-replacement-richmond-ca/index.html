    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair and Replacement in Richmond, CA

    Trenchless technology, such as pipe bursting or pipe lining, offers a less invasive option to traditional methods. Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one, fracturing the old pipe in place. Pipe lining installs a new, durable resin liner inside the existing pipe. Both methods significantly reduce the need for excavation, preserving your yard and landscaping in Richmond, CA while providing a long-lasting solution.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Replacement in Richmond, CA

    To minimize disruption, trenchless sewer line replacement replaces pipes without extensive digging. We offer pipe bursting, where a new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward. This is ideal when protecting landscaping or driveways is a priority. The method works well for many homes in Richmond and can often be completed faster than traditional methods, using high-density polyethylene (HDPE) pipe known for its long life.

    Partial Sewer Line Repair in Richmond, CA

    Not every damaged sewer needs full replacement. If the camera shows a single break or one root-damaged joint, a spot repair may solve the problem. We locate the exact spot and depth, excavate a small area and replace that section. This costs far less than replacing the whole lateral. We only recommend it when the rest of the line is in good condition.
