    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Richmond

    A collapsed run or a long belly usually means we dig. We open a trench along the old line, pull the broken pipe and lay new PVC on compacted bedding at a steady fall of an eighth to a quarter inch per foot. Sod gets cut and rolled aside first when the lawn allows it. The inspector sees open pipe. Backfill goes in compacted lifts so the trench line does not sink into a ditch a year later.

    Sewer Permits and Code in Richmond, CA

    Sewer replacement nearly always needs a permit. We file it, schedule the inspection and meet the inspector on site, so you never have to take a morning off to stand in the yard. The inspector looks at pipe size, slope, bedding and cleanouts. Unpermitted sewer work can show up on a home inspection when you sell.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Replacement in Richmond, CA

    Orangeburg is tar-soaked wood fiber. It was installed in a lot of homes in the decades after the war, and over time it softens, blisters and squashes into an oval that catches everything going by. Clay is tougher but leaks roots. When the camera shows trouble in three or more places, a full replacement is usually cheaper than chasing spot repairs.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Richmond

    No replacement quote without video. We run a self-leveling color camera from the cleanout to the main and record every joint, crack, root mass and low spot along the way, with footage you keep. A sonde in the camera head lets us mark the depth on the lawn. That one step decides between a spot repair and a full run.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement in Richmond, CA

    Two holes instead of one long trench. With bursting, a steel head splits the old pipe into the soil and pulls new HDPE into the same path. Lining is the other trenchless route. Lining puts a resin tube inside the old pipe and cures it hard, but only if the host pipe is still mostly round and has no major sags.

    Emergency Sewer Replacement in Richmond, CA

    Sewage coming up a floor drain is an emergency. The first visit gets a camera in the line so we know if it has caved in or only needs clearing to buy time. If it has collapsed, we start permit and locate requests that day. FBL Plumbing can often run a temporary bypass. That keeps one toilet and the kitchen sink usable while the dig gets scheduled.

    Cured-in-Place Sewer Lining in Richmond

    Lining builds a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line first, often with a cutter and a jetter, then pull a resin-saturated felt tube into place and inflate it so it cures hard against the host pipe. Collapsed pipe will not work. Lining shines under finished basements and slabs. You will get a straight answer on whether yours is a candidate.

    How Pipe Bursting Works in Richmond

    A winch pulls the bursting head. Because the head is a little wider than the old pipe, it cracks it into the surrounding dirt as the new HDPE follows. You get a pit at each end. The fused pipe has no joints, so roots cannot get in again.
