    Disposal Installation Cost Comparison in Richmond, CA

    Cost differences come mostly from the unit and the prep work. The cheapest job is a Badger 5 swap with a cord kit. An Evolution Excel in a kitchen that needs a new flange and a lowered trap arm sits at the top, and most jobs land somewhere between. We put horsepower and warranty side by side.

    Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Richmond

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. Flip the switch and it runs until you flip it off. Batch feed only runs with its stopper turned in place, so nobody can reach into a running unit, which some families with small kids prefer and which needs no wall switch at all. Each type gets wired its own way.

    Badger Series Installation in Richmond, CA

    The Badger line runs from the 1/3 HP Badger 1 up to the Badger 5XP. Every Badger shares one quick-lock mount. That shared mount is why a Badger-to-Badger swap is often a 45-minute job, since the upper ring and flange can stay put if they are not corroded. Badgers ship without a cord, so we bring the cord kit.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Richmond

    An air switch puts a push button on the countertop or sink deck instead of a wall switch. The button pushes a puff of air down a thin tube. A control box under the sink reads that pulse and flips the power, keeping live wiring well away from the water. If there is a spare faucet hole, we use it, otherwise we drill.

    Commercial Disposer Installation in Richmond, CA

    Restaurant disposals are a different class of machine. A commercial disposer runs 1 to 5 HP on its own circuit, and many come with a wall control panel and a water solenoid. We set the unit, plumb the water feed and coordinate with the electrician on the circuit. Health inspectors look at these. Local code may also limit where a disposer can discharge relative to the grease interceptor, so we check that rule before the job starts.

    Badger Replacement for Richmond Homes

    Builder-grade kitchens almost always started with a Badger, and a worn one usually gets replaced with another. First the old unit comes off.     Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Richmond

    Nearly every InSinkErator has a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. The plug gets knocked loose and pulled out of the grind chamber. The hose clamps onto the inlet and runs through an air gap or a high loop, which keeps dirty sink water from siphoning back into the tub. We test both appliances at once.
