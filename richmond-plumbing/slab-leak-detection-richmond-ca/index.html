    Slab Leak Reroutes in Richmond

    Rerouting leaves the failed pipe in the slab. New pipe runs through the walls or attic instead, which avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than opening a finished floor. We usually use PEX because it handles heat and resists corrosion. Tile and hardwood floors make this the favorite.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Richmond, CA

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. That makes them harder to spot. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, so we isolate the cold side, map the pipe with a line tracer and listen along the route with a ground microphone.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Richmond

    Many newer homes sit on post-tension slabs. These slabs have steel tendons stretched under tens of thousands of pounds of force, and cutting one by mistake is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We scan for tendons with ground-penetrating radar or a rebar locator before opening anything, or recommend a reroute that never touches the concrete.

    Camera Inspection for Under-Slab Drains in Richmond, CA

    Drain lines under the slab can leak too. A broken cast iron or PVC drain will not move the water meter, but it can cause odors, soggy floors and slow drains, so we run a camera through the line to find cracks, separated joints and bellies. We show you the video before discussing any repair in your Richmond home.

    Supply and Drain Leaks Under the Slab in Richmond, CA

    A supply leak and a drain leak show different signs. Supply leaks are pressurized, so they move the meter, warm the floor on the hot side and run whether or not anyone uses water. Drain leaks only leak when water goes down, and they show up as odors, slow drains or damp floors near bathrooms. Each needs its own test in your Richmond, CA home.
