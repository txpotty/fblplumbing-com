    Condensing Unit and Neutralizer Service in Richmond

    High-efficiency condensing tankless heaters produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized before it goes down a drain. We inspect the condensate trap, line and neutralizer cartridge and replace the media when it is spent. A clogged trap can cause shutdowns or water leaking inside the cabinet. We also check that the line is protected where it runs outdoors.

    Recirculation and Combi System Service in Richmond, CA

    Many tankless units now include built-in recirculation pumps or buffer tanks to deliver faster hot water. These systems move more water through the exchanger and scale up sooner. We flush the recirculation loop, check pump operation and verify crossover or dedicated return line settings. Correct schedules can cut wasted energy while still giving you quick hot water.

    Rinnai Tankless Heater Servicing in Richmond

    Rinnai units are popular for their efficiency and compact design. Our Rinnai-specific maintenance includes cleaning the air intake filter, checking the flame sensor and verifying the operation of the bypass valve. We also inspect the combustion chamber and ensure proper gas combustion. Our technicians are familiar with Rinnai's diagnostic codes and can address any issues proactively, improving performance for your Richmond home.

    Isolation Valve Kit Installation in Richmond, CA

    Some tankless heaters were installed without service valves, which makes proper flushing very difficult. We install a set of isolation valves with purge ports on the hot and cold lines so future maintenance takes minutes. The kit also lets you shut off water to the heater without closing the main. It is a one-time upgrade that pays for itself in simpler service.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Richmond

    Most tankless descaling visits cost $175 to $350, depending on brand and access. Adding an isolation valve kit usually runs $150 to $300 more.

    Tankless water heater maintenance focuses mainly on descaling the heat exchanger and checking electronic components for efficiency. Hot water tank maintenance typically involves flushing sediment from the bottom of the tank, checking the anode rod for corrosion and inspecting the pressure relief valve. Both aim to prolong life but address different system needs.

    No, routine maintenance, such as flushing and inspection, usually does not require a permit in Richmond. Permits are usually only needed for new installations, major repairs that involve altering gas or water lines, or replacing an entire unit. We will advise if any permit is needed for complex repairs.

    Many tankless water heater manufacturers require proof of regular, professional maintenance to keep your unit's warranty valid. Skipping annual service can void the warranty, leaving you responsible for costly repairs that would otherwise be covered. Always check your specific warranty terms.

    With regular descaling, many tankless heaters last 15 to 20 years. Neglected units in hard water can fail in under half that time.

    The descaling process involves shutting off the unit, isolating the water lines and connecting a circulating pump to flush a mild acidic solution through the heat exchanger. This solution dissolves mineral deposits like calcium and magnesium, which are common in hard water, improving heat transfer efficiency.

    Most visits take one to two hours, including the descaling cycle. Installing isolation valves on the same trip adds roughly an hour.
