    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Richmond

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves without binding. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.

    Wayne Sump Pump Replacement in Richmond

    Wayne sump pumps offer a range of effective models, from entry-level to professional-grade. If your Wayne sump pump is failing, FBL Plumbing provides prompt Wayne sump pump replacement in Richmond.     Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Richmond

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in Richmond for their quiet operation and ability to sit fully submerged in the sump pit. When replacing a submersible pump, we carefully remove the old unit, clean the pit and install a new, properly sized pump. We make sure the check valve and discharge piping are correctly connected to prevent backflow and improve efficiency. That is key for handling heavy rainfall.
