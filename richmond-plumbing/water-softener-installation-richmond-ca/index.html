    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Richmond, CA

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. FBL Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. They work best on very hard water.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Richmond

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. We install salt-based softeners with a resin tank sized to your grains per gallon, a brine tank, a bypass valve and a drain line that ends at a code-approved air gap. Then we program it. We set regeneration overnight.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Richmond

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. It complements a softener. Filters get changed on schedule.
