    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Brentwood

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. We find it with a moisture meter, a thermal camera that shows the cool wet spot, and a pressure test on the supply lines. Then we cut a clean access hole. We replace the leaking fitting or pipe section. Wet insulation comes out, and fans dry the cavity before we close it up.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Brentwood

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, overlapped a foot at every seam and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and hold it in place with termination strip. Cheap fix, big difference.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Brentwood

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most reliable fix for a leaking basement, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing so water has somewhere to go. Expansive clay soils are common across Northern California, and seasonal swelling and shrinking can crack slabs and buried sewer laterals. Backfill goes in last.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Brentwood, CA

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head aimed at the house instead of the bed, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. After that the wall gets cleaned, dried and coated with a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.
