    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Brentwood

    Gray polybutylene pipe was installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s. It breaks down from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water, and fittings can crack with no warning. Some insurers raise premiums or decline coverage on homes that still have it. We remove polybutylene supply lines and replace them with PEX-A or copper. A completed repipe can also help when selling the home.

    PEX Repiping in Brentwood

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale buildup and expands slightly if water freezes, which helps in colder homes. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe for better flow. Systems can be run as a home-run manifold with a shutoff for each fixture or as a trunk and branch layout. PEX installs faster than copper with many fewer fittings in the walls. It is our most requested repipe material.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Brentwood, CA

    Many older homes in Brentwood, CA still have galvanized steel pipes, which are prone to internal corrosion over time. Our pipe replacement service targets these corroded sections, replacing them with new, non-corrosive materials like PEX or copper. This restores proper water flow and ensures clean, fresh water throughout your home.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Brentwood, CA

    Cast iron drain pipe in older homes can rust through at the bottom, while some ABS from the 1980s was made with defective resin that cracks. Under-slab drains can be tunneled, rerouted or lined depending on location. We explain the options with the camera footage on screen.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Brentwood

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping has become a common choice for residential pipe replacement due to its flexibility, affordability, and resistance to freezing.
