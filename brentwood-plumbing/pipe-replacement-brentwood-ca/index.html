    What Goes Into a Repipe in Brentwood, CA

    Whole-Home Re-Piping in Brentwood

    A whole-home repipe replaces every supply line from the main shutoff to each fixture, including the water heater connections. Most single-story homes take two to three days. We run new trunk lines through the attic or crawlspace, branch down to fixtures through small wall openings, with service restored each evening when possible. Then patching.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Brentwood

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. Scale and rust do not affect it. Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running hard. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings or PEX-b with crimp rings depending on the job, and we run it in home runs from a manifold or in trunk-and-branch layouts. Fewer joints, fewer leaks.

    Slab Leak Pipe Replacement in Brentwood

    Rerouting is often better than digging. Jackhammering floors is a last resort. We run new pipe through the attic or walls to bypass the leaking line under the concrete, and usually costs less than repairing in place. Fast too.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Brentwood

    Polybutylene is gray or blue plastic installed from roughly the late 1970s to mid 1990s. Chlorine breaks it down over time. Failures tend to be sudden splits at fittings rather than slow drips, and some insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Replace it.

    Pipe Replacement for Multi-Unit Buildings in Brentwood

    Apartments and condos share risers. Shared lines mean shared problems. We plan riser replacement in phases, one stack at a time, keeping each unit informed. Organized work.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Brentwood, CA

    Corrosion shows up as green or white crust on copper, rust on steel, or pinholes that drip. Water chemistry plays a role. We replace corroded sections or the whole system depending on how widespread the damage is, and we check the water heater connections and shutoffs since they often corrode too. Look at the joints.

    PEX Repiping in Brentwood

    PEX repipes go faster than copper. Flexible lines snake through walls with fewer openings, while manifold setups give you separate shutoffs. Popular choice.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Brentwood

    PEX bends around corners and comes in long rolls, so a typical repipe needs fewer fittings than copper would on the same layout. Freezing is uncommon along the coast and on valley floors in Northern California, but frosty nights do happen and can crack unprotected outdoor pipes. PEX can stretch a little in a freeze instead of splitting. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings where possible, which give a full-bore connection and hold up well over time. Faster install.
