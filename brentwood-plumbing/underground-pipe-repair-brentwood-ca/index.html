    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Brentwood

    Polybutylene was used in a lot of homes built in the 1980s and early 1990s. It fails without warning. The gray plastic turns brittle as chlorine in the water attacks it, with failures most common at fittings and tight bends. Patching it just moves the next leak. We use PEX or copper.

    Galvanized and Polybutylene Line Replacement in Brentwood

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside until little water gets through. Polybutylene splits instead. If your buried service is either one, patches are usually temporary, which is why we recommend a full swap to PEX or copper threaded along the existing route. You will notice the pressure.

    Storm Drain and Lateral Repair in Brentwood, CA

    Pipe Bursting Services in Brentwood, CA

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. New pipe follows the old path. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, and fused HDPE follows right behind it, often at the same size or one size larger than the original. It works on badly damaged lines. Lining cannot do that.

    Buried Sewer Lateral Repair in Brentwood, CA
