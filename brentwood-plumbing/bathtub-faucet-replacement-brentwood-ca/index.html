    Older homes hide odd tub plumbing. Expect rusted galvanized supplies, valves with no stops, and steel spout nipples that snap. Adapters for old pipe ride along. Replacing a short run of galvanized is common.

    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Brentwood, CA

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We replace Moen and Delta cartridges or old compression stems and seats, and we grease the O-rings with silicone plumber's grease so the handle turns smoothly for years. Old seats need a seat wrench. About an hour, usually.

    Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Brentwood

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently. A single-handle valve often takes new trim and a cartridge from the front, while a three-handle valve usually has to be cut out of the wall and replaced with a modern pressure-balance body. The valve gets identified before we price it. Two-handle models fall in between.

    Delta Tub Faucet Replacement in Brentwood, CA

    Delta tub valves are some of the easiest to update. Many Delta Monitor valves accept a new cartridge and trim from the front, and the MultiChoice universal rough-in lets you switch trim styles later without touching the pipes in the wall. Popular Delta cartridges are on the truck. Older Delta valves sometimes need full replacement.

    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Brentwood

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. On a single-handle faucet the cartridge seals have worn, and on a two- or three-handle faucet the stem washers and brass seats have worn, and either way the drip gets worse every month. Replacement parts stop it. Cracked bodies get a new valve.

    Tub Spout Replacement in Brentwood

    Spouts mount two ways. Slip-on spouts slide over a 1/2 inch copper stub and lock with a set screw underneath, while threaded spouts screw onto a 1/2 inch pipe nipple, and putting the wrong type on causes leaks behind the wall. We check yours before buying. Diverter spouts must match too.

    Tub Faucet Style Upgrades in Brentwood

    Wall-mount tub fillers are a popular upgrade. Swapping a standard spout for a long-reach filler or a handheld shower on a slide bar changes how the tub works, and both usually need a new valve or a diverter added inside the wall. Rough-in gets planned before trim. The trim goes on last.
