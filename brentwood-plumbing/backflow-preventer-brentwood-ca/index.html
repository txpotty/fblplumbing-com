    Frequently Asked Questions About Backflow Preventer in Brentwood

    Backflow preventer testing in Brentwood, CA typically costs between $75 and $150. This fee covers the technician's visit, the test itself, and the submission of required reports to your local water authority. Complex commercial systems or multiple devices may incur higher costs.

    We service Watts, Febco, Zurn Wilkins, Apollo and other common brands. Our trucks carry rebuild kits for common models. Less common parts can usually be ordered quickly.

    The lifespan of a backflow preventer usually ranges from 10 to 20 years, depending on the type, manufacturer, water quality, and maintenance. Regular testing and timely repairs can extend its life. However, certain components like rubber seals may need replacement sooner due to wear and tear.

    If your backflow preventer fails its annual test, you will typically receive a notice from the Brentwood water department requiring prompt repair or replacement. Our technicians can diagnose the failure, perform the necessary repairs, and retest the device so it passes inspection and avoids any service interruptions or penalties.

    Water providers may send follow-up notices, charge fees or eventually suspend service. Scheduling early avoids these problems. We can often fit urgent tests in on short notice.

    Backflow preventers stop contaminated water from flowing backward into the public supply during pressure drops. Irrigation, fire lines and commercial equipment are frequent sources of contamination. Water providers require them to protect public health.

    Signs of a failing backflow preventer include discolored water, strange odors from your tap, a sudden drop in water pressure, or visible leaks around the device itself. If you notice any of these, contact a professional plumber right away to assess the issue.
