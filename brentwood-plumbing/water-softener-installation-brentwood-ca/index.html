    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Brentwood

    Reverse osmosis systems remove dissolved solids, which can be high in some water supplies. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet.     High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Brentwood

    Modern water softeners offer improved efficiency, using less salt and water during the regeneration process.     Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Brentwood

    Salt-based (ion exchange) water softeners are the most common and effective solution for fully softening water. These systems use a resin bed to exchange hard minerals like calcium and magnesium with sodium ions. FBL Plumbing carefully installs various models of salt-based softeners, ensuring proper connection to your main water line, correct drainage setup and accurate programming for regeneration cycles in your Brentwood home.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Brentwood

    Accurate testing is essential for proper softener settings. We test your water for hardness, iron and chlorine before installation. Then we program regeneration frequency and salt dosage.
