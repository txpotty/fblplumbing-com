    Gas Burner and Venting Checks in Brentwood

    A blue flame is what we want to see. Yellow or lazy flames mean poor combustion, so we clean the burner and combustion chamber, check the thermocouple or flame sensor, and confirm the vent has a proper draft and tight connections. Loose vents leak CO. We test the gas connection too.

    Tune-Up Versus Replacement in Brentwood, CA

    A tune-up costs a fraction of a new heater. Installed tanks often run well over a thousand dollars, and regular flushing and anode replacement help a tank reach the high end of its rated lifespan, while tankless units can last many years with proper descaling. Old tanks may be done. We give you real numbers for both.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Brentwood

    Some water eats anode rods fast. Water chemistry can wear a rod out in a few years, so we check it every time. We swap worn ones. It keeps rust off the tank lining.

    Attic, Closet and Garage Units in Brentwood

    Location changes the risks. Attic heaters need pans and drain lines, and garage gas units often need elevation or protection. We check the pan and drain. We check venting and access.

    Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Brentwood

    Flushing is the heart of tank maintenance. Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running hard. We run a hose from the drain valve, open it up and let the tank drain until the water runs clear, then refill and check the anode rod, the relief valve and every connection for drips or corrosion. Plan on an hour. Less sediment means more years.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Brentwood

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment piles up around the lower element, it runs hotter than it should, and eventually it burns out, which is why you suddenly get only a few minutes of hot water. We test both elements. Thermostats and wiring get checked with the power off.

    Anode Rod Checks in Brentwood
