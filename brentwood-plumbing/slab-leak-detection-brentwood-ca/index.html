    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Brentwood

    Our technicians use highly sensitive acoustic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a leak under your slab. This method involves listening for the subtle sound of escaping water, even through concrete. It's particularly effective for pressurized water lines, allowing us to find leaks without unnecessary excavation and minimizing disruption to your Brentwood home.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Brentwood

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs that use steel cables under high tension. Cutting into one without care is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We locate the cables with scanning equipment before any repair access is opened. This lets us repair the leak without risk or recommend a reroute that avoids the slab entirely.

    Slab Leak Repair Through Direct Access in Brentwood, CA

    When a single leak is found on otherwise healthy pipe, direct access can be the most economical repair. We cut a small opening in the floor at the marked spot, repair the pipe and pressure test it. Then we patch the concrete and leave the area ready for flooring. Accurate detection keeps that opening as small as possible.

    Emergency Slab Leak Detection in Brentwood

    A sudden drop in water pressure, visible dampness or the sound of running water can signal an emergency slab leak.     Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Brentwood

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat speeds up corrosion and causes expansion and contraction. Signs include warm spots on the floor, a water heater that runs constantly and higher gas or electric bills. Thermal imaging shows the heat pattern spreading through tile or concrete. We confirm with a pressure test and acoustic listening before marking the spot.
