    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Brentwood, CA

    Older cast iron drains corrode and build scale on the inner wall, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. A jet clears that crust. Badly corroded cast iron with thin walls or cracks needs gentler settings or no jetting at all, and the camera tells us which before we start, along with whether the pipe is a candidate for lining.

    Sewer Camera Inspection Before Hydrojetting in Brentwood

    Jetting a broken pipe can make the break worse. The camera always goes in first. The video shows the pipe material, the condition of each joint, and any cracks, offsets or sags, which tells us the nozzle and pressure to use or whether we should not jet that line at all.

    Hard Water Scale Removal in Brentwood, CA

    Scale builds slowly inside drain pipe, narrowing it a little more each year. Rural properties around Brentwood often rely on private wells, which may need filtration for iron, sediment, or other minerals. Scouring nozzles remove the scale, and badly scaled cast iron may get a descaling pass too. You can see the change on video.

    Same-Day Jetting for Backups in Brentwood

    Sewage in a tub or floor drain is a main line blockage. Hold off on running water. We open the line, jet out the cause, and camera it to see if a repair is needed.

    Hydrojetting for Shops and Offices in Brentwood, CA

    Retail and office buildings tend to have long drain runs where buildup settles. Low-slope lines clog more. After-hours jetting keeps equipment out of hallways while tenants are working.

    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Brentwood

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. Jetting the main clears roots, grease and sludge all the way to the city connection. Four-inch mains get a heavy nozzle and high flow to push debris downstream.

    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Brentwood, CA

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. A jet scours the entire pipe wall. Snaking suits a one-time clog, while jetting fixes a line that clogs repeatedly.

    Sewer Lateral Jetting in Brentwood, CA

    The lateral from your house to the street handles every drain in the house, so buildup there causes backups everywhere at once. The whole lateral gets jetted to the tap. Settled low spots get extra passes, and we mark them on the video for future reference.
