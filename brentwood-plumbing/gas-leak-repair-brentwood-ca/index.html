    Commercial Gas Leak Repair in Brentwood

    Corrugated Stainless Steel Tubing Repair in Brentwood, CA

    CSST is the yellow-jacketed flexible tubing used in many newer homes, and it can be damaged by a nearby lightning strike if it was never bonded. The bond is a heavy copper wire tying the gas pipe to the house ground. Plenty of older installs lack it. Repairs use the same brand's fittings as the existing tubing, since manufacturers do not allow mixing parts. A splice that skips that rule fails the inspection.

    Gas Leak Detection in Brentwood

    Gas Line Replacement and Upgrades in Brentwood, CA

    Adding a gas range or a tankless heater can overload pipe that was sized years ago for just a furnace and a water heater. Sizing starts with the total BTU load and the longest run from the meter.     Propane Gas Leak Repair in Brentwood, CA
