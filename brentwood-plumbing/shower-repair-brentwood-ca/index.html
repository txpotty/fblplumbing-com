    Shower Diverter Repair in Brentwood, CA

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. A worn spout gate or a tired diverter stem lets water keep running from the spout, and the head pressure drops off. Usually a new spout fixes it. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Brentwood, CA

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. A hand auger through the strainer clears most of them, and a drum machine handles clogs that have moved past the trap. Drain cleaner usually misses the clog. We recommend a hair catcher once it is clear.

    Mixing Valve Repair in Brentwood

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. Over time the cartridge or spool sticks, and you get a shower that will not stay at one temperature or a handle that turns past where it should before the water warms up. We replace the cartridge. Valves with no parts left get a replacement plan and an honest look at the tile.

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Brentwood

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year.     Shower Valve Replacement in Brentwood

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Brentwood

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss.     Hot Water Pressure Problems in Brentwood, CA

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead.     Dripping Showerhead Repair in Brentwood

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. Water that keeps coming out after the handle is off has to be getting past a worn cartridge or seat, so replacing the showerhead alone will not stop it. The fix is in the valve. Leaks at the arm threads are different, and those get new thread tape and a snug turn.
