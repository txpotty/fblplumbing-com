    Annual Sump Pump Check-ups in Brentwood

    Regular annual maintenance is key for the longevity and reliability of your sump pump. Our technicians perform a thorough inspection, checking the motor, impeller, float switch and discharge lines for any signs of wear or blockage. We also test the system's operation by filling the pit with water to ensure it activates correctly and pumps water away efficiently, protecting your Brentwood home from unexpected flooding.

    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Brentwood

    Our team is experienced in maintaining submersible sump pumps, a popular choice in Brentwood due to their quiet operation and compact design. Maintenance involves pulling the pump to inspect the watertight seals, clearing the intake screen and checking the internal components for corrosion or wear. We also verify the electrical connections are secure and free from moisture, preventing electrical hazards and supporting longevity.

    Battery Backup Pump Maintenance in Brentwood

    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails. We load test the battery, check the charger and alarm and run the backup pump on its own. Most backup batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Brentwood, CA

    If your sump pump is acting up, making odd noises or not turning on, our emergency diagnostic service can quickly identify the problem. We use specialized tools to troubleshoot electrical issues, motor failures and float switch malfunctions. Prompt diagnosis and repair are essential to prevent water buildup, especially during unpredictable rainy seasons in Brentwood, CA.

    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Brentwood, CA

    The float switch is the most common reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get stuck.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Brentwood

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to premature wear. We make sure these vital parts are functioning correctly to maintain an effective water removal system for your Brentwood residence.

    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Brentwood, CA

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil around the slab or foundation.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Brentwood, CA

    A power outage during a heavy storm can render your primary sump pump useless. Our maintenance service includes complete testing of your battery backup sump pump system. We check the battery charge, verify the charger's function and confirm the backup pump activates when the main power is interrupted. This dual protection is vital for homes in Brentwood, CA where power interruptions can coincide with severe weather.
