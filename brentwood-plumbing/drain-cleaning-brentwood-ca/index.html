    Jetting Versus Cabling in Brentwood, CA

    Cabling and jetting solve different problems. A sectional cable is built to chop and pull a clog out. A jetter scrubs the pipe wall clean and flushes the debris downstream to the city main or septic tank, so we cable first on a hard root mass and jet afterward when the line has years of buildup on it.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Cleaning in Brentwood

    Coffee grounds and eggshells are a sink's worst enemies. Both sink to the bottom of the pipe and pack down hard. We cable from the trap arm or a cleanout and work the cutter back and forth until the head runs free, then checks the disposal and the dishwasher connection, since either one can push food into the line. Most kitchen sinks take less than an hour.

    Hydro Jetting for Drains in Brentwood

    Jetting does what a cable cannot. A jetter sprays the full circumference of the pipe at high pressure. A cable punches a hole, while a jetter cleans the pipe back to near its original diameter. It works best on a kitchen or main line that keeps clogging every few months no matter how often it gets cabled.
