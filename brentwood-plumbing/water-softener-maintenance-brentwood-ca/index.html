    Resin Bed Cleaning and Regeneration Optimization in Brentwood, CA

    The resin beads are responsible for ion exchange, removing hard minerals from your water. Over time, these beads can become fouled with iron or other contaminants, reducing their effectiveness. Our maintenance includes cleaning the resin bed and adjusting the regeneration cycle settings for maximum softening capacity. We make sure your system is regenerating efficiently for your water quality, providing truly soft water.

    Brine Tank Cleaning and Salt Bridge Removal in Brentwood, CA

    The brine tank is crucial for salt dissolution and regeneration. Over time, salt residue and other impurities can accumulate, forming a 'salt bridge' that prevents the system from properly regenerating. Our maintenance service includes thorough cleaning of the brine tank and removal of any salt bridges, so your system can draw brine effectively for full softening. This is a common issue we address in Brentwood homes.
