    Sewer Line Repair & Replacement in Brentwood, CA

    Sewer laterals fail from roots, settling, or plain age. Clay and cast iron lines from older homes crack at the joints, and roots find those cracks within a few seasons.     French Drain Installation in Brentwood

    A French drain is a gravel-filled trench with perforated pipe that collects groundwater and carries it away. Filter fabric around the stone keeps silt out. The pipe needs at least a 1 percent slope to a daylight outlet, a dry well or a sump, and the outlet end needs a grate so animals do not nest in it.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Brentwood

    A hydro jetter sends water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full pipe wall. Unlike a cable, the jet cleans the whole pipe wall, not just a hole through the clog. Restaurants need it most. A camera check comes first to confirm the pipe can take it.

    Sump Pump Services in Brentwood, CA

    A sump pump keeps groundwater out of basements and crawlspaces. We test the float, check the discharge line and check valve, and replace pumps that hum, run nonstop, or cannot keep up during a hard rain. Never pipe it to the sewer. Many sump floods come from a blocked discharge line, not a dead pump.
