    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Brentwood

    Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. Signs include crusted aerators, clogged showerheads, slow toilet fill and heater sediment, and we can test hardness during the visit. Then we explain your options. Softeners, filters and scale reducers all help, and we suggest the one that matches the reading we took.

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Brentwood, CA

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. Our plumber identifies the supply pipe, whether copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, poly or Kitec, and explains how each usually fails. Certain types need a plan. Polybutylene and Kitec are known for sudden failures, and galvanized slowly closes up with rust, so those get flagged with replacement options.

    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Brentwood, CA

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. High pressure ages fixtures fast. Anything over 80 psi cuts the life of hoses, fixtures and heaters, and we note the supply pipe type, from copper and CPVC to poly, Kitec and galvanized.

    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Brentwood, CA

    Inland valleys get hot summers that work water heaters and exterior lines hard, while coastal areas stay mild and damp year round.
