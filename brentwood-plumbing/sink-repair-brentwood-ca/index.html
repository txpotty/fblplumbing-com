    Low Water Pressure at the Sink in Brentwood, CA

    Unscrew the aerator before anything else. Most single-faucet pressure problems are a packed aerator screen, and after that we look at the cartridge and the angle stop for debris. Low pressure everywhere points to a different problem. We test the pressure regulator in that case.

    Office and Restaurant Sink Repair in Brentwood

    Office breakroom sinks get abused in ways a home sink never sees. Coffee grounds and paper towels go down all day, and nobody checks the trap until water is on the carpet. A strainer basket goes in after we clear it. For restaurants, we keep three-bowl sinks running between the lunch and dinner rush.

    Leaking P-Trap Fix in Brentwood

    Check the slip nuts first. Overtightened plastic slip nuts crack, and the beveled washers inside them flatten until each joint weeps. The whole trap gets replaced as a kit. A 1-1/4 inch kit for bath sinks, 1-1/2 inch for kitchens.

    Pop-Up Stopper Repair in Brentwood, CA

    Stoppers that will not hold water have a linkage problem. Either the clevis slipped on the lift rod or hair has wrapped the stopper so it will not seat. Cleaning fixes half of them. The other half get a new pop-up drain in a matching finish.

    Cracked or Loose Basin Repair in Brentwood

    Undermount clips let go more often than people think. Failed epoxy lets the bowl sag a little, the caulk splits, and counter water runs right into the cabinet. New clips go in and the bowl gets pulled tight. Hairline cracks in porcelain near the drain mean replacement.

    Sink Parts Replacement in Brentwood, CA

    Most sinks do not need replacing. A new tailpiece or pop-up assembly costs a fraction of a new sink and installs in an hour, and the bowl and faucet you already like stay right where they are. Finishes get matched when the part exists. Discontinued faucets sometimes force a full faucet swap.

    Faucet Base Leak Repair in Brentwood

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. That points to worn O-rings on the spout or a bad base gasket, and sometimes to a cracked faucet body that cannot be rebuilt. Each faucet style rebuilds its own way. We identify yours before ordering parts.

    Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in Brentwood

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. Even in mild areas, wrapping irrigation backflow devices before a frosty night is cheap insurance against a surprise leak. UV light cracks plastic traps and supply lines left outdoors, and dirt from the yard clogs the drain. Hose bib leaks are part of the same call. A frost-free sillcock is worth it on exposed walls.
