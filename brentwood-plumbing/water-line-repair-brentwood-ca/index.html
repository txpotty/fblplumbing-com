    What Our Water Line Service Covers in Brentwood, CA

    Main Water Line Repair in Brentwood

    Your main line is the pipe from the meter to the house, usually 3/4 inch or 1 inch, buried anywhere from a foot to several feet down depending on frost depth. A leak here shows up as a soggy strip of lawn and a meter dial that never rests. We start by closing the house shutoff and watching the dial. A moving dial with the house valve closed means an underground leak. Then a ground microphone narrows the spot, so the crew opens one small pit rather than trenching the whole run.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Brentwood

    A trench through a paver driveway or a mature garden can cost more to restore than the pipe itself. Trenchless avoids most of that. We dig a pit at each end and pull HDPE or PEX through with a boring tool or pipe puller, following the old line or cutting a new path beside it. Most replacements finish in a day. You also get the manufacturer warranty on the new pipe.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Brentwood

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Copper became standard in many mid-century homes here, and decades later pinhole leaks and worn fittings are a common repair call. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. We pinpoint the leak with acoustic gear and thermal imaging. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or abandon the old pipe under the floor and run new PEX above it. We price each one for you.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Brentwood

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. Straight runs are ideal. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, while new HDPE comes through right behind the head. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.

    After-Hours Water Line Breaks in Brentwood, CA

    Water lines tend to break on a Saturday night. We answer around the clock. FBL Plumbing sends a plumber who can shut off the curb stop, pump the hole and make a permanent repair on the spot, which saves you from a weekend on a temporary patch. If the break is on the utility side of the meter, we tell you right away and help you get the provider out. It is more common than most people expect.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Brentwood, CA

    One leak on a newer copper or PEX line is a spot repair. Two or more leaks on galvanized is a replacement. Age matters too, and a line past about 40 years is usually near the end no matter the material. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars while a full replacement costs more and resets the clock for decades. You see the cut-out section yourself.

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Brentwood

    Trenchless work makes sense when the line runs under a driveway, patio or a row of mature shrubs. We dig a pit at the meter and another at the house. The new line travels between them without a trench. A pneumatic boring tool or a cable puller drags HDPE or PEX through the soil, or through the old pipe if it is a straight run, usually in one working day from start to backfill.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Brentwood

    Buried copper is tough, but it is not forever. Rocks pressing against the pipe wear pinholes over time, and acidic soil or high water velocity can thin the wall from the inside. A two-foot section is typical. The new piece goes in with soldered or pressed couplings, then we look over the pipe on either side for more pitting before we backfill.
