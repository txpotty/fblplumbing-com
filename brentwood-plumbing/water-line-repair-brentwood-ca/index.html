    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Brentwood, CA

    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Brentwood

    Underground water line leaks are often hard to detect until significant damage has occurred. Common signs include sudden spikes in your water bill, damp spots in your yard, or a noticeable drop in water pressure. Our team uses acoustic leak detection and ground-penetrating radar to precisely locate these hidden leaks in Brentwood, reducing the need for extensive excavation.     Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Brentwood

    Slab leaks, where water lines running beneath your home's concrete foundation leak, are a particularly challenging issue. Copper became standard in many mid-century homes here, and decades later pinhole leaks and worn fittings are a common repair call. These leaks can undermine your foundation, leading to major structural damage. We use specialized listening devices and camera inspections to pinpoint slab leaks precisely and offer repair options including direct slab penetration, re-routing, or pipe lining. Addressing a slab leak quickly in Brentwood is crucial to prevent extensive, costly foundation damage.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Brentwood

    If your service line runs under a driveway, pool deck or mature landscaping, trenchless replacement can save a lot of restoration cost. We dig small pits at each end and pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through using a pneumatic boring tool or pipe pulling equipment. The process usually takes one day. You keep your pavers and trees, and the new line carries a lengthy manufacturer warranty.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Brentwood, CA

    A single leak on a newer copper or PEX line is usually a simple spot repair. Repeat leaks, galvanized pipe or a line older than about 40 years usually justify full replacement. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars, while a full replacement costs more but resets the clock for decades. We show you the condition of the pipe we expose so you can make the call with real information.
