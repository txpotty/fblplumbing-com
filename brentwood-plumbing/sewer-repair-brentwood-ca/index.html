    What Sewer Repair Covers in Brentwood, CA

    Pipe Burst & Collapse Repair in Brentwood, CA

    A collapsed sewer pipe stops all flow at the point of failure. Every drain backs up. Collapses usually come from corroded cast iron, crushed clay under a driveway or tar-paper pipe that has finally flattened, and we locate the break and its depth before we start digging. We dig at the mark and replace the broken length with PVC. Lining cannot fix a full collapse.

    Emergency Sewer Repair in Brentwood

    Raw sewage on a basement floor is not a next-week problem. We answer at night and on weekends. The first goal on an emergency call is to get the line flowing, usually with a cable or jetter, and then camera it to find out whether you have a clog or a structural failure that will back up again tomorrow. The real repair may be scheduled for the next morning. In the meantime, we flag which drains to avoid.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Brentwood, CA

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. The joint is the real problem. After clearing the line, we look at each rooted joint on camera and decide between a short sectional liner, a full liner or cutting in new pipe, depending on how many joints are affected and how far apart they sit. One or two joints is a small job. Ten or twelve usually justifies lining the run.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Brentwood, CA

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Usually two small pits. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one and splits the old pipe outward, while cured-in-place lining pushes a resin-soaked felt tube into the old pipe and hardens it into a new wall. Both save driveways and mature trees. We tell you which one your pipe can take.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Brentwood

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. A fully collapsed section is one example. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. We see this most in older neighborhoods with clay pipe. The dig is staged so the house is without a sewer for as little time as possible.

    Commercial Sewer Repair in Brentwood

    Multifamily buildings and offices put several units' worth of flow through one lateral. Small defects turn into big backups. We repair larger diameter lines, add two-way cleanouts so future maintenance is easy, and give managers recorded footage for owners, insurers and their own files. Night and weekend work is available.

    After-Hours Sewer Repair in Brentwood

    Main lines like to fail on holidays. Our phones stay on. An after-hours call gets a tech with a cable machine and a camera, which is enough to restore flow in most cases and to find out whether the trouble is a blockage or a structural break. If it is structural, we schedule the dig for the earliest safe window. We avoid digging blind at night near gas and electric lines.

    Sewer Line Backups and Blockages in Brentwood

    Water coming up in the tub when the washer drains is a classic main line symptom. The clog is past the branches.     Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Repair in Brentwood

    Clearing a clog can be as little as $300. Structural repairs cost more. Replacing a long run with trenchless methods can run $6,000 or higher, with the total set by footage, access, material and how much digging is involved. We price it once the footage is in.

    A rental auger can open a soft clog. Structural damage stays. Forcing a cable through a broken or offset joint can snag, tear the pipe further or leave a cutter head stuck underground, which turns a camera visit into an excavation job. Camera first if the clog keeps coming back.

    Watch for clogs that keep coming back in more than one drain. Gurgling toilets are another clue. A sewer smell in the yard, a strip of grass that is greener than the rest, or water rising in the shower when the washing machine empties usually mean the main line is involved, not just one drain. Video tells you for sure.

    Digging up and replacing a lateral means trenching near gas and electric lines. It also means raw sewage. The new pipe has to hit the right slope, sit on proper bedding and connect with the correct transition fittings, which is why many cities only permit licensed plumbers to do it. Leave the cable machine work to us too.

    Clay, cast iron, ABS, PVC and Orangeburg all come up in Brentwood and around Northern California. We work on all five. Joining new PVC to old clay or cast iron takes a shielded transition coupling sized to the outside diameter of each pipe, and getting that fitting right is what keeps the repair from leaking at the joint later. We stock the common sizes.

    We use Schedule 40 PVC or ABS that meets local code. Transitions get shielded couplings. The coupling has to match the outside diameter of both pipes, and for lining work we use epoxy-saturated liners that cure into a rigid, jointless pipe inside the host. Everything we use is listed for underground sewer.

    For a spot repair, you lose the sewer for a few hours. Replacements take longer. On a full replacement we stage the work so the house is reconnected each evening where possible, so you know exactly what you can run and what to hold off on. Most families stay home through it.

    One slow sink is usually a local clog. Multiple fixtures point to the main. If the toilet bubbles when the tub drains, or the lowest drain in the house backs up whenever someone runs water upstairs, the blockage is almost certainly past the point where the branches join the main line. Time to put a camera in it.
