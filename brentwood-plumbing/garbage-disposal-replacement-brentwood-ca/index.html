    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Replacement in Brentwood

    The cost for garbage disposal replacement in Brentwood, CA typically ranges from $200 to $600. This includes the new unit itself, which can vary from $100 for a basic 1/3 HP model to over $300 for a high-end 1 HP unit, plus labor costs. Factors like the disposal's horsepower, brand, and any necessary plumbing modifications can affect the final price.

    Leaks from the body, frequent resets, constant jams, loud grinding and age over ten years are common signs.

    Most disposals last 8 to 12 years. Hard water, heavy use and grease can shorten that life.

    Most replacements take about an hour. Upgrades with new electrical or drain changes may take longer.

    If your current disposal jams often or struggles with food waste, a 3/4 or 1 HP model can make a real difference.

    Generally, a direct replacement of a garbage disposal with a similar unit does not require a plumbing permit in Brentwood, CA. However, if there are significant modifications to your kitchen plumbing or electrical system involved, a permit might be necessary. Our plumbers are knowledgeable about local codes and will let you know if any permits are needed.

    If your disposal is old, frequently leaking, or making grinding noises without grinding food, replacement is usually more cost-effective and provides a longer-lasting solution. Minor issues like a simple jam can usually be repaired.

    A continuous feed disposal operates when you flip a wall switch, allowing you to add food waste while it's running. A batch feed disposal only operates when a special stopper is inserted into the drain, making it usually safer as it prevents accidental activation while hands are near the opening.
