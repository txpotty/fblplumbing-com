    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Seaside

    Rooting a sink, tub or toilet usually costs $125 to $300. Main sewer rooting through a cleanout usually runs $200 to $500. With hydro jetting added, expect roughly $450 to $1,000, depending on how long the line is and how heavy the grease is. Nights and weekends run higher.

    Rooting uses a spinning cable with cutting blades to break up clogs and roots. Jetting is different. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scrub the pipe walls clean. Tough lines often get both, rooting to open the path and jetting to clean out what remains.

    Drain snaking, also called augering, uses a flexible cable and cutting head to break up or pull out the clog. Hair, wipes and roots respond well to it. Jetting scours the whole pipe with high-pressure water and strips the grease, scale and sludge a cable slides past. For recurring clogs, jetting is the better bet.

    Chemical drain openers rarely clear a real clog and can eat at old pipe. Old cast iron and the thin brass traps found in Seaside's older homes take the most damage. They also make our job riskier. Let us know if you used a chemical, because that water splashes when the trap comes off.
