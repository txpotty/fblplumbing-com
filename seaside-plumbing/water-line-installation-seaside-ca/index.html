    We prioritize high-quality, reputable brands for our water line installations. For PEX, we often use Uponor Wirsbo or Viega PureFlow. For copper, we use Type L or Type K copper from trusted manufacturers. Our material selection focuses on durability and performance in your local conditions.

    For most homes, PEX and copper are usually the best materials. PEX is flexible, corrosion-resistant and handles minor ground shifts well. Copper is very durable, long-lasting and resists bacterial growth. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. The choice often depends on budget, water chemistry and site conditions.
