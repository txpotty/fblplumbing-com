    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Seaside

    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Earthquakes and hillside soil movement are real risks in Northern California, so flexible connections and seismic straps help protect plumbing from ground movement. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use advanced detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Repipe Specialists in Seaside

    The cost of a whole-home repipe in Seaside varies a great deal depending on the size of your home, the number of fixtures, and the chosen material (PEX or copper). Generally, you can expect to pay between $4,000 and $15,000 for a PEX repipe, and $8,000 to $25,000 or more for copper. We provide a detailed, itemized quote after an on-site assessment.

    Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. Hard water can lead to mineral buildup, or scale, inside pipes over time. This buildup constricts water flow, reduces pressure, and can speed up corrosion, especially in older metal pipes. Repiping with materials like PEX or properly installed copper, along with considering a water softener, can reduce these effects and extend the life of your plumbing system.

    Common signs you need repiping include persistent low water pressure throughout your home, discolored or rusty-looking water, frequent pipe leaks, visible corrosion on exposed pipes, and a metallic taste or odor in your water. If your home is over 50 years old with original pipes, it's a smart move to have them inspected, especially if they are galvanized or polybutylene.

    We provide a written workmanship warranty, and PEX manufacturers such as Uponor offer long-term warranties when certified installers use their system. We explain the details before work begins.

    A documented repipe can make your home easier to sell and insure, especially when replacing polybutylene or Kitec. Buyers and inspectors look for it.

    Repiping is a complex plumbing project that requires specialized tools, knowledge of plumbing codes, and real experience. Attempting a DIY repipe can lead to costly mistakes, leaks, and potential code violations. It's strongly recommended to hire professional repipe specialists like FBL Plumbing to make sure the job is done correctly, safely, and to code.

    A whole-home repipe requires permits, pressure testing and code knowledge. Most homeowners are better served by a licensed crew that can finish fast and pass inspection.
