    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Seaside, CA

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already penetrated the foundation walls. This typically involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it effectively stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and works well where exterior access is limited.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Seaside

    Crawl space encapsulation involves sealing the crawl space entirely with heavy-duty vapor barriers and often includes a dehumidifier. This not only waterproofs the walls but also controls humidity, stopping mold growth and wood rot in the crawl space. Coastal fog and moisture can speed corrosion on exposed fittings, so outdoor valves and hose bibs deserve an occasional check. An encapsulated crawl space can noticeably improve indoor air quality and energy efficiency by keeping moisture from moving up into the home. We use durable, reinforced poly sheeting for walls and floors, sealed at all seams and penetrations.
