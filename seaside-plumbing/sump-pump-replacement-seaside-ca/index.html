    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Seaside, CA

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more long-lasting. We provide professional pedestal sump pump replacement in Seaside, CA, with proper alignment and secure mounting.     Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Seaside

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves freely. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats suit larger pits.
