    Water Heater and Appliance Leak Repair in San Ramon, CA

    Water heaters, softeners and washing machines often sit in basements, and their leaks pool on the floor out of sight. Once the tank itself leaks, it needs replacing. Leaks at the T&P valve, the drain valve or a supply connector can usually be fixed in one visit, and we add a drain pan or a leak sensor that shuts off the water automatically.

    Floor Drain and Backwater Valve Repair in San Ramon

    A floor drain that backs up when the washer drains or during a storm usually points to a blockage downstream or a failed backwater valve. After clearing the line, we run a camera to confirm it is open. Where the city sewer surcharges in storms, a backwater valve on the building drain keeps sewage from coming up through the floor drain, and it needs its flapper cleaned once a year. Dry traps smell.

    Foundation Crack Repair in San Ramon

    Hairline cracks in a poured wall are common and often stay dry, but a crack that seeps after rain needs attention. Earthquakes and hillside soil movement are real risks in Northern California, so flexible connections and seismic straps help protect plumbing from ground movement. We inject leaking cracks from the inside with polyurethane foam, which expands into the crack and stays flexible as the wall moves. Most injections take an hour or two.

    After-Hours Basement Leak Service in San Ramon, CA

    Basement leaks rarely wait for business hours. Tanks tend to split at night when nobody is watching. The on-call truck carries couplings, valves and a wet vac, so the first visit usually stops the leak and restores water to the rest of the house.

    Drain and Sewer Pipe Leak Repair in San Ramon, CA

    Cast iron drain stacks in older homes crack and scale from the inside out. We cut out cracked cast iron and splice in new pipe with shielded no-hub couplings. Plastic joints can fail too. Loose PVC joints usually trace back to poor support or a fitting that was never fully seated.

    Sump Pump Installation & Repair in San Ramon

    A sump pump lives in a pit cut below floor level and kicks on when a float rises. Most failures come from a stuck float switch, a burned-out motor, or a check valve that lets the discharge water fall back into the pit and short-cycles the pump. We test yours with a bucket. Add a battery backup if storms knock out power.

    Clogged Drain Tile Repair in San Ramon

    Footing drains clog with silt and roots over the years, and once they stop flowing the water table at the wall rises. We camera the tile from the sump inlet to see where it is blocked. Jetting clears it. If the old clay tile has collapsed, it gets replaced with new perforated pipe.
