    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Line Cleaning in San Ramon

    Sewer line cleaning in San Ramon typically costs $200 to $500 with a cable machine, or $400 to $900 with hydro jetting. Adding a recorded camera inspection usually runs $150 to $350.

    Key signs include multiple drains backing up at once, gurgling sounds from toilets or drains, a strong sewage odor in or around your home, and water pooling in your yard. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a licensed plumber.

    Common causes include tree root intrusion, buildup of grease and food particles, flushing non-biodegradable items like wet wipes or feminine hygiene products, and structural issues like cracked or collapsed pipes. Older homes often have clay pipes that are prone to root invasion.

    We use professional equipment from trusted brands such as Spartan Tool, RIDGID, and Gorlitz for our sewer cameras, drain snakes, and hydro jetting machines. These brands are known for their durability and effectiveness in tackling tough sewer line blockages.

    Hydro jetting is usually safe for most pipe materials, but it is crucial to have a professional assess your sewer line's condition first. We always perform a camera inspection to confirm your pipes can handle the high-pressure water without damage, especially for very old or severely corroded lines.

    We strongly advise against chemical drain cleaners for sewer line clogs. These products often contain harsh chemicals that can corrode pipes, especially older ones, and are generally ineffective against severe blockages like tree roots. They can also pose a health risk and make professional cleaning more difficult.

    Root treatment products can slow regrowth after a mechanical cleaning, but they do not remove a root mass. Cleaning first is the essential step.

    Rental machines can work on simple clogs, but they can kink or get stuck on roots. A pro also offers a camera to confirm the line is clear.
