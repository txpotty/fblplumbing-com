    Backflow Preventer Installation in San Ramon

    Irrigation systems and fire sprinkler lines are the usual reasons a home needs a backflow preventer. It keeps fertilizer-laced lawn water from being pulled back into the drinking supply when a main break or hydrant flush drops pressure. Type matters. We pick the assembly for the hazard level and local requirement and test it on install day.

    Gas Line Installation in San Ramon, CA

    A new range or an outdoor kitchen all start with the same math: total BTU load and the length of the run. From there we size the pipe, choose between black iron and CSST, and dig the trench for any buried section. Each joint is checked with leak solution. After that, the line sits on a pressure gauge until the inspector signs off.

    PEX Pipe Installation in San Ramon, CA

    PEX bends around corners, shrugs off corrosion and needs far fewer fittings than copper. In a repipe, fewer fittings means fewer hidden joints and fewer chances for a leak to soak a wall. It can also stretch when water freezes. Mountain and foothill homes in Northern California see real winters, so pipes there need insulation, heat tape, or proper draining when a cabin sits empty. That stretch is not a license to skip insulation, so exterior-wall and crawl-space runs still get wrapped.

    Backflow Assembly Testing and Installation in San Ramon

    Fire lines and irrigation systems account for most of the backflow assemblies we install. Each one is tested right after installation by a certified backflow tester, and the report goes to the water purveyor. Then the clock starts. Most jurisdictions want a retest every 12 months, and we can put you on a reminder list so it does not slip.

    PEX Pipe Installation and Repiping in San Ramon

    Pinhole leaks in copper keep coming back once the pipe walls start to thin. Repiping in PEX stops the cycle. The layout is a manifold with home runs for balanced pressure, or trunk and branch when the framing makes home runs a fight. We cut small, clean access holes and patch them, or hand off to your drywall contractor if you prefer.

    Whole-House Water Softener Installation in San Ramon

    Rural properties around San Ramon often rely on private wells, which may need filtration for iron, sediment, or other minerals.
