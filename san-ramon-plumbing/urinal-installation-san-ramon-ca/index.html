    Residential Urinal Installation in San Ramon, CA

    Basement and garage bathrooms are where most home urinals go.     Home Gym and Garage Urinals in San Ramon, CA

    A garage urinal saves tracking dirt into the house. The drain ties to the nearest stack, a vent or AAV goes in where allowed, and a compact bowl fits the space. Freeze protection matters in garages. Ask us about the layout.

    Sensor Urinal Installation in San Ramon, CA

    Sensor valves stop missed flushes and double flushes. We install battery or hardwired units, set the sensing range so the valve does not fire when someone walks past, and turn on a sanitary flush cycle if the restroom sits empty for long stretches. Expect years between battery changes. Hardwired units need an electrician first.

    Commercial Urinal Installation in San Ramon

    Offices and restaurants want urinals that survive heavy use. Each unit goes on a carrier with new gaskets and stainless bolts, paired with a matched Sloan or Zurn valve. Partitions get spaced to code. Most codes want 15 inches from centerline to the side.

    American Standard and Kohler Urinal Installation in San Ramon

    American Standard and Kohler make the china we install most. Models like the Washbrook and the Bardon come in versions from 0.125 to 1.0 gallons per flush, and each one needs a flush valve matched to that rating or the bowl will either splash or not rinse clean. The spec sheet makes matching easy. We order both together.

    Waterless Cartridge Urinals in San Ramon

    Each waterless brand uses its own cartridge. Falcon, Waterless Co, Kohler and Zurn units all trap odor differently, and the cartridge or sealing liquid has to be replaced after a set number of uses, which in a busy restroom can mean every few months. A replacement schedule comes with the install. Spare cartridges should stay on site.

    Drainage and Venting for Urinals in San Ramon, CA

    Urinal drains clog with scale, not paper. Low-flow and waterless units leave mineral buildup in the line, so proper slope and a cleanout matter. A proper vent keeps odor out. We tie in within code distance.
