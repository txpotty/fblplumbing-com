    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in San Ramon

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit typically costs $600 to $1,500. Installing a new pit with slab cutting and excavation often costs $1,500 to $4,000. Battery backups usually add $500 to $1,500.

    Not every home does, but homes with basements, crawl spaces, sunken patios or lots that collect storm runoff can benefit. A sump pump protects against fast-rising storm flooding.

    Most standard sump pump installations can be completed within 3 to 5 hours. However, if a new sump pit needs to be excavated or if there are extensive plumbing or electrical adjustments, the process might take a full day. We always aim for efficiency without compromising quality.

    A submersible sump pump sits entirely inside the sump pit, submerged in water, making it quieter and taking up less space. A pedestal sump pump has its motor mounted on a shaft above the pit, making it easier to reach for maintenance and typically more affordable. Both types are effective, and our plumbers can help you choose the best fit for your San Ramon basement.

    To keep your new sump pump running efficiently, test it monthly by pouring water into the pit to ensure it activates. Annually, inspect the discharge pipe for obstructions, clean the pit and check the float switch. Regular professional maintenance from FBL Plumbing can extend its lifespan in your San Ramon home.

    Water should discharge away from the foundation to a safe area on your property. Many cities do not allow sump discharge into the sanitary sewer.

    Strange noises, constant running, failure to turn on and visible rust are common signs. Testing your pump before storm season helps catch problems early.

    A simple replacement usually does not need a permit. New pits, electrical work or discharge connections may require one, and we will let you know.
