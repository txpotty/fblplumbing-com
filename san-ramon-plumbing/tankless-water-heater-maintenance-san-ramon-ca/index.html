    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Descaling in San Ramon, CA

    Noritz tankless heaters are known for their solid build. Our descaling service for Noritz units focuses on their intricate heat exchangers, which can be prone to scale buildup from mineral-rich water. We use manufacturer-approved descaling agents and follow precise flushing procedures to prevent damage. We also inspect the fan motor and exhaust system for proper airflow, a frequent area for attention in San Ramon.

    Navien Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in San Ramon

    Navien tankless water heaters often feature condensing technology, making them highly efficient but also requiring specific maintenance. Our tune-ups include checking the condensate trap for blockages, cleaning the secondary heat exchanger and verifying the operation of the circulator pump. We also confirm a proper air-fuel mixture and calibrate sensors for peak efficiency in San Ramon homes.

    Error Code Diagnostics in San Ramon, CA

    Codes like Rinnai LC, Navien E003 or Noritz 90 tell us a lot about what is going wrong. We read the code history, test flame rods, flow sensors and igniters and check gas pressure at the inlet. Many codes trace back to scale or a dirty filter and clear after maintenance. When a part has failed we explain the repair and the cost before doing anything.

    Inlet Filter and Air Intake Cleaning in San Ramon

    Every tankless unit has an inlet water screen that catches grit and scale flakes. Those screens can clog with sand and debris, especially after municipal line work. We pull and clean the screen and inspect the air intake filter, which can load up with dust and dryer lint. Clean filters keep the flow sensor reading accurately and the burner breathing properly.

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Tune-Ups in San Ramon, CA

    Noritz tankless water heaters are robust systems, but they still require annual tune-ups to maintain their performance through every season. Our maintenance includes checking the unit's exhaust system, ensuring proper combustion air intake and verifying the integrity of the heat exchanger. We also calibrate the temperature and flow settings for the best efficiency and comfort, preventing common issues like cold water sandwiches.

    Commercial Tankless Rack Maintenance in San Ramon

    Restaurants, salons and multi-family buildings often run several tankless units linked together. Commercial racks see heavy use and scale faster than home units. We descale each heater, check cascade controls and balance the load across the system. Quarterly or semiannual service keeps the hot water flowing during busy hours.

    Isolation Valve Kit Installation in San Ramon, CA

    Some tankless heaters were installed without service valves, which makes proper flushing impossible. We install a set of isolation valves with purge ports on the hot and cold lines so future maintenance takes minutes. The kit also lets you shut off water to the heater without closing the main. It is a one-time upgrade that pays for itself in simpler service.
