    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in San Rafael

    The old pipe gets cleaned first. After that, we insert the liner. We inflate an epoxy-soaked liner against the wall and cure it with heat or UV, leaving a smooth pipe with no joints. Lining works best for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in San Rafael

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. Once inside the cleaned pipe, it gets inflated against the wall and cured with heat or UV, forming a new pipe with no joints. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your San Rafael yard.
