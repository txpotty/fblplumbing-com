    Pipe Bursting in San Rafael, CA

    Pipe bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. A bursting head is pulled through the old pipe, fracturing it outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind. The new pipe is fused into one continuous length with no joints. Bursting requires an entry and exit pit but avoids digging the full length.

    Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in San Rafael, CA

    Pipe bursting is another trenchless method used for replacing severely damaged pipes. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is excellent for completely replacing a pipe segment with a new, stronger material, such as HDPE. It's often chosen when the existing pipe is collapsed or too damaged for lining. FBL Plumbing uses specialized bursting heads and winches for a smooth and efficient replacement, reducing the overall project time and cost for homeowners in San Rafael, CA.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in San Rafael, CA

    Main water lines can also be replaced with trenchless methods. We can pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through the old path using bursting or a directional boring tool. This avoids digging across lawns, driveways and walkways. The new line is pressure tested before service is restored.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in San Rafael

    CIPP lining is a popular trenchless method where we insert a resin-saturated felt tube into the existing damaged pipe. The liner is then cured in place using heat or air, creating a new, seamless pipe within the old one. This method is well suited for repairing cracks, breaks, and root intrusion without digging up your yard in San Rafael.
