    Type L Copper Repiping in San Rafael

    Copper has been plumbing homes for generations, and done right it lasts a long time. Our copper repipes use Type L with proper hangers and isolators so the pipe does not wear against framing. All joints use lead-free solder. Because some water chemistries eat copper, we look at your leak history before recommending it over PEX.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in San Rafael

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. Rerouting one branch overhead and leaving the bad pipe in the slab is often cheaper than both a repipe and a jackhammer repair. It suits a first-time leak. After multiple leaks, we will show you why a full repipe may cost less over the next ten years.

    Repiping for Hard Water in San Rafael, CA

    Rural properties around San Rafael often rely on private wells, which may need filtration for iron, sediment, or other minerals. Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe, and over years it narrows the bore and speeds up corrosion in metal lines. Pressure suffers. For homes with hard water, we usually recommend PEX-A, which resists scale and does not corrode, and we can add a softener loop or a whole-house filter during the repipe. Planning it now saves a second visit later.

    Condo, Townhome and Multi-Family Repipes in San Rafael

    Shared walls and HOA rules make repiping attached homes more involved. Our office works with your HOA on approvals and shutoff times, and we take steps to protect the units next door. Building-wide jobs go in phases. Big buildings get repiped by stack or wing, which lets residents stay home.

    Commercial Repipe Services in San Rafael

    Older commercial buildings in San Rafael often have galvanized risers, copper that has pinholed or plastic pipe that no longer meets code. Our crews swap out risers, branches and drains with as little interruption to tenants as the building allows. Shutoffs get scheduled ahead.     PEX Versus Copper Repipe Pricing in San Rafael, CA

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper because the material is cheaper and installation goes faster. Copper costs more. For a typical house, copper often runs 20 to 40 percent above PEX, though some buyers and homeowners like the look and feel of metal pipe and are willing to pay for it. Our quotes for either include permit, fittings, patching and cleanup.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in San Rafael, CA

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly. Brown water after the house sits overnight, weak flow at upstairs showers and leaks at threaded joints are the usual signs that galvanized is near the end. New PEX or copper takes its place. Owners usually notice stronger flow the first day.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in San Rafael

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. Type L tolerates UV, stays rigid and has decades of field history. It costs more. Our plumbers solder joints with lead-free solder or use press fittings, support the pipe at the spacing the code calls for and pressure test the system before any wall is closed.
