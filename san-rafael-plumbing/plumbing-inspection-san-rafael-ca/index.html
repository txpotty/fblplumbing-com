    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspections in San Rafael

    Our whole-home inspections focus on the problems we see most in Northern California houses.     Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in San Rafael, CA

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. Our plumber identifies the supply pipe, whether copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, poly or Kitec, and explains how each usually fails. Certain types need a plan. Polybutylene and Kitec are known for sudden failures, and galvanized slowly closes up with rust, so those get flagged with replacement options.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in San Rafael

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. Our checks include the relief valve and its discharge line, the expansion tank and the drain pan. Gas units get a CO check. A heater that backdrafts can spill combustion gases into the house, so we test the draft at the vent hood and note any problems right away.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in San Rafael
