    First-Time Disposal Installation in San Rafael

    For kitchens that have never had a disposal, we start by checking the sink flange, drain height and electrical supply.     Dishwasher Connection With Disposal Installation in San Rafael

    New disposals come with a knockout plug in the dishwasher inlet. We knock it out with a screwdriver and hammer, fish the plug out of the chamber and connect the hose with a new clamp. Forgetting the plug floods the dishwasher. An air gap or high loop goes in too, which keeps sink water from running back into the dishwasher.

    High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in San Rafael

    A 3/4 or 1 HP unit takes up more room and weighs more than a basic unit, so we measure the cabinet first. Taller units can push the discharge outlet below the wall stub, and when that happens we either lower the drain at the wall or pick a model with a higher outlet so water does not sit in the chamber. Power needs a check too. Bigger motors draw more at startup.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in San Rafael, CA

    An air button on the sink deck or counter turns the disposal on. The button pushes air through a tube to a control box in the cabinet, which keeps electricity away from the button and avoids a new wall switch. Islands use them often.

    Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in San Rafael, CA

    Going from batch-feed to continuous-feed means adding a way to switch it on. That can be a wall switch, an air switch button on the sink deck or a switched outlet under the sink, and the choice depends on how hard it is to run wire to the spot you want. An air button skips the wall wiring. The plumbing side is usually a straight swap.

    Disposal Rough-In for New Construction in San Rafael

    At rough-in we set the drain height and the outlet location before the cabinets go in. For a kitchen with a disposal, we usually set the drain stub lower than a plain sink would need, so the trap can sit below the disposal outlet with room for proper fall. The electrician adds the switched outlet. Its location gets marked to stay clear of the trap.

    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in San Rafael, CA

    Double bowl sinks put the disposal on one side and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. The layout keeps both bowls draining and stops the disposal from pushing water into the second bowl, a problem when the tee is too close to the discharge. We use heavy-wall fittings.
