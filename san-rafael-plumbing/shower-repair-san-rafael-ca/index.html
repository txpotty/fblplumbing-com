    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in San Rafael, CA

    Stains on the ceiling below are often the first clue. We use a moisture meter on the walls and a flood test on the pan to tell a leaking valve from a cracked pan, a failed grout line or a loose drain body. The source gets repaired. On slab homes, these leaks can spread into the flooring of the next room before anyone sees them.

    Slow Shower Drain Repair in San Rafael, CA

    Standing water at your ankles is the first sign. Minerals bind soap scum and hair into a tough mat that clings to the pipe walls and gets thicker with every shower. An auger cuts through it. For stubborn buildup in the branch line, we jet the line with water instead of using acid cleaners.

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in San Rafael

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Grit and scale scratch the sealing surfaces, the O-rings flatten and harden, and eventually the valve cannot fully stop the water when the handle is closed. A new cartridge fixes it. The new cartridge goes in with silicone grease, which keeps the handle smooth.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in San Rafael, CA

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. We pull the strainer and run a hand auger through the trap, and if the clog sits past the trap in the branch line, we switch to a drum machine with a smaller cable. Drain cleaner usually misses the clog. We recommend a hair catcher once it is clear.

    Emergency Shower Repair in San Rafael, CA

    A shower that will not shut off is an emergency. Close the main valve where the water line enters the house, or the shower's own stops if it has them behind an access panel. Then call us. FBL Plumbing sends a plumber with cartridges on the truck, and we can often get your water back on within the hour.

    Shower Drain Body Repair in San Rafael

    Drain bodies can crack or work loose under the pan. A loose or cracked drain lets water go under the pan, which shows up as a soft floor beside the shower or a ceiling stain. The drain gets cleared and tested. Loose drains get resealed, and cracked ones get replaced.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in San Rafael

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Minerals collect behind the face and inside the restrictor, which turns the spray patchy or weak with the valve fully open. Soaking helps sometimes. When it does not, we install a new head with soft rubber nozzles that you can wipe clean with a thumb.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in San Rafael, CA

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. That means a worn diverter steals pressure from the head, a clogged tub drain slows the shower and a leaky valve shows up at both the spout and the head. Each part gets tested. The overflow gasket gets a look too, since it is a common hidden leak.

    Swapping the head, yes. Cartridge replacements and leak repairs behind tile are better handled by a plumber to avoid damage.
