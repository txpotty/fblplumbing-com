    Electric Water Heater Installation in San Rafael, CA

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. Before hooking up the new tank we check that the breaker and wire size match the elements, since a mismatch is a common cause of tripped breakers and scorched terminals. They do not need a vent. That makes placement easier.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in San Rafael

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Every job gets a fresh connector, a drip leg, a shutoff and a vent connector with the right rise toward the chimney or flue. We test draft before leaving. Big families do well with gas.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in San Rafael

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. Every attic heater needs a drain pan with a line to the outside or a drain, a relief line routed properly and a clear path for the next tank to come in and out. A leak lands on drywall. If moving it makes sense, we can relocate the heater.
