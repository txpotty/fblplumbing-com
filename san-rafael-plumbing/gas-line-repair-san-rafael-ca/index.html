    What Our Gas Line Plumbers Fix in San Rafael, CA

    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in San Rafael, CA

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. Many tankless heaters are rated at 150,000 to 199,000 BTU, which can be more than double what an old tank heater used, so the line feeding it often has to be upsized back toward the meter. Load calculation comes before any pipe. Then we size the new piping so every appliance in the house still gets full pressure at the same time.

    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in San Rafael, CA

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. A pool heater can pull 250,000 BTU or more, which can rob gas from the furnace and water heater if the line was sized for a grill. We size outdoor lines for the full load. New and repaired lines get tested, buried to the required depth and laid with tracer wire.

    After-Hours Gas Line Repair in San Rafael

    Gas problems do not wait for business hours, and losing heat or hot water overnight is a real problem. We answer late calls. After you and your family are outside and the utility has made the scene safe, our plumber comes out to locate the leak, make the repair and run the pressure test. We coordinate with the utility on the meter.

    CSST Repair in San Rafael

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing is a flexible gas pipe with a plastic jacket, often yellow, that runs through attics and walls in newer homes. It can be damaged. Nails from a remodel, rodents chewing the jacket and nearby lightning strikes are the usual causes, and a damaged run has to be cut out and spliced with the manufacturer's own fittings. Bonding gets checked on every system. A bonding clamp and conductor tie the CSST to the home's grounding system, following the maker's instructions and code.

    Gas Permits and Inspections in San Rafael

    Repairs that change piping, and all new gas lines, usually need a permit and inspection from San Rafael building officials. We pull the permit, book the inspection and meet the inspector at the gauge so you do not have to. Inspectors often come within the week. Once it passes, the utility can restore service and we come back to relight appliances if needed.

    Propane Gas Line Service in San Rafael, CA

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. We repair buried and above-ground propane lines, replace leaking regulators and fittings and check that each appliance has been converted with the correct orifices for propane. Wrong orifices make a burner run dirty and unsafe. The test procedure is the same as for natural gas.
