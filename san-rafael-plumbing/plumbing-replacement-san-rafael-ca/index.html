    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Replacement in San Rafael

    Costs vary by job. A tank water heater replacement usually runs $1,400 to $3,000, a water softener $1,500 to $3,500, a water line $1,800 to $5,500 and a sewer line $4,000 to $15,000 or more.

    Key signs include frequent, recurring leaks in the same area, discolored water, very low water pressure throughout the house, extensive corrosion visible on pipes or a major component like a water heater being over 10 to 15 years old and failing. If repair costs approach the cost of replacement, replacement is often the smarter long-term investment.

    Equipment comes with manufacturer warranties, often 6 to 12 years for water heaters and longer for some pipe materials. We also warranty our installation labor.

    The timeline for plumbing replacement depends on the complexity of the job. A water heater replacement usually takes 2 to 4 hours. A main water line or sewer line replacement can take 1 to 3 days. Whole-home repiping projects typically span 3 to 7 days, depending on the size of the house and accessibility.

    Hard water causes scale and sediment, while heat and shifting soil stress pipes and equipment. Regular maintenance helps, but replacement sooner or later becomes the smarter choice.

    Replacing old pipes with PEX or copper offers many benefits. PEX is flexible, corrosion-resistant and less prone to bursting in freezes, while copper is durable and long-lasting. Both improve water quality, eliminate leaks from old pipes and can noticeably boost water pressure and flow throughout your San Rafael home.
