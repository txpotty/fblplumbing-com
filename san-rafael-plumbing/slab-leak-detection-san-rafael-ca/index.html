    Water and Sewer Line Slab Leaks in San Rafael, CA

    Slab leaks can affect both freshwater supply lines and sewer drain pipes. Our detection services are equipped to identify both types of leaks, which present different challenges. Sewer line leaks often involve foul odors and slow drainage, while water line leaks can appear as wet spots or high water bills in your San Rafael, CA home. We have specific methods for each.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in San Rafael

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat speeds up corrosion and causes expansion and contraction. Signs include warm spots on the floor, a water heater that runs nonstop and higher gas or electric bills. Thermal imaging shows the heat pattern spreading through tile or concrete. We confirm with a pressure test and acoustic listening before flagging the spot.

    Video Pipe Inspection for Sewer Leaks in San Rafael, CA

    Sewer line issues under a slab can also mimic slab leak symptoms. We use high-resolution video cameras to inspect the interior of your drain lines, identifying cracks, blockages or separations. This is vital if the issue isn't a pressurized water line, helping us determine if a sewer line repair is needed instead for your San Rafael home.

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in San Rafael

    Our technicians rely on highly sensitive acoustic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a leak under your slab. This method involves listening for the faint sound of escaping water, even through concrete. It's particularly effective for pressurized water lines, allowing us to find leaks without unnecessary excavation and minimizing disruption to your San Rafael home.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in San Rafael

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the slab and runs new pipe through the walls or attic. It avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than direct access under finished floors. We typically use PEX for reroutes because it handles heat and resists hard-water corrosion. This option is a good fit when the slab is covered in tile or hardwood.
