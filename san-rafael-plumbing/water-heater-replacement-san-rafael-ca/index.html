    Gas Water Heater Replacement in San Rafael, CA

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in San Rafael, CA homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Every joint gets checked.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in San Rafael, CA

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need space and airflow, often a room of about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they work best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.

    Bradford White Water Heater Replacement in San Rafael

    Bradford White sells through professional plumbers only, so you will not find one at a big-box store. Parts are easy to get.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in San Rafael

    Copper became standard in many mid-century homes here, and decades later pinhole leaks and corroded fittings are a common repair call.     Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in San Rafael

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is common, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually has to be upsized, and the old metal vent gets replaced with stainless or PVC run to the maker's specs.
