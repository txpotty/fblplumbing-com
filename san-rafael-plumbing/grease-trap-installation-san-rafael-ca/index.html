    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Installation in San Rafael

    The cost of grease trap installation in San Rafael, CA varies widely based on the type and size of the trap, whether it's an indoor or outdoor unit, and the complexity of the installation. For a small under-sink hydro-mechanical unit, expect to pay between $1,500 and $4,000. Larger outdoor gravity interceptors can range from $7,000 to $20,000 or more, including excavation and concrete work.

    Signs you might need a new grease trap include frequent drain clogs, slow drainage in multiple fixtures, persistent foul odors coming from drains, or if your current unit is old, corroded, or no longer meeting local code requirements. An inspection by a professional plumber can verify the need for replacement.

    While the terms are often used interchangeably, 'grease trap' usually refers to smaller, indoor hydro-mechanical units, often found under sinks. 'Grease interceptor' usually refers to larger, outdoor gravity-fed units with higher capacities. Both serve the same purpose of separating FOG from wastewater.

    The primary purpose of a grease trap is to intercept fats, oils, and greases (FOG) from commercial kitchen wastewater before they enter the public sewer system. This prevents pipe blockages, protects municipal infrastructure, and helps businesses comply with environmental regulations.

    Indoor traps should be close to the fixtures they serve and easy to open for cleaning. Outdoor interceptors are typically placed where a pump truck can reach them without blocking customer traffic.

    We install a variety of reputable grease trap brands, including Zurn, Big Dipper, Schier, and Rockford Separators.     Determining the correct grease trap size involves calculating your kitchen's wastewater flow rate, fixture count, and peak operating hours.
