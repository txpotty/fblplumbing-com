    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Roseville

    The cost of water heater replacement in Roseville, CA usually ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the type of unit (gas, electric, tankless or hybrid), its capacity and any necessary plumbing modifications. Our team will provide a detailed, upfront quote after assessing your needs and existing setup.

    Most new water heaters come with a manufacturer's warranty ranging from 6 to 12 years on the tank and components, depending on the model and brand. FBL Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation, so your investment is covered.

    Common signs include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, a metallic taste in hot water, strange noises like rumbling or popping from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit or a water heater that is over 10 to 12 years old. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a professional.

    Flushing a traditional tank water heater once a year is recommended in most homes. Rural properties around Roseville often rely on private wells, which may need filtration for iron, sediment, or other minerals. This helps remove sediment buildup, which can reduce efficiency and shorten the lifespan of the unit. Regular flushing can also prevent scale from damaging heating elements.

    Leaks at the base, rusty water, rumbling noises, inconsistent hot water and an age over 10 years are common signs. A leaking tank should be replaced right away.

    New water heaters usually come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and parts, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. FBL Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation, giving you added peace of mind. Specific warranty details vary by brand and unit.

    Most standard tank-style water heater replacements can be completed in 3 to 5 hours, assuming no major complications or plumbing reconfigurations. Tankless or hybrid installations might take a bit longer, often 5 to 8 hours, due to more complex venting or gas line changes.

    In general, if your water heater is over 10 years old, frequently breaks down or has a major issue like a leaking tank, replacement is often more cost-effective in the long run. For newer units with minor, easily fixable issues, repair might be a sensible option. Our technicians can provide an honest assessment.
