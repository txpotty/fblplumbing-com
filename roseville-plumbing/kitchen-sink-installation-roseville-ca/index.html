    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Roseville

    Sitting on the counter by their rim, drop-in sinks are a natural fit for laminate, butcher block and tile. It is often the quickest swap. After checking the cutout, we seal the rim and draw the clips down evenly, and in older homes and rentals it is often a two-hour job.

    Disposal and Dishwasher Hookup with Sink Installation in Roseville, CA

    Most new kitchen sinks get paired with a disposal and a dishwasher. The disposal gets mounted, the dishwasher drain goes through an air gap or high loop, and the dishwasher gets its own hot line and shutoff. One visit costs less than three. We test it all the way you will use it.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Roseville

    Undermount sinks rely on clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone and brace it while the adhesive sets, which usually takes overnight before the sink can hold a full load of water. Then come the strainers and trap.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Roseville

    A double bowl drains both sides through one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. The layout depends on your disposal and dishwasher, because the disposal side changes where the tee sits and how much slope each bowl gets. Heavy-wall fittings resist leaks.

    New Construction Sink Rough-In in Roseville, CA

    The rough-in decides where the drain and valves end up long before the cabinets arrive. We usually set the kitchen drain stub around 16 to 20 inches off the floor, then adjust for deep bowls or a disposal so the trap does not end up below the wall outlet. Venting happens at this stage. We work from your sink and faucet specs to avoid moving pipe later.

    Apron-Front Sinks in Roseville, CA

    Apron sinks need a cabinet cut to fit the front panel, and the bowl must be set before the counter gets measured. We set the sink and frame early, so the fabricator measures from the actual bowl instead of a spec sheet. That avoids an uneven reveal. A cast iron apron sink can weigh 150 pounds or more, so two of us show up.
