    Navien Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Roseville, CA

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Navien also makes combi units that heat the house and the water. We set up both.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Roseville, CA

    Pulling a 50 gallon tank out of a garage frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss. Gas is the catch, since tankless units usually need 3/4-inch pipe or larger and a new vent. Before quoting, we check the meter and pipe run, and afterward we cap the old flue and seal the roof. Plan on a full day.

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in Roseville

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. They run on a single 20 or 30 amp circuit and work well for a far bathroom or an office sink. Swaps take an hour or two.

    Whole-House Electric Tankless Replacement in Roseville

    Whole-house electric tankless performance drops when the incoming water is cold, since the unit has to raise the temperature further at the same amperage. We replace Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart units. A heat pump water heater is sometimes the better fit, and we will tell you.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Roseville

    Rinnai units we pull are usually 12 to 18 years old, and the failure tends to be a leaking heat exchanger or a dead control board. A current Rinnai often mounts on the same bracket. That keeps wall patching small. We match the vent type or convert to PVC on a condensing model.

    Leaking Tankless Replacement in Roseville

    A leaking tankless can drip inside the cabinet for weeks before anyone notices the puddle. Close the cold isolation valve and the gas cock first. We then find out whether it is a fitting or the heat exchanger, since one is cheap and the other is replacement.

    Tankless vs. Tank Replacement Cost in Roseville, CA

    Tankless costs more up front than a tank, often twice as much installed. Over 20 years it can come out close, since a tankless can outlast two tanks and skips standby loss, but the payback depends on how much hot water your house uses. You see both quotes.

    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Roseville

    A cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board on an older tankless is usually not worth repairing past the ten-year mark. The replacement gets matched to the existing gas, vent and mount where possible. Plan on four to six hours.
