    Wayne Sump Pump Replacement in Roseville

    Wayne makes everything from a thermoplastic quarter-horse pump at the hardware store to cast iron models with vertical switches, and the cheap ones are what we replace most. Most dead Wayne pumps we see have a cooked motor or a float tangled in the power cord. You get a matching Wayne or a sturdier cast iron model, your call. Takes about two hours.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Roseville

    If your pump runs every 30 seconds during a storm and still loses ground, it is undersized for the water coming in. We time the cycles and measure how fast the pit refills. The rest is simple arithmetic. A half-horse or three-quarter-horse pump with a higher gallons-per-minute rating at your lift height may be the answer, sometimes paired with a 2-inch discharge.

    Zoeller Switch and Pump Repair in Roseville

    Zoeller units with a piggyback float plug the pump cord into the switch cord, and if that piggyback plug fails the pump never gets power. That is the first thing we test. Some calls only need a new switch, while others need the whole pump. We tell you which before we sell you anything.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Roseville

    Basement bathrooms below the sewer line drain into a sealed, vented basin, and the ejector pump in that basin has to pass 2-inch solids. That is not a sump pump job. We replace the ejector and its discharge check valve, reseal the lid gasket, and confirm the vent ties in. A bad seal shows up as sewer odor.

    Elevator and Garage Pit Pumps in Roseville

    Elevator pits and parking garage sumps often require oil-sensing controls that stop the pump from sending hydraulic fluid or motor oil into the storm drain. We replace those pumps and controls to meet code. Duplex setups with alternating pumps give you a spare if one quits. Some insurance carriers ask for test records.

    Commercial Sump Pump Service in Roseville

    Commercial sumps run harder than residential ones, and a restaurant or office basement often has a duplex system with two pumps that alternate on a controller. Both pumps and the alternator get swapped together. Work can be scheduled early or after closing so nobody is stepping around an open basin.

    Sizing a Submersible Sump Pump in Roseville

    Horsepower matters less than people think. For most houses a 1/3 HP pump is enough, provided the water only has to climb 10 to 15 feet to get out. We check the flow chart against your actual lift before we pick a size. Oversize it and it short cycles.
