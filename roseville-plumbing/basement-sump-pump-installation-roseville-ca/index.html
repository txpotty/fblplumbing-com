    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, built to handle everyday water removal. Both work together for the best protection.

    Older neighborhoods in Northern California include prewar and postwar homes with galvanized supply lines and cast iron or clay sewer pipe that are past the end of their life.     Strong winter storms can bring days of heavy rain to Northern California, saturating ground and pushing water into crawlspaces and sewer lines.
