    Water Softener Installation in Roseville, CA

    Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe.     Expansion Tank and Sewage Ejector Installs in Roseville, CA

    PEX Repiping in Roseville

    Copper repipes cost more and take longer, which is why most whole-house repipes today use PEX-A or PEX-B tubing with brass or poly fittings. You also hear less of it, since PEX softens water hammer. Freeze damage is less likely too. Even in mild areas, wrapping irrigation backflow devices before a cold snap is cheap insurance against a surprise leak. We use fittings matched to the tubing brand.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Roseville, CA

    PEX bends around corners, shrugs off corrosion and needs far fewer fittings than copper. On a whole-house repipe that means fewer joints hidden behind drywall and fewer places for a slow leak to start rotting a stud. It can also stretch when water freezes. Even in mild areas, wrapping irrigation backflow devices before a frosty night is cheap insurance against a surprise leak. That stretch is not a license to skip insulation, so exterior-wall and crawl-space runs still get wrapped.
