    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Roseville, CA

    With PEX-A, the tech slips a ring over the pipe end and stretches both with a battery tool.     Uponor PEX System Installation in Roseville

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool plus a ring slipped over the pipe end. We stretch the pipe and ring, seat the fitting, and within seconds the plastic shrinks back around the barb into a tight seal. No crimp tool to calibrate. The fittings have a wide bore, so flow loss through each joint stays low.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Roseville

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A bends tighter, recovers from a kink with a heat gun, and its expansion fittings flow more water, while PEX-B costs less and its crimp joints are just as reliable when they are gauged properly. Both last decades. For tight attics we lean toward PEX-A.
