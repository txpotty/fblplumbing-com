    Sump Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Roseville

    Silt and gravel wash into a pit through the drain tile every time it rains, and over a few years that layer can bury the intake screen. Our plumber drains the basin and digs the sediment out by hand. A clean pit lets the float move freely. The pump runs cooler too.

    Check Valve and Discharge Pipe Repair in Roseville, CA

    A bad check valve can nearly double how often the pump starts. Every startup puts heat into the motor. The water should exit well away from the slab or foundation so it does not soak back toward the pit.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Roseville

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. A backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or quits. Our plumber load tests the battery, tests the charger and alarm, then runs the backup alone with the primary unplugged. Expect a new battery every three to five years.

    Emergency Sump Pump Repair in Roseville

    A pump that runs nonstop, hums without moving water or will not start at all needs a plumber the same day, before the pit overflows.     Zoeller Sump Pump Maintenance in Roseville

    Zoeller pumps like the M53 and M63 use cast iron housings and an integral float switch, and they tend to outlast cheaper plastic units. Even so, we look for cracks in the casting and make sure the float moves freely. We carry the common Zoeller parts on the truck.

    Pit Cleaning and Liner Checks in Roseville

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, leaves and gravel settle at the bottom of the basin. Sooner or later the pump sucks it into the screen and locks up the impeller. Our plumber pumps the basin down, rinses the screen and inspects the liner for cracks that let dirt seep in through the walls. Takes about twenty minutes.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Roseville, CA

    Storms knock out power when the pit fills fastest. That is the moment a backup pump earns its keep. We check the battery date, clean the terminals and confirm the charger is actually charging, since a dead charger quietly drains the battery to nothing over a few months.
