    Control Valve and Timer Diagnostics in Roseville, CA

    The control valve runs every stage of regeneration, from backwash through brine draw to the final refill. We step it through each cycle by hand and watch for a motor that stalls, a drive gear that skips, a piston that drags or a drain that never shuts off. Worn pistons turn up a lot.

    Hardness Testing at the Tap in Roseville

    We test with a titration kit, counting drops until the sample changes color, because test strips are too rough for setting a valve. We take the raw sample from an unsoftened hose bib or with the softener on bypass. The softened sample comes from a kitchen faucet.

    Regeneration Programming and Hardness Testing in Roseville, CA

    Plenty of softeners still run factory settings. Either the installer left the defaults in, or the family got bigger and the settings stayed put. We enter your raw hardness and household size, then set a metered valve to regenerate by gallons used rather than on a fixed timer schedule.

    Resin Rebedding in Roseville

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water slowly swell and soften resin beads, and iron or sediment can coat them until capacity drops off. We try a resin cleaner first. If the bed is spent, we empty out the old resin and refill the tank with new high-capacity media, which runs far less than a new softener.

    Emergency Softener Repairs in Roseville, CA

    A softener stuck in backwash can run water to the drain all night, and a cracked brine tank can empty across the floor. Either one is worth a same-day call. First, put the valve in bypass and the rest of the house keeps running, then call (669) 319-2034 and we will get a plumber out.

    Breaking Up Salt Bridges in Roseville

    To check for a bridge, we push a broom handle straight down through the salt. If it hits a hard layer and then falls through, there is a bridge. We break the crust, vacuum out the salt mush below it, then check the brine well, safety float, brine line and grid plate before refilling to about half full.

    Full-System Softener Inspections in Roseville
