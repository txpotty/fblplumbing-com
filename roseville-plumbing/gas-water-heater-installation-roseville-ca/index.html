    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Roseville

    Atmospheric vent water heaters rely on natural draft to move exhaust through a metal flue. They are common in older homes and are often the most affordable gas option. We inspect the existing vent for damage, proper slope and clearance. If the vent is undersized or corroded, we replace it. Proper draft is essential for safety.

    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Roseville

    Direct vent water heaters draw combustion air from outside and vent exhaust through a sealed system. They are a good choice for tight utility rooms or interior closets. We install the vent kit through an exterior wall with proper clearances. Many direct vent models do not need electricity. They offer safe, dependable operation.

    We install all major trusted brands of gas water heaters, including Rheem, Bradford White, A. O. Smith, State Water Heaters and Navien for tankless models. We can help you choose the best brand and model for your particular needs and budget.

    Most new gas water heaters come with a manufacturer's warranty ranging from 6 to 12 years on the tank and components. FBL Plumbing also provides a warranty on our installation labor. We will explain both during your consultation.

    Installing a gas water heater involves working with natural gas lines, water lines and exhaust venting, which can be dangerous if not done correctly. Improper installation can lead to gas leaks, carbon monoxide poisoning or water damage. It is strongly recommended to hire a licensed professional for safety and code compliance.

    Many jurisdictions require a permit for gas water heater installation. We pull permits when required and make sure the work passes inspection.
