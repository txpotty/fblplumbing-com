    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Roseville

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off quickly in an emergency.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Roseville, CA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant option more and more used in homes across Roseville, CA. While PEX is less prone to leaks than older materials, damage can still occur from improper installation, rodent chewing, or impact. Our team is fully experienced in PEX repair, using specialized crimping tools and fittings to make solid, leak-proof connections. We ensure your PEX system continues to perform optimally.

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Roseville

    For some water line repairs, traditional trenching can be avoided. Trenchless repair methods, like pipe bursting or pipe lining, allow us to fix or replace underground pipes with little disruption to your landscaping, driveway, or foundation.     Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Roseville, CA

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which chokes flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe rarely lasts because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and stops repeat leaks.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Roseville

    Slab leaks, where water lines running beneath your home's concrete foundation leak, are a particularly challenging issue. Newer homes across Northern California mostly use PEX or copper with plastic drains, and many owners are repiping older houses to restore water pressure. These leaks can undermine your foundation, leading to major structural damage. We use specialized listening devices and camera inspections to pinpoint slab leaks precisely and offer repair options including direct slab penetration, re-routing, or pipe lining. Addressing a slab leak promptly in Roseville is crucial to prevent extensive, costly foundation damage.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Roseville

    If your service line runs under a driveway, pool deck or mature landscaping, trenchless replacement can save a lot of restoration cost. We dig small pits at each end and pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through using a pneumatic boring tool or pipe pulling equipment. The process typically takes one day. You keep your pavers and trees, and the new line carries a long manufacturer warranty.

    Main Water Line Leak Repair in Roseville

    Most main line leaks show up as a soggy strip of yard, a sudden jump in your water bill or a meter dial that moves with every fixture off. We verify the leak by isolating the house shutoff and watching the meter. Then we use ground microphones to find the exact spot. A single section is cut out and replaced with repair couplings or a new length of pipe, and the line is pressure tested before backfill.
