    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Roseville, CA

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. A flushometer from Sloan or Zurn delivers a measured volume from a 3/4 inch supply with no tank, and it recovers instantly for the next user, which matters in a stadium or school restroom. The volume matches the fixture rating. Wrong settings stain bowls.

    Sloan Flush Valve Installation in Roseville, CA

    Sloan Royal valves are on most commercial urinals we see. We install them with the correct inside parts kit for the bowl's rated volume, set the control stop so the flush is strong without splashing, and keep rebuild kits on the truck for later service. Sensor versions mount the same way. Battery models need no wiring.

    Residential Urinal Installation in Roseville, CA

    Basement and garage bathrooms are where most home urinals go. We tie into the existing drain and vent with a 2 inch line, run a 3/4 or 1/2 inch supply depending on the flush valve, and frame in blocking or a compact carrier behind the wall. Many use 0.125 gallons per flush. That barely touches the water bill.

    Flushometer Urinal Installation in Roseville

    Manual flushometers are still the most common setup. We install Sloan Royal or Zurn AquaFlush valves on a 3/4 inch supply, adjust the control stop for proper flow, and set the vacuum breaker the right distance above the bowl so it protects the drinking water. Handles can go left or right. ADA units need the handle within reach.

    Sensor Urinal Installation in Roseville, CA

    Sensor valves stop missed flushes and double flushes. Battery or hardwired valves get a tuned sensing range and an optional sanitary flush for idle periods. Expect years between battery changes. Hardwired units need an electrician first.

    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Roseville

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. That means a rim no higher than 17 inches above the floor, an elongated bowl projecting at least 13-1/2 inches from the wall, and a 30 by 48 inch clear floor space in front. Flush controls must be reachable. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Urinal Permits and Compliance in Roseville, CA

    New urinals usually need a permit. New fixtures mean rough-in and final inspections, and commercial jobs often need plans. Permits are on us. Like-for-like swaps may not need one.
