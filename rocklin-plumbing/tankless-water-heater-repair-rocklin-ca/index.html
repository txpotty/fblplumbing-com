    Tankless Error Code Diagnostics in Rocklin, CA

    Every tankless brand flashes its own codes, and the same number can mean different things on two units.     Navien Tankless Water Heater Repair in Rocklin

    Navien condensing units run a second stainless exchanger that pulls heat from the exhaust.     Ignition Failure Repair in Rocklin, CA

    No ignition codes have four usual causes: a dirty flame rod, a bad igniter, low gas pressure or a blocked intake. Undersized gas pipe is the one people miss. A tankless burner can need 150,000 to 199,000 BTU, and the half-inch line that fed a 40,000 BTU tank often cannot carry that, which is why it fires at low flow and quits when two showers are on.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling Service in Rocklin

    Scale is calcium and magnesium carbonate that comes out of hard water as it heats up, and a tankless exchanger heats water fast through narrow passages. We hook a small pump and hoses to the isolation valves and circulate descaler for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with clean water. The inlet screen gets cleaned too.
