    Homes from the 1950s and 1960s often still have original galvanized pipe. It is near the end. A full repipe brings new PEX or copper to every fixture, improves pressure, clears up discolored water, and removes the leak risk from aging threads. Worth it.

    Whole-Home Re-Piping in Rocklin

    A whole-home repipe replaces every supply line from the main shutoff to each fixture, including the water heater connections. Plan on a few days for a typical house.     Commercial Pipe Replacement in Rocklin

    Apartments and condos share risers. One leak can affect several units. We plan riser replacement in phases, one stack at a time, notify tenants ahead of shutoffs, and test each section before moving on. Organized work.
