    Picking a Gas Water Heater for Your Rocklin, CA Home

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Rocklin, CA

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. We never swap a natural gas tank onto propane without the right conversion. We confirm pressure with a manometer.

    Bradford White Tank Replacement in Rocklin

    Most of the tanks on our trucks are Bradford White, partly because their Defender combustion system and ICON gas valve are simple to service. Model choice follows your hot water use. The install meets current code.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Replacement in Rocklin, CA

    Rheem Performance and Performance Platinum tanks cover most of the range we install, from basic 6-year models to 12-year tanks with better anodes. More warranty years generally buy a heavier anode rod. We help you pick.

    Standard Gas Water Heater Replacement in Rocklin

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon atmospheric tank is what most homes have, and swapping one like for like is a two to three hour job when the venting and gas line are in good shape. The old tank gets drained and hauled off, and the new one goes on a pan. Every install gets new gas and water connectors. Then we leak test with soap solution and check draft at the hood.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Upgrades in Rocklin, CA

    Condensing gas tanks run around 90 percent efficient or better and vent through PVC, while a standard atmospheric tank sits closer to 60 percent. You will need a condensate drain and a nearby outlet. Big households see payback sooner.

    Choosing Between Gas Tank Brands in Rocklin, CA

    Warranty length on a tank mostly tracks anode size and burner design, so a 12-year tank is a different build from a 6-year one. Self-cleaning dip tubes help on hard water. Powered anodes outlast standard rods. Your plans for the house help decide it.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Service in Rocklin, CA

    Rheem tanks and their Ruud twins share most parts, which helps when a gas valve fails a few years in. We install both. We set them up per the factory manual and test draft.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Rocklin, CA

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through 2 or 3-inch PVC, so they can sit far from a chimney. A standard outlet has to be within reach of the cord. We run the vent within the maker's length and elbow limits.
