    Pinhole leaks in copper tend to come in clusters. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. A switch to PEX ends it.     Whole-Home PEX Repiping in Rocklin

    Low pressure at every faucet, across the whole house, usually points at the pipe and not the fixtures.     PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Rocklin

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A makes tighter bends, can be heat-fixed if it kinks, and its fittings flow better, while PEX-B is cheaper and its crimps hold just as well when each one is gauged. Either one lasts for decades. In a cramped attic, we usually pick PEX-A.

    Emergency PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Rocklin, CA

    PEX rarely fails on its own. When it does, the cause is usually a nail or screw driven through it, a rodent chewing on an attic run, or a fitting that was never crimped all the way. We carry the common sizes. Most emergency PEX repairs in Rocklin, CA come down to a cut and a splice, usually under an hour after the wall is open.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Rocklin, CA

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A bends the easiest and uses expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and uses crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C handles more like B. All three are code-listed for drinking water. Pipe, fittings and tools come from the same maker on our jobs, which keeps the warranty valid.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Rocklin, CA

    Think of a manifold like an electrical panel, but for water lines. Each fixture gets its own line from a central header, and each line gets its own valve, so you can shut off one toilet without killing water to the kitchen. You buy more pipe. The trade-off is almost no fittings hidden in the walls and steadier pressure when two showers run at once.
