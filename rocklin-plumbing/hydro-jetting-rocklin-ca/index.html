    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Rocklin

    The cost of hydro jetting in Rocklin, CA typically ranges from $300 to $800, depending on the severity of the clog, the length and diameter of the pipe, and ease of access. More complex jobs, like main sewer line descaling or extensive root removal, may be on the higher end. We provide a transparent quote after an initial inspection.

    While hydro jetting is very powerful, it is generally safe for most sound plumbing systems, including PVC, cast iron, and concrete pipes. However, it is not recommended for severely damaged, corroded, or fragile pipes, which is why a pre-jetting camera inspection by FBL Plumbing is crucial to assess pipe integrity and prevent further damage.

    Usually, a permit is not required for standard hydro jetting aimed at clearing blockages in existing lines.     Hydro jetting is very effective against a wide range of stubborn clogs that traditional snaking cannot fully clear. This includes heavy grease buildup, mineral scale, sludge, soap scum, hair clogs, and even invasive tree roots that have penetrated your sewer lines. It is designed to scour the entire pipe surface.

    Snaking uses a rotating cable to break through a clog. Jetting uses pressurized water to clean the entire pipe wall and wash debris away.
