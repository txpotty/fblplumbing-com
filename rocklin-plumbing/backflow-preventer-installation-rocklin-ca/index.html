    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Rocklin

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a widely used backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Rocklin, DCVAs are frequently installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers precisely install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.

    Backflow Preventer Brands We Install in Rocklin

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Rocklin

    Most water providers require testable backflow assemblies to be certified at installation and then annually. Our certified testers check each valve's opening point, relief valve operation and differential pressure. If a device fails, we can repair or rebuild it on the spot with genuine parts. We submit passing results so you avoid compliance letters or service interruption.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Rocklin

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules allow. We size them to avoid excessive pressure loss on your system.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Rocklin

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are often required for high-hazard applications in Rocklin. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. FBL Plumbing focuses on precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with top brands like Watts and Febco.
