    Water-Tested Sump Pump Maintenance for Rocklin, CA Homes

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Rocklin

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. A backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or quits. Our plumber load tests the battery, tests the charger and alarm, then runs the backup alone with the primary unplugged. Most batteries need replacing every three to five years.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Rocklin

    Strong winter storms can bring days of heavy rain to Northern California, saturating ground and pushing water into basements and sewer lines. We test the pump a few weeks before the wet season by filling the pit with a hose until the float trips. We time the drawdown and listen for bearing noise. That leaves time to replace a weak pump before the first big storm, not during it.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Check-Ups in Rocklin, CA

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the water, so the wear shows up in the shaft, the bearings and the float rod. The float rod gets checked for bends, and the stops get checked for slipping. Bearings get a listen. A motor that whines or rattles on startup is usually close to done.

    Float Switch Checks in Rocklin, CA

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. We watch the float through a full cycle. Then we set the on and off points so the pump runs longer, less often.

    Sump Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Rocklin

    Silt and gravel wash into a pit through the drain tile every time it rains, and over a few years that layer can bury the intake screen. We pump the basin down and scoop out the sediment by hand. A clean pit lets the float move freely. The pump runs cooler too.

    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Rocklin

    Submersible pumps sit at the bottom of the pit, which keeps them quiet but hides the problems. Our plumber pulls the unit out so we can see the cord and the shaft seal. A cracked cord usually means a new pump. The intake screen gets cleaned, and we look over the housing for corrosion, since pumps sitting in standing water for years rust from the bottom up.

    Pit Cleaning and Liner Checks in Rocklin

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, leaves and gravel settle at the bottom of the basin. Sooner or later the pump sucks it into the screen and locks up the impeller. Our plumber pumps the basin down, rinses the screen and inspects the liner for cracks that let dirt seep in through the walls. Takes about twenty minutes.

    Emergency Sump Pump Repair in Rocklin

    A pump that runs nonstop, hums without moving water or will not start at all needs a plumber the same day, before the pit overflows. We send a plumber with floats, check valves and spare pumps on the truck. A jammed impeller usually clears in a few minutes. Burned-out motors get a new pump installed before we leave, so the pit is protected again that day.
