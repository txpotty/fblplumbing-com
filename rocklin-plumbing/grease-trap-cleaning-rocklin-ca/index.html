    Grease Line Jetting in Rocklin

    Grease that slips past a full trap coats the 4-inch line out to the main. It builds up in rings, thickest at the bottom of the pipe. Snaking just bores a small path that closes up in weeks, but a rotating jetter nozzle strips grease off the full wall of the pipe. A camera confirms it afterward.

    Grease Trap Cleaning Cost in Rocklin, CA

    Cost follows trap size and fill level. A 20-gallon under-sink trap is a short visit.     Grease Trap Inspection and Repair in Rocklin

    An empty trap is the only time anyone can see what is wrong with it. We look for cracked baffles and missing outlet tees. A missing outlet tee lets the floating grease cap pour straight into the sewer, and it is one of the most common failures we find on older concrete interceptors. We can often fix small problems on the spot.

    Grease Trap Hydro-Jetting in Rocklin, CA

    Some traps have years of grease baked onto the walls and inlet piping. That crust survives a normal pump-out. Jetting the empty tank and its inlet line strips it back to bare walls and gets back the capacity that crust was taking. Plan on about an extra hour.

    Scheduled Grease Trap Maintenance in Rocklin

    Pumping frequency comes from two numbers: how big the trap is and how fast your kitchen fills it. The first few visits, we measure fill rate with a sludge judge. From there we set a recurring date, and the truck shows up without you having to call the office and remind anyone. Schedules shift if your menu does.

    Under-Sink Grease Trap Maintenance in Rocklin, CA

    Under-sink hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, usually 10 to 50 gallons per minute, and they hold far less than people expect. In a busy kitchen, that means cleaning every week or two. The service covers grease and solids removal, a baffle scrape, a look at the inlet flow control fitting, and a new lid gasket if the old one is flattened. Most odor from these units comes from a bad gasket.

    Preventive Maintenance Programs in Rocklin, CA

    A maintenance plan is just a pumping interval plus someone who tracks it. Your manager does not have to remember it, because we track the dates. If the sludge judge shows the trap hitting 20 percent between visits, we pull the next date in, and if it reads 10 percent, we push it out and save you a pump-out. Adjustments happen at every visit.

    Outdoor Grease Interceptor Pumping in Rocklin, CA

    In-ground interceptors need a vacuum truck and usually a pressure wand to break up the cap. Every compartment gets opened and pumped to the bottom. While the tank is empty, we look at the inlet and outlet tees, the baffle wall, the risers and the lid seals, because a cracked riser lets groundwater and dirt in. Lids go back on tight.
