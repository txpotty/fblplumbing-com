    Jetting a Sewer Line in Rocklin, CA

    Grease and scale are jetting work. Cables leave them behind. A jetter nozzle with rear-facing jets pulls itself down the line and blasts the buildup off the pipe wall, restoring close to the full diameter of the pipe and slowing the next clog.

    Clogged Main Sewer Line Solutions in Rocklin, CA

    Several drains backing up at once is a main line problem. Every drain shares that one pipe. We clear it with a sectional cable or a jetter, then camera the line to see whether the cause was roots, debris or a structural problem like a broken joint or an offset section.

    Dealing with Grease Buildup in Rocklin, CA

    Grease hardens as it cools. Then it narrows the pipe. Kitchen grease coats the sewer line the same way plaque coats an artery, and over a few years the opening can close to the size of a finger, which is when drains start to back up, and jetting is the right fix.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Rocklin, CA

    Cast iron rots from the inside. Newer homes across Northern California mostly use PEX or copper with plastic drains, and many owners are repiping older houses to restore water pressure. The bottom of the pipe wears first. Because a heavy cutter can punch through weak iron, we use chain descalers and a lower-pressure jet, then check the wall thickness on camera.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Rocklin

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Each fails in its own way. Clay joints invite roots, and Orangeburg, a tar-paper pipe from the mid-1900s, flattens under the weight of the soil over time, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.
