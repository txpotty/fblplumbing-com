    For most homes in Rocklin, an annual or twice-yearly sewer cleaning is enough for preventative maintenance, especially if you have older pipes or mature trees nearby.     If our cleaning or camera inspection reveals damage like cracks, collapses, or severe root intrusion that cleaning alone cannot resolve, FBL Plumbing will give you a detailed assessment and options for sewer line repair or replacement.
