    Replacing Old Expansion Tanks in Rocklin

    Expansion tanks do not last forever, and an old, waterlogged or corroded tank offers no protection. If your existing tank in Rocklin is showing signs of failure, such as a dripping relief valve or fluctuating water pressure, it is time for a replacement.     Expansion Tank Sizing for Large Water Heaters in Rocklin

    A standard 2 gallon tank works for many 40 and 50 gallon heaters at moderate pressure. Larger heaters, higher incoming pressure or high-capacity systems may need a 4.5 gallon tank or larger. We use manufacturer sizing charts that factor in heater volume, supply pressure and temperature setting. Correct sizing keeps the tank from over-flexing and wearing out early.

    Brands and Tank Materials in Rocklin, CA

    We install potable-rated thermal expansion tanks from brands such as Watts, Amtrol and A.O. Smith. Potable tanks use a butyl diaphragm and a lined interior so water never touches bare steel. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. Stainless steel connections hold up against corrosion from hard or aggressive water. We avoid tanks designed only for hydronic heating, since they are not approved for drinking water.
