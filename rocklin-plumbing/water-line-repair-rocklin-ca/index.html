    Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Rocklin, CA

    Galvanized steel service lines rust from the inside out. Once one spot leaks, the pipe on either side is usually just as thin. We can clamp or replace a short section to get you through, which can hold for a while if the pipe is not too far gone. More often we recommend replacing the whole run from the meter to the house with copper or PEX. You get your flow back as a bonus.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Rocklin

    Buried copper is tough, but it is not forever. Pinholes show up where stones press on the pipe, and acidic soil or high water velocity can thin the wall from the inside. We usually cut out two feet. The new piece goes in with soldered or pressed couplings, then we look over the pipe on either side for more pitting before we backfill.

    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Rocklin

    Underground leaks hide well. The first clue is often a water bill that jumped for no reason. Others notice a patch of grass that grows greener than the rest, or a spot near the walk that stays muddy days after rain. We confirm the leak at the meter, trace the pipe route and listen along it. The dig follows the sound.

    Water Pressure Restoration in Rocklin

    Low pressure at every fixture points to the service line or the regulator, not the faucets. We put a gauge on a hose bib first. Then we check the pressure reducing valve, the house shutoff and the meter-side valve for a partly closed gate, and look at the pipe itself. Old galvanized can be choked to half its size with rust. The fix might be a new valve or a new line.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Rocklin

    A trench through a paver driveway or a mature garden can cost more to restore than the pipe itself. Trenchless avoids most of that. We dig a pit at each end and pull HDPE or PEX through with a boring tool or pipe puller, following the old line or cutting a new path beside it. Most replacements finish in a day. The new pipe carries its own manufacturer warranty.

    PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Rocklin

    Copper and PEX fail in different places. Copper tends to pinhole where a rock rubs it or where the water chemistry eats the wall from inside. PEX usually fails at a fitting. We cut out the bad section of copper and sweat or press in a new length, and for PEX we use the matching crimp or expansion tool so the joint is made the way the manufacturer intends.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Rocklin, CA

Before we touch anything, we figure out whether the leak is before or after the meter, since the utility is in charge of the meter and the pipe feeding it. When it is on your side, we repair it. That often means new fittings and a new customer shutoff valve on the same visit.
