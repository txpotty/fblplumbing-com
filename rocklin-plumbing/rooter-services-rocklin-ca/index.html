    While chemical drain cleaners are available, FBL Plumbing generally advises against them. They often only partially clear clogs, can damage pipes over time, and pose safety risks.     Rooting cuts roots back but does not seal the pipe joints, so roots will eventually regrow. Root treatments slow regrowth, and pipe lining or replacement provides a permanent fix.

    Rooting uses a spinning cable with cutting blades to break up clogs and roots. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the pipe walls. Many tough lines do best with both.
