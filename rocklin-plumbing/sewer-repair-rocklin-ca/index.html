    What Sewer Repair Covers in Rocklin, CA

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Rocklin, CA

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. The old pipe gets jetted clean before anything else. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, which seals cracks and root entry points in a single continuous tube. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. On a four-inch residential line, that rarely matters.

    Restaurant and Retail Sewer Repair in Rocklin

    Restaurants, retail centers and multifamily buildings load a lateral far beyond a single house. Grease is the usual culprit. We jet the line, camera it, and repair the weak section with options that limit how long you are closed, such as overnight work or temporary bypass pumps that keep the building running. A two-way cleanout usually goes in at the same time.

    Root Intrusion Removal in Rocklin, CA

    Roots enter through hairline cracks and loose joints, then grow into a mat that catches paper and grease. Cut roots regrow within a year or two. We clear the line with a root cutting head or a jetter with a chain flail, camera it afterward to see the condition of each joint, and tell you whether sealing or replacing those joints makes more sense than cleaning every spring. Mature trees in Rocklin yards make this a common call.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Rocklin

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. Crushed pipe cannot be lined. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. We see this most in older neighborhoods with clay pipe. We stage the work so you lose sewer service for a few hours, not days.

    Commercial Sewer Repair in Rocklin

    Multifamily buildings and offices put several units' worth of flow through one lateral. Minor sags clog quickly under that load. We repair larger diameter lines, add two-way cleanouts so future maintenance is easy, and provide inspection video that property managers can hand to owners or insurers. Night and weekend work is available.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Rocklin

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. The full length gets recorded. Cracks, root intrusion, offset joints and standing water all show up on the screen, and the sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the ground above, so any excavation stays small and targeted. Older homes in Rocklin often hide two or three problems in one line, and the video catches all of them.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Rocklin

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. A cable only punches a hole. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and it is the standard prep step before any pipe lining job. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.

    Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in Rocklin, CA

    A spot repair is the cheapest fix when the rest of the line is sound. Lining costs more per foot. It avoids excavation, though, and on long runs under driveways or patios the savings on concrete and landscaping often outweigh the higher price of the liner itself. Full replacement is right for Orangeburg or a line broken in several places. We price all three so you can compare.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Repair in Rocklin

    Most jobs fall between $500 for a small spot repair and about $3,000 for a trenchless section. Full replacement costs more. A complete open trench replacement can reach $10,000 or more, depending on the length of the line, how deep it sits and what has to be removed and restored on top of it. We quote after the camera inspection.

    Trenchless repair fixes the pipe through one or two small access pits. No long trench. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE pipe through the old one while splitting the old pipe apart, and cured-in-place lining inserts a resin-soaked tube that hardens into a new pipe wall inside the old one, so driveways, patios and mature trees stay where they are. Not every line qualifies.

    A spot repair or a jetting job usually takes a few hours. Pipe lining runs one to two days. A full replacement with excavation can take two to five days, depending on the length of the run, the depth of the pipe and how long the inspection takes to schedule. We give you a timeline with the quote.

    Clearing a soft clog with a rental auger is one thing. Fixing a broken lateral is not a weekend project. Sewer work means trenching near buried utilities, working around raw sewage and meeting code on slope, bedding and fittings, and in Rocklin the work often needs a permit and an inspection before the hole can be closed. Bad work gets buried and backs up later.

    Watch for clogs that keep coming back in more than one drain. Gurgling toilets are another clue. A sewer smell in the yard, a strip of grass that is greener than the rest, or water rising in the shower when the washing machine empties all point to a problem in the main line rather than a single fixture. A camera inspection settles it.

    Most spot repairs are done in one day, excavation and backfill included. Some take longer. Larger replacements, or jobs that need a right-of-way permit to dig near the street, can take two to four days depending on the inspection schedule and the length of pipe. We put the schedule in writing.

    A rental auger can open a soft clog. Structural damage stays. Forcing a cable through a broken or offset joint can snag, tear the pipe further or leave a cutter head stuck underground, which turns a camera visit into an excavation job. Camera first if the clog keeps coming back.

    Our labor warranty is written into the quote. It varies by job type. A spot repair with new PVC and a full lining job carry different terms because the materials and risks differ, and we explain exactly what is covered and for how long before the work starts. Manufacturer warranties stack on top.
