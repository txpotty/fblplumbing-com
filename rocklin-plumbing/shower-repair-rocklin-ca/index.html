    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Rocklin

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water leaking behind the trim slips into the wall cavity at the valve opening and soaks the drywall while the tile face stays dry. We pull the trim and look. It is usually a loose bonnet nut or a worn O-ring.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Rocklin

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Minerals collect behind the face and inside the restrictor, which turns the spray patchy or weak with the valve fully open. Soaking helps sometimes. Otherwise we put on a new head with rubber nozzles that rub clean with your thumb.

    Mixing Valve Repair in Rocklin

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. Over time the cartridge or spool sticks, and you get a shower that will not stay at one temperature or a handle that turns past where it should before the water warms up. We replace the cartridge. Valves with no parts left get a replacement plan and an honest look at the tile.

    Commercial Shower Repair in Rocklin, CA

    Gyms and apartment buildings run showers dozens of times a day. Vandal-resistant heads are available. Limit stops are set to code on each valve.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Rocklin, CA

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. A hand auger through the strainer clears most of them, and a drum machine handles clogs that have moved past the trap. Chemical cleaners rarely reach it. We recommend a hair catcher once it is clear.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Rocklin, CA

    Stains on the ceiling below are often the first clue. We use a moisture meter on the walls and a flood test on the pan to tell a leaking valve from a cracked pan, a failed grout line or a loose drain body. The source gets repaired.     Shower Valve Replacement in Rocklin

    Shower Drain Body Repair in Rocklin

We clear and test the drain. Loose drains get resealed, and cracked ones get replaced.
