    High-Efficiency Toilet (HET) Upgrades in Rocklin, CA

    Comfort-Height Toilet Replacement in Rocklin

    Going from a standard bowl to a comfort-height model raises the seat two or three inches, which matters a lot for seniors and anyone recovering from knee or hip surgery. No drain changes are needed. Pair it with a grab bar if you can, and we can anchor that into a stud during the same visit.

    Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in Rocklin, CA

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Measure to the closet bolts, not the baseboard.
