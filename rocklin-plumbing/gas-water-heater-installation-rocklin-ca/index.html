    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Installation in Rocklin

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon gas tank usually costs $1,800 to $4,000 installed. Power vent and tankless units run higher due to vent and gas work.

    Gas water heater installs usually require a permit. Code here requires seismic strapping on water heaters and low-flow fixtures, and many local agencies require sewer lateral inspections at sale. FBL Plumbing takes care of permits and inspections in Rocklin.

    Once a year is the right interval for a gas heater. A yearly service flushes sediment, checks the anode and burner and tests the relief valve, which catches problems early.

    A standard tank replacement usually takes three to four hours. Vent or gas work adds time.

    Gas heaters usually carry a 6 to 12 year manufacturer warranty on the tank and parts. FBL Plumbing adds a labor warranty on top of that.

    We advise against doing it yourself. A mistake in the gas joint, the vent or the water lines can be dangerous or expensive to fix later. Hire a licensed pro.

    Flush the tank once a year and check the anode rod every few years. Sediment and a worn anode are the two things that kill gas tanks early. We offer yearly maintenance.

    Fuel and burner setup are the difference. Propane runs at a different pressure than natural gas and needs its own orifice. Either fuel is fine.
