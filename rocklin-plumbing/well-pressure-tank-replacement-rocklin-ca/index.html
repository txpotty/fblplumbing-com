    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Rocklin

    The tank tee is the brass manifold that connects tank, switch, gauge, drain and relief valve. Threads on old tees weep. The relief valve should open before the system reaches a dangerous pressure, so we replace a crusted or corroded one rather than trust it. Whole set, one visit.

    Preventative Well Pressure Tank Maintenance in Rocklin, CA

    Check the air charge once a year. Shut off the pump, drain the system to zero, and put a tire gauge on the valve at the top of the tank, topping it up to two psi below the cut-in if it reads low. Water out of the valve means a torn bladder.

    Pressure Tank Sizing and Upsizing in Rocklin

    Drawdown is the usable water between cut-out and cut-in. Small tanks give surprisingly little. We divide pump flow by drawdown to see how many starts per hour the pump will face, aiming for at least one minute of run time per cycle. That often points to a bigger tank.

    Constant pressure systems use a variable speed drive that adjusts pump speed to demand. Pressure stays steady even with two showers running. These systems can run on a smaller tank, though the drive and wiring add to the price. We explain the trade-off.
