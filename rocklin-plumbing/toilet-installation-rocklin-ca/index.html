    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Rocklin, CA

    High-efficiency toilets flush on 1.28 gallons or less, against 3.5 gallons or more for toilets from before the early 1990s. Check for the WaterSense label.     Two-Piece Toilet Installation in Rocklin

    Two-piece toilets come with the tank and bowl separate, joined by a rubber gasket and two or three bolts. We assemble them on the floor before setting.     Low-Flow WaterSense Toilet Installation in Rocklin, CA
