    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Rocklin, CA

    Two or three slab leaks in a few years point to the pipe, not bad luck. That is when a repipe pays off.     Ground Radar and Acoustic Locating in Rocklin

    Ground-penetrating radar shows what is inside the slab before we cut. Radar maps steel in the concrete.     Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Rocklin

    A reroute abandons the leaking pipe under the slab. The new pipe runs through the attic. For older Rocklin homes, it bypasses the whole buried run of tired copper or galvanized and removes the chance of another leak there. We cut small openings at fixture walls.
