    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in Rocklin

    The cost of sump pump replacement in Rocklin, CA usually ranges from $500 to $1,500, depending on the type of pump, submersible or pedestal, its horsepower and any necessary pit changes. Battery backup systems will add to this cost, usually between $300 and $800. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after assessing your needs.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from 3 to 5 years. We also offer a labor warranty.

    Most standard sump pump replacements can be completed within 2 to 4 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new pit excavation or extensive discharge line work, might take longer, up to a full day.

    To maintain your new sump pump, regularly check the pit for debris, test the pump monthly by pouring water into the pit, and make sure the discharge pipe is clear. An annual professional inspection by FBL Plumbing can also help lengthen its lifespan.

    It is recommended to test your sump pump at least every three months, and especially before and after rainy seasons. To test, simply pour a bucket of water into the sump pit. The pump should activate, drain the water and then shut off automatically. If it does not, it is time for a professional inspection.

    Constant running, loud grinding, frequent cycling or failure to start are typical signs. Rust, age and a burning smell also point to trouble.

    Simple replacements typically do not require permits. New discharge lines or electrical work may, depending on your city.

    If storms knock out power in your area or you have finished living space below grade, a backup is a wise investment. A battery unit with a high-water alarm typically runs six to ten hours of intermittent pumping on a full charge.
