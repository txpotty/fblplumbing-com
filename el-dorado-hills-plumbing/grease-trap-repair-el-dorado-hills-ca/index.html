    Grease Trap Odor Control & Repair in El Dorado Hills

    A sewer smell near the trap means gas is getting out somewhere it should not. The usual suspects are a torn lid gasket or a floor drain trap that has dried out. We check venting on the fixture side too, because a sink with no vent can siphon its own trap seal and let sewer gas back into the kitchen. Pumping the trap helps for a week, but it will not fix a gasket that no longer seals.

    Grease Trap Leak Repair in El Dorado Hills

    Plastic and fiberglass trap bodies do not rust, but they can crack from a dropped lid or a dishwasher dumping water hotter than the trap is rated for. Hairline cracks sometimes take a plastic weld. Anything through the floor of the tank or along a molded seam we treat as a replacement, because patches there fail under the weight of a full trap. We will show you the crack before recommending anything.

    Flow Control Device Repair in El Dorado Hills

    Hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, like 20 or 50 gallons per minute, and the flow control fitting is what holds the water to that rate. Without it, a full sink dump pushes grease straight through. Inspectors look for it. The air intake gets cleared too, because the fitting needs air to work.

    Residential Grease Trap Repair in El Dorado Hills, CA

    Homes rarely need a grease trap, but some have one, usually a small plastic unit under the kitchen sink on a house with a septic system or a home bakery. Most leaks come from the lid or the threaded inlet fitting. We replace the gasket, reset the fittings with fresh thread sealant, and clear any grease plug in the outlet. On most homes this is under an hour of work.

    Clogged Grease Trap Repair in El Dorado Hills

    Hardened grease in the inlet tee is the usual culprit when a trap backs up. The fix starts with pumping the trap down and jetting the inlet line until it flows again. Food solids packed against the baffle get scraped out by hand. Then we look for the reason it clogged, such as a dishwasher dumping too much hot water at once or a missing sink strainer upstream. A clog that keeps coming back nearly always has a cause you can see.

    Commercial Grease Interceptor Repair in El Dorado Hills, CA

    In-ground gravity interceptors run from about 500 gallons to several thousand, usually precast concrete with two compartments. Over the years, hydrogen sulfide gas softens the concrete above the water line. We pump the tank, inspect the walls and the crossover, and repair spalled concrete with a cementitious patch or an epoxy liner rated for that service. Entry follows confined space rules.

    Preventative Grease Trap Maintenance & Repair in El Dorado Hills, CA

    Most trap repairs we do trace back to pumping intervals that stretched too long. Pretreatment programs commonly set the limit at 25 percent of the liquid depth for grease and solids combined. After a repair, we measure the grease cap and solids with a sludge judge and suggest a pumping schedule based on how fast your trap actually fills. A kitchen running two fryers can hit that mark in a couple of weeks.

    Damaged Baffle Repair in El Dorado Hills

    Baffles are the walls inside the trap that slow the water down long enough for grease to float and solids to sink. A cracked or loose baffle turns the trap into a pass-through. We pump it out and pull the old baffle. On plastic units we order the factory part for that model number. Steel ones often need a fabricated baffle or a new trap.
