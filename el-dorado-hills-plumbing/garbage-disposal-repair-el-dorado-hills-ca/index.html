    Humming Disposal Repair in El Dorado Hills

    Humming with no spin usually means something is stuck. Spoons, glass, bones and pits cause most of them. We cut power, work the flywheel free with a hex wrench on the bottom of the unit, remove the object and then test the motor and the reset button with water running.

    After-Hours Disposal Service in El Dorado Hills

    A disposal that quits while you are cooking stops everything, especially if the sink is full of water. Do not try drain chemicals. Drain chemicals can eat at the seals and splash on whoever opens the trap, so leave the water where it is and give us a call.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in El Dorado Hills

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A steady whine is different. That whine often comes from worn motor bearings, and once bearings start to fail the noise only gets worse until the motor seizes. Odors usually trace to the splash guard. The guard comes out, the chamber gets cleaned and a torn guard gets replaced.

    Electrical Issues and Motor Repair in El Dorado Hills

    A disposal that hums but will not grind is getting power but is stuck, while one that is silent has no power at all. They are different problems. We check the breaker, the reset button and the switch for silent units, and the flywheel and motor windings for humming ones, then decide if a repair makes sense.
