    How We Fix Basement Leaks in El Dorado Hills, CA

    Foundation Crack Repair in El Dorado Hills

    Hairline cracks in a poured wall are normal and usually dry, but a crack that seeps after rain needs attention. Many older homes here sit on raised foundations with crawlspaces, which make supply and drain repairs more accessible than slab work. We inject leaking cracks from the inside with polyurethane foam, which expands into the crack and stays flexible as the wall moves. Most injections take an hour or two.

    Wall Leak Solutions in El Dorado Hills, CA

    Damp walls without a crack often point to water soaking through porous block or concrete. Tape foil to the wall for a full day, and if the room side is wet it is condensation, not a leak. Condensation needs a dehumidifier. Seepage needs drainage outside or a channel inside.

    Crack Injection for Basements in El Dorado Hills

    Injection works on poured concrete walls, not block. We set ports along the crack, seal the face with epoxy paste, and pump in polyurethane or epoxy from the bottom port up until resin shows at the next port. Epoxy adds strength. For a crack that is actively wet, polyurethane works better since it reacts with water.

    Water Heater and Appliance Leak Repair in El Dorado Hills, CA

    Water heaters, softeners and washing machines often sit in basements, and their leaks pool on the floor out of sight. Once the tank itself leaks, it needs replacing. Valve and connector leaks are usually a same-visit fix, and an automatic shutoff sensor is worth adding.

    Interior Drainage Systems in El Dorado Hills

    Where digging outside is not practical, an interior drain system does the same job from inside. A strip of slab comes out along the walls, perforated pipe goes in on stone, and it all drains to a sump. The wall gets a dimpled membrane. Water then runs behind it into the channel.

    Commercial Basement Waterproofing in El Dorado Hills

    Commercial basements hold inventory, records or mechanical rooms that cannot sit in water. We look at floor drains, elevator pits and sump systems as one system, since a single failed check valve or a clogged pit inlet can flood the whole lower level. Duplex pumps help. Alarms on the pit tell staff before water reaches the floor.

    Sump Pump Installation & Repair in El Dorado Hills

    A sump pump sits in a pit below the slab and kicks on when a float rises. Stuck floats, worn motors and failed check valves cause most of the service calls we run on sumps. We test yours with a bucket. A battery backup is worth adding where power drops in storms.

    Clogged Drain Tile Repair in El Dorado Hills

    Footing drains clog with silt and roots over the years, and once they stop flowing the water table at the wall rises. A camera run from the sump inlet shows us where the tile is plugged. A jetter usually opens it. If the old clay tile has collapsed, it gets replaced with new perforated pipe.
