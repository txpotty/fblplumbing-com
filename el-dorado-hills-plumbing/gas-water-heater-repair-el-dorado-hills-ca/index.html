    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in El Dorado Hills

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it detects vapors or overheating.     Burner Cleaning and Repair in El Dorado Hills, CA

    A yellow, lazy or noisy flame points to a dirty burner or poor combustion air. We remove and clean the burner, check the orifice size for natural gas or propane and adjust as needed.
