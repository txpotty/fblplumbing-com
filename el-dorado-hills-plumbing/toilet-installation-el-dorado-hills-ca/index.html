    Types of Toilet Installation in El Dorado Hills, CA

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in El Dorado Hills, CA

    Comfort height bowls stand about two inches taller than standard ones. Consider who uses the bathroom first. Kids and shorter adults may not like their feet off the floor, but people with sore knees or a new hip usually love the extra height. Installation is the same as a standard bowl.

    Comfort-Height and ADA Toilets in El Dorado Hills

    Aging in place usually starts in the bathroom. A grab bar and a taller bowl help a lot. While the wall is open, we add 2 by 6 blocking between the studs at grab bar height, so the bar can later be screwed into solid wood instead of drywall anchors that pull loose. We also center the bowl to ADA spacing.

    Commercial Toilet Installation in El Dorado Hills, CA

    Commercial restrooms take a beating, so we install vitreous china bowls with flushometer valves rather than tank toilets. A flushometer needs a 1 inch supply and steady pressure. Before setting fixtures in an office or shop, we check that the building supply can deliver the flow the valve is rated for, often 20 to 25 gallons per minute at the fixture. Fixture counts follow code.

    Standard Toilet Installation in El Dorado Hills

    A standard floor-mount toilet is a two-hour job when the flange is sound. The stop gets shut, the tank drained, and the old bowl lifted out. After scraping the old wax off, we check the flange for cracks, set new stainless bolts, and lower the new toilet straight down onto a fresh seal so it compresses evenly. The old one leaves with us.

    Commercial Restroom Toilets in El Dorado Hills, CA

    Restaurants and offices get heavy-duty fixtures and accessible stalls built to code. Downtime costs a business money. We schedule commercial installs early in the morning or after hours, and we stage the fixtures and valves on site so each stall is down for as short a time as possible. We work with your GC on inspections.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in El Dorado Hills

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. The frame height chart from the maker gets followed to the letter. A frame set wrong puts the bowl too low or too high, and once tile is up there is no adjusting it. Measure twice here.

    New Construction Toilet Installation in El Dorado Hills

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. A flange belongs flush with or just above finished flooring. Set it too early and the tile setter has to cut around it, which leaves the flange low and forces a thick seal or an extender later on. Each bowl gets a fresh seal.

    Commercial Flushometer Toilets in El Dorado Hills

    Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. They recover instantly between flushes. Manual and sensor valves go on both floor-mount and wall-hung bowls, with the control stop adjusted so the flush is full but does not splash. Sensor valves cut down on touch points.

Soft plywood around the flange gets cut out and replaced with new subfloor, glued and screwed, so the toilet sits on something solid. It adds an hour, sometimes two.
