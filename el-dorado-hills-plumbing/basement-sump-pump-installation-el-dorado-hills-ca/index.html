    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in El Dorado Hills

    Pedestal sump pumps are an good choice for narrow or shallow sump pits, as their motor sits above the water level. This design makes them easier to maintain and can often offer a longer lifespan because the motor stays out of the water. FBL Plumbing can properly install pedestal sump pumps in your El Dorado Hills home, with the right sizing and setup for effective water removal without crowding your pit.

    Pedestal Sump Pumps in El Dorado Hills

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a solid fit for narrow pits in older homes. They typically last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we usually recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod adjusted to your pit depth.
