    Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in El Dorado Hills, CA

    A continuous-feed unit needs a switch, which a batch-feed kitchen may not have. That can be a wall switch, an air switch button on the sink deck or a switched outlet under the sink, and the choice depends on how hard it is to run wire to the spot you want. Air switches avoid new wiring in the wall. The drain usually stays the same.

    High-Capacity Disposal Installation in El Dorado Hills, CA

    Disposal Upgrades in El Dorado Hills, CA

    Upgrading from an old 1/3 HP unit to something quieter is a common request.     InSinkErator Model Selection in El Dorado Hills, CA

    We help you pick between the Badger and Evolution lines based on how you cook, how much noise bothers you and how much cabinet room you have.     Dishwasher Connection With Disposal Installation in El Dorado Hills

    New disposals come with a knockout plug in the dishwasher inlet. We knock it out with a screwdriver and hammer, fish the plug out of the chamber and connect the hose with a new clamp.
