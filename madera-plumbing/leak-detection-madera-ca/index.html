    Slab Leaks From Ground Movement in Madera

    Pipe set in concrete cannot flex when the ground moves, so it cracks at elbows and where it rises through the slab.     Slab Leak Signs in Madera, CA

    Warm floor spots, cracked tile and a meter that never stops are the common signs. So is the sound of water with everything off. We isolate hot and cold lines with pressure tests, then trace and listen through the slab, and once it is located we go over spot repair, reroute and repipe options with prices for each.

    Pool and Spa Leak Detection in Madera

    Heat and wind can pull a lot of water out of a pool, so it is hard to tell evaporation from a leak. A bucket test settles it. We then pressure test the plumbing lines and dye test around lights, skimmers and return fittings, since a leak left alone can wash out the soil behind the shell.

    Water Line Leak Detection in Madera, CA

    Supply leaks hide inside walls, above ceilings and under yards. The bill usually tells you first. We read the meter, close fixture stops one at a time and use a moisture meter to find the wet framing, then confirm with acoustic gear on pressurized lines, which keeps the drywall cut small in your Madera home.

    Gas Line Leak Locating in Madera

    Gas leaks are found by test, not by nose. Our plumbers pressure test the system with a gauge, then check each joint with a detector and soap solution. We also walk the yard line, since a dead strip of grass over a buried gas pipe often marks a leak that has been there a while. Utility coordination is included.
