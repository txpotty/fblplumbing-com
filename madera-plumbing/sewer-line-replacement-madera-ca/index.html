    What Goes Into a Sewer Line Replacement in Madera, CA

    Pipe Bursting Under Driveways in Madera, CA

    Concrete is expensive to replace. With pipe bursting, the new line goes in under the driveway or tree from one pit to another, following the old pipe's route. The HDPE is butt-fused on site into one long piece. Most bursts finish in a day. Restoration is two small holes instead of a sawcut slab.

    Spot Sewer Repairs in Madera, CA

    Sometimes one joint is the whole problem. With only one bad section, we excavate a single small pit and splice in new PVC using shielded couplings. That costs much less. You see the footage that supports the call.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Madera

    Cast iron rots from the inside out. The bottom of a cast iron pipe goes first, and the camera will show a trench rusted through even when the top looks fine. Scale snags paper and grease. We swap the outdoor section for plastic pipe. Under-slab cast iron gets a separate look at lining or rerouting.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Replacement in Madera, CA

    Orangeburg is tar-soaked wood fiber. Many mid-century houses got it, and by now most of it has blistered and flattened enough to snag waste. Clay is tougher but leaks roots. When the camera shows trouble in three or more places, a full replacement is usually cheaper than chasing spot repairs.

    Sewer Permits and Code in Madera, CA

    Sewer replacement nearly always needs a permit. Our office files the permit and books the inspection, and our crew meets the inspector so you do not have to. The inspector looks at pipe size, slope, bedding and cleanouts. Unpermitted sewer work can show up on a home inspection when you sell.

    Commercial Sewer Lateral Work in Madera, CA

    Apartment buildings, offices and restaurants in Madera, CA need lateral work too. Commercial lines carry more volume and more grease, so we size the pipe, place cleanouts for regular jetting, and coordinate grease interceptor connections with the inspector. Traffic control is sometimes part of the job. We schedule around tenants and posted hours.

    Residential Sewer Replacement in Madera, CA

    Most of our sewer work is single-family homes. Earthquakes and hillside soil movement are real risks in Northern California, so flexible connections and seismic straps help protect plumbing from sudden movement. We see the same failures over and over, roots in clay joints, cast iron rotting along the bottom, and pipe that has dropped and bellied after the ground settled. A new line keeps sewage from coming up through the basement floor drain or a ground floor shower.

    After-Hours Sewer Collapse Response in Madera

    Raw sewage on a basement floor is a health problem. Our on-call tech can clear a partial blockage, scope the line and tell you if the house can safely use its drains overnight or if everything needs to stay off until the repair. Mop-up waits until the line is sound. We can point you to a water damage company if the backup soaked carpet or drywall.
