    Main Sewer Line Clog Clearing in Madera, CA

    A clogged main sewer line can affect all drains in your home and is a serious issue. Causes often include tree root intrusion, pipe collapse, or significant grease buildup, common in older homes. We use advanced camera inspections to find the blockage and hydro-jetting to thoroughly clear it, restoring full drainage to your entire property.

    Bathroom Sink and Vanity Drain Clogs in Madera, CA

    Bathroom sinks clog from hair, toothpaste and soap that bind together on the pop-up stopper and in the P-trap. Hard water can add a mineral crust that makes the pipe narrower over time. We pull and clean the stopper, clear the trap and run a small cable through the wall line. We can also replace worn pop-up assemblies and corroded tailpieces while we are there.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Clogs in Madera

    Kitchen drains clog from cooking grease, coffee grounds and starchy food that stick to the pipe walls and harden. Garbage disposals can push food further down the line where it collects in horizontal runs. We remove the trap, run a cable through the trap arm and branch line and clear the buildup back to the main stack. For heavy grease, we may follow with a small jetter to wash the walls clean.

    Tub and Shower Drain Clogs in Madera

    Hair and soap scum are the most common causes of slow tubs and showers. We remove the strainer or overflow plate and pull the clog out with a drum auger designed for small diameter drains. If the drain is still slow, we check the trap under the slab or in the access panel. We take care not to scratch acrylic, fiberglass or porcelain finishes.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Madera, CA

    For severe or recurring clogs, hydro-jetting is an effective solution. This method uses high-pressure water streams to blast away buildup, grease, scale, and even tree roots from your pipes. It's a thorough cleaning process that leaves pipes scoured, reducing the likelihood of future blockages and extending pipe life, particularly in homes with hard water deposits.

    Emergency Clogged Drain Service in Madera

    A backed up drain right before guests arrive or during a holiday meal is not something that can wait. Our emergency technicians respond fast with full drain equipment. If several fixtures are backing up at once, we check the main line too, since that usually means the clog is further downstream.

    Floor Drain and Garage Drain Clearing in Madera

    Floor drains in garages, utility rooms and commercial kitchens collect dirt, sand and debris, especially after storms track grit indoors. When their traps dry out from sitting unused, sewer gas can also escape. We clear the drain, flush the trap and can install trap primers or trap seals to keep odors out.

    Bathroom Sink & Shower Clog Repair in Madera, CA

    Hair, soap residue, and toothpaste are common causes of bathroom drain clogs. FBL Plumbing provides thorough cleaning for these drains, using augers and sometimes chemical-free solutions to clear blockages.
