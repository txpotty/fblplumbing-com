    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Madera

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage.     Battery Backup Pump Maintenance in Madera

    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails.     Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Madera

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, gravel and leaves settle in the pit. That debris can get sucked into the intake screen and jam the impeller. We pump down and scoop out the basin, rinse the intake and inspect the pit liner for cracks. A clean basin helps the pump run cooler and last longer.

    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Madera, CA

    The float switch is a key component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to ensure reliable operation for your home in Madera, CA.
