    Error Code Diagnostics in Madera, CA

    Codes like Rinnai LC, Navien E003 or Noritz 90 tell us a lot about what is going wrong. We read the code history, test flame rods, flow sensors and igniters and check gas pressure at the inlet.     Commercial Tankless Rack Maintenance in Madera

    Restaurants, salons and multi-family buildings often run several tankless units linked together. Commercial racks see heavy use and scale faster than home units. We descale each heater, check cascade controls and balance the load across the system. Quarterly or semiannual service keeps the hot water flowing during busy hours.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Madera

    The heat exchanger in a tankless heater is a coil of small tubes that water races through while the burner fires. Hard water drops calcium and magnesium on those hot surfaces, narrowing the passages over time. We connect a submersible pump and hoses to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with fresh water to rinse the system completely. Restored flow means steadier temperatures and fewer shutdowns.
