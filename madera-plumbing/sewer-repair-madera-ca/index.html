    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Madera

    Clay and cast iron crack from settling soil, tree roots and vehicles parked over the line. The crack lets soil in and sewage out. Most of these are one-day jobs.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Madera, CA

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Two access holes, sometimes one. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one and splits the old pipe outward, while cured-in-place lining pushes a resin-soaked felt tube into the old pipe and hardens it into a new wall. Either way, the driveway and the trees stay put. Not every line qualifies, and we will say so.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Madera, CA

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. The old pipe gets jetted clean before anything else. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, which seals cracks and root entry points in a single continuous tube. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. For most houses the flow difference is negligible.

    Root Intrusion Removal in Madera, CA

    Roots enter through hairline cracks and loose joints, then grow into a mat that catches paper and grease. They come back fast.     Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Madera, CA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Paper and solids settle there. Earthquakes and hillside soil movement are real risks in Northern California, so flexible connections and seismic straps help protect plumbing from ground movement. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and set it back to a quarter inch of fall per foot, which is the typical code minimum for a four-inch line. Not every sag needs digging, and we will tell you which yours is.
