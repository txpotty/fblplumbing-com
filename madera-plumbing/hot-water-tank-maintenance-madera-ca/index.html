    Water Heater Expansion Tank Service in Madera, CA

    Many modern plumbing systems include an expansion tank connected to the water heater.     Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Madera

    A properly working temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank.     Sediment Flush and Descaling in Madera

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause loud popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution rated for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    The cost of hot water tank maintenance in Madera, CA typically ranges from $100 to $250, depending on the tank type, age and specific services required. This usually includes flushing, anode rod inspection and safety valve checks. We provide upfront pricing before any work begins.

    While some basic steps like draining a small amount of water can be done by a homeowner, professional hot water tank maintenance involves specialized tools and experience to properly inspect electrical or gas components, anode rods and pressure relief valves.     A typical hot water tank maintenance service generally takes about 1 to 2 hours. This includes draining, flushing, inspection and any minor adjustments or part swaps like an anode rod.
