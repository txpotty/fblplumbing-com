    Drop Pipe and Wire Replacement in Livermore

    Galvanized drop pipe from the 1970s and 1980s rusts from the inside, and a pinhole at 150 feet will bleed pressure every time the pump shuts off. Depending on depth and pump weight, it gets replaced with schedule 120 PVC or poly. The wire gets checked too. Cracked insulation and taped splices get cut out.

    Pulling a Submersible Pump in Livermore

    Pulling a submersible means lifting the pump and every length of drop pipe out through the pitless adapter, sometimes hundreds of feet of it. We check the torque arrestor and every splice on the way up. Figure on one day.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Livermore

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes a healthy pump look sick. Short cycling, where the pump starts every few seconds of water use, usually means the tank bladder has failed and the pump is paying for it. A tire gauge on the air valve tells us a lot. Precharge should sit about 2 psi below the cut-in pressure.

    Well Pump Brands We Install in Livermore, CA

    Goulds, Grundfos, Franklin Electric and Sta-Rite cover nearly every well pump we install. Each brand has strengths, and we choose based on your well. Parts for Goulds and Franklin are easy to get, which matters when a control box dies on a weekend.

    Submersible Well Pump Replacement in Livermore

    A submersible pump hangs on drop pipe inside a 4-inch or 6-inch casing, often 100 to 400 feet down, so pulling one is a two-person job with a pump hoist. Sand in the impellers and lightning surges are the usual culprits. Before pulling, we test the windings with a megohmmeter. The reading shows whether the fault is the motor or the wire.

    Jet Pump Seal and Motor Replacement in Livermore, CA

    Single-stage and two-stage jet pumps wear out at the seal and impeller, and a leaking shaft seal shows up as a puddle under the motor. We replace the pump and prime it properly. Then we watch the gauge through a full cycle.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Livermore, CA

    No-water calls often end at the control box, not the pump. Three-wire pumps have a control box with a start capacitor and relay, and those parts fail far more often than the motor itself. We test them first. A $60 part beats a $2,500 pump pull.

    Constant Pressure Well Pump Installation in Livermore, CA

    Constant pressure systems hold the house at one pressure, say 50 psi, whether one faucet is open or the shower and the irrigation are running together. A variable frequency drive speeds the pump up and slows it down to match demand. No more pressure dip mid-shower.
