    Jetting a Sewer Line in Livermore, CA

    Grease and scale are jetting work. A cable only scratches them. The rear jets drive the hose forward while the spray scours the wall, so the line ends up much closer to its original size.

    Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Livermore, CA

    Grease bonds with mineral scale into a hard crust. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. Flow slows a little each year. A cable can punch a hole through the crust, but a jetter cleans the walls back to full size and leaves less for new grease to grab onto, and the method depends on pipe material and condition.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Livermore

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Each fails in its own way. Clay joints invite roots, and Orangeburg, a tar-paper pipe from the mid-1900s, flattens under the weight of the soil over time, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.
