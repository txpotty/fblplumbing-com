    Backflow Prevention for Irrigation Systems in Livermore, CA

    Sprinkler heads sit in fertilizer and standing water, and a pressure drop on the main can siphon that water back into the house.     Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Livermore, CA

    Commercial kitchens and chemical injection systems need an RPZ. The relief valve can dump a lot of water, so the drain gets planned first, then install the assembly above grade with enough room around it for the tester to connect a gauge kit without lying on the floor in Livermore, CA.     Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Livermore, CA

    Pressure vacuum breakers are the low-cost answer for lawn irrigation in Livermore, CA. A spring-loaded air inlet opens when the pressure falls, which breaks the siphon, but they only protect against back-siphonage, so they must sit above every downstream outlet and cannot be used where back pressure is possible. Clearance and drainage get checked as well.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Livermore

    A double check valve assembly, or DCVA, uses two spring-loaded checks in series with four test cocks. It is the usual pick for low-hazard lines such as many fire sprinklers, and because it does not discharge water, it can go in a vault where local rules allow it, sized so it does not rob your system of pressure in Livermore. We leave room for the tester.

    Pool and Spa Fill Backflow Protection in Livermore, CA

    Pool auto-fill lines and hose connections give chlorinated water a straight path back to the supply. We add the right device on the fill line, check that every hose bibb has a vacuum breaker, and can fold the work into a pool build or remodel so it is done before the deck goes in. Hose bibb breakers are cheap.
