    Frequently Asked Questions About Drainage Services in Livermore

    Drain clearing in Livermore usually runs $125 to $500 depending on the line, and hydro jetting runs $350 to $800. Yard drains cost more. French drain and downspout jobs usually land between $2,000 and $8,000, based on length and hardscape.

    Slow sinks and gurgling drains are the early signs. Sewer odor near a drain or cleanout often follows. Outside, look for water that stands more than a day after rain, soggy spots along the foundation, or mulch and soil washed onto the walk, all of which mean water is not leaving the yard the way it should.

    Our cable machines come from Spartan Tool and RIDGID, and our cameras are RIDGID SeeSnake units with built-in locators. Jetters are trailer or cart units. Good equipment matters because a camera with a locator tells us exactly where and how deep a problem is, which keeps a sewer repair to one small excavation instead of a long trench.

    Most homes never need scheduled drain cleaning. A slow drain is the signal. Homes with root-prone sewers, old cast iron, or a kitchen that sends a lot of grease down the drain do better with a cleaning every 12 to 24 months, timed before the line slows to a crawl.

    We advise against it. Chemical cleaners can damage older pipe and metal traps. When they fail, the trap stays full of caustic liquid that the plumber has to deal with carefully.

    Call when more than one drain is slow, or when a toilet gurgles while the washer drains. Those point to the main line. Outside, call when water stands in the yard for more than a day after rain, when a downspout overflows the gutter in every storm, or when water shows up in the crawlspace or basement after a heavy rain.

    Roots grow toward the water and nutrients inside sewer pipe. A hairline crack is enough. A small root at a cracked joint becomes a mass that traps paper and grease, especially in clay pipe.

    Store-bought cleaners rarely clear a real clog. They often make it worse. The chemicals sit in the trap, heat up and can damage older pipe or soften glue joints, and if the clog does not clear, the plumber now has to open a trap full of caustic liquid. A hand auger is the better tool.
