    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Livermore

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year.     Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Livermore

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Scale builds behind the face plate and in the flow restrictor, so the spray goes uneven, shoots sideways or drops to a trickle even when the valve is wide open. A vinegar soak can work.     Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Livermore

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Grit and scale scratch the sealing surfaces, the O-rings flatten and harden, and eventually the valve cannot fully stop the water when the handle is closed. A new cartridge fixes it. The new cartridge goes in with silicone grease, which keeps the handle smooth.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Livermore

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. If the head drips with the valve closed, water is slipping past the cartridge, and a new head will not change that. We fix the valve. Drips from the arm threads just need fresh tape and a snug turn.

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Livermore

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck. The spool should shift to keep the mix steady when one side loses pressure, but scale can lock it in one spot. A new cartridge fixes it. Water that never gets hot enough may only need the limit stop turned.
