    Resin Testing and Replacement in Livermore

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Chlorine and chloramine in city water slowly break resin down until it turns to mush. After a capacity test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced, and a good tank and valve with new resin performs like new. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Livermore, CA

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. Installing a new quality softener usually runs $1,500 to $3,500. Sound tank and valve, repair wins. If the valve is obsolete and parts are scarce, replacing the head or the whole unit may make more sense than chasing used parts online.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Livermore

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. We break the bridge, clean out the mushy salt and check the float and brine well. Cracked tanks get replaced.

    Water Softener Repair vs. Replacement in Livermore, CA

    Age and parts availability decide whether a softener is worth repairing. FBL Plumbing gives honest advice based on both. A ten-year-old unit with a sound tank and a rebuildable valve is usually worth fixing, while an obsolete head with no parts available is a different conversation. We lay out both options.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Livermore

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. The usual causes are a dead timer motor, a bad board, a stuck meter or settings wiped by an outage. Each part gets tested. After the repair, the valve is programmed for your household size and hardness so you have soft water by the next cycle.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Livermore, CA

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Its small ports clog with sediment and salt. Once it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying, and the softener regenerates with plain water that does nothing to the resin. We clean or replace it and check the brine line for air leaks.

    Control Valve Rebuild in Livermore

    Service, backwash, brine and rinse are the four cycles the control valve directs every time the softener regenerates, usually in the middle of the night. Hard water and chlorine wear out the seals, spacers and pistons. Leaks at the valve or water running to the drain are common signs. The valve gets rebuilt with the kit made for it.

    Hard Water Symptoms & Diagnostics in Livermore

    Scale on faucets means trouble. FBL Plumbing checks hardness at the tap, looks at each part of the system and gives you a repair plan for your Livermore home. We test hardness before and after the repair, so you see the grains per gallon fall.
