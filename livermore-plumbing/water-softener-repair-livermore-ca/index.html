    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Livermore, CA

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates suction to draw brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. When it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying. We clean or replace the injector and screen and check the brine line for leaks.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Livermore

    A softener using far too much salt or water often has settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units may regenerate on a schedule regardless of use. We retest hardness, reprogram the valve or upgrade the head to a metered control. Proper settings can significantly cut salt and water use.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Livermore, CA

    While less common than traditional softeners, no-salt water conditioners also require maintenance and periodic repair. These systems work by conditioning water to prevent scale buildup without removing minerals. If you notice scale returning or reduced water pressure, your no-salt system may need attention. FBL Plumbing is experienced in diagnosing and repairing issues with these specialized units in Livermore, CA, ensuring they continue to protect your plumbing and appliances effectively.

    Hard Water Symptoms & Diagnostics in Livermore

    If you're noticing scale buildup on faucets, cloudy dishes, dry skin and hair, or clothes that feel stiff after washing, your water softener likely needs attention. FBL Plumbing offers complete diagnostic services to identify the root cause of these hard water symptoms. We check water hardness levels, inspect all system components, and provide an accurate assessment and repair plan tailored to your home in Livermore.

    Control Valve Troubleshooting & Repair in Livermore, CA

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, regulating the flow of water and initiating regeneration cycles. When it malfunctions, your system can stop softening water, regenerate too often, or leak. We diagnose issues like stuck pistons, worn out seals, motor failures, or programming errors. Our plumbers carry a range of replacement parts for popular brands like Fleck, Clack, and Autotrol, ensuring a quick and effective repair to restore proper operation.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Livermore

    A common issue with salt-based water softeners is a salt bridge forming in the brine tank, preventing the salt from dissolving properly. This leads to inefficient regeneration and hard water. Our technicians are skilled at breaking up these bridges, cleaning the brine tank, and making sure the float switch and brine well are functioning correctly. We also inspect for any leaks or cracks in the tank itself that could be compromising your system's performance, offering precise repairs or replacements.

    Water Softener Leak Repair in Livermore, CA

    Leaks in a water softener can range from minor drips around fittings to significant puddles from a cracked tank or faulty valve. These leaks can cause water damage and waste water. Our plumbers promptly pinpoint the source of leaks, whether from seals, O-rings, hoses, or internal components, and perform lasting repairs. We use appropriate sealing compounds and replacement parts for a lasting fix in Livermore, CA.
