    Batch-Feed Units for Kitchens Without a Switch in Livermore

    Batch-feed disposals only run when the stopper lid is in place, which adds safety for families with kids. They need no wall switch, so they are a good choice for kitchens where adding a switch would mean cutting into finished drywall or tile backsplash. The drain hookup is the usual setup.

    First-Time Disposal Installation in Livermore

    If your kitchen has never had a disposal, we start by checking the sink flange, drain height and electrical supply.     Disposals in Remodels and New Builds in Livermore

    On new builds and remodels in Livermore, we work with your contractor so the disposal, the sink and the dishwasher are planned together.     High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in Livermore
