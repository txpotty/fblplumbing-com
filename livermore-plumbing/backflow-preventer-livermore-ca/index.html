    Frequently Asked Questions About Backflow Preventer in Livermore

    A standard backflow test in Livermore, CA usually runs $75 to $150. Repairs usually land between $200 and $600 based on the parts, and new assemblies run $450 to $1,500 or more for bigger commercial units. Size drives the price. You get a quote before any work starts.

    Look for a steady leak from the device, a sudden drop in water pressure or discolored water at the tap. On an RPZ, water spitting from the relief port every time pressure changes is a sign a check is leaking back. Have it looked at.

    Backflow preventers stop contaminated water from flowing backward into the public supply during pressure drops. Sprinkler systems, fire lines and commercial equipment are the usual sources, and providers require protection on all of them. It is the law in most places.

    Constant dripping from the relief valve, low water pressure and visible cracks are the usual signs. Discolored water also counts. Prompt repair keeps you in compliance and keeps the water provider from sending a second notice with a new deadline attached.

    Main breaks, hydrant use and fire flow can drop city water pressure in seconds. When that happens, any cross-connection, a sprinkler line, a boiler or a hose in a bucket, can pull water back toward the main. That is the job the device does.

    A brass assembly often lasts 10 to 20 years, sometimes longer. The rubber parts inside wear out much sooner, which is why rebuild kits exist and why most failed tests are fixed without replacing the body. Cold snaps are hard on them.

    Water providers may send follow-up notices, charge fees or eventually suspend service. Scheduling early avoids these problems. Rush tests are often possible within a day or two of your call.

    An RPZ has two checks with a relief valve between them, and it is rated for high-hazard lines. A double check has the two checks but no relief valve, which limits it to lower hazards such as many fire and irrigation lines. Code decides which one.
