    Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Livermore

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. It runs while the switch is on. Batch feed only runs with its stopper turned in place, so nobody can reach into a running unit, which some families with small kids prefer and which needs no wall switch at all. Each type gets wired its own way.

    Hard Water and Disposals in Livermore

    Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. Scale builds up on grind rings and drain tailpieces over the years. A scaled unit gets loud.     Air Switch Disposal Installation in Livermore

    An air switch puts a push button on the countertop or sink deck instead of a wall switch. The button pushes a puff of air down a thin tube. That pulse trips a switch in a control box plugged in under the sink, so no live wiring sits anywhere near the water, which is the main reason people like them. If there is a spare faucet hole, we use it, otherwise we drill.

    Nearly every InSinkErator has a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. We drive the plug out and fish it out of the chamber.
