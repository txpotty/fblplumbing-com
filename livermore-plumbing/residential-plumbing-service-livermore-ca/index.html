    Drain Cleaning and Sewer Camera Service in Livermore

    Wipes are not flushable, whatever the package says. Kitchen grease, hair and wipes cause most slow drains, and for a main line backup we cable it open, then run a camera to look for roots, offset joints or a belly where water sits in the pipe. You get the video.

    Emergency Plumbing Repair in Livermore

    Sewage in the tub at night counts as an emergency. We send a plumber with a cable machine and a wet vac, clears the line, and checks whether the backup came from a simple clog or a main line that needs a camera and a real repair. You get both answers before we leave.

    Repiping and Supply Line Replacement in Livermore

    Polybutylene, the gray plastic pipe used for years in home construction, fails from the inside without warning. It gets replaced with PEX or copper, same as rusted galvanized and pinholed copper, routing new lines through the attic and walls so the slab stays intact and the house keeps water each night. We patch the access holes, ready for paint.

    Faucet and Fixture Repair in Livermore

    Most dripping faucets need a cartridge, not a new faucet. We carry cartridges for Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister, and when you do want new fixtures we install them, connect new braided supply lines, and check the angle stops under the sink while we are there. Old angle stops often break when turned.

    Toilet Repair and Installation in Livermore

    A running toilet can waste a couple hundred gallons a day. Most fixes are a flapper, a fill valve or a new wax ring, and when the bowl is cracked or the toilet is older than the last bathroom remodel, we set a new water-saving model and level it on the flange. A toilet that rocks gets shimmed so the wax seal holds.

    Emergency Home Plumbing Help in Livermore

    A gas smell or sewage backup cannot wait until Monday. Our trucks carry enough parts to stop most emergencies on the first visit, and while the plumber is driving over, the dispatcher walks you through shutting off the water main or the gas valve at the meter. Fast shutoff saves floors and cabinets.

    Leak Repair and Slab Leak Service in Livermore

    On a slab foundation, a supply leak can run for weeks unseen. Warm spots on tile, a meter that spins with every tap off or a water bill that doubles are the usual clues. We pinpoint it before quoting. Repeat leaks usually point to a repipe instead of another patch.
