    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Livermore

    Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE pipe through the old line while breaking the old pipe apart. It needs only small access pits at each end. This protects landscaping, driveways and patios. It works well for clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe. Most jobs are finished in one day.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Livermore, CA

    Some lines have severe sagging, wrong slope or are too shallow for trenchless methods. In these cases, open-trench replacement is the right option. We excavate, remove the old pipe and install new PVC with proper bedding. We restore the trench once it passes inspection. This method allows full correction of slope problems.

    Sewer Pipe Material Upgrades in Livermore, CA

    Many older homes in Livermore, CA still have original sewer lines made of clay, cast iron, or Orangeburg pipe, which are prone to degradation, cracking, and root intrusion over time. When we perform a sewer replacement, we often suggest upgrading to modern, durable materials like PVC (polyvinyl chloride) or ABS (acrylonitrile butadiene styrene). These materials are more flexible, resistant to corrosion and roots, and have a much longer lifespan, providing a significant upgrade to your home's plumbing infrastructure.
