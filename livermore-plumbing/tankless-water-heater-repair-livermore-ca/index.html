    What Our Tankless Techs Fix in Livermore, CA

    Recirculation Pump Repair in Livermore

    Recirculation cuts the wait for hot water at a far bathroom from a minute or more to a few seconds. When the pump fails or the check valve sticks, you get long waits again or lukewarm water in the cold line. We test the pump, look for a thermal bypass valve under the farthest sink, and set the schedule so it runs around your mornings and evenings. Two settings, mostly.

    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Livermore

    Rinnai shows an LC code and Navien posts a scale warning when the exchanger runs hot from mineral buildup. Clear the code without flushing and it comes right back, because the scale is still in there. We connect a circulation pump to the service valves, circulate descaling solution through the exchanger for close to an hour, then flush and clean the inlet screen. Yearly is right for most hard-water homes.

    No Hot Water Tankless Repair in Livermore

    No hot water at all usually points to ignition, gas supply or a heat exchanger choked with scale. The service menu's error history is where we start, then test the igniter, flame rod and gas pressure in that order, because a weak flame signal and low pressure can look identical on the display. Venting gets checked too. Common igniters and flame rods ride on the truck.

    Tankless Repair vs Replacement Cost in Livermore, CA

    Descaling usually runs $175 to $350, and sensor or igniter repairs land between $250 and $600. A heat exchanger out of warranty can cost $800 to $1,500 in parts and labor. Replacement is a bigger number. Installed, a new gas tankless typically runs $3,000 to $5,500, so on a unit past 15 years with a failed exchanger we usually lean toward replacing.

    Igniter & Flame Rod Repair in Livermore, CA

    Gas tankless heaters light with a hot surface or spark igniter, then prove flame with a flame rod. A rod coated in oxide reads low on a microamp meter and the board shuts the gas valve after a few seconds. We clean or replace the rod, check the igniter for cracks or weak spark, and confirm the gas valve opens on call. Five-minute fix sometimes.

    Tankless Flush & Scale Removal in Livermore

    Lukewarm water at high flow and a unit that runs loud are two early signs of scale. Well-done installs usually include isolation valves with purge ports for this, and if yours does not, we can add them during the visit so future flushing takes less time. Vinegar works in a pinch. Commercial descaler works faster.

    Emergency Tankless Water Heater Repair in Livermore, CA

    A tankless heater that dies on a cold morning in Livermore is an emergency for a family of five. We take no-hot-water calls first, and the truck carries the parts that fail most: flame rods, igniters, flow sensors, thermistors and inlet filters. A leaking heat exchanger gets isolated at the service valves and the part goes on order that day. Control boards take longer.

If you smell gas, leave the house first and call the gas utility from outside, then call us, and we can shut the gas cock at the heater and isolate the water until the repair is done.
