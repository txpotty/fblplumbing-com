    Pressure Testing and Isolation in Livermore

    Pressure isolation is always the first step. Our tech closes the main, caps or valves off the hot and cold sides, and puts a gauge on each, then watches which side loses pressure over a set time. The gauge points us to the right line. It also rules out a toilet or faucet leak masquerading as a slab leak in your Livermore, CA home.

    Direct Access Slab Leak Repair in Livermore, CA

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Livermore

    Hot lines leak under slabs more often than cold ones.     Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Livermore

    Many newer homes sit on post-tension slabs. We scan for tendons with ground-penetrating radar or a rebar locator before opening anything, or recommend a reroute that never touches the concrete.
