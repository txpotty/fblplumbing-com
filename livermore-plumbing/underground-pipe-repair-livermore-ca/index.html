    Water Main Underground Repair in Livermore

    Your water main is the pipe between the meter and the house. The homeowner side is yours. When it breaks, we locate the leak without digging, expose the pipe and cut out the bad section and join in new pipe with buried-rated fittings. Rusted steel lines rarely patch well.

    Trenchless Underground Pipe Repair in Livermore

    Digging a trench across a mature yard is expensive to restore. Pull-through methods save the lawn. We open small access pits at each end, then pull new pipe through the old path or line the old pipe in place, which saves sod, irrigation, pavers and concrete that would otherwise have to be torn out and replaced. Faster, too.

    Burst Main Line Repair in Livermore, CA

    A ruptured main can flood a yard or leave you with no water at all. Locate your shutoff. We help you close the meter valve over the phone, then send a crew to locate and repair the break, while sorting out with the utility whether the leak is on their side. We keep you posted.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Livermore, CA

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. New pipe follows the old path. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, while a continuous HDPE pipe is pulled in behind at the same diameter or bigger. It works on badly damaged lines. Lining cannot do that.

    Buried Sewer Lateral Repair in Livermore, CA

    Buried laterals crack from roots, settling and age. The camera finds the spot. We excavate at the mark, cut out the damaged length and replace it with Schedule 40 PVC, joining it to the existing pipe with shielded couplings sized for the outside diameter of each material. We confirm slope before backfill.

    Irrigation and Yard Line Repair in Livermore

    Sprinkler supply lines sit shallow and break easily. Shovels and roots get them. A broken irrigation main can run up a water bill fast in the growing season, so we repair PVC and poly mains, replace failed backflow connections and fix the supply line to the valve manifold. Vulnerable runs can be moved.

    Sewer Line Underground Repair in Livermore, CA

    Roots and settling soil cause most buried sewer failures. Old pipe gives up. We offer open trench repair, pipe bursting and cured-in-place lining, and the camera footage decides which one fits the depth, the damage and what sits on top of the line.
