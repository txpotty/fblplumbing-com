    Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in Merced, CA

    A key part of any toilet installation is ensuring a proper seal between the toilet and the waste pipe, typically achieved with a wax ring and a secure flange.     Comfort-Height and ADA Toilet Installation in Merced

    Comfort-height toilets put the seat at about 17 to 19 inches, which is gentler on knees and backs. For accessible bathrooms, we follow ADA guidelines for seat height, centerline distance from the side wall and grab bar clearance. We can install elongated bowls and add blocking for grab bars while the wall is open. These upgrades are popular with homeowners planning to age in place.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Merced, CA

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, with the tank hidden behind an access plate. They free up floor space and make cleaning easy, but they need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep or a furred-out chase. We install Geberit, Grohe and Kohler carrier systems and set the bowl height to your preference. This work is best done during a remodel when the wall is open.

    Bathroom Remodel Toilet Installation in Merced, CA

    Remodels often raise the floor with new tile, which can leave the old flange too low. We use flange extenders or replace the flange so the seal compresses properly. If you are moving the toilet, we can relocate the drain, which on a slab foundation requires cutting and patching concrete. We coordinate timing so the toilet goes in after tile and grout have cured.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Installation in Merced

    The cost of toilet installation in Merced, CA usually ranges from $200 to $500, not including the toilet fixture itself. This price can vary based on factors like the type of toilet, any necessary plumbing modifications, or the difficulty of removing an old unit.

    Replacing a toilet in the same location generally does not need a permit, but new bathrooms, additions and drain relocations typically do. Requirements vary by city, and we check with the local building department for your project.

    A standard toilet installation, assuming no major plumbing modifications are needed, typically takes about 1 to 3 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new rough-ins or dealing with damaged subflooring, may take longer.

    While it's possible for a homeowner to attempt a DIY toilet installation, it's often best left to professionals. Improper installation can lead to leaks, wobbling, inefficient flushing, and potential water damage. Professional plumbers deliver proper sealing, secure mounting, and adherence to codes.

    A comfort height toilet has a bowl that is taller than standard models, typically around 17 to 19 inches from the floor to the top of the seat. This height is closer to a standard chair, making it more comfortable for many adults, especially those with mobility issues.

    Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. Hard water can lead to mineral buildup in toilet tanks and bowls over time, affecting performance and requiring more frequent cleaning. While it doesn't directly impact installation, choosing a toilet with a good glaze and strong flush can help reduce these effects.

    Installing a new toilet can offer several benefits, including improved water efficiency, lower water bills, better flushing power, a better look, and increased comfort. Modern toilets are also designed to be more durable and simpler to clean.

    Common signs that you might need a new toilet include frequent clogs, constant running, visible cracks in the bowl or tank, excessive wobbling, or a toilet that is very old and inefficient. If you're constantly repairing it, replacement is often more cost-effective.
