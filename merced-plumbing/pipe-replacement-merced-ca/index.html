    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Merced

    Gray polybutylene pipe was installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s. It breaks down from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water, and fittings can crack without warning. Some insurers raise premiums or decline coverage on homes that still have it. We remove polybutylene supply lines and replace them with PEX-A or copper. A completed repipe can also help when listing the home.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Merced, CA

    Many older homes in Merced, CA still have galvanized steel pipes, which are prone to internal corrosion over time. This corrosion chokes water flow, reduces water pressure, and can introduce rust particles into your drinking water. Our pipe replacement service targets these corroded sections, replacing them with new, non-corrosive materials like PEX or copper. This restores proper water flow and ensures clean, clear water throughout your home.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Merced

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping has become a common choice for residential pipe replacement due to its flexibility, affordability, and resistance to freezing. Freezing is uncommon along the coast and on valley floors in Northern California, but frosty nights do happen and can crack exposed outdoor pipes. Unlike rigid pipes, PEX can expand, reducing the risk of burst pipes during sudden cold snaps. FBL Plumbing specializes in PEX installation, which often allows for less invasive replacement methods compared to traditional materials. This modern solution offers strong performance and water quality for your home.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Merced, CA

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. They are caused by a mix of water chemistry, high velocity and thin Type M pipe, and once they begin they tend to keep coming back. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes. We help you compare the cost of both paths.
