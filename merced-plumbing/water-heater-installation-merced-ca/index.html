    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Merced

    The cost of water heater installation in Merced, CA usually ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the type of water heater (tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any needed plumbing or electrical modifications. We provide a written, upfront quote after assessing your particular needs.

    To improve efficiency, make sure your new water heater is properly sized for your home, consider a tankless model for on-demand heating, insulate hot water pipes and set the thermostat to 120°F (49°C) to prevent scalding and reduce energy consumption.

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve or check valve, an expansion tank is typically required. It protects your water heater and pipes from pressure spikes.

    We install Rheem, A.O. Smith, Bradford White, Navien, Rinnai and other trusted brands. We recommend models based on reliability, warranty and budget.

    Yes, we stand behind our work. Our water heater installations come with a labor warranty, and the water heater unit itself will have a manufacturer's warranty, typically ranging from 6 to 12 years, depending on the model.

    A standard tank replacement typically takes two to four hours. Tankless installs or relocations can take most of a day.

    A traditional tank water heater typically lasts between 8 and 12 years with proper maintenance. Tankless water heaters often have a longer lifespan, ranging from 15 to 20 years, thanks to their durable components and on-demand operation.

    The decision to repair or replace often depends on the age of your unit, the cost of the repair and its energy efficiency.
