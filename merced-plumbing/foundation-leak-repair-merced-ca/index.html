    Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Merced, CA

    Foundation Leak Detection in Merced

    Accurate detection is the most important step in foundation leak repair. We isolate hot and cold lines and pressure test them to confirm a leak exists. Then we use acoustic listening equipment and electronic line tracing to find the exact spot beneath the foundation. Thermal imaging helps verify hot water leaks by showing warm spots on the floor.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Repair in Merced

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs with steel cables under high tension. Cutting into these slabs takes care and scanning to avoid hitting a cable. We use ground-penetrating radar or other scanning methods before opening the floor. When risk is too high, rerouting the plumbing is usually the safer option.

    Crawl Space Foundation Leak Repair in Merced, CA

    Crawl space foundations can suffer leaks from plumbing, groundwater, or HVAC condensation. These leaks can lead to wood rot, mold, and structural damage if not handled quickly. Our team carefully inspects the crawl space and identifies the source of water intrusion. We then put in place solutions such as pipe repair, sump pump installation, or vapor barrier replacement to dry out and protect your crawl space in Merced, CA from future moisture issues.
