    Tankless water heaters offer several key benefits: endless hot water on demand, real energy savings from heating water only when needed, a longer lifespan (often 20+ years) and a compact, space-saving design. They also reduce the risk of tank leaks and the water damage that comes with them.

    Sizing a tankless water heater depends on your household's peak hot water demand, measured in gallons per minute (GPM), and the temperature rise needed. Our plumbers will assess the hot water fixtures in your home and review your usage patterns to recommend the right GPM and BTU rating for your needs in Merced.

    In Northern California, plumbing and often gas or electrical permits are usually required for tankless water heater installation. FBL Plumbing handles all necessary permit applications and makes sure your installation passes all required inspections by local authorities.

    Tankless water heaters benefit from annual flushing to remove mineral buildup, especially in areas with hard water. This helps maintain efficiency and extend the unit's lifespan. We offer maintenance services to keep your system running optimally.
