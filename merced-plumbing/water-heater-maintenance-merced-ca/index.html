    Descaling Tankless Units in Merced

    Error codes often mean scale. Tankless heaters are especially sensitive to hard water, and once scale coats the heat exchanger, flow drops, the unit overheats and the control board shuts it down to protect itself.     Tankless Water Heater Service in Merced, CA

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale inside the heat exchanger chokes flow until the unit throws codes or shuts off mid-shower. We flush it with descaler. We also check the igniter, the fan and the exhaust vent for safe operation.

    Relief Valve and Safety Testing in Merced, CA

    The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense. We lift the lever, confirm water discharges and the valve reseats without dripping, and check that the discharge pipe runs full size to an approved spot within a few inches of the floor or outside. Leaky valves get replaced. We check the expansion tank too.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Merced

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment piles up around the lower element, it runs hotter than it should, and eventually it burns out, which is why you suddenly get only a few minutes of hot water. We test both elements. Thermostats and wiring get checked with the power off.

    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Merced

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Scale on the tank bottom traps heat and causes water to pop and rumble as it heats. We flush until it runs clear. Bad tanks need a few rounds.
