    Resin Cleaning and Regeneration Tuning in Merced, CA

    Iron is the most common resin fouler we see. It coats the beads orange and blocks the exchange sites. We feed a resin cleaner through the brine well, run a regeneration and retest, and if the capacity comes back we shorten the time between regenerations so the iron does not build back up.

    Service for Most Softener Brands in Merced, CA

    Full-System Softener Inspections in Merced

    An inspection covers more than the two tanks. We trace the inlet and outlet plumbing, the bypass, the drain line and the brine overflow. Green crust on copper fittings, a slow leak at the bypass or a drain line jammed down into a standpipe all go on the written report.

    Regeneration Programming and Hardness Testing in Merced, CA

    Plenty of softeners still run factory settings. Either the installer left the defaults in, or the family got bigger and the settings stayed put. We enter your raw hardness and household size, then set a metered valve to regenerate by gallons used rather than on a fixed timer schedule.

    Softener Brands We Work On in Merced

    Most softeners sold in the last 20 years run on a handful of valve platforms, mainly Fleck, Clack, Autotrol and a few proprietary heads. Once you know the valve, the name on the cabinet matters less. Kinetico and Culligan use their own designs, so we check part availability before quoting.

    Yearly Water Softener Tune-Ups in Merced

    A tune-up starts with two hardness readings and ends with a full manual regeneration that we watch from start to finish. In between, we check the salt level, the brine well and the float. It takes about an hour on most homes.

    Commercial Softener Service in Merced

    Commercial units work harder. Restaurants and laundromats can burn through a softener's capacity in a single day, so twin-tank systems swap tanks, keeping one in service while the other regenerates. We set visits around your business hours so the water never goes hard during a rush.

    Control Valve and Timer Diagnostics in Merced, CA
