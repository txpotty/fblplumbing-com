    PEX Versus Copper Repipe Pricing in Merced, CA

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper because the material is cheaper and installation goes faster. Copper costs more. Copper commonly costs 20 to 40 percent more than PEX, and some owners prefer it anyway for its long history. Our quotes for either include permit, fittings, patching and cleanup.

    Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Merced, CA

    A whole-home PEX repipe for an average house often runs $5,000 to $12,000, with copper costing more. One slab leak with access and repair often costs $1,000 to $4,000. Do the math. After two or three leaks, a repipe often pays for itself, and we give you a side-by-side comparison so you can make that call with real numbers in front of you.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Merced, CA

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. It fails without warning. Brittle pipe walls and cracked fittings mean one polybutylene leak usually signals risk across the whole house. Full replacement is the fix. Many insurers ask about it.

    Condo, Townhome and Multi-Family Repipes in Merced

    Shared walls and HOA rules make repiping attached homes more involved.     Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Merced

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. It holds up well, does not mind sunlight in an exposed run and has a long track record behind it. It costs more. We sweat joints with lead-free solder or press them, hang the pipe at proper support intervals and test the whole system before patching.

    Slab Leak Repiping in Merced

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Earthquakes and hillside soil movement are real risks in Northern California, so flexible connections and seismic straps help protect plumbing from ground movement. Instead of breaking concrete to patch one pipe, we reroute new supply lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, then abandon the old lines in the slab. No jackhammer needed. It ends this leak and takes the rest of the slab plumbing out of the picture.

    Type L Copper Repiping in Merced

    Copper has been plumbing homes for generations, and done right it lasts a long time. We use Type L, not the thinner Type M, and we support it with the right hangers so it does not rub or rattle in the walls. Lead-free solder only. Copper can pinhole in aggressive water, so we ask about your water and any past leaks before recommending it over PEX.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Merced, CA

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Flexible PEX soaks up some of the pressure spike behind water hammer, and its long fitting-free runs leave fewer weak points. Attic PEX gets kept away from flues and out of UV.

    Fixing one leak at a time works until the leaks start coming every few months. That is the turning point. Each repair means a service call, opened drywall and sometimes an insurance claim, while a repipe replaces all of the aging pipe at once and ends the cycle for decades.

    Both work well, and the best choice depends on your house and your water. PEX is cheaper. PEX bends, resists scale and handles freezes better, and copper is rigid, UV-tolerant and proven but pricier and vulnerable to aggressive water.

    A whole-home repipe requires permits, pressure testing and code knowledge. A licensed crew usually finishes faster and passes inspection the first time.

    A documented repipe removes a known risk that buyers and inspectors look for. Buyers notice it. Replacing polybutylene, Kitec or old galvanized makes a home easier to insure and easier to sell, and a permit record plus a transferable warranty gives the next owner proof the work was done.
