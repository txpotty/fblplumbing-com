    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Merced

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to three days.

    Emergency Repipe and Leak Repair in Merced

    While a full repipe is a planned project, sometimes a major pipe burst or multiple severe leaks can call for immediate action. Our repipe specialists in Merced are equipped to handle emergency situations, providing temporary fixes and assessing the need for a more complete repiping solution. We can quickly isolate damaged sections and offer options for immediate repair or a phased repiping approach to restore your water service safely and efficiently, preventing extensive water damage.

    Copper Repiping in Merced, CA

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes. Joints are soldered with lead-free solder or pressed with ProPress fittings. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a bit longer than PEX.

    Cost-Effective Repiping Comparisons in Merced, CA

    Understanding the cost of repiping is important for homeowners in Merced, CA. We provide clear comparisons between PEX and copper repiping options, detailing material costs, labor, and expected lifespan. PEX is generally more affordable for materials and installation, while copper offers a premium, long-lasting solution with excellent resale value. Our estimates include all necessary permits, fittings, and cleanup, so you have a clear picture of your investment before any work begins, with no hidden fees.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Merced

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab. This is a good option for newer homes or a first-time leak. If the home has had multiple leaks, we will explain why a full repipe may save money.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Merced, CA

    Many older homes in Merced, CA still have galvanized steel pipes, which are vulnerable to corrosion, rust, and mineral buildup. This leads to low water pressure, discolored water, and eventual leaks. Our team specializes in the complete removal and replacement of these outdated systems with modern PEX or copper piping. This upgrade not only resolves existing problems but also greatly improves water quality and flow, boosting your home's value and plumbing reliability for decades to come.

    Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Merced, CA

    A whole-home PEX repipe for an average home often runs $5,000 to $12,000, with copper typically costing more. A single slab leak repair can run $1,000 to $4,000 including access and restoration. After two or three leaks, a repipe often pays for itself. We give you a side-by-side comparison so you can make the call.
