    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Merced

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. Since a crimp is only as good as the tool that made it, every ring gets a go/no-go gauge check, and any ring that fails gets cut off and redone. That takes seconds. Skipping it is how leaks start.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Merced

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and it really depends on the house. PEX-A bends tighter, recovers from a kink with a heat gun, and its expansion fittings flow more water, while PEX-B costs less and its crimp joints are just as reliable when they are gauged properly. Both last decades. For tight attics we lean toward PEX-A.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Merced, CA

    Gray polybutylene was installed widely from the late seventies into the mid nineties. Chlorine in treated water attacks the plastic and the acetal fittings from the inside, so it can look fine from outside right up to the day a joint lets go. Insurers ask about it. Swapping it for PEX can help with insurance and with resale.
