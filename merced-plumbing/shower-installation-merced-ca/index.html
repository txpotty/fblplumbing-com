    Curbless Shower Installation in Merced

    Curbless showers have no step, which makes them easier to use and gives a modern look. They require a recessed floor and careful drain placement, often with a linear drain. On a slab, we may need to lower the floor in the shower area. We work closely with your contractor to plan the slope and waterproofing.

    Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Merced, CA

    Accessible showers need low or no thresholds, handheld showers on slide bars and properly placed controls. We install grab bar blocking while walls are open and set valves within easy reach of the seat. These features help seniors and people with mobility needs bathe safely. We follow ADA guidelines when requested.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Merced, CA

    Fiberglass and acrylic shower units are affordable and quick to install. We set the base, connect the drain and install the valve and trim. Prefab units are ideal for guest baths, rentals and guest houses. We make sure the base is fully supported so it does not flex or crack.

    Standard Shower Stall Installation in Merced

    FBL Plumbing installs all types of standard shower stalls, from fiberglass units to acrylic enclosures. We ensure proper leveling, sealing, and connection to your existing plumbing system. Our team handles the entire process, including securing the base and connecting the drain line, to prevent future leaks and ensure stability. We also verify that water pressure is optimal for your new showerhead.

    Shower Pan Installation in Merced

    A correctly installed shower pan is fundamental for preventing leaks and protecting your bathroom subfloor. FBL Plumbing expertly installs various shower pan types, including pre-fabricated units and custom-tiled pans with proper waterproofing membranes. We make sure the pan is level and correctly sloped toward the drain, a vital step that prevents expensive water damage in the long run. We use high-quality sealants for lasting protection.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Merced

    Steam showers need a steam generator, a dedicated water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator, plumb the supply and drain, and connect the steam head. A licensed electrician handles the circuit. Steam showers also need sealed enclosures and sloped ceilings.

    Custom Shower Enclosure Installation in Merced

    FBL Plumbing provides comprehensive services for custom shower enclosures, including multi-head showers, body sprays, and steam units. These installations require precise planning for water lines, pressure balancing valves, and drainage systems. We ensure all components are correctly sized and installed to deliver strong performance and water flow, taking into account the water pressure variations sometimes seen in older neighborhoods. Proper sealing is critical for these complex systems.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Merced, CA

    Converting an old tub to a walk-in shower is one of the most requested bathroom upgrades. We remove the tub, relocate or resize the drain and install a new shower valve at the right height. On slab foundations, the drain may need to be moved, which involves cutting concrete. The result is a safer, more usable shower.
