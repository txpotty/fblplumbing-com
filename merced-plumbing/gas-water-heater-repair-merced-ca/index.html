    Gas Water Heater Noise Troubleshooting in Merced

    Odd noises coming from your gas water heater, such as rumbling, popping or whistling, often indicate sediment buildup in the tank or a burner problem. Hard water can add to sediment accumulation. Our repair service includes flushing the tank and inspecting for sediment, addressing pressure issues or checking other components that might be causing the noise, improving efficiency and quiet operation.

    Burner Assembly and Venting System Repair in Merced

    A worn burner assembly can result in poor heating or unusual noises. We carefully inspect the burner for blockages, corrosion or wear.     Venting and Draft Repair in Merced, CA

    Atmospheric gas water heaters rely on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home. Disconnected sections, rusted draft hoods and poor slope can let combustion gases spill back. We repair or replace vent sections and confirm draft with a test. Proper venting is essential for your family's safety.

    Pilot Light Repair in Merced

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most frequent gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and clean or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are finished in about an hour.
