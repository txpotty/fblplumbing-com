    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Florin

    The cost of water heater replacement in Florin, CA typically ranges from $1,200 to $2,500 for a standard tank unit, including installation.     Generally, if your water heater is over 10 years old, frequently breaks down or has a major issue like a leaking tank, replacement is often more cost-effective in the long run.     Choosing the right size depends on the number of people in your household, their hot water usage habits and peak demand times.     New water heaters typically come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and parts, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. FBL Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation, giving you added peace of mind. Specific warranty details vary by brand and model.

    While it might seem tempting, water heater replacement involves gas lines, high voltage electricity, water pressure and heavy equipment.     Flushing a traditional tank water heater annually is recommended in most homes. This helps remove sediment buildup, which can reduce efficiency and shorten the lifespan of the unit. Regular flushing can also prevent scale from impacting heating elements.
