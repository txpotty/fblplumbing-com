    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Florin, CA

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter placed ahead of the softener removes those chemicals and protects the resin, since chlorine slowly breaks resin beads down over the years. You will taste it. Setups can be two tanks or one dual-stage tank.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Florin

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. Pairs well with a softener. We can handle filter changes.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Florin, CA

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. FBL Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. Most effective option.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Florin

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. That protects every faucet, shower, water heater and appliance downstream, which is where hard water does most of its damage over the years. We size before we sell. Hardness and household use set the grain capacity.
