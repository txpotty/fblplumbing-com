    Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Florin

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, gravel and leaves settle in the pit. That debris can get sucked into the intake screen and jam the impeller. We pump down and scoop out the basin, rinse the intake and inspect the pit liner for cracks. A clean basin helps the pump run cooler and last years longer.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Maintenance in Florin

    FBL Plumbing is experienced in servicing Zoeller sump pumps, a widely trusted brand known for its durability. We stock common replacement parts for efficient repairs, keeping your Zoeller pump in peak condition.

    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Florin, CA

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil around the slab or foundation.

    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Florin, CA

    The float switch is the most frequent reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get stuck.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Florin, CA

    If your sump pump is acting up, making odd noises or not turning on, our emergency diagnostic service can quickly identify the problem. We use specialized tools to troubleshoot electrical issues, motor failures and float switch malfunctions.     Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Florin, CA

    The float switch is a key component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off.
