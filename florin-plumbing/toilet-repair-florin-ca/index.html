    Running Toilet Repair in Florin

    A running toilet is not just annoying, it can waste hundreds of gallons of water a day, leading to much higher water bills. This common issue is often caused by a faulty flapper, a misaligned fill valve, or an improperly adjusted float. Our plumbers quickly identify the exact cause, whether it's mineral buildup from hard water affecting the flapper seal or a worn-out component, and replace the necessary parts to stop the continuous flow and restore efficiency.

    Clogged Toilet Clearing in Florin, CA

    Stubborn toilet clogs are a frequent problem that can bring your bathroom to a halt. While some can be cleared with a plunger, deeper blockages may indicate issues further down the drain line or even in the main sewer line. Our technicians use professional-grade augers and sometimes even camera inspections to locate and remove obstructions effectively. We handle everything from foreign objects to excessive toilet paper, ensuring your toilet drains freely again.

    Leaking Toilet Base Repair in Florin, CA

    Water around the base usually means the wax ring has failed or the toilet has been rocking on a loose flange. On slab foundations, the flange often sits on concrete and can crack when bolts are overtightened. We pull the toilet, inspect the flange, install a repair ring or new flange if needed and reset the bowl on a fresh seal. We also check for soft flooring so you know if any water damage needs attention.

    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Florin, CA

    A hairline crack in the tank or a bulging braided supply line can let go without warning and flood a bathroom. We inspect the tank, tank-to-bowl bolts and gasket, and the supply line during every visit. If the porcelain is cracked we will price a replacement tank or new toilet, and we upgrade old plastic or vinyl supply lines to braided stainless.

    Toilet Flush Mechanism Repair in Florin, CA

    If your toilet isn't flushing properly, it could be due to issues with the flush chain, handle, flapper, or fill valve. A weak flush may mean the tank isn't filling correctly, while a loose handle might not be engaging the flapper. Our plumbers are adept at diagnosing these internal tank problems, replacing worn or broken components like flush valves, fill valves, and toilet handles to restore full and effective flushing power.
