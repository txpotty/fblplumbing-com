    Breaking Up Salt Bridges in Florin

    To check for a bridge, we push a broom handle straight down through the salt. If it hits a hard layer and then falls through, there is a bridge.     Brine Tank Cleanout and Inspection in Florin, CA

    Brine tanks collect a gray sludge of salt fines and insoluble grit, and it builds up faster with cheap rock salt. The tank gets emptied, scrubbed and rinsed. Then we look for cracks around the brine well and overflow fitting and refill with fresh salt.

    Control Valve and Injector Cleaning in Florin, CA

    Brine draw depends on a venturi injector, a tiny nozzle and throat that pulls brine out of the salt tank as water rushes past. A few specks of grit can stop it cold.
