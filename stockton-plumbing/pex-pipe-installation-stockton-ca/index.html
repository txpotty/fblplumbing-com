    Copper to PEX Conversions in Stockton

    Pinhole leaks in copper tend to come in clusters. Water chemistry, high flow velocity and contact with soil under a slab can all pit the pipe wall from the inside, and once a few pinholes appear, more are usually on the way. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. Converting to PEX stops the cycle. Where new PEX meets existing copper stubs or valves, we use listed transition fittings.

    Commercial PEX Pipe Installation in Stockton

    Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Stockton

    Rough-in heights matter more than people think. If the shower valve sits two inches low or the vanity stubs miss the cabinet, the wall gets reopened after tile, and that costs time and money. We check heights with your contractor before drilling. The test happens before drywall goes up.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Stockton

    PEX-B is stiffer, costs less per foot, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. Since a crimp is only as good as the tool that made it, every ring gets a go/no-go gauge check, and any ring that fails gets cut off and redone. It is a five-second check. Skipping it is how leaks start.

    Commercial PEX Pipe Solutions in Stockton, CA

    Apartment buildings, small offices and churches in Stockton, CA can benefit from PEX as much as houses can. Each unit can get its own manifold and shutoff, so a leak in one apartment shuts down one apartment instead of the whole stack. Fire-rated walls need care. Every penetration through a fire-rated wall gets a listed firestop sleeve or sealant.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Stockton, CA

    With PEX-A, the tech slips an expansion ring onto the pipe and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting goes in while the plastic is still expanded, and the material's memory pulls it back down around the barb, sealing harder over the first minute. Cold weather slows it. Joints get time to set before we pressurize.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Stockton, CA

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. Every one is listed for potable water. Pipe, fittings and tools come from the same maker on our jobs, which keeps the warranty valid.
