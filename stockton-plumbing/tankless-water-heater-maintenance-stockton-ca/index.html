    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Tune-Ups in Stockton, CA

    Noritz tankless water heaters are sturdy systems, but they still require annual tune-ups to maintain their performance through every season. Our maintenance includes checking the unit's exhaust system, confirming proper combustion air intake and verifying the integrity of the heat exchanger. We also calibrate the temperature and flow settings for maximum efficiency and comfort, preventing common issues like cold water sandwiches.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Stockton

    The heat exchanger in a tankless heater is a coil of small tubes that water races through while the burner fires. Hard water drops calcium and magnesium on those hot surfaces, narrowing the passages over time. We connect a submersible pump and hoses to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with fresh water to rinse the system thoroughly. Restored flow means steadier temperatures and fewer shutdowns.
