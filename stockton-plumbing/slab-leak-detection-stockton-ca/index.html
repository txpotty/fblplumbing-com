    While you can look for the signs, precisely detecting a slab leak requires specialized equipment and expertise that most homeowners don't have. DIY attempts often lead to more damage or misdiagnosis, making professional detection a more affordable and reliable solution in the long run.

    Common signs of a slab leak in Northern California homes include unexplained increases in water bills, warm spots on your floor, the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use, damp or mildewy smells and visible cracks in your foundation or walls. Pooling water outside your home near the foundation can also be a strong indicator.

    A water line slab leak involves pressurized fresh water escaping from supply pipes, often resulting in higher water bills, warm spots or the sound of running water. A sewer line slab leak involves wastewater leaking from drain pipes, which typically causes foul odors, slow drains or visible sewage seepage, and is not usually pressurized.

    Most slab leak detection services can be completed within 1 to 3 hours, depending on the severity and location of the leak. Our goal is always to be as efficient and non-invasive as possible, providing a quick diagnosis so repair work can begin soon.
