    Backflow Preventer Testing in Stockton

    Many Northern California water providers require a yearly test on every testable assembly. Our certified testers connect a calibrated differential gauge to the test cocks and measure the pressure drop across each check valve and the opening point of the relief valve. Each reading has a minimum. RPZs, double checks and PVBs all get tested, and the report goes to your provider.

    Febco Backflow Preventer Experts in Stockton

    Febco assemblies are common on irrigation and commercial lines. We work on the 765 pressure vacuum breaker, the 850 double check and the 825Y and 860 reduced pressure assemblies.     Backflow Preventer Repair and Rebuild in Stockton, CA

    Worn check rubbers, grit under a seat and a relief valve that drips are the common failures. We isolate the assembly, take it apart and replace the worn parts with the manufacturer's rebuild kit, cleaning each seat before it goes back together. Then we retest. Rebuilding usually costs much less than replacing the whole assembly.

    New Backflow Preventer Installation in Stockton
