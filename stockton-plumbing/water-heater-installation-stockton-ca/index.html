    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Stockton

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, particularly in closed-loop systems. Code here requires seismic strapping on water heaters and low-flow fixtures, and many local agencies require sewer lateral inspections at sale. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to ensure proper system operation and prevent premature wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Stockton.
