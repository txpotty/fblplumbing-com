    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Replacement in Stockton

    The cost of toilet replacement in Stockton, CA usually ranges from $400 to $800, including the new toilet fixture and labor. This can vary based on the toilet model you choose, any necessary repairs to the flange or subfloor, and the difficulty of the installation.

    A like-for-like toilet replacement in the same location usually does not require a permit. If drains are moved or a new bathroom is added, a permit is usually required.

    Frequent clogs, cracks in the porcelain, constant repairs, rocking that returns after resetting and very high water use are common signs. Toilets more than 25 years old are usually good candidates for replacement.

    Manufacturers typically warranty parts for 1 to 5 years and porcelain longer. We guarantee our workmanship and will return if a leak is caused by the installation.

    We recommend reliable brands known for their quality, efficiency, and availability of parts. Popular choices include Kohler, American Standard, Toto, Delta, and Gerber. Each offers a range of styles and features, from basic models to advanced low-flow or comfort height options. We can help you select the right fit for your needs.

    Most replacements take 1.5 to 2.5 hours. If the flange or subfloor needs repair, it may take longer.

    Older toilets can use 3.5 to 7 gallons per flush, while WaterSense toilets use 1.28 gallons or less. For a typical household, that can mean thousands of gallons saved each year.

    The 'rough-in' measurement is the distance from the finished wall behind the toilet to the center of the drainpipe. Common rough-ins are 10, 12, or 14 inches. It is essential for selecting a new toilet that fits correctly without extensive changes to your bathroom plumbing.
