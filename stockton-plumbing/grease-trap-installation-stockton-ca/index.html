    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Trap Installation in Stockton, CA

    Hydromechanical traps use baffles and a vented flow control to pull grease out of water moving at a set rate, usually 20 to 100 gallons per minute. Rust shows up too. We also look downstream, because grease in the outlet pipe means the trap is letting it through.
