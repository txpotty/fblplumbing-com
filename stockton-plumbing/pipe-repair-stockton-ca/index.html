    PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Stockton

    PEX is flexible and handles freezing better than rigid pipe. It still fails. Most PEX leaks happen at a crimp ring that was not fully seated, an early brass fitting that dezincified or a section damaged by sunlight in an attic, and we cut out the bad fitting and replace it with a crimp, clamp or expansion connection rated for the pipe. Usually a quick fix.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Stockton

    Pinhole leaks form when the copper wall thins from the inside. Water chemistry causes most. High velocity, poor grounding and aggressive water all speed it up, and the leak starts as a tiny drip that stains drywall or a ceiling, so we cut out the affected section and replace it with new copper or a PEX transition. One usually means more are coming.

    Underground Pipe Repair in Stockton

    Buried pipe takes stress from settling soil, roots and vehicles. You rarely see them.     Frozen and Burst Line Repair in Stockton

    Pipes in exterior walls, attics and crawlspaces freeze first. Frozen water cracks it. The split usually does not leak until the ice thaws, which is why many burst pipes are found hours after the cold snap. We repair the split and add insulation. Heat tape is an option on problem runs.

    Galvanized Pipe Repair in Stockton

    Threaded steel pipe rusts from the inside until flow drops and threaded joints start to leak. It can be patched. The trouble is that every fitting you disturb on old galvanized can start a new leak, which is why we use dielectric transitions and lay out a replacement plan for the rest. You decide the timing.
