    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Stockton, CA

    Calcium scale is the leading cause of tankless heater failure. Rural properties around Stockton often rely on private wells, which may need filtration for iron, sediment, or other minerals. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides extra protection.
