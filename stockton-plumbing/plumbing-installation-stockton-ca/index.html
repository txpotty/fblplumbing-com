    Natural Gas and Propane Lines in Stockton, CA

    Natural gas and propane run at different pressures, and the pipe sizing changes with them. Our licensed plumbers size each run from the appliance BTU ratings and the developed length, then choose black iron or CSST to suit the route. CSST must be bonded.
