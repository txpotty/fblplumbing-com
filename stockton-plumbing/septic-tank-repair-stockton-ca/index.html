    Septic Baffle and Tee Repair in Stockton

    Baffles and tees keep scum and solids in the tank so they do not flow out into the leach field. Concrete baffles crumble from hydrogen sulfide gas, and plastic tees can break off. When they fail, solids clog the field fast. We replace damaged baffles with durable PVC tees and add an effluent filter where it makes sense.

    Septic Tank Lid and Riser Repair in Stockton

    Cracked or deteriorated concrete lids are a serious safety hazard. We replace failing lids and install risers that bring access up to grade. Risers make pumping and inspection faster and cheaper over time. Secure, child-resistant lids are standard on our installations.
