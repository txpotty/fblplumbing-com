    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Stockton, CA

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, with the tank hidden behind an access plate. They free up floor space and make cleaning simple, but they need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep or a furred-out chase. We install Geberit, Grohe and Kohler carrier systems and set the bowl height to your preference. This work is easiest during a remodel when the wall is open.

    Smart Toilet and Bidet Installation in Stockton
