    Metered Softener Replacement in Stockton

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly used up. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first few weeks.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Stockton

    Large households and homes with very hard water can benefit from a dual tank system that provides soft water all day and night. While one tank regenerates, the other stays in service. Kinetico and similar non-electric twin tank systems are popular for this reason. We size dual tank systems to your peak demand. They cost more upfront but avoid hard water bleed-through during regeneration.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Stockton, CA

    Fleck is a well-known brand for dependable water softener control valves, found in many residential and commercial systems across Northern California. If your Fleck-controlled softener is beyond repair or simply outdated, FBL Plumbing can expertly replace the entire unit. We are familiar with many Fleck models and can recommend an equivalent or upgraded system that fits seamlessly with your existing plumbing in Stockton.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Alternatives in Stockton, CA

    Some homeowners prefer a salt-free scale reduction system that uses template-assisted crystallization media. These systems do not remove hardness minerals but change them so they are less likely to stick. They need no drain and no salt. We explain the differences plainly, since they work differently than true softeners. They can be a good fit when drain access is limited or sodium is a concern.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Stockton, CA

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more economical than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes better sense.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Stockton

    A standard single tank softener replacement usually costs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Dual tank or premium systems usually run $3,000 to $5,000. Adding a new loop or drain line can raise the cost.

    Salt-based softeners remove hardness minerals, calcium and magnesium, through ion exchange, effectively softening the water. Salt-free conditioners do not remove minerals but alter their crystal structure so they do not stick to surfaces. That prevents scale buildup without actually softening the water.

    Most residential water softener replacements can be completed within 3 to 6 hours, assuming no major plumbing changes are required. Our experienced plumbers work efficiently to minimize disruption, restoring soft water to your home on the same day.

    In many areas, a plumbing permit is not typically required for a like-for-like water softener replacement, especially if there are no major changes to the existing plumbing lines. However, if new lines are run or the system type changes substantially, a permit might be necessary. We handle all needed checks and make sure the job complies with local codes.

    Most softeners last 10 to 15 years. Resin and valves wear faster with very hard or chlorinated water.

    A new, properly sized water softener can sometimes improve water pressure if your old unit was undersized or had heavy mineral buildup restricting flow. However, a water softener's main job is to remove hardness minerals, not to boost pressure. If low water pressure is your main concern, we can investigate other causes.

    Requirements vary by city. Simple replacements may not require one, but new plumbing connections may. We check local rules for you.
