    Sewer Camera Inspections for Trenchless Repair in Stockton

    Before any trenchless repair, a detailed sewer camera inspection is crucial. Our high-definition cameras travel through your sewer lines, providing real-time video of the pipe's interior.     Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Stockton

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while fracturing the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is very resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Stockton homes. This method is ideal when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Stockton

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot penetrate. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their shape.

    Trenchless Drain Pipe Repair in Stockton, CA

    Beyond main sewer lines, trenchless techniques can also be applied to repair damaged drain pipes on your property. Whether it is a cracked kitchen drain or a clogged laundry line, we can often reline or burst smaller diameter pipes without tearing up your walls or floors in Stockton, CA. This preserves your home's interior and reduces renovation costs.

    Sectional Point Repair in Stockton

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are a good fit on otherwise healthy lines.
