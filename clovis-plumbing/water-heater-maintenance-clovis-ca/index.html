    Anode Rod Replacement in Clovis

    Some water eats anode rods fast. Water chemistry can wear a rod out in a few years, so we check it every time. We swap worn ones. A healthy rod keeps the tank from rusting through.

    Tankless Water Heater Service in Clovis, CA

    Tankless units need descaling. Mineral scale coats the inside of the heat exchanger, restricting flow and making the unit work harder, and eventually it starts throwing error codes or cutting out in the middle of a shower. We flush it with descaler. We also check the igniter, the fan and the exhaust vent for safe operation.

    Attic, Closet and Garage Units in Clovis

    Location changes the risks. Attic units need a working drain pan and drain line so a leak does not come through the ceiling, and garage gas units often need to be raised off the floor or protected from vehicles under local code. We check the pan and drain. We check venting and access.

    Water Heater Problem Diagnosis in Clovis, CA

    Something sounds or feels wrong. If the tank is rumbling, the hot water ran out early or there is water in the pan, we can come out, find the cause and tell you whether it is a simple repair or a sign the tank is near the end. We lay out choices. No pressure either way.

    Yearly Service Plans in Clovis, CA

    Forgetting is the main reason tanks go unflushed. A yearly plan puts the visit on the calendar, sends a reminder a week ahead, and gives you priority scheduling and repair discounts if the heater ever acts up between visits. No remembering required. Long-term savings add up.

    Descaling Tankless Units in Clovis

    Error codes often mean scale. Scale in the exchanger cuts flow and trips the unit's safety shutoff. A pump kit handles it. Yearly descaling is standard advice for hard water.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Clovis

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment piles up around the lower element, it runs hotter than it should, and eventually it burns out, which is why you suddenly get only a few minutes of hot water. We test both elements. With power off, we check thermostats and connections.

    Relief Valve and Safety Testing in Clovis, CA

    The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense. We test the valve, confirm it reseats and check that the discharge pipe is routed correctly. We swap bad ones. We check the expansion tank too.
