    A submersible pump hangs on drop pipe inside a 4-inch or 6-inch casing, often 100 to 400 feet down, so pulling one is a two-person job with a pump hoist.     Jet Pump Replacement in Clovis, CA

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet.     Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Clovis

    Most well pump replacements in Clovis, CA land between $1,500 and $5,000. Depth and horsepower drive most of the price, along with any new tank or wire the job needs. The estimate is written before work starts.

    If the tank is old or waterlogged, replace it with the pump. A failed tank causes rapid short cycling, which is one of the most common reasons submersible motors burn out early.
