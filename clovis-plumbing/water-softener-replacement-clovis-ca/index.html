    Picking the Right Softener for Clovis, CA Water

    Metered Softener Replacement in Clovis

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. Metered valves like the Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 count gallons and regenerate only when the resin is close to spent. Salt bills usually drop in the first month.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Clovis, CA

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. Rebedding with new resin can be cheaper than a new system. It depends on the valve. New resin under a tired valve rarely lasts.

    Long-Term Softener Costs in Clovis, CA

    Long-term cost on a softener is mostly salt and water. A metered valve with an efficient brine setting can cut salt use by a third or more compared with an old timer unit, and that adds up over fifteen years. We compare brands on that basis.

    Residential Water Softener Replacement in Clovis

    Scale in the water heater is the expensive part of hard water. A tank water heater on hard water can lose a noticeable share of its efficiency as scale builds on the bottom, and tankless units scale up even faster. A working softener stops that.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Clovis

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. Old clock-timer units come out and metered valves go in. Salt use usually drops. We set the brine refill to your hardness so the system is not dumping salt down the drain every few days.

    Clack Water Softener Replacement in Clovis

    Clack WS1 valves are easy to program and easy to rebuild, which is why a lot of water treatment shops have switched to them. We can replace just the valve on a good tank. Or the whole system.

    Salt-Free Alternatives in Clovis, CA

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. A TAC conditioner is the honest alternative there, and we tell people plainly that it prevents scale but will not give them the slick feel of soft water. Low-sodium diets are another reason.
