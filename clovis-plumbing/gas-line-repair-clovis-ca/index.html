    Gas Leak Detection in Clovis

    A rotten egg smell, hissing near a line, dead grass in a stripe across the yard or a gas meter that moves with everything off are the common leak signs. Trust your nose. We isolate sections of the system and use electronic combustible gas detectors to narrow the source, then confirm with soap solution at fittings, valves and unions. Once we find it, you get a written repair price before work starts.

    Gas Appliance Connector Repair in Clovis, CA

    Weak flames or a smell near one appliance often point to the connector or shutoff behind it. Older uncoated brass connectors can crack at the flare nut, and they are no longer accepted by most codes or utilities. New coated connectors go in. Each appliance also gets a shutoff you can reach and a sediment trap where required, and we check that the connector length and diameter match the appliance rating.

    Gas Line Extensions and Relocations in Clovis

    Adding a gas fireplace, moving a laundry room or switching a range from electric to gas usually means extending the gas line. Sizing comes first. We add up the BTU load of every appliance on the system, measure the longest run and pick a pipe size that keeps pressure up at the farthest appliance when everything runs together. Then we route it, tie it in and test it.

    CSST Repair and Bonding in Clovis, CA

    Newer homes often have corrugated stainless steel tubing running through the attic. CSST can be damaged by nails, rodents or lightning, and older yellow-jacket CSST needs proper bonding to the electrical grounding system to reduce the risk of a lightning arc. Bonding is a short job. We repair damaged sections with manufacturer-approved fittings and verify bonding before we leave. Drought years alternate with very wet years here, so conservation rules and low-flow fixtures are a constant part of plumbing decisions.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Clovis, CA

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. We repair buried and above-ground propane lines, replace leaking regulators and fittings and check that each appliance has been converted with the correct orifices for propane. Wrong orifices make a burner run dirty and unsafe. We test the system the same way we test natural gas piping.

    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Clovis, CA

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Years of vibration from a furnace or a house settling can loosen threaded fittings, and pipe in a damp garage or an exterior wall can rust from the outside in. Bad sections get replaced with new pipe. Outdoor runs get coated or painted after the repair so rust does not start again.
