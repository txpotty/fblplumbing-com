    Leaking Toilet Repair in Clovis

    Toilet leaks can cause serious water damage to your bathroom floor, subfloor, and even the ceiling below. Leaks can start from the base of the toilet, the tank, or the supply line.     Fill Valve Replacement in Clovis

    A noisy, slow or hissing tank often points to a worn fill valve. Mineral scale clogs the small ports inside, which causes long refill times and phantom refills in the night.     Toilet Flush Mechanism Repair in Clovis, CA

    Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Clovis, CA
