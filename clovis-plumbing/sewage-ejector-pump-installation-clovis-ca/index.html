    Why Choose FBL Plumbing for Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Clovis, CA?

    Ejector pumps move raw waste, so a sloppy install shows up as odors, backups and expensive cleanup. Lids get gasketed at each penetration, the basin vents through the roof, and a check valve keeps the discharge from draining back after each cycle. Sewer gas stays out.

    • Alarm Protection, An alarm on the basin sounds before waste reaches the floor.
    • Solids Rated, Two-inch solids handling on sewage applications.
    • Proven Brands, We install Zoeller and Liberty that have years of field history.

    Citywide Coverage

    From older homes with toilets below grade to new custom builds with lower-level suites, we install ejectors all over Clovis. Older systems often have an open or poorly sealed pit that lets sewer gas into the basement, and replacing the lid with a gasketed, vented one is often the first thing we recommend. Smells mean leaks.

    Experienced Team

    Our team follows the pump curves, not a rule of thumb. Lift, run length, fittings and discharge size all change a pump's output, and a pump chosen without that math tends to short-cycle or strain. We do the numbers.

    Safety-First Approach

    Safety and code compliance start with the vent on every ejector install we do in Clovis. Because sewage basins produce methane and hydrogen sulfide, we gasket every penetration, vent the basin to the roof and tie the vent in where code permits. Odors stay in the pipe.

    Ejector Pump Replacement in Clovis

    Ejector pumps usually last 7 to 10 years, depending on how often they cycle and what gets flushed. Watch for constant running, a hum or grind, repeated alarms and odor at the pit, since any of them means trouble is close. The float usually goes first.

    Zoeller Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Clovis

    Zoeller's sewage line covers home and heavier-duty use, in cast iron with tethered or vertical float switches. The model comes from the pump curve for your lift, and most basement baths need a four-tenths or half horsepower unit that passes two-inch solids. Tall lifts need bigger pumps.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin Installation in Clovis, CA

    The basin is where the system lives, and a 30-gallon size is common for a single basement bathroom. Bigger basins let the pump run longer, fewer times a day, which is easier on the motor and switch, so we size up when the pit serves a laundry or several fixtures. Every lid gets sealed.

    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Clovis

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so every fixture drains to a sealed pit instead of flowing by gravity. We cut the slab, trench the drain lines to the basin, set it, run a two-inch vent up through the house to the roof and pipe the discharge to the building drain with a check valve and shutoff. Then we patch the concrete.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin & Pit Installation in Clovis

    Digging the pit starts with a saw cut through the floor, deep enough to set the basin flush with the finished floor. Many older homes here sit on raised foundations with crawlspaces, which make supply and drain repairs easier to reach than slab work. The basin sits on gravel with backfill around it so rising groundwater cannot float it, and new concrete seals the floor and locks it down. Floating basins crack floors.

    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Clovis, CA

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes often need duplex systems with an alternating control panel. The panel switches the lead pump each cycle so wear is shared, and if the lead pump fails or the flow is heavy, the second pump starts automatically and the panel trips an alarm so someone knows to call for service. Two pumps, one alarm.
