    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Clovis, CA

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. They are simple to program, easy to rebuild and parts are stocked almost everywhere, which matters when a seal fails ten years in. Settings match your test. We confirm the setup before leaving.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Clovis

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. Every whole-house install gets a bypass valve, and we add a pressure reducing valve if incoming pressure is too high. Every tap gets soft water. We can skip the hose bibs.

    Softener Loop Installation in Clovis

    No loop? A softener loop is a pair of capped stubs on the main line, usually in the garage, that lets a softener be connected without cutting pipe later. We add the loop. Outdoor taps can stay on hard water, which saves salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Clovis

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. We install salt-based softeners with a resin tank sized to your grains per gallon, a brine tank, a bypass valve and a drain line that ends at a code-approved air gap. Programming is the last step. We set regeneration overnight.

    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Clovis, CA

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Modern units meter water use, send salt reminders by app and come in larger capacities for growing families. The savings add up. We swap it out in a few hours.

    Water Softener Installation for New Construction in Clovis, CA

    Building a home in Clovis, CA? Adding a loop during framing is easy and cheap, since the plumber can route the main through the right spot before drywall goes up. Builders call us in early. We keep outdoor taps off the softener.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Clovis

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. Compared with an older timer-based unit, a modern metered softener can use roughly half the salt and noticeably less regeneration water each month. That pays back over time. We install several models.

    Pelican Water Softener Installation in Clovis, CA

    Pelican sells both salt-based softeners and salt-free conditioners, often direct to homeowners. If you bought a Pelican system for your home, we install it with secure connections, a proper bypass and the startup steps the manual lists for flushing the media. Startup matters. We follow it step by step.
