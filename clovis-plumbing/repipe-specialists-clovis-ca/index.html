    What Our Repipe Crews Do in Clovis, CA

    Drywall Patching and Restoration in Clovis, CA

    Repiping needs small openings in walls and ceilings to reach fixtures and pull pipe. Our crew cuts neat rectangles, covers nearby surfaces and patches each opening after the pressure test. Textures get matched. We match knockdown, orange peel or smooth walls, so the patches are ready to prime and paint.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Clovis, CA

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Because PEX flexes, it absorbs some of the shock that causes water hammer, and long runs with few fittings mean fewer places for a leak to start inside a wall. We keep PEX out of sunlight and away from water heater flues.

    PEX Repiping in Clovis, CA

    PEX is flexible plastic tubing that can run long distances without a fitting, which means fewer joints hidden in your walls. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. PEX shrugs off scale and chlorine, survives freezing better than copper and installs faster, so it is our most common repipe material. Our PEX-A installs use expansion fittings that do not shrink the flow path. Flow stays strong at the last shower.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Clovis

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Many homes built from the 1980s through the early 2000s have one or the other. We can usually identify it from the pipe at the water heater, and full replacement makes the house easier to insure and sell.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Clovis

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab, which costs far less than a repipe and avoids breaking the floor. It suits a first-time leak. After multiple leaks, we will show you why a full repipe may cost less over the next ten years.

    Repiping for Hard Water in Clovis, CA

    Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe, and over years it narrows the bore and speeds up corrosion in metal lines. Pressure suffers. In hard water homes, PEX-A is often the better pick since it resists scale, and a softener loop or filter can be added during the job. Planning it now saves a second visit later.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Clovis

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. That is often the first sign. Replacing galvanized with PEX or copper ends the rust, brings pressure back and stops the leaks that start at the threaded joints where galvanized is thinnest. Old pipe stays in walls when pulling it would wreck finished surfaces.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Clovis

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water and long attic runs. Our crews use Uponor or similar PEX-A, where the pipe end gets expanded and shrinks back over the fitting for a full-diameter connection. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. Two or three days covers most houses.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Repipe Specialists in Clovis

    Most PEX repipes for Northern California homes cost $5,000 to $12,000, depending on size, bathrooms and access. Copper repipes usually run 20 to 40 percent more.

    Both work well, and the best choice depends on your house and your water. PEX is cheaper. PEX bends, resists scale and handles freezes better, and copper is rigid, UV-tolerant and proven but pricier and vulnerable to aggressive water.

    Fixing one leak at a time works until the leaks start coming every few months. Then the math changes. Each repair means a service call, opened drywall and sometimes an insurance claim, while a repipe replaces all of the aging pipe at once and ends the cycle for decades.

    We have to open some drywall, but the openings are small and planned. Openings are usually small. Each cut is a clean square at a fixture or along the route, and we patch and texture all of them so the walls are ready for paint.

    Having the repipe documented with a permit can make your home easier to sell and insure, especially when replacing polybutylene or Kitec. Buyers and inspectors notice it.

    We provide a written workmanship warranty, and PEX makers such as Uponor offer long-term warranties when certified installers use their system. You get the details before we start.

    For copper, we use domestic Type L from mills such as Mueller and Cerro. For PEX, we use Uponor PEX-A most often. Viega and Zurn PEX show up on some jobs, but we keep one brand throughout each house so the warranty holds.

    Most repipes in Clovis, CA take three to five working days for an average house. Big houses take more time. Copper takes a little longer than PEX because every turn needs a fitting, and the drywall patching usually adds a final day after the plumbing passes inspection.
