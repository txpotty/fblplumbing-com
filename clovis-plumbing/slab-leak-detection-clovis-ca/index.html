    Pressure Testing and Isolation in Clovis

    Pressure isolation is always the first step. Our tech closes the main, caps or valves off the hot and cold sides, and puts a gauge on each, then watches which side loses pressure over a set time. That tells us where to look. It also rules out a toilet or faucet leak masquerading as a slab leak in your Clovis, CA home.

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Clovis

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. Our ground microphones hear it. We move the pickup across the floor in a grid over the traced pipe route, finding where the sound is loudest and confirming it from another position, which works best on copper and less well on PEX.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Clovis, CA

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. They are harder to see. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, which is why we isolate the cold line, trace its route and listen along it with acoustic gear.

    Camera Inspection for Under-Slab Drains in Clovis, CA

    Under-slab drains crack as well. A broken cast iron or PVC drain will not move the water meter, but it can cause odors, soggy floors and slow drains, which is why we push a camera through to look for cracks, bellies and pulled joints. You see the footage yourself before any repair talk starts in your Clovis home.

    Non-Invasive Slab Leak Locating in Clovis, CA

    The floor gets opened last. Pressure tests, line tracing, thermal scans and acoustic listening all happen from the surface in your Clovis, CA home, without drilling or cutting anything. Only after two methods agree do we mark the spot and talk about repair options, including ones that skip the concrete entirely.

    Thermal Scans on Large Floors in Clovis, CA

    Big open floor plans hide leaks well. A thermal camera scans an entire living area in a few minutes, showing temperature differences of a degree or less across tile or concrete, so we can rule out half the house before setting up the microphone.     Supply and Drain Leaks Under the Slab in Clovis, CA

    A supply leak and a drain leak show different signs. Supply leaks are pressurized, so they move the meter, warm the floor on the hot side and run whether or not anyone uses water. Drain leaks only leak when water goes down, and they show up as odors, slow drains or damp floors near bathrooms. Each needs its own test in your Clovis, CA home.
