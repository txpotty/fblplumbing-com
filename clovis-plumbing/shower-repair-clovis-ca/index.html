    Shower Valve Replacement in Clovis

    If you are experiencing inconsistent water temperature, low water pressure, or difficulty turning your shower on or off, the shower valve may be failing. A faulty mixing valve can lead to scalding or freezing water, which is a safety hazard. We can replace old or broken shower valves with new, dependable models, including pressure-balancing and thermostatic options for improved safety and comfort. This often involves accessing the valve behind the shower wall.

    Hot Water Pressure Issues in Clovis, CA

    Lack of hot water or weak hot water pressure in your shower can be incredibly frustrating. This can stem from several issues, including a failing water heater, sediment buildup in the pipes, or a problem with the shower mixing valve itself. Our technicians will diagnose whether the issue is with your water heater, a blocked pipe, or a faulty component within the shower.     Showerhead and Arm Repair in Clovis

    Leaking Shower Valve Repair in Clovis

    A shower that drips after it is turned off usually has a worn cartridge or seals. Hard water scale scratches cartridge surfaces and breaks down O-rings faster than soft water does. We replace the cartridge with a genuine part and lubricate the valve with silicone grease. This stops the drip and makes the handle turn easily again.
