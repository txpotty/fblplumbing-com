    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Clovis

    The cost of drain repairs in Clovis, CA can vary a lot, typically ranging from $150 for a simple clog removal to over $3,000 for complex main sewer line repairs.     To prevent future drain problems, avoid pouring grease down sinks, use drain screens to catch hair and food particles and be mindful of what goes down your toilets. Regular professional drain cleaning every one to two years can also help head off clogs and extend the life of your plumbing system.

    Drain lines can break or collapse due to several factors, including shifting soil, tree root intrusion, corrosion from age, extreme temperature changes or heavy loads passing over underground pipes. Poor installation or defective materials can also contribute to early failure.

    Most trap and fixture drain repairs take one to two hours. Replacing drain pipe inside a wall or under a floor can take half a day to a full day. We give you a time estimate when we quote the job.

    The length of drain repairs depends on the complexity of the problem. Simple clog removals might take an hour or two, while more extensive repairs like replacing a section of a main sewer line could take a full day or more. We'll give you an estimated timeline during our initial assessment.

    Minor clogs can sometimes be resolved with plungers or enzymatic drain cleaners. However, for stubborn blockages, recurring issues or suspected pipe damage, it is best to call a professional. DIY attempts with harsh chemical cleaners can sometimes damage pipes or mask a larger problem.

    You should call a professional if your DIY efforts with a plunger or basic snake haven't cleared the clog, or if you have recurring clogs, foul odors or multiple slow drains. These issues usually point to a deeper problem that requires specialized tools and expertise to resolve effectively.
