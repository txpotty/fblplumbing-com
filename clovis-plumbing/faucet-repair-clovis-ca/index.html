    Common Faucet Repairs in Clovis, CA

    Compression Faucet Repair in Clovis

    Two-handle faucets with separate hot and cold handles are often compression faucets, which seal by pressing a rubber washer down onto a brass seat inside the valve. Over time the washer hardens and the seat wears rough. New washers go in and the seat gets dressed or replaced with a seat wrench, and the packing gets redone if the stem leaks.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Clovis, CA

    Ball faucets have a single lever over a domed cap, and the drip usually comes from the two small rubber seats and springs that press against the ball. Most of them just need new seats and springs. Loose handles happen too. If the handle leaks, snugging the adjusting ring under the cap often fixes it.

    Emergency Faucet Repair in Clovis

    A faucet that will not shut off, or one with a cracked body spraying under the sink, needs the water off before anything else happens. Turn the two small valves under the sink clockwise. If they will not turn, use the main shutoff for the house. We come fast for these.

    Bathroom Faucet Repair in Clovis, CA

    Bathroom sinks see less wear than kitchen sinks, but scale still builds up in cartridges, and pop-up drains break at the pivot rod under the sink. Both are quick fixes. We match cartridges for widespread, centerset and single-hole faucets, and pop-up assemblies are replaced to fit the finish.

    Low Water Pressure Faucet Fix in Clovis, CA

    Weak flow at one faucet usually points to a clogged aerator or a cartridge packed with scale, not a problem with the house pressure. Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running mineral-heavy. The aerator comes off with a key and gets cleaned or replaced, then check the cartridge screens. Low flow everywhere is a different problem.

    Delta and Moen Faucet Repair in Clovis, CA

    Delta and Moen are the faucets we repair most, and both back their cartridges with lifetime warranties for the original owner on many models. We can check whether yours qualifies. Kohler, Pfister and American Standard each use proprietary cartridges, and we stock the common ones for each brand.

    Tub Spout and Diverter Repair in Clovis, CA

    Water still running from the tub spout while the shower is on means the diverter is worn, either the gate inside the spout or a diverter stem in the valve. There are slip-on and threaded spouts. The pipe stub gets measured so we bring the right spout, and the fix gives you full pressure at the showerhead again.

    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Clovis, CA

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. A diverter costs very little. Touchless faucets add a solenoid and a battery pack to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.
