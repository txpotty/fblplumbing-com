    Backflow Testing and Certification in Clovis

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage. Our tester opens each test cock in order, records the check valve and relief valve readings on a calibrated gauge and compares them against the pass limits printed on the test form.     Commercial Leak Detection in Clovis, CA

    Water bills that jump 30 percent with no change in business usually mean a hidden leak or a stuck fixture. Start with the meter. We shut fixtures off zone by zone and watch the meter, then use acoustic sensors, thermal cameras and tracer gas to find leaks in walls, ceilings and buried service lines. Sometimes the culprit is just a running toilet. A single stuck flush valve can burn through thousands of gallons in a week.

    Backflow Prevention Assembly Service in Clovis

    A backflow assembly keeps water from your building, including irrigation lines, boiler water and chemical feeds, from flowing back into the public supply. Most providers want a yearly test. Our certified testers check reduced pressure zone assemblies, double check valves and pressure vacuum breakers with calibrated differential gauges, then file the report with your water provider. Failed devices usually need a rebuild kit, not a new valve. We carry Watts, Wilkins and Febco kits for that reason.

    Commercial Restroom Fixture Repair in Clovis, CA

    Restroom calls are the most common commercial work we do. Wall-hung toilets that keep clogging or rocking get a carrier check, since a loose bowl can leak inside the wall. Weak flow at one sink often comes down to a plugged stop valve.
