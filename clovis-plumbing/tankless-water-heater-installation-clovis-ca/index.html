    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Clovis, CA

    Calcium scale is the most common reason tankless heaters fail. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. Once scale coats the heat exchanger, efficiency drops and error codes start, and over time it can crack the exchanger and void the warranty. A yearly flush prevents it. Service valves make it easy.
