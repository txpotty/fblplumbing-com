    Burst Supply Line Repair in Clovis

    Faucet Base Leak Repair in Clovis

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. It usually means worn O-rings on a swivel spout or a failed base gasket, and on some faucets it means the body has cracked and the whole faucet needs replacing. Every faucet type rebuilds differently. We identify yours before ordering parts.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Clovis

    A faucet that drips once a second wastes thousands of gallons a year. Older compression faucets need a washer and seat, and newer cartridge or disc faucets need the cartridge itself or its seals. Cartridges for Moen, Delta, Pfister and Kohler are on the truck. Odd brands take a day to order.

    Loose or Wobbly Sink Repair in Clovis

    A sink that moves when you lean on it is pulling on the drain. Undermount sinks hang on clips glued under the counter, and pedestal sinks hang on a bracket that can pull loose from drywall. The clips get reset and the rim resealed. Pedestals get blocking behind the wall when needed.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Clovis, CA

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Most codes want 2 to 4 inches of trap seal, and a leaking or cut-down trap can lose it and let sewer odor into the house. We replace bad traps with a matched kit. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.

    Kitchen Sink Repair in Clovis

    Kitchen sinks take the most abuse in the house. A cracked composite or porcelain bowl is a replacement, and we will say so.
