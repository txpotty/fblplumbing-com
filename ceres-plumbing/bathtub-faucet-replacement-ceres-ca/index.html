    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Ceres, CA

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. When someone flushes a toilet, the valve cuts hot and cold flow in proportion so the mix stays steady, and a thermostatic valve goes further by holding a set temperature even as the water heater cools. Either one satisfies scald rules. Thermostatic costs more.

    Tub Faucets in Older Homes in Ceres

    Older homes hide odd tub plumbing. We find galvanized supplies that rust shut, valves soldered to copper with no stops anywhere, and spouts threaded onto steel nipples that snap when you try to turn them. Adapters for old pipe ride along. Replacing a short run of galvanized is common.

    Diverter Valve Repair and Replacement in Ceres, CA

    Water running from the spout during a shower means a failing diverter. On a single-handle setup the diverter is usually the lift gate in the spout itself, and on a three-handle faucet it is the middle stem, and both wear out until water splits between the spout and the head. A new diverter spout takes minutes. Middle stems need a seat too.

    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Ceres

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. Single-handle drips mean worn cartridge seals, and multi-handle drips mean worn washers and seats. Replacement parts stop it. Cracked bodies get a new valve.

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Ceres

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. A worn 1222 causes drips or weak hot water, and replacing it takes about an hour with a puller. Older Moentrol valves take another cartridge. We carry both.

    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Ceres, CA

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We replace Moen and Delta cartridges or old compression stems and seats, and we grease the O-rings with silicone plumber's grease so the handle turns smoothly for years. Old seats need a seat wrench. About an hour, usually.

    Tub Faucet Style Upgrades in Ceres

    Wall-mount tub fillers are a popular upgrade. Swapping a standard spout for a long-reach filler or a handheld shower on a slide bar changes how the tub works, and both usually need a new valve or a diverter added inside the wall. Rough-in gets planned before trim. The trim goes on last.

    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Ceres

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler. The supply lines come up through the floor under the filler, which means cutting the slab or working from a crawlspace or basement, and the filler needs a mounting bracket anchored to the subfloor. Deck-mount fillers sit on the rim. Tub-only fillers need a temperature limit.
