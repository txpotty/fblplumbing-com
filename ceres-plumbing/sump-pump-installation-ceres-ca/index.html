    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Ceres, CA

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. A battery backup pump sits beside the primary and runs on its own float. Most run five to seven hours on a full charge. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Ceres, CA

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. We pull the old pump and inspect the pit. Undersized pumps get upsized. We fill the pit and watch it run.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Ceres

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi models send a text too. Landlords like them. They can go on a new pump or one that is already working.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Ceres, CA

    Price depends mostly on whether a pit already exists. A swap in an existing pit is the low end. Battery backups and alarms add to the total. We list each option on a written quote.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Ceres, CA

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. They are louder than submersibles. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Ceres

    No pit yet? We saw-cut the slab at the low corner, break out the concrete and dig down through soil, hardpan or rock to set an 18 by 22 inch basin. Washed gravel goes around the basin so water can reach it. The slab gets patched with new concrete. The pump, lid and discharge go in last.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Ceres

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. Sizing comes from two measurements we take on site, the pit diameter and the vertical lift to the discharge. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. A 1-1/2 inch discharge line handles the flow from either size without straining the motor.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Ceres, CA

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary handles daily water. Finished basements are the usual reason. They are worth it anywhere a few inches of water would mean new drywall.
