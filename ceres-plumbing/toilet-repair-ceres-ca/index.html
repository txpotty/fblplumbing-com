    Running Toilet Repair in Ceres

    A tank that keeps hissing and refilling every few minutes is almost always losing water past the flapper or overfilling into the overflow tube. It adds up fast.     Wall-Hung Toilet Repair in Ceres

    Restaurant restrooms get the hardest use we see, and wall-hung bowls there can loosen on their carriers until the seal leaks behind the wall.     Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Ceres, CA

    Needing two flushes on a regular basis usually comes down to scale in the rim jets, a clogged siphon jet, a partial blockage in the trapway, or a flush valve that does not open all the way. A few first-generation 1.6 gallon toilets never flushed well, and we will tell you plainly if yours is one of them.

    Leaky Toilet Repair in Ceres

    Food coloring in the tank is the cheapest test we run.
