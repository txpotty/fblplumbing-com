    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Installation in Ceres

    The cost of grease trap installation in Ceres, CA varies widely based on the size, type, and complexity of the system. Small under-sink units might range from $1,000 to $3,000, while large outdoor interceptors requiring excavation can cost anywhere from $5,000 to $20,000 or more. We provide clear, upfront pricing after a thorough on-site assessment.

    The main purpose of a grease trap is to intercept fats, oils, and greases (FOG) from commercial kitchen wastewater before they enter the public sewer system. This prevents pipe blockages, protects municipal infrastructure, and helps businesses comply with environmental regulations.

    We install all common types of grease traps, including gravity grease interceptors, hydro-mechanical grease traps (automatic grease removal units), and compact under-sink grease traps. Our team helps you determine the best system for your commercial kitchen's specific needs and local code requirements.

    Indoor traps should be close to the fixtures they serve and easy to open for cleaning. Outdoor interceptors are typically placed where a pump truck can reach them without blocking customer traffic.

    Signs you might need a new grease trap include frequent sewer backups, persistent foul odors coming from drains, slow drainage throughout your kitchen, or if your current unit is visibly corroded or leaking. If your existing trap is old and no longer meets current local codes in Ceres, an upgrade is also a good idea.

    Most food service businesses that cook, wash dishes or prepare food with fats, oils or grease are required to have one. Local pretreatment programs set the specific rules, and we can help you confirm what applies to your site.

    The timeline for grease trap installation depends on the project's scope. A simple under-sink unit might take a few hours. Larger outdoor gravity interceptors, which involve excavation and more extensive plumbing, can take anywhere from one to several days. We always aim to limit disruption to your business operations.

    Grease trap installation is a complex commercial plumbing task that requires specialized knowledge, tools, and adherence to strict local codes. Attempting a DIY installation can lead to costly errors, code violations, and potential health hazards. It is always best to hire licensed and qualified plumbers like FBL Plumbing.
