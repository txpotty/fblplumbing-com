    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Ceres

    A collapsed run or a long belly usually means we dig.     Emergency Sewer Replacement in Ceres, CA

    Sewage coming up a floor drain is an emergency. The first visit gets a camera in the line so we know if it has caved in or only needs clearing to buy time.     Emergency Sewer Collapse Repair in Ceres

    Every drain in the house stops when a lateral collapses. The tech scopes the line, marks the break and lets you know whether the dig can start next day or has to wait on locates. Locates usually take a couple of business days. We can often set up a bypass so one bathroom stays usable.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Replacement in Ceres, CA

    Orangeburg is tar-soaked wood fiber. Many mid-century houses got it, and by now most of it has blistered and flattened enough to snag waste. Clay is tougher but leaks roots. Several bad spots on one run usually make full replacement the better buy.

    After-Hours Sewer Collapse Response in Ceres

    Raw sewage on a basement floor is a health problem.     Fixing a Bellied Sewer by Trench in Ceres, CA

    A belly is a low spot that holds water. Bursting and lining follow the old path, so they keep the belly, and the only way to correct the fall is to dig, regrade the bedding and lay new pipe at a steady slope. Rock adds time.     What Sewer Replacement Costs in Ceres, CA

    Pipe Bursting Under Driveways in Ceres, CA

    Concrete is expensive to replace. Bursting lets us run new HDPE under a driveway, patio or mature tree by pulling it through the path of the old pipe from a launch pit to a receiving pit. The HDPE is butt-fused on site into one long piece. Most bursts finish in a day. Restoration is two small holes instead of a sawcut slab.
