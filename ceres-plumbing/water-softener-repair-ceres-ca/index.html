    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Ceres, CA

    A crust of salt across the brine tank stops regeneration, and hard water shows up at every tap within a day or two. The bridge gets broken up and the tank cleaned out. Cracked brine tanks on older units get replaced. Fill the tank halfway.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Service in Ceres, CA

    The tank looks full but makes no brine. A salt bridge has formed and stopped the salt from dissolving. We break up bridges, clean out mushy salt, inspect the float and brine well and repair the safety valve. In a hot or damp garage, switching to pellets cuts down on bridging.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Ceres, CA

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. Installing a new quality softener usually runs $1,500 to $3,500.     Resin Bed Problems in Ceres

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads and block the exchange. We test the resin, clean it with a resin cleaner or replace it. Fresh resin brings capacity back.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Ceres

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. The usual causes are a dead timer motor, a bad board, a stuck meter or settings wiped by an outage. We test each part. After the repair, the valve is programmed for your household size and hardness so you have soft water by the next cycle.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Ceres

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units regenerate on a schedule no matter how much water you used. After retesting hardness, we reprogram the valve or swap in a metered head that regenerates only when needed. Salt bills drop.
