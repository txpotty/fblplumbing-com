    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Ceres

    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Expansive clay soils are common across Northern California, and seasonal swelling and shrinking can crack slabs and buried sewer laterals. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the compromised pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use advanced detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    While often overlooked, your home's drain lines are just as crucial as your supply lines. Old cast iron or corroded drain pipes can lead to frequent clogs, slow drainage, and even sewage backups. FBL Plumbing provides complete drain line repiping in Ceres, replacing failing waste pipes with new, smooth PVC or ABS lines.     Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Ceres

    Copper piping offers excellent durability, corrosion resistance, and a long lifespan, making it a premium choice for many homeowners in Ceres. FBL Plumbing specializes in PEX pipe replacement in Ceres, CA, offering a system that resists freezing, corrosion, and scale buildup.
