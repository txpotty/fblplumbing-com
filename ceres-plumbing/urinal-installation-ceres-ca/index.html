    Drainage and Venting for Urinals in Ceres, CA

    Urinal drains clog with scale, not paper. Urine leaves a hard mineral buildup inside the trap and the drain line, especially on waterless units and low-flow fixtures that send less water through, so we slope the line properly and add a cleanout where we can. Venting stops gurgle and odor. We tie in within code distance.

    Waterless Urinal Installation in Ceres

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. A cartridge or sealing liquid blocks sewer gas while letting urine through, and it must be replaced on schedule. Your custodial team needs training. We show them how.
