    Pipe Bursting in Petaluma, CA

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. You'll see an entry and exit pit. The head fractures the old pipe as it travels, with new HDPE trailing behind, fused into one length with no joints.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Petaluma

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Petaluma yard.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Petaluma, CA

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. The old pipe gets split. As a winch drags the head through, the old pipe fractures into the soil and new HDPE is pulled into the same path behind it. HDPE does not rust or leak at joints in Petaluma, CA soil.

    Lateral and Spot Lining in Petaluma

    Not every pipe needs lining end to end. A few bad joints need a short fix. When damage is limited to a few joints or a short section, a sectional liner costs less than a full-length one and goes in quickly, but we confirm the rest of the line is sound by camera before recommending it.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Petaluma

    The old pipe gets cleaned first. After that, we insert the liner. We inflate an epoxy-soaked liner against the wall and cure it with heat or UV, leaving a smooth pipe with no joints. Lining works best for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.
