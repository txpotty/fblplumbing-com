    Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in Petaluma, CA

    Roots find sewer lines by following moisture. They need only a tiny gap. Once inside, they grow into a mat that catches paper and grease, and cutting them out with a machine only buys a few months, because the opening is still there. New PVC with glued joints gives them no way in.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Petaluma

    Clay and Orangeburg Pipe Replacement in Petaluma, CA

    Clay pipe came in short sections with a joint every two or three feet. That is a lot of joints for roots to find. Orangeburg is worse, since the tar-paper wall softens and deforms under the weight of the soil, and both are good candidates for bursting because they break apart easily. Fast pulls.
