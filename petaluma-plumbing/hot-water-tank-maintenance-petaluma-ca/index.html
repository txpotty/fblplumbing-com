    Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Petaluma, CA

    Many homes put the water heater in a garage or attic where temperatures can swing widely. Heat speeds up the wear on plastic drain valves, gas valve seals and wiring insulation. We check drain pans, pan drain lines, platform height for gas units in garages and clearances around the tank.     Anode Rod Inspection & Replacement in Petaluma, CA

    The anode rod is your hot water tank's sacrificial defense against corrosion. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. During our maintenance, we pull and inspect the anode rod. If it's significantly depleted, we recommend and perform a replacement to extend the life of your tank in Petaluma, CA, preventing rust from eating away at the tank lining.

    Electric Hot Water Tank Element Check in Petaluma

    Electric hot water tanks rely on heating elements to warm the water. During maintenance, our plumbers inspect these elements for signs of wear, corrosion or burnout. We also check the thermostat settings and wiring connections to ensure they are functioning correctly and safely. This preventive check can identify failing elements before they stop heating altogether, avoiding unexpected cold showers for your family in Petaluma.

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Petaluma, CA

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can clog the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a stable blue flame and lower gas bills.

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Petaluma

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause noisy popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution approved for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Service in Petaluma, CA

    Many modern plumbing systems include an expansion tank connected to the water heater. This tank takes up excess pressure created during the heating cycle, protecting your hot water tank and other plumbing fixtures. Our maintenance includes checking the expansion tank's air pressure and overall condition. Proper expansion tank function is key to preventing premature wear on your hot water tank and protecting system integrity.
