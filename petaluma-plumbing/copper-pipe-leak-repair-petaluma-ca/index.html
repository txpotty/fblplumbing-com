    Preventive Copper Pipe Inspection in Petaluma, CA

    An inspection catches thin spots before they leak. It takes about an hour. We look at exposed copper for green crust and pitting, test static pressure, check the bond wire at the water heater and main, and run a thermal scan on walls where pipe runs if the house has had leaks before. You get a written summary.

    Corrosion Leak Repair in Petaluma, CA

    Water can be hard on copper over time. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. Thinning walls lead to leaks. We look at corrosion patterns on the pipe we remove, because the location and shape of the pits tell us whether water chemistry, velocity or electrical current is doing the damage. Then we fix that too.

    Hot Water Copper Line Repair in Petaluma, CA

    Hot water lines corrode faster than cold lines. Temperature drives it. Recirculation pumps that run around the clock push water through the copper at high velocity and wear it from the inside, which is why we often add a timer to the pump after fixing the leak. It slows the next pinhole.

    Copper Slab Leak Repair in Petaluma

    Many slab homes have copper water lines running under the concrete. Listen for running water. When those lines leak, you may notice warm spots on the floor, the sound of running water or a meter that turns with every fixture off, so we find it electronically first and then quote a slab repair or a reroute. Both get a price.

    Solder Joint Leak Repair in Petaluma, CA

    Older solder joints can fail from poor original work, thermal expansion or vibration. Look for white or green crust. A leaking joint usually weeps for a while before it drips, and we clean, reflux and resolder the joint or replace the fitting with new pipe on each side when the old copper is too thin. The joint gets tested.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in Petaluma

    Pinholes form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. Hot lines get it worst. They often appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher, which we fix by replacing that length with heavier pipe. More pinholes often follow.

    Burst Copper Pipe Repair in Petaluma, CA

    Copper in an exterior wall or attic can split when the water inside freezes. Mountain and foothill homes in Northern California see real winters, so pipes there need insulation, heat tape, or proper draining when a cabin sits empty. Shut the main first. The split often does not leak until the ice thaws, which is why many bursts are found hours after the cold. We cut out the split and solder in new pipe. Then we add insulation.

    Underground Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Petaluma

    Buried copper service lines can leak for months before anyone notices. Bills tell the story. We locate the leak with acoustic gear and tracer gas, and either spot repair it or swap in a new service line with minimal digging. Every repair gets a pressure test.
