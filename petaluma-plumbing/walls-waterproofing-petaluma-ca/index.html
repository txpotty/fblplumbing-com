    Wall Waterproofing Work We Handle in Petaluma, CA

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Petaluma

    Copper became standard in many mid-century homes here, and decades later pinhole leaks and worn fittings are a common repair call. Any wall below grade sits in soil that gets saturated after heavy rain or snowmelt, and that water pushes against the wall with steady hydrostatic pressure. We inject cracks, coat the wall inside or out, and install drain tile where water pools along the footing. A sump goes in if groundwater sits high. Not every basement needs one.

    Commercial Wall Waterproofing in Petaluma

    Restaurant kitchens, gym locker rooms and salon wash stations put water on walls all day. We waterproof commercial showers, mop sink walls and restroom wet walls with membranes rated for constant water exposure, and we repair the supply and drain piping behind them when we find a leak during demo. After-hours scheduling is available.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Petaluma, CA

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and tie it into a sump pit so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. It just stops puddling on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and makes sense when a patio or driveway sits against the foundation.

    Waterproofing Costs vs. Repeat Water Damage in Petaluma, CA

    Paying once beats paying every two years. Waterproofing a shower wall usually lands in the low thousands, while repeat drywall, cabinet and floor repairs add up fast, and mold remediation on top of that can double the bill. Exterior block sealing depends on wall length. Your quote lists each option with its price.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Petaluma

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most dependable way to stop a basement leak, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing so water has somewhere to go. Expansive clay soils are common across Northern California, and seasonal swelling and shrinking can shift slabs and buried sewer laterals. Backfill goes in last.

    Drainage System Integration in Petaluma, CA

    Water has to go somewhere once a wall is sealed. Exterior footing drains, interior drain tile and surface French drains all do the same job at different depths. We match the drain to where the water comes from, which might be a high water table, a yard that slopes toward the house, or a gutter dumping at the corner. Downspouts get extended at least four feet out.

    Cost-Effective Waterproofing Solutions in Petaluma, CA

    Not every wet wall needs a $15,000 excavation. Sometimes the fix is a downspout extension and regrading the soil so it slopes away from the house, a drop of about six inches in the first ten feet, and that costs a few hundred dollars. We start with the cheapest fix that will actually work. Every option comes written out with its price.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Petaluma

    Crack injection takes about two hours per crack. We set injection ports every 8 to 12 inches along the crack, seal the surface with epoxy paste, and pump in resin from the bottom port up until it pushes out the next port. Polyurethane stays flexible if the wall moves a little, and epoxy bonds the two sides back together. The surface paste gets ground off once it cures.
