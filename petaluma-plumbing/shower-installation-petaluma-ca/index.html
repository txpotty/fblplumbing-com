    Emergency Shower Replacement in Petaluma

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Petaluma, CA

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. The tub comes out, the drain shifts to the pan location and the valve moves up to about 48 inches. On a slab, we cut concrete. No more stepping over a tub wall.

    Custom Shower System Installation in Petaluma, CA

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. A standard pressure-balance valve can feed one head, but rain heads, body jets and handhelds need a thermostatic valve with separate volume controls and supply lines sized up to 3/4 inch. Layout comes first. The water heater gets checked for capacity too.

    Digital Shower Systems in Petaluma, CA

    Digital shower systems like U by Moen and Kohler DTV need a little more planning. These valves usually sit in a nearby closet or vanity and need low-voltage wiring to the controller and an outlet close by. The outlet is an electrician's job. We plumb the valve and outlets.

    Shower Pan Installation in Petaluma

    Shower pans fail quietly. A pan with a pinhole in the liner or a poorly clamped drain will hold water for years while it slowly soaks the subfloor, so we flood-test every tiled pan for 24 hours before tile goes down. We level prefab pans carefully. Either way, the slope runs to the drain.

    Walk-In Shower Installation in Petaluma

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. Without a curb to hold water back, the floor has to pitch a quarter inch per foot toward the drain, and the waterproofing has to run well past the shower opening. We set that slope. We add blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Petaluma, CA

    Prefab units are the fastest route to a new shower. We set the base on mortar, connect the drain, install the valve and trim and make sure the walls are fastened so nothing flexes when someone leans on them. Good for rentals and guest baths. Most installs finish in a day.
