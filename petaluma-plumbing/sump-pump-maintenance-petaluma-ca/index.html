    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Petaluma, CA

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil around the slab or foundation.

    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Petaluma, CA

    The float switch is a critical component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to support reliable operation for your home in Petaluma, CA.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Petaluma, CA

    Many Petaluma, CA homeowners rely on battery backup sump pumps for protection during power outages.     Commercial and Elevator Pit Pumps in Petaluma

    Commercial buildings often have sump pumps in elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms. These pumps may need oil-minder controls or alarms to meet code and insurance requirements.
