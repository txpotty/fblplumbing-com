    Tracer Gas Leak Detection in Petaluma

    Plastic pipe muffles leak noise. For those, we drain the line and push in a safe hydrogen and nitrogen tracer gas, which rises through the slab and gets picked up by a sniffer at the floor surface, often through grout joints and hairline cracks. It finds leaks acoustic gear misses in Petaluma homes.

    Slab Leaks in Older Homes in Petaluma, CA

    Older homes in Petaluma, CA often have soft copper or galvanized steel under the slab, and both wear out with time. Copper fails at bends and where it rubs concrete, while galvanized rusts from the inside until it splits. We know those failure points. It lets us test the most likely spots first.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Petaluma

    A lot of newer houses are built on post-tension slabs. These slabs have steel tendons stretched under tens of thousands of pounds of force, and cutting one by mistake is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We scan for tendons with ground-penetrating radar or a rebar locator before opening anything, or recommend a reroute that never touches the concrete.

    Repiping After Repeat Slab Leaks in Petaluma, CA

    A second slab leak is often a sign of more. The rest of the pipe has likely aged the same way, so repiping replaces every supply line with new PEX or copper through the walls and attic, ending the cycle of floor openings and surprise water bills. A full repipe takes about two to four days.

    Supply and Drain Leaks Under the Slab in Petaluma, CA

    Supply leaks and drain leaks act differently. Supply leaks are pressurized, so they move the meter, warm the floor on the hot side and run whether or not anyone uses water. Drain leaks only leak when water goes down, and they show up as odors, slow drains or damp floors near bathrooms. Each needs its own test in your Petaluma, CA home.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Petaluma

    Rerouting leaves the failed pipe in the slab. New pipe runs through the walls or attic instead, so there is no concrete work and the cost is often lower than going through tile. We usually use PEX because it handles heat and resists corrosion. Tile and hardwood floors make this the favorite.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Petaluma, CA

    Hot water leaking under the slab warms the floor above it. A thermal camera shows that warm bloom through tile, vinyl and even thin carpet, often spreading outward from the leak point in a recognizable shape. Cold-side leaks will not show. It shrinks the search area before we listen.

    Why Locating First Saves Money in Petaluma

    Detection usually costs a few hundred dollars. A wrong guess costs more. Breaking up a floor in the wrong place means paying for concrete work, flooring and a second opening, while a precise mark keeps the job to one opening or a reroute that leaves the slab alone in your Petaluma property.
