    Thorough Tankless Water Heater Maintenance Services in Petaluma, CA

    Inlet Filter and Air Intake Cleaning in Petaluma

    Every tankless unit has an inlet water screen that catches grit and scale flakes. Those screens can clog with sand and debris, especially after municipal line work. We pull and clean the screen and inspect the air intake filter, which can load up with dust and dryer lint. Clean filters keep the flow sensor reading correctly and the burner breathing properly.

    Isolation Valve Kit Installation in Petaluma, CA

    Some tankless heaters were installed without service valves, which makes proper flushing impossible. We install a set of isolation valves with purge ports on the hot and cold lines so future maintenance takes minutes. The kit also lets you shut off water to the heater without closing the main. It is a one-time upgrade that pays for itself in easier service.

    Rinnai Tankless Heater Servicing in Petaluma

    Rinnai units are popular for their efficiency and compact design. Our Rinnai-specific maintenance includes cleaning the air intake filter, checking the flame sensor and verifying the operation of the bypass valve. We also inspect the combustion chamber and confirm proper gas combustion. Our technicians are familiar with Rinnai's diagnostic codes and can address any issues early, improving performance for your Petaluma home.

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Petaluma, CA

    Noritz tankless heaters are known for their solid build. Our descaling service for Noritz units focuses on their intricate heat exchangers, which can be prone to scale buildup from mineral-rich water. We use manufacturer-approved descaling agents and follow precise flushing procedures to avoid damage. We also inspect the fan motor and exhaust system for proper airflow, a frequent area for attention in Petaluma.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Petaluma

    The heat exchanger in a tankless heater is a coil of small tubes that water races through while the burner fires. Hard water drops calcium and magnesium on those hot surfaces, narrowing the passages over time. We connect a submersible pump and hoses to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with fresh water to rinse the system thoroughly. Restored flow means more stable temperatures and fewer shutdowns.

    Error Code Diagnostics in Petaluma, CA

    Codes like Rinnai LC, Navien E003 or Noritz 90 tell us a lot about what is going wrong. We read the code history, test flame rods, flow sensors and igniters and check gas pressure at the inlet. Many codes trace back to scale or a dirty filter and clear after maintenance. When a part has failed we explain the repair and the cost before doing anything.

    Recirculation and Combi System Service in Petaluma, CA

    Many tankless units now include built-in recirculation pumps or buffer tanks to deliver faster hot water. These systems move more water through the exchanger and scale up sooner. We flush the recirculation loop, check pump operation and verify crossover or dedicated return line settings. Correct schedules can cut wasted energy while still giving you quick hot water.

    Annual Tankless Water Heater Inspection in Petaluma

    An annual inspection of your tankless water heater in Petaluma is a smart measure against costly repairs. Our complete check includes examining the gas line connections for leaks, testing the pressure relief valve, inspecting the igniter and cleaning the air intake filter. We look for any signs of wear or potential failure, giving you recommendations to keep your system running smoothly all year long, through hot spells and cold snaps alike.
