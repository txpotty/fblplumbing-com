    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Petaluma, CA

    No-water calls often end at the control box, not the pump. Three-wire pumps have a control box with a start capacitor and relay, and those parts fail far more often than the motor itself. We test them first. A $60 part beats a $2,500 pump pull.

    Jet Pump Replacement in Petaluma, CA

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet. Deep-well jets with a two-pipe setup can reach further, but they work harder. The common failure is losing prime. Most of the time a bad foot valve or a suction leak is letting water drain back, and we check those before condemning the pump.

    Well Pump Brands We Install in Petaluma, CA

    Goulds, Grundfos, Franklin Electric and Sta-Rite cover nearly every well pump we install. Each brand has strengths, and we choose based on your well. Parts for Goulds and Franklin are easy to get, which matters when a control box dies on a weekend.

    Pump and Pressure Tank Matching in Petaluma

    Pump and tank work as a pair. A tank that is too small or has lost its air charge makes the pump start dozens of extra times a day, and every start heats the motor. Every pump job includes a tank check.

    Agricultural and Ranch Well Pumps in Petaluma

    Irrigation and stock wells often run 3 to 10 HP pumps, sometimes on three-phase power with a motor starter and overload protection at the panel. We replace those pumps and the starters that protect them. Timing matters on a ranch.

    Submersible Well Pump Replacement in Petaluma

    A submersible pump hangs on drop pipe inside a 4-inch or 6-inch casing, often 100 to 400 feet down, so pulling one is a two-person job with a pump hoist. Most fail from sand chewing up the impellers or a lightning surge that cooks the windings. Before pulling, we test the windings with a megohmmeter. The reading shows whether the fault is the motor or the wire.

    Jet Pump Seal and Motor Replacement in Petaluma, CA

    Single-stage and two-stage jet pumps wear out at the seal and impeller, and a leaking shaft seal shows up as a puddle under the motor. New pump, primed and tested. Then we watch the gauge through a full cycle.
