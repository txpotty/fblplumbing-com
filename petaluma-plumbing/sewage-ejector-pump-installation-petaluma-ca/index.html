    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Petaluma

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. Never mix the two.

    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Petaluma, CA

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes often need duplex systems with an alternating control panel. The panel switches the lead pump each cycle so wear is shared, and if the lead pump fails or the flow is heavy, the second pump starts automatically and the panel trips an alarm so someone knows to call for service. Redundancy matters here.

    Liberty Pumps Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Petaluma, CA

    Liberty builds simplex and duplex packages that arrive with the basin, pump, lid and fittings matched. Matched packages speed up the install, and Liberty's float design is less likely to snag on the basin wall, a common reason older pumps fail to start. Stuck floats are common.

    New Construction Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Petaluma, CA

    On a new build, we set the basin ahead of the slab pour. That is the cheapest time, since there is no concrete to cut, and we stub the drains, the vent and the discharge to the plans so the rough-in inspection goes smoothly. Builders like the timing.

    Ejector Pump Replacement in Petaluma

    Ejector pumps tend to run 7 to 10 years, depending on how often they cycle and what gets flushed. Watch for constant running, a hum or grind, repeated alarms and odor at the pit, since any of them means trouble is close. Old switches fail first.

    Zoeller Ejector Pump Installation in Petaluma, CA

    Zoeller has made ejector pumps for decades, and its cast iron models are the ones we see still running in older basements. They work well for new systems and for drop-in replacements, since many Zoeller models fit existing pits and discharge piping as is. Parts are easy to find.

    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Petaluma

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so the toilet, shower and sink drain to a sealed basin instead of flowing by gravity. We saw the floor, trench drains to the basin, set it, carry a two-inch vent to the roof and run discharge to the main drain through a check valve and shutoff. The slab gets repoured.

    Cost-Effective Sewage Ejector Pump Upgrades in Petaluma, CA

    Upgrading an old ejector can often keep the existing pit. With a sound basin, a new pump, fresh check valve, shutoff and alarm give you most of a new system for far less. Cracked basins get replaced.
