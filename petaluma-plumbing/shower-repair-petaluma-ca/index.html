    Shower Drain Body Repair in Petaluma

    Drain bodies can crack or work loose under the pan. A loose or cracked drain lets water go under the pan, which shows up as a soft floor beside the shower or a ceiling stain. The drain gets cleared and tested. Loose drains get resealed, and cracked ones get replaced.

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Petaluma

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Grit and scale scratch the sealing surfaces, the O-rings flatten and harden, and eventually the valve cannot fully stop the water when the handle is closed. We swap in a new one. The new cartridge goes in with silicone grease, which keeps the handle smooth.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Petaluma, CA

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. The problem is on the hot side, either sediment in the water heater outlet, a partly closed valve at the heater or a cartridge whose hot port is packed with scale. Each side gets tested. If the sinks have good hot pressure and the shower does not, the cartridge is the first suspect.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Petaluma, CA

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. Because everything is linked, a weak diverter starves the head and a leaky valve drips from the spout and the head alike. We check all of it. The overflow gasket gets a look too, since it is a common hidden leak.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Petaluma, CA

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. That points to the head, the flow restrictor or the cartridge, not the main or the water heater, and it is usually something we can fix within an hour. The head comes off first. Low pressure everywhere sends us to the pressure regulator and main valve instead.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Petaluma, CA

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. Many of these carry a lifetime warranty to the original owner.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Petaluma, CA

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. We replace the spout or stem. It is a short job.
