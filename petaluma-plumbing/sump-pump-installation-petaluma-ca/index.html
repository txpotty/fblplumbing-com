    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Petaluma

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. Sizing comes from two measurements we take on site, the pit diameter and the vertical lift to the discharge. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. A 1-1/2 inch discharge line handles the flow from either size without straining the motor.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Petaluma, CA

    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options. We install their 250 and 280 series often. Replacement switches are easy to get. We drill a 3/16 inch weep hole in the discharge to stop air lock.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Petaluma

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. For those areas we set an exterior basin with a submersible pump and run the discharge to a safe point, often a pop-up emitter in the yard. Lots that slope toward the house need this most. We keep the outlet away from your foundation and the neighbor line.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Petaluma, CA

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. Expect more motor noise than a submersible. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Petaluma, CA

    Price depends mostly on whether a pit already exists. A swap in an existing pit is the low end. Battery backups and alarms add to the total. Your written quote shows each option and its price.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Petaluma

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi models send a text too. Rentals need these. We add them to new installs and to pumps already in place.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Petaluma

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. Because everything comes assembled on a single riser, they fit into an 18-inch pit that would be too tight for two separate pumps and two check valves. That saves some labor. We set them in homes that want two layers without a bigger pit.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Petaluma, CA

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. We set a basin at the lowest point, run perforated drain pipe to it where needed and seal the vapor barrier around the lid so the space stays dry. Access is often tight. The discharge exits through the rim joist and drains away from the house.
