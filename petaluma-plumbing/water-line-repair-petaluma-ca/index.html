    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Petaluma

    If your service line runs under a driveway, pool deck or mature landscaping, trenchless replacement can save a lot of restoration cost. We dig small pits at each end and pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through using a pneumatic boring tool or pipe pulling equipment. The process usually takes one day. You keep your pavers and trees, and the new line carries a lengthy manufacturer warranty.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Petaluma, CA

    A single leak on a newer copper or PEX line is usually a simple spot repair. Repeat leaks, galvanized pipe or a line older than about 40 years usually justify full replacement. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars, while a full replacement costs more but resets the clock for decades. We show you the condition of the pipe we expose so you can make the call with real information.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Petaluma, CA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant option increasingly used in homes across Petaluma, CA. While PEX is less prone to leaks than older materials, damage can still occur from improper installation, rodent chewing, or impact. Our team is fully experienced in PEX repair, using specialized crimping tools and fittings to make strong, leak-proof connections. We make sure your PEX system continues to perform well.

    Underground Leak Detection in Petaluma, CA

    Many water line leaks occur underground, making them difficult to detect without specialized equipment. Our technicians use advanced acoustic and thermal imaging tools to accurately locate hidden leaks without unnecessary digging. Early detection saves you money on water bills and prevents expensive damage to your foundation. Many older homes here sit on raised foundations with crawlspaces, which make supply and drain repairs easier to reach than slab work.     Main Water Line Leak Repair in Petaluma

    Most main line leaks show up as a soggy strip of yard, a sudden jump in your water bill or a meter dial that moves with every fixture off. We confirm the leak by isolating the house shutoff and watching the meter. Then we use ground microphones to find the exact spot. A single section is cut out and replaced with repair couplings or a new length of pipe, and the line is pressure tested before backfill.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Petaluma

We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins.
