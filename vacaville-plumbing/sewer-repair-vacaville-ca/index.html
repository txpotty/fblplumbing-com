    Sewer Camera Inspection in Vacaville

    Every sewer repair starts with a look inside the pipe. We run a self-leveling camera from your cleanout or a pulled toilet and record the full length of the line.     Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Vacaville, CA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after heavy rain. Expansive clay soils are common across Northern California, and seasonal swelling and shrinking can shift slabs and buried sewer laterals. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is usually a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.
