    Sewer Pipe Relining in Vacaville, CA

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line with a jetter first. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, so every crack and leaking joint is covered by one jointless pipe. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. For most houses the flow difference is negligible.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Vacaville

    Clay and cast iron crack from settling soil, tree roots and vehicles parked over the line. Dirt falls in through the crack. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded stainless couplings rated for the transition between materials, then test the repair with water and video before closing the hole. Figure on a single day for a typical spot repair.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Vacaville

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. We record the whole run. Older homes in Vacaville often hide two or three problems in one line, and the video catches all of them.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection and Repair in Vacaville, CA

    Sewer smell outside the house points to a leak. A lush stripe over the line is another clue.     Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Vacaville

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. A cable only punches a hole. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and it is the standard prep step before any pipe lining job. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We camera first to make sure the pipe can take the pressure.
