    Whole-Home PEX Repiping in Vacaville

    Low pressure at every faucet, not just one, usually points at the pipe and not the fixtures.     PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Vacaville, CA

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A bends the easiest and uses expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and uses crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C handles more like B. All three are code-listed for drinking water. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.

    Leak Repair with PEX in Vacaville, CA

    A single bad section is not always a reason to repipe. We can cut out a split or corroded stretch of copper, transition to PEX with a listed fitting, and have the water back on in an hour or two. That works for one leak. Once you are on the third patch, we will tell you straight whether the rest of the copper is heading the same way.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in Vacaville, CA

    Price on a repipe comes down to a few things: bathroom count, square footage, how many stories, and how easy the attic and walls are to work in. We price PEX-A, PEX-B and copper side by side when you ask.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Vacaville

    PEX-B is stiffer, costs less per foot, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. Since a crimp is only as good as the tool that made it, every ring gets a go/no-go gauge check, and any ring that fails gets cut off and redone. That takes seconds. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Vacaville

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool plus a ring slipped over the pipe end. The tech expands the pipe and ring together, pushes the fitting in, and the plastic shrinks back around the barb over the next few seconds, tighter than when it started. There is no crimp gauge to check. The fittings have a wide bore, so flow loss through each joint stays low.
