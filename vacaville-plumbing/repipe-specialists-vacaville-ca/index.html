    Copper Repiping in Vacaville, CA

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes. Joints are soldered with lead-free solder or pressed with ProPress fittings. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a bit longer than PEX.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Vacaville, CA

    Polybutylene pipes, installed in many homes between the 1970s and 1990s, are notorious for premature failure due to chemical reactions with disinfectants in the water supply. If your home in Vacaville, CA has polybutylene, a full replacement is essential to prevent catastrophic water damage. FBL Plumbing offers specialized polybutylene plumbing replacement, carefully removing the old, brittle pipes and installing a dependable new system.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Vacaville

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab. This is a good option for newer homes or a first leak. If the home has had multiple leaks, we will explain why a full repipe may save money.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Vacaville

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to four days.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Vacaville, CA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and very durable piping material that's gaining popularity for repiping projects. FBL Plumbing specializes in PEX pipe replacement in Vacaville, CA, offering a system that resists freezing, corrosion, and scale buildup. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points. PEX also significantly dampens water hammer noise, providing a quieter plumbing system for your home.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Vacaville

    Kitec, with its orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe and brass fittings, and gray polybutylene pipe are both known for failures. Many homes built in the 1980s through early 2000s contain one or the other. We replace these systems completely so your home is easier to insure and sell. Visible pipe at the water heater often tells us which type you have.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Repipe Specialists in Vacaville

    Most PEX repipes for Northern California homes cost $5,000 to $12,000, depending on size, bathrooms and access. Copper repipes usually run 20 to 40 percent more.

    We commonly use Uponor PEX-A with ProPEX fittings and Type L copper from domestic mills. We also use Viega ProPress fittings for copper work.

    Copper is a rigid, durable metal with a long lifespan, known for its resistance to bacteria. PEX is a flexible plastic tubing that is more affordable, easier to install, resistant to freezing and corrosion, and very quiet. Both are excellent choices for repiping, and our specialists can help you decide which material is best suited for your home and budget in Vacaville, CA.

    While fixing individual leaks might seem cheaper in the short term, it's often a temporary solution for an aging plumbing system. If you're experiencing multiple leaks, low pressure, or discolored water, it indicates widespread pipe wear. Repiping addresses the root cause by replacing the entire system, providing a long-term, reliable solution and preventing future problems.

    We primarily install quality copper piping from reputable manufacturers like Mueller Streamline and Cerro, known for their durability and purity. For PEX systems, we often use leading brands such as Uponor (Wirsbo Aquapex), Zurn PEX, or Viega PEX, chosen for their proven reliability and performance in homes.

    Common signs include discolored or rusty water, low water pressure at multiple fixtures, frequent leaks or pinhole leaks, and strange noises coming from your pipes. If your home has galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes, a repipe is often a preventive and wise investment to prevent future problems.

    Most homes are repiped in two to four days, plus a day for drywall patching. Water is typically restored each evening.
