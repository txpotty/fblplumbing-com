    Lining and Bursting in Vacaville, CA

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Vacaville

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Vacaville yard.

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Vacaville, CA

    A broken water service line wastes water and soaks the yard. Pressure falls too. After locating the leak, we burst new HDPE through the old path or bore a new one, keeping the lawn and walk intact. The new line gets pressure tested.

    Commercial Trenchless Pipe Repair in Vacaville

    Commercial properties have sewer and storm lines under parking lots, sidewalks and buildings. Concrete is costly to replace. Trenchless repair avoids that demolition and the disruption to tenants and customers, and we line larger diameter pipe and multiple laterals on a schedule that limits the impact on business.

    Sectional Point Repair in Vacaville

    Sometimes only one joint or a few feet of pipe is bad. A spot liner fixes that. We position a short resin-soaked sleeve over the damaged area with a camera, inflate it and let it cure, so you pay for a few feet of liner instead of the whole run. It suits isolated damage in Vacaville laterals.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Repair Cost in Vacaville, CA

    Pipe Bursting in Vacaville, CA

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. You'll see an entry and exit pit. The head fractures the old pipe as it travels, with new HDPE trailing behind, fused into one length with no joints.
