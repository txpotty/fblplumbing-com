    Pressure Switch Replacement in Vacaville

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are frequent causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most budget-friendly well repairs.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Vacaville

    Submersible pumps are widely used in residential wells, designed to operate submerged within the well casing. When these pumps fail, symptoms often include no water, low water pressure, or strange noises from the well. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like motor burnout, damaged impellers, or faulty check valves that can affect submersible pump performance. We use dedicated equipment for safe and efficient pump extraction and reinstallation, ensuring the integrity of your well system.

    Constant Pressure System Repair in Vacaville, CA

    Variable speed systems like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by adjusting motor speed. When they fault out, the drive usually shows a code that guides diagnosis.     Well Pump Control Box & Electrical Repair in Vacaville, CA

    Many well pump failures stem from electrical issues within the control box or wiring. This can include tripped breakers, faulty capacitors, or corroded connections.     Diagnosing Low Water Pressure in Vacaville

    Low water pressure from a well system can be a stubborn problem, often indicating issues beyond just the pump itself.
