    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Vacaville

    Bladder tanks hold a cushion of air that lets the pump rest between cycles. When the bladder fails, the tank fills with water and the pump starts and stops every time someone opens a faucet. The bladder, the switch and the tank fittings all get checked. A properly sized tank can add years to pump life.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in Vacaville, CA

    Franklin Electric motors and control boxes are in a lot of local wells. Our technicians know them well, from the QD control boxes to the SubDrive constant pressure units. We carry the common Franklin parts. Replacing a failed motor usually costs less than a rewind once shipping is counted.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Vacaville, CA

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Most failures start with an air leak somewhere in the suction line. FBL Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and confirms it builds pressure. A jet pump that runs nonstop without reaching pressure has almost always lost prime or sucked air through a cracked fitting.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Vacaville, CA

    Electrical faults cause a big share of well calls, from a tripped breaker to a burned contact in the pressure switch. The control box capacitor and relay are next on the list. We lock out the power before testing anything. All wiring and splices meet Northern California electrical code before power goes back on.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Vacaville

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Most switches fail from pitted contacts, a plugged sensing tube or insects in the housing. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank, typically 40 and 60 psi. This is among the least expensive well repairs.

    Well Pump Maintenance and Repair Cost in Vacaville, CA

    Grundfos Well Pump Repair in Vacaville

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have built-in electronics that protect the motor and report faults. Most Grundfos calls involve a sensor or a drive fault. FBL Plumbing reads the fault code, checks the wiring and tests the pressure sensor before replacing anything in a Vacaville, CA well. Electronics fail less often than people think.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Vacaville

    Submersible pumps hang inside the well casing on drop pipe, often 100 feet or more below the wellhead. The usual signs are no water at all, weak pressure or a hum with no flow. Testing starts at the surface. If it has to come up, a pump hoist lifts the pipe safely, and we replace the burned motor, worn impellers or a bad check valve before setting it back.
