    Tankless Water Heater Relocation in Vacaville

    Moving the heater is common on a tankless job since the tank's location may not suit the new unit. Mounting near the gas meter on a garage or outside wall shortens the gas line and venting, though it can add distance to the bathrooms. We weigh both. A far bath may need recirc.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Vacaville

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, because the unit has to sense flow and fire first. A recirc pump on a timer or sensor keeps the hot line primed, cutting the wait at the far sink from about a minute to a few seconds. Less water goes down the drain.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Vacaville, CA

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Vacaville, CA homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. Whole-house electric units pull a lot of current. Some need two or three 40 to 60 amp double-pole breakers, and a 100-amp panel often cannot carry that on top of the air conditioner and range. Panel capacity gets checked up front.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Installation in Vacaville

    Sizing a home unit comes down to two numbers: peak gallons per minute and temperature rise. A shower head flows about 2 gallons per minute and a kitchen faucet a bit less, so a house that runs two showers and a sink together needs roughly 5 to 6 gallons at the rise your inlet water calls for. It shortens the list quickly.

Tank size matters too.
