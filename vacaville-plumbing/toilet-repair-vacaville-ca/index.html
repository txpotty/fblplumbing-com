    Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in Vacaville

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the china that compresses air with incoming water pressure, and the cartridge inside that vessel eventually wears out. You will not find most of these parts at a hardware store. Low pressure makes them sound weak.

    Toilet Flush Repair in Vacaville, CA
