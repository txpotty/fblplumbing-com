    Water Leak Repair in Vacaville

    Hidden leaks sit inside walls, under sink cabinets and in the buried yard line that runs between the meter and the house. We narrow it down with a moisture meter and a thermal camera.     Well Pump Service in Vacaville, CA

    Submersible pumps sit deep in the casing, so diagnosis happens at the surface first. FBL Plumbing checks the pressure switch, the control box capacitor and the tank precharge, then measures amp draw on the motor. High amp draw usually means a worn motor or a seized impeller. Jet pumps are simpler, and most of the time a bad foot valve or a lost prime is to blame.

    Sewer Pipe Repair in Vacaville, CA

    Clay and Orangeburg laterals crack and separate as the ground shifts, and roots find those gaps within a season or two. Every repair gets a final camera pass before the hole is closed.

    Gas Leak Repair in Vacaville

    Drain Repairs in Vacaville
