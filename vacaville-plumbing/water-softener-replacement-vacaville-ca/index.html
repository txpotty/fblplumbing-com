    Metered Softener Replacement in Vacaville

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly exhausted. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first month.

    Traditional Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Vacaville

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and proven solution for hard water in Vacaville. These systems use ion exchange to remove calcium and magnesium minerals, which are notorious for causing scale buildup. We replace old, inefficient salt-based units with modern, high-efficiency models that use less salt and water. Our plumbers confirm proper brine tank installation and regeneration settings for strong performance.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Vacaville, CA

    Fleck is a well-known brand for dependable water softener control valves, found in many residential and commercial systems across Northern California. If your Fleck-controlled softener is beyond repair or simply outdated, FBL Plumbing can expertly replace the entire unit. We are familiar with many Fleck models and can recommend an equivalent or upgraded system that fits cleanly with your existing plumbing in Vacaville.

    Softener Loop Installation in Vacaville, CA

    Many homes have a softener loop near the main water line that allows easy installation. When a home lacks one, we can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. We also route a drain line to a laundry box, floor drain or approved location. A good loop keeps outdoor hose bibs on hard water to save salt. This makes future service simpler.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Vacaville

    A standard single tank softener replacement usually costs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Dual tank or premium systems usually run $3,000 to $5,000. Adding a new loop or drain line can raise the cost.

    The right size depends on your household's daily water usage and the hardness of your water in grains per gallon. Our technicians will perform a water test and calculate the appropriate softening capacity in grains per day for your Vacaville home, so the system keeps up.

    With proper maintenance, most quality water softeners can last between 10 and 15 years, and sometimes even longer. Factors like water hardness, usage volume and the quality of the original installation can affect its lifespan. Regular servicing helps lengthen the life of your unit.

    New water softeners typically come with two types of warranties. There is a manufacturer's warranty on the equipment itself, which can range from 5 to 10 years or more for tanks and control valves, and an installation warranty from FBL Plumbing. We stand behind our work with a solid warranty for your peace of mind.

    In many areas, a plumbing permit is usually not required for a direct like-for-like water softener replacement if no significant changes to the existing plumbing are made. However, if new water lines or drain lines are being installed or relocated, a permit may be necessary. We will advise you on any permitting requirements.

    While it might seem like a straightforward task, water softener replacement involves working with water lines, drain connections and sometimes electrical components.
