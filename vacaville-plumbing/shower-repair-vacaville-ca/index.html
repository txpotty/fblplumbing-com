    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Vacaville, CA

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. That means a worn diverter steals pressure from the head, a clogged tub drain slows the shower and a leaky valve shows up at both the spout and the head. We check all of it. The overflow gasket gets a look too, since it is a common hidden leak.

    Emergency Shower Repair in Vacaville, CA

    A shower that will not shut off is an emergency. Shut the main, or use the shower's integral stops if your valve has them behind the trim or an access panel. Next, call us. FBL Plumbing sends a plumber with cartridges on the truck, and we can often get your water back on within the hour.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Vacaville, CA

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. A hand auger through the strainer clears most of them, and a drum machine handles clogs that have moved past the trap. Drain cleaner usually misses the clog. A hair catcher over the strainer keeps the next one from forming.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Vacaville, CA

    Stains on the ceiling below are often the first clue. A meter on the walls and a flood test on the pan show whether the leak is the valve, the pan or the drain. The source gets repaired. On slab homes, these leaks can spread into the flooring of the next room before anyone sees them.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Vacaville, CA

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Minerals fill the nozzles and the screen behind them, and the spray fades so gradually you barely notice until it is a mist. A soak can bring it back. If the cartridge is clogged too, we replace it and test the flow again.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Vacaville, CA

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. It comes down to the hot side, whether that is a sediment-choked heater outlet or a cartridge with scale packed in the hot port. We test both sides. Good hot flow at the sinks points us straight at the shower cartridge.

    Commercial Shower Service in Vacaville

    Hotels and dorms cannot wait days on a shower. We carry commercial cartridges and thermostatic parts, and we can stage repairs room by room so only one unit is down at a time. After-hours work is available. We set and test the limit stop on each valve before the next room.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Vacaville

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. If the head drips with the valve closed, water is slipping past the cartridge, and a new head will not change that. The fix is in the valve. Leaks at the arm threads are different, and those get new thread tape and a snug turn.
