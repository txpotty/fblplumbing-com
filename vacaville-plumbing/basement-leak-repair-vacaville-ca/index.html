    Interior Drainage Systems in Vacaville

    Where digging outside is not practical, an interior drain system does the same job from inside. A strip of slab comes out along the walls, perforated pipe goes in on stone, and it all drains to a sump. The wall gets a dimpled membrane. Seepage runs down behind it and into the pipe.

    Groundwater Pressure Relief in Vacaville

    High groundwater pushes up through the slab as well as in through the walls. Water rising through floor cracks or around a floor drain during wet weather is the usual sign. A sub-slab drain line to a sump drops the water table right under the floor. It is not a quick job.

    Sump Pump Installation & Repair in Vacaville

    A sump pump lives in a pit cut below floor level and kicks on when a float rises. Stuck floats, worn motors and failed check valves cause most of the service calls we run on sumps. We test yours with a bucket. A battery backup is worth adding where power drops in storms.

    Wall Leak Solutions in Vacaville, CA

    Damp walls without a crack often point to water soaking through porous block or concrete. A simple foil test tells you whether it is seepage or condensation: tape a square of foil to the wall for a day and see which side gets wet. Condensation needs a dehumidifier. Seepage needs drainage outside or a channel inside.

    Water Heater and Appliance Leak Repair in Vacaville, CA

    Water heaters, softeners and washing machines often sit in basements, and their leaks pool on the floor out of sight. Once the tank itself leaks, it needs replacing. Leaks at the T&P valve, the drain valve or a supply connector can usually be fixed in one visit, and we add a drain pan or a leak sensor that shuts off the water automatically.

    Exterior Waterproofing Solutions in Vacaville, CA

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing, cleaning the wall, and applying a membrane with a drainage board over it.
