    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Vacaville, CA

    A pump replacement in an existing pit usually costs much less than a new pit installation. New pits require slab cutting, excavation and concrete patching, which adds labor.     New Sump Pit Installation in Vacaville

    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can create one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, hardpan or rock and set a durable basin. We add gravel around the basin to help water flow in. Then we patch the concrete and install the pump and cover. A new pit gives you a dependable place to collect and remove water.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Vacaville, CA

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to reach for maintenance and repair.
