    Pipe Repair and Repiping in Vacaville, CA

    Pinhole leaks and rust-colored water from the tap usually point to supply pipe near the end of its working life in an older house. We patch single leaks with a copper repair or PEX transition. Repeat leaks mean a repipe. A pipe split by a hard freeze gets cut out and replaced, then pressure-tested.

    Drain Cleaning and Clog Removal in Vacaville, CA

    Grease is the big one in kitchen lines. Bathroom drains clog with hair and soap scum, and we clear both with hand augers, sectional cable machines or a hydro jetter, depending on how far down the blockage sits and what the pipe is made of. A camera tells us if roots or a broken joint are behind a repeat clog.

    Repiping and Supply Line Replacement in Vacaville

    Polybutylene, the gray plastic pipe used for years in home construction, fails from the inside without warning. We replace it, along with corroded galvanized and pinholed copper, using PEX or copper, routing new lines through the attic and walls so the slab stays intact and the house keeps water each night. Drywall patches are included.

    Gas Line Repair and Installation in Vacaville, CA

    Smell gas? Leave the house and call the gas utility first, then call us. We install new gas lines in black iron or CSST to ranges, dryers and outdoor grills, then pressure-test the line and check every joint with a leak detector before the appliance gets lit. All gas work is permitted where required.

    Gas Lines for New Appliances in Vacaville, CA

    Switching from an electric range to gas means a new line and a shutoff. We size the pipe for the appliance's BTU load, run it in black iron or CSST, bond it where the code requires and pressure-test before the gas company or inspector signs off on the work. Then we light it and check the flame.
