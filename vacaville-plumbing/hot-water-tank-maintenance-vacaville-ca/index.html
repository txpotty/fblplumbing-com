    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Vacaville

    Street pressure over 80 psi is hard on every fixture in the house, and plumbing codes generally call for a pressure reducing valve above that number. A gauge on a hose bib tells us the static pressure. The relief valve lever gets lifted next. If the valve opens and reseats cleanly and the expansion tank holds its charge, the tank is safe from pressure spikes.

    Standard Tank Flushing in Vacaville

    Flushing is the one job every tank needs, gas or electric, new or old. The harder the water, the sooner it matters. We drain, partly refill and drain again until the hose runs clear, sometimes four or five rounds. Then we close the valve and check that it reseals without a drip.

    Anode Upgrades for Odor in Vacaville, CA

    A rotten egg smell from the hot side only is almost always sulfate-reducing bacteria reacting with a magnesium anode. A different rod usually solves it. We replace it with an aluminum-zinc rod or a powered anode, then chlorinate the tank to kill the bacteria living in it. Powered anodes never wear out and draw very little electricity.

    Rheem and Bradford White Tank Service in Vacaville

    Rheem, Bradford White, AO Smith and State make most of the tanks in local garages, and each has quirks a tech should know before opening a drain valve. On some models the anode sits under a plastic top cap. Bradford White often uses a combo anode in the hot outlet nipple. Our trucks carry sockets for each brand.

    Electric Thermostat Service in Vacaville

    Each element on an electric tank has its own thermostat, and the upper one carries a reset button for the high-limit switch. A tripped reset is a symptom. We find out why it tripped, which usually comes down to a shorted element or a thermostat stuck closed, before we press it back in. Power stays off at the breaker the whole time.
