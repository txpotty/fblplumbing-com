    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Porterville

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Earthquakes and hillside soil movement are real risks in Northern California, so flexible connections and seismic straps help protect plumbing from sudden movement. We replace corroded sections with polyethylene pipe laid with tracer wire, using an approved transition riser where the line comes up out of the ground at the house. Above-ground runs stay in steel. We test before backfilling so any problem joint is fixed while the trench is still open.

    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Porterville, CA

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Years of vibration from a furnace or a house settling can loosen threaded fittings, and pipe in a damp garage or an exterior wall can rust from the outside in. We reseal or replace the bad sections. Outdoor runs get coated or painted after the repair so rust does not start again.
