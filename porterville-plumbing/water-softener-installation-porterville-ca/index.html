    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Porterville, CA

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell.     Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Porterville

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. Every whole-house install gets a bypass valve, and we add a pressure reducing valve if incoming pressure is too high. Every tap gets soft water. Outdoor taps can be left off.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Porterville

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds.
