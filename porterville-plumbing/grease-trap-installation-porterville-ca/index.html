    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Porterville

    Hydro-mechanical grease interceptors, often called HGI units, are compact and designed for indoor use, usually under a sink or on the floor.     Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Porterville

Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid easily.
