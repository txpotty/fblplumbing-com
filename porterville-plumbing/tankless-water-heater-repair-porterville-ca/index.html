    Tankless Error Code Diagnostics in Porterville, CA

    Every tankless brand flashes its own codes, and the same number can mean different things on two units.     Electric Tankless Element Repair in Porterville, CA

    Electric units fail in their own way. We check each element's resistance, read voltage under load and clamp amperage on each leg, then confirm the panel and wire size match the unit's rating.

    Ignition Failure Repair in Porterville, CA

    No ignition codes have four usual causes: a dirty flame rod, a bad igniter, low gas pressure or a blocked intake. The gas line is the sneaky one.     No Hot Water Tankless Repair in Porterville

    No hot water at all usually points to ignition, gas supply or a heat exchanger choked with scale.
