    Recirculation Pump Repair in Porterville

    Many tankless systems use internal or external recirculation to deliver hot water faster. Pump failures, check valve problems or wrong schedule settings can cause long waits or lukewarm water.     Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Porterville

    Tankless heaters rely on flow sensors to start and thermistors to regulate temperature. Temperature becomes steady again once readings are accurate.
