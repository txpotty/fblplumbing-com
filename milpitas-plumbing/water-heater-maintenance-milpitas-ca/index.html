    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Milpitas

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Scale on the tank bottom traps heat and causes water to pop and rumble as it heats. We flush until it runs clear. Heavy buildup may take several cycles.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Milpitas

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment buries the lower element until it overheats and fails, leaving you with a short supply of hot water. We test both elements. Thermostats and wiring get checked with the power off.

    Anode Rod Checks in Milpitas

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. Once the rod is eaten down to its core wire, the water goes after the tank itself. It comes out for inspection. Replacing the rod is much cheaper than replacing the heater.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Milpitas

    Some water eats anode rods fast. Depending on your water chemistry, a rod that should last five years might be spent in three, so we pull it on every visit instead of assuming it is fine because the tank is still young. We swap worn ones. A healthy rod keeps the tank from rusting through.
