    Frequently Asked Questions About Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Milpitas

    Most full poly repipes take three to five days, including drywall patching. Larger homes take longer. The schedule depends on the number of fixtures, attic access and whether you choose PEX or copper, and water is usually back on at the end of each work day.

    Rural properties around Milpitas often rely on private wells, which may need filtration for iron, sediment, or other minerals. Hard water does not cause poly to fail the way chlorine does.
