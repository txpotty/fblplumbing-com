    Softener Cleaning and Testing in Milpitas

    Hard water scale builds in pipes and appliances fast. Brine, injector, resin and regeneration all get checked, and we test the water at a tap. Resin cleaner helps. Worn resin gets swapped.

    Water Softener Service in Milpitas

    A softener only helps if it regenerates. We check the salt level, break up any salt bridge in the brine tank, clean the brine well and injector, and run a manual regeneration to confirm the valve cycles correctly. We test the water afterward. A softener that quietly stopped working lets scale back into every pipe and appliance.

    Drain Cleaning as Maintenance in Milpitas

    Slow drains rarely fix themselves. Camera checks and cleaning on problem lines keep grease and roots from causing a holiday backup. We jet greasy lines. Rooted lines get regular cutting.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Milpitas

    Tankless units need descaling. A small pump circulates descaler. The inlet filter, venting and gas line get checked too.
