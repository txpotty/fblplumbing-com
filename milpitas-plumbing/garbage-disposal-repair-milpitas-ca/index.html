    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in Milpitas, CA

    A disposal that grinds but drains slowly usually has a clogged discharge tube or trap arm. Grease, rice and coffee grounds are the usual culprits. We clear the line with the right tools and check for proper slope. We also explain what to keep out of the disposal.

    Unusual Noises and Odor Diagnosis in Milpitas

    Strange grinding, clanking, or humming noises, along with persistent foul odors, are clear signs your garbage disposal needs attention. Our plumbers in Milpitas can diagnose the cause of these issues, whether it's loose parts, worn-out impellers, or food buildup. We provide solutions ranging from tightening components to thorough cleaning and odor elimination, restoring quiet and freshness to your kitchen.

    Leaking Disposal Repair in Milpitas, CA

    Leaks can come from the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube or the disposal body itself. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty, and replace gaskets or tubes when needed. A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the internal seal has failed. In that case, replacement is the better option.

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Milpitas

    A common issue with garbage disposals is a jam, often caused by hard food items like bones or fibrous vegetables. Our technicians in Milpitas are equipped to safely clear these jams and, if necessary, repair or replace the motor if it has been damaged by repeated stalling. We carry purpose-built tools to free impellers without causing further damage to the unit or your plumbing system, delivering a lasting fix.

    Jammed Disposal Repair in Milpitas

    A humming disposal that will not spin almost always has a jam. Bones, fruit pits, glass or utensils can wedge the flywheel. We cut power, use a hex wrench on the bottom of the unit to free the flywheel and remove the object. Then we test the motor and press the reset button when needed.

    Disposal Reset and Electrical Repair in Milpitas

    When a disposal will not turn on at all, the thermal overload may have tripped or the switch may be bad. We check the reset button, breaker, wall switch and wiring. Air switches and batch-feed lids are tested too. Most electrical issues are easy to fix once found.

    Noisy or Grinding Disposal Repair in Milpitas

    Loud metal-on-metal sounds may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. We inspect the chamber, tighten the mounting ring and remove debris. If the bearings are failing, the noise will continue. In that case, we can quote a less noisy replacement unit.
