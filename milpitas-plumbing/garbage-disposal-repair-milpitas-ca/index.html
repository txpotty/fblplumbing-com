    What Fails on a Disposal, and How We Fix It in Milpitas, CA

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Milpitas

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps wedge between the impeller lugs and the grind ring, and the motor stalls. We shut off power, insert a 1/4 inch hex key into the socket on the bottom of the unit and work it back and forth until the flywheel turns freely. Next we pull the object with pliers. A motor that stalled for a long time may have overheated, so we test it under load before calling the job done.

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Milpitas, CA

    The flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube and body are where leaks start. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the internal seal has failed, and in that case replacement is the better option. That seal cannot be replaced.

    Jammed Disposal Repair in Milpitas

    Jams usually trace back to one hard object, like a peach pit, a chicken bone or a twist tie wrapped around the impeller. We free the flywheel from below with the hex key and pull the object with pliers, never a hand, then run it with water to make sure nothing else is stuck. We press the reset after clearing it. Repeat jams point us to the trap arm.

    Emergency Disposal and Sink Backup Repair in Milpitas

    A failed disposal can push water back into the sink and dishwasher. If water is rising or reaching the floor, shut off the disposal and the dishwasher and call us, since running either one just adds more water to the cabinet. We respond fast.

    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in Milpitas, CA

    Good grinding but slow draining usually means the discharge tube or the trap arm is packed. Coffee grounds and rice cause most of it. We pull the trap, clear the tube and the arm with the right size cable, and check that the arm has at least a quarter inch of fall per foot toward the wall.

    Leak Detection and Repair in Milpitas, CA

    There are four usual leak points on a disposal: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Most leak points can be repaired. A leak from the housing means it is time for a new unit.

    Disposal Reset and Electrical Repair in Milpitas

    If a disposal is completely dead, the thermal overload may have tripped or the switch may be bad. We check the reset button, breaker, wall switch and wiring, and we also test air switches and batch-feed lids that use a magnet to start the motor. Most electrical faults are easy once found.

    InSinkErator Disposal Repair in Milpitas, CA

    The InSinkErator lineup runs from Badger units to the Evolution series, and we repair both. Badgers are simple single-stage units, while Evolution models have multi-stage grinding and, on some models, a separate stage that can jam on its own. Common parts are on board. Motors on these units are not sold separately.
