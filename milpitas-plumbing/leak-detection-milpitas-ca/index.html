    Frequently Asked Questions About Leak Detection in Milpitas

    Leak detection in Milpitas usually costs $150 to $450 for a typical home, depending on the size of the system and the number of areas tested. Pool leak detection runs about $250 to $500. We tell you the price before we start.

    Common signs include a sudden spike in your water bill, damp spots on walls or floors, mildew odors, visible mold growth, the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use, or a constantly running water meter even when all water is off.

    Older homes may have galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes, both known for higher leak rates due to corrosion or material degradation. Copper pipes can develop pinhole leaks over time, while PVC and PEX are usually more reliable but can still leak at fittings or due to installation errors.

    A slab leak is a leak in the water pipes embedded within or beneath your home's concrete slab foundation. Earthquakes and hillside soil movement are real risks in Northern California, so flexible connections and seismic straps help protect plumbing from sudden movement. Soil that shifts and settles puts stress on plumbing lines, and temperature swings cause pipes to expand and contract over the years.

    Rural properties around Milpitas often rely on private wells, which may need treatment for iron, sediment, or other minerals. Hard or aggressive water can cause pinhole corrosion in copper pipes over time. Slab foundations on shifting soils also put stress on pipes underneath the house, and high water pressure in some areas adds more stress.

    Leak detection itself usually does not require a permit. However, if the leak requires significant repairs, such as replacing a section of a main water line, gas line or extensive slab repair, then a plumbing permit from Milpitas may be necessary. We will advise you on any permit requirements.

    Most residential leak detection appointments take one to three hours. Larger homes, commercial buildings or multiple leaks can take longer. We give you an estimate when we schedule.
