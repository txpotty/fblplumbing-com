    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Milpitas

    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous. We lift the lever, confirm it opens and reseats and check the discharge line for proper material and termination. We also check your thermal expansion tank pressure with a gauge, since a waterlogged expansion tank lets pressure jump every heating cycle. High street pressure is common in many service areas, so we test static pressure at a hose bib too.

    Standard Hot Water Tank Tune-Up in Milpitas

    FBL Plumbing offers a complete tune-up for conventional hot water tanks, focusing on critical components. This includes draining the tank to remove sediment buildup, which is crucial wherever water carries minerals, and inspecting the anode rod for signs of corrosion.     Electric Element and Thermostat Service in Milpitas

    Electric tanks rely on upper and lower heating elements that get crusted with scale in hard water. We test each element with a meter, check the thermostats and look for scorched wires at the terminal block. If an element has failed we replace it with the matching wattage and voltage. Plenty of families who think they need a new heater just need a lower element and a flush.

    Electric Hot Water Tank Element Check in Milpitas

    Electric hot water tanks rely on heating elements to warm the water. During maintenance, our plumbers inspect these elements for signs of wear, corrosion or burnout. We also check the thermostat settings and wiring connections to ensure they are functioning correctly and safely. This preventative check can identify failing elements before they stop heating altogether, preventing unexpected cold showers for your family in Milpitas.

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Milpitas, CA

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can block the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a steady blue flame and lower gas bills.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Milpitas

    Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. Mineral content adds to sediment buildup in hot water tanks, which reduces efficiency and can damage heating elements or tank linings over time.     Regular, professional maintenance can frequently be a condition for keeping your manufacturer's warranty valid. Check your warranty documentation, but generally, proof of annual service can help keep your warranty intact.

    Many manufacturers, including Rheem, A.O. Smith and Bradford White, expect regular maintenance and can reject claims for sediment damage. Keeping service records safeguards your warranty.

    That sound is water trapped under a layer of sediment boiling as the burner fires. A flush usually quiets it, though very old hardened scale may not come out completely.
