    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Milpitas

    Gas tankless units in Milpitas are the common pick for whole-house hot water because a single 199,000 BTU burner can feed two showers and a sink at the same time without losing temperature. The catch is gas supply. Most homes need the line upsized from the meter, often from half-inch to three-quarter inch or larger. We test the new gas piping before first fire.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Milpitas

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, because the unit has to sense flow and fire first. A recirculation pump on a timer or a motion sensor keeps the hot line primed, so the wait at the farthest sink drops from a minute to a few seconds. Less water goes down the drain.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Milpitas, CA

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Milpitas, CA homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. Whole-house electric units pull a lot of current. Some models need two or three 40 to 60 amp double-pole breakers, more than a 100-amp panel can usually spare alongside the range and AC. We check the panel first.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Installation in Milpitas

    Navien builds condensing tankless units with stainless heat exchangers, and several models come with a recirculation pump built in. The built-in pump can move hot water toward a distant bath on a timer, cutting the wait at the tap without a separate pump. Navien vents in PVC. Condensing units also need a condensate drain.

    Propane Tankless Water Heater Installation in Milpitas

    Propane homes in Milpitas can run tankless units too, using a model built or converted for propane. Since propane runs at different pressure from natural gas, the regulator, line sizing and gas valve must all match, and the supplier may need to swap the tank regulator. A small tank can run short.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Services in Milpitas, CA

    Businesses in Milpitas, CA with steady hot water use, like salons, restaurants and small gyms, often get more out of two or three tankless units racked together than one big tank. If one unit goes down, the others keep running. We size the rack by peak demand. Commercial installs usually need a larger gas service and a mechanical permit.
