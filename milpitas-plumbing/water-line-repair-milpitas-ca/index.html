    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Milpitas, CA

    One leak on a newer copper or PEX line is a spot repair. Two or more leaks on galvanized is a replacement. Age matters too, and a line past about 40 years is usually near the end no matter the material. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars while a full replacement costs more and resets the clock for decades. We show you the pipe we exposed.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Milpitas

    A trench through a paver driveway or a mature garden can cost more to restore than the pipe itself. That is where trenchless pays off. We dig a pit at each end and pull HDPE or PEX through with a boring tool or pipe puller, following the old line or cutting a new path beside it. Most replacements finish in a day. You also get the manufacturer warranty on the new pipe.

    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Milpitas

    Underground leaks hide well. Many people find out from the bill before they see anything in the yard. Others notice a patch of grass that grows greener than the rest, or ground that stays soft long after the sprinklers shut off. We confirm the leak at the meter, trace the pipe route and listen along it. The dig follows the sound.

    Emergency Water Line Break Repair in Milpitas, CA

    A full break can flood the yard and leave the house dry at the same time. We shut it down first. The crew pumps the hole, finds the break and decides whether a coupling will do or whether a section needs replacing, setting up a temporary supply to the house if the job will take a while. Same-day repairs are the norm. We photograph the damage for your insurance or HOA.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Milpitas

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. Straight runs are ideal. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, while new HDPE comes through right behind the head. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Milpitas, CA

    PEX itself rarely fails underground, but the joints and the first few feet near the house take a beating. Rats and gophers sometimes chew it. A shovel or a fence post auger can nick it, and pipe left in the sun before it was buried can get brittle. We cut out the damaged section and install new pipe with crimp, clamp or expansion fittings, matched to the PEX brand already in the ground.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Milpitas

    High static pressure shortens the life of the service line and every fixture behind it. Most codes cap it at 80 psi. We gauge the pressure at a hose bib, and if it reads high we replace the pressure reducing valve with a Watts or Zurn Wilkins unit. Old gate valves that will not close get replaced with full-port ball valves. So the shutoff works when you need it.

    Main Water Line Replacement in Milpitas, CA

    Replacement beats repair once a line has leaked two or three times, or when it is galvanized or polybutylene. Patches on those materials rarely hold. We run new Type K copper or PEX from the meter to the house, typically finishing in one day whether we trench or pull the pipe. We pull the permit, set up the inspection and pressure test the new line before it is covered.
