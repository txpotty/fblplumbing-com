    Water-Saving Toilet Upgrades in Santa Cruz, CA

    Older toilets are one of the largest indoor water users in a house, and replacing a 3.5 gallon model with a 1.28 gallon one cuts toilet water use by more than half. Dual-flush models go lower for liquids. We recommend models with high MaP scores so the savings do not come with double flushing.

    Gravity-Flush Toilet Replacement in Santa Cruz

    Gravity-flush toilets are the simplest and most forgiving, and a well-made one with a 3 inch flush valve will outflush most of the older toilets it replaces. Parts are easy to find. We set it level with shims if the floor is out, then caulk the base and test several full flushes.

    ADA and Commercial Toilet Replacement in Santa Cruz, CA

    Public restrooms need bowls rated for heavy use, and many need to meet ADA height and clearance rules when a fixture is replaced. Clearances get checked before we order anything. Sensor flush valves are an easy add during the swap, and they cut down on unflushed bowls.

    Commercial Flush Valve Upgrades in Santa Cruz

    Manual flush valves on older commercial toilets leak at the handle and stick open, and swapping to a sensor valve during a replacement improves hygiene without changing the rough-in. You can pick battery or hardwired sensors. Work can happen after hours, and we check that the new fixtures meet accessibility requirements.

    Emergency Toilet Replacement in Santa Cruz

    A toilet that cracked overnight and is still seeping into the floor cannot wait for next week, so we bring a standard round and elongated model on emergency calls. Shut the angle stop first. Most emergency swaps are done the same day, and we check the subfloor for soaked plywood before setting it.

    Toilet Flange Repair During Replacement in Santa Cruz

    Pulling the old toilet is the only chance to see the flange, and a cracked plastic ring or a rusted cast iron flange will ruin a new install within a year. We check it every time. For a cracked flange, a stainless repair ring is often enough, while a flange that sits below the finished floor gets a spacer or extender so the seal compresses properly.

    One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Santa Cruz, CA

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, so there are fewer leak points and fewer crevices to clean. They are heavier and pricier than two-piece models. Getting one up a staircase takes two people, and we set it without dragging it across your tile so the floor stays unscratched and the wax ring lands centered on the flange.
