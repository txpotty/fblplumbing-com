    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Maintenance in Santa Cruz

    We recommend professional water softener maintenance at least once a year. For homes with very hard water or high water usage, a twice-yearly check-up might be beneficial to support good performance and longevity of the unit.

    Once a year is a good schedule for most homes. High hardness and heavy water use can warrant service every six to nine months.

    Look for spots on dishes, scale on faucets, stiff laundry or soap that will not lather. Salt that never goes down in the brine tank is another clear sign.

    While you can perform basic tasks like checking salt levels and refilling the brine tank, professional maintenance involves inspecting internal components, testing resin efficiency and diagnosing control valve issues. These tasks require professional tools and experience to confirm the system functions correctly and safely.

    Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running mineral-heavy. Hard water carries minerals like calcium and magnesium, and regular water softener maintenance is crucial to prevent scale buildup in pipes and appliances. A well-kept system keeps fighting those minerals and safeguards your home's plumbing.

    Common signs include a return of hard water symptoms such as mineral spots on dishes, dry skin and hair, or scale buildup on fixtures. You might also notice your softener using more salt than usual, odd noises or the system not regenerating properly. Don't wait for a total failure.
