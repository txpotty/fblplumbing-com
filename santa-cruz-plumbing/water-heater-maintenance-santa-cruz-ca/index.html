    Descaling Tankless Units in Santa Cruz

    Error codes often mean scale. Tankless heaters are especially sensitive to hard water, and once scale coats the heat exchanger, flow drops, the unit overheats and the control board shuts it down to protect itself. A pump kit handles it. Yearly descaling is standard advice for hard water.

    Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Santa Cruz

    Flushing is the heart of tank maintenance. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. We run a hose from the drain valve, open it up and let the tank drain until the water runs clear, then refill and check the anode rod, the relief valve and every connection for drips or corrosion. Plan on an hour. A clean tank heats faster and lasts longer.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Santa Cruz

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment piles up around the lower element, it runs hotter than it should, and eventually it burns out, which is why you suddenly get only a few minutes of hot water. We test both elements. With power off, we check thermostats and connections.

    Attic, Closet and Garage Units in Santa Cruz

    Location changes the risks. Attic heaters need pans and drain lines, and garage gas units often need elevation or protection. We check the pan and drain. Access platforms and venting get looked at too.

    Commercial Tank and Tankless Service in Santa Cruz, CA
