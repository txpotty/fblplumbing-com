    What a Plumbing Checkup Covers in Santa Cruz, CA

    Sump Pump Maintenance in Santa Cruz, CA

    Strong winter storms can bring days of heavy rain to Northern California, saturating ground and pushing water into basements and sewer lines. Pumps sit unused for months, so we test the float, clean the pit and check that the discharge runs clear. The check valve gets a look. We test any backup pump.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Santa Cruz

    Sediment is the tank's slow killer. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be mildly corrosive to older copper pipe. The tank gets flushed, the anode rod gets checked, the relief valve gets tested and the connections get inspected. Flushing takes about an hour. Less sediment means quicker heating and a longer tank life.

    Gas Line and Pipe Material Checks in Santa Cruz, CA

    Gas leaks are the one item we never skip. Visible gas piping, connectors and shutoffs get checked, and old flex connectors get flagged. We note pipe types. Polybutylene or old galvanized supply lines go on the report with repipe options.

    Gas Connector and Shutoff Checks in Santa Cruz

    Gas feeds water heaters, furnaces, ranges and dryers. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections with leak detector solution, make sure each appliance shutoff valve turns and replace any old or kinked flexible connectors. Gas work is done to code. If you smell gas, get outside before calling the gas company.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Santa Cruz

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale builds up inside the heat exchanger, especially with hard water, and the unit starts throwing error codes, running lukewarm or cutting out mid-shower long before it actually wears out. We pump descaler through the service valves. We also clean the inlet screen and check venting and gas supply.

    Hot Water Tank Service in Santa Cruz, CA

    Gas and electric tanks need different checks. On gas units we look at the burner flame, the flue draft and the gas connection for leaks, and on electric units we test the elements and the thermostats and check the wiring for heat damage. Both get flushed. Mineral buildup on the bottom wastes fuel and can lead to early tank failure.

    Scheduled Service for Businesses in Santa Cruz

    Commercial plumbing takes a beating. We service large water heaters, restroom fixtures, backflow preventers and kitchen drain lines on a set schedule, so failures are caught on a quiet Tuesday morning instead of a busy Friday night. Inspections go smoother. You get records for every visit.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Santa Cruz

    Backflow devices protect the drinking water. Many water providers require yearly testing on irrigation and commercial backflow preventers, and our certified testers check each check valve and relief valve and file the results with your utility. Failed devices get rebuilt. Weather protection gets checked too. Testing on time avoids fines.
