    Bathroom Faucet Replacement in Santa Cruz, CA

    Old lav faucets rarely come off clean. Lav faucet nuts hide behind the bowl in a tight gap, and after a couple of decades they tend to fuse to the threaded shank.     Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Replacement in Santa Cruz

    Pull-down spray heads take a beating. The hose wears, the spray button sticks, and after a few years the docking magnet or weight stops pulling the head back into place. We swap the whole faucet. We clip the new hose weight at the right height and connect fresh braided supplies to both stops.

    Delta Faucet Replacement in Santa Cruz, CA

    Delta faucets come in a few cartridge families, and the model number tells you which. Older single-handle Delta faucets use a ball valve with seats and springs, while newer ones use a cartridge, and that difference decides whether a repair is worth doing. We tell you which you have. A sound body sometimes only needs parts.

    Water-Saving Faucet Upgrades in Santa Cruz

    Federal rules cap new kitchen and lav faucets at 2.2 gallons per minute. WaterSense lav faucets go lower, to 1.5 gallons per minute, and most people do not notice the difference because the aerator mixes air into the stream. It adds up over a year. A WaterSense lav faucet is an easy swap.

    Utility Sink Faucet Replacement in Santa Cruz

    Utility faucets in garages and laundry rooms take hard use. Buckets hung on the spout and hoses yanked off the threads loosen the faucet and eventually crack its supply joints. We use brass-bodied replacements. We mount it tight to the wall or tub deck.
