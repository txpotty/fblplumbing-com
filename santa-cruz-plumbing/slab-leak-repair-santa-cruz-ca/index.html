    Locating and Repairing Slab Leaks in Santa Cruz, CA

    Slab Leak Reroute in Santa Cruz

    Older copper under a slab tends to leak again. Rerouting ends that. New PEX runs through the attic and down the walls while the old pipe stays buried, with openings placed behind vanities or in closets.

    Thermal and Acoustic Tools in Santa Cruz

    A thermal camera shows the warm streak a hot water leak leaves on the floor. Cold leaks need the mic. On cold lines we rely on an acoustic sensor that listens through the slab, marking the point where the sound is loudest. Both tools ride on every Santa Cruz truck.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Santa Cruz, CA

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Heat is the reason. Each time hot water runs, the pipe expands against concrete and gravel, and recirculation pumps keep that cycle going all day, which grinds the pipe wall thin at the rub points. We repair or reroute the line and can set the recirculation pump on a timer.

    Ground Radar and Acoustic Locating in Santa Cruz

    Ground-penetrating radar shows what is inside the slab before we cut. It finds rebar and cables. Combined with a listening device, radar finds leaks in any pipe type, even with traffic noise, since radar maps the route and the mic confirms the spot. We use both in Santa Cruz.

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Santa Cruz

    A reroute abandons the leaking pipe under the slab. The new pipe runs through the attic. For older Santa Cruz homes, this works well with aging copper or galvanized pipe, since it bypasses every foot of buried line on that run. We cut small openings at fixture walls.

    Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Santa Cruz

    Locating comes before cutting. We use a ground mic and a thermal camera. With the mic catching the hiss through the concrete and the camera showing warm water spreading, we can usually narrow the leak to about a foot. We test hot lines, cold lines and drains in your Santa Cruz home.
