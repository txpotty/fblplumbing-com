    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Santa Cruz, CA

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We swap cartridges or stems and seats, greasing the O-rings so handles turn easily. Old seats need a seat wrench. About an hour, usually.

    Upgrading Tub Faucets in Santa Cruz, CA

    Plenty of people replace a working tub faucet for looks. Old cross handles can become a modern lever trim, and on a universal valve the wall never opens. We can match your sink finish. Delta and Moen make this simplest.

    Diverter Valve Repair and Replacement in Santa Cruz, CA

    Water running from the spout during a shower means a failing diverter. On a single-handle setup the diverter is usually the lift gate in the spout itself, and on a three-handle faucet it is the middle stem, and both wear out until water splits between the spout and the head. Spout diverters are a quick swap. Middle stems need a seat too.
