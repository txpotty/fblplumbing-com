    Hydro-Jetting Services in Santa Cruz, CA

    A jetter pushes water at thousands of psi through a nozzle that sprays backward and pulls itself down the pipe. The spray cleans the pipe all the way around. Cables punch a hole through a clog, while jetting removes the buildup that would start the next one. It works best on lines that clog again and again.

    Preventative Drain Maintenance in Santa Cruz

    Drains that clog once a year can be cleaned once a year instead. That is the whole idea.     Outdoor Drain & Storm Drain Clearing in Santa Cruz, CA

    Patio drains, driveway trench drains and downspout lines fill with leaves, grit and mud.     Main Sewer Line Clog Repair in Santa Cruz

    When the toilet gurgles as the washer drains, the main line is usually the problem. Everything shares that pipe. We go in through the outside cleanout with a large sectional cable, then camera the line if the backup returns to check for roots or a sag. Hydro jetting handles the worst of it.

    Kitchen Sink Clog Repair in Santa Cruz

    Kitchen sinks clog from grease that cools and hardens on the pipe wall. Then food scraps and soap pile on. We pull the trap, run a cable through the trap arm and down the branch line to the main stack, and check the disposal for a jam or worn impeller. A heavy grease line may need a quick jet afterward.

    Floor Drain and Garage Drain Clearing in Santa Cruz

    Floor drains in garages, utility rooms and basements collect sand, dirt and debris. Their traps also dry out. We clear the drain and the line behind it, refill the trap and install a trap seal if it rarely gets water. Bad smell, dry trap.

    Laundry and Utility Drain Clogs in Santa Cruz, CA

    A washing machine dumps up to 20 gallons in a couple of minutes. Fibers and lint go with the water. Over months that lint packs the standpipe and the branch line behind it, which is why a clogged laundry drain usually overflows at the standpipe in the middle of a cycle. We cable it and suggest a lint trap. Some laundry lines share the kitchen branch, so we check both.

    Recurring Drain Clogs and Camera Diagnosis in Santa Cruz, CA

    The same drain clogging every few weeks is a pipe problem, not bad luck. Something is catching debris. We run a small inspection camera through the line, letting you see any sag, crack or heavy scale on the screen. Then you decide. A deeper cleaning, a spot repair or a new section are the usual options.
