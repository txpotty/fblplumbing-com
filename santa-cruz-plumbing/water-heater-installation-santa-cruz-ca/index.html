    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Santa Cruz, CA

    Gas Water Heater Installation in Santa Cruz, CA

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Santa Cruz

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Every job gets a fresh connector, a drip leg, a shutoff and a vent connector with the right rise toward the chimney or flue. Then a draft test. Gas suits busy households.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Santa Cruz

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. Each gas swap gets a new connector, a drip leg, a draft test at the hood and a leak check with a detector at every fitting. Venting is what keeps CO out. We follow code on all of it.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Santa Cruz

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. An expansion tank gives that water somewhere to go, so the relief valve stops dripping and pipe joints stop taking pressure spikes every time the burner runs. We pre-charge it to your house pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.
