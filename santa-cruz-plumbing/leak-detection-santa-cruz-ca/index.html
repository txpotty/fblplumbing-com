    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Santa Cruz, CA

    The buried line from your meter to the house can leak for months without surfacing, especially in old galvanized pipe. A green stripe in a dry yard is a hint. We trace the line with an electronic locator, then use a ground microphone to hear the leak, and on plastic pipe we inject tracer gas and sniff for it at the surface.

    Fixture and Appliance Leaks in Santa Cruz, CA

    A silent toilet leak can lose hundreds of gallons a day. Worn faucet cartridges, softener valves and water heater connectors add more. We dye test tanks, check flappers and fill valves, and inspect supply lines and water heater fittings, with most of those fixed in under an hour from truck stock. Cheap fixes, real savings.

    PEX and Copper Leak Detection in Santa Cruz, CA

    Copper leaks are loud on a ground microphone. PEX is quieter, since plastic pipe does not carry sound well, so we lean more on thermal scans, moisture readings and tracer gas when the house is plumbed in it. We handle both every week. Pinholes in copper and failed crimp fittings in PEX top the list of what we find.

    Our Leak Detection Equipment in Santa Cruz

    Our trucks carry acoustic correlators and ground microphones, thermal cameras, line tracers, moisture meters and tracer gas kits. Each tool has a job. Acoustic gear hears pressurized water escaping, thermal cameras show temperature differences from hot water lines and wet drywall, while tracer gas catches silent leaks on PEX and PVC.

    Hidden Wall and Ceiling Leaks in Santa Cruz

    Bubbling paint, soft drywall and a musty smell are the signs we hear about most. All of them mean water. A thermal scan shows the wet patch, and a moisture meter confirms it. Acoustic tools help when the leak is on a pressurized line, and once the source is pinned down we cut a single neat access opening instead of chasing the stain across the wall.

    Gas Line Leak Locating in Santa Cruz

    You cannot locate a gas leak by smell alone. Our plumbers pressure test the system with a gauge, then check each joint with a detector and soap solution. We also walk the yard line, since a dead strip of grass over a buried gas pipe can signal a leak that has run for a long time. We coordinate with the gas utility.

    Commercial Building Leaks in Santa Cruz, CA

    Restaurants, offices and apartment complexes in Santa Cruz pay for every hour a leak keeps running.     Slab Leak Detection in Santa Cruz

We pressure test the hot and cold sides separately, then listen through the slab with a ground microphone and confirm with a thermal camera when the leak is on the hot side.
