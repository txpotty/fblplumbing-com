    Hot Water Copper Line Repair in Santa Cruz, CA

    Hot water lines see more corrosion than cold lines because heat speeds up chemical reactions. Recirculation pumps that run constantly can also wear copper from the inside.     Corrosion-Related Leak Repair in Santa Cruz

    Corrosion is a natural enemy of copper pipes and is made worse by certain water chemistries. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. This can lead to thinning pipe walls and eventual leaks. Our team assesses the extent of corrosion, which might show up as blue-green stains on pipes or fixtures. For localized corrosion, we perform spot repairs, but if corrosion is widespread, we may recommend re-piping sections to prevent recurring issues. We make sure all new connections are properly sealed and protected.

    Copper Fitting and Valve Repair in Santa Cruz
