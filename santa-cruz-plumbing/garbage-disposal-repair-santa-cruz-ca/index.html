    Electrical Issues & Motor Repair in Santa Cruz

    If your garbage disposal hums but doesn't grind, or doesn't turn on at all, it could be an electrical issue or a motor problem. We check circuit breakers, reset buttons, and internal wiring. For motor failures, we assess if a repair is feasible or if replacement is the more cost-effective solution, always keeping your safety first in Santa Cruz.

    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in Santa Cruz, CA

    A disposal that grinds but drains slowly usually has a clogged discharge tube or trap arm. Grease, rice and coffee grounds are the usual culprits. We clear the line with the right tools and check for proper slope. We also explain what not to put the disposal.

    Leaking Disposal Repair in Santa Cruz, CA

    Leaks can come from the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube or the disposal body itself. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty, and replace gaskets or tubes when needed. A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the internal seal has failed. In that case, replacement is the smarter option.
