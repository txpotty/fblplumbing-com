    What Goes Into a PEX Job in Santa Cruz, CA

    Leak Repair with PEX in Santa Cruz, CA

    A single bad section is not always a reason to repipe. We can cut out a split or corroded stretch of copper, transition to PEX with a listed fitting, and have the water back on in an hour or two. That works for one leak. By the third leak, though, we will talk to you honestly about whether the rest of that copper is close behind.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Santa Cruz, CA

    With PEX-A, the tech slips an expansion ring onto the pipe and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting goes in while the plastic is still expanded, and the material's memory pulls it back down around the barb, sealing harder over the first minute. Cold weather slows it. Joints get time to set before we pressurize.

    Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Santa Cruz

    Rough-in heights matter more than people think. If the shower valve sits two inches low or the vanity stubs miss the cabinet, the wall gets reopened after tile, and that costs time and money. We confirm heights with your contractor first. Then we pressure test with the walls open.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Santa Cruz, CA

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. All three are code-listed for drinking water. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Santa Cruz

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. A crimp joint is only as good as the tool, so we check every ring with a go/no-go gauge, and a ring that does not pass gets cut out and redone. That takes seconds. Skipping it is how leaks start.

    Whole-Home PEX Repiping in Santa Cruz

    Low pressure at every faucet, across the whole house, usually points at the pipe and not the fixtures. In an older house that usually means rust-choked galvanized or polybutylene with tired fittings, and a new faucet cartridge will not touch it. A whole-home repipe will. Every line from the main valve to each angle stop gets replaced, and the system is tested before anything gets closed up.

    Emergency PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Santa Cruz, CA

    PEX rarely fails on its own. When it does, the cause is usually a nail or screw driven through it, a rodent chewing on an attic run, or a fitting that was never crimped all the way. We carry the common sizes. Most emergency PEX repairs in Santa Cruz, CA are a cut-and-splice job that takes under an hour once the drywall is open.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in Santa Cruz, CA

    Price on a repipe comes down to a few things: bathroom count, square footage, how many stories, and how easy the attic and walls are to work in. PEX almost always comes in below copper because the material is cheaper and a crew can run a house in two to four days instead of a week. Patching is sometimes quoted on its own line. Ask and we will price all three, PEX-A, PEX-B and copper, side by side.
