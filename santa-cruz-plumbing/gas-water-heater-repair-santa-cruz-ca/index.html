    Gas Valve and Thermostat Repair in Santa Cruz, CA

    Lukewarm showers and a tank that never catches up often trace back to the gas control. We check the thermostat setting, the immersion sensor and the valve's outlet pressure with a manometer. On most residential tanks the thermostat lives inside the gas valve body, so a bad sensor or a stuck magnet usually means replacing the whole control rather than one small part. Plan on roughly an hour for the swap. Every joint on the new valve gets leak tested.

    Tank Leaks & Anode Rods in Santa Cruz

    Corrosion starts at the anode rod and works its way into the steel once the rod is spent. Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. We pull the anode, check how much magnesium or aluminum is left and replace it if the core wire is showing. A fresh anode, a drained tank and a relief valve that seats properly can add years to a heater in a Santa Cruz home that has not started leaking from the shell. Water from the shell seam is another story. That tank needs replacing.

    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Santa Cruz

    Power vent heaters push exhaust through PVC with a blower on top of the tank. When one quits, it is usually the blower motor, the pressure switch or the hot surface igniter. Condensate pooling in a vent with the wrong slope can also trip the pressure switch, so we check the pipe run all the way to the termination before we sign off on a part. The control's error code is our starting point. Every suspect part then gets tested with a meter.

    Repairing Older Gas Water Heaters in Santa Cruz

    Older tanks with standing pilots and no vapor sensor are still out there in Santa Cruz, mostly in basements and utility closets. Their parts are simple, and a universal thermocouple fits nearly all of them. We can keep a pre-2003 heater running when the tank is sound, but we also check the draft hood, the flue connector and the gas cock, since those fittings on an old install are often original and corroded. Plenty of older installs never got a sediment trap. One goes in while we are there.

    Pilot Light Repair in Santa Cruz

    The thermocouple is the first thing we test when a pilot will not stay lit. With a millivolt meter, it takes about a minute to see if it is putting out enough.     Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Santa Cruz

    Since about 2003, most residential gas tanks have been built with a flammable vapor ignition resistant combustion chamber. A flame arrestor screen at the base lets air in and keeps flame from getting out. When lint, dust or pet hair coats that screen, the burner starves, the chamber overheats and a thermal switch shuts the whole heater down, sometimes several times a week. We clean the screen and the chamber. A tripped thermal switch gets replaced.
