    Tank Leaks and Pressure Problems in Santa Cruz

    Even gas water heaters can develop tank leaks, often due to corrosion from hard water or high water pressure. Water hardness in Northern California varies a lot by source, with snowmelt-fed systems running soft and many inland groundwater systems running mineral-heavy.     Gas Control Valve Replacement in Santa Cruz, CA

    The gas control valve regulates temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something goes wrong. If the valve itself has failed, we replace it with an exact match or approved universal model. We leak test and confirm temperature control afterward.

    Gas Valve and Thermostat Repair in Santa Cruz, CA

    Issues with the gas valve or thermostat can lead to uneven water temperatures or a complete lack of hot water. Our team can test these crucial components for proper operation, checking for electrical continuity and gas flow. If a part is defective, we replace it with a quality, manufacturer-approved component so your water heater heats efficiently and safely.

    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Santa Cruz, CA

    A yellow, lazy or noisy flame points to a dirty burner or poor combustion air. We remove and clean the burner, check the orifice size for natural gas or propane and adjust as needed. A clean blue flame heats faster and produces less carbon monoxide. We also check for rust flakes dropping from the flue.

    Burner Assembly and Venting System Repair in Santa Cruz

    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Repair in Santa Cruz

    The cost of gas water heater repair in Santa Cruz, CA can vary a lot depending on the issue. Minor repairs like a thermocouple replacement might range from $150 to $350, while more complex issues such as a gas valve replacement could be $400 to $800 or more. We always provide a detailed, upfront estimate once we diagnose the problem.

    We repair Rheem, Ruud, A.O. Smith, State, Bradford White, American Standard and Whirlpool gas tanks, among others. We stock common pilot and valve parts.

    Most common gas water heater repairs, such as replacing a thermocouple or thermostat, can be completed within 1 to 3 hours. More complex issues or those requiring special parts might take longer, but we always aim for same-day repair whenever possible.

    On average, a well-maintained gas water heater can last 10 to 12 years. Regular maintenance, including flushing the tank, can help extend its lifespan. If your unit is nearing this age and having frequent issues, replacement might be more sensible than continuous repairs.

    Many Honeywell and White-Rodgers valves use flash codes to report problems like a weak thermopile or a tripped sensor. We read the code and test the matching part.

    Because of the risks that come with natural gas and possible carbon monoxide poisoning, gas water heater repairs should always be handled by a licensed professional. DIY attempts can be dangerous and may void warranties or violate local Santa Cruz, CA plumbing codes.

    Look out for a lack of hot water, inconsistent water temperature, a pilot light that won't stay lit, unusual noises like rumbling or popping, a rotten egg smell, which can point to a gas leak, or visible leaks around the tank. Any of these call for a professional inspection.

    A thermocouple is a safety device in a gas water heater that senses if the pilot light is lit. If it doesn't detect heat, it shuts off the gas supply to prevent gas leaks. A faulty thermocouple is a leading reason for a pilot light not staying lit and is a critical safety component.
