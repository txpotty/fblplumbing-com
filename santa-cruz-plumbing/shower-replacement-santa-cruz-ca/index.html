    Frequently Asked Questions About Shower Replacement in Santa Cruz

    Shower replacement in Santa Cruz, CA runs from about $1,500 for a basic unit swap to over $10,000 for a custom tiled walk-in. Most of the spread comes from the shower type, the materials you pick and how much plumbing has to move, especially if the drain shifts on a slab. Tile costs more. You get a detailed quote after we inspect.

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. The tub comes out and a new pan, walls and fixtures go in, with the drain moved as needed. It is easier to use.

    We install Moen, Delta, Kohler, Grohe, Hansgrohe and American Standard. Sterling and American Standard units are common choices too.

    Leaks that come back after repairs are the clearest sign. Cracks in the pan or surround, mold that returns after cleaning, a soft floor near the shower and a drain that clogs constantly all point toward replacement. Looks count too.
