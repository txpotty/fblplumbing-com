    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Santa Cruz

    Salt-based (ion exchange) water softeners are the most common and effective solution for truly softening water. These systems use a resin bed to exchange hard minerals like calcium and magnesium with sodium ions. FBL Plumbing expertly installs various models of salt-based softeners, ensuring proper connection to your main water line, correct drainage setup and accurate programming for regeneration cycles in your Santa Cruz home.

    Common signs of hard water include soap scum buildup on fixtures and shower doors, dry skin and hair, dull laundry and scale buildup inside pipes and appliances like water heaters. We can perform a water hardness test in your Santa Cruz home to confirm your need for a softener.
