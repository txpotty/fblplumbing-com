    Metered Softener Replacement in Santa Cruz

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. A metered valve, Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1, tracks gallons and regenerates when the resin actually needs it. Most people notice less salt in a few weeks.

    Commercial Water Softener Replacement in Santa Cruz

    Restaurants and laundromats run softeners hard, and a commercial unit often needs a 1.5-inch or 2-inch valve and twin tanks to keep up with flow. We size it to peak gallons per minute. We work around your business hours.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Santa Cruz, CA

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. A Fleck needs hardness, household size and regen time entered right, otherwise it cycles too often or runs out of capacity. It is programmed before we pack up.

    Upsizing a Whole-Home Softener in Santa Cruz

    Undersized softeners are common in homes that added a bathroom or a couple of teenagers since the original system went in. What worked for three people can run out of capacity in a few days with five. We size for the house you have today.

    Replacing Pelican Water Systems in Santa Cruz

    Pelican systems usually combine a carbon filter with either a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner in one setup on the main line. Depending on how old it is, we swap media or the full unit. Following the install specs keeps the warranty intact.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Santa Cruz, CA

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. New resin in the old tank sometimes beats a full replacement on price. It depends on the valve. An old valve on new resin is a short-lived fix.

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. A TAC conditioner is the honest alternative there, and we tell people plainly that it prevents scale but will not give them the slick feel of soft water. Sodium concerns are another reason.
