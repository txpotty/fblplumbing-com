    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Santa Cruz

    An undermount depends on its clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone and brace it while the adhesive sets, which usually takes overnight before the sink can hold a full load of water. Strainers and traps go in after.

    Granite and Quartz Composite Sinks in Santa Cruz

    Composite sinks outweigh stainless bowls and usually thicker at the drain, so standard strainers sometimes need a longer threaded body. Strainer fit gets checked first. When the strainer is too short for the sink thickness, we replace it instead of cranking it down and chipping the drain opening.

    Emergency Sink Replacement in Santa Cruz

    A cracked bowl or a rusted-through strainer can flood a cabinet within hours. First, close both angle stops and stop using the sink. We carry common sizes. If you have a standard 33 by 22 inch drop-in, we can usually remove the damaged sink and set a new one that day.

    Apron-Front Sinks in Santa Cruz, CA

    Apron sinks need a cabinet cut to fit the front panel, and the sink has to be in place before the counter is templated. We set the sink and frame early, so the fabricator measures from the actual bowl instead of a spec sheet. That avoids an uneven reveal. A cast iron apron sink can weigh 150 pounds or more, so two of us show up.

    Sink and Disposal Installed Together in Santa Cruz, CA

    Adding a disposal costs least when the sink is already out. We mount the disposal on the new flange, set the trap height for its outlet, and knock out the dishwasher plug so the hose has somewhere to go. You also need power. There needs to be a switched outlet under the sink.

    Disposal and Dishwasher Hookup with Sink Installation in Santa Cruz, CA

    Most new kitchen sinks get paired with a disposal and a dishwasher. The disposal gets mounted, the dishwasher drain goes through an air gap or high loop, and the dishwasher gets its own hot line and shutoff. Doing it all at once saves money. Every connection is tested under real use.

    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Santa Cruz

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and the lower the number, the thicker the steel. Either way, we hook up the faucet, strainers and disposal.
