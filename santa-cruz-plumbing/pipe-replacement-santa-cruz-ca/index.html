    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Santa Cruz, CA

    Many older homes in Santa Cruz, CA still have galvanized steel pipes, which are prone to internal corrosion over time. This corrosion chokes water flow, reduces water pressure, and can introduce rust particles into your drinking water. Our pipe replacement service targets these corroded sections, replacing them with new, non-corrosive materials like PEX or copper. This restores proper water flow and ensures clean, clear water throughout your home.

    PEX Repiping in Santa Cruz

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale buildup and expands slightly if water freezes, which helps in colder homes. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe for stronger flow. Systems can be run as a home-run manifold with a shutoff for each fixture or as a trunk and branch layout. PEX installs faster than copper with far fewer fittings in the walls. It is our most popular repipe material.

    Commercial Pipe Replacement in Santa Cruz

    Beyond residential services, FBL Plumbing also provides commercial pipe replacement for businesses and properties in Santa Cruz. Commercial plumbing systems often face higher demands and require specific knowledge. Whether it's replacing supply lines in an office building, drainage pipes in a restaurant, or addressing complex industrial piping, our experienced commercial plumbers ensure minimal downtime and durable solutions tailored to your business needs.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Santa Cruz, CA

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. They are caused by a mix of water chemistry, high velocity and thin Type M pipe, and once they begin they tend to keep coming back. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes. We help you weigh the cost of both paths.

    Whole-Home Re-Piping in Santa Cruz

    For homes with widespread pipe issues like frequent leaks, discolored water, or low water pressure, a full-home re-piping might be the best solution. We thoroughly assess your existing system, often finding old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes that are past their lifespan. Our team provides efficient replacement with modern, durable materials like PEX or copper, significantly upgrading your entire plumbing infrastructure. This complete service in Santa Cruz ensures consistent water flow and eliminates future pipe-related headaches.

    Water Line Replacement vs. Repair in Santa Cruz, CA

    Deciding between pipe repair and full replacement depends on several factors, including the age of your pipes, the extent of the damage, and the frequency of past issues. A small, isolated leak might be repaired, but widespread corrosion or multiple leaks usually warrant replacement. FBL Plumbing provides straightforward assessments in Santa Cruz, CA, explaining the pros and cons of each option. We help you make an informed decision that offers the most cost-effective and durable solution for your home.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Santa Cruz, CA

    Copper piping is a long-standing, proven choice known for its durability and resistance to corrosion. While it's a premium material, its longevity often makes it a cost-effective solution over time. FBL Plumbing offers expert copper pipe replacement for sections or entire homes in Santa Cruz, CA.     Drain Pipe Replacement in Santa Cruz, CA

    Cast iron drain pipe in older homes can rust through at the bottom, while some ABS from the 1980s was made with defective resin that cracks. Signs include sewer odors, slow drains and stains on ceilings below upstairs bathrooms. We camera the line first, then replace reachable sections with PVC or ABS.
