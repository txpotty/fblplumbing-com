    Gas Water Heater Efficiency Ratings in Santa Cruz

    Efficiency ratings on tank heaters are listed as UEF, uniform energy factor, and a higher number means less gas per gallon heated. A standard tank sits around 0.60 to 0.65. High-efficiency condensing tanks reach 0.80 or more and can qualify for rebates.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Upgrades in Santa Cruz, CA

    Condensing gas tanks run around 90 percent efficient or better and vent through PVC, while a standard atmospheric tank sits closer to 60 percent. You will need a condensate drain and a nearby outlet. Payback depends on usage.

    A.O. Smith Gas Water Heater Service in Santa Cruz

    A.O. Smith makes tanks under its own name and also builds the State line, so parts overlap a lot between the two brands. We handle the sizing and the gas connection. Exhaust venting gets checked against their specs.

    40 and 50 Gallon Tank Replacement in Santa Cruz

    Forty and fifty gallon gas tanks are the workhorse in most Northern California homes, and a leak at the bottom seam means the tank is finished. Current tanks are often taller and wider. We measure the closet before ordering. Plan on about two to three hours.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Replacement in Santa Cruz, CA

    Rheem Performance and Performance Platinum tanks cover most of the range we install, from basic 6-year models to 12-year tanks with better anodes. More warranty years generally buy a heavier anode rod. We help you pick.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Santa Cruz, CA

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. We never swap a natural gas tank onto propane without the right conversion. We confirm pressure with a manometer.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Service in Santa Cruz, CA

    Rheem tanks and their Ruud twins share most parts, which helps when a gas valve fails a few years in. We install both. Setup follows the factory manual, including the draft test.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Santa Cruz, CA

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through 2 or 3-inch PVC, so they can sit far from a chimney. A standard outlet has to be within reach of the cord. We run the vent within the maker's length and elbow limits.
