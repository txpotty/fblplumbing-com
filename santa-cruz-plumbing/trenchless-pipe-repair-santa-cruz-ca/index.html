    Perma-Liner and Picote Solutions in Santa Cruz

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Both matter. Picote milling heads clear roots, scale and old fittings out of the pipe before lining, and after the liner cures, they cut open the lateral connections so every drain in your home flows again. A clean pipe holds a liner best.

    Sectional Point Repair in Santa Cruz

    Sometimes only one joint or a few feet of pipe is bad. A spot liner fixes that. We position a short resin-soaked sleeve over the damaged area with a camera, inflate it and let it cure, so you pay for a few feet of liner instead of the whole run. It suits isolated damage in Santa Cruz laterals.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Santa Cruz, CA

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe. As a winch drags the head through, the old pipe fractures into the soil and new HDPE is pulled into the same path behind it. HDPE resists corrosion and leaks in Santa Cruz, CA soil.

    Pipe Bursting in Santa Cruz, CA

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. It needs two pits. The head fractures the old pipe as it travels, with new HDPE trailing behind, fused into one length with no joints.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Repair Cost in Santa Cruz, CA

    Per foot of pipe, trenchless can cost more than open trenching. That's not the whole bill.     Sewer Lateral Lining in Santa Cruz

    Laterals are the most common candidates for trenchless repair. Clay and Orangeburg fail often. Over time, clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe crack, sag or fill with roots, so we jet, scope and then pick lining or bursting. The new line restores full flow and stands up to roots.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Costs in Santa Cruz, CA

    On paper, the two methods can price out close. Then add restoration. A trench under a driveway or through an established garden means paying to replace concrete, plants and irrigation after the plumbing is done, and trenchless avoids most of those costs. We price both so you can compare.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Santa Cruz

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Santa Cruz yard.
