    Storm and Yard Drain Jetting in Fremont, CA

    Yard drains, channel drains and downspout lines fill with sand, silt and leaves between storms. Drought years alternate with very wet years here, so conservation rules and efficient fixtures are a constant part of plumbing decisions. A clogged yard drain sends that water back toward the house, so we jet the lines from the outlet end and flush the catch basins before the wet season starts. We also check the pop-up emitters.

    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Fremont

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. Cabling only cuts a narrow channel. Jetting with a spinning nozzle strips the grease from the full circumference so it flushes downstream instead of re-forming a plug at the next fitting.

    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Fremont

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. Jetting the main clears roots, grease and sludge all the way to the city connection. On a 4-inch line we use a heavier nozzle with more flow, which moves the loosened debris downstream instead of leaving it to settle in a low spot.

    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in Fremont

    Preventive jetting makes sense when the same line clogs more than once a year. Camera findings set the interval. Grease-heavy lines may need six-month service, while root-prone sewers can often go 18 to 24 months.

    Whole-House Drain Jetting in Fremont

    Slow drains in several rooms point to buildup in the main or a branch line. We jet the main from the cleanout and the branches from roof vents or from fixture access points as needed for that layout. Most homes take two to three hours.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Fremont, CA

    Older cast iron drains rust and scale from the inside, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. A jet clears that crust. Thin or cracked cast iron gets gentler settings or a lining recommendation instead.
