    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Installation in Fremont

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon gas water heater usually costs $1,800 to $4,000 installed. Power vent and tankless gas units often cost more due to venting and gas line requirements.

    The main difference is the type of gas they use and the burners and orifices required for combustion. Natural gas is typically supplied by a utility line, while propane is stored in a tank. We install both types and confirm correct setup for your fuel source.

    New gas water heaters typically come with a manufacturer's warranty ranging from 6 to 12 years on the tank and components. FBL Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation services, giving you peace of mind.

    Most standard gas water heater installations take between 3 and 5 hours. More complex installations, such as converting to a tankless unit or significant gas line adjustments, might take a full day. We work quickly to minimize disruption to your home.

    We install Rheem, A.O. Smith, Bradford White, Navien and Rinnai. We recommend models based on reliability, efficiency and warranty.

    Regular maintenance, such as annual flushing of the tank to remove sediment and checking the anode rod, can noticeably extend your gas water heater's lifespan. Our team can advise on best practices and offer maintenance services.

    Common signs include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, strange noises like rumbling or popping from the tank, or visible leaks around the base of the unit. Older units, especially those over 10 to 12 years old, are also candidates for replacement.

    Installing a gas water heater involves working with natural gas lines, water lines and exhaust venting, which can be dangerous if not done correctly. Improper installation can lead to gas leaks, carbon monoxide poisoning or water damage. It is highly recommended to hire a licensed professional for safety and code compliance.
