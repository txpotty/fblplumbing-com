    Pop-Up Drain and Stopper Repair in Fremont, CA

    Leaking P-Trap Repair in Fremont

    Most puddles under a bathroom sink start at the P-trap. Slip-joint washers flatten over time, plastic traps crack, and old chrome traps rust through from the inside.
