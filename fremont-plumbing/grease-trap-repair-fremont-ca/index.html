    Why Choose FBL Plumbing for Grease Trap Repair in Fremont, CA?

    Missing outlet tees are the single most common repair we make. They get knocked loose during pumping more than you would think. Without the tee, the outlet draws from the top of the tank, right where the grease floats, and the trap sends grease to the sewer while looking perfectly fine from above. Replacing one is about a half-hour job.

    • Honest Advice, If a trap is past saving, we say so and quote a new one.
    • Quality Parts, Manufacturer parts or commercial-grade materials that hold up to grease and hot water.
    • Specialized Tools, Jetters and sewer cameras let us find the blockage and clear it instead of guessing.

    Citywide Coverage

    Indoor traps under three-compartment sinks and outdoor interceptors under the parking lot are both on our list. We service each type every week. For buried tanks we bring a vacuum truck partner, and for indoor units the plumber can usually pump the trap by hand into sealed drums and carry it out the back door. Grease waste goes to a licensed disposal site.

    Experienced Team

    Zurn, Watts, Schier, Thermaco and Endura traps all come through our shop. Each brand has its own weak point, and our crew knows them. Schier units are tough polyethylene but the lid gaskets wear, older steel Zurn and Watts traps rust out at the bottom seams, and Big Dipper automatic units need their skimmer wheel and heater checked. Parts get ordered by model number.

    Safety-First Approach

    Health codes treat a grease backup as a sanitation problem, not just plumbing. Waste stays in sealed hoses and drums from start to finish. Tools and boots that touched the trap do not cross into food prep areas without being cleaned, and we wipe down every surface near the trap before the kitchen staff steps back in. Nothing should be left for an inspector to note.

    Commercial Grease Trap Repair in Fremont

    Restaurants, school cafeterias, hospital kitchens and bakeries all push more grease than a small trap can hold. Undersized traps fail in predictable spots. The inlet tee clogs, the outlet tee gets coated, and grease starts showing up in the sewer manhole down the street, which is usually how the city finds out before the owner does. We fix the failed parts and tell you plainly if the trap is too small for the kitchen.

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Fremont

    Inside a concrete gravity interceptor the baffle is usually a poured or precast wall with a crossover opening. A crumbling wall lets grease slip into the second compartment and out the outlet. We can rebuild the wall with form work and a sulfate-resistant mix or set a polyethylene baffle panel, depending on the tank. Either way, it needs a full pump-out first.

    Commercial Grease Interceptor Repair in Fremont, CA

    In-ground gravity interceptors run from about 500 gallons to several thousand, usually precast concrete with two compartments. Hydrogen sulfide from the waste eats the concrete above the waterline over time. We pump the tank, inspect the walls and the crossover, and repair spalled concrete with a cementitious patch or an epoxy liner rated for that service. Entry follows confined space rules.
