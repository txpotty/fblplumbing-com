    Frequently Asked Questions About Repipe Specialists in Fremont

    Most PEX repipes for Northern California homes cost $5,000 to $12,000, depending on size, bathrooms and access. Copper repipes usually run 20 to 40 percent more.

    PEX resists scale and pinhole corrosion and handles expansion well, which makes it a solid choice for hard water. Copper is durable too but costs more and can pinhole in aggressive water.

    Homes on hard water see scale on fixtures and water heaters, while very soft water can be slightly corrosive to older copper pipe. Hard water can lead to mineral buildup, or scale, inside pipes over time. This buildup constricts water flow, reduces pressure, and can accelerate corrosion, especially in older metal pipes. Repiping with materials like PEX or properly installed copper, along with considering a water softener, can mitigate these effects and extend the life of your plumbing system.

    Most whole-home repipes can be completed within 3 to 5 days, depending on the size and complexity of your home and the chosen piping material. We strive to minimize water shut-off periods, often restoring water service by the end of each workday. Our team works efficiently to get your plumbing fully operational as soon as possible.

    Copper is a rigid, durable metal with a long lifespan, known for its resistance to bacteria. PEX is a flexible plastic tubing that is more affordable, easier to install, resistant to freezing and corrosion, and very quiet. Both are strong choices for repiping, and our specialists can help you decide which material is best suited for your home and budget in Fremont, CA.

    While fixing individual leaks might seem cheaper in the short term, it's often a temporary solution for an aging plumbing system. If you're experiencing multiple leaks, low pressure, or discolored water, it points to widespread pipe wear. Repiping addresses the root cause by replacing the entire system, providing a long-term, reliable solution and preventing future problems.

    Most homes are repiped in two to four days, plus a day for drywall patching. Water is usually restored each evening.

    Repiping is a complex plumbing project that requires specialized tools, knowledge of plumbing codes, and real experience. Attempting a DIY repipe can lead to costly mistakes, leaks, and potential code violations. It's highly recommended to hire professional repipe specialists like FBL Plumbing to make sure the job is done correctly, safely, and to code.
